The Performance Marketing Association (PMA) 2025 industry study is the firmest macro anchor. Published program terms change often - verify against the live terms page before relying on any single number.

Gross-margin ceiling logic behind the spread:

- 30-60% margin physical goods sustain 5-15%.
- 75-85% margin SaaS sustains 20-30% recurring.
- ~100% margin digital products sustain 30-50%.

## Published SaaS program terms (data points, verify live pages)

- HubSpot: 30% recurring for 12 months, 180-day cookie, $10 minimum payout.
- Notion: 50% recurring for 12 months on net-new paid workspaces, 180-day cookie.
- Teachable: 30% recurring for the first 12 months, 30-day cookie (own page, directories conflict).
- Hunter.io (verbatim terms): "30% commission on every customer who upgrades to a paid account and remains a paid customer for at least 45 days... limited to the first 12 months... Cookie duration: 30 days... Delay: within 45 days... Threshold: minimum payout of $100".
- Semrush: moved off recurring to a flat $200/sale plus $10/trial CPA, 120-day cookie.

## Validation, net terms, thresholds, clawback

- Validation/locking: 30-60 days typical. CJ's locking cycle is configurable 7-60 days after the event. Adam Riemer (independent consultant, adamriemer.me, July 2026), verbatim: "The industry has an average 30 day window for a company to void commissions where customers cancel or when credit cards get chargebacks."
- Net terms: net-30 is the norm affiliates expect, while net-60 applies for higher return risk (Tolt payout guide, 2025 - vendor blog). Riemer's stacking warning: validation plus net-30 means "the affiliate now has to wait between 3 and 1/2 to 4 months to get paid" - and affiliates "are going to go with the one that pays the fastest if most other things are equal." Payout speed is a recruiting lever.
- Minimum threshold: $25 / $50 / $100 tiers benchmarked. $50-100 is recommended for most SaaS programs (Tolt, 2025). $50 is the SaaS default.
- Observed clawback rate: 2.55% of gross commission value reversed after refunds/cancellations, across 9.9M tracked transactions (FirstPromoter, 2026, own-customer telemetry).
- Managed payout processing fees: 2-3% typical for automated payout services (Tolt, 2025). B2B partnership platforms can charge ~15% of commissions paid (PartnerStack published pricing). Include these fees in the cost-per-customer math.

## Macro anchor

PMA Industry Study 2025 (fielded by London Research, with data from eight networks including Awin, CJ, Partnerize, Rakuten Advertising, ShareASale): US affiliate spend grew from $9.1B (2021) to $13.62B (2024), driving $113B in ecommerce sales - 9.4% of all US ecommerce. ROAS: travel $19:1, retail $11:1. Firmest observed data in the space, though 2026-dated vendor projections should be treated as estimates.
