# Per-Format Placement Rules

These rules are format-driven, not platform-driven: judge an ephemeral story-like unit the same way on any platform. Layer Italy's Digital Chart rules on top when the audience includes Italy.

| Format                              | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Regulator behind it                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Short-form video                    | Disclosure in the video itself - on-screen and spoken - not only in the caption or description; place it at the start, before the endorsement.                                                                   | FTC (Disclosures 101); CMA (beginning of videos); IAP (overlay at opening plus description)                                         |
| Livestream                          | Repeat the disclosure periodically so viewers who see only part of the stream still get it; once at the start fails.                                                                                             | FTC                                                                                                                                 |
| Story / ephemeral                   | Superimpose the disclosure over the image itself, on screen long enough to notice and read; caption-only or bio-only fails.                                                                                      | FTC; IAP (overlaid on the promotional element)                                                                                      |
| Static image post caption           | Disclosure in the visible, untruncated opening of the caption - never behind the "more" cut, in a hashtag wall, or on the profile page.                                                                          | FTC ("anywhere that requires a person to click MORE"); CMA (no scrolling required); IAP (first information or first three hashtags) |
| Long-form video                     | In-video disclosure at the start; for a mid-video endorsement, also right before or at the endorsement itself. Description-only fails.                                                                           | FTC                                                                                                                                 |
| Podcast / audio                     | Spoken disclosure at volume, speed, and cadence an ordinary listener can hear and understand - matching the ad read's own prominence. No podcast-specific FTC page exists; the audible 255.0(f) factors govern.  | FTC (16 CFR 255.0(f)); CMA (beginning of podcasts)                                                                                  |
| Blog / review article               | Disclosure close to - better, part of - the endorsement it covers: top of page and before any affiliate link, not footer-only, not after distracting outbound links.                                             | FTC                                                                                                                                 |
| Newsletter / email                  | No format-specific FTC rule exists - say so if asked. Apply the general standard: disclose adjacent to the sponsored block or affiliate link, before the reader acts, visually separated from editorial content. | FTC (general clear-and-conspicuous principle only)                                                                                  |
| Affiliate roundup / comparison page | The reader must see the disclosure and the affiliate link at the same time; a page-top disclosure separated from the links fails. "Paid link" adjacent to a link is adequate.                                    | FTC                                                                                                                                 |
| Carousel (multi-item post)          | Label each item that contains promotional messaging, not just the first slide.                                                                                                                                   | CMA                                                                                                                                 |

Reading the table:

- Treat the rows as gates, never as a ranked menu: every applicable row must pass, and no row is traded off against another because it is cheaper to satisfy.
- Treat each row as the placement minimum for that format; the wording still has to pass on its own.
- When two regulators apply, apply the strictest applicable row element - e.g. a video for a US-and-Italy audience needs the spoken/in-frame disclosure and the description line and the opening overlay.
- When the artefact can't show a row's element (a script can't prove overlay duration), mark the check "not assessable from the artefact provided" and list what to verify on the rendered asset.
