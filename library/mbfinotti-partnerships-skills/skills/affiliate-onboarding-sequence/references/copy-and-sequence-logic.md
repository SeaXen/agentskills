# Touch Copy and Sequence Logic

How to draft each touch's copy, and the flow-control rules that keep the sequence honest once affiliates start behaving differently from the plan.

## Copy rules per touch

- Ask one action per touch, with one CTA. A second ask halves the first's chance; move it to the next touch.
- Put the tracking link or coupon code in the first two lines of touch 1 - the single highest-leverage placement decision in the sequence.
- Keep touch 1 under ~300 words. Later touches: 50-125 words for action-only touches (T4-style nudges), 150-300 for enablement touches (T2/T3-style).
- Write CTAs as action + outcome ("Copy your link and place it in your top review post"), never "learn more".
- Name the affiliate's archetype context, not the program's ("your listeners", "your comparison page"), so the ask lands in their world.
- State money mechanics plainly wherever they appear: commission, cookie window, payout threshold, hold period, clawback state. Vague money copy reads as evasive to professional affiliates.
- Frame the activation nudge as an opportunity with a finish line ("earn X by <date>"), never as a condition for staying in the program - the threat framing is the documented counterproductive version of the same message.
- Match sender to job: automated program address for provisioning and mechanics; the named AM (when one exists) for the connection touch and anything asking for a reply. A reply-seeking email from a no-reply address answers itself.

## Subject lines

- Lead with the concrete thing inside: "Your GlowKit link + code", "Your sandbox is live", "One email to send this week".
- Keep the affiliate's action or asset in the subject, not the program's excitement ("Welcome to the family!" says nothing to act on).
- Milestone-trigger subjects name the milestone: "Your first sale just tracked".

## The humanizer pass

Run every drafted email through your preferred humanizer skill before presenting it as final; reject raw first-draft model output. Affiliates are professional recipients of partner email - template-scented copy trains them to skim, and the sequence's entire mechanism is getting one action read and taken. The pass must preserve the one-action structure and the money mechanics verbatim; it rewrites tone, rhythm, and phrasing only.

## Exit, suppression, and re-entry rules

Define these before launch; a sequence without exits keeps selling activation to people who already activated.

- **Exit on activation**: the moment the activation event tracks, leave the calendar track. Fire the celebration touch, then hand over to ongoing program communications - continuing the onboarding drip past activation reads as nobody-watching automation.
- **Exit on the never-activated branch**: entry into the branch ends the main sequence; the branch's own final-notice/archive rules take over.
- **Suppression**: pause calendar touches while a reply thread with the AM is open, and honor unsubscribes program-wide immediately. Behavior-triggered transactional notices (commission approved, payout sent) survive marketing unsubscribes only where the program's terms and local law say so - flag the question, don't assume.
- **Trigger-overrides-calendar**: when a behavior trigger fires within a day of a scheduled calendar touch, send the trigger touch and skip or delay the calendar one - two emails in a day from the same program is the fastest route to the skim reflex.
- **Re-entry**: an affiliate reactivated from the archive re-enters at T2 (tools), not T1 - they don't need re-approval, they need a working asset and one action.

## Diagnosing copy vs structure

This separation runs after the ordered checks in the skill's diagnosis section, never instead of them - it names which plane a confirmed fault sits in, and carries no ordering of its own to compete with theirs. Ranking these four branches would be false precision: they are mutually exclusive readings of one engagement pattern, so you land on exactly one and never choose between them.

When a running sequence underperforms, separate the two failure planes before rewriting anything:

- Opens fine, clicks poor → copy plane: ask unclear, link buried, wrong asset for the archetype.
- Opens poor from touch 2 onward → structure plane: touch 1 overloaded or mis-targeted; the welcome dump trains ignoring.
- Clicks fine, activation poor → neither: check tracking first (the click may be converting invisibly), then the asset kit's fit, then the nudge's deadline.
- Everything fine until the nudge, then silence → the incentive's economics, not its copy - route to the commission skill.
