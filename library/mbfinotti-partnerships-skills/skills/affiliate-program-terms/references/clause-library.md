# Canonical Clause Library

The clause skeleton below recurs across real published agreements from major SaaS vendors, ecommerce marketplaces, and affiliate networks. It is the same for B2B SaaS and B2C ecommerce; only the economic clauses diverge, and workflow step 4 loads the B2B/B2C contrast alongside this file for exactly that reason. Draft every clause or mark it "intentionally omitted - <reason>".

## Acceptance mechanics

- Draft as clickwrap: a standard acceptance clause incorporating the agreement by reference when the affiliate clicks "I agree" at signup. No wet signatures, no negotiation.
- Expect no redlining; these are contracts of adhesion. Optionally allow named high-value partners to redline specific clauses via a side letter.
- Bind amendments through "continued participation equals acceptance", paired with the notice mechanics in the Amendment clause below.

## The clauses

| Clause                                       | What it typically says                                                                                                                  | Variations & drafting cautions                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Definitions                                  | Defines Affiliate, Merchant/Company, Qualifying Purchase/Transaction, Affiliate Link, Commission, Net Revenue, cookie/attribution terms | SaaS adds Customer Transaction, Subscription; define Net Revenue precisely - it drives every payment dispute                                                                                                                                            |
| Eligibility & approval                       | Participation subject to written acceptance; company may reject or revoke at any time                                                   | Manual vs auto-approval; site-quality review; prohibited content categories; geography; age; tax documentation (W-9/W-8BEN); one-account rules; activity minimums                                                                                       |
| Licence to brand assets                      | Limited, non-exclusive, revocable licence to approved creatives only; no modification; no use in domain names or social handles         | Auto-terminate the licence on any termination, with asset destruction within ~5 business days - the most commonly missing sub-clause                                                                                                                    |
| Commission & payment                         | Rate, triggering event, Net Revenue basis, minimum payout threshold, method, schedule                                                   | Cite the rate structure decided elsewhere; never invent numbers here. One-time vs recurring; percentage vs flat                                                                                                                                         |
| Tracking & attribution                       | Tracking method, cookie/attribution window, last-click default, discrepancy resolution                                                  | Last-click dominates; state whose tracking data is authoritative - unlocked attribution methodology is a top affiliate complaint                                                                                                                        |
| Prohibited tactics & enforcement             | Enumerated bans plus a graduated enforcement ladder                                                                                     | Drafted in full at workflow step 5; never a bare catch-all                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Disclosure obligations                       | Affiliate must clearly and conspicuously disclose the material connection in every promotion                                            | US: FTC-compliant; UK: "Ad" label per ASA/CAP. Some programs mandate an exact phrase. Writing the obligation is this document's job; auditing content is not                                                                                            |
| IP & confidentiality                         | Protects non-public program data, rates, customer data                                                                                  | Confidentiality commonly survives termination for a fixed period (multi-year at major networks)                                                                                                                                                         |
| Data protection                              | Allocates privacy-law roles; incorporates a DPA                                                                                         | Contested territory: the advertiser is always a controller, but processor vs joint controller vs independent controller for the network/affiliate is genuinely disputed. Pick a position deliberately and record it in the DPA; always `[LEGAL REVIEW]` |
| Representations & warranties                 | Affiliate warrants legal compliance, site ownership, no IP infringement                                                                 | Keep symmetric where possible; one-sided reps read as adhesive overreach                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Indemnity                                    | Affiliate indemnifies company for affiliate conduct and content                                                                         | Often one-sided toward the company; consider company indemnity for product claims                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Limitation of liability                      | Caps company exposure, often to fees paid                                                                                               | Near-universal; `[LEGAL REVIEW]` the cap level and carve-outs                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Term & termination                           | For-cause (immediate) and for-convenience (typically 30 days' notice); at-will near-universal                                           | EU/UK business users: 30-day notice plus statement of reasons under P2B                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Effect of termination on pending commissions | States what happens to earned-but-unpaid commissions                                                                                    | The single most commonly missing clause; split fraud (forfeit) from non-fraud (pay accrued)                                                                                                                                                             |
| Clawback & reversal                          | Reverses commissions on returns, cancellations, chargebacks, fraud                                                                      | Bounded by the validation/locking window during which a commission may still be approved, declined, or reversed; cite whatever duration the commission structure already set                                                                            |
| Amendment                                    | Company may amend; notice by electronic means; continued participation is acceptance                                                    | EU/UK: 15-day minimum notice, non-retroactive, affiliate may terminate instead of accepting                                                                                                                                                             |
| Assignment                                   | Affiliate may not assign without consent; binding on successors                                                                         | Some programs bar assignment "by operation of law or otherwise" without approval                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Governing law & disputes                     | Company's home jurisdiction; arbitration common                                                                                         | Mandatory arbitration is frequent but jurisdiction-sensitive; always `[LEGAL REVIEW]`                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Tax & independent-contractor status          | No employment relationship; affiliate bears own taxes; tax forms required                                                               | Pair with the eligibility clause's tax-documentation requirement                                                                                                                                                                                        |

## Ownership and baseline

- In-house/SaaS-hosted program: the company drafts its own full agreement; the program manager owns it operationally, counsel holds final sign-off, finance reviews payment and clawback terms.
- Network-hosted program: the network's master publisher agreement governs; draft only the program-specific layer (commission terms, validation window, prohibited-tactics additions, brand rules) that sits on top.
- Marketplace-style programs impose a single non-negotiable operating agreement plus incorporated policy documents - incorporation by reference lets policies update without amending the master.
