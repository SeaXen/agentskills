# Examples

One full positive worked example (B2C), the B2B criteria-weighting variant, and one annotated negative example. Every brand, creator, and number below is invented for illustration - never present these figures as real industry data. In a real engagement, every score comes from cited evidence gathered in the workflow.

## Positive: B2C discovery brief with scored shortlist

Scenario (fictional): Loam & Leaf, a DTC houseplant-subscription brand.

- Objective: conversion.
- Target customer: urban renters 25-40 who own fewer than five plants.
- Converting markets: US, UK.
- Platforms: Instagram, TikTok.
- Budget: mid, cash + product.
- Creators to contract: three.
- Redlines: no pesticide-misuse content, no competitor subscription boxes.

```
DISCOVERY BRIEF - Loam & Leaf, 2026-08-25
Header    : Loam & Leaf | conversion | urban renters 25-40, <5 plants | US+UK | IG, TikTok
Criteria  : approved via brainstorming gate (objective-led fixed weights)
```

| Criterion              | Weight | How measured                                     | Pass floor                     |
| ---------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Audience fit           | 35%    | Audience-insights demo export + comment sampling | >=50% in 25-40 urban segment   |
| Engagement quality     | 25%    | Platform-native ER + manual comment scan         | Above pool median for tier     |
| Niche consistency      | 15%    | Last 30 posts categorized                        | >=60% plant/home content       |
| Geography match        | 15%    | Geo split from export                            | >=55% US+UK audience           |
| Sponsored track record | 10%    | #ad posts vs. organic baseline                   | No collapse on sponsored posts |

Knockouts (applied before scoring):

- Redline scan of last 3 months.
- Active competitor-box partnership.
- Undisclosed-sponsorship pattern.
- Authenticity red flags stacking.
- Geography below floor.

Shortlist (scores 1-5 per criterion; every cell carries an evidence link in the real artifact):

| Candidate                    | Aud. fit | Engage. | Niche | Geo | Sponsored | Weighted | Rank |
| ---------------------------- | -------- | ------- | ----- | --- | --------- | -------- | ---- |
| @rootedinbrooklyn (IG, 24K)  | 5        | 4       | 5     | 5   | 4         | 4.65     | 1    |
| @flatplantclub (TikTok, 61K) | 4        | 5       | 4     | 4   | 4         | 4.25     | 2    |
| @thegreenlandlady (IG, 112K) | 4        | 3       | 5     | 4   | 3         | 3.80     | 3    |

Overlap check: @rootedinbrooklyn and @flatplantclub share <10% sampled frequent commenters; no overlap with the existing roster. No demotion.

Rejected with reason:

- @plantsandpups (89K): niche consistency 40% - below the 60% floor; audience follows for the dogs.
- @urbanjungleguy (240K): knockout - active partnership with a competitor subscription box.
- @leafdropdaily (33K): parked "authenticity unverified" - generic comment wall, engagement out of line with reach.
- @conservatorycait (150K): geography knockout - 71% of audience outside US/UK per export.

Why this brief passes the quality gate:

- Weights were approved before scoring.
- Knockouts ran first.
- Every score cites an export or profile link.
- Nobody was ranked on follower count.
- Geography and overlap are verified.
- Every rejection names its reason.

## B2B variant: criteria weighting for a thought-leader program

Scenario (fictional): Brightledger, an accounting-automation SaaS.

- Objective: trust-building.
- Target customer: heads of finance at 50-500-employee EU companies.
- Platforms: professional network + newsletters.

| Criterion                       | Weight | How measured                                       | Pass floor                            |
| ------------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Comment-section ICP match       | 30%    | Sample commenter roles on last 15 posts            | Majority practitioner/buyer roles     |
| Professional credibility        | 25%    | Title, employer, talks, bylines                    | Verifiable finance-domain credibility |
| Niche consistency               | 20%    | Last 30 posts categorized                          | >=70% finance-ops content             |
| Newsletter subscriber quality   | 15%    | Subscriber roles, reply culture (creator-provided) | Evidence of ICP subscribers           |
| Audience fit (geo/firmographic) | 10%    | Available insights + stated audience               | EU-weighted audience                  |

Follower reach is deliberately absent - near-zero weight in B2B. Note in a real B2B brief: this weighting is practitioner reasoning.

## Negative: the follower-count shortlist

A shortlist built the way this skill exists to prevent, annotated line by line.

> **Shortlist: top 5 biggest plant accounts we could find**

Sourcing by size, not criteria. No approved weights, no knockouts run - scoring started before the manager agreed what "good" means.

> **1. @plantfluencer_official - 2.1M followers. Huge reach, obvious pick.**

Follower-count worship. No audience-fit evidence, no engagement check; a mega account's audience is broad by construction, and the campaign converts on a narrow segment.

> **2. @greenhome_daily - 890K. Beautiful feed, very on-brand aesthetic.**

Over-indexing on aesthetics: production quality scored as if it were audience fit. Nobody sampled who follows or comments.

> **3. @viralgardenhacks - 1.4M. Went viral twice last month.**

Virality audience, not a niche audience - followers came for two spectacle posts, not for plant care. Niche consistency never checked.

> **4. @plantsofworld - 640K, mostly SEA-based audience.**

Geography mismatch, known and ignored. The converting markets are US/UK; most of this reach can never buy.

> **5. @leafqueen_x - 720K. Partnered with Fernbox last quarter.**

Brand-safety screening skipped: a live exclusivity conflict surfaces only after outreach, wasting the contact and risking public association.

> **All five confirmed interested - let's pick the two biggest.**

Final selection by size again:

- No overlap check (accounts 1-3 share large follower bases - paying twice for the same eyeballs).
- No evidence trail.
- No rejected-with-reason list.

Nothing in this shortlist is auditable. Every line is fixed by the positive example's structure: weights first, knockouts second, evidence-backed scores third, rank last.
