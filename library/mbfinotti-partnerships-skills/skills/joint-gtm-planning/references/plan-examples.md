# Joint GTM plan examples

## Worked example - passes the checklist

Context: a mid-market data-quality SaaS ("Datarine") and a BI platform ("Chartview") shipped a two-way integration last quarter. Both sell to revenue-operations teams; 61 shared accounts verified via account mapping. First joint initiative.

```
JOINT GTM PLAN - Datarine x Chartview: integration launch campaign, 10 Mar
Objectives      : Datarine - 150 qualified leads and 25 integration activations by 30 Jun.
                  Chartview - 100 qualified leads and a published joint customer story by 30 Jun.
Joint value prop: "Dashboards you can finally trust" - Chartview visualizes what Datarine has
                  verified. Neither-alone test: Datarine has no visualization layer, Chartview
                  no data-quality engine; the claim requires both products.
Audience        : RevOps leads at 200-2,000-employee B2B companies. Evidence: 61 mapped shared
                  accounts + integration waitlist. Excluded: accounts already using both products.
Campaign shape  : joint webinar + gated benchmark report. Rejected: newsletter swap (no lead
                  capture, pipeline objective) and event booth (no fitting event before Q3).
Assets & split  : Datarine writes the report (its telemetry data); Chartview designs it and
                  builds the registration page (its design team); each approves the other's work.
                  Reused: Chartview's webinar setup, Datarine's shared-customer draft story.
Budget & funding: Datarine $9k (content, promotion); Chartview $7k (design, hosting, promotion).
                  Chartview's cloud-marketplace MDF: $5k pre-approved for paid promotion,
                  proof = invoices + published URLs, claim within 60 days of activity.
                  Claim owner: Chartview partner-marketing manager.
Sequencing      : sign 14 Mar → assets done 18 Apr (one review round each, 1-week feedback
                  buffer per draft) → both-side approval gate 25 Apr → pre-launch 28 Apr →
                  launch 6 May (both sides, same day) → leads transferred within 3 business
                  days of each capture → mid-flight check-in 20 May → debrief 24 Jun.
                  (Solo estimate was ~4 weeks; plan carries ~8 - the doubling is the buffer.)
Leads & tracking: registration form names both companies, separable consent line per company,
                  withdrawal link. Joint-controller split documented: Chartview hosts the form
                  and answers data-subject requests; both privacy notices name the sharing.
                  Qualified lead = RevOps or analytics title, 200+ employees, business email.
                  Dedup: leads already open opportunities for one side are flagged, not
                  transferred. Shared campaign identifier; source parameter split per company.
                  Sourced = first touch on a campaign link; influenced = existing open
                  opportunity that engaged; 90-day window, both sides.
Owners          : Datarine - J. Okafor (partner marketing, owner), CMO (sponsor).
                  Chartview - L. Mestre (alliances, owner), VP Marketing (sponsor).
Measurement     : leads per party via tagged links, qualified-lead conversion, activations,
                  sourced/influenced pipeline reported separately, per-side return on own spend.
                  Reporting: shared dashboard weekly. Debrief booked 24 Jun.
```

Why it passes:

- Two separate goals with numbers.
- The value prop names why both products are required.
- Four named people.
- One launch date with visible buffer.
- Consent, transfer timing, dedup and attribution all written down.
- MDF carries proof and deadline with an owner.
- The debrief is a calendar date.

## Negative example - the logo-swap plan

The most common bad joint GTM plan. Annotated with which checklist items each line fails.

```
JOINT MARKETING PLAN - Acme + Bolt
Goal            : grow both brands and generate leads together        [no per-side goals, no
                                                                       numbers, no dates]
Value prop      : "Acme and Bolt: better together"                    [slogan, not a claim -
                                                                       either logo could be
                                                                       swapped for any vendor;
                                                                       fails neither-alone]
Audience        : both companies' audiences                           [no segment, no overlap
                                                                       evidence, no exclusions]
Campaign         : co-branded ebook, social posts, maybe a webinar    [no chosen shape, no
                                                                       rejected alternatives -
                                                                       "maybe" is not a plan]
Split           : both teams will collaborate on content              [no named creator or
                                                                       approver per asset]
Budget          : shared marketing budget, TBD                        [no per-side lines; TBD
                                                                       is a placeholder, and
                                                                       unowned MDF goes
                                                                       unclaimed]
Timeline        : launch in Q2                                        [no date, no phases, no
                                                                       approval buffer - the
                                                                       slip is pre-written]
Leads           : leads will be shared between the teams              [no consent wording, no
                                                                       mechanism, no timing, no
                                                                       dedup - this line is
                                                                       where the post-campaign
                                                                       dispute starts; possible
                                                                       data-protection breach]
Owners          : Acme marketing team + Bolt marketing team           [teams, not names; no
                                                                       sponsors - nobody to
                                                                       escalate to when Bolt
                                                                       goes quiet]
Success         : engagement, reach, pipeline impact                  [unmeasurable - no
                                                                       per-party links, no
                                                                       sourced/influenced rule,
                                                                       no debrief date]
```

The pattern to recognize: every line reads agreeably and commits nobody to anything. When a draft plan looks like this, do not polish the wording - go back to the Interview and the readiness gate, because the missing content is missing decisions, not missing prose.
