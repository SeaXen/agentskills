# Vendor Program Examples (2022-2026)

**Staleness warning: every program below revised its structure repeatedly between 2022 and 2026, and all revise roughly annually. Re-verify against the vendor's primary program documentation before citing any detail to a user. If you can browse the web, check the current program guide; if not, present these as historical structure patterns, not current requirements.**

These are examples for study - never copy a structure because a large vendor uses it; their partner counts and budgets are orders of magnitude beyond most programs.

## The shared 2022-2026 trend

Every major program moved the same direction: away from pay-to-play and volume-based tiers toward validated designations combining skilling (certified individuals), performance (customer additions/usage), and customer success (references, satisfaction). Credentials converted from static badges into performance-gated status.

## Microsoft - weighted composite score

- Oct 2022: 18 legacy Gold/Silver competencies replaced by 6 Solutions Partner designations; legacy Gold/Silver benefits last sold Jan 2025.
- Attainment via a Partner Capability Score across performance, skilling, and customer success: 100 points max per solution area, minimum 70 to attain, with points required in each category - a weighted composite where strength in one category partially offsets another.
- Separate enterprise vs SMB attainment tracks (different thresholds) for most solution areas; partners auto-assigned by customer size.
- Designation is prerequisite to specializations and expert programs.

## AWS - discrete checklist validation

- Tier thresholds tie directly to certified headcount: Advanced tier required 6 certified individuals, Premier 25 (at least 10 Professional/Specialty level), plus launched-project counts with minimum MRR per tier.
- Specializations (Competency, Service Delivery, MSP) validated by discrete checklists - each control satisfied individually, no trading between categories; MSP adds an independent annual audit.
- From 2026: Specialization renewal requires launched co-sell opportunities over a rolling 12-month window - outcome-gated renewal, the clearest instance of the trend.
- A small-business competency was added in 2024 after partner feedback that certified-headcount requirements walled out smaller partners.

## The structural contrast worth teaching

Microsoft = weighted composite (flexible, partners trade strengths); AWS = discrete checklist (rigid, every control individually). Both wire certified-headcount into tier attainment - certification as tier criterion, not standalone artifact. A program designer picks composite scoring for flexibility and partner-friendliness, checklist validation for defensibility and consistency.

## Google Cloud - two-level organizational credential

- Expertise: entry, company-level, valid ~2 years. Specialization: highest, valid ~3 years, requiring named counts of individuals holding specific professional certifications plus customer success stories, vetted by the vendor and a third-party assessor - the strongest published example of outcome validation via independent assessment.

## HubSpot - free individual certs, gated organizational accreditation

- Individual certifications: free through the vendor's academy, most taking only a few hours, lapsing roughly annually - the design template for high completion at entry level.
- Accreditations: organizational, requiring prerequisite certifications plus case studies, restricted to upper-tier partners, re-evaluated every 2 years.
- The economic pull: the vendor's internal partner-matching favors accredited partners for referrals - certification wired directly to lead flow.
- 2026: a monthly program membership fee introduced, and a sourced-revenue-points requirement from 2027 - status further performance-gated.

## Salesforce - competency consolidation

- Replaced a ~170-distinction navigator system with ~28 competency areas measured on certifications, project delivery, and customer satisfaction; consulting tiers collapsed from 4 to 2 (announced for FY27). Lesson: credential taxonomies that grow past what partners can explain get consolidated.

## ServiceNow, Cisco, Palo Alto Networks - briefly

- ServiceNow (2023): replaced a 37-area point system with explicit tiers scored on capacity (certified individuals), competency, customer success, capability; specializations added yearly since.
- Cisco (2020 overhaul): consolidated 10 associate-level tracks into one, moved specialization up a level, removed prerequisites - reducing entry burden while keeping depth optional.
- Palo Alto Networks (2023): specialization made mandatory to keep upper partnership status - certification converted from optional differentiator to tier-maintenance requirement.

## What to reuse, structurally

1. Free-and-short at the individual entry level; expensive validation only at organizational level.
2. Certified headcount counted by role, wired to tiers - via the tiering sibling skill.
3. Outcome-gated renewal for top designations (delivered projects, launched opportunities, references).
4. A lighter track for small partners before headcount requirements wall them out.
5. Consolidate credential taxonomies before partners need a decoder ring.
