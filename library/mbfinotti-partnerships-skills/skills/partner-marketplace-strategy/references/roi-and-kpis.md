# Marketplace ROI and KPIs - With Provenance Flags

Rule for every number in this file and every number the memo quotes: attach who published it and who paid for it. In this field, nearly every quotable ROI figure originates with a party that profits when the number looks good: the platforms, the GTM-tooling vendors, and the agencies.

The directional claims (marketplace/co-sell deals close faster, larger, at higher win rates; contribution margin decides consumer channels) are corroborated widely, but the precise percentages are marketing. No peer-reviewed validation of the headline uplift claims was found.

## KPI set - B2B

- Marketplace-sourced vs marketplace-influenced revenue (define both with finance before launch).
- Marketplace revenue as % of total; net-new vs relocated revenue, separated.
- Time to first transaction; private-offer share of marketplace revenue.
- Deal-size, cycle-time, and win-rate deltas vs the direct baseline (measure your own, don't quote vendors').
- Co-sell pipeline: registered opportunities, field-seller engagement.
- Cost-to-serve per marketplace vs the fee-plus-ops envelope in the memo.

## KPI set - B2C

- Contribution margin per marketplace after referral, fulfilment, ads, and returns - the primary success variable.
- TACoS (ad spend ÷ total revenue) for business health; ACoS for campaign efficiency. A rising TACoS with flat revenue means ads are propping up demand that won't survive without them.
- Featured-offer/buy-box win rate, share of search, conversion rate, sell-through, returns rate, order-defect rate.

Use the published benchmarks with this discipline:

- Use the vendor-flagged figures only as ceilings or direction.
- Use the two analyst forecasts for market sizing.
- Use the 89%/22% listing-to-revenue gap as the honest base-rate warning: most listings produce little revenue without co-sell or ad investment.

## Building a defensible ROI expectation

1. Baseline first: capture direct-motion win rate, cycle time, deal size (B2B) or channel contribution margin (B2C) before the listing goes live - without it, relocated revenue will masquerade as marketplace ROI.
2. Build the base case from your own funnel and the platform's verified economics, at the operator's future rate, never from a published uplift figure.
3. Treat commissioned-study uplifts as the ceiling scenario and label them as such in the memo.
4. Set expectations against the base rate: plan for the modal outcome (a listing that needs co-sell or ad investment to produce), not the case-study outcome.
5. Define the measurement plan in the memo: metric owners, sourced/influenced definitions agreed with finance, review cadence, and the delist trigger's metric and date.
6. If you can browse the web, re-verify each quoted benchmark and fee against its current source at engagement time. Published figures in this field change and rot quickly. If you cannot, say which numbers are unverified as of this run.
