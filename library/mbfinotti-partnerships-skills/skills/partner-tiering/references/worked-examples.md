## A fully worked structure

Fictional mid-size B2B SaaS vendor: 160 partners - 95 resellers/MSPs, 40 agencies (both transacting), 25 ISV referral partners (non-transacting). Pain: flat 20% discount for everyone, no ladder, top performers asking what more they get. Numbers below use the sourced ranges from this skill's criteria and benefits references, which workflow steps 5 and 7 load.

It takes the starved mechanic on purpose: partners here transact across sourced and assisted motions and hold certifications, so no single metric ranks them in an order the channel team would defend - the stated condition that promotes a points pathway over the default engagement mechanic. It also splits the ISV partners into their own track, since their benefits barely overlap with the transacting ladder's.

```
TIER STRUCTURE - example vendor, transacting track (135 partners)
Mechanic   : points pathway - 1 pt / $100 sourced MRR, 0.5 pt / $100 assisted,
             +25 pts per certified individual; trailing-4-quarter rolling window
Tier count : 3 earned + unranked Registered entry rung
Registered : signed agreement only; portal + training access; no discount ladder
Silver     : 150 pts; 2 certified staff | 15% discount, standard support
             Backtest: 78 partners (58%) | cost ~ covered by cohort revenue
Gold       : 500 pts; 4 certified staff; >=70% GRR on managed book
             | 20% discount, deal-reg +10 margin pts (90-day protection -
             110-day average cycle, so 82%, clearing the 75% rule),
             defined 8-business-hour SLA, MDF pool access
             Backtest: 42 partners (31%) | bundle cost < cohort contribution
Platinum   : 1,500 pts; 8 certified staff; >=80% GRR; vendor-run CSAT gate
             | 25% discount, deal-reg +15 pts, dedicated PAM (1:12),
             70% of the 4%-of-channel-revenue MDF pool, 2% back-end rebate
             Backtest: 15 partners (11%) | largest bundle, largest contribution
ISV track  : separate criteria - integration maturity, joint customers,
             marketplace listing; benefits are co-marketing + directory placement
Cadence    : quarterly recalculation, rolling window, one-cycle (90-day) grace,
             appeals to channel chief, decision dates published
Migration  : best-status-in-trailing-year grandfathering for 2 quarters,
             advisory-council preview 1 quarter before announcement
Pass check : top tier 11% of base (>=10% ✓, not crowded ✓); every step adds
             discount + deal-reg + service (✓ visible differential); each
             bundle modelled under cohort revenue (✓); no self-reported
             criterion (✓ - pipeline counts only as accepted registrations)
```

Adapt the numbers, never copy them: the discounts, point weights, and MDF split come from practitioner ranges, not audited benchmarks, and the right values depend on the user's margin structure.

## The B2C contrast

| Dimension     | B2B channel                         | Affiliate / creator                        | Consumer loyalty                                     |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Currency      | Revenue + certifications + CSAT/GRR | Sales or GMV on a rolling window           | Annual qualifying spend / nights / miles             |
| Typical count | 3                                   | 3-5                                        | 4-5                                                  |
| Reset         | Rolling or annual, with grace       | Rolling, near-real-time                    | Hard annual status clock; status valid ~12-15 months |
| Benefits      | Margin, deal protection, MDF, PAM   | Higher commission %, early access, bonuses | Upgrades, lounge access, multipliers                 |
| Human touch   | High - PAM, QBRs                    | Low - self-serve                           | Fully automated                                      |

Sourced B2C data points (each single-source - directional only):

- Three-tier affiliate commission ladders average roughly 10% / 14% / 19%.
- A DTC skincare brand (ClearStem, via its platform's case write-up) runs 10% / 15% / 20% at $2,500 and $5,000 revenue milestones.
- Social-commerce creator tiers commonly start at 5-10% commission, reaching 20-25%+ past roughly $2,500 GMV.
- Creator ladders (Seeding → Affiliate → Ambassador) qualify on audience size, engagement rate, and conversion history - signals with no B2B analogue.

The loop is identical everywhere - qualify → status → perks → requalify or demote - and the peer-reviewed demotion findings generalize across both worlds. The operational split is testability: A/B test an affiliate or loyalty ladder on a slice of the base. Never A/B test a B2B channel tier - it is negotiated and relationship-bound, so model it, pilot it with a cohort, and socialize it through the advisory council instead.
