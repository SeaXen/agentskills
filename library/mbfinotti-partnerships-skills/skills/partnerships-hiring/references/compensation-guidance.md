# Compensation guidance

Contents: why sources disagree by 2-3x · directional figures with sources · comp structure by sub-discipline · the attribution-model decision.

## No single trustworthy benchmark exists

Public sources disagree by 2-3x on the same title. For "Director of Partnerships": Salary.com reports roughly $206-215k median, PayScale reports roughly $74k average with a 10th-90th percentile base range of $44k-$142k. The gap traces to methodology and population: Salary.com skews toward tech/VC-backed company self-reports at the senior end; PayScale's sample skews wider and includes smaller/non-tech employers under the same title. Never quote one figure to a candidate or in a posting as "the" market rate.

## Directional figures by title (state the source with every number)

- VP Partnerships: Salary.com median ~$215k (2025). Related titles on the same source: Partnerships Lead ~$258k, Partnerships Director ~$207k, Head of Partnerships ~$133k, Partnerships Manager ~$133k, Partnerships Associate ~$78k, Partnerships Coordinator ~$75k.
- Partner Manager (tech-sector, crowdsourced): Levels.fyi reports a median around $226k total comp - a different population (large tech, self-reported total comp including equity) than Salary.com's base-salary figures; never quote the two against each other as comparable.
- Affiliate Marketing Manager: sources range roughly $64k-$125k average base depending on source and progressing to $110-176k at director; a Glassdoor-derived synthesis reports ~$117k total pay once bonus/commission is included.
- The field's own specialist benchmark: **Partnership Leaders' compensation database/calculator**, built from practitioner-submitted data. Check it live before publishing a posting's range.

## Compensation structure by sub-discipline

- **Affiliate Manager**: commonly three-part - base salary, a performance bonus (roughly 10-30% of base at junior/mid, 30-50% at senior), and commission tied to revenue managed through the program. This structure is what distinguishes affiliate/partnerships comp from a flat-salaried marketing role.
- **Channel/Alliance Manager** (enterprise): more often base-plus-variable tied to partner-sourced or partner-influenced pipeline/revenue targets, mirroring quota-carrying sales comp shape.
- **Equity** appears at executive levels (VP/CPO) per the general VP-comp pattern, not as a partnerships-specific feature.

## Decide the attribution model before publishing

The underlying attribution mechanics behind any variable pay - sourced, influenced, attached, or delivered revenue - are contested in the field and not standardized across companies. Decide which model triggers this role's variable pay before writing the posting, and state it explicitly in the offer. An undecided attribution model is a common source of comp disputes once the role is in seat, not just a hiring-stage ambiguity.

## What this means for the posting

Publish a range sourced to a named benchmark (or the field-specific database), state the pay structure (base-only, base-plus-bonus, base-plus-commission), and name the attribution model for any variable component. A posting that states none of these is indistinguishable, to a candidate reading it against this field's own unreliable public data, from one hiding a below-market offer.
