# Interview loop and case studies

Contents: the channel-manager loop, stage by stage · case-study design · the two judgment probes · the negotiation role-play · what to build instead for the other three sub-disciplines.

## Channel/Alliance Manager loop, documented pattern

1. Recruiter/HR screen - background and fit.
2. Hiring-manager round(s), often with cross-functional stakeholders from sales, marketing and product sitting in.
3. Behavioral plus scenario/case-study questions on channel strategy and partner conflict.
4. Technical/tooling round - CRM, KPIs, analytics fluency.
5. At senior levels: a multi-department panel, often a mock 30/60/90-day channel plan or a mock partner business review. This stage should feel like a general-manager interview - evaluate cross-functional leadership, not deal execution alone.

## Case-study design

- **Channel conflict resolution.** Present a near-simultaneous deal-registration clash between a partner and direct sales. Score the candidate on process discipline over loyalty: do they pull the deal-registration timeline and CRM activity, bring both reps into one conversation, and treat recurrence as a symptom of unclear rules of engagement - or do they arbitrate by gut feel?
- **Cross-functional collaboration.** Ask how they operate simultaneously with marketing, product and sales. Score for concrete mechanisms named, not "I communicate well" - co-building campaigns so MDF ties to sourced pipeline, a feedback loop carrying partner input into product, clean rules of engagement so partner and direct teams work the same number.
- **Partner onboarding/enablement design.** Ask how they'd evaluate a new partner (expertise, target audience, market reach, compatibility) and what materials/cadence they'd ramp them on.

## The two judgment probes

Ask both explicitly - they are rehearsable but reveal genuine judgment, with no obvious "right answer" a candidate can memorize.

- **"Would you close this deal in January the exact same way you closed it in December, just to finish the quarter on target?"** The strong answer is no - score for whether the candidate treats the relationship, not the quarter, as the unit they optimize.
- **"Walk me through the last partner you de-prioritized, and how that conversation went."** Tests whether the candidate has actually said no to a partner, not just built relationships. A candidate with no answer to this has likely never operated with real prioritization discipline.

## The negotiation role-play

A two-sided simulation: candidate plus an interviewer or panel member playing the counterparty, set in a business-partnership or vendor-deal context with hidden constraints or interests the candidate must surface through questioning. Give a short brief with a pressure point and one or two optional positions - not a full script. Score on how the candidate structures win-win terms, handles objections, and defends or finds a rational walk-away point - never on whether they "won" the simulated negotiation.

## What has no comparably documented pattern - build from outcomes instead

Affiliate Manager, Influencer/Creator Partnerships Manager and Referral Program Manager loops are not documented with anything specific to their structure. Do not import the channel-manager case studies wholesale - the day-to-day differs enough that those scenarios test the wrong thing. Build each loop from the role's own outcome metrics instead:

| Sub-discipline                          | Build the loop around                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Affiliate Manager                       | Program performance the candidate has owned, commission-economics literacy, network/tooling fluency                                                                                                                          |
| Influencer/Creator Partnerships Manager | Campaigns sourced and negotiated, platform-landscape fluency, FTC disclosure-compliance literacy                                                                                                                             |
| Referral Program Manager                | Incentive-design decisions and their measured effect, cross-team coordination with marketing - confirm the candidate is applying to the customer/growth track, not the employee-referral-recruiting track sharing this title |

## Scoring mechanics

Score independently per interviewer with a written evidence note before any debrief, then combine mechanically against the scorecard's pre-set competency weights - a general employment-selection finding, not specific to partnerships, but it transfers directly.
