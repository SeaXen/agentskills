# Sourcing channels

Contents: the one dedicated job board · why sourcing overlaps with the field's communities · the internal-transfer sourcing implication · title-fragmentation search advice.

## One dedicated job board exists

The **Partnership Leaders Job Board** (run by the organization behind the field's largest community and its own compensation database) is the field's one dedicated board - there is no devreljob.com-equivalent second board. This asymmetry matters practically: a partnerships req gets less benefit from board-spray than a DevRel req would, and proportionally more benefit from the community and referral channels below. The board is explicitly candidate-pool-aware: a large share of its base is in Canada, and it asks remote postings to state whether Canadian candidates are welcome - worth deciding before posting if hiring in North America.

## Sourcing overlaps almost entirely with the field's information-ecosystem communities

This is a relationship-driven field where people find roles through the same communities they use to stay current, not through separate "jobs" channels:

- **Partnership Leaders** - the highest-leverage single community for both sourcing and candidate research; a hiring manager can cross-reference a candidate's visibility here against their resume claims.
- **Cloud Software Association** - skews toward tech-alliance and channel-program practitioners; a stronger fit for channel/alliance hires than affiliate or influencer hires.
- **PartnerHacker / nearbound** - skews toward the ecosystem-led-growth and revenue-attribution crowd; useful for sourcing candidates already fluent in the field's current vocabulary shift.
- **Crossbeam/Reveal's ELG Insider and ELG Summit** - candidates active here tend to be partner-ops or ecosystem-tooling literate specifically.
- **CompTIA/GTIA with ChannelCon** - the traditional IT-channel side; a better fit for reseller/VAR/distributor-facing channel-manager hires than for SaaS-native alliance or affiliate hires.

Match the community to the sub-discipline before posting - a channel/alliance req in an affiliate-focused community (or the reverse) under-returns.

## The internal-transfer route is a sourcing implication, not just a candidate tactic

Because so much of this field's entry happens by a sales or BD rep being noticed doing partnerships-adjacent work inside their own company, look inside the sales/BD organization before posting externally for a first dedicated partnerships seat. The person already fielding partner inquiries informally is a validated, low-risk candidate pool the job-board channels above will not surface.

## Search by function, not title alone

LinkedIn remains the generic fallback for both sourcing and candidate self-promotion. Because titles are so fragmented (see the ladder finding), a title-only search under-returns - combine title keywords with function keywords (deal registration, co-sell, MDF, affiliate program, creator partnerships) instead of relying on title match alone.
