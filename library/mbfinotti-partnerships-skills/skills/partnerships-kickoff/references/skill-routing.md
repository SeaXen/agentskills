## Per-skill signals - macro channel / alliance strategy

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@partner-ecosystem`

- Route: map the ecosystem as it stands - inventory every partner type (resellers, distributors, MSPs, ISVs, SIs, affiliates, marketplaces; retail, creator, licensing, co-branding for consumer brands), account overlap, coverage gaps, conflict zones; "partner landscape", "ecosystem whitespace".
- Do not route: program design, tier criteria, conflict rules. Macro mapping only.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@partner-ecosystem-expansion`

- Route: which partner CATEGORIES to add next and in what SEQUENCE - an ecosystem map turned into a staged roadmap with readiness gates, capacity limits, kill criteria; "which partner type do we launch next".
- Do not route: ranking named candidate companies; designing program tiers.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@partner-channel-program`

- Route: design a B2B partner/channel program's STRUCTURE from scratch - readiness gate (can conclude "not yet"), which motions to run, tier count, benefit stack, program-wide economics envelope; "build a partner program", "am I ready for partners". Covers B2C analogs (dealer, franchise, ambassador).
- Do not route: tier criteria, ecosystem mapping, one partner's economics, enablement, co-sell rules, conflict resolution, partner scorecards.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@partner-tiering`

- Route: design or redesign the TIER STRUCTURE inside an EXISTING B2B program - qualification criteria, benefit bundles, promotion/demotion rules, migration of the current base; tier compression, reseller/MSP/agency tier design. Covers the B2C affiliate/creator/loyalty tier contrast.
- Do not route: designing a program from scratch, per-partner scorecards/QBRs, affiliate commission payout tiers.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@co-selling-strategy`

- Route: the OPERATING MODEL for direct and partner sellers on shared deals - deal registration policy (protection windows, approval SLA, rejection grounds), deal credit and attribution split (partner-sourced vs partner-influenced, comp neutrality), rules of engagement; building or overhauling a B2B co-sell motion.
- Do not route: resolving a live contested deal, joint GTM with one partner, program tiers or economics. Macro strategy only.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@partner-channel-conflict`

- Route: write the CONFLICT RULES governing contested deals between direct and partners or between partners - account segmentation, named-account carve-outs, comp neutrality, escalation/adjudication ladders. Consumer: D2C vs retail, MAP policy, channel-exclusive assortment; "partner deal poaching".
- Do not route: detection, and co-sell collaboration design. Rule-setting and adjudication design only.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@partner-enablement`

- Route: sequence the TRAINING, CONTENT, CERTIFICATION that makes B2B partners self-sufficient sellers - the enablement roadmap, not the training content itself; skills-gap assessment, tracks by partner role/type, certification design, pilot cohort. Covers B2C analogs (franchise, dealer, ambassador education).
- Do not route: program structure, tier criteria, partner scorecards, affiliate onboarding emails.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@alliance-prioritization`

- Route: RANK NAMED CANDIDATE alliances and technology partnerships by expected value, effort, risk - longlist-to-shortlist stage-gate, weighted scorecard, ranked shortlist plus go/no-go memo; "which of these partners do we invest in". B2B tech alliances and B2C brand/creator partnerships.
- Do not route: single-partner P&L modeling; post-selection joint GTM planning. Portfolio-level comparison only.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@partner-economics`

- Route: model the UNIT ECONOMICS OF ONE partner relationship - partner P&L, margin stack, cost-to-serve, partner CAC vs direct CAC, ramp and payback - to decide sign / scale / renegotiate / exit; reseller/VAR/MSP/OEM/marketplace deal economics. Covers B2C affiliate/creator economics.
- Do not route: portfolio ranking, program-wide economics, tier design, affiliate payout-tier design. One relationship at a time.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@partner-marketplace-strategy`

- Route: WHICH third-party marketplaces to list on, how much to invest in each, what ROI to expect, when to stop - cloud marketplace listing strategy, app store / integration directory prioritization, retail marketplace selection (1P vs 3P vs hybrid); readiness gate, weighted scorecard, depth-of-participation, delist trigger.
- Do not route: listing-form or ad execution. Macro strategy only.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@joint-gtm-planning`

- Route: plan ONE joint go-to-market motion with ONE ALREADY-CHOSEN partner - joint value proposition, co-marketing shape, market-development funds, lead-sharing and attribution agreement; joint webinar or integration launch, "better-together" story. B2B co-marketing and consumer co-branding / creator collaborations.
- Do not route: ad copy, email bodies, asset production; partner selection; co-sell deal policy; program tiers; partner unit economics. Macro strategy only.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@partner-performance`

- Route: measure and review INDIVIDUAL partners in a B2B program - per-partner scorecard (dimensions, weights, targets, thresholds), QBR structure, underperformance ladder through corrective action to de-tier or exit; "how do I handle an underperforming channel partner". Covers the B2C affiliate/creator review contrast.
- Do not route: tier design, single-partner P&L modeling, ecosystem-level metrics, building the joint GTM plan itself.

## Per-skill signals - affiliate operations

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@affiliate-program-terms`

- Route: DRAFT the terms an affiliate accepts on joining a commission-based program - clause set, prohibited-tactics clauses with an enforcement ladder, validation/clawback and termination mechanics, jurisdiction overlays (FTC, EU P2B, UK ASA/CAP, GDPR roles), brand-bidding policy. B2B SaaS and B2C ecommerce. Output: clause-complete draft flagged for mandatory legal review.
- Do not route: designing commission rates or tiers, auditing published content for disclosure, fraud detection.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@affiliate-commission-structure`

- Route: design the COMMISSION STRUCTURE AND PAYOUT TERMS - rate model (revenue share, flat CPA, hybrid) derived from unit economics, payout tiers, recurring duration and caps, cookie window, validation, clawback, payment terms; set or change a rate, lifetime vs 12-month recurring, migrate a rate card. B2B SaaS + B2C ecommerce, professional affiliates.
- Do not route: end-customer referral rewards, drafting program T&Cs, payout-run auditing, fraud-detection rules.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@affiliate-recruitment-outreach`

- Route: draft the OUTREACH COPY AND SEQUENCE PLAN to recruit affiliates at LIST SCALE - qualify and tier an identified prospect list, pitch angle, first-touch email, follow-up sequence with a breakup touch, channel mix; content/review sites, coupon and cashback partners, newsletter operators, communities, podcasters, agencies.
- Do not route: designing the commission, writing program terms, onboarding approved affiliates, fraud detection, pitching one individually-vetted influencer.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@affiliate-onboarding-sequence`

- Route: the ONBOARDING SEQUENCE carrying a NEWLY APPROVED affiliate from approval to first tracked conversion - touch plan (timing, channel, one action per touch), asset kit, activation nudge, never-activated follow-up branch; "why do newly approved affiliates never send a click". B2B SaaS + B2C ecommerce.
- Do not route: recruitment outreach, program terms drafting, commission-rate design. Starts at approval.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@affiliate-fraud-detection`

- Route: build the DETECTION RULE SET for fraudulent PROFESSIONAL-AFFILIATE activity, then investigate and escalate - cookie stuffing, fake lead / form-fill fraud, self-referral rings, traffic quality baselines, hold and clawback mechanics, dossiers, separating real fraud from low-incrementality coupon/cashback partners. B2B lead-gen + B2C ecommerce.
- Do not route: auditing payout accuracy, drafting program terms, policing consumer refer-a-friend gaming.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@affiliate-payout-audit`

- Route: AUDIT a commission payout run for ACCURACY BEFORE money is disbursed - recompute rate and tier per line, catch double-counted conversions, verify clawback and validation windows, period cutoff, currency, rounding, tax-form status; report with severities and recommended holds.
- Do not route: detecting fraud, designing commission structures or tiers, building a standing dashboard.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@affiliate-performance-dashboard`

- Route: define the METRIC SET AND LAYOUT for an affiliate dashboard, delivered as a SPECIFICATION document - metric definitions with explicit formulas, views per audience, refresh cadence, alert thresholds; program ROI reporting, KPI spec, revenue concentration reporting.
- Do not route: building the dashboard in any BI tool, auditing a payout run, defining fraud rules, designing commission structures, writing channel-partner scorecards.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@affiliate-disclosure-compliance`

- Route: CHECK A SPECIFIC PIECE OF CONTENT (caption, video script, transcript, screenshot, live URL) against disclosure regulations. Return a pass/fail review per requirement, with placement, wording, per-format fixes: FTC (16 CFR 255 and 465), UK CAP/CMA, EU UCPD and DSA, German/French/Italian overlays. Applies pre-publication or already live (then remediation); "missing or buried #ad".
- Do not route: program terms, campaign briefs. Reviews content only.

## Per-skill signals - influencer / creator operations

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@influencer-discovery-brief`

- Route: define CREATOR SOURCING CRITERIA and produce the discovery brief used to SHORTLIST candidates BEFORE ANY CONTACT - weighted criteria for audience fit, engagement quality, niche consistency, brand safety, knockout screening, ranked shortlist with evidence per score. B2C brand campaigns + B2B creator/thought-leader programs.
- Do not route: writing creator outreach copy; producing the campaign creative brief.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@influencer-outreach`

- Route: draft the PERSONALIZED FIRST-TOUCH PITCH and follow-up plan for ONE ALREADY-VETTED creator - personalization angle, state the compensation on offer, fit the message to the channel the creator actually lists (email, DM, or their manager), bounded follow-up cadence. B2C brand collabs + B2B creator programs (newsletter writers, podcasters, industry experts).
- Do not route: choosing which creator to pitch, list-scale affiliate recruitment, negotiating terms, writing the campaign brief. Stops the moment a positive reply arrives.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@influencer-negotiation-playbook`

- Route: prepare a brand-side manager to NEGOTIATE DEAL TERMS from the creator's first positive reply to a signed contract - term-by-term opening positions, concession ladder, walk-away thresholds covering usage rights and exclusivity, revision limits, payment terms, kill fees; negotiating with a talent manager.
- Do not route: BENCHMARKING creator rates (bring your own rate intel), reviewing a signed contract, negotiating on the user's behalf, writing the campaign brief.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@influencer-campaign-brief`

- Route: draft the CREATIVE CAMPAIGN BRIEF handed to an ALREADY-SIGNED creator for one campaign - deliverables with exact specs, key messages with proof points, brand guardrails, disclosure requirements, bounded review rounds, built on a non-negotiables vs preferences split. B2C brand collabs + B2B creator programs.
- Do not route: picking or vetting the creator, negotiating deal terms, running the pre-publication disclosure check, designing the measurement framework. Starts after the deal is signed and reflects those terms exactly.

### `mbfinotti/partnerships-skills@influencer-measurement-framework`

- Route: design the PRE-LAUNCH MEASUREMENT PLAN for one creator campaign - KPIs matched to the objective, an attribution method and data source behind each KPI, reporting cadence per stakeholder, declared blind spots; delivered before launch. B2B and B2C.
- Do not route: building the ongoing affiliate dashboard, writing the creative brief, vetting creators, payout math.

## Per-skill signals - referral operations

B2C and B2B SaaS.
- Do not route: professional-affiliate traffic fraud, referral reward-structure design, contract drafting.

## Boundary pairs and clusters

Where siblings collide on keywords, decide from these declared-scope boundaries. The first four are CONTESTED - the collection has not resolved them. For each: route as resolved below, say out loud that the boundary is contested, and point at the collection's review backlog rather than forcing a confident match.

**CONTESTED 1 - partner-ecosystem vs partner-ecosystem-expansion.** Both touch partner-category prioritization with two contradictory methods; decide by TIME DIMENSION.

- No sequencing, no roadmap ("what does our ecosystem look like today and which segments matter") → partner-ecosystem.
- An ordered roadmap of which category launches NEXT and WHEN, with readiness gates and kill criteria → partner-ecosystem-expansion.
- Wanting both a map and a sequence: run partner-ecosystem first and hand its map over.

**CONTESTED 2 - consumer refer-a-friend TERMS drafting (referral-abuse-guardrails vs affiliate-program-terms).** A mutual routing loop: guardrails routes terms drafting to affiliate-program-terms, which routes it back. Neither owns it - do NOT bounce the user between them. Say the collection has no clear owner here; note that affiliate-program-terms is the closest fit (it drafts program agreements, but for professional affiliates) and that referral-abuse-guardrails owns the enforcement-ladder and eligibility content feeding those terms.

**CONTESTED 3 - reward CAPS (referral-incentive-design vs referral-abuse-guardrails).** Both claim reward caps; decide by PURPOSE.

- A cap protecting unit economics, how much to pay → referral-incentive-design.
- A cap stopping gaming, farming or ring abuse, alongside velocity limits, cooldowns, hold windows → referral-abuse-guardrails.

State which purpose you inferred so the user can correct it.

**CONTESTED 4 - creator rate benchmarking: NO OWNER.** influencer-negotiation-playbook explicitly refuses to build a rate benchmark and requires the user to bring their own rate intel; influencer-outreach, influencer-discovery-brief and influencer-campaign-brief all treat the rate as user-supplied. Say plainly that no skill benchmarks creator rates, and that the user must source that number externally before influencer-negotiation-playbook is useful. NEVER route a rate-benchmarking ask to a sibling.

**affiliate-recruitment-outreach vs influencer-outreach.** List-scale recruitment into a standing commission program → affiliate-recruitment-outreach. One individually-vetted creator, personalized first touch → influencer-outreach.

**affiliate-fraud-detection vs referral-abuse-guardrails.** Professional affiliates gaming traffic → fraud-detection. End customers gaming refer-a-friend → abuse-guardrails.

**affiliate-fraud-detection vs affiliate-payout-audit.** Is the ACTIVITY real? → fraud-detection. Is the MATH right on a run about to be paid? → payout-audit.

**affiliate-commission-structure vs partner-economics vs referral-incentive-design.** The rate card for professional affiliates → commission-structure. One partner relationship's P&L and the sign-or-exit call → partner-economics. End-customer refer-a-friend reward → referral-incentive-design.

**affiliate-performance-dashboard vs partner-performance vs influencer-measurement-framework.** Standing program-wide affiliate metric spec → performance-dashboard. Per-partner B2B scorecard, QBR, underperformance ladder → partner-performance. One creator campaign's pre-launch plan → influencer-measurement-framework.

**partner-channel-program vs partner-tiering.** Program not built yet → channel-program. Program exists, tiers need adding or fixing → tiering. Note: the two ship conflicting default tier counts; do not present either default as settled.

**co-selling-strategy vs partner-channel-conflict.** How direct and partner sellers COLLABORATE (registration, credit split, comp neutrality) → co-selling-strategy. The rules that ADJUDICATE a contested account or deal, including D2C vs retail and MAP → partner-channel-conflict. These two overlap heavily and contradict each other on rejection grounds and protection windows; when the ask is really "who wins this deal", prefer partner-channel-conflict and flag the overlap.

**alliance-prioritization vs partner-ecosystem-expansion vs partner-economics.** Rank NAMED companies → alliance-prioritization. Sequence CATEGORIES → ecosystem-expansion. Model ONE relationship's numbers → partner-economics.

**influencer-campaign-brief vs affiliate-disclosure-compliance.** The brief SPECIFIES disclosure requirements up front; the compliance skill REVIEWS a finished piece of content. Note: the two currently disagree on multi-frame story labeling; when disclosure correctness is the actual question, affiliate-disclosure-compliance wins.

**joint-gtm-planning vs co-selling-strategy.** One named partner, one campaign or launch motion → joint-gtm-planning. Program-wide policy for how deals are shared → co-selling-strategy.

## Ordered chains

Propose a chain only when the task genuinely decomposes this way; never fabricate a sequence. Each chain is listed in dependency order, not efficiency order - a later link consumes what the earlier one produces, so there is no ratio to rank.

- `partner-ecosystem` → `partner-ecosystem-expansion` → `partner-channel-program` → `partner-tiering` → `partner-enablement` - map of today's types and gaps → which category launches next → a structure for it → tiers once partners exist → tiered partners made self-sufficient.
- `alliance-prioritization` → `partner-economics` → `joint-gtm-planning` - ranked shortlist → the candidate's P&L and sign/no-sign call → one planned joint motion with the signed partner.
- `co-selling-strategy` → `partner-channel-conflict` - registration and credit split → the rules adjudicating the contested deals that model produces.
- `affiliate-commission-structure` → `affiliate-program-terms` → `affiliate-recruitment-outreach` → `affiliate-onboarding-sequence` → `affiliate-performance-dashboard` - rate card → the agreement's commission clauses → a pitchable program → approvals entering onboarding → activated affiliates to measure.
- `affiliate-fraud-detection` → `affiliate-payout-audit` - fraud rules place holds and flag lines → the audit verifies the surviving run's math before disbursement.
- `influencer-discovery-brief` → `influencer-outreach` → `influencer-negotiation-playbook` → `influencer-campaign-brief` → `influencer-measurement-framework` → `affiliate-disclosure-compliance` - who to pitch → a positive reply → a signed contract → a brief reflecting those terms → the plan set before launch → the published content reviewed.
- `referral-incentive-design` → `referral-abuse-guardrails` - the reward structure defines what can be gamed → the guardrails cap, delay and police exactly those rewards.
