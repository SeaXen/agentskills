# Arqiv Assemble: goals doc, not ready to lock

**Don't lock it as written. Tightening the wording won't fix it.** Under the corporate-event-strategy skill, a goal needs four fields: a metric, an owner, a source system, and a read date. Your draft fails all four. The fix is to decide a few things before Monday, not to polish.

## Why each line fails

| Your line | Problem |
|---|---|
| Goal: pipeline and community | Two goals with no priority. "Community" has no number, so it will get reported as attendance. Attendance isn't an outcome, so it has no place in a goals doc. |
| Metric: pipeline generated, community engagement | There's no attribution rule. Whoever builds the number picks the rule, and they'll pick the one that flatters them. "Engagement" names nothing. |
| Owner: events team | You don't hold the sales system or product telemetry. You'd be asking two functions for numbers nobody asked them to produce. |
| Data source: TBD | This means no join exists between your attendee list and the sales systems. If it doesn't exist, the pipeline goal isn't available yet. |
| Read-out: post-event wrap-up | That isn't a date. It will happen whenever someone remembers, usually after the planning cycle that renews the budget line. |

## What to decide before Monday

Registration opening is the deadline. A goal chosen after registration opens only describes what happened.

1. **Who will be in the room?** Mostly existing users, or mostly people who have never bought? And does your invite list actually reach the people you want?
2. **Does the developer who attends also sign?** If someone else signs, any pipeline number is *influence*, not attribution, and the doc must say so.
3. **Does a join exist today** between attendees and the sales CRM? Or between attendees and product telemetry that identifies accounts? Sales ops can answer this in one message.
4. **Who owns the budget line, and who else funds it?** Sales, product, DevRel, an exec sponsor?
5. **When is the date the goal and budget get agreed?** That is, when is the exec review or budget lock?
6. **Is Assemble a one-off or the first year of an annual program?**
7. **Who can run a standing measurement job through the quarter after the event?**

## Goal options

- **Adoption-first (my provisional recommendation).** This is the default, and the one an invite list built from your own users can support. It needs one data owner (product telemetry) and one approval. Community becomes a rule for how the event behaves, not a second goal to report.
- **Pipeline-first.** Choose it only if all three hold:
  - There's a hard reporting date.
  - The room will be mostly people who have never bought.
  - The CRM join exists today.

  "TBD" on your data source already rules it out for now.
- **Blended adoption and pipeline.** This is the most durable option, because you have a second story to tell in a bad quarter. It needs a compounding annual mandate, both joins, and a named person to reconcile the two numbers. Pick it only if you can answer yes to all three.
- **Community only.** I would delete this option. Once any other function co-funds, they will want a number, and a goal with none gets cut first.

I haven't had your answers to the questions above, so the recommendation is provisional. If the room is mostly existing users and no CRM join exists, adoption-first is the honest answer.

## Three reviews to send out before the registration form goes live

Two of these can't be fixed after the fact.

1. **Privacy.** If attendee data goes into sales systems, your privacy owner or counsel must sign off on the lawful basis, the registration notice, retention, and internal reuse. Registration opens Monday, so this is the most urgent. A list collected under the wrong notice can't be un-collected. I'm naming no rules; that's for your counsel.
2. **Legal.** Review the published terms, how the code of conduct binds staff as well as guests, and insurance.
3. **Guest hospitality.** Check your anti-bribery and gifting policy, and also the guests' employers' policies. Public-sector and regulated-industry guests are the usual problem. Do this before invitations go out.
