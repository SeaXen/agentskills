# Verdict: ❌ Don't move to Paris during their week

## Why it fails

1. **You'd compete head-on and lose.** You said people pick one of the two. Same city and same week means the choice is made on size, and they're 4x yours. Your 350 become maybe 100.
2. **You'd give up your edge.** Lyon is your differentiator: smaller, closer, easier to get speakers' attention, and cheaper for non-Paris attendees. In Paris you're just the smaller copy.
3. **The joint push has nothing in it for them.** They're the incumbent, and every attendee you gain is one they lose. The pitch also admits they have the stronger event. Expect "no", or a yes on terms that favor them.
4. **The move is hard to undo.** Venue, sponsor, and speaker commitments in Paris would be sunk costs if they say no.

## What I'd do

1. **Check the premise first (⏳ about 1 week).**
   - Survey last year's attendees: did they also consider RustForge, and why did they pick you?
   - Look at ticket postcodes and employers to see how much of your crowd is regional versus Paris.
   - "Same crowd" is an assumption until this data says so.
2. **Stay in Lyon and separate the dates.** Put yours 6 to 10 weeks away from theirs, so people can attend both and the choice stops being either/or. Dates apart is the cheapest fix.
3. **Sharpen what only you offer.**
   - Smaller scale with better access to speakers.
   - A hands-on or workshop track.
   - French-language or regional-community talks.
   - Lower cost for people who'd travel to Paris anyway.
4. **If you want to partner, make a smaller, mutual ask:**
   - Swap CFP overflow: good rejected talks get pointed to the other event.
   - Do community-newsletter shout-outs once the dates don't clash.
   - Offer a ticket discount to the other event's attendees.
   - Share a speaker's travel when one is invited to both.

   Position these as a sequence of events that grows the Rust calendar, not as a joint push.

## What would change my mind

- Survey data shows most of your attendees are Paris-based and only came to Lyon because of the date.
- RustForge says, unprompted, that they want a co-located or co-run event and offers real terms.
- Your Lyon venue or sponsor situation is breaking.

Even then, I'd test it with a smaller step first, such as an adjacent-day satellite event, before moving the whole edition.
