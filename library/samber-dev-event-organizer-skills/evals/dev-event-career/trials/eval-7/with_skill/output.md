## What would flip the lean to A

Ask A these questions before deciding:

1. Is this one person owning programme, sponsorship and production, or scoped to production only?
2. Is there a written career path? Which rung is "Senior Producer", and what comes next?
3. What are the team size and budget for the next edition?
4. What does success look like in year one, and who decides?
5. Is freelance or contractor work tolerated alongside the job later on?

Choose A if the answers show a real path to Conference Manager or Director with end-to-end ownership, and you'd rather build depth in one flagship series than breadth across clients.

## What would flip the lean further to B

- You're serious about freelancing within about 2–3 years. B builds client range and vendor relationships.
- B's tech client list includes names you'd want in your portfolio.
- B has a defined progression from Producer to Senior Producer, in writing.

## What to ask B

- How are projects staffed: production-only, or client-facing too?
- How many live events will you personally run each year?
- Is there a written ladder?
- Are freelance or side projects allowed?

## Before you decide

- Weight the deciding questions above over the title.
- Whichever offer you take, keep a portfolio. Interviews and freelance clients both ask for events run (named, with scale) and metrics such as attendee satisfaction, on-budget delivery and repeat-attendee rate.
