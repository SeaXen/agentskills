**Don't publish this as written.** The wording is fine. The mismatch between scope and pay is the problem, and candidates will spot it in seconds.

## What I'd change, in priority order

1. **Scope vs. pay ($58,000).** The role bundles four jobs: sales (six-figure deals), content (CFP and lineup), production (AV, hands-on), and ops (volunteers). In most markets those are 2–3 salaried roles. Likely results:
   - Senior candidates skip it.
   - Juniors apply, then burn out or miss sponsor targets.
   - Your brand reads as "devtools company that undervalues events."

   Pick one:
   - Raise the pay or add variable comp.
   - Cut scope: no hands-on AV (contract a production agency) and no deal-closing (the founder or sales team closes, the manager supports).

2. **Sales without sales comp.** "Close six-figure deals" implies a quota. If it's a real quota, add commission or OTE. If not, change "close" to "manage sponsor relationships and support closing."

3. **"Individual contributor" vs. "you'll build it all."** These contradict each other. Does the person build a team (hire, manage) or do everything alone? Candidates will read "founding" as a path to leading a team. Say what's true:
   - *"No team at launch. Budget for contractors and a production partner is approved."* (if true)
   - Or: *"Solo for the first year. Team growth depends on KlyntConf results."*

4. **"Founding" with no upside.** "Founding" signals equity or a seat at the table. If there's no equity, drop "Founding" or state the package. Otherwise it reads as bait.

5. **Pay transparency and exempt status** (verify with HR or legal before posting):
   - Many US jurisdictions (CA, CO, NY, WA, and others) require a salary range in the posting. EU pay-transparency rules also apply if you hire there. A single fixed number may or may not comply.
   - $58k is below the exempt-salary thresholds in some states (California and Washington, possibly New York City). If this is an overtime-heavy event role there, you risk misclassification. I haven't checked the current thresholds, so have someone confirm them for the role's location.

6. **Missing basics.** The posting says nothing on:
   - location or remote policy
   - event date, size, and city (what does "from scratch" mean? Is there a budget?)
   - travel, and benefits
   - who the role reports to

   Candidates filter on these first.

7. **"Fully own … from scratch" and "you'll build it all."** This is vague and a little ominous. Replace it with concrete outcomes (e.g. "first edition in Q3 2027, ~500 attendees, $X sponsorship target").
