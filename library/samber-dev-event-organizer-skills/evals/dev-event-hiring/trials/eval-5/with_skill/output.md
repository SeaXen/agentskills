## 2. Interview loop and exercise

### Exercise brief (candidate-facing, send as-is after you fill the brackets)

> - last year's community day recap
>
> **Deliver (max 2 pages):**
> 1. Three findings, each with the evidence behind it.
> 2. One 90-day experiment with a success metric.

> - Treat all data as confidential [NDA attached].

>
> We'll discuss it live in the final round.

### Audit rubric

Score each line 1–4 and note the evidence:
- Findings backed by the data given (data-driven).
- Prioritization and a clear recommendation (leadership).
- Reads member sentiment, not just counts (empathy).
- Experiment has a measurable success metric (growth, data-driven).
- Clear writing in two pages (communication).

### Interview questions

**Growth mindset**
1. What would you like to achieve in this role within a year?
2. What motivates you to do your job well?
3. Tell me about an event or program of yours that underperformed. What did you change?

**Data-driven.** Don't ask for metrics; score whether she gives them unprompted when describing past projects.
4. Walk me through your best-performing meetup or community. What were the results?

**Empathy**
5. What would your current or past colleagues say about working with you?
6. A long-time Discord member is being abrasive to newcomers. What do you do?

**Leadership**
7. What are three things you would change about our current community?
8. If the community wants one thing and your boss wants something else, what would you do? *Score for a concrete, specific answer, not a diplomatic non-answer.*
9. You have 3 cities and one of you. How do you decide where your time goes?

**Communication.** No scripted question. Score the quality of follow-up conversation across the whole loop.

**Scenarios** (borrowed from the event-planner sequence; score real-time troubleshooting, not memorized process):
10. The venue cancels 48 hours before your meetup. Walk me through the next two days.
11. A speaker no-shows at the community day. What happens?
12. You're $X under budget for the community day. What do you cut first? (Fill in the figure.)
