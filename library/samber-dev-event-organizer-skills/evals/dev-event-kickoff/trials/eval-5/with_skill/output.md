**Routing check: TerraPipe Summit, edition 2, in-person, 3 tracks**

Your format is fixed, so I used it directly as the gates. `in-room`, `conference` and `recurring` are open. `hackathon` and `company` don't apply, and I did not ask any interview questions.

| # | Skill | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@startup-pitch-contest` | Not hackathon judging |
| 2 | `samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-side-event-coordination` | Vetting the sponsor's meetup |
| 3 | `samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@cross-event-promotion` | A different skill from question 2 |
| 4 | `samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-speaker-cold-outreach` | The first invitation email |
| 5a | `samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-team-structure` | Decides whether to hire at all |
| 5b | `samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@dev-event-hiring` | Writes the job posting |

1. **Not the hackathon judging skill.** `hackathon-judging` covers work built during the event. Your demo day judges companies that already existed and have shipped products, which is `startup-pitch-contest`'s scope. That skill has no format gate, so it applies to a 3-track in-person conference. The `hackathon` gate is closed anyway, since you aren't running a hackathon.

2. **`event-side-event-coordination` owns vetting and approving the sponsor's meetup.** It covers third parties running satellites around your dates. The sponsor's bar meetup the evening before is exactly that.
   - It is not `event-official-social-program`, which covers your own social slots.
   - It is not `event-vip-social-program`, which covers a private gathering you host beside the programme.

3. **Different skill: `cross-event-promotion`.** It covers reciprocal swaps with another organizer's event, and a newsletter-mention swap with another conference is exactly that. The split between the two is where the satellite event sits:
   - A third party running something inside your dates goes to `event-side-event-coordination`.
   - A swap between your event and another organizer's event goes to `cross-event-promotion`.
   - `event-media-partnerships` is for barter with media outlets and communities, so it doesn't fit another conference.

4. **`event-speaker-cold-outreach`.** It covers the invitation to a speaker who is already shortlisted, and your 12 dream speakers are shortlisted.
   - `event-speaker-sourcing` stops at the ranked shortlist, so you're past it.
   - `event-speaker-experience` starts at the acceptance email, so it comes later.
   - Later in the same lane, `event-schedule-design` places accepted talks in the grid.

5. **Two skills in sequence.**
   - **Deciding whether to hire: `event-team-structure`.** It decides whether a first paid role is warranted at all and which one. It is `recurring`-gated, and edition 2 opens that gate.
   - **Writing the posting: `dev-event-hiring`.** It produces the posting, the scorecard and the interview loop, but only once the verdict is "hire".
   - Don't use `devrel-hiring` here. That belongs to the DevRel collection and covers developer-advocate roles, not event coordinators.

**Chain for question 5:** `event-team-structure` → `dev-event-hiring`. The verdict (hire, and which role) is what the second skill consumes.

**Gap:** none for these five questions.
