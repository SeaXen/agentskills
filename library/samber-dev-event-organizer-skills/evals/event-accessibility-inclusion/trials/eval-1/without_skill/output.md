# PixelForge Conf accessibility page, ready to publish, plus what to check before Friday

I did not copy the foundation's page as your baseline. Its commitments (captioning in every room, free childcare, a nursing room, equipment rental) are budgeted line items for a large organization. If you publish them without funding, attendees will plan around promises you can't keep, and the people who relied on them are the ones who get hurt.

The page below promises only what is free or cheap, and it lists everything else as "not offered yet", with a way to ask. Fill in the `[brackets]` before it goes live.

## Before you sign Friday

The venue is the one accessibility decision you can't fix after signing. Get answers in writing, or add them to the contract:

1. **Step-free access.** Is there a step-free route from street to every room, the toilets, and the catering area? Ask for the venue's accessibility documentation.
2. **Lifts.** Is each lift large enough for a wheelchair or scooter, and what is the backup if one is out of service?
3. **Accessible toilets.** How many are there, and on which floors?
4. **Stage access.** Can a speaker who uses a wheelchair reach the stage without a lift or ramp that you'd have to rent?
5. **Quiet space.** Can you get one small room for the whole event at no extra cost? It serves as a quiet room, a nursing or pumping space, and a prayer space.
6. **Induction loop.** Do rooms have a hearing loop or other assistive listening, or can the AV team add one, and at what cost?
7. **Lighting and sound.** Can the venue dim the lights and avoid strobe effects? Does the PA have a mixer output you can route to a captioning or streaming feed?
8. **Streaming and caption feed.** Can a captioner or live-caption tool plug in without an extra fee?
9. **Assistance animals.** Are they permitted?
10. **Contract terms.** Is access to the quiet room and step-free routes written in? Can you cancel or renegotiate if the venue's accessibility claims prove false?

If the venue fails items 1 to 3, that is a reason to delay signing. Everything else on the page can be adjusted later.

## Decisions for you

- **Captioning:** the one real cost decision. Human live captioning for a single room typically runs in the low hundreds of euros per hour. That figure is my rough estimate, so get quotes. Free AI captions are a reasonable best-effort fallback if you label them honestly, as the page does.
- **Accessibility fund:** I suggest a small line in the ticket budget. A pay-what-you-can ticket tier or an optional "add a euro" donation could fund it.
- **Childcare:** this needs licensed staff and insurance, so I left it out. If you want it later, the page says so.

# Accessibility at PixelForge Conf

PixelForge Conf is a community-run conference for about 350 people in Lyon, organized entirely by volunteers. We want everyone to be able to take part. We have a small budget, so below we say what we **will do**, what we will **try to do**, and what we **can't offer this year**. We would rather be honest than promise something we can't deliver.

**To ask for anything on this page, email [accessibility@pixelforgeconf.example].** We read every message. Please write at least [4 weeks] before the conference, and earlier if you can, so we have time to act. We will reply within [5 working days]. You do not need to share medical details or give a reason.

## The venue

- **Location:** [venue name and address], Lyon, [dates].
- **Step-free access:** [confirm: yes or no, and describe the route]. All session rooms, the catering area, and the toilets are [confirm] reachable without stairs.
- **Accessible toilets:** [number and floors].
- **Lifts:** [number, and what we do if one is out of order].
- **Transport:** nearest metro or tram stop is [stop], about [X] minutes on foot [step-free? confirm]. Accessible parking: [confirm or "ask us"].
- We will publish a **venue access guide** with photos and a floor plan by [date].

## What we commit to

### Seeing and reading
- Slides: we require speakers to use large type (minimum [24 pt]) and high contrast, and to describe important visuals out loud.
- Slides will be shared online before or right after each talk.
- Our website and ticketing pages aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. If you hit a barrier, tell us and we will fix it or give you the information another way.

### Hearing and understanding
- **Captions:** [choose one and delete the others]
  - *Live captions are provided in [main room] by a professional captioner.* Other rooms use automatic captions, which are less accurate, particularly on technical terms and accents.
  - *Automatic live captions are provided in all rooms. They are not perfect, especially for technical vocabulary and accented speech.*
- Speakers use a microphone in every room, including for audience questions.
- Hearing loop or assistive listening: [confirm yes, no, or "contact us"].
- Talks will be recorded and posted with corrected captions after the event, where we are able to [confirm].
- Sessions are in [English / French / both]. [State this clearly.]

### Moving around
- Reserved seating for wheelchair users and anyone who needs it, near exits and with clear sightlines.
- Clear, wide aisles. We will not place cables or signs across walkways.
- Speakers who use a wheelchair can reach the stage [confirm].
- Volunteers wearing [colour] shirts can guide you or carry things. Ask at the registration desk.
- Seats will be available in the queue and catering area.

### Sensory needs
- **Quiet room:** a room open all day [confirm the room] for rest, decompression, or prayer. It is dimly lit and has no amplified sound.
- We ask presenters not to use flashing or strobe effects, and to warn the room before loud sounds.
- Name badges have a coloured sticker option: green for "happy to talk", yellow for "talk to me carefully", red for "please don't approach". It is optional and entirely your choice.

### Food and health
- Catering will label major allergens and mark vegetarian, vegan, halal, and gluten-free options [confirm what you can really label].
- Tell us about dietary needs when you register. We will do our best, but we can't guarantee a separate meal for every requirement. Bring your own food if you have severe allergies. Contact us first and we will help.
- Free water at all times.
- Assistance dogs are welcome.
- A first aid kit and a named first-aid contact are at the registration desk.

### Neurodiversity and anxiety
- The detailed schedule will be published [X weeks] before the event, with room, speaker, and topic for every session.
- Breaks of at least [15 minutes] between sessions.
- Seats near the door are always available if you want to leave without disruption.
- You are welcome to move, stand, stim, fidget, or leave the room during talks.

### Respect and safety
- Our code of conduct applies to everyone: [link]. Report concerns to [contact] or any organizer.
- Photography: a "no photos" sticker on your badge means do not photograph or film that person.

## What we can't offer this year

We are volunteers with no earmarked accessibility budget, so these are **not** available:

- Professional live captioning in every room [adjust to your decision above]
- Sign language interpretation. We can try to arrange it if you ask early, but we can't promise it.
- Childcare or a children's programme. Children are welcome in the main room if they stay with you.
- A dedicated nursing room. The quiet room can serve this purpose and is private on request [confirm].
- Mobility equipment hire (wheelchairs, scooters). A local rental service can supply this: [add local service if you find one].
- Travel or accommodation funding, except where noted below.

If one of these would decide whether you can attend, **tell us**. We may not be able to solve it, but we will tell you honestly, and we want to know what barriers matter most so we can plan next year.

## Before it goes live

1. **Delete every `[confirm]` you can't confirm.** An unverified line is exactly the kind of promise that causes harm.
2. **Choose one captioning option** and delete the other.
3. **Set up the email address** and give two volunteers access, so requests don't depend on one person.
4. **Decide the fund and companion policy**, or delete those sections.
5. **Review the page against the final venue contract** once it's signed.
6. **Name an accessibility lead** among the volunteers, so it has one owner.
