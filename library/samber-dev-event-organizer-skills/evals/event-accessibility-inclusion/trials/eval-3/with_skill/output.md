## Fix 1 and 2: the field

Make it one optional field. The skill's own copy, adapted [sourced pattern]:

> **Do you need anything from us to attend?** Tell us here. For example a step-free route, captioning or interpretation, a nursing room, dietary needs, or childcare. [Named owner] reads these and replies within [M] days, including if the answer is no. We keep what you tell us only until [event date + 2 weeks], share it only with the organizers who arrange what you asked for, and delete it after that.

Rules for the field:
- **Optional, never required.** A required field forces a disclosure or a false answer.
- **Free text, not a condition list.** Ask what they need, not what they have.
- **Examples and a named person.** Someone who has never seen an event book an interpreter does not know they can ask.
- **Retention printed on the field.** People disclose when they can see what happens to it.

Before you use the platform's field, check these. If you cannot answer them, use the inbox only (Fix 3):
1. Can you export the answers and delete them at the debrief?
2. Who at the platform can read them, and for how long do they keep them?
3. Can you turn the field off for the platform's own exports and integrations?

The field reaches more people than an inbox does. The price is that the data sits in the platform's records. You choose that trade knowingly. [sourced]
