## Three things I changed

1. **No "tickets almost gone."** You're at about 40% of 600 seats. That claim is false at send time, and the skill treats a made-up scarcity claim as a banned move. The real deadline already creates urgency. The only scarcity claim I'd allow is a true one. For example, if the early-bird tier has its own allotment and it's nearly used up, add "only [N] early-bird tickets left." Tell me the number and I'll add it.
2. **One email, Thursday, not two.** The rule is one cutoff, one last-call email, and a second last-call is on the don't-send list. A Thursday send is also the answer to your weekend-stats worry. It reaches people on a weekday with three days left, and nobody has to read anything on the weekend. A Sunday-morning email would be a second last-call to the same people, so I haven't written one. If you want a Sunday touch, make it a social post, not another email.
3. **Don't quietly extend to Monday.** Announcing Sunday and then moving it is an invented deadline. Your Thursday email would be a false claim, and the people who bought early on trust would find out. Pricing cutoffs belong to `event-ticket-pricing`, and I don't set them. If Monday is the real cutoff, change it openly and use Monday in every email, including this one. Then there's no need for the quiet extension, because Thursday already lands on a weekday.
