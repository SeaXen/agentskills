# OpenGridCon: badges, chat-only help, lost property

**Short version:** skip the printed badges, don't rely on chat alone, and give lost property one named owner. One small staffed info point per building answers the second and third questions. It also gives you the professional look the badge advocate wants.

## 1. Printed name badges: skip them

**Throughput.** With no registration, every name must be typed at the door.
- At about 10 seconds each, 5,000 people is roughly 14 hours of single-station work.
- You would need several stations and a queue in front of every building.
- A free, open-door event would then open with a line, which is the opposite of the experience you want.

**It creates a registration you said you don't want.**
- A name list is personal data, and it carries GDPR obligations if you are in the EU.
- You would also have to decide how long to keep it and who can see it.

**It doesn't add security.** Nobody verifies identity at an open event, so the badge is cosmetic.

**What the teammate really wants is "we look organized."** You can get that more cheaply:
1. **Staff, volunteer and speaker badges or lanyards only.** This is the part that matters. Attendees need to see who they can ask.
2. **A self-serve sticker table at each entrance.** Offer blank stickers and markers, so attendees write their own name, or "ask me about X". It collects no data and has no queue.
3. **Door counters (clickers) if you want a headcount.** Count people in and out per building without recording who they are.

## 2. Chat server as the only support channel: not enough

**Reasons it fails as the only channel:**
- **Newcomers aren't on the server.** An open-door event draws people who have never heard of your community. They arrive with no account and no idea where to ask.
- **Connectivity.** 5,000 phones on campus wifi, plus dying batteries, will degrade it. Room changes are exactly the information that must still reach people when it does.
- **Wayfinding is physical.** Someone standing in the wrong building needs a sign or a person. They won't open an app to ask.
- **Noise.** A single busy help room buries room-change announcements.

**Keep the chat, but make it a second layer:**
1. **Signs are the primary source of truth for room changes.** Post the change on the old room's door and at building entrances. Add a QR code on each sign to a static schedule page, which loads fast and works on poor wifi.
2. **Chat has two channels.** One read-only announcements channel, and one help channel with a named volunteer on shift to watch it.
3. **One info point per building, staffed only at peak.** Cover arrival and mid-morning on each day, not all weekend. Two volunteers on 2–3 hour shifts is enough.
4. **A few roving volunteers in a recognizable shirt.** They cost little and cover the gaps.
5. **A printed one-page map and schedule at every entrance.** It is the fallback when wifi or chat fails.

## 3. Lost jackets and chargers: assign an owner

The problem is not the pile. Nobody is named to deal with it, so it falls to whoever is left on Sunday night. The fix is to name the owner in advance.

1. **Name one owner** in the run-of-show, with a deputy. A role works too, such as "info point lead, building A".
2. **Make the info points the drop-off and pickup spot.** One central lost-property shelf, with items brought there at the end of each day.
3. **Log each item with a tag** showing time and place found. Keep valuables (laptops, phones, wallets) in a locked box. Release them only to someone who can describe an identifying detail.
4. **Publish the policy** on the chat and on signs: where to ask, and until when.
5. **Set the endgame now.** For example, hold items 30 days, with a contact address for claims. After that, donate jackets and keep or donate the chargers. Decide who does the handoff on Sunday evening so it doesn't fall through.

## Net effect

| Concern | Plan | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Professionalism | Staff badges plus visible volunteers; sticker table for attendees | Low |
| Questions | Signs first, then info points at peak, chat as backup | Medium; volunteer shifts only |
| Lost property | Named owner plus info-point shelf plus a stated policy | Almost nothing |
