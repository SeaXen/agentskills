# ShipItCon swag plan: 4 problems with the current plan, and a fix for each

I haven't changed any schedule. I don't have your agenda file, and you told me to stay out of `skill-wip/`. The break change is in section 4 below.

## 1. The size lookup has no data behind it ❌

- Volunteers can't look up a size in the spreadsheet, because registration never asked for sizes.
- The spreadsheet has no size column, so the check-in plan can't work as written.

**Fix: send a one-question size survey to all registered attendees now.**
- Ask for size and cut (unisex or fitted) in one click.
- Even a 50–60% response rate beats guessing. Apply that response mix to the non-responders.
- Add the answers to the spreadsheet and print the size on each badge or on a sticker.
- Volunteers then read the badge and don't look anything up.
- ⏳ Send it today and close it in 3–4 days. Check your vendor's lead time against the event date, which I don't know.

## 2. Exactly 350 will give some people the wrong size ⚠️

- Any guessed spread, even an accurate one, leaves some sizes short and others in surplus.
- 350 also omits volunteers, speakers, sponsors and organizers, who all expect a hoodie.
- No-shows work in your favour, but you can't say by how much until the event.

**If the survey gets you real numbers, use those plus about 8%.**

**If it doesn't, here is a fallback spread for about 385 units.** The percentages are my assumptions, not data. Replace them with survey results as soon as you have any.

| Size | Share | Units |
|---|---|---|
| XS | 3% | 12 |
| S | 12% | 46 |
| M | 25% | 96 |
| L | 27% | 104 |
| XL | 18% | 69 |
| 2XL | 10% | 38 |
| 3XL | 5% | 19 |
| **Total** | | **384** |

- I added XS and 3XL. S–2XL leaves out people at both ends.
- I weighted M and L the highest, not L and XL. For a mixed crowd, that is the safer default.
- Leftovers can go to volunteers, sponsors or a post-event giveaway.

## 3. The "ladies junior fit tees in a different colour" idea ⚠️

- **Different garment:** women would get a tee while everyone else gets a hoodie.
- **Different colour:** it labels people by gender in a room full of matching shirts.
- **Unknown demand:** you don't know how many people want a fitted cut.
- **Sizing risk:** junior sizing runs small and is hard to guess.

**Fix: offer the same hoodie in the same colour, in a fitted cut.**
- Let people choose the cut in the survey rather than assuming it from gender.
- Order that cut in proportion to the survey answers. Without answers, start at 15% of the order as a placeholder and adjust.
- Many vendors sell a unisex and a fitted version of the same hoodie, so check yours.

## 4. The swag line and the break ⚠️

The break extension and the swag plan don't connect:
- You said swag goes out at the check-in desk, but a longer morning break doesn't help check-in.
- A single desk that checks in and hands out swag will queue badly at doors-open.

**Fix: separate the two steps.**
1. Check-in only scans the badge.
2. A swag table, ideally open all day, hands out pre-sorted stacks by size. Open it right after check-in and keep it open through the breaks.

If you still want a 30-minute morning break, the change has these steps:
1. Move every session after the break 15 minutes later, or take 15 minutes out of another slot.
2. Update the end time. Tell the venue, caterers, A/V and any hard stop.
3. Tell speakers, then update the published schedule and app.

Send me the agenda file and I'll make the edit. Tell me which slot absorbs the 15 minutes.
