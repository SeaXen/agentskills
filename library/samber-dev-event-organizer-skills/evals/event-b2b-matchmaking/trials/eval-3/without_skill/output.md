# OrbitConf prospectus and contract language for Kelvin Data

**Don't print "20 guaranteed meetings with senior platform engineers" as written.** You can't deliver it as stated, and a printed guarantee in a $25,000 package becomes a refund claim or a misrepresentation dispute.

- "Senior" is not something you verify. Attendees describe their own role in their own words.
- Each meeting needs the attendee to opt in, accept the request, and show up. You control none of those steps.
- Your matching pool is limited to opt-in attendees whose profile fits. Other sponsors draw on the same pool.

Instead, guarantee the part you control (the number of accepted, scheduled meetings with profile-matched attendees) and say so plainly. That closes nearly as well as the original line, and it's one you can keep.

## 1. Check the numbers before you sign

Pull the current opt-in profiles, or last year's rate if this edition's program hasn't opened. Compute:

1. Opt-in attendees (out of 1,200).
2. Of those, how many have a role in their own words matching platform, infrastructure, SRE, DevOps, or similar.
3. Of those, how many are evaluating Kelvin Data's category.
4. Your expected acceptance rate for sponsor requests.

If the matching pool times the acceptance rate is not comfortably above 20, with a margin for no-shows and other sponsors, lower the guaranteed number. A guarantee of 12 plus a target of 20 is safer than a guarantee of 20.

## 2. Prospectus text

Use one of these. Option A is the better fit for the deal.

**Option A (guaranteed minimum plus target):**
> **Curated meeting program.** Through OrbitConf's opt-in meeting program, we guarantee a minimum of **[12–20] scheduled meetings** between your team and attendees who match your target profile, with a target of 20. Attendees self-declare their role and what they are evaluating. We match on those declarations, and each meeting requires the attendee's acceptance.

**Option B (shortest, if the sales lead needs a punchy line):**
> **Up to 20 pre-scheduled meetings** with platform and infrastructure engineers who've told us they're evaluating tools in your category. Matched from attendee self-declared profiles in our opt-in meeting program.

Option B has no guarantee, so don't pair it with a refund clause.

Add this footnote to either option:
> Meetings are with opt-in attendees whose self-declared profile matches the criteria agreed in your sponsorship agreement. Roles and interests are self-reported and not independently verified.

Avoid "guaranteed," "senior," and "verified" in any marketing copy unless the contract defines them.

## 3. Contract clauses

Have counsel review these. They're a working draft, not legal advice.

**Definitions**
- **"Target Profile":** the role keywords and evaluation topics listed in Schedule A, such as "platform engineer," "SRE," "infrastructure," or "DevOps" in the attendee's own role description, plus "evaluating [category]" in the evaluation field. Titles and seniority labels are not part of the definition.
- **"Matched Attendee":** an attendee who opted into the Meeting Program and whose self-declared profile matches the Target Profile.
- **"Scheduled Meeting":** a meeting request from Sponsor that a Matched Attendee has accepted and that is on the calendar for a slot during the Event.

**Guarantee**
> Organizer shall deliver no fewer than **[N]** Scheduled Meetings to Sponsor, with a target of 20. Organizer's obligation is to present Sponsor's requests to Matched Attendees and to schedule accepted requests. Organizer does not guarantee that any individual attendee will accept a request or attend a meeting.

**Sponsor obligations (this protects you)**
- Sponsor submits its meeting requests by [date], with at least [N×3] requests so the guarantee has enough volume to work with.
- Sponsor staffs every Scheduled Meeting and answers requests within 48 hours.
- Meetings that Sponsor cancels or misses still count as delivered.

**No verification**
> Attendee-provided information, including role, seniority, employer, and evaluation interests, is self-reported. Organizer makes no representation as to its accuracy and does not verify it.

**Remedy (sole and exclusive)**
> If fewer than [N] Scheduled Meetings are delivered, Sponsor's sole remedy is a credit of **$[X] per missing meeting**, capped at **[20]%** of the Sponsorship Fee, applied to the next OrbitConf edition or refunded at Organizer's election. Meetings that attendees accepted but did not attend count as delivered.

A pro-rata credit with a cap keeps the exposure bounded. The "sole remedy" wording is what stops a shortfall from turning into a breach claim.

**Data and consent**
> Organizer shares attendee information with Sponsor only for attendees who have consented in the Meeting Program terms to sharing with sponsors, and only the profile fields the attendee agreed to share. Sponsor uses it solely to arrange and conduct the meeting and makes no further use without separate consent.

## 4. Your own housekeeping

- ⚠️ Check the opt-in form's consent wording now. If it doesn't say that profile data goes to sponsors, you can't hand it to Kelvin Data, and the deal's core mechanism is blocked under GDPR.
- Cap how many sponsors get a meeting guarantee, so they don't all draw on the same pool.
- Tell your sales lead the line can stay punchy as long as the guarantee in the contract is the narrower, defined one. The prospectus shouldn't promise more than the contract does.
