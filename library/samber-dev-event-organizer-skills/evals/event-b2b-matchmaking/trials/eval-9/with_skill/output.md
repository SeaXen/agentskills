# Climate infrastructure day: matchmaking setup

## 1. Run the program? Yes.

You invited two populations separately (9 startups, 6 corporate development partners) so they would meet. That is the genuine two-population structure that justifies the program.

I'm assuming no sponsor package names a meeting number. If one does, tell me, because it changes sections 4 and 5.

## 2. Matching: organizer brokering, with mutual acceptance

**Mechanism.** Fifteen people is small enough for one named person to hold in their head. That makes brokering the best choice for relevance and effort.

- The person reads all 15 entries once, in one sitting, before pairing anything.
- They draft pairings with a one-line reason each.
- They send each side the same introduction, with a way to decline without explaining.
- Your ops platform's tag matching can order a candidate list for that person. It must never create a pair. A pair exists only after both sides accept.

**Matching criterion (write this before the form):**

> "A meeting is a good match when a corporate partner who [stated problem or investment area they entered themselves] meets a startup that [stated capability they entered themselves], and both accepted the pairing."

Replace the bracketed parts with your real situation and capability wording.

## 3. Intake form: what to change

| Your sketch | Verdict | Replace with |
|---|---|---|
| Dropdown of 40 job titles | ❌ Flattens the distinction the criterion turns on | A free-text "role, in your words" field |
| Company size filled from LinkedIn | ❌ Refuse. It is inferred data the person never typed or agreed to share | Drop it. If it matters, make it an optional self-entered field |
| Annual procurement budget bracket | ❌ Refuse. It turns the form into lead qualification performed on partners, and makes the organizer hold a qualification record | Drop it. Partners can state a budget in their own words if they choose |
| Optional "what are you hoping to get out of this" | ⚠️ Make it **required**, one line. It is the only field that establishes intent. Optional means a pool of people who asked for nothing | Required one-liner |
| 25 topic checkboxes | ⚠️ Too many. People select few of them and the match matrix goes sparse | A short fixed list. Cut to the topics your criterion consumes |

**Fields to add:**

- **Side**: startup or corporate partner.
- **Availability windows** within the meeting block. Collect them only after the block's placement is fixed, or you collect twice.
- **A consent record**, stored as a record rather than a preference.

**Consent wording.** It must be in the person's line of sight when they submit. Settle it before collecting anything, because retrofitting consent is the one error here that can't be undone. It must state:

1. Which of their fields a counterpart sees.
2. Who sees them: one accepted counterpart, or the organizer only. Name which.
3. What triggers the sharing: a mutual acceptance.
4. What they get: the meetings, stated plainly.
5. How they withdraw, and what happens to pairings already accepted.
6. When the data is deleted, and by what date.

Read items 1-3 aloud and ask whether anyone would be surprised on the day. Promise the pairing, never the conversation.

## 4. Scheduling: organizer-assigned grid

Both populations were invited specifically to meet each other, and neither has another reason to be in the building. That meets the skill's promotion condition for an organizer-assigned grid. It delivers the most meetings and costs the most autonomy, and each side must still confirm.

**Rules:**

- **No meeting without acceptance.** Never place a meeting on someone's schedule they haven't accepted. This is the one thing deleted outright.
- **Block placement.** Take the block's placement from your published grid, and never move a session for it. Route any conflict to your schedule design.
- **Slot construction.**
  - Count real accepted pairs, plus a margin for late ones.
  - Make the slots equal.
  - Leave gaps between slots.
  - Choose the length from your block and your pair count. I won't state one, because any number I gave would be invented.
- **Conflict check.** Check each person's slots against the sessions they attend or speak at. Resolve conflicts by moving the slot.
- **Confirmation before publishing.** Confirm every pair with both sides first. Publishing is the point of no return.
- **One view per person.** Don't publish the full grid, which would disclose who meets whom.
- **Staffing.** Name one organizer on duty for the block.

## 5. Sponsor promises and no-shows

**Quota.** Make no meeting promise unless a sponsor demands one. If one does, offer a best-efforts target with written criteria and no remedy. Never print a bare number with no criteria and no make-good. A meeting quota is access only. It never buys a speaking slot or a share of the agenda.

**No-shows:**

- **Default:** require an explicit acceptance at booking, plus a reminder before the block.
- **If someone is staffed at the meeting area:** add a standby queue to refill emptied slots.
- **Never** overbook, because you hold no measured show-up count from this program.
- **Never** use deposits or penalties on the attendee side.

## 6. Deliverables

**One meeting brief**, identical for both sides:

- The matching criterion sentence.
- Who is eligible and how to join.
- The data collected and who sees it.
- That both sides must accept every pairing.
- Block placement and the physical location.
- How to decline or withdraw.
- What happens to a missed slot.
- The named organizer on duty.

**One outcome record**, one row per pairing:

- The two sides.
- Whether both accepted.
- Whether the meeting was held.
- Whether the slot was refilled from standby.
- Which criteria it was checked against.

Record nothing about what was said in the meetings.

**Measure**, all self-set and meaningful only across your own future editions:

- Held against booked.
- Empty-slot count.
- Mutual-acceptance rate, reported as a proxy for relevance.

Two gates:

- **Unwanted-meeting complaints:** the target is zero.
- **Confirmation completeness:** every pair accepted by both sides before publication.

Don't quote vendor fill rates or no-show figures.
