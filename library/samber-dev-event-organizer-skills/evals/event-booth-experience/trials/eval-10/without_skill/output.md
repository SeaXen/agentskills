**Verdict: rent the cart, drop the premium zone for this year.**

## Why the cart is worth it
- **Foot traffic.** Developers queue for coffee, and a central cart gives every sponsor a captive crowd.
- **Cost.** Cart rentals with a barista usually run about $1k to $3k per day. That's a rough guess, so get quotes. It's roughly 3 to 10% of your $32k sponsor revenue (8 × $4k).
- **Capacity.** One barista makes about 60 to 80 drinks an hour. At 300 people, a single barista means long queues at breaks. Budget for two baristas or stagger the breaks.
- **Venue rules.** Many venues have exclusive catering. Check this before you sign anything.

## Why the premium zone is the problem
- **Broken promise.** All 8 sponsors paid the same $4k for identical benefits. If you give your favorites a better spot, the other sponsors paid the same for less. Check your contract for any wording on booth location or visibility. Even without any, it will feel unfair.
- **Renewals.** Eight sponsors is a small group. The sponsors who got the worse spot will notice, and you need them back next year.
- **Favorites.** "The ones we like most" isn't a sponsorship criterion. Sponsors need a rule they can see, such as price, order of purchase, or a draw.

## What to do instead
1. **Make the layout fair around the cart.** Put the 8 booths in a ring around it, all equally close. Assign spots by a draw, or by order of purchase if you can defend it.
2. **Sell the coffee.** Offer all 8 sponsors the same optional add-on, such as "Coffee cart presented by X" for $1.5k to $2.5k. Sell it to the first to say yes, or run a sealed bid, and it can pay for the cart.
3. **Create tiers next year.** Publish them before sales open, for example $4k standard and $7k premium with cart-adjacent placement. This turns the favoritism into revenue that everyone can see.
4. **Avoid layout traps.** Don't let the coffee queue block the booths. Put the cart where the queue forms in open floor, not in front of a booth. Check power, water and the health permit.

## Questions for you
- What does the sponsor contract say about booth location?
- Does the venue have exclusive catering?
- Is the cart budget separate from sponsor revenue, or does it come out of that $32k?
