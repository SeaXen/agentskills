## What to add

Sponsors will ask about these first.

**Booth**
- Size, for example 10×10 ft or 3×3 m.
- Location: is placement assigned by tier, first-come, or by sponsor choice?
- Furniture: table size, number of chairs, and whether a cloth, signage, or backdrop is included.
- Whether booths are open or have walls, and whether pop-up banners are allowed.
- Whether sponsors can bring their own furniture or display.

**Power and internet**
- Number of outlets and amperage, and whether sponsors need to bring their own power strip or adapters.
- Internet type (shared Wi-Fi or wired), expected speed, and whether it's guaranteed.
- A fallback plan for when it fails. Sponsors running demos will ask.

**Schedule and logistics**
- Load-in and load-out times and dates.
- Booth hours.
- Whether shipping to the venue is allowed, the address and deadlines, and where boxes are held before and after.
- Parking and loading dock access.
- Whether the venue is secured overnight.
- Whether sponsors need event insurance or a certificate of insurance.

**People**
- How many badges or passes each tier gets.
- Whether extra passes can be bought, and at what price.
- Whether sponsors get attendee-list access or lead retrieval (badge scanning).
- Whether staff meals are included.

**Contract and deadlines**
- Payment terms and due dates.
- Cancellation and refund policy.
- Deadlines for logo, copy, and booth-assignment requests.
