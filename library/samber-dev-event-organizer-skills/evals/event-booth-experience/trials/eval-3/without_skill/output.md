# NorthStack Summit: load-in and load-out schedule (draft)

## ❌ The overnight build can't happen as planned

The venue has confirmed no access before 06:00 on day one and no overnight storage. An evening-before build would need access the venue has refused. Don't sell it as a perk until the venue agrees in writing.

Options, best first:

1. **Early-morning priority build.** The Platinum sponsor gets the first and longest build window, starting at 06:00. This is the only option that needs no venue exception.
2. **Pre-build off-site.** The sponsor assembles and tests the stand elsewhere, then ships it as labelled, pre-wired modules. This cuts on-site time to a fraction of a from-scratch build.
3. **Ask the venue for a paid exception.** This would cover evening-before access with their security and staff. Ask once, in writing, and treat a yes as a bonus. Don't sell the perk until it's confirmed.
4. **Later day-one doors.** Open doors at 11:00 instead of 09:00, which gives Platinum five hours. This costs every other sponsor and attendee, so use it only if options 1 and 2 fall short.

Rename the perk "Priority build window + dedicated crew and dock slot". That is honest and still premium.

## Risks to resolve before you publish this

- **Packing during the keynote.** This only works if the keynote is in a separate room and sponsors accept losing the last hour of traffic. Make it opt-in, soft-pack only, with staff still present. Otherwise sponsors lose leads, and the keynote competes with their packing.
- **Platinum finishing after doors open.** If the build isn't done at 09:00, that area becomes a hazard. Keep the stand at the back or in a roped-off zone, and set a hard "build must be complete by" time. Otherwise cut the stand's scope.
- **Dock capacity.** 16 booths with freight need staggered dock slots and a named dock manager. Confirm bay count and forklift availability.
- **Crates and empties.** There's no overnight storage, so decide where empties go on day one and who removes them.
- **Penalties.** Add a clause: anything left past 21:00 is removed at the sponsor's cost, plus a fee. The venue will likely charge you.
