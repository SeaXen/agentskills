# Recommendation: passport card, with badge scans as an opt-in way to validate stamps and one central prize

Your plan is the right backbone. The two alternatives each solve a sponsor problem, but neither solves your actual goal, which is for attendees to visit every booth and sponsors to get real engagement.

## Why each option lands where it does

| Option | Drives "visit every booth"? | Real engagement? | Main problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passport with engagement-gated stamps | ✅ Yes, completion is the goal | ✅ Yes, if gated | Staff must enforce the gate consistently |
| Badge scan = stamp | ⚠️ Partly | ❌ No, it rewards drive-by scans | Junk leads and GDPR exposure |
| Per-booth raffles | ❌ No | ⚠️ Shallow | Nothing links the booths, and big sponsors win on prizes |

- **Badge scan as the stamp:** Attendees will tap and run, so sponsors get a list of people who never talked to them. If a stamp requires handing over contact data, consent isn't freely given. Under GDPR (relevant if StackFest has EU attendees) that is risky for you as the organizer.
- **Per-booth raffles:** Each raffle is a separate game, so attendees visit only the booths with prizes they like. Sponsors with bigger budgets pull the crowd, and small sponsors get ignored. You also take on prize logistics and complaints times the number of booths.

## What to run

1. **Keep the passport.** Each booth gets one stamp. Staff give it only after a real conversation or after the attendee watches a 2-minute demo.
2. **Make badge scan an optional validation method, not the requirement.**
   - Booth staff scan the badge after the conversation or demo, and the scan grants the stamp.
   - Attendees who don't want to share data get a hand stamp or a staff-scanned booth QR instead. They get the same stamp and the sponsor gets no data.
   - Show a short consent line at registration ("share my contact with sponsors whose booths I engage with"). Sponsors only receive contacts from attendees who agreed.
3. **Add a one-tap qualifier on the scan.** Staff pick "conversation" or "demo". Sponsors get a lead that says what happened, which beats a raw list.
4. **Run one central prize, not booth raffles.** Completed passports enter a single draw at the closing session or at the organizer desk.
   - Require something like 8 of 10 stamps, not all of them. One empty or closed booth then can't block anyone.
   - Pool the prize across sponsors so no single sponsor's budget decides the outcome.
5. **Let sponsors keep a small raffle on the side** if they want one. It's their own add-on and not part of the mechanism.
6. **Brief every booth beforehand.** Give them a stamp rule in one sentence, a 2-minute demo script or loop, and a staffing rule so someone is always free to stamp.

## Sponsor pitch

Tell the sponsors who asked for scanning: "You get the contact list, and each lead is tagged with what happened. We only count scans after a real interaction, so your list is warm."

## Questions that could change this

- **Attendee and booth count:** Above roughly 15 booths, require fewer than all stamps. Under 8, require all.
- **EU or UK attendees:** If yes, have your DPO or lawyer check the consent wording before you print anything.
- **Do you have an event app that scans badges?** If so, the digital passport is nearly free. If not, a paper card with hand stamps is fine, and sponsors can use their own scanners.
