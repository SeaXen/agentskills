# Floor brief: Platinum custom stand and ceiling banner (KernelDays)

Skill used: `event-booth-experience`, organizer side. The skill asks its interview questions one at a time. Here I've stated the assumption each answer would settle, and listed the open questions at the end.

## Bottom line

Their ask lands on the "custom structure for the top tier only" rung. That rung needs two things at once:

1. The Platinum package promises floor presence a table can't deliver.
2. The venue permits rigging.

Item 1 is arguably true. Item 2 is **unknown**, and it decides everything. The ceiling banner is the risky part. Truss rigging at a commercial convention hall usually needs the venue's approval and often the venue's own contractor. The venue's answer arrives on its own timeline and **can't be worked around once it's a no**. Ask it first, in writing, this week.

Do not freeze the print plan around a 4 m stand until you have that answer.

## What to line up, in order

### 1. Rigging approval (blocks the banner)

Ask the venue in writing:

- Can anything hang from the ceiling truss or attach to the building?
- What sign-off is needed, and who approves it?
- Must the venue's contractor or a union crew do the work?
- What does it cost, and who pays (venue, you, or the sponsor)?
- What are the lead time and the submission deadline?
- What does the sponsor need to submit (drawings, weights, engineer's stamp, insurance)?

I won't state a universal weight limit or lead time. Those are venue-specific.

### 2. Fire, egress and occupancy review (before print)

- A 4 m custom stand changes aisle geometry and sightlines. Have the fire marshal or the venue safety officer review the plan **before** the print run, not before doors.
- A printed, signed plan is your least reversible artifact. Fixing a narrow lane on the morning means moving a sponsor who is already built.

### 3. Venue labour and electrical rules

- Does the venue require its own or union crew for stand build, load-in or dock work?
- Who may run a power drop, and what supervision does the venue require?
- Ask these in writing too. They can't be renegotiated mid-setup.

### 4. Contract check: what "premium floor presence" promised

- Re-read the Platinum agreement and prospectus. Does it say a custom stand, a ceiling banner, or a set footprint?
- If it only says "premium floor presence", you owe a premium position and a clear spec. You don't owe a hung banner.
- If the venue refuses rigging, the sponsor is owed a make-good only if the package promised it. Decide your make-good option now, before you need it.
- Route contract questions to `event-sponsor-agreement` and `event-sponsor-pricing`. This skill defines the physical spec only.

### 5. Write the Platinum spec row

Fill all eight rows. The vertical limit and signage rule are the two organizers skip and regret.

| Row | Platinum (custom stand) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Footprint | 4 m stand, with plot depth and width stated as dimensions | **You decide** |
| Furniture | Sponsor-built; you state what the venue provides | **You decide** |
| Position class | Premium zone, adjusted for the larger plot | **You decide** |
| Power | Drop count, supplier, cost and request deadline | **Venue answers** |
| Network | Event WiFi, no extra cost; wired only if the venue offers it | **Venue answers** |
| Vertical limit | Maximum stand height. Suspended or ceiling-attached items allowed only with written rigging approval. | **Venue answers** |
| Signage rule | Nothing overhanging the aisle or fire lane. Banner location and size per approved rigging plan. | **You decide, venue sign-off** |
| Staffing | Minimum and maximum people, plus credentials | **You decide** |

Don't write "custom stand permitted" without dimensions and a height cap. The blanks get filled at 08:00 by whoever is standing there.

### 6. Protect the neighbors

- A 4 m stand eats floor. Don't reopen the venue's square footage. Redistribute within the signed floor plan.
- Check sightlines. A tall stand can hide the booth behind it.
- Confirm that the Platinum zone, the next tier's booths and the circulation path still work with the larger plot.

### 7. Setup and teardown schedule

A custom build won't fit a one-hour shared window. Use **tiered windows** for Platinum if the venue allows an earlier slot, with these rows:

- Shipping window opens, with venue address and fees.
- Power, A/V and rigging **request deadline** (published row, not an email sentence).
- Platinum move-in slot, earlier or longer than the shared window.
- Floor walk before doors: aisles clear, nothing overhanging, drops live.
- Teardown **never** before the last session ends.

An overnight pre-day build exists only if the venue grants out-of-hours access **and** secure storage. Don't offer it otherwise.

### 8. Sponsor-side deliverables to request

Request these in writing, with a date:

- Stand drawings with dimensions and height.
- Banner dimensions and weight.
- Rigging submission, engineer sign-off and insurance, as the venue requires.
- Contractor details for build and install.
- Power load needed.

## Timeline (work back from print)

P = print date, about 5 weeks from today (around 2026-11-16).

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| **This week** | Send rigging, labour, electrical and fire-review questions to the venue in writing. Re-read the Platinum contract. Ask the sponsor for drawings. |
| **P − 4 weeks** | Venue answers. Decide: build a custom stand with banner, stand only, or table spec with a make-good. |
| **P − 3 weeks** | Draft the floor plan with the Platinum plot. Publish the draft spec row to the sponsor. |
| **P − 2 weeks** | Fire and egress review of the draft plan. Collect the sponsor's rigging submission. |
| **P − 1 week** | Final sign-off on the plan and the venue's written rigging answer. |
| **P** | Print. |

If the venue hasn't answered by P − 3 weeks, **print without the banner** and treat it as a make-good conversation. Don't hold the plan hostage to the venue.

## Decision tree

- **Rigging approved:** custom stand and banner go in the spec, with the approved dimensions and the venue's contractor.
- **Rigging refused, stand allowed:** the stand goes ahead. Offer a free-standing banner behind the stand, within the height limit, plus a make-good if the contract promised hanging signage.
- **Both refused:** fall back to the standard Platinum table spec in a premium zone, plus a make-good. Settle that before print, not on the morning.

## Rules to keep

- Don't quote a booth-position value or lead-lift figure to the sponsor. No usable data exists.
- Name one organizer on duty as the floor contact, and tell the sponsor who it is.
- Publish this as one generic floor brief, then hand it to `event-sponsor-fulfillment` to execute for this sponsor. Don't build a per-sponsor tracking matrix here.
- Self-set elements (the tier ordering, the sample timeline offsets) are my defaults, not published standards.

## Open questions

1. Does the venue permit rigging, and what sign-off does it need?
2. Does the venue require its own or union labour for stand build and rigging?
3. What exactly did the Platinum agreement and prospectus promise?
4. What is the print deadline, exactly? I assumed about 5 weeks out.
5. Who supplies booth power, what does a drop cost, and by when must it be requested?
6. Is this a one-off edition or recurring? If recurring, this spec becomes next year's starting point.
7. Do you have the political capital to tell Platinum "no hung banner" if the venue refuses?
