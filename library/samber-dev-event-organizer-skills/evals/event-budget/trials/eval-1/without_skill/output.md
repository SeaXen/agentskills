# KernelKraft Conf: budget and ticket price

## Answer

- **Break-even ticket price is about €77 (range €74–79)** under the assumptions below, with sponsors at €45k gross and the venue at a placeholder.
- **Recommended pricing:** €89 standard and €69 early-bird, with the early-bird capped at about the first 100 tickets. At about 250 attendees that blends to roughly €83, which leaves a small cushion over break-even.
- **The price is much more sensitive to sponsor money than to headcount.** Each €10k of sponsorship you don't land adds about €32 to the ticket price (see the sensitivity table).

- **Fiscal sponsor:** 8% of all revenue, ticket and sponsor money alike.
- **Card fees:** 3% + €0.30 per ticket, a blended rate. Sponsors pay by bank transfer, so they carry no card fee.
- **Sponsors:** €45,000 gross, which is €41,400 after the 8% fee. This is illustrative and unsigned.
- **No-shows:** tickets sold = attendees ÷ 0.78. No-shows pay but don't eat, so they carry no variable cost.

## Results (sponsors at €45k gross)

| Scenario | Attendees | Tickets sold | Total cost | Sponsor net | Break-even price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 150 | 192 | 53,955 | 41,400 | **€74** |
| Mid | 250 | 321 | 63,305 | 41,400 | **€77** |
| Ceiling | 390 | 500 | 76,395 | 41,400 | **€79** |

The price barely moves with headcount. Catering and swag scale with attendees, and the fixed costs are mostly covered by sponsors.

Formula for the price: net per ticket = 0.89 × price − 0.30. The 0.89 is 1 − 8% fiscal fee − 3% card fee, and the €0.30 is the per-transaction card fee.

## Announcing the date while the bank account isn't live

- **Announce the date and open an interest or waitlist form.** Don't open paid ticket sales or invoice sponsors until the account is live.
- **Don't publish prices yet.** Every price above depends on a placeholder and unsigned sponsors.
- **Check who signs the venue contract and pays any deposit.** That should be the fiscal sponsor, not an individual, and the deposit has to be paid from somewhere before the account exists.
- **Confirm the 8% terms in writing.** Ask whether it applies to gross revenue including sponsor money, and how in-kind sponsorship is treated.
- **Confirm the VAT treatment.** Portugal's standard VAT is 23%. If tickets must show VAT-inclusive prices, €89 nets about €72. The model assumes net-of-VAT figures, so gross up the prices if VAT applies.
