## What to do instead

You have two years of costed budgets and per-line actuals. That is better evidence than any blog percentage, and it lets you size the contingency from your own events. The skill builds it as a stack of three tools that run together. They are not alternatives, and I'm not ranking them.

1. **Floor.** A fixed cash reserve for the small, certain surprises. Set it from the smallest total of your own late-arriving unplanned costs over the last two editions. It is a single number, decided once.
2. **Pad on estimates.** A percentage added to expense estimates before summing. It protects against line-item underestimates, not a revenue shortfall. Derive it from your data:
   - Compute actual vs. planned **per line** for both years. Don't use the total, because a total nets overruns against underruns and hides the pattern.
   - Pad only the lines that overran in both years, at the size of their typical overrun.
   - Leave stable lines unpadded.
   - Label the result "self-set, derived from our 2 prior editions."
3. **Downgrade ladder (Good/Better/Best).** This is the only tool that absorbs a whole-event shortfall. You already hold last year's fully costed versions, so for a recurring event it is nearly free. Re-price the tiers at current quotes, add a dated checkpoint per tier driven by revenue actually received, and attach the cut order:
   1. Discretionary extras: evening event, swag upgrades.
   2. Per-head quantities, such as catering and badges, only before the guarantee locks.
   3. Fixed structural lines last, because attrition and F&B minimums are owed on what you committed, not on turnout.

With two editions of data, label the number "self-set, n=2". Two points show a pattern but aren't a statistical basis.

## Check before sign-off

- **Lowest cash point.** Compare when deposits leave against when sponsor and ticket money lands. A budget can be profitable and still run out of cash. Real sponsor terms often take 50-100% at signing, but verify yours.
- **Head count.** Per-head lines should use the expected show-up number, not registrations.
- **Quotes.** Every line needs a dated quote or an explicit "no quote yet." Silent placeholders mean the budget isn't built yet.
- **Off the top.** Fiscal-host share (published range 7-14%) and processing fees on both ticket and sponsor payments come off before any line is funded.
