# HackHarbor budget

## Bottom line

- **Budget for about 330 participants plus 40 staff, not 640 people.** Free events usually see 40–60% of registrants show up. I assumed 55%, so replace that with your own past check-in rate.
- That gives roughly **$28k** for food, drinks, shirts and contingency. Your current plan costs about **$39–40k** on the same prices. Ordering for no-shows wastes about $12k.
- **A $10 fee won't close the gap.** It would raise about $3k, not $6k, and it would lower sign-ups. Raise the money from sponsors instead.

## Why "$6k right there" doesn't hold

1. $6k assumes all 600 registrants pay and show up. At 55% attendance, 330 × $10 is $3,300, or about **$3,000 after card fees** (roughly 3% + $0.30 each).
2. Moving from free to paid lowers conversion, so you'd collect from fewer than 330 people.
3. $3k covers about 11% of the variable budget. The fee changes the headline ("free") and the audience, and it barely changes the gap.
4. Check your university's student-org rules on collecting fees before you consider this at all.

## What I'd do instead of a fee

- **Refundable $10 deposit, returned at check-in.** It cuts no-shows, which is the real cost driver, and it isn't meant as funding.
  - Waive it on request.
  - Confirm your university allows it.
- **A "reconfirm or lose your spot" email about 3 weeks out.** This is free and gives you a firm headcount for the shirt order.
- **Sponsors.**
  - Put sponsor logos on the shirts.
  - Ask a local caterer or pizza chain for in-kind food.
  - A rough ask is 5–6 sponsors at $4–6k each.
