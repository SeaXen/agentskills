Channels, best fit for a hard date:
- **Referral asks.** Ask past speakers and Denver platform-engineering leaders for 2-3 names each, with the slot brief attached. They arrive vouched and answer fast.
- **Prior-talk review.** Use it for the opener and any slot where one weak talk would sink the day.
- **A second channel outside your circle.** Directory search or community scouting. Referrals alone return your network's network.

## Reusable from the CFP design

- **Published criteria.** Put them in every invitation and on the program page. They come from the DevOpsDays six-point list:
  - No sponsor-bought slots, ever.
  - New content over rehashed talks.
  - Local and out-of-town balance.
  - Favor new speakers.
  - Bold subjects.
  - Tool or product pitches go to open space, if you have one.
- **Speaker benefits.** State only what's budget-confirmed. The floor is a free ticket.
- **Honorarium.** There is no industry norm. Two public data points disagree by more than 2x: !!Con pays $256 and ffconf pays £500. Gate any number on your own budget and legal review.
- **Blind review.** It doesn't apply to invited speakers, because their identity is why you invited them. Decide the review lane now: either confirm at invitation, or review on a named track against the same criteria.
