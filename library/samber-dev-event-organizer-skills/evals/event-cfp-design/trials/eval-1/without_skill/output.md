## What we're looking for

Talks about the real work of DevOps and platform engineering. Topics we'd love to see:

- Internal developer platforms: what you built, what you killed, what teams actually used
- Infrastructure as code, GitOps, and delivery pipelines at real-world scale
- Reliability, incident response, and postmortems with honest lessons
- Observability, cost control, and capacity decisions
- Security and supply-chain practices that engineers adopted willingly
- Team and organizational patterns: ownership, on-call, platform-as-product
- Migrations, rewrites, and failures, including what you'd do differently

Not on this list? Submit it anyway if it will help a room of engineers on Monday.

- Concrete experience over product tours
- Specific numbers, decisions, and trade-offs over general advice

**We can't accept:** vendor sales pitches, or talks whose main purpose is to promote a product. Talks about open-source or commercial tools are fine when they teach something transferable.
