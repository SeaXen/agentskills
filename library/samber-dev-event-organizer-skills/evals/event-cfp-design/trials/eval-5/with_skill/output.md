# Quarry Conf: honorarium answer and speaker perks section

## 1. Honorarium: there is no industry standard

I found no market rate, so I can't give you a number to be competitive against. The two named community events that publish a figure disagree by more than 2x:

| Event | Published honorarium | Other perks |
|---|---|---|
| !!Con | $256 (USD) flat, every speaker, unchanged 2018-2024 | n/a |
| ffconf (UK) | £500 flat | Paid travel, hotel and meals |
| SeaGL | Restricted to Black and Indigenous People of Color speakers, amount "not yet determined" | n/a |

Treat these as calibration points, not a benchmark.

**Recommendation: put no honorarium in this call.** Your only confirmed money is free tickets, and a published figure is a promise to strangers. If you want an honorarium policy later, set the amount against your own budget and get legal and tax review first. Paying speakers directly can raise tax, visa and conflict-of-interest or employment-agreement issues.

## 2. Don't write "travel covered for all speakers"

- **It isn't confirmed.** The €4,000 is pending board approval next month, and it won't be decided before the CFP goes out.
- **It can't be true as written.** A 300-person conference probably has 20-30 speakers. €4,000 is roughly €130-200 each, which is a pool, not full coverage.
- **A broken promise costs you more than a missing perk.** If the board cuts or delays the pool, you'd be walking back a public promise to speakers who have already planned around it.
