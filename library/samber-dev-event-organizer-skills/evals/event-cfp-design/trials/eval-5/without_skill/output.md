## 1. Honorarium: there is no industry standard

I can't give you a number I'd stand behind, and I did no research for this answer. From general knowledge:

- **Community conferences of your size** (a few hundred attendees, platform-engineering and DevOps style) mostly pay no honorarium. Speakers get a free ticket and some travel or hotel support.
- **Paid fees** are mostly for keynotes and invited speakers at commercial events. They are negotiated one by one and vary widely.
- **Your confirmed budget** is free tickets, plus a 4,000 euro pool that isn't approved yet. A fee in the call would be an unfunded commitment.

My recommendation: put no honorarium number in this CFP. If you want a competitive pitch, say what is true: free ticket, travel support, a 300-person audience of platform engineers. If you want a real number later, benchmark 3-5 comparable European community conferences. Check their published speaker pages, or ask their organizers directly.

## 2. "Travel covered for all speakers" is risky

The 4,000 euro pool isn't approved, and it may not cover everyone. With 20 speakers, it works out to 200 euros each. That wouldn't cover a flight plus a hotel from outside Europe. Speakers decide whether to submit, and whether to book non-refundable tickets, based on this sentence. Walking it back after acceptance costs you trust and possibly speakers.

Use conditional wording that is true today and stays true if the board says no.

### Draft perks section (ships this week)

> ## Speaker perks
>
> **Free conference ticket.** Every accepted speaker gets a complimentary Quarry Conf ticket.
>
> **Travel support.** We are setting aside a limited travel fund for speakers. It is subject to final approval and we will confirm the details before we send acceptances. Support will be partial or full depending on how many speakers need it and how far they travel. If travel cost would keep you from speaking, tell us in the submission form. Requesting support has no effect on whether your talk is accepted.
>
> **What we can't promise yet.** We can't guarantee that travel or accommodation will be covered for every speaker. We will tell you exactly what is available when we notify you of acceptance, before you commit to anything.

### Version for after the board approves

Fill in the brackets once the budget is final:

> **Travel support.** Accepted speakers can request travel support of up to [€X] each, reimbursed against receipts, from a fund of €4,000. Requests are handled [first-come / by need] and confirmed with your acceptance.

### Decisions for you

1. **Notification date.** Set acceptance notifications after the board meeting, so you know the real budget before you tell speakers anything.
2. **Speaker count.** How many speakers are you planning for? Divide 4,000 by that number, then decide whether to cap per person or fund by need.
3. **Selection fairness.** The form should ask about travel need separately from the talk review. That keeps funding from influencing acceptance.
