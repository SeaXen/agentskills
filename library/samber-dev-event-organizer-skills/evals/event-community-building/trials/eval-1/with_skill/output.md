## 1. Cadence posture: dormant-but-open, with a named owner

**Verdict on your draft:** it combines the two heaviest rungs. Weekly prompts are *continuous light*. A monthly live AMA plus new topic sub-channels is *continuous heavy*.

- **Continuous heavy** needs all three of these. You have none:
  - the Discord is the audience's primary home
  - a funded moderator roster exists
  - the team is compounding
- With nobody on duty, the skill treats continuous heavy as a conditional delete. An unmoderated standing space carries a conduct duty with no responder.
- **Occasional pulse** needs someone with recurring hours in the gap. Q8 says nobody has them.
- The skill's own negative example is a twelve-slot theme-per-month calendar written the week after an edition. It is abandoned by the fourth slot, and the abandonment is more visible than the silence.

**Chosen rung: dormant-but-open.** Its downside is that members who want activity won't get it. The upside is that it cannot fail publicly.

| Rung | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Dormant-but-open | ✅ Chosen | Fits Q8 and cannot fail publicly |
| Occasional pulse | Rejected for now | No named person with gap-month hours |
| Continuous light (weekly prompts) | Rejected | Someone must have something to say every week with no edition to talk about |
| Continuous heavy (AMAs, sub-channels) | Rejected | Fails all three promotion conditions |
| Close the server and reopen fresh | ❌ Never | Discards the member list and archive |

**Owner:** one named person owns the space through spring. Write the name in the dormancy notice. If nobody will put their name on it, the posture is dormant by decision, and the notice should say so.

**Sub-channels:**
- Don't create the new topic sub-channels.
- Keep any per-team or per-project channels from the edition.
- Leave existing channels readable and untouched, not folded into general.

## 2. What to post

Three items in October. That is the whole calendar.

**Post 1: thank-you with the archive (this week).**
- Link the talk recordings and the edition's resources.
- Sign it as a person, in your register.
- Make no promise about future activity.

**Post 2: pinned dormancy notice (same sitting).** Based on the skill's template:

> **This space is quiet until {spring build-up month, e.g. "PortoBuild 2027 planning opens"}.**
> The archive stays open and searchable. That is why we keep it here.
> Nobody is moderating day to day right now. {Name} owns the space and reads reports at {reporting route that actually works}.
> You'll hear from us here when PortoBuild {year} opens.
> Posted {date}.

It needs four things: a date, a named owner, a conduct route that works, and a promise small enough to keep. Avoid "we'll be back soon with exciting stuff 🎉". It names no date, no owner and no conduct route, and a member reading it in month five concludes the event is over.

**Post 3: the handoff document and an ownership post (see § 6).**

**Optional, only if one person volunteers and can hold it:**

- One spotlight on a member's work.
- Source material must already exist. Skip this if it needs original work.
- If it lapses, record the missed month. Two missed cycles means demote, and that is a signal, not a discipline problem.

## 4. Conversion: one re-engagement touchpoint in spring

Chosen rung: **single re-engagement touchpoint**, timed to the next edition's opening, not to the calendar.

- It reaches all 900 attendees for an afternoon, which matches your capacity.
- It names something that happened at the fourth edition, not something coming.
- It asks for one thing. A talk idea, "are you coming?" or a topic request is easier to answer than a ticket sale.
- Sign it as a person.
- It must still work if the reader ignores it.
- ❌ Avoid "Tickets live, early bird ends Friday!". It converts the people already coming and teaches the rest that you only appear when you want money.

Rejected for now:
- **Participation ladder.** Every rung must be climbable asynchronously in the gap, and that needs hours you don't have. Revisit if you get a named owner with gap-month time.
- **Leaderboards and badges.** They measure posting rather than returning, reward the loudest members, and need upkeep in months nobody has hours.

Your 1,400 members versus 900 attendees:
- About 500 members are probably non-attendees, volunteers, speakers or sponsors.
- ❓ Confirm what each group was told when they joined (see § 5).
- Past volunteers belong to `event-volunteer-experience`. Their thank-you and alumni loop aren't built here.

## 6. Handoff, written now

Chosen rung: **documented ownership and access transfer**. It is one written page, about an afternoon, and it fixes the failure that orphans most spaces: the server registered to one volunteer's personal account.

Fill this in together, on screen, while the team is still reachable:

| Asset | Holder today | Holder after |
|---|---|---|
| Discord server (registered to whose account?) | ❓ | |
| Admin and mod roles (at least two, one not the current owner) | ❓ | |
| Billing, boosts or bots, if any | ❓ | |
| Vanity invite or custom domain | ❓ | |
| Standing invite links | ❓ | |
| Member list export, with consent basis | ❓ | |
| Archive and recordings (reachable without Discord?) | ❓ | |
| Conduct reporting route | ❓ | |

Order of operations:
1. Move ownership of record first. An admin who isn't the owner can be removed by the owner who left.
2. Add a second admin who is neither of you.
3. Export the list and note where it lives.
4. Restate the retention decision, dated.
5. Check the conduct route reaches someone who reads it.
6. Post that the owner has changed (if it has).
7. Date the document and set a review date. If the team may turn over before spring, the "named successor with overlap" rung applies instead.

## 7. Measures (all self-set, none is an industry benchmark)

Fix these before the gap starts:

| Measure | Definition | Target (yours to set) |
|---|---|---|
| **Carry rate** | Share of fourth-edition attendees who register for the fifth | Set a number before spring; hand to `event-market-fit`. Industry-blended retention is "barely above 30%" (Freeman, 2025), but that blends all event types, so it isn't a target |
| **Cadence kept** (gate) | Posts planned vs made, on the chosen posture | 3 of 3 by end of October; any later pulse slots count too |
| **List integrity** (gate) | Owner and written retention decision exist | By the day the gap starts |
| **Handoff readiness** (gate) | Access document exists, dated, names a living holder per account | Re-check on a schedule (e.g. at the start of spring planning), not when someone resigns |

The count of messages, members or reactions is not a measure here. A noisier Discord isn't a returning audience.
