- **Chapter-lead application form, brand usage guidelines, quality standards for local events: yes.** Lisbon, Krakow and Zagreb would run "GridForge Lisbon" editions under your banner. That is brand licensing: recruiting, vetting, standards and enforcement for teams you don't run.

- **What to do with that half.**
  1. Take the brand-licensing decision to `event-portfolio-strategy`, and to your own counsel for the trademark and licence questions.
  2. Don't promise the three groups "GridForge Lisbon" events yet.
  3. Don't treat it as "free growth". Whether this scales the event is `event-growth-strategy`'s call, not mine.

The engagement half is mine, and it follows below. Keeping local groups warm in your Discord doesn't need anyone to run an event under your name.

## Section 2: Conversion mechanism (for approval)

Scope: non-volunteer members only. Past volunteers belong to `event-volunteer-experience`, which runs as a segment inside this Discord, not a second space.

1. **Single re-engagement touchpoint** (default). One message to last summit's attendees, timed to next edition's opening. It names something that happened, asks for one thing (a talk topic, or whether they're coming), is signed by a person, and works if ignored.
2. **Fed local cohort**, promoted because Q5 is multi-city and Q10 names willing locals. This is where Lisbon, Krakow and Zagreb fit.
3. **Participation ladder** (optional later): attendee, regular, contributor, then flagged to `event-team-structure`. Every rung must be climbable asynchronously. Ask consent before naming anyone publicly.
4. **Deleted, never demoted:** auto-enrolling every registrant into the Discord. It spends a ticketing consent basis on a purpose it never granted.

## Section 3: Local posture, with the line drawn (for approval)

**Yours: a fed local cohort on your Discord**
- One per-city thread or sub-space: `#lisbon`, `#krakow`, `#zagreb`.
- One local "keeper" per city who keeps the thread warm and has no admin rights.

**Not yours, so stop here**

- A thread that starts needing a venue, a date or a speaker roster. That is an event property, so route it to `event-portfolio-strategy`.
- Anyone using the banner, logo or "GridForge Lisbon" naming.

**What to tell the three groups now.** Yes to a city thread and a warm local home inside the GridForge Discord. Not yet to events under the banner. That decision is pending a governance answer you don't have.

**Informal meetups.** Members may still meet up on their own. Post a one-line norm in each city thread that independent meetups aren't GridForge events and can't use the name until a licensing decision exists. That is an engagement-space rule, not a brand policy.
