# RustReef community KPI sheet (draft for board review)

I can't finalize it as proposed. Items (a), (b) and (c) each break the event-community-building rules, and I've rewritten them below. Four inputs are still open (listed at the end), and they change the numbers.

## What changed from the draft

| Draft item | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (a) DAU and monthly messages as headline | ❌ Demoted | They measure the Slack space, not the edition. A noisier space isn't a returning audience. |
| (b) 45% return target, justified by beating a ~30% industry rate | ⚠️ Keep the metric, change the basis | The ~30% figure is a blended retention rate across all event types, and none of them is a technical conference. It shows return rates run lower than assumed. It is not a target to beat. A 45% target is acceptable only as a self-set number, fixed before the gap, with the baseline stated. |
| (c) "5/12/45 thresholds, 50 of 3,100 talking = failing, activation push" | ❌ Withdrawn | The numbers are misquoted, the thresholds don't apply to this case, and the conclusion doesn't follow. Details below. |

## Why (c) fails

- **Misquote.** Christopher Allen's thresholds (Life With Alacrity, 2009) are 7, 13, 50, 90, 150. "5/12/45" is a garbled version.
- **Wrong object.** The thresholds describe groups with ongoing interaction. A 3,100-person member list is a stock, not a group. About 50 active talkers out of 3,100 is a normal shape for an event's space, not a failure against any threshold.
- **Use them only to set expectations** about how many members talk to each other if you run a continuous cadence. Never use them as a target membership number or a benchmark.
- **No activation push yet.** An activation push is a "continuous" cadence. It needs a named owner with recurring hours in the quiet months, and ideally a funded moderator. Without those it lapses by month three, and the lapse is more visible than the silence it replaced.

## Proposed KPI sheet

**Headline (the only number that answers the board's question)**

| KPI | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Carry rate | Share of one edition's attendees who register for the next. Denominator: 2,800 attendees. | Self-set, proposed 45%, to be confirmed against the baseline below. |

How to present the 45%:
- Label it "self-set". Do not present it as beating an industry benchmark.
- Cite the ~30% blended figure as context only (Freeman, *Trade Show Executive*, 2025).
- Fix the target before the gap starts, and never move it after the data arrives.

**Gates (binary, all three must pass)**

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Cadence kept | Posts planned versus posts made, on the chosen posture. Two missed cycles is a signal to move down a rung, not a discipline problem. |
| List integrity | The member list has a named owner and a written retention decision on the day the gap starts. Retention period and jurisdiction go to counsel. |
| Handoff readiness | A dated access document names a living holder for every account, admin role, invite link and list. Check it on a schedule, not at resignation. |

**Secondary (report as counts, not rates)**
- Ladder movement: how many members moved a step, only if you run a participation ladder. The denominator is meaningless at these sizes.
- Space activity (DAU, messages): demoted to a labeled trend line. It carries no target and isn't in the board headline.

## Cadence recommendation

Default to **dormant-but-open**, with one dated notice that names the owner. Move to **occasional pulse** (a digest or spotlight) only if someone has recurring gap-month hours. "Continuous" needs Q8 answered first, and it is not the right response to 50 talkers.

Also run **one re-engagement touchpoint** to last edition's attendees, timed to next edition's opening. It should name something that happened, ask for one thing, and be signed by a person.

## Open before this is final

1. **Baseline.** What was the actual carry rate from the last edition to this one? Without it, 45% is a guess. How many editions has RustReef run, and is it annual?
2. **Owner.** Who owns the Slack, whose account is it registered to, and who has recurring hours in the quiet months? Is anyone funded to moderate?
3. **Overlap.** How many of the 3,100 members attended an edition, and what was the consent basis for the list?
4. **Hard dates.** Is there a next-edition announcement date, or an organizer departure, that sets the live-by date?

Once I have those, I'll set the baseline, confirm or revise the 45%, and name the owner and the cadence rung.
