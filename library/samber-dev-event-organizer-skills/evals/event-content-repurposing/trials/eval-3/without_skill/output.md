## 1. Before you produce anything

1. **Check speaker consent.** Confirm the recording releases cover re-use on the company brand channel and LinkedIn, not just the event channel. If any talk lacks that, hold it back.
2. **Check the bumper.** An intro bumper on a speaker's talk implies company endorsement. Get a yes from any speaker whose employer is sensitive about branding.
3. **Check the LinkedIn length limit.** Native video has a maximum duration. Confirm it for your account, because 30-40 minute talks may not fit. Fall back to a 2-5 minute cut plus a link to the full talk on YouTube.
4. **Keep one canonical home.** Post the full talks on the brand YouTube channel as a playlist. Use LinkedIn for clips and short cuts, with the YouTube link in the first comment, since LinkedIn deprioritises posts that carry external links. That gives you the reach you want without 12 near-duplicate long videos competing with each other.
