  - A machine transcript, then a human correction pass, then translation, then a Portuguese review.

## 6. Rights gate per clip (run before editing starts)

Every planned clip gets this check first.

1. Session is Usable, or Restricted with its condition honoured.
2. Frames and audio contain no:
   - Music.
   - Another company's logo, screenshot or interface in a demo.
   - Licensed images or other people's charts.
   - Non-speaker faces.
3. **Customer data beyond slide 14.** Look at all slides, demos and terminals in every cut. If a clip can't be cleared, drop it; there is no partial version.
4. **Attendee faces.** Vertical crops zoom in, so people who were incidental in the wide shot become identifiable. Check the badge opt-out. If it isn't retrievable per person, use only the speaker, slide or screen framing.
5. **Workshop recordings.** Even after the hosts sign, check participants' faces and screens.
