## Why "fair use" and "YouTube just mutes it" don't clear them

- **Fair use is a defense, not a clearance.** It is decided case by case, after a dispute starts. The nonprofit status of the conference doesn't settle it. The editor can't give you that answer. This skill's checklist isn't legal advice either. If you want a real answer, ask a lawyer.
- **"Worst case it mutes" understates the risk.**
  - Platform action isn't capped at a mute. The DevOpsDays guide warns that background music can get a stream taken down.
  - Clip A's value is the audio and the demo, so a mute wouldn't save it.
  - The downside lands on the channel and on the speaker whose name is on the clip.
- **The skill's rule is: "Do not publish and wait to see."** The three allowed actions are clear it, cut those frames, or drop the clip. There is no partial version.
- **The day of editing is sunk.** The gate was meant to run before production. Don't let the sunk cost decide, because a platform complaint or a speaker takedown request costs more than the day did.

## Licence line

- Publish none of the three under an open licence.
- A licence on a clip carrying third-party material would license someone else's work along with yours.
- Say plainly on the artifact that no open licence applies.
