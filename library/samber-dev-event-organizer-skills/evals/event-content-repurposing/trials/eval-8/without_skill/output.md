# PortAndAdapter Conf outreach drafts

I changed four of your five instructions. Each change is below, with the reason.

## What I changed

| # | Your plan | What I did | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One email to all 30 speakers, with their clip | 3 variants: 4 clips reviewed, 2 clips unreviewed, 24 speakers without a clip | Only 6 of 30 speakers have a clip. |
| 2 | Tag the keynote speaker on the event post | Tag her, plus a short personal note with a ready-to-post text | A tag alone is easy to miss, and a notification from a big account is easy to ignore. A personal note is cheap. |
| 3 | Sponsor report: 10k views per clip, 50% of speakers sharing | Baseline-first wording, with targets set after day 7 | The numbers have no baseline. A missed target in a sponsor report costs more than a modest one. |
| 4 | Send the two unreviewed clips with the share ask | Send a "review first" email: approve or request changes, and share only after that | A speaker who finds an edit they dislike after the ask has gone out may refuse to share. They may also ask for a takedown. |
| 5 | Keep the 20-second workshop teaser live | **Take it down until the host signs the release**, and leave it out of every email | Length doesn't matter without a release. It exposes you to a takedown or a rights claim, and the sponsor report would then count views on an unlicensed clip. |

## Sponsor report: wording

> **Clip performance.** Six clips went live on {date}. We will report views, watch time and shares per clip after 7 days and after 30 days. Targets for the next event will be set from these baselines.
>
> **Speaker amplification.** We asked all 30 speakers to share. We will report the share rate, counted from tracked links ({UTM scheme}), against a goal of {N} speakers. We will set N after the first week.

**Why I left out 10,000 views and 50%:**
- 50% of 30 speakers is 15 people. For comparison, in the first week you can expect a handful of shares from this kind of ask.
- Short conference clips often fall well below 10,000 views per clip unless they are paid or pushed by a large account.
- You can add hard targets in the next report, once you've measured these.

If you want targets in this report anyway, I'd put in a range for views and "at least 8 of 30 speakers" for sharing. Tell me and I'll rewrite the paragraph.
