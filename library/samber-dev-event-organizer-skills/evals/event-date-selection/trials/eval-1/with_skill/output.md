## 1. Day type: weekday, not Saturday–Sunday

- **Your audience:** working SREs and platform engineers, so Q1 is "employer-time".
- **The rule:** the DevOpsDays organizing guide recommends weekdays for this audience. Its reason is that "DevOps is part of people's work life, and the weekends are typically used for refreshing energy with family."
- **Your reasoning is inverted.** "So attendees don't have to take days off work" assumes the event is a personal-time ask. For this audience a weekend competes with family, not with the office. Two days of cross-border weekend travel is a bigger ask than two days off.
- **The other pole:** the MLH weekend rule is for students and hobbyists, who are not your audience.
- **Do not compromise on Friday.** A Friday-plus-Saturday split takes the worst half of each rule.
- **Recommendation:** run Tuesday–Wednesday. That keeps both days off the Monday and Friday boundaries.
- **Limits of the rule:** both guides are community-run, and neither gives the size of the penalty for a wrong day type. I'm not putting a number on it.
- **One exception:** if your last edition's survey or attendee conversations show people wanting a weekend, that evidence beats the guide. Test it with two or three prospective attendees before overriding.
