## Why not the second weekend of May

In 2027, May 1 is a Saturday, so the second weekend is **May 8–9**. That weekend has these problems:

| Date | Issue | Hits |
|---|---|---|
| Thu May 6 | Ascension Day, a public holiday | NL, DE |
| Fri May 7 | Bridge day. Many people take it off and travel. | NL, DE |
| Sun May 9 | Mother's Day (second Sunday of May) | NL, DE |

- **Ascension long weekend:** May 8–9 is the tail of the Ascension long weekend. Two thirds of your audience (NL and DE) will have family and holiday plans. Travel and Rotterdam hotels will cost more on May 7 and 8.
- **Mother's Day:** asking people to be away on that Sunday costs you ticket sales.
- **Poland:** Ascension isn't a Polish holiday, so Poland is unaffected. That's still only a third of attendees.

## Is Saturday–Sunday a good idea?

I'm not convinced yet. Your stated reason is that attendees won't need days off work.

- **Employer-funded attendance:** for working SREs and platform engineers, employers usually pay for conferences and count them as work time. Weekdays are the norm for that reason. A weekend can turn a work trip into a personal expense and a lost weekend.
- **Cross-border travel:** a third of attendees come from Poland and a third from Germany. That makes it worse, because they give up Friday evening or Monday travel too.
- **Sponsors and speakers:** they usually prefer weekdays. Their staff, booth teams and speakers also lose a weekend.
- **Slow sell-out:** that points more to price, awareness or timing than to weekday versus weekend. A weekend won't fix it by itself.
- **On-call:** weekend on-call rotations may keep some SREs from attending.

Weekends do have benefits: cheaper venue rates in some cases, and a signal that this is a community event. Make the choice with data, not with the days-off argument alone.

## Checks before you announce (all needed)

1. **Last year's buyer data:** who paid for the ticket, the company or the individual? What share said they needed PTO? This is your strongest evidence for or against a weekend.
2. **Venue:** availability on May 22–23 in writing, ideally with a signed hold. Also check venue weekend pricing.
3. **Rotterdam calendar:** other large events that weekend, and hotel availability.
4. **Competing events:** DevOpsDays, KubeCon, and regional platform and SRE conferences in NL, DE and PL around mid-to-late May 2027.
5. **Eurovision final:** it is normally a Saturday in mid-May. Check the 2027 date.
6. **Speakers and sponsors:** a quick pulse from 5 to 10 of your likely anchors.
