**You need about 2–3 hours of date research, not 3–4 days.**

The dates are fixed, so last year's due diligence doesn't apply. Last year's work existed to *choose* a date. This year there is nothing to choose: the foundation gave you one co-location slot, it's confirmed, and it can't move. Holiday maps and week-scoring are inputs to a decision you no longer get to make. Research is only worth doing where it can change what you do, and the date is not one of those places.

## What to do instead

1. **Confirm the dates in writing (10 min).** Get the exact dates from the foundation. Check that "confirmed" means a signed or written allocation, not a verbal yes. Confirm the venue and whether your two days are in the main venue or off-site.
2. **Check holidays on only those dates (45–60 min).** Cover the two event days plus one travel day on each side. For each of the 12 countries, include national holidays, major religious observances, and school holidays if your contributors have children. Count how many contributors each clash affects, and who they are. A single sheet with one row per country is enough.
3. **Check Amsterdam for the same window (30 min).** Look for other large events, hotel availability and price spikes, and any transport strikes or planned disruption. A tight hotel market is the one item that could force action sooner. Book contributor rooms early.
4. **Check visa and travel lead times (30 min).** For contributors from visa-required countries, this is the item with a real deadline. Start it first, because it can fail even when the date is fine.
5. **Plan mitigations for affected people (30 min).** For each clash, pick one option:
   - a remote or hybrid participation path
   - scheduling that person's key sessions on the unaffected day
   - recorded sessions or async notes
   - a travel stipend, if the clash makes travel costly

## When to do more research

Do the bigger pass only if the quick check shows one of these:

- A **quorum-critical group** (for example, the people who must be present for governance votes or release decisions) is blocked on a clash.
- The dates fall on a **major holiday in a country that holds a large share** of your contributors.
- **Accommodation or visa constraints** can't be met for the dates.

Even then, the output is "what do we do about it," not "which other week." The one exception is if the foundation says it would swap slots. In that case, ask them what alternatives they can offer, and research only those.
