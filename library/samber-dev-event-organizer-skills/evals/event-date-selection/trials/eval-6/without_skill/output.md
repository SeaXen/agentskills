# RustAtlantic 4: postpone or hold?

**Recommendation:** Don't postpone yet. Run a 2-week recovery sprint with a go/no-go gate. Postpone only if the gate fails.

I don't know the event date, venue contract or sponsor terms, so the gate below is relative. Plug in real dates.

## Why not postpone now

- ❌ **It doesn't fix the problems.** The keynote, CFP and workshop gaps are fixable in 2–4 weeks. Six extra weeks only helps if you work differently during them.
- ❌ **"Most would be fine" is a guess.** Of the 180 buyers, some have booked flights or hotels, and they are the ones who complain publicly. Ask a sample of about 10 before deciding.
- ❌ **The cost is hidden.** A move forces a new venue and hotel block, sponsor consent, new speaker availability, and a new Halifax weather and flight picture. It may also collide with other Rust events (EuroRust, RustConf and similar).
- ❌ **It looks like a troubled event.** A second date announcement is also a bigger ask of buyers and sponsors than the first.
- ⚠️ **180 of 900 isn't a crisis yet.** Conference sales are back-loaded and follow the lineup announcement. Compare to editions 1–3 at the same point in the curve. If you're on pace, the real problem is the program, not demand.
- ⚠️ **900 may be the wrong target.** If past editions never hit it, cap at 600–700 and cut venue cost.

## Recovery sprint (2 weeks)

1. **Keynote.** First ask the speaker why they pulled out. A date conflict is a signal; personal reasons aren't.
   - Ask them for referrals, or a recorded or remote slot.
   - Invite 5 names at once: Rust project members, regional or Canadian maintainers, and company engineers with a real story.
   - Fallback: a keynote panel, or a "state of Rust" talk from the best CFP submitter.
2. **CFP.**
   - Extend the close date by 2–3 weeks, but publish a hard review and notification date.
   - Invite 10–15 specific people directly.
   - Push the call through This Week in Rust, Rust community Zulip and Discord, local user groups, and your past speakers.
   - Offer travel support or a free ticket.
   - Speakers need 6–8 weeks to arrange travel, so count back from the event date.
3. **Workshops.** Recruit them; don't wait for submissions.
   - Approach 4–5 trainers directly (Rust consultancies, training firms, past speakers).
   - Offer a fixed fee or revenue share, sold as a paid add-on ticket.
   - Two confirmed workshops beat a full track that never fills.
4. **Sales.** Announce the lineup in waves, starting with the confirmed keynote and the first 5 speakers. Each announcement is a sales event.

## Gate (hold on a date about 2 weeks out, set now)

Postpone if 2 of 3 fail:

- ☐ Keynote confirmed, or a fallback format committed.
- ☐ CFP closed with at least 3× submissions per slot.
- ☐ At least 2 workshop leads signed.

Also check these before deciding:

- Venue and hotel contract: penalties, and whether the dates are movable.
- Sponsor commitments and what they say if asked.
- Whether confirmed speakers can attend the new date.
- Whether the event is under about 8 weeks away. If so, the speaker-travel lead time tips toward postponing.

## Middle options

- Move by 2 weeks instead of 6.
- Hold the main date and run workshops a few weeks later as a standalone.
- Cut to one track this year.

## If you do postpone

- Decide once, announce once, with a firm new date.
- Say the real reason: the program wasn't ready and you'd rather do it properly.
- Offer a no-questions refund, or a free transfer to the new date.
- Tell sponsors and speakers before the public announcement.
- Send the email within days of the decision. Delay costs trust.
