## 4. Run of show: 10-minute transitions (rule done, rewrite needs the file)

Two things to settle before you apply 10 minutes everywhere:

1. **"25 minutes long all afternoon" is ambiguous.** It could be a constant offset or a growing one.
   - **Constant offset:** one slip, probably at or after lunch, carried through the afternoon. A flat 10 minutes on every transition won't fix the cause.
   - **Growing offset:** the transitions are slipping every time. A 10-minute buffer is the right fix.
   - Check actual start times against the run of show for each Track A session.
2. **Day length arithmetic.** The day extends by n × (10 − current transition length) for n transitions.
