# HackTheHarbor feedback plan: sanity check and survey intro

## Verdict

The plan is mostly sound. Three parts need changes before launch: the anonymity claim, rating judges by name, and the quote publishing.

## Issues to fix

1. **"Fully anonymous" is not true as designed.** ❌
   - **Draw vs. anonymity:** The iPad draw needs an email address. Collect it in a separate second form after submission, with no link to the answers. Say so in the intro.
   - **Mentor rating vs. anonymity:** With 90 hackers, each mentor probably has only a handful of mentees. "My mentor" plus a free-text comment identifies the respondent.
   - **Better claim:** Call it "confidential, unlinked to your identity". Only report mentor ratings when at least 5 responses exist for that mentor. Otherwise pool them.

2. **Fairness ratings by judge name are risky.** ⚠️
   - **Bias:** Teams that lost rate "fairness" lower. Judges also saw different teams, so the scores aren't comparable.
   - **Volunteer risk:** Six named people get scored by a few dozen students. Free-text comments next to a name invite harsh or personal remarks.
   - **Better option:** Ask about the judging process: clarity of criteria, quality of feedback, transparency. Add an optional per-judge question only for judges who evaluated that hacker's team. Share per-judge results with the judge privately, never publicly.

3. **Quotes need consent at collection time.** ⚠️
   - People answer differently when told "anonymous" and later see their words in a blog.
   - Add an opt-in checkbox: "We may quote my answers anonymously."
   - Only quote opted-in answers.
   - Remove team names, project details and any named individual.
   - Never publish criticism of a named judge or mentor.

4. **The iPad draw is fine, with housekeeping.** ℹ️
   - **Rules:** Publish a short rule text covering eligibility, deadline, how the winner is picked and the contact method.
   - **Data:** Delete the email list after the draw. If you are in France, check the local rules for prize draws, and check whether any participants are minors.
   - **Bias:** Entry for completing, not for positive answers, is the right design. Say "any answer counts".

5. **Missing items worth adding.**
   - **Timing:** Open it Sunday while everyone is still in the room, and close it after 7 days. Send one reminder on day 3.
   - **Length:** Keep it to about 5 minutes. Make it mobile-first.
   - **Conduct:** Add a channel for code-of-conduct issues. An anonymous form can't follow up, so give a named contact.
   - **Other content:** Ask about logistics (food, venue, Wi-Fi, schedule), whether they'd come back (a 0–10 score), and one open question: "What should we change?"
   - **Mentors:** Rate mentors on helpfulness and availability, plus one open comment.

## Suggested survey structure

1. **Overall experience:** satisfaction (1–5) and likelihood to return or recommend (0–10).
2. **Mentoring:** helpfulness, availability, and one open comment.
3. **Judging process:** clear criteria, useful feedback, and transparency.
4. **Optional per-judge rating:** only for judges who evaluated their team.
5. **Logistics:** food, venue, Wi-Fi, schedule.
6. **Open questions:** "What should we keep?" and "What should we change?"
7. **Consent checkbox** for anonymous quoting.
8. **Redirect** to the separate draw-entry form.

## Intro text (copy-paste)

> **HackTheHarbor feedback: 5 minutes, one iPad to win**
>
> Thank you for building with us this weekend. Your feedback decides what we keep, fix and drop next year.
>
> **How your answers are handled**
> - We don't ask for your name or email in this survey.
> - Mentor and judge ratings are only reported in groups of 5 or more responses. Individual comments are read by the organizing team only.
> - Please don't include names or details that identify you or your team in free-text answers. We can't remove what we can't see.
>
> **The iPad draw**
> - Finish the survey and you get a link to a separate entry form. It collects only your email, and it isn't connected to your answers.
> - Every completed survey counts, whatever you answered. Honest and critical answers are welcome.
> - One entry per person. The draw takes place on [date]. Rules: [link]. We delete all emails after the draw.
>
> **Quotes**
> - At the end, you can allow us to quote parts of your answers anonymously in our recap blog post. If you don't tick the box, we won't quote you.
>
> **Need to report something?**
> Contact [name/email] directly for any code-of-conduct concern. This form can't follow up with you.
>
> The survey closes on [date]. Thank you!
