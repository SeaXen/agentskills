# ShipItCon post-event survey: polished and cut to 12 questions

I didn't polish all 32 questions. More questions rarely gives more insight. Completion drops with length, and answers get sloppier as people tire. With 220 attendees and a typical 25-40% response rate, you'll get about 55-90 responses, and every extra question thins the data further. I only saw 10 of the 32, so I can't edit the other 21. Test each of those against the rule at the end.

## Problems in the draft

- **Q1 is leading.** "Our amazing speaker lineup" pushes people toward yes, and a yes/no answer tells you little.
- **Q4 and Q7 duplicate each other.** Both ask about the venue.
- **Q2-Q5 are four separate 1-10 scales.** Ten points is too fine-grained, and satisfaction and content scores will correlate heavily. A 5-point grid is faster to answer and just as informative.
- **Q10 has no decision behind it.** Unless you plan to post on those networks, drop it.
- **Q9 is free text.** Fixed categories can be counted and compared.
- **No question asks which talks worked.** For a single-track event that is your most useful data.

## The survey

**Intro text:** Thanks for joining us at ShipItCon yesterday. This takes about 2 minutes, and your answers shape next year's event.

**1. How likely are you to recommend ShipItCon to a colleague?**
0 (not at all likely) to 10 (extremely likely)

**2. How would you rate each of the following?**
Scale: 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent), plus "Didn't use / N/A"
- The talks overall
- The venue
- The catering
- The schedule and breaks
- Event organization and communication

**3. Which talks were most valuable to you?**
Select up to 3 from the talk list.

**4. Which talks would you not want to see again?** *(optional)*
Select any from the talk list.

**5. What was the best part of ShipItCon?** *(open text)*

**6. What is the one thing we should change next year?** *(open text)*

**7. Would you attend a paid workshop day next year?**
Definitely / Probably / Not sure / Probably not / No

**8. If you answered Definitely, Probably, or Not sure, which price would feel fair for a full day?**
Under €150 / €150-250 / €250-400 / Over €400

**9. How did you hear about ShipItCon?**
Colleague or friend / Social media / Newsletter or email / Search / Speaker or sponsor / Previous attendee / Other: ___

**10. Which best describes your role?**
Software engineer / Engineering manager / Architect or staff+ / Founder or CTO / Other: ___

**11. Anything else you'd like to tell us?** *(optional)*

**12. May we contact you with follow-up questions?** *(optional)*
Email: ___

## Fielding notes

- Send it today, remind non-responders on day 3, and close it on day 7.
- Q8 is only useful if you decide a price threshold in advance, for example "run the workshop if at least 40% say Probably or better".
- Q3 and Q4 depend on a pre-filled talk list. Use the real schedule.

## Rule for the other 21 questions

Keep a question only if you can finish the sentence "If most people answer X, we will do Y." If you can't, cut it. Send me the remaining 21 and I'll apply that rule to each.
