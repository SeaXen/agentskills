# DataStack Summit, 2nd edition: board update

## Blended score: 7.1/10

I converted each metric to a 0–10 scale and weighted the three equally.

| Metric | Raw | Out of 10 |
|---|---|---|
| Overall satisfaction | 4.1 / 5 | 8.2 |
| Likely or very likely to return | 58% | 5.8 |
| Operations rating | 3.6 / 5 | 7.2 |
| **Blend (equal weights)** | | **7.1** |

With weights of 50% satisfaction, 30% return and 20% operations, the blend is 7.3. The weights are a judgment call and move the score by only 0.2. Your "8.2" is satisfaction alone, so a blend cannot reach it. The 58% return figure pulls the score down.

## Caveats

1. **The blend has no natural unit.** It mixes two 5-point ratings with a percentage, so the 7.1 depends on my choice of equal weights. I'd show the three components next to it on the slide, as drafted.
2. **Survey bias is probably upward.** 210 of 700 is a 30% response rate, and the 650 or so attendees who didn't respond likely skew less satisfied. The 58% is stated intent among respondents. It is about 122 people, or roughly 14% of the 850 attendees. I'd label it "stated intent".
3. **Moving sponsors to the appendix hides the signal most tied to revenue.** One sponsor lost out of two answers is a small sample, but the board will care about it. I kept the sponsor line in the Slide 1 bullets. I'd keep it there even if you drop everything else on sponsors. If you want it fully in the appendix, delete the last bullet on Slide 1.
