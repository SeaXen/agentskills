# PlatformCon Oslo feedback plan: what I'd change

**Main change:** collect the feedback on the day, in the room, instead of by email on Monday. Your 350 attendees are all in one place until the 5pm closing keynote. After that they scatter, and a Monday email reaches them four days later, when they remember little and have moved on.

## What to change

1. **Move the main survey into the closing slot.**
   - Put a 3-minute "fill it in now" block just before the prize ceremony, with a QR code on the big screen. The room is full and people are waiting for the prizes.
   - Keep it to 5 questions or fewer:
     - overall rating, 0–10
     - likelihood to recommend, 0–10
     - best session
     - one thing to fix
     - one free-text box
   - It should take under 2 minutes on a phone.
   - I can't promise a rate, but captive in-room collection usually does far better than a follow-up email. Track it so you know.

2. **Draw the raffle live at the ceremony, and only from people who completed the survey.**
   - Doing this makes the raffle a reason to answer now. It doubles as a hook for the ceremony.
   - Consider several smaller prizes instead of one $75 voucher. More winners makes the draw feel more winnable.
   - Keep the contact details used for the draw in a separate form from the answers. Responses stay anonymous, which gets you more honest feedback and keeps GDPR simple.
   - Check Norwegian rules on prize draws with your venue or legal contact before you announce it. I'm not certain of the details.

3. **Keep the email, but move it earlier.**
   - Send it Thursday evening or Friday morning, not Monday.
   - Send one reminder 3 days later, only to people who haven't responded.
   - Use it for the longer questions (talk topics for next year, logistics, networking).
   - Shorten it to match the in-room survey. Don't send the same form twice.

4. **Use the two volunteers for in-person capture, not for email chasing.**
   - Volunteer 1 stands at the main exit after the ceremony with QR cards or a tablet. They catch anyone who skipped the survey, with a one-line ask: "30 seconds, 2 questions."
   - Volunteer 2 collects session-level ratings. Put a QR code on a card at each room door, or have them ask a few people at each break "best and worst session today?"
   - If time allows, volunteer 2 also does 10–15 short hallway conversations at lunch and breaks. Aim for a mix of roles and company sizes. These give you quotes you can't get from a form.

5. **Add 2–3 live pulse checks earlier in the day.**
   - Run a one-question poll before lunch and one mid-afternoon, on screen or via QR, for example "Which track should get a bigger room next year?"
   - Responses are instant, and they get people used to answering before the main survey.

## What to watch out for

- **Raffle bias:** a prize pulls in people who want the prize. Compare the quality of free-text answers between raffle respondents and others. Don't read the response rate alone.
- **Day-of positivity:** people tend to rate higher while the event is still going. Treat the in-room scores as the "warm" number and keep the next-day email as your "cooler" check. Report the two separately.
- **Don't run the survey after the keynote ends.** People leave at 5:00. Do it before the ceremony.
- **Wi-Fi:** 350 phones on venue Wi-Fi may choke. Ask the venue to check capacity, and put a short URL next to the QR code.

## Timeline (3 weeks out)

1. **This week:** write the 5-question survey and pick the tool. Decide the prize structure and check the Norwegian rules on draws.
2. **Next week:** build the QR assets (screen slide, door cards, exit cards). Book the 3-minute slot with the keynote speaker and the ceremony host.
3. **Event week:** run a test on a few phones. Brief both volunteers on their roles.
4. **Day of:** run the pulse checks, the closing survey block, and the live draw.
5. **Friday morning:** send the short email. Send the reminder 3 days later.

## Decisions for you

- How many prizes, and at what value?
- Which survey tool are you using? Do you want the draw form built in or separate?
- Can the keynote or ceremony host give up the 3-minute slot?
