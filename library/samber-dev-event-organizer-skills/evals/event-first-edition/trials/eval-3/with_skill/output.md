## Why the team gate matters here

- **Neutrality.** You said this is not a Hexlify event. With a Hexlify DevRel lead and a Hexlify colleague as the only organizers, attendees, sponsors and speakers will read it as one. For a community-run event, cross-organization neutrality is the credibility source.
- **Demand check.** The skill treats a lead who can't recruit two outside co-organizers as having failed an early market test. "Nobody committed yet" is that signal. It may be only a timing issue, but you should find out before you sign.
- **Order of operations.** "Recruit after the venue is locked" is backwards. The venue is the commitment you can't easily undo, and the team gate sits before it for that reason.

## What to do before Monday

1. **Ask the venue for a hold.** Ask for a 2–3 week option on the date, or a contract with a refundable deposit and cancellation terms. Most venues will agree when you have 180 paid tickets behind you. The venue-contract-falls-through risk is the first Tiger in the skill's pre-mortem.
2. **Recruit 1–2 outside organizers this week.** You don't need a full team, only a real commitment from people at other companies. Good sources are the meetup's most active members, speakers from past meetups, and other local community leads. Verbal "yes, I'm a co-organizer" is enough to sign on Monday with the gate met.
3. **Fix the admin items before any public announcement.**
   - Publish a code of conduct.
   - Set up a neutral alias such as `hello@edgeconf…`, not a Hexlify address.
   - Set up a shared credential vault.
   - Don't put Hexlify's name or logo on the event.
   - Say openly that you both work at Hexlify.
4. **Run the pre-mortem before signing.** The likely launch-blockers:
   - **Venue contract.** Cancellation terms and the backup venue.
   - **Legal entity.** Who signs the contract, and who holds the 180 payments and refund liability? Not Hexlify, and ideally not either of you personally.
   - **Bandwidth.** Whether two people with day jobs can really carry this. Each of you should state weekly hours out loud.

## If you can't meet the gate by Monday

Don't sign Monday as is. You have two options:

- **Lose the date.** If the venue won't hold it and you can't recruit, losing this date costs less than a Hexlify-owned conference with no neutrality.
- **Make Hexlify the owner.** If you do sign with only the two of you, say so in the brief and accept the company-run pole. The gate relaxes only by design, never silently.
