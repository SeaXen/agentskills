# ShipItCon Tampere: first-edition pre-mortem (reviewed and rewritten)

## Verdict on your draft

Your list has five risks and no owners, and all five are external. The first-edition method calls this the failure pattern: a pre-mortem where nothing is about the team, the venue, or the money has skipped its most useful category.

- **#1 (nobody shows up)** is probably a paper tiger, but only if you have a community baseline. See the first assumption below.
- **#2 to #5 (rain, wifi, competing conference, late catering)** are cheap or contractual fixes. They don't belong at the top of the list.
- **"We'll figure out who handles it if it happens"** fails the risk gate. Every launch-blocking risk needs a named owner and a decision date *before* it fires.
- **Missing entirely:** the venue contract, sponsor payment timing, speaker cancellation, day-of volunteers, and your own bandwidth.

## Assumptions to confirm

I didn't get to run the full interview, so these are guesses. Each one changes the table below.

1. **Baseline audience.** Do you have an existing meetup, community, or list to size attendance from? If yes, "nobody's heard of us" is a paper tiger. If no, it becomes a real risk, and the answer is the validation gate (real registrations), not worry.
2. **Venue.** I'm assuming it isn't locked. At T-8 months you are already about 1.5 months behind the sourced conference timeline, which locks the venue at T-9.5.
3. **Company-run or community-run.** I'm assuming community-run, because you described three people from three employers.
4. **Date.** Is it fixed? Has anyone checked the Tampere calendar yet?

## Administrative gates before anything is public

Do these before the first announcement:

1. Publish the code of conduct. It is the earliest hard gate, ahead of venue and date.
2. Set up a neutral shared event email alias. Don't use a Brightloop, Vektor Systems, or personal address.
3. Set up a shared credential vault.
4. Join a peer-organizer network.

## Go/no-go and budget-tier dates to write now

Pick the dates yourselves. The mechanism is the method's, and the dates are yours.

- **Sponsor revenue checkpoint:** the Best→Better→Good downgrade is triggered by money received, not by ticket speed. A soft ticket signal downgrades the tier. It doesn't cancel the event.
