## Gate 2: Budget and risk

Venue and catering dominate cost. Everything else is small by comparison.

1. Get 3 venue quotes in Leeds (university hall, civic venue, tech space). Ask for day rate, catering minimums, AV, and cancellation terms.
2. Build the model: `break-even tickets = (fixed costs + per-head cost × N) ÷ (ticket price − per-head cost)`.
3. Count sponsors as upside only. Budget so the event survives at 40% of sponsor target.
4. Set a maximum loss the four of you accept, in writing. Agree who pays if it goes over.
5. Set up a legal entity or fiscal host, a separate bank account, and event insurance (cancellation and public liability) before taking money.
6. Cap early commitments: choose venue contracts with a late cancellation window.

## CFP setup

You have never run one, so keep it simple and fair.

**Tools:** Sessionize (free tier, handles submissions, review, speaker pages and schedule export). Alternatives: Pretalx, PaperCall.

**Timeline (backward from a Sept-Oct 2027 date):**

1. **Now to Dec 2026:** pass the gates, fix the date, announce ElixirNorth with a landing page and "save the date".
2. **Jan 2027:** write the code of conduct, speaker policy, and CFP text. Recruit reviewers.
3. **Feb 2027:** open CFP. Keep it open 6-8 weeks.
4. **Mid-Apr 2027:** CFP closes. Send reminders a week and a day before close; most submissions arrive at the deadline.
5. **May 2027:** review, then notify. Hold 2-3 slots for invited speakers.
6. **June 2027:** schedule published. Speakers confirm in 2 weeks, with waitlisted backups.
7. **July-Aug 2027:** speaker coaching, travel booked, ticket sales ramp.
8. **Sept 2027:** run-of-show, rehearsals, final briefing.

**Call text must state:**
- audience level and the talk themes you want (production Elixir, Phoenix/LiveView, Nerves, ML/Nx, OTP in practice, team stories)
- talk lengths and formats, plus whether first-time speakers are welcome
- what you pay: ticket, travel, accommodation. Decide this before opening the CFP, since it affects who can apply.
- review process, deadlines, and notification date
- code of conduct link

**Speaker support policy (decide now):**
- Free ticket plus a travel and hotel budget for accepted speakers. If money is tight, say "up to £X" openly and prioritize speakers who couldn't otherwise come.
- Do not make speakers pay or require sponsorship to get a slot.

**Review process:**

1. Use 5-6 reviewers: the four organizers plus 2-3 people outside the organizing team (ideally from other UK/EU Elixir communities).
2. Review **anonymously** (hide names) in round one. Sessionize supports this.
3. Score each talk 1-5 on: topic fit, clarity of abstract, new insight, speaker's ability to deliver. Use the same rubric for everyone.
4. Reviewers score independently, then you meet to resolve ties and curate. The aim is a good program, not just the top scores. Balance topics, levels, and speaker backgrounds.
5. Declare conflicts of interest. An organizer who submits a talk skips scoring it.
6. Write short rejection emails with a real reason when you can. Offer rejected talks the lightning or meetup slot.

**Volume expectation:** about 3-5 submissions per slot. For 10-14 slots, plan for roughly 40-70 submissions. Fewer than 25 means you need more outreach. Directly invite 10-15 people whose talks you want, before the CFP opens.

**Outreach to widen the pool:** post in Elixir Forum, ElixirConf/Code BEAM speaker networks, Slack, UK meetup groups, LinkedIn, and underrepresented groups' communities. Offer a short "write your abstract" session at a meetup.

## Tickets and money

1. Price three tiers: early bird, standard, and a late/corporate tier. Add a small number of subsidized tickets for students and people between jobs.
2. Release a **blind-bird** batch right after the CFP announcement, before the program exists. It gives cash and a demand signal.
3. Use Tito, Luma or Eventbrite. Check fees against ticket price.
4. Sponsors: build a simple 3-tier prospectus. Target Elixir employers, hiring companies, and tool vendors. Start outreach in Jan 2027. Give recruiters visible but non-intrusive presence; no sponsor selects speakers.
5. Keep a running budget sheet shared by all four. Review monthly.

## Success measures (decide now)

- Tickets sold against the break-even number
- Attendance rate (tickets bought vs. people who show up)
- Share of attendees from outside Leeds
- Post-event survey: would you return, talk rating
- Money: final profit or loss vs. cap
- Whether a second edition looks viable

Judge the event on the trend across 2-3 editions. First edition will underperform steady state.

## Risks to watch

- **Burnout.** Four people, many months. Schedule a check-in each month and cut scope early.
- **Late sponsor money.** Don't sign big venue costs before sponsor commitments.
- **Low CFP volume or weak talks.** Mitigate with invited speakers.
- **Illness, no-shows.** Keep 2 backup speakers.
- **Safety and conduct.** Publish a code of conduct, name a contact person, brief volunteers.
