## 1. Sponsored keynote: decline

- Edition 1 sets your reputation. Developers read a bought keynote as "vendor event". That costs more than the cash gains.
- A keynote is the one slot that frames the day. You cannot sell it and keep a community identity.
- Offer a "Sponsor session" instead. See step 2.

## 2. Sponsor panel: accept with 6 conditions

Put these in the contract:

1. **Label.** The program says "Presented by X". It is not a keynote and not an opening or closing slot.
2. **Slot.** Place it mid-afternoon, around 2:30 to 3:15, 40 minutes max. Not first, not last.
3. **Moderator.** You pick a neutral moderator. The sponsor CTO never moderates their own panel.
4. **Topic.** Frame it on a problem, such as "What broke when we ran Helm at scale". The product is not the topic. Customers speak about real production experience.
5. **Q&A.** Keep 10 minutes of open audience questions. No pre-screened questions.
6. **No editorial veto.** The sponsor has no say over the rest of the program. They cannot veto speakers or competitor sponsors, and they get no attendee list unless attendees opt in.

If the sponsor refuses any of 1 to 3, walk away. Those three protect the event.

## 3. Money: lock it before you plan around it

1. Ask: is the doubled amount tied to the panel, or to the keynote too? Get it written.
2. Counter: the doubled amount holds for the labeled panel alone, with the keynote declined.
3. Ask for payment before the event. Do not commit spend until cash lands, or at least a signed invoice.
4. Check what Platinum already includes. If it covers a speaking slot, the panel costs them nothing extra, so negotiate.
5. Keep a 15 to 25% reserve of the new money. This is edition 1 and 220 registered will not all show up.

## 4. The 4pm slot: skip the leaders panel

Why:
- You would have two panels in one day. That is panel fatigue.
- "Local leaders" panels are easy to fill, but nobody remembers them.
- 4pm is when attention dies. A panel sits worst there.

Better, in order:
1. **Lightning talks.** Run 5 to 6 talks of 7 minutes each, from an open call to the local community. They cost nothing and they energize the room. Speakers bring their friends, which grows edition 2.
2. **"Bring your broken chart" clinic.** Local engineers debug attendee problems live.
3. **Cut the slot.** End the day early and start the social hour earlier. This is free.

Keep the local leaders anyway. Invite them as lightning speakers or as the clinic crew. You keep the local ties without the panel.
