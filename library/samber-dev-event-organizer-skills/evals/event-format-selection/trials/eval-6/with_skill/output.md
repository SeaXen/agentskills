## KernelDay format brief

**Recommendation:** run the day as one room in the morning and open space in the afternoon. Put 5-minute Ignite talks between them as the first-timer on-ramp.

Your draft is nine 45-minute talks back to back. That fails both goals:
- It gives first-timers a 45-minute slot, the hardest way to start.
- It leaves about 10 minutes of hallway time per break.

This recommendation assumes a community-run event with an open CFP and no sold stage slots. It also assumes in-person delivery.

### Day architecture

```
09:00  Doors, coffee
09:30  Opening (10 min)
09:40  Curated talk 1 (30)
10:15  Curated talk 2 (30)
10:45  Break (30)
11:15  Curated talk 3 (30)
11:50  Curated talk 4 (30)
12:20  Lunch (75)
13:35  Ignite block: 8 x 5 min, auto-advance (50 incl. changeovers)
14:25  Break (25)
14:50  Open-space marketplace (20): topics claim slots on the wall
15:15  Open-space round 1 (45)
16:00  Break (25)
16:25  Open-space round 2 (45)
17:10  Closing circle (20)
17:30  Social
```

- **Speakers:** 12 (4 + 8), up from 9 in your draft.
- **Hallway time:** about 130 minutes of breaks, plus the whole afternoon.
- **Source:** this follows the DevOpsDays day architecture.
- **Ignite rules:**
  - Slides auto-advance. The format itself enforces the length.
  - Collect slides as PDF in advance.
  - Run the block from one laptop.
  - Tell speakers they cannot advance slides and have no notes.
  - No straight sales pitch.

### Goal 1: first-time speakers

1. **Reserve Ignite slots.** Write the rule down before the CFP closes: at least 6 of 8 Ignite slots go to first-timers. Ignite is a lower-commitment, higher-volume format built for this.
2. **Reserve curated slots.** At least 2 of the 4 curated talks go to first-timers. A 30-minute slot is a real step up, so pair each first-timer with a returning speaker as mentor and hold one rehearsal call a week before.
3. **Build a pipeline.** Anyone who gives an Ignite this year gets priority for a 30-minute slot next year.
4. **Option:** run blind CFP review for equity. Most CFPs are not blind, so this is a choice, not a norm.

### Goal 2: conversations between sessions

- **Breaks:** long breaks and a 75-minute lunch are format choices, not leftover time. Monktoberfest even moved its start time for the hallway track.
- **Open space:** the afternoon is the conversation. Anyone who is neither contributing nor learning moves elsewhere (the Law of Two Feet).
- **Speakers:** ask them to stay all day. GOTO reports about 75% do, and its hallway talk is better for it.
- **Space:** keep a quiet room and good food.
- **Recording:** record talks and Ignites. Do not record open space.

### The vendor-demo pile

Never give them the main stage. This matches CNCF and DevOpsDays practice: tool-specific content goes to open space, and the stage stays conceptual. A single curated track is what makes that rule enforceable.

Offer submitters three exits instead of a flat rejection:

1. **Convert.** Invite them to resubmit as a talk about the problem, not the tool. Some will, and those are real talks.
2. **Open-space topic.** They post a "Demo: X" sticky on the marketplace wall and attendees choose whether to come. This is honest, so promise no turnout.
3. **Sponsor route.** Paid sponsors get expo tables or a side event. Nobody buys a stage slot.

The costs:
- Open space does not guarantee an audience. Some vendors will decline that and walk away.
- Ignite is not a back door for demos. Its no-pitch rule is socially enforced, so say so in the CFP.

Put the ban in writing in the CFP: "community conference, no product or vendor sales pitches" (CNCF's wording).

### Candidates compared

| | A. Single track + open-space afternoon (recommended) | B. Single track, curated talks only | C. Your draft, or two tracks |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-timer slots | 8 Ignite + 2 talks | About 10 talks, no short on-ramp | Few 45-minute slots |
| Hallway time | High | Medium | Low |
| Vendor demos | Have a home | No home | No home |
| Organizer effort | Medium | Low | High for two tracks |

- **C, two tracks:** it needs a CFP of roughly 8-10x the slots, about 100 submissions or more for 12 slots. Count only real talk submissions. The demo pile should not count.
- **Panels:** none. A panel needs a prepared moderator who will interrupt. Add one only if you have that person.

### Delivery mode

In-person with record-and-post.
- **Not hybrid:** you have no evidence a remote audience would pay. Hybrid costs roughly double in AV and logistics, and it works against your hallway goal.
- **Revise when:** remote registrations stay above about 25-30% of paid attendance and cover their marginal cost.

### What would change each choice next year

| Choice | Revise when |
|---|---|
| Single track | CFP of at least 8-10x slots, after removing demos |
| Ignite block | Applications under about 1x the 8 slots (self-set): cut it, or reopen the CFP |
| Open space | Marketplace wall under half full by 15:00 (self-set): add a facilitator or shorten it |
| Hallway goal | A survey question ("did you have the conversations you came for?") drops against this year (self-set) |

Write the self-set gates down before the event.
