## 5. The money model

Fill this in this week. It takes one afternoon.

- **Revenue** = (tickets × average price) + sponsors + other (workshops, recordings, job board).
- **Cost** = fixed (hall, AV base, production, insurance, marketing) + per-head (catering, badges, swag, staffing ratio) + step costs (extra stage, extra track, second registration desk).
- **Break-even attendees** = (fixed + steps − sponsor revenue) ÷ (average price − per-head cost).

Things that move the model:

- **Price.** Move to tiers (early, standard, late, student, group) instead of one low price. Hold some inventory back for a late price that sits above today's.
- **Sponsors.** Sponsor revenue should carry as much of the fixed cost as possible. Before G2, aim for 3-5 sponsors signed, or in writing at a named tier, not just interested. The inbound email is the first of those.
- **Step costs.** Going from 400 to 900 is not a straight multiply. Budget extra for a second screen and sound zone, queue management at registration, more catering stations, more security and first-aid cover, and hotel room blocks.
