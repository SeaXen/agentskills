# PratoConf lunch brief: format and meal count

This follows the `event-hospitality` skill and its two references. It's a draft for your approval. Don't send anything to the caterer yet.

## Answer

- **Format:** individually packaged, labelled, name-matched meals. One sealed item per person, handed over at a pickup point.
- **Meals to tell the caterer:** **567 per lunch.** That is 1,134 across the two days if day two matches day one.

## Why this format

- **Standing room only** deletes plated service, which needs a seat for everyone. It also makes a buffet awkward, because people would balance plates and cutlery on their feet.
- **No chafing dishes or open flame** deletes family-style outright. It would also need seating.
- **60 minutes for about 567 people** makes a single buffet line the risk. Handing over a packaged item is the fastest service, and people can eat wherever they are talking.
- **Restrictions travel with the food.** Each label sits on the item and protects the person it was made for. A buffet depends on guests picking correctly, and a wrong pick lands on exactly the person who declared a restriction. An unlabelled buffet is not an option here.
- **Outside caterers are allowed**, so this is feasible. Ask the caterer for meals that eat well cold or at room temperature, with no knife needed. Hot-holding is limited by the chafing-dish ban. Check with the venue before the caterer plans any hot items.

## The 567

| Population | Meals |
|---|---|
| Ticket holders, expected to show up | 480 |
| Volunteers | 40 |
| Organizers | 12 |
| Speakers | 25 |
| Sponsor booth staff (about 10) | 10 |
| **Total per lunch** | **567** |

- **480 is your registrations person's projection, not mine.** I took it as the expected-show-up figure. I did not derive a number of my own.
- **The 170-ticket gap (650 minus 480) is buffer, and it stays out of the order.** Don't send 650. Ask the caterer how late the count can be raised, and by how much.
- **Day two:** ask your registrations person for a per-day projection. Two-day events often drop on day two.
- **Check for double counting.** I assumed speakers, volunteers, organizers and sponsor staff are not among the 650 tickets. If some speakers hold tickets, subtract them.
- **The headcount guarantee date** is the real deadline. Find it before you commit.

## Dietary counts

Declared on the registration form, among all 650 ticket holders:

| Category | Count |
|---|---|
| Vegetarian | 38 |
| Vegan | 12 |
| Gluten-free | 6 |
| Nut allergy | 3 |
| **Total** | **59** |

- **Carry all 59 as named, labelled meals.** Within the 567, that is at least 59 special meals and at most 508 standard ones. Trim only at the guarantee date, once you know who has dropped out.
- **The 59 do not cover everyone fed.** The form only reached ticket holders. Ask the volunteer and organizer leads, the speakers and the sponsor staff directly, since those 87 people are the group most often left out. Add their counts per category.
- **Check for overlap.** One person can be both vegan and nut-allergic. Count people, not boxes ticked.
- **The nut-allergy meals are the safety-critical ones.** Ask the caterer whether they can prepare them without cross-contact. Seal and label them separately.
- **I haven't confirmed which dietary categories PratoConf has committed to.** That list belongs to `event-accessibility-inclusion`. If halal, kosher or other categories are in scope, the form missed them.

## Fallback if the caterer can't cover a restriction

1. **Default:** a one-off addition to the standing order, so the person still eats with everyone else.
2. **Only if that is truly impossible:** tell the person well in advance and give them a delivery credit to arrange their own meal. Never let them find out at the pickup table.

## Declaration

- Print allergen detail on every item's label.
- Post the same information at the pickup point.
- If the information is lost anywhere between the caterer's delivery and the pickup table, the label fails. Assign one person to receive each delivery, check the labels against the dietary counts, and carry that through to the table.

## Handoffs

- **`event-vendor-sourcing`:** the style, the counts, the caterer and the contract. Questions for them:
  - Can the caterer accommodate the dietary counts inside the standard order?
  - Who may serve?
  - What is the late-adjustment limit on the count?
  - What is the headcount guarantee date?
  - Can hot items be delivered in insulated carriers?
- **`event-schedule-design`:** a service-window requirement, not a grid change. The 60-minute gap stays as is. Meals must be staged and ready before the gap opens, with several pickup points so the line doesn't eat the hour.
- **`event-attendee-experience`:** where the pickup points go and how name-matched restricted meals reach the right person.
- **`event-no-show-management`:** record covers ordered against covers eaten after each day, so the next edition's number improves.
