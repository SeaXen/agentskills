Tell the caterer: **600 individually boxed grab-and-go lunches per day, no cutlery needed, with 67 of them as labeled dietary boxes.**

**Headcount, per day**
1. Attendees: 480 projected show-ups.
2. On-site staff: 40 volunteers + 12 organizers + 25 speakers + 10 booth staff = 87. Feed all of them, since speakers and booth staff may be there both days.
3. Subtotal: 567.
4. Buffer: about 6% (33 meals), for walk-ups and a wrong projection. Total: 600.
5. The 650-ticket ceiling plus staff is 737, so 600 sits well under the worst case.
6. Ask the caterer for a final-count adjustment window 24 to 48 hours before each day. Order day 1 at 600. Use day-1 scan-in numbers to set day 2.

**Format**
- Use pre-boxed lunches: a wrap or sandwich, fruit, a snack, and water. Each box is eaten one-handed while standing.
- Skip plated food, bowls, soup, and anything that needs a fork. The hall is standing room only.
- Skip hot buffets. Chafing dishes and open flame are banned, and insulated carriers would create queues.
- Set up about 6 pickup points of around 100 boxes each. A single table serves roughly 10 people per minute, so 567 people clear in about 10 to 15 minutes. That leaves most of the 60 minutes for eating.
- Put drinks at separate water stations away from the box tables.

**Dietary split of the 600**
- Vegetarian: 42 (38 registered, plus a margin)
- Vegan: 14 (12 registered, plus a margin)
- Gluten-free: 7 (6 registered, plus a margin)
- Nut-free: 4 (3 registered, plus a margin)
- Standard: 533

The margin covers the roughly 9% of staff and speakers who probably have dietary needs too. I ordered the full registered counts, not counts scaled to 480 show-ups. Scaling would give about 44, but dietary boxes are cheap and a person left without food is a real problem.

**Caterer instructions**
- Label every dietary box with a color-coded sticker that names the diet, and pack each in a sealed bag.
- Put dietary boxes on their own table, away from the standard boxes, to avoid cross-contact.
- Make the nut-free boxes from a nut-free kitchen process, with the allergen declared on the label.
- Make gluten-free boxes certified gluten-free. Vegan plus gluten-free overlap is likely.
- Ask for a written allergen list for the standard menu.
- Offer two standard options, for example a meat or fish wrap and a vegetarian one.

**Open points**
- Check the form for people who ticked two categories (vegan and gluten-free, say). That could shrink the special total.
- The form only covered ticket holders. Email speakers, volunteers, and booth staff for their dietary needs, including halal and kosher, which the form didn't ask about.
- Ask for the caterer's last-minute change deadline before you sign.
