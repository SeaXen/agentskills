## 2. Discounted hotel block

- **Size:** start with 15–20% of attendees needing rooms, so about 60–80 room-nights per night. Adjust once you know how many travel in.
- **Structure:** one or two hotels near the venue, with one at a lower price tier. Ask for a **courtesy block** (no financial liability) rather than a contract block.
- **Terms to negotiate:**
  - Discounted rate of 15–25% off the public rate, valid 2 nights before and after.
  - Attrition clause of at least 80% (you only pay if fewer than 80% of rooms are used).
  - Release date 30 days before the event, so unbooked rooms go back to the hotel at no cost.
  - Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival.
  - A booking link or code, not a spreadsheet.
- **Rooming:** speakers get a separate small block, paid by you.
- **Communication:** put the code on the ticket confirmation, the website, and a reminder email 45 and 14 days out.
- **Check:** a "discounted" rate sometimes beats nothing and loses to an online travel agency. Compare before announcing.

## 3. Meal-line placement and signage

**Principle:** the queue must never run through a corridor or in front of a session room door.

- **Serving area:** use the largest open space (foyer, atrium, or hall), not a corridor. Set up **4 double-sided buffet islands**, which gives 8 serving lines.
- **Queue lanes:** each island gets a lane of at least 15 metres, folding back on itself inside the room (serpentine barriers). Start lines at least 3 metres from any room door and any stairs or fire exit.
- **Corridors:** keep a 2-metre clear walkway at all times. Station a volunteer at each corridor entrance to redirect.
- **Drinks and cutlery:** place them away from the food islands, on separate tables, so people who only want water don't join the queue.
- **Seating:** put tables on the far side of the room, away from the queue.
- **Signage** (A-frame or hanging, large type, visible from 10 metres):
  1. Entry: "LUNCH → Hall B" with an arrow, at each session room exit.
  2. Line labels: "Island 1–4", with a colour per island.
  3. Diet markers: V, VG, GF, Halal, Nut-free on every dish.
  4. "Queue starts here" at each island.
  5. "Drinks and desserts" and "Tables" signs.
  6. A "walk-up time" board: "Lunch is open 12:30–13:45".
- **Staff:** 1 volunteer per island plus 2 floaters and 1 corridor marshal per exit.
- **Release:** the room hosts ask the speaker to release rooms in two halves (rows A, then B) so 400 people don't leave at once. Tell people what to expect.

## 4. Lunch timing (45 → 75 minutes)

**Why 45 doesn't work.** With 8 serving lines at about 3 people per minute, everyone is served in about 17 minutes. Add 15–20 minutes of eating and the first-served people have just finished when the last arrive. There is no slack for the latest session overrunning, so you'd hit 45 minutes with a queue still standing.

**75 minutes works:**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Rooms release in two waves |
| 5–25 | Service (all 8 lines open) |
| 25–60 | Eating, networking |
| 60–75 | Last seconds, coffee, back to rooms |

**What moves:**
- Afternoon sessions start 30 minutes later on both days.
- Either the final session ends 30 minutes later, or you trim 30 minutes from the afternoon breaks.
- Update: printed schedule, app, speaker briefings, sponsor booth hours, and the evening dinner start time.
- Tell speakers and volunteers at least 2 weeks before the event.

## 5. Lunch format

**Recommended: staffed buffet.** Chosen because it handles dietary needs well, looks generous, and 75 minutes is enough for it.

- **Islands:** 4 double-sided islands, each with the same full menu so any line works.
- **Menu rhythm (rotate by day):**
  - Two hot dishes (one meat or fish, one vegetarian), a starch, and a cooked vegetable.
  - Two cold salads and a bread basket.
  - Fruit and a small sweet.
  - Drinks (water, one soft drink, coffee and tea) on a separate station.
- **Diet:** at least 30% vegetarian or vegan, with one gluten-free hot option on every island, each labelled.
- **Staffing:** caterer serves portions (not self-serve), which is faster and wastes less food.
- **Quantities:** plan for 400 plus a 5% buffer, scaled down by the day's expected attendance.
- **Plan B:** if the 75-minute change is refused, switch to **grab-and-go boxed lunch** (pick-up at 8 points, no queue lines, eat anywhere). It runs in 45 minutes but costs more per head and has fewer hot options.
