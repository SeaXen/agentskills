## The problem to solve before Monday

⚠️ **The timeline fails your audience.** Travel budget approvals close at the end of June. Selections finish June 30 and tickets open in July, so the people flying in can't get approval for a ticket they can't yet buy or justify. Pick at least two of these:

1. **Announce speakers on a rolling basis.** Close the CFP earlier, for example at the end of May. Publish first accepted talks in early-to-mid June, so approvers see a real program.
2. **Open a "travel-approval pre-registration" in early June.** Use a refundable deposit or a waitlist with a published price range. A manager can then approve a known cost.
3. **Publish a one-page "ask your manager" kit now.** Include dates, a price range or estimate, the expected cost of attending, and a justification paragraph. It's cheap and it works immediately.
4. **State the ticket price range on Monday**, even if sales open in July.

I recommend all four, with 3 and 4 published Monday. Moving the schedule needs your decision, so I haven't assumed it on the page.
