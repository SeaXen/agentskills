## 3. Group sizes

Ask each host to confirm these numbers. Book 44–46 sign-ups for 40 seats, since 5–10% usually drop out.

| Site | Whole group | Split | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fab | 40 | **4 groups of 10** | Escorted groups in controlled areas are usually capped at 10–15. Cleanrooms and gowning limit it further. |
| Datacenter | 40 | **4 groups of 10** | Security escort ratios, and narrow aisles. |
| Robotics lab | 40 | 2 groups of 20 | Startup floor space, and demos need a view. |

**How to run 4 groups with 2 guides:**
- Two groups tour in parallel for about 45 min while the other two attend a talk and Q&A.
- Then they swap.
- With only one guide, 4 tours of 30 min fills the whole slot. The tours are shallower, but it still works.
- Put this question in each email: "How many guides can you supply?"
