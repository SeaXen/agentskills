## What to ask to see

The office has nothing to look at, so ask for people and screens.

1. **Merge queue talk (25 min).** An engineer explains the design and the trade-offs.
2. **Flaky-test quarantine talk (25 min).** Cover how tests get detected, quarantined, and released.
3. **Live walkthrough (15 min), only if approved.** Show a queue dashboard or a quarantine report. Sanitized or demo data is fine. No customer or repo names.
4. **Q&A panel (30 min).** Two or three engineers answer. Questions are collected in advance, so they can vet them.
5. **Open tables (30 min).** Members get an informal chat with the speakers.

Questions to submit ahead of time:

*Merge queue*
- What problem did it solve at your size?
- How do you batch changes, and what happens when a batch fails?
- How long do merges wait, and how do you measure it?
- How do you handle a broken main branch?
- Which parts did you build, and which did you buy?

*Flaky tests*
- How do you decide a test is flaky: a threshold, or reruns?
- Who owns a quarantined test, and how long can it stay there?
- What stops quarantine from becoming a graveyard?
- How do you measure that it's working?
- What went wrong in early versions?
