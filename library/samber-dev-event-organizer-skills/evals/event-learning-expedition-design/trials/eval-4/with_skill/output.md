# Draft announcement for the Cobaltine Analytics tour (hold it until Cobaltine confirms)

⚠️ **I wrote the page without "photos and blogging welcome!" or the filming promise.** Your contact hasn't mentioned any restrictions, but that doesn't mean there aren't any. A network operations center is the kind of place with screens, customer names and security rules. The skill's rule is "silence is off-limits, not permission." Your contact is a developer advocate, so he may not speak for security or legal. If Cobaltine later says no to filming, you'd have told 30 people and a videographer something you can't deliver.

## Before you publish: 4 items in writing from Cobaltine

1. Their yes, with any conditions.
2. A named counterpart. Ask whether he can commit his colleagues' time, or only his own.
3. Their photo, blogging and filming rules, including NOC areas, screens and customer names.
4. Who will clear anything attendees want to publish afterwards, if Cobaltine wants a review.

Send them a short brief first: what your group wants to understand, what you're asking their people to do, and what you plan to tell attendees they can share. Include the videographer and ask directly: "Can we film the whole tour? Which areas are off-limits?"

## Two decisions to make

- **Dinner:** you need to decide this, and I'm not going to guess. Ask Cobaltine whether they're providing food. If you cater it yourselves, catering and transport headcount belong to your hospitality planning, not this page. The headcount can't be corrected once the bus leaves, so ask people to confirm dinner at RSVP.
- **Filming:** once Cobaltine answers, you pick one of three versions of the "Photos, notes and sharing" section:
  - **Everything allowed:** "photos and blogging welcome" can come back, but only with their written yes.
  - **Ground rules:** name the restricted areas, for example no photos in the NOC. This is the most likely outcome.
  - **Clearance:** attendees can see everything, but Cobaltine reviews anything before it's published, and you name the person who runs that queue.

Your videographer's footage is a separate step. Cobaltine's consent covers filming, and anything you publish from it needs their review on top. If they only allow the office and not the NOC, "film the whole tour" won't happen.
