## Before you send

- **Confirm the date, headcount and day length** against what Volthaus already agreed in writing.
- **Ask about NDA and photo rules early.** Firmware rollout internals may be sensitive. If they can't share details, ask what they can show, so you don't promise members something they can't deliver.
- **Check test-floor safety.** Grid-scale battery racks usually mean PPE, group limits and escorts. This may force the four-group format.

## If they resist

1. **Protect the talk and floor time first.** Give up the partner showcase and the CEO slot length before you give up those two.
2. **Fall back to one change.** Swap the 45-minute roadmap for the rollout talk, and make the lab visit 90 minutes.
3. **Escalate through the VP of Engineering, not around her.** She's your sponsor, and the events team will likely defer to her.
4. **Prepare your members.** If the marketing agenda wins entirely, tell members up front what the day will and won't cover.
