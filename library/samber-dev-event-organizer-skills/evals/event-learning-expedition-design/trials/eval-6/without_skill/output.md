## 2. Success metrics

Two problems with your proposal:
- **NPS uplift is weak at n=35.** One NPS point is about 0.3 of a person, so the margin of error swamps any uplift. The tour is also one day of a 600-person event, so it can't be isolated. Tour-goers chose to go, which biases the comparison.
- **"Influenced pipeline" needs an owner.** An event organizer has no pipeline of its own. Decide whose it is first: Foundry, your sponsors, or attendees' employers.

**Pick one primary outcome:** value to the attendees, value to the host, or value to your sponsors.

**Recommended scorecard (counts first, scores second):**
1. **Show rate:** attended ÷ registered for the tour. Target is yours to set; there is no benchmark I'd trust.
2. **Rule compliance:** 0 photography violations, 0 escort lapses.
3. **Tour rating:** a 1–5 score plus "would you recommend this tour?" yes/no. Report raw counts (e.g. 29 of 33).
4. **Host outcome:** would Foundry host again, yes or no. This protects next year's relationship.
5. **Event link (optional):** do tour-goers attend more sessions or rate the event higher than non-tour-goers? Label this as correlation only.

**If you keep NPS:** ask it about the whole conference. Compare tour against non-tour attendees. Show n and the raw promoter/detractor counts, and don't call it "uplift".

**If you keep pipeline:**
- Define attribution before the event: a self-reported "how did you hear about X" field, plus a 90-day influence window.
- Get explicit consent before sharing attendee data with Foundry or sponsors.
- Never claim last-click credit.

## 3. Transport and boxed lunches

**Transport:**
- Sintra is roughly 25–30 km from Lisbon. Expect about 35–50 min each way, depending on traffic and pickup point.
- 35 attendees plus 2 organizers plus a spare margin means **one 50-seat coach**.
- Book a licensed operator that meets driver-hours rules and, if anyone needs it, has an accessible coach. Ask for a named driver and a vehicle registration for the gate list.
- Use **one pickup point**, ideally the main conference hotel. Set departure 15 min earlier than the math says.
- Put a standby coach company on your call list.

**Boxed lunches:**
- **Ask Foundry first whether food is allowed on site.** Assume none in the halls. Plan to eat in their lobby or meeting room, on the coach, or at a nearby spot.
- Collect dietary needs and allergies at registration. Label every box and order 3–5 spare, including vegetarian.
- Order lead time depends on the caterer, so confirm it when you book. Add water and a bag for waste.

**Day-of items:**
- A schedule with a buffer for gate checks, which are often slow.
- An emergency contact sheet: you, Foundry, the coach company.
- A printed attendee list matching passport names exactly.
