### Status thresholds

- 🟢 **On track:** at or above 95% of the date's target.
- 🟠 **Behind:** 80–95%. Add a push within one week.
- 🔴 **Off track:** below 80%. Escalate and change the offer or channel mix.
- A miss at Jun 15 or Aug 20 matters most. If you are 🔴 at Aug 20, the Sep–Oct surge cannot make up the gap.
