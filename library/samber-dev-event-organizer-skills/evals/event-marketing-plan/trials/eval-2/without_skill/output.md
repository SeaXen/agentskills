# BitBloom signup targets and registration close

**Target: about 1,300 signups, with 750 confirmed one week out, to get 600 people in the building.**

## Assumptions to check first

⚠️ **Date check:** 11 weeks from today (Wed 2026-10-14) is **Wed 2026-12-30**, which is winter break on most campuses. I use "T-minus weeks" below. If the real date is that one, expect worse turnout, or move to the weekend of Dec 11–13 (T-8.5) and compress the curve.

⚠️ **Show rates are industry-typical guesses, not your data.** If you have last year's signup and check-in numbers, send them and I'll replace the rates.

- **Signup to check-in on a free event: about 45%.** The plausible range is 40–55%. Free tickets cost nothing, so people drop out easily.
- **Confirmation step:** about 58% of signups confirm, and about 80% of confirmed people show up. That is 0.58 × 0.80 ≈ 46%.
- **Planning range:** 1,100 signups if things go well, 1,500 if they go badly. I plan for **1,300**.
- **MLH listing:** it will bring out-of-area signups who won't travel. Expect them to show up at roughly 30%, versus about 55% for your own campus. A high MLH share means you need more total signups.

## Confirmation funnel (the number that matters)

| Point | Target |
|---|---|
| T-14 days | Email every signup: "Confirm you're coming by T-7" |
| T-7 days | **750 confirmed** (about 58% of signups) |
| T-7 days | Release unconfirmed spots to a waitlist |
| Event day | **600 checked in** (about 80% of confirmed) |

At T-7, if confirmed is under 650, you are short. Open waitlist invites on a rolling basis and over-admit by about 10%.

## When to close registration

1. **Soft close at T-8 days.** This gives the confirmation email one week to work before the T-7 headcount. Catering and shirt counts lock around T-5 days.
2. **Close earlier if you hit your venue or fire-code cap.** Take a fixed number of accepted people, such as 750 confirmed, and send the rest to the waitlist.
3. **Keep the waitlist open until the event.** Waitlist walk-ins fill no-shows. At the door, admit them in order once you are past the first-hour check-in.
4. **Don't close later than T-5 days.** Food, swag and badge printing need a firm number.
