**Short answer: no, don't apply it as written.** ❌

## Why the template doesn't fit

1. **It assumes a funded company with a paid funnel.** The 30-40% on ads only makes sense if you can measure cost per acquisition against lifetime value, and you can't do that for a once-a-year event.
2. **The audience is tiny and specific.** Rust developers who might travel to your conference are reached through a few channels (Rust newsletters, meetups, Reddit, Discord, speaker networks). Broad ad platforms mostly pay to reach people who don't care.
3. **Your real constraint is organizer hours, not euros.** Five people working nights and weekends can't run a content calendar plus ad experiments. A budget that ignores this produces a spreadsheet nobody executes.
4. **Several lines are too small to matter at this size.**
   - 10-15% for tools is 300-450 EUR, and most of what you need is free.
   - 5-10% for testing is 150-300 EUR. Testing is a method, not a budget line.

## What I'd do instead (3,000 EUR)

This assumes your goal is ticket sales and you have 8-12 weeks left. Change it if the goal is CFP submissions or sponsors.

| Bucket | EUR | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| Held reserve | 600 | Not spent until week 3. It goes to whichever channel shows the best signal. |
| Targeted paid tests | 900 | 3-4 small tests of 150-300 EUR each: Rust newsletter or podcast slots, and a narrow Reddit or LinkedIn test. |
| Community and meetup partnerships | 700 | Cover pizza or venue costs for Rust meetups in exchange for a 5-minute announcement and a flyer or QR code. This is the highest-intent audience you can reach. |
| Shareable assets | 600 | A freelance designer for speaker cards and a short teaser video, plus editing of past talks if you have them. Speakers and sponsors then do the distribution for you. |
| Tracking and tools | 200 | Domain and email costs, and UTM links per channel. Use free analytics. |

## Rules that make it work

1. **Do the free things first.**
   - Post in This Week in Rust and r/rust.
   - Announce in Rust community Discords.
   - Give every speaker a ready-to-post kit.
   - Ask your sponsors to promote you. They already paid.
2. **Give each paid test one tracked link.** Check results after 2 weeks.
3. **Scale or kill each test on one number.** That number is cost per ticket sold. Set the cutoff before you spend, for example no more than 25% of the ticket price.
4. **Release the reserve only to the best performer.** If nothing works, keep the money for the event itself.
5. **Name one promotion owner.** Give them a hard cap of about 3 hours a week. Everyone else does one-off tasks.
