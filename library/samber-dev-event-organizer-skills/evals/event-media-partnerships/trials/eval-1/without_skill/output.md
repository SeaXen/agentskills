# DeltaLoop × Backend Monthly: deal structure

## Recommendation

Sign one master agreement with two separate schedules, and make the discount conditional on the newsletter feature actually running.

⚠️ I'm assuming the trade works like this: we give them a EUR 2,000 discount on Silver, and the feature is what we get back. If instead they want the feature as extra exposure for themselves as a sponsor, the structure still works, but the feature schedule changes (see Open questions).

## Why not "half price plus a feature"

- **Cash.** We net EUR 2,000 instead of EUR 4,000, and Silver still costs us badges, a booth or table, and logo and staff time. At break-even, that gap matters.
- **Price precedent.** Silver is listed at EUR 4,000. If other sponsors learn it sold at half, the list price is dead.
- **Enforceability.** If the discount is unconditional, the feature is a promise with no consequence for missing it.
- **Trust.** Paid coverage that reads as editorial damages both parties with the community. The label has to be honest.

## Structure

**Master agreement** (one document, one signature)
- Parties and term, running until 30 days after the event.
- Cross-reference clause: the schedules are linked, but each has its own deliverables and remedies.
- Governing law and venue, force majeure, and a cancellation clause (see below).
- Confidentiality of pricing, so the discount can't be quoted to other sponsors.

**Schedule A: Silver sponsorship**
- Silver benefits exactly as listed in our prospectus. Attach the list.
- List price EUR 4,000. Invoice the **full EUR 4,000** at signature, payable within 30 days.
- A **EUR 2,000 media credit** is applied by credit note once Schedule B is delivered (the discount, in effect).
- Category exclusivity: none, unless Silver already includes it.
- Attendee data: none shared. Lead capture only through opt-in scans, per GDPR.

**Schedule B: media partnership (the feature)**
- Deliverable: one feature on DeltaLoop in the newsletter sent to the full 22,000 list, or a stated Elixir/Europe segment (see Open questions).
- Send date: a fixed date or window, tied to our ticket and CFP milestones. A feature after tickets sell out is worth nothing.
- Content: a factual summary of the event, with a link to our site carrying UTM parameters. We get a factual-accuracy review before it goes out, with no say over their editorial judgment.
- Labeling: it is marked "Media partner" or "Sponsored" in the newsletter. Whichever it is, it is not presented as independent editorial.
- Reporting: sends, opens, and clicks within 7 days of the send.
- Credit value: stated in the contract as EUR 2,000, so both sides can book it.

**Linkage clauses** (the part that protects us)
1. If the feature isn't published by the long-stop date, the credit lapses and the full EUR 4,000 stays due. Pick the long-stop date about 6 weeks before the event.
2. If the feature runs but is materially wrong or off-spec, the credit is reduced pro rata.
3. If DeltaLoop cancels the event, Schedule A is refunded and Schedule B is void.
4. The cash portion is never contingent on our approving or rejecting their coverage.

## Money check

- You net EUR 2,000 in cash if the feature runs. If it doesn't, you get EUR 4,000.
- EUR 2,000 over a 22,000 list is about EUR 91 per thousand subscribers. That is probably well above what a sponsored slot normally costs, and I haven't checked their rate card. Ask for it. If their standard slot is worth less than EUR 2,000, counter at EUR 1,000 credit, or ask for two touches (the feature plus a reminder send).
- Relevance matters more than list size. Most backend developers on a general list don't use Elixir, and few can get to Porto. A segment or geographic cut is worth more than the full 22,000.
- Barter between businesses is generally taxable, and VAT may apply to both sides of the exchange. Ask your accountant before signing, and make sure the invoices and credit note reflect it.

## Open questions for Backend Monthly

1. Does the feature also name them as sponsor? If so, they are getting extra value from it, and the credit should be smaller.
2. Can they send to an Elixir or Europe segment, and can they do a second reminder?
3. What is the send date, and what is their normal rate for that slot?
4. Is the label "sponsored"? Do they have a house rule on this?
5. What do they want from Silver beyond the logo (booth, talk slot, attendee access)?
