## Media Partnership Agreement: KubeNorth [2027]

**1. Parties and edition**
- KubeNorth [2027], [dates], Manchester ("the Event"), run by [community name].
- Ship It Fridays podcast ("the Partner").
- This agreement covers this edition only. Renewal is a separate, deliberate decision.

**2. The Event delivers**
- **Logo and listing:** Partner logo and link in the "Media Partners" block on the KubeNorth site, from signature to [post-event date].
- **Social:** two thank-you posts on the Event's social accounts, one in announcement week ([date]) and one in event week ([date]).
- **Recording space:** [one table / recording corner] at the venue for one live episode on [day]. This is offered only after the sponsor-exclusivity check.
- **Tickets:** [N] complimentary tickets for the host and guests. Delete this line if you don't offer them.
- **Guest:** one KubeNorth organizer is available as a guest for the episode in section 3.

**3. The Partner delivers**
- **Calendar listing:** KubeNorth on the Partner's event list or show notes page by [date].
- **Promotional episode:** one episode featuring a KubeNorth organizer as guest, published by [date, before early-bird close].
- **Promo line:** one KubeNorth plug with the tracked link, in the show notes and the host's spoken read, in that episode.
- **Live episode:** one episode recorded on-site at the Event, published by [date, within X weeks after the Event].
- **Social:** two posts on the Partner's accounts, one at the promotional episode's release and one in event week.
- Every mention carries a **tracked link or code unique to the Partner** (e.g. `kubenorth.example/?ref=shipitfridays`).

**4. Dates**
- Each item above has a due date, and the Event's items are scheduled in its own social calendar.
- Missed dates are flagged to the other side in writing within [7] days.

**5. No editorial obligation**
- This agreement covers promotional inventory only: listings, logos, plugs, guest slots.
- The Event has no say in the Partner's editorial judgment, and the Partner has no say in the Event's program.
- Any further coverage the Partner chooses to run (a recap article, extra episodes) is its own decision and is outside this agreement.
- It is neither required nor promised.

**6. No program influence**
- This agreement gives the Partner no talk, keynote or track slot. Talks are selected through the Event's CFP.
- The Partner or its hosts may submit to the CFP like anyone else, on equal terms and without a guaranteed outcome.

**7. No audience data transfer**
- Each side posts and mails to its own audience.
- No subscriber, listener, attendee or registration lists cross this deal.
- Each side keeps its own leads.

**8. Branding approval**
- Each side approves the other's name and logo usage, and the copy of the first promotional item, before first publication.
- Approval is by written reply within [3] working days.

**9. Cash**
- No money changes hands. Any paid sponsorship or discounted tier is a separate sponsor agreement at the published rate.

**10. Exit**
- Either side may withdraw before delivery starts, with [14] days' written notice.
- Items already delivered stay delivered.

**11. Measurement and renewal**
- After the Event, both sides review delivery against section 12 and the registrations from the Partner's tracked link.
- Renewal, a format change or an end to the partnership is decided from that review.

## Before you send

1. **Check exclusivity.** Confirm the recording space doesn't clash with a sponsor's booth or category exclusivity, and that no sponsor owns "podcast" or "media" rights. This is the sponsor-fulfillment check. Don't announce the recording until it clears.
2. **Share it with your co-organizer.** Frame it as "same value for them, but nothing that compromises our CFP or buys coverage." They keep the audience reach they wanted: a 9k-download show, a guest episode and a live episode.
3. **Send the ask.** Use the skill's four-part ask:
   - One line on KubeNorth: Manchester, 350 people, community-run.
   - One specific overlap: name a real Ship It Fridays episode that matches your attendees.
   - The exchange in one sentence.
   - A small ask, such as a 20-minute call.

   Send it from a named organizer.
4. **If they push back for the keynote or the article:** hold the line on the keynote and point them to the CFP. If they want more visibility than a media partner gets, that is a paid sponsor tier, handled separately at the real rate.

## Measurement (all targets self-set)

- Registrations from the tracked link. Zero across two editions means drop a rung or end it.
- Delivery-ledger completion, both directions.
- Audience match: ticket-type mix or a registration poll answer, compared with "exactly our audience."
- Whether they ask to renew unprompted.
