# KubeNorth × Ship It Fridays: Media Partnership Terms (draft v1)

Draft for discussion. Have someone with legal training review it before signing. Square brackets mark values we need to fill in.

## 1. Parties

- **KubeNorth**, [legal entity or named signatory], organizer of KubeNorth, Manchester, [dates] ("the Conference").
- **Ship It Fridays**, [legal entity or named signatory], publisher of the podcast of that name ("the Partner").

## 2. Purpose

The Partner becomes Official Media Partner of the Conference. No money changes hands. Each side's contribution is the other's consideration.

## 3. What the Partner delivers

1. **Two podcast episodes** about the Conference, each at least [20] minutes of the Partner's own content.
   - **Episode A (pre-event)** is published at least [14] days before the Conference. It is an interview with organizers or speakers, or a preview.
   - **Episode B (post-event)** is published within [21] days after the Conference. It is a recap or discussion.
2. **One written recap article** of at least [800] words on the Partner's blog, published within [14] days after the Conference. It links to kubenorth.[tld].
3. **Promotion.** Each episode and the article are announced on the Partner's main social channels at least once. Show notes link to the Conference site and ticket page.
4. **Disclosure.** Every episode and the article state clearly that the Partner is an official media partner of the Conference and received a speaking slot and logo placement. Spoken disclosure comes in the first [2] minutes. The article carries a visible note at the top. This keeps both parties within UK advertising and consumer-protection disclosure rules.
5. **Reporting.** Within [30] days after Episode B, the Partner shares verified download counts for the episodes (platform screenshot or export) and link clicks to the Conference site, if available.

## 4. What KubeNorth delivers

1. **A 30-minute main-track slot** for one speaker nominated by the Partner (the "Partner Session").
   - Slot position: [e.g. opening of day 1 / mid-morning]. KubeNorth confirms it by [date].
   - The session is recorded with the rest of the main track, if KubeNorth records.
2. **Logo and link** on the Conference website's sponsors/partners page, labelled "Official Media Partner". Placement and size are KubeNorth's choice, and the logo is no smaller than the smallest sponsor logo. It stays up until [date, e.g. 30 days after the Conference].
3. **Mentions.** KubeNorth names the Partner in the partner announcement post and in [one] social post. It also lists the Partner in the printed or digital programme.
4. **Press access.** [Two] complimentary tickets and a recording corner or quiet space, subject to venue limits.

## 5. Conditions on the Partner Session

The slot is generous for a media partner, so KubeNorth keeps editorial control over it.

1. **Topic and speaker.** The Partner proposes a speaker and title by [date]. KubeNorth's programme committee approves both, using the same quality bar as other main-track talks. If it declines, the Partner proposes a replacement. If none is accepted by [date], KubeNorth may convert the slot into a live-recorded panel or fireside chat hosted by the Partner.
2. **Content.** The session is technical and vendor-neutral. It contains no product pitch and no promotion of the Partner's own sponsors beyond a neutral mention. The Partner is responsible for any rights in its slides or recordings.
3. **Code of Conduct.** The speaker and all Partner staff follow the Conference Code of Conduct.
4. **Slot, not a title.** The slot is listed as "Partner Session", not "Keynote", unless the programme committee decides otherwise.

## 6. Editorial independence

- The Partner keeps full editorial control over its content. KubeNorth does not review or approve episodes or the article before publication.
- KubeNorth keeps full control over its programme, speaker selection, and CFP. The partnership gives the Partner no vote or influence on other talks.
- The Partner's sponsors and advertisers get no rights under this agreement.
- No exclusivity applies on either side. KubeNorth may appoint other media partners and the Partner may cover other conferences.

## 7. Rights and content

- Each party keeps its own trademarks, logos, and content. Each grants the other a non-exclusive licence to use its name and logo for the purposes in this agreement only, ending [30] days after the Conference.
- The Partner may record audio and take photographs at the Conference for the episodes and the article. It must not record other speakers' sessions without their consent. It must respect "no photo" badges and any areas KubeNorth marks as off-limits.
- Recordings of the Partner Session are published by KubeNorth under the same terms as the other talks. The Partner may also publish its own recording of that session.

## 8. Non-delivery and remedies

- If the Partner publishes an item more than [14] days late, or not at all, KubeNorth may remove the logo and the "Official Media Partner" label, and may withdraw the Partner Session if it has not yet taken place.
- If the Partner Session has already taken place, KubeNorth's remedy is to remove the logo and label. The Partner agrees to publish the missing item within [30] days of written notice.
- If KubeNorth cancels or postpones the Conference, both parties' obligations are released. If it is rescheduled, this agreement carries over to the new dates unless either party gives notice within [14] days.
- Neither party is liable for failures caused by events outside its reasonable control (illness, venue closure, force majeure).

## 9. Data and privacy

KubeNorth does not share attendee lists or contact data with the Partner. The Partner does not collect attendee data on site without the attendee's consent. Each party handles personal data under UK GDPR.

## 10. Term and termination

The agreement runs from signature to [30] days after the Partner's last deliverable. Either party may terminate for material breach after [10] days' written notice with no cure. Sections 6, 7, and 9 survive termination.

## 11. General

- No partnership, agency, or employment relationship is created.
- Neither party may assign this agreement without the other's written consent.
- Changes must be in writing and signed (email counts).
- This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The courts of England have non-exclusive jurisdiction.

## Notes for you and your co-organizer

I drafted your co-organizer's proposal as given, but three points deserve a decision before you send this.

1. **The keynote slot is the expensive part.** On a 350-person single-track event, a 30-minute main-track slot is the most valuable asset you have, and it is given away without a CFP. Section 5 keeps your control by letting the programme committee approve the speaker and topic, and by offering a live-recorded fireside as a fallback. A simpler option is to offer a 20-minute slot or a recorded panel instead. Pick one before sending.
2. **Their audience figure is self-reported.** 9,000 downloads per episode is their claim. Section 3.5 asks for platform-verified numbers afterwards. Consider asking for a screenshot of their host dashboard now, before you commit the slot.
3. **Independence and disclosure.** If your own coverage ever depends on this deal, say so publicly. Section 3.4 puts the same duty on them, and it protects both of you.
