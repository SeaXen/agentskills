## Deal rules (all formats)

- **Partner types:** media outlets and blogs, newsletters, podcasts, communities and ambassador programs. Favor Python-specific ones. A general "tech" newsletter fails audience fit even with a large list.
- **Scoring:** rate each candidate 1–5 on audience fit, audience size, brand alignment, engagement quality, reciprocity potential and ease of execution. These are judgment scores, not measurements. Accept on fit plus at least one of size or engagement. Ask partners for their own open and listen numbers.
- **Written terms:** a one-page agreement per partner with countable, dated deliverables in both directions.
- **No editorial obligation.** The deal buys promotional inventory, never coverage, a talk slot or program influence.
- **No list exchange.** Each side mails its own audience.
- **Logo approval:** each side approves the other's logo use before first publication.
- **Exit:** either side can withdraw before delivery starts.
- **Cash:** if anyone offers half price plus a mention, split it. The cash goes to the sponsor block at the real price, and only the barter leg stays here.
- **Delivery ledger:** one row per promised item, in both directions. Your own thank-you posts are the ones most often dropped, so schedule them in your social calendar.
- **Sponsor exclusivity:** the check is now needed only if a partner's logo block overlaps a category a paying sponsor owns.

## Measurement (all targets self-set)

- **Referral signal:** one tracked link per partner. A partner at zero across two editions gets moved down a rung or dropped.
- **Delivery completion:** compare promised against delivered, both directions, before any renewal.
- **Audience match:** check ticket-type mix or a poll question against your Q2 assumption.
- **Renewal:** count partners who come back unprompted next edition.
