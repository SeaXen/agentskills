# PyCoastal online media-partner plan, re-ranked

ℹ️ I don't know your partner list or audience mix, so this ranking rests on what you told me: 1,200 registrations last year, five volunteers with day jobs, and ticket sales opening in about six weeks (around 2026-11-25). Hour figures are my estimates.

## Why the old order fails

- The on-site table can't exist online.
- The live podcast recording is the most expensive item, at roughly 15-25 volunteer hours for scheduling, tech rehearsal, moderation and promotion. It also reaches only the partner's listeners after the fact.
- The old order ranked by in-person prestige, but you now need registrations per volunteer hour.

## New ranking

| # | Channel | Est. effort | Why here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Newsletter swaps** | ~1 h per partner, one blurb, one tracked link | Closest to a signup click and cheapest per registration. Issues are booked weeks ahead, so start first. |
| 2 | **Calendar listings** | ~3 h once | Near-zero effort and durable. It reaches people actively searching for events. Reach per listing is modest. |
| 3 | **Podcast exchange** (recorded guest spots, no live event) | ~3-4 h per episode | High trust, but long lead times. Book now so episodes land around ticket opening. Cap at 2 episodes. |
| 4 | **Logo-and-social swaps** | ~3 h for a partner kit, then ~15 min per partner | Cheap but low conversion. Ship it as a kit with every partnership, not as a separate campaign. |
| 5 | **Full media-partner tier with live recording** | 15-25 h | **Retired as a tier.** Revisit only if a volunteer asks to own it after tickets open. |

## Replace the old tier with one simple offer

Every partner gets the same default package: a calendar listing, one newsletter swap and the social kit. A podcast guest spot is opt-in, limited to the first two partners who confirm. Nobody gets a table or a live session. You can drop the tier language, which also removes any pricing or benefits-matrix work.

## Six-week sequence

1. **Weeks 1-2:** Publish the calendar listings. Build the partner kit (blurbs, logo, dates, tracked links). Pitch newsletters, targeting 6-8, since issue slots book out.
2. **Weeks 3-4:** Pitch and book the two podcast guest spots. Confirm newsletter issue dates to land at or just after ticket opening.
3. **Week 5:** Send partners the final kit with the exact ticket-open date and a reminder to schedule.
4. **Week 6 and on-sale:** Run the swaps. Post the logo-and-social swaps on the same day.

## Measure it

- Give each partner a unique UTM link and, if your ticketing tool supports it, a unique discount or referral code.
- After two weeks of sales, rank partners by registrations per hour spent and put your next outreach into the top performers.
- Total planned volunteer time is about 30-40 h across five people, roughly 6-8 h each.

## One decision for you

Last year's partners may have been promised tables or a recording slot. Tell them now that the event is online-only and offer the default package instead. Do it before the pitching starts, so nobody is surprised at ticket opening.
