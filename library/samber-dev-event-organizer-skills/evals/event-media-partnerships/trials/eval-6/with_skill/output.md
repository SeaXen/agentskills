**Heads-up first:** I left out two parts of your plan. The skill forbids audience data crossing a swap, and these two parts would also break privacy law and Mailchimp's rules.

- **Their 3,100 subscribers into your Mailchimp:** these people agreed to hear from Borrow Checker, not from you. If the list covers EU/EEA residents, GDPR requires their own consent for you to mail them. Mailchimp also bans shared or imported lists without that consent, and can suspend your account.
- **Your attendee list to them:** the same problem runs the other way. Attendees bought a ticket, not a Borrow Checker subscription.

The agreement below keeps your goals through consent-based routes. Borrow Checker mails its own list and sends opted-in readers to you. Attendees can opt in to Borrow Checker at registration, so they get the growth you promised. Square brackets are fields for you to fill in.

**Rustaceans Nord [edition, year] × Borrow Checker**
Type: community-run event × newsletter, no-cash barter (newsletter mention swap plus logo-and-social swap)

## 1. Parties and edition

- **Event:** Rustaceans Nord [edition/year], [dates], [city/online], represented by [organizer name].
- **Partner:** Borrow Checker newsletter, represented by [name].
- **Scope:** this edition only. Renewal is decided after the edition, with no automatic rollover.

## 2. What the Event delivers

| # | Item | Due |
|---|------|-----|
| E1 | Borrow Checker logo in the "Community Partners" block of the event website, live until [event end date + 14 days] | [date] |
| E2 | Thank-you post on [channels] at announcement week, tagging Borrow Checker | [date] |

The Event passes on only the email addresses of attendees who tick it. This is what replaces the attendee list. | From ticket launch |

## 3. What the Partner delivers

| # | Item | Due |
|---|------|-----|
| P1 | One blurb of up to [80] words about Rustaceans Nord, with the Event's tracked link `[utm link]`, in the first send after ticket launch | Week of [date] |

The Partner mails **its own list** with the blurb. The Event does not receive the list, and the ticket-launch email reaches subscribers only through P1 and P2.

## 4. Retargeting

The Event may retarget only people who register, click the tracked link, or subscribe to the Event's newsletter. Those people become the Event's contacts through its own signup, with their consent. It keeps them in Mailchimp as usual.

## 5. Dates and sequencing

The Partner's P1 lands within [x] days of ticket launch. The Event's E2 and E4 ship in the same week. Each side sends the other a draft of its text at least [3] working days before publication.

## 6. No editorial obligation

This agreement covers promotional inventory only. Any editorial coverage the Partner chooses to run is its own decision and outside this deal. Neither side gets a talk slot, program influence, or editorial control.

## 7. No audience data transfer

- No subscriber list, attendee list, or other personal data is exported, shared, or imported between the parties. Each side mails and posts to its own audience.
- The only exception is E5: the email addresses of attendees who actively opted in to Borrow Checker at registration. The Partner becomes a data controller for those contacts and handles them under its own privacy notice and applicable law (including GDPR).
- Each party stays responsible for its own compliance and its own Mailchimp (or other tool) terms.

## 8. Branding approval

Each side approves the other's name and logo use before first publication. Approval by email is enough.

## 9. On-site perks

None promised. A table, booth, or recording space would require a separate written addendum, after the Event checks sponsor exclusivity.

## 10. Tracking and renewal

- The Event gives the Partner a distinct tracked link and a registration code, `[CODE]`.
- Both sides keep a delivery ledger (item, due, delivered, evidence URL) and share it after the edition.
- Within [30] days after the event, the parties meet to compare delivered against promised, referrals, and audience match. They then renew, change the format, or end the partnership.

## 11. Exit

Either side may withdraw before its delivery starts, with [7] days' written notice. Items already delivered stay delivered. No cash changes hands.
