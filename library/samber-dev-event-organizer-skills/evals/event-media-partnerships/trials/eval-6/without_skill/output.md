# Partnership and Audience Exchange Agreement

**Between**
**Rustaceans Nord**, [legal form, registered address, contact] ("**Rustaceans Nord**"),
and
**Borrow Checker**, [legal form, registered address, contact] ("**Borrow Checker**").

Effective date: [date]. Each is a "Party".

⚠️ **One change from your plan.** The plan was to export all 3,100 Borrow Checker subscribers into your Mailchimp and email and retarget them directly. I did not write it that way. Under GDPR and the ePrivacy rules, a newsletter list can't be handed to a third party for the third party's own marketing unless each subscriber agreed to that sharing. A swap between two organizers almost never has that consent. If it were done anyway:
- Mailchimp can suspend your account.
- The data protection authority can fine both parties.
- Borrow Checker's subscribers would be getting mail they never asked for.

The agreement below keeps every benefit you wanted. Rustaceans Nord still gets the blurb, the thank-you post and a path to those subscribers, and Borrow Checker still gets the attendee list. The difference is that people move onto the other party's list through opt-in, not through export. Clause 3 also allows a direct list transfer for subscribers who gave consent to partner offers.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Parties promote each other's audiences. Borrow Checker promotes the Rustaceans Nord event [event name, date, venue] (the "**Event**") to its subscribers. Rustaceans Nord thanks Borrow Checker publicly and gives it access to Event attendees who agree to it.

## 2. Promotion commitments

**2.1 Borrow Checker will:**
- (a) Send one blurb about the Event to its subscriber list, in one issue, by [date or window]. Rustaceans Nord supplies the text, the logo and a tracked ticket link with UTM parameters.
- (b) Place the blurb [position, e.g. above the fold or in the sponsor-style slot] and label it as a partner message.
- (c) Include in the blurb a clear sign-up link ("Get ticket-launch news from Rustaceans Nord") that leads to a Rustaceans Nord Mailchimp form with an unchecked consent box.
- (d) Send the blurb to the whole list, not a reduced segment.

**2.2 Rustaceans Nord will:**
- (a) Publish a thank-you post naming and linking Borrow Checker on [channels, e.g. website, LinkedIn, Mastodon] by [date].
- (b) Keep the credit visible on the Event page until [date, e.g. 30 days after the Event].
- (c) Share the click and sign-up counts from its side within 14 days of the blurb, so both Parties can compare results.

**2.3** Each Party approves the other's text before publication. Silence for 3 business days counts as approval.

## 3. Subscriber access (Borrow Checker to Rustaceans Nord)

**3.1 Default route: opt-in.** Subscribers reach Rustaceans Nord's Mailchimp audience only by completing the sign-up form in clause 2.1(c). Rustaceans Nord uses double opt-in and stores proof of consent (timestamp, source, wording shown).

**3.2 Optional route: consented list transfer.** Borrow Checker may export only subscribers who, at sign-up, explicitly agreed to receive offers from selected partners or event organizers. Before any export:
- (a) Borrow Checker gives Rustaceans Nord the consent wording and a count of eligible subscribers.
- (b) Rustaceans Nord confirms the wording is sufficient. If it isn't, there is no export and route 3.1 applies.
- (c) The export contains email address and first name only, and is sent by encrypted transfer.
- (d) Rustaceans Nord's first email to these contacts states who it is, why they are receiving it and how they were reached. It includes a one-click unsubscribe and a link to its privacy notice.

**3.3 Subscribers outside the consented set.** Borrow Checker's non-consenting subscribers stay Borrow Checker's. Rustaceans Nord will not receive their data and will not email them, except through clause 3.1.

**3.4 Retargeting.** Rustaceans Nord may retarget only people who joined through 3.1 or 3.2 and who accepted advertising or personalisation in the consent wording. It will not upload the list to advertising platforms (custom audiences, lookalikes) without that specific consent.

## 4. Attendee access (Rustaceans Nord to Borrow Checker)

**4.1** The Event registration form includes an unchecked box, worded in substance: "Share my name and email with Borrow Checker so it can send me its newsletter." The wording is approved by Borrow Checker before registration opens.

**4.2** Within [14] days after the Event, Rustaceans Nord sends Borrow Checker the contact details (name, email) of attendees who ticked that box. It excludes all others, and tells Borrow Checker how many attendees did and didn't consent.

**4.3** Borrow Checker's first email to these contacts states the source and includes a one-click unsubscribe. It then adds them to its list. If Borrow Checker adds them without a double opt-in confirmation, it keeps the consent record.

**4.4** Both Parties apply the same rules to list-based contact: the rules in clauses 3.2(d) and 5 apply to Borrow Checker as to Rustaceans Nord.

## 5. Data protection

**5.1 Roles.** For shared contact data, each Party is an independent controller of the data it receives. Neither is the other's processor.

**5.2 Obligations of the receiving Party:**
- (a) Use the data only for the stated purpose: the Event for Rustaceans Nord, the newsletter for Borrow Checker.
- (b) Not sell, rent or pass the data on to third parties, apart from processors such as Mailchimp under a data processing agreement.
- (c) Honor unsubscribe and erasure requests within 30 days, and tell the other Party when a request concerns data that came from it.
- (d) Provide the information required by GDPR Art. 14 at the first contact and within one month at the latest.
- (e) Keep the data secure and restrict access to people who need it.
- (f) Notify the other Party within 48 hours of discovering a breach that affects shared data.
- (g) Delete the data, or any contact who never confirmed, after [12] months of no engagement.

**5.3** Each Party may rely on the other's consent records only if it holds a copy of the consent wording and timestamp for the contacts transferred.

**5.4** If a regulator or a data subject challenges a transfer, the Parties cooperate in good faith and suspend further transfers until resolved.

## 6. Costs and exclusivity

Each Party bears its own costs. No fee is due either way. The swap is non-exclusive. The Parties can run similar arrangements with others.

## 7. Intellectual property

Each Party keeps its names, logos and content. Each grants the other a limited, non-exclusive licence to use them for this agreement only, for its duration.

## 8. Liability

Each Party is liable for its own breach of clause 5 and of data protection law as to the data it handles. Neither guarantees the number of clicks, sign-ups or ticket sales. Neither is liable for indirect loss. Nothing here limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

## 9. Term and termination

The agreement runs from the effective date until [date, e.g. 60 days after the Event] and ends then. Either Party may end it earlier on written notice (email suffices) if the other breaches it and does not fix the breach within 7 days. Clauses 5, 7 and 8 survive termination.

- No partnership or agency is created.

## Before you send it

1. ❌ **Ask Borrow Checker what its sign-up form said.** This decides whether clause 3.2 applies or whether clause 3.1 alone does the job.
2. ✅ **Update your registration form** with the clause 4.1 consent box before ticket sales open. Otherwise you will have nothing to hand over after the Event.
3. ✅ **Fill in the bracketed items:** legal entities, dates, event details, governing law.
4. ⚠️ **Get a lawyer to look at it.** It is a working draft, not legal advice. A short review is cheap compared with a Mailchimp suspension or a regulator complaint.
5. ℹ️ **Opt-in does reach most of the list.** Expect a fraction of the 3,100 to sign up, but those who do are people who will open your ticket-launch mail and buy.
