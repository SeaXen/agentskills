## Next steps

1. **Backend Monthly.** Ask for the same two blurbs plus a third slot, such as an early-bird or last-chance slot. Keep the tracked link. Check the price before you commit.
2. **Elixir Bits.** Ask for the missing mention now, before you decide on renewal. Pay for 2026 only if you owe a balance, and tie any 2027 deal to delivery.
3. **Discord.** Don't renew. If you want to stay in touch, post in the community yourself instead of buying a slot.
4. **Local news site.** If the calendar listing is free, keep it but rewrite it for senior engineers and put the ticket price in the first line. Don't pay for anything there.

## Gaps in the data

- **Registrations aren't paid tickets.** Check how many of the 41 and the 9 became paid attendees. Renewal is only solid for Backend Monthly if those 41 paid. If many were comps or dropouts, the comparison changes.
- **I don't know what each partner cost.** Cost per paid attendee is the real metric. If Elixir Bits was cheap, the case for renewing it gets stronger.
- **Check whether the Discord link was tracked.** Two zeros in a row suggest no clicks, but confirm the link worked before you write it off.
- **Elixir Bits is a niche audience.** Their 9 may be a topic-fit problem rather than a weak channel. If DeltaLoop had little Elixir content, that explains it.
