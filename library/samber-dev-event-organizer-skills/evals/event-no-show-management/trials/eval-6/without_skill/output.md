# Ceiling for Brightwater Tech Day

**Set the room ceiling at 250 people at once, standing included. Cap registration at about 450, not 700.**

## Why 250 and not 268 or 290

- **The contract is the binding limit.** Going over 250 breaches the venue contract, even though the fire certificate allows 268. The venue can stop the event or charge you, and its insurer may refuse to cover an incident above contract capacity.
- **268 is a legal ceiling, not a target.** Treat it as your margin for error, not extra seats.
- **Standing people count.** Fire certificates count everyone in the room, so the rear area doesn't add capacity. 250 plus 40 standing is 290, which is over the legal limit. If an incident happened, the organizers would be personally exposed.
- **Your 240 was never counted.** You may have been over 250 at peak without knowing it. You need a real count this time.

## Why 450 and not 700

Free events usually see 40–60% of registrants show up. That's why 240 showed last time, but I don't know how many people had registered then.

- Your ceiling is 250 ÷ your show rate. At 55%, that's about 450.
- At 700 registrations, you'd get roughly 280–420 people for a 250-person room. You would be turning people away at the door, which is bad for a free community event.
- "Let whoever turns up come in" fails the moment 300 turn up. At that point you're either over the limit or refusing people in a queue.

## What to do

1. **Pull the registered-vs-attended numbers from the last two editions.** Show rate = attended ÷ registered. The registration cap is 250 ÷ that rate, rounded down. I assumed 55% above, so replace it with your real number.
2. **Close registration at the cap and open a waitlist.** Release waitlist spots automatically when people cancel.
3. **Send a reminder 48 hours before** asking people to confirm or cancel. This lowers no-shows and frees spots.
4. **Count at the door.** Use a clicker or a check-in app, with one person whose only job is the count. Stop admitting at 250. If a session is full, send people to the overflow area.
5. **Add an overflow option.** Use a second room or a screen in the foyer, or a livestream. Late arrivals still feel included.
6. **If you want a bigger room, get it in writing.** Ask the venue to amend the contract to 268, with the fire certificate attached. Don't go above that.

"We can register more people than we have seats, because free events always have no-shows. We can't let more than 250 in at once. That's the contract limit, and standing people count toward the fire limit too. I'd cap registration at about 450, run a waitlist, and count at the door."
