## Which number to use

The board's 50% rule gives the same ceiling: 300 / 0.5 = 600. So the ceiling is not the disagreement. The disagreement is what you hand to catering and what you plan the room against.

- **Ceiling (the cap):** 600. This is the sourced 2x rule, and your own data agrees with it.
- **Planning and catering (the forecast):** your measured 47.4%. Two editions in the same venue and format, 0.3 points apart, beat an industry average for your event, and only for your event.
- **Catering gets 285, not 300 and never 600.**
  - 285 × 22 EUR = 6,270 EUR.
  - 300 × 22 EUR = 6,600 EUR, so the 50% rule over-orders by about 330 EUR.
  - The registration count of 600 would be 13,200 EUR, which is more than double what is eaten.
- **State the buffer separately:** 600 registered, 285 expected, 315 no-show buffer.
- **Do not average 47.4% and 50%.** Report each with its scope: yours as the forecast, 50% as the cap.

Tell the board: "We close at 600 exactly as the rule says. We forecast with our own two editions, 47.4%, because it saves about 330 EUR and tells us the real room size."

## Occupancy gate (not tradeable)

- **Arrival assumption:** the show-up rate stays at or below 50%. That is the breakeven, because 600 × 0.50 = 300 = capacity.
