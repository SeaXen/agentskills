# Wednesday evening: sanity check and social programme

## Verdict

⚠️ Not fine as it stands. Each event passes its organiser's sign-off, but together they break your accessibility page, and the post would publish that breach.

| | Hotel bar (ground) | Karaoke (2nd floor) | Pizza social (mezzanine) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Step-free | ✅ | ❌ stairs only | ❌ steps |
| Alcohol-free | ❌ beer on tap | ✅ | ❌ beer available |
| Family-inclusive | ❌ probably a bar with no kids (please confirm) | not stated | ✅ |

Each commitment is met by a different event, and nothing covers all three:

1. **Step-free to every official space.** Two of the three spaces fail it outright. This is a plain contradiction of the page, not a matter of interpretation.
2. **Alcohol-free option.** The only alcohol-free space is the one reachable by stairs. A wheelchair user has no step-free evening without beer.
3. **Family-inclusive programming.** The only family event is up steps. A parent with a pushchair or a wheelchair can't attend.

Bar plus pizza plus karaoke looks like three options. For anyone who needs step-free access, it is one option: the beer bar.

## Fixes, cheapest first

Pick one per gap. All of them depend on the hotel.

1. **Mezzanine steps.** Ask the hotel for a ramp or a portable platform lift for the short flight. Check the gradient (about 1:12 or gentler), fire-exit rules, and the width for a wheelchair and a pushchair.
2. **Karaoke.** Move it to a ground-floor room, or swap it with the pizza social if the mezzanine can't be fixed. Karaoke's alcohol-free bar then becomes the step-free alcohol-free option.
3. **Alcohol-free option on the ground floor.** If neither of those works, set up an alcohol-free zone in the hotel bar. It needs separate tables, a mocktail menu, and a clear sign. It must be a real space, not a soft drink behind a beer tap.
4. **Pizza social with beer.** Optional but worth considering: put the beer at a separate station away from the kids' tables. It's not a breach, but "alcohol-free option" reads better if the family event is clearly usable by people who avoid alcohol.

If none of these is confirmed by Friday, change the accessibility page before posting. Don't leave the commitment live while the programme contradicts it.
