## Ranking of your three options [hospitality]

Hospitality ranks the formats on separate axes, so the order depends on what you optimise.

| Option | Effort (high to low) | Value | Efficiency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mixer at the venue | 3rd | **1st** | **1st** |

- **Default pick: venue mixer.** It leads on value and on efficiency.
- **Doing nothing** is a legitimate rung. It sits above the brewery trip on efficiency and below it on value.

### What each one serves

- **Venue mixer**
  - It serves the whole badge-holding audience and gets people talking to people they didn't arrive with.
  - Nobody travels, pays, or needs a seat, and the room, licence question and staff already exist.
  - Hospitality's guidance caps it at about two hours.
- **Dinner with a programme**
  - It serves depth per person: speakers, sponsors or a thematic thread.
  - Promote it when the room seats all 300, or when a sponsor buys the evening.
  - A seat count excludes anyone beyond it, and it costs a named owner's evening.
- **Brewery coach trip**
  - It serves a memorable, signature moment for whoever boards.
  - It leaves most of the audience unhosted, and it spends transport, another building's rules and a headcount you can't fix once the coaches leave.
  - Promote it only if it becomes the event's signature and all 300 fit.
  - A brewery is also an alcohol venue, so it collides with whatever posture you set.
- **Nothing**
  - It serves the cheapest outcome, and it's fine if you publish it.
  - "Let people find a bar on their own" with no announcement is **deleted** from the menu. That's the unhosted drift, with no owner and the code of conduct at its weakest. Publish "the day ends at the last session" early, so people can plan.
