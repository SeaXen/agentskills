**Don't post the six-option list on Monday.** The skill (`event-official-social-program`) rules out your plan in three places. I couldn't run its interview, so the copy below is built around what I don't know.

## Why the plan fails the skill

1. **Six options is not more inclusive by default.** The skill deletes the rung "a list of options with no statement of which one clears the access floor". The floor is that every hour offering anything has at least one option that clears the published access set. Pool night, city walk and escape room are the likeliest to exclude someone, and the venue details aren't known yet. A long list isn't an access provision.
2. **Publishing before the access page is a named failure mode.** People plan around the slate the moment it appears. A later statement only reaches whoever is still deciding.
3. **Quietly dropping two reads as a cancellation.** The skill says a slate published and then shortened reads as one. Anyone who arranged care, travel or time off for a dropped activity is hurt, and they are the people the floor exists for.
4. **Six activities is a themed track.** That rung needs all three of these:
   - an owner per activity,
   - access commitments that no single format can carry,
   - a recurring edition.

   If any fails, you drop to two occasions on separate slots, or to one.

## Questions I couldn't ask

- Do the six run in the same hours or on separate slots? Overlap is what creates the floor problem.
- Who owns each activity?
- What did the accessibility bar publish, at what depth? Which options are step-free, alcohol-free, family-inclusive?
- Does Braylin recur?
- By what date must each venue be held?
