**Two changes to the plan, which the copy below already reflects:**

- **Signups before the access details.** If people pick activities before the a11y page exists, the ones who most need that detail can't choose, or choose wrong. The copy has Monday collect interest only, and the a11y page is the signal to confirm.
- **Quietly dropping two.** Attendees who signed up for those two will notice, and a quiet lounge or city walk is often the pick of people who need a quieter or lower-effort option. The copy states the threshold up front, so a cut is an announced rule rather than a quiet one.
