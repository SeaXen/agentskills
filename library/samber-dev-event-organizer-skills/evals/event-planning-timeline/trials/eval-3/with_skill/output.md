# NordStack Summit: hold 6 February, cut scope at dated checkpoints

I read the event-planning-timeline skill and both its references. Neither option as framed is right.

Two assumptions to check first:
- **Timeline.** Today is 14 Oct, which makes 6 Feb about 16 weeks away, not 8. I used your "8 weeks", written as S-8 (S = weeks before doors-open). If it really is 16, the same plan runs with more slack.

## Verdict

1. **Do not slide to March.** The date has been public since September. The skill treats an announced date as a dead lever: "the date holds and scope moves." Moving it burns trust with speakers, sponsors and ticket holders. That costs most if this is an annual event.
2. **Do not cancel the Thursday workshop day today.** The board is right about the type of lever: scope, not date. It is wrong about the timing. Cutting now is an argued-in-crisis decision with no condition behind it.
3. **Turn the workshop day into the first rung of a scope ladder.** It downgrades on a dated checkpoint, driven by sponsor revenue actually signed.
4. **Hold every Thursday-only commitment open** until its latest-safe date (see the list below).

**Why the slide fails:**
- The program problem is a few weeks of selection work. A month of extra calendar does not fix it.
- Sponsors who budget by fiscal year will not suddenly find money in March.
- You would trade a visible problem (40% sponsorship) for a worse one (an event that visibly can't hold its date).
- A slide is only defensible if a foundation breaks, such as losing the venue. Nothing you described does that.

**Why the workshop cut is probably still right, later.** Gate logic says to cut the item whose removal unblocks the most downstream work. A Thursday day is a second venue day, instructors, catering and staffing for four people. It is the best candidate for the first downgrade, but it should fire at a checkpoint.

## The scope ladder (Good / Better / Best)

You need numbers from your budget before 18 Dec. Cost these three tiers (the `event-budget` skill covers this):

- **Best** (today): two tracks plus the Thursday workshop day.
- **Better:** two tracks, no Thursday. The 2–3 strongest workshops fold into a main-event slot.
- **Good:** the floor below which it doesn't run. For example, fewer or merged sessions, and simpler catering and swag.

Thresholds are self-set from your cost floor and signed revenue. I have not invented figures.

## What would change this

- **Workshops are ticketed and sold well.** Then cutting them loses real revenue, so raise the downgrade threshold or cut something else first.
- **The venue contract has a penalty clause.** Read the cancellation terms before any Thursday decision.
- **The venue is lost, or sponsors pull out en masse.** A foundation has broken. Re-run the replan, and only then is a date move on the table.
- **Sponsorship is a demand problem, not a timing problem.** If prospects say no to the pitch itself, a later date won't help either.
