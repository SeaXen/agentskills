## 1. Decision first: Monday's announcement is a no-go unless three gates close by Friday

The sourced hard rule is that you don't announce a date until you can handle money. Payment infrastructure doesn't exist yet. The code of conduct (CoC) is also a prerequisite for the first public listing, and it is still a Google Doc draft.

1. Close three gates by **Fri 16 Oct**:

   - The CoC is published at a public URL with a named reporting contact.

2. All three closed by Friday: announce Mon 19 Oct.
3. Any one still open: announce Mon 26 Oct. Re-check Fri 23 Oct. Hard stop Mon 2 Nov, then fire the replan (checkpoint C0).

A one- or two-week slip costs little against a 190-day runway. A paid-ticket or sponsor-invoice failure after announcing is the failure the rule exists for.

Two things to settle before any announcement, because announcing ends the date as a variable:

- **Is 22 April final?** You mention a competing "weekend," but 22 April 2027 is a Thursday. Confirm the year and whether the event runs into the weekend. If the overlap with the meetup's audience is real, that is an `event-date-selection` question. Decide it before announcing, not after.
- **A flag-planting option I'm adding, not from the skill:** if the gates slip, post a season-only teaser ("2nd edition, spring 2027, details soon") with no date. It claims nothing you can't back and doesn't spend the date lever.

## 3. Method choices

- **Granularity: dependency-gated milestones.** Task-level critical path is deleted from the menu. Its weekly upkeep exceeds volunteer hours, and it goes stale inside a month. Use the simplest shared tracker the team will actually update.
- **Buffers: scope-tier ladder first, then delayed commitment.** The ladder is promoted because 80% of the budget is unbooked revenue (Q10). No uniform milestone padding. Slack appears only on irreversible commitments, as latest-safe dates.
- **Replanning: pre-written checkpoints.** The cadence variance review is deleted because there is no standing meeting.
- **Date policy:** after announcement the date holds and scope moves.
- **Dead zone:** no milestones between 18 Dec and 4 Jan. This is my suggestion.
- **Task phrasing:** every task below is a finished state. Buckets (S-n) are weeks before doors-open, so next year's plan reuses them.
