# Foundry Jam: milestone walkthrough

**Two caveats first.**
- I can't see the 9-month timeline you found, so the milestones below are my own reconstruction for a 48-hour hardware hackathon. Paste the original and I'll map it phase by phase.
- Today is 2026-10-14, so you probably don't need to compress much. From today you have about 31 weeks to mid-May 2027 and about 35 weeks to mid-June. A 9-month plan is about 39 weeks, so you're cutting 10–20%, not 50%.

**Next action:** ask the university for the decision deadline, then plan backward from the earliest candidate weekend.

## How to plan with an unknown date

1. Set T-0 to the earliest candidate weekend (a May one). Every date-independent task runs on that clock.
2. If the university grants a later weekend, you gain slack and lose nothing.
3. Date-dependent tasks wait for confirmation: venue contract, public announcement, and sponsor deadlines. Hold them at "ready to send" until the date is confirmed.
4. Ask for a decision deadline in writing. Without a firm date, don't plan around anything later than roughly T-24 weeks (late Nov for a mid-May event).

## What compresses and what doesn't

- **Can shrink:** brand and website, outreach, and run-of-show planning. These depend on effort, not on other people's lead times.
- **Cannot shrink:** the venue answer, insurance, sponsor cycles (usually 6–12 weeks per sponsor), and hardware shipping.
- **The June weekend is the safest option** because it gives you extra buffer. A May weekend is tighter on exam periods, so check the academic calendar. Finals could hurt turnout and volunteer availability.
