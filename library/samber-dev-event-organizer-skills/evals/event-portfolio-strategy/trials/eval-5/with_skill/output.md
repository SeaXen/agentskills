## Opening line

**"Bundled partnerships are pretty much the standard now for event families" doesn't land.**

- It's untested. Co-located conferences share a venue, a week and a sponsor pool, but that is different from one deal written across several properties. Neither organizers selling such a bundle nor sponsors asking for one is established practice.
- A sponsor who tests the claim against their other sponsorships will find it hollow. A false anchor also sets a price you may not be able to sustain.

Describe it as new instead. For example: "You already back the conference and the meetup separately. We'd like to try one agreement covering both, plus the podcast, for 2027. If it doesn't suit you, per-property pricing stands."
