# Verdict: not as it stands

Don't give both venues a yes on the current shape. The attendee benefit is real, but your crew is the constraint, and four of your six already told you the answer.

## Why the shape fails

- **Same people, no recovery.** The four who said "never again" are two-thirds of your crew. If they run both again, expect them to quit, to burn out, or to do the second event badly.
- **Saturday night is the weak point.** Conference teardown, a trip across town, and hackathon setup all land in the same evening. A hackathon also usually runs long, so nobody gets to sleep.
- **Two venues means two of everything.** Each venue has its own load-in, wayfinding, registration, AV, catering and venue contact. Nothing is shared except the people.
- **The travel benefit is thinner than it looks.** Visitors do save a trip. But the conference crowd and the hackathon crowd probably overlap less than you hope, and your crew pays full cost for that overlap.

## Check these first (5 minutes)

1. **What exactly made last year "never again"?** If it was the overnight hackathon, the cross-town move, or the lack of hands, each has a different fix. If nobody can say, don't commit.
2. **How many attendees do you expect to do both?** If it's under about a quarter, the "one trip" benefit doesn't justify the load on the crew.
3. **Can you get more hands by the deadline?** Volunteers, paid event staff, or venue staff covering registration and AV.

## What would make a yes safe

Say yes to both only if you can commit to all of these:

- **Two separate leads with separate teams.** No one works both events, except one shared floating coordinator.
- **A reduced second day.** HackHydro runs in daytime hours or has a fixed finish, with no all-nighter for staff.
- **No Saturday-night setup for the same people.** Pre-build HackHydro on Friday, or hand setup to a different team or the university.
- **Venue support in writing.** The university provides its own AV, registration desk and security.
- **A shuttle or a clear transit plan.** Otherwise the cross-town gap costs you attendees as well as crew.

## Recommended answer

- **If you can meet the conditions above:** yes to both.
- **If you can't:** yes to one venue and a "not this year" to the other. Keep whichever event has the bigger audience or revenue. Ask the other venue for a different weekend or an option to rebook.
- **If you can't decide by the deadline:** a conditional yes to one venue and a request to the other for a short extension is better than two yeses you may have to break.
