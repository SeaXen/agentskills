## Build list

1. **Shared mailbox `press@` (20 min).**
   - Rota owner per week.
   - Reply within 2 working days.
   - Auto-reply states the written-only policy.
2. **Press page (3 h).** Public, no form.
   - Three descriptions: 25, 80 and 200 words.
   - Facts: dates, city, venue, number of tracks.
   - **Define "about 700"**: registered or attended, and which year. Reporters check this.
   - Logos and 6-10 photos from last year, with licence stated.
   - Links to last year's two articles.
   - Code of conduct link and incident contact.
   - The quote policy in plain words.
   - The mailbox and your response time.
3. **Accreditation (1 h, opens with the programme, not with the CFP).**
   - Form fields: name, outlet or channel, URL, two sample links, what they will cover.
   - Criteria: staff or freelancer with a commissioned or published byline. Bloggers and creators are judged case by case.
   - Deal: free pass. No copy approval, no coverage obligation.
   - Rules: ask attendees before filming them and respect the no-photo lanyard.
   - Deadline 2 weeks before the event, answer within 3 working days, cap about 10.
4. **Press desk instead of a press room (1 h plan).**
   - A quiet table with power and wifi.
   - A printed one-pager.
   - A distinct lanyard colour.
   - A volunteer hosts but never speaks on record. They point reporters to `press@`.
   - A no-photo lanyard option for attendees.
5. **Crisis kit (1.5 h). This is the part that protects you.**
   - One decision-maker, with a named backup.
   - Holding statement template: what happened, what you are doing, when you will update.
   - Code of conduct incident path. Reporters go to the mailbox, never to individuals.
   - Rule: no one replies on social media, in any thread, until the decision-maker approves.
