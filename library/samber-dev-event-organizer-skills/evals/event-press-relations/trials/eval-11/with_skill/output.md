# TerraformDays (3rd edition, 1,400 attendees): press brief and KPI sheet

## 1. What I can't give the board

⚠️ **I can't give an honest forecast of article count or of the percentage of ticket sales that press will drive.**

- **No published figure exists.** The skill I followed (`event-press-relations`) says no published figure quantifies what coverage volume does to registrations. It states none rather than substituting a proxy.
- **Any number would be invented.** Whatever I wrote would be presented to the board as a forecast, and it would have nothing behind it.

- **A spend ceiling can't depend on a number nobody can source.** Section 3 shows what the spend buys.

## 2. Posture (Menu A)

**Recommended: dedicated press function.** This rests on a rule, but I haven't run the interview, so confirm it.

- **Rule:** the skill promotes to this rung above roughly 500–1,000 attendees (a self-set band, not a measured threshold), or when sponsors make their own product news. 1,400 is past the band.
- **Cost:** this rung is top of effort and compliance cost.
- **Deleted if Q8 is "nobody will be the single voice on the record."** Without a spokesperson this rung must not exist.
- **Opt-out wins first.** If Q1 names no outlet that covers this subject at this scale, and Q2 says registration meets its curve without coverage, the answer is no press function. Then there is no press spend to approve.

2. Is registration on its curve without coverage?
3. Is the event community-run or vendor-run? Will sponsors announce products there?
4. How many days, and is there a spare room? In-person, hybrid or online?
5. When is the lineup reveal, and is anything in it genuinely news (a name, a first, a format change)?
6. How many comped seats can you spare, and who is the single voice?
7. Has anyone been refused a press pass before?
8. Is the event ticketed and capped?

## 3. What press spend actually buys

- **Buys:** organizer hours, comped seats, a media page, photography and B-roll, a press corner, and a briefed spokesperson.
- **Doesn't buy:** articles. Anything promised in return for words (a discount, a speaking slot, an exclusive) is paid editorial. That belongs to `event-media-partnerships` and is priced honestly. Don't put it in a press line.

Components, in the order to build them:

| # | Item | Spend type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ungated media page: fact sheet, frozen boilerplate, logo pack, past-edition stats, one named contact | Hours | Cheapest version is my judgement call, not a published minimum |
| 2 | Two-lane accreditation policy with published bar, caps and refusal path | Hours | Creator bar must be at or above the press bar; set it yourself |
| 3 | Photo and B-roll from this edition | Hours or freelancer | Feeds next year's page |
| 4 | Lineup-reveal press moment, with early or exclusive offer to one outlet | Hours | The single highest-leverage beat of the year |
| 5 | Comped press and creator seats | Seats | The number is the constraint, not the policy text |
| 6 | Press corner or room, plus a written recording rule | Floor space | Past the band, consider a staffed room |
| 7 | Spokesperson plus backup, three cold lines | Hours | Zero cost, and it prevents the irreversible failure |

Gated wire-service distribution is not needed unless sponsors embargo their own news. If it's community-run with no sponsor news, that rung is deleted.

## 4. KPI sheet

Every row is **self-set** or **binary**. Nothing is a published benchmark. Fix the targets before the edition, never after the data arrives.

| # | KPI | Type | Target | How measured | Board action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lineup-beat coverage: outlets pitched vs outlets that published | Self-set | **TBD, set from the edition 2 baseline (below)** | Pitch log vs coverage log | Judge the year on this row |
| 2 | Beat coverage for the other beats (CFP, programme, on-sale, wrap-up): pitched vs published | Self-set | TBD, same method | Same log | Drop beats that never publish |
| 3 | Published-vs-accredited: of all credentialed, how many published anything | Self-set | TBD, same method | Credential list vs coverage log | Input to next year's comp allocation |
| 4 | Embargo integrity | Binary | **0 breaks** before the stated lift time | Check each embargoed item | Any break resets trust with that outlet |
| 5 | Consent breaches | Binary | **0** published images or recordings of anyone who used the opt-out | Review press output against opt-out list | Withdraw credential on breach |
| 6 | Voice discipline | Binary | **0** on-record statements from anyone but the spokesperson and backup | Incident and press log | Re-brief team |
| 7 | Refusals handled against a published criterion | Binary | **100%** refused in writing with the criterion quoted | Refusal log | Fix the policy, not the applicant |
| 8 | Media page live before the first pitch | Binary | Yes | Date check | Gate for all pitching |

Rows 1–3: "TBD" is deliberate.

- **Why TBD:** I won't invent counts. The board sets them from a baseline.
- **Baseline method:** count editions 1 and 2 retroactively. For each edition, list who published, on which beat, and whether it was earned or traded.
- **Starting target:** use "no regression vs edition 2" until a baseline exists. That commits the team to something it can actually control.
- **Strict reading of the skill:** the skill lists only the beat-coverage and published-vs-accredited ratios as count-type signals. Treat any absolute article count as a report, not a promise.

### Ticket-sales effect: measure, don't forecast

- This is outside the skill's own measurement list, so it is my addition and it has no target.
- **Instrument:** a "how did you hear about us" field and UTM links on press-origin URLs.
- **Reading:** report it after the edition as an observation. It will undercount, because press effects are indirect.
- **Don't:** state a percentage in advance, and don't read a number from other events across.

## 5. Beat calendar

Lead times are from real events' own cycles, not industry averages. Anchor to the edition date and fill in the days.

| Beat | Lead time | Who covers it | Work it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| CFP opens | ~8–12 months out | Niche and developer press | Only if genuinely news |
| **Lineup reveal** | ~2–4 months out | Trade and developer press | **Yes, a dedicated press moment** |
| Full programme | ~6–12 weeks out | Trade and developer press | Bundle with the lineup if close |
| On-sale or early bird | Often 5–7 months out | Local and trade press | Give it a hook, such as first-N tickets or a dated cutoff |
| Day-of | n/a | All tiers, including creators | Press corner and recording rule |
| Wrap-up and stats | Days to ~2 weeks after | Trade and local business press | Feeds next year's fact sheet |

**Pitch rules**

- **Lead with what changed.** A schedule page going live is a publication date, not news.
- **Attach the fact sheet and boilerplate.**
- **State the embargo lift with a timezone, or say there is none.**
- **Name one contact.**
- **Send it once.**

## 6. Accreditation policy outline

- **Press lane:** editorial journalists and freelancers on assignment. Eligibility is by function, not title, and commercial roles are excluded in one or two sentences. Proof is two recent bylined links, or an assignment letter for freelancers.
- **Creator lane:** bloggers, podcasters and video creators. The bar is at or above the press lane's. Set it proportionate to the TerraformDays audience and label it yours. MWC's 100,000-follower bar is one event's number for its own lane; never copy it.
- **Comped seats and caps:** publish the number before the first application. A cap stated in advance makes a later "no" arithmetic.
- **Conditions:** disclose press status when interviewing, and respect the attendee opt-out. The only lever is withdrawing the credential.
- **Refusal:** in writing, quote the criterion, say what would change the answer, and offer a standard ticket plus the ungated media page. All of it goes through the single voice.

## 7. Embargo, on-site and spokesperson

- **Embargo (Menu D):** honour-system send to a named short list. If the lineup is the beat and a past-edition outlet exists, offer one or two pre-briefs. Credential-gated distribution only if sponsors embargo their own news. Never embargo something merely scheduled to publish.
- **On-site (Menu C):** at 1,400, multi-day, past the band, a staffed press room is the promotion path. Minimum is a named contact plus a written recording rule. State where cameras may go, how much of a session may be recorded, how the opt-out is respected, and that a breach costs the credential. The opt-out mechanism itself belongs to `event-accessibility-inclusion`.
- **Interviews:** direct-to-speaker is enough for a community event. Tell reporters which speakers agreed, and tell speakers a reporter may contact them.
- **Spokesperson:** name one person and one backup in writing, and brief everyone else to say nothing. Prepare three cold lines (what the event is, what is known, when the next update comes) and a refusal line.
