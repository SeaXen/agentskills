# RustDays Bordeaux #2: press brief (draft for your approval)

**Verdict:** Do the embargo, but only on the keynote and only for people who agree to it. Skip the gated portal. Don't embargo the schedule.

I've done this from the skill's rules and your message. I haven't run the full interview, so section 8 lists what I assumed and what could change the answer.

## 1. Posture: staged build, if the promotion test passes

- **Default:** reactive contact only. You're community-run with no sponsor news, and that is the lightest rung.
- **Promotion:** the skill moves you up one rung when a beat is genuinely news and at least one outlet or creator has covered this subject before. Your keynote is the news.
- **Open question:** have any of your twelve covered edition 1 or the Rust scene? If yes, you're at "staged build". If none have, stay at reactive contact. A bigger name doesn't change that.

## 2. Media page (ungated, one page)

This is a judgement call, not a published standard. Build it before the 14 March announcement, because the pitch links to it.

- **Fact sheet:** dates, venue, format, ticket types, expected attendance.
- **Boilerplate paragraph:** one paragraph, frozen, reused every year.
- **Logo pack.**
- **Edition 1 stats:** attendance, countries, CFP submissions. These are your credibility. Publish your own figures.
- **One named contact:** one monitored address, answered by the designated voice.
- **Keynote bio and headshot:** add it at lift time, not before.

## 3. Accreditation policy

Pursue this only if someone asks for a pass.

- **Default:** open. Anyone who says where they'll publish gets a comped seat.
- **Move up** to a published two-lane bar at the first refusal, or when requests exceed your spare seats.
- **Write down** the number of comped seats before anyone applies.
- **If you add a creator lane,** its bar must not be lighter than the press lane's. Set the number yourself and call it yours.

## 4. Beat calendar

| Beat | Date | Is it news? |
|---|---|---|
| Keynote reveal | 14 March, at lift time | **Yes.** Pitch it. |
| Full schedule live | 14 March | **No.** A page going live on a planned date is a publication date, not news. |
| On-sale or wrap-up | Not yet dated | Decide after Q6. |

The skill treats the lineup reveal as the highest-leverage earned-media moment of the year. Lead the pitch with the keynote. Attach the schedule as a link.

## 5. Embargo posture

You asked for an embargo on both items. The skill pushes back on that in three ways:

1. **The schedule isn't embargo-worthy.** "Dressing it as one teaches a journalist that your embargoes do not mean anything." Say "schedule goes live 14 March" and leave it there.
2. **Never send an embargo nobody asked for.**
   - Message the twelve first: "We have keynote news for 14 March, 09:00 CET. Want it early under embargo?"
   - Send only to those who reply yes.
   - State the timezone every time.
3. **The gated portal is deleted.**
   - It only earns its place when sponsors make product news under their own embargoes.
   - You have none, so it would guard a keynote name for a handful of friendly recipients. It also adds a vendor and a data review.
   - It also doesn't make embargoes hold. A gate controls who sees the material, not whether they wait.

**Recommended rung:** honour-system embargo to a short list.
- **Pre-brief, if it qualifies:** it applies only if at least one of the twelve covered edition 1, in which case it beats a file download. Offer one or two of them a 15-minute call with the organizer who will be the voice.
- **Early angle:** give one outlet or creator an early exclusive angle ahead of the general send.
- **Send once.** A nudge to someone who already declined spends the goodwill this relies on.

The skill's pitch template (an unsourced template, not observed practice):
1. Lead with what changed: the keynote.
2. Attach the fact sheet and boilerplate.
3. State the lift time with timezone.
4. Name the one person who can answer follow-ups.
5. Send it once.

## 6. On-site (at the event)

- **Named contact plus a written recording rule.** This is the default and the only rung that stays cheap.
- **The rule covers:** where cameras may go, how much of a session may be filmed, how the attendee opt-out is respected, and that breach means credential withdrawal.
- **Working corner:** add one (a table, power and wifi) if the event runs more than a day.

## 7. Spokesperson

- Name one person and one backup, in writing.
- Brief everyone else in one sentence: "You don't speak to press; point them to X."
- Write three lines the spokesperson can say cold: what the event is, what is known now, and when the next update comes.

## 8. Before you send anything

These affect the embargo, so please answer them:

1. **Keynote consent:** has the maintainer agreed to the 14 March timing and to being named then? Their consent is what the whole embargo depends on, and the speaker-experience skill owns that conversation.
2. **Who are the twelve?** Journalists, creators and newsletters that cover Rust? Or friends and community members? If they're friends, you don't need an embargo, only a courtesy note.
3. **Who has covered edition 1?** This decides pre-brief versus honour-system, and staged build versus reactive.
4. **Event date:** the skill puts a lineup reveal about 2-4 months before the event and the full programme 6-12 weeks before. 14 March fits a late spring or early summer event, but I haven't checked your date.
5. **Comped seats:** how many can you spare, and who is the single voice on record?
6. **Anything already refused or disputed** in edition 1?

**Not published:** no source quantifies how much coverage moves registrations. Track published-vs-pitched per beat, and treat embargo integrity (zero early breaks) as the only pass/fail metric.
