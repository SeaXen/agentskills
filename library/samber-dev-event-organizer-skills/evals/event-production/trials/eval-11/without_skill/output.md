# Where to spend the 4k

**Short version:** spend on two things, in this order. First, make sure no recording can silently die. Second, shorten the four-month wait between the event and the videos. Skip new cameras, training and stream upgrades.

## Why not the usual places

- **Cameras:** you have four for three rooms, and nothing you've said points to image quality.
- **Training:** eight people with four or more editions each are already experienced.
- **Stream:** the main room stream works, and nothing you've said suggests otherwise.

## Proposed split

| # | Item | Budget | Fixes |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Capture safety: third recorder or backup path, plus oversized media | ~1,200 € |lost-afternoon failure |
| 2 | Post-production capacity | ~2,000 € | four-month delay |
| 3 | Reserve | ~800 € | failures, overruns |

### 1. Capture safety (~1,200 €)

**Process fixes (0 €), which matter more than hardware:**
1. Size media to at least 2× the longest unattended block. Check each recorder's bitrate to calculate this.
2. Name one owner per recorder per day.
3. Run a card and space check at every break and write it on a sheet.
4. Do a swap-or-confirm check before every session starts.

**Spend on:**
- **A third recorder (~500–900 €)**, a cheap external one. You have two recorders for three rooms, so any room can end up without a clean path.
- **Extra cards or SSDs (~300 €)** so swaps are routine and never emergencies.
- **A cheap spare power and cable kit**, only if the current rack has no spares.

### 2. Post-production capacity (~2,000 €)

I don't know where the four months go. The most likely causes are volunteers editing serially in their spare time, or files waiting on transfers. Pick the fix that matches:

- **If it's volunteer editing time:** pay freelancers per talk for a basic edit (trim, audio level, title and end card). At roughly 30–40 € per talk, that covers about 50 talks. Set a deadline of two to three weeks. Your crew keeps quality control.
- **If it's ingest and transfer:** buy 2 rugged NVMe SSDs and fast card readers (~400–600 €) so footage is copied and backed up on site each evening.
- **Either way, do these for free or nearly free:**
  - Build one shared edit template.
  - Generate captions with local Whisper.
  - Publish unedited-but-trimmed talks first and polish later.

### 3. Reserve (~800 €)

Hold it back until the event. Typical uses are a replacement part, a rental, or extra freelance hours if the edit backlog is larger than planned.

## What I'd confirm first

1. Where do the four months go: waiting for editors, editing time, or file transfer?
2. Which recorders do you use, and at what bitrate or codec? This sets the real storage numbers.
3. Roughly how many talks are there? I assumed about 50.
4. Is the third room recorded today, and by what?

I assumed prices and the 50-talk count above, so check them against real quotes before you commit. Answer questions 1 and 2 and I'll tighten the split to exact numbers.
