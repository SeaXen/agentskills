**Subject:** [Event name] – technical requirements for room pricing, [dates]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your message. Below is what we need to price the rooms, followed by technical questions we'd like answered in writing. We'd rather have your confirmed figures than quote our own, since they will end up in the contract.

**1. The event**

- Dates: [dates], plus load-in and load-out days [dates]
- Developer conference, two days, about 700 attendees
- Three rooms running in parallel: a main room (plenary and keynotes) and two breakout rooms [capacities needed: main ~700, breakouts ~[X] each]
- Changeover between sessions: as short as [X] minutes

**2. What we will capture, by room**

| Room | Capture |
|---|---|
| Main | Recorded and streamed live |
| Breakout 1 | Recorded |
| Breakout 2 | Recorded |

- Recording takes its audio from the room's mixing desk, not from a camera microphone.
- The live stream needs a dedicated operator, a production position, and a rehearsal in the room beforehand.
- We plan to bring our own AV crew or supplier. Please confirm this is allowed (question 16).

**3. Questions for the venue, in writing**

*Connectivity*
1. What upstream bandwidth is **guaranteed** (not best-effort, not the shared attendee figure) to the main room? Can a dedicated or reserved connection be provided, and at what cost?
2. Is a wired connection available at the production position?
3. Is there a second, independent path to the internet (another provider, or a cellular fallback)?
4. Who administers the network during the event, and are they reachable throughout our hours?
5. Are any ports, protocols or destinations blocked by default? Who lifts the restrictions, and how fast?

*Rooms and rigging*
6. What is already installed in each room: projection, screens, sound system, mixing desk, microphones, lectern, confidence monitor?
7. Where are the rigging points, what load do they take, and who may rig: us, you, or a nominated contractor only?
8. Can cameras go at the back and side of each room without blocking fire routes? Can cables be run across those routes?
9. Can we take a feed from the room's mixing desk, and in what form (analogue, digital, other)?
10. What stage lighting exists, and can it be controlled separately from the house lights?

*Space and access*
11. Is there a production or control-room space in or next to each room, especially the main room?
12. What are the load-in times, access route and lift dimensions? Is storage available between days?
13. What power is available at the stage and at the production position, and on how many circuits?
14. Is a house technician included? For which hours, and responsible for what?

*Rehearsal, permissions and accessibility*
15. Can we access the main room for a full technical rehearsal before the event? When, and for how long?
16. Are there restrictions on recording or streaming here, such as an exclusive AV supplier, an exclusivity clause or a fee? Can we bring our own supplier?
17. What hearing-loop and captioning provisions exist in each room (installed loop, space for a captioner or screen)?

**4. What we need in your pricing**

- Room hire for the three rooms, itemised separately from any technical services (house technician, network upgrade, power, rigging)
- Costs for any item above that is chargeable

We'd appreciate your answers by [date]. Happy to discuss any item by phone.

Best regards,
[Name, role, phone]
