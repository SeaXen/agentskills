## 8. Redundancy required

Each item names the failure it protects against.

| Item | Failure it protects against | Required in |
|---|---|---|
| Second handheld mic live at the lectern, spare batteries, a spare cable per run, a spare adapter per connector from section 5 | The session in the room dies | Both rooms |
| Independent audio recorder fed from the board, outside the live mix | Mixing or encoding chain fails silently | Both rooms |

## Follow-up for the 11k supplier

"Record the talks in HD" leaves the whole video scope open. Ask for written answers to:

1. How many cameras per room, operated or fixed?
2. Is audio taken from the board or from the camera?
3. What are the format, naming and handover moment?
4. What is the streaming plan: operator, rehearsal and uplink?
5. What is the backup audio recorder and who owns it?
6. Which crew roles are included?

If the answers are "one camera, camera audio, no stream operator," the price gap is a scope gap. Compare that quote to the others only after it is re-priced on this structure.
