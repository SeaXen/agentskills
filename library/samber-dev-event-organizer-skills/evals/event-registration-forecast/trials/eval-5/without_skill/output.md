## 3. Cancel if the number is under 380?

**No, not automatically.** Break-even says whether the event loses money. It does not say whether cancelling loses less.

- **Cancel cost:** 50% of the venue fee `F` if you cancel at the board, 75% if you cancel after the step.
- **Proceed cost:** the shortfall below 380, times net contribution per registration.
- **Cancel only when** the low end of the range falls below `380 × (1 − f/2)`. Here `f` is the venue fee as a share of total event cost.
  - If `f` = 25%, cancel only below about 332.
  - If `f` = 50%, cancel only below about 285.
  - If `f` = 100%, cancel only below about 190.
- **Not in this formula:** sponsor refunds, speaker commitments and other sunk costs. Add any that cancelling triggers to the cancel side.
- **Timing matters more than the threshold.** The board meets at 32 days and the fee steps at 30. Day 32 is the last decision at 50%, so a "wait and see" vote effectively raises the fee by half.
- **Cheaper than cancelling:** ask the venue about a smaller room or a reduced contract before the board.
