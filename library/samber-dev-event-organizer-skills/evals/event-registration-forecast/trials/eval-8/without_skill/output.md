# Harrowgate API Days: close date and room decision

## Recommendation

1. **Take the 600-seat hall.** Sign before the option expires in 6 days, once the price check below passes.
2. **Close registration at 520 paid tickets, or at your catering and badge-print deadline, whichever comes first.** Do not close at 400.
3. **Don't overbook yet.** Revisit it at about 10 days out, using measured no-show data.

## Why the 400 plan fails

- **The 400 hall is already full.** 372 paid plus 55 comps (30 speakers, 25 sponsor staff) is 427 people against 400 seats.
  - Comps don't all sit at once, but you can't count on that at peak sessions.
  - Paid capacity in that hall is about 345, and you passed it.
- **Overbooking doesn't rescue it.** With 10% no-shows you could register about 444 people in total, which is about 389 paid. You're at 372, so you'd sell out within days.
- **The plan contradicts itself.** "Close at 400, then keep selling" is not a close. It also stacks overbooking on a hall you're about to leave.

## Forecast

The 600 figure isn't supported by last year's curve.

| Method | Paid at the end |
|---|---|
| Last year's ratio: 372 × (420/300 = 1.4) | ~520 |
| Same absolute late surge (+120) | ~490 |
| Conservative, ratio 1.25 | ~465 |

- Realistic range: 465 to 520 paid, plus 55 comps.
- The forecast rests on one earlier edition, so treat it as soft. Reaching 600 would need a 1.6× multiplier.
- The decision holds anyway. Even the low case is 65 paid tickets above the most the 400 hall can take.

## Fit in the 600 hall

| Scenario | Paid | Registered incl. comps | Present at 10% no-show | Fill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 465 | 520 | ~470 | ~78% |
| Central | 520 | 575 | ~520 | ~87% |

- The low case still fills the hall acceptably.
- The 520 cap leaves about 25 seats for crew, press and walk-in fixes. Adjust it if your fire-code count includes more staff.

## The one check before signing

The upgrade pays off if (extra hall cost) < (about 130 extra paid tickets × net margin per ticket).

- 130 is the gap between the ~520 forecast and the ~390 maximum the 400 hall could take.
- At typical conference ticket prices this almost certainly passes.
- I don't have your price or the hall cost, so run that one number.

## Hedges to ask the venue for this week

- A 3 to 5 day extension on the option.
- A partition or curtain that cuts the 600 hall to about 450 if sales stall.
- A downsize or cancellation clause, if it costs little.

## Other points

- **Verify the 10% no-show rate.** Compare last year's badge scans to the 420 registrations. If you never measured it, don't plan on it.
  - Paid no-shows lose you nothing in revenue. They only matter for seats.
- **Turning away a paying attendee costs a refund and goodwill.** That's the main reason not to sell past a hall's real capacity.
- **Legal capacity is a hard ceiling.** Everyone in the building counts toward it, including crew and staff.

## If the co-organizer insists on the 400 hall

Close paid sales today, since you're already past the seated limit. Run a waitlist, and don't sell beyond about 390 paid.

You'd give up roughly 130 tickets of revenue.
