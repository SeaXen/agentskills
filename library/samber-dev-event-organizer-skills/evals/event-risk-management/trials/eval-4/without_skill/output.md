**Notes for you, not for the email:**
- ℹ️ **Sunk costs.** The 71k is covered only if the cause of loss happens after the policy starts. Bind early.
- ⚠️ **Weather.** Rain alone is almost never insurable. Storm or wind above a threshold is. Expect a deductible.
- ⚠️ **Drones.** A net around the cage doesn't remove the aviation exclusion. That is why drones are a separate line.
- ℹ️ **Authorisations.** The marquee safety paperwork and the temporary alcohol permit come from the mairie, not the insurer. The broker may ask for them.
