1. **Group insurance or risk manager.** Ask for the programme summary and a named contact, and ask these six questions:

Fix thresholds now, before pressure arrives. These are placeholders to agree with your boss:

## 4. Emergency plan

This is a separate printed document. Do not rewrite the venue's procedures. Take facilities' existing auditorium plan and write only the delta. Facilities' own procedures own fire and evacuation. Name which scenarios they own, so you can state that instead of re-authoring them.

Event-specific scenarios, none ranked:
- Medical emergency.
- Power failure, including which circuits the robots draw on.
- Equipment theft.
- Any other evacuation.
- **A robot incident:** e-stop and who calls it, and who stops the demo.

Contacts to fill in:

Print copies for T-7 and tell local emergency services about the event. This is not needed by 23 Oct, only by G3.
