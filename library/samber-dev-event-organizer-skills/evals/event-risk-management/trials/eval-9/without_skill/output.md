# Where to start: 19 Nov customer day, 250 people

## ⚠️ Check the dates first

- Today is Wed 14 Oct 2026. 19 Nov is **5 weeks away**, not 10.
- At 6 h/week that's about **30 hours** in total.
- Confirm with your boss whether the date moved or the "10 weeks" was a miscount. Every step below assumes 5 weeks.

## What "the risk plan" should be by Fri 23 Oct

Aim for 2 to 3 pages:

1. A risk register of 10 to 15 rows. Each row has the risk, likelihood, impact, owner, mitigation, and the date it must be closed.
2. The insurance position: what's covered, what isn't, and who confirmed it.
3. Open decisions for your boss.
4. The go/no-go checkpoints.

Don't write more than that. A short register with named owners beats a long one nobody reads.

## This week's actions (about 6 h)

1. **Ask facilities for the files from their last 2 or 3 events (30 min).** Get the risk assessments, evacuation plan, headcounts, and incident logs. This is your best source, because it's already tuned to your auditorium.
2. **Check the auditorium's licensed capacity (15 min).** Ask facilities for the fire-safety capacity, including any staging, demo area, or catering that reduces it. Ask whether 250 is above their usual event size.
3. **Email group risk or insurance (30 min).** Ask for the policy schedule and a named broker contact. Use the questions below. This has the longest lead time, so send it today.
4. **Brief in-house legal (30 min).** Ask them to review the visitor terms, NDA, photo consent, and any hospitality or gift rules. Legal being bored is useful here, so give them concrete items.
5. **Book a 1-hour working session with the product or robotics lead (1 h).** The robot demo is your biggest risk (see below). You need to know whether robots will be moving near visitors.
6. **Draft the register from the table below (2 h).** Fill in owners as you learn them.
7. **Send your boss a 5-line status and the date question (15 min).**

## Starter risk register

| # | Risk | Why it matters | First mitigation |
|---|------|----------------|------------------|
| 1 | **Live robot demos near visitors** | Physical injury. This is the one that matters most. | Fenced demo zone, e-stop, trained operator, demo-specific risk assessment from your safety/engineering team. Visitors never share floor space with moving robots. |
| 2 | Auditorium capacity and evacuation | 250 may exceed the normal use. | Written confirmation of capacity, exits, and a marshal plan. |
| 3 | Insurance gap | You don't know what's covered. | See the questions below. |
| 4 | Visitor site safety | Visitors on a manufacturing or test site. | Induction briefing, badges, escorts, restricted zones. |
| 5 | Confidential info leaks | Competitors or partners in the room, photos of unreleased product. | Registration vetting, NDA or visitor terms, photo policy, locked areas. |
| 6 | Personal data (GDPR) | Registration lists, dietary or accessibility data, photos. | Data minimisation, retention date, consent wording, processor check. |
| 7 | Gifts and hospitality | Anti-bribery rules, especially for public-sector customers. | Check the group policy limits, and screen the guest list. |
| 8 | Third-party suppliers (catering, AV, security) | They bring their own liability and allergen risk. | Contracts, insurance certificates, allergen sheet. |
| 9 | Export or access restrictions | Some visitors may not be cleared to see certain tech. | Screen the guest list against your export-control policy. |
| 10 | Low attendance or late dropouts | Wasted cost, a half-empty room. | Registration target and a reminder cadence, plus a decision date for scaling the room down. |
| 11 | Key speaker or AV failure | Event quality. | Backups, a tech rehearsal the day before. |
| 12 | Accessibility and welfare | Duty of care, reputation. | Step-free access, dietary and mobility questions on the registration form. |
| 13 | Travel disruption | Customers can't reach the site. | Clear travel guidance and an in-person contact number. |
| 14 | Medical incident | Any large gathering. | First-aiders, defibrillator location, and a nearby emergency contact. |

## Insurance: questions to send to group risk or the broker

1. Does the global programme include **public/general liability** that covers visitors on our premises, and what are the limit and excess?
2. Does **product liability** cover robot demos given to customers, including physical contact?
3. Is **event cancellation** covered? If not, is it worth quoting?
4. Are there **territory, headcount, or event-type** exclusions or notification requirements?
5. Do we need to **notify the insurer** about a 250-person event on site?
6. What **insurance certificates** must third-party suppliers provide?
7. Is there a **cyber/data** cover relevant to the registration platform?
8. Who is the named contact if something happens on the day?

Ask for the answers in writing. You need them for the plan, and the broker often needs 1 to 2 weeks.

## Questions for your boss

1. Is 19 Nov fixed? It's 5 weeks out, not 10.
2. Will robots demo live, and who owns that safety sign-off?
3. Is the audience open registration or invite-only? Are any of them competitors or public-sector?
4. Who else besides you is working on this? 6 h/week for logistics, content, and risk is thin.
5. Who signs off on the risk plan?

## Biggest watch-outs

- 🔴 **Robot demos.** Get a specific risk assessment from the people who run the robots.
- 🟠 **Insurance.** Don't assume. A broker reply can take two weeks, so send the request today.
- 🟠 **Capacity.** Get the licensed number in writing.
- 🟢 **Venue contract.** Not needed, and facilities has run this before, so lean on them.
