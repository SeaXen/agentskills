## 4. Slot sheet (the core, one row per session)

Build one per room per day. 41 sessions in total.

| Time | Room | Type | Title | Speaker | Host | Intro card ✔ | Slides ✔ | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Per-session cycle** (assuming the grid gives start and end only):
1. **T-10:** speaker at the room, clicker and mic fitted, slides confirmed.
2. **T-2:** host takes the stage with the intro card.
3. **T0:** intro in ≤30 seconds, speaker starts.
4. **End-5:** the host's timer card shows "5 min". Show "1 min" at the end.
5. **End:** host thanks the speaker and announces the next session. Q&A moves to the hallway if the clock is tight.
6. **Changeover:** AV swaps slides and mics. The next speaker waits at the side of the stage.

If the grid has no changeover gaps, tell me. Back-to-back talks need a hard rule that Q&A goes to the hallway.

## 5. Speaker intros: process

- Request a 2-line bio and the pronunciation of their name from each speaker.
- Template per card: *name (phonetic if needed), role and company, one sentence on why this talk matters to this audience, title of the talk.*
- Hard limit: 30 seconds. No bio recital.
- Priya reviews the main-room cards. Room hosts rehearse theirs on T-1.
- Missing bio by T-7: use name, role and title only.
- Pronouns: use the ones the speaker gives. Don't infer any.

## 6. Contingencies

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Speaker is late or a no-show | Host tells the show caller on radio. Programme contact calls the speaker. The host fills with a 5-minute housekeeping and sponsor thank-you, then moves to the next slot's host. If the speaker doesn't show, the room stays open as a hallway track. No swapping across rooms. |
| Priya is unavailable on stage | Show caller or deputy MC takes over from her cards. Name a deputy by T-7. |
| Projector or mic failure | AV lead has a spare mic and a spare laptop with all slides. Switch within 2 minutes. |
| Room over capacity | Volunteers hold the door. Overflow goes to the stream in the sponsor hall or a spare space. Fire limit is the venue's call. |
| Registration queue > 10 min | Volunteer lead moves floaters from the info desk. Open the walk-up lane. |
| Medical or safety incident | Venue contact and first aider first. Show caller holds all sessions, announces via the main room. Emergency plan in the run sheet. |
| Code of Conduct report | Info desk logs it and escalates to the named CoC contact. Nothing is announced publicly. |
| Schedule overruns | The show caller decides what to compress. Rooms never run ahead of the published grid. |
