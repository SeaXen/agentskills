## Radio rules (print on a card for every handset)

1. Say the callsign you're calling, then your own, then the message ("Control, Room 2: projector down").
2. Keep it under 10 seconds. Anything longer, call face to face or by phone.
3. No names, medical details, or incident detail on air. Scanners exist. Use "Lead, come to the desk" and move to a phone.
4. Emergency prefix: "Priority, priority", then location. Everyone stops talking until Control clears.
5. Radio check at doors-open, midday, and close. Control does a roll call.
