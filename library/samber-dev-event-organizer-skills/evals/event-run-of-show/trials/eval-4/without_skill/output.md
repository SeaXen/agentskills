# Shift handoff process: two-day conference

**Core rule:** a handoff is complete only when the incoming person has proved they can act. Saying they were told doesn't count.

## Assumptions (correct me if wrong)

- Dan's train is at 14:05, so he leaves the venue by about 13:30. **His real end of shift is 13:30, not 14:00.** Adjust if the station is further than about 30 minutes away.
- The afternoon person is called **PM** below. The morning person is **AM**. On day one, AM is Dan.
- One person is the **Handoff Owner**, named by the lead organizer a week out. They aren't Dan or PM, so the process never depends on someone who is leaving or arriving.
- Dan will be unreachable after 13:30, so everything he knows must be written down by then.

## Part 1: Access (fixes the 16:30 failure)

Last edition failed because access was assumed. Now access is provisioned early and tested.

**T-7 days (Handoff Owner)**
1. List every on-duty person for both days, with name, block, and phone number.
2. Add each person to the organizer channel, and to every other tool the role needs. Examples are the shared inbox, the volunteer sheet, the run-of-show doc, the vendor contact list, and the emergency contacts.
3. Fill in the access table (below) and share it.

**T-2 days (Handoff Owner)**
4. Every on-duty person posts "here, <name>, <block>" in the organizer channel. Anyone silent gets a phone call. Don't rely on a reminder message.
5. Everyone confirms they can open each item in the access table.

**T-1 evening (Handoff Owner)**
6. Post a roll-call message with the on-duty roster for both days and the day-two early-exit notice for Dan.
7. Anyone not yet confirmed gets a phone call that evening.

**Day-one arrival check:** PM is also confirmed in the channel at their first arrival. Someone physically looks at the channel member list.

## Part 2: Written handoff note

Whoever is going off duty writes it. It must be posted in the organizer channel **30 minutes before the boundary**. For Dan on day two, that means **12:45 at the latest**, with no exceptions.

**Template (copy, fill, post)**

```
HANDOFF  Day __  from <AM> to <PM>  posted __:__
1. Right now: what's happening / next 60 min
2. Open issues: issue | owner | next step | deadline
3. Promised / pending: things I told someone would happen
4. Watch list: people, rooms, vendors likely to cause trouble
5. Decisions made that PM must not undo
6. Money / approvals used or pending
7. Reachability: where I am and until when (phone ok? yes/no)
8. Anything not written elsewhere
```

Rules for the note:
- Every open issue has an owner's name. "We" doesn't count as an owner.
- Items still unresolved at handoff are marked **OPEN**, never left out.
- If a section is empty, write "none". A blank section can't be told apart from a forgotten one.

## Part 3: Live walkthrough (day two: 12:45–13:30)

PM arrives by **12:45**. On day one the same walkthrough runs at 13:45–14:00.

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 12:45 | AM posts the note. PM reads it in silence for 5 minutes. |
| 12:50 | Walkthrough, 15 minutes, on foot. Visit the main room, registration, speaker area, and catering. PM asks questions throughout. |
| 13:05 | **PM reads back** the top three open issues and the next hour in their own words. AM corrects anything wrong. |
| 13:15 | **Access test:** PM posts "PM live" in the organizer channel and answers one question from the Handoff Owner. PM also opens the run-of-show doc and the contact sheet. If anything fails, fix it now. |
| 13:25 | Handoff Owner sign-off in the channel: "Handoff done <time>". |
| 13:30 | AM is released and PM is on duty. The sign-off message is the moment of transfer. |

The Handoff Owner joins the walkthrough from 13:05 on day two, and from the start if they can.

## Part 4: Dan's early-exit protocol (day two)

1. At T-1 evening, the early exit is stated in the channel roster. Nobody should be surprised at 13:30.
2. Before the afternoon starts, Dan lists everything he must do before leaving. These become checklist items for the morning: close out vendor confirmations, hand over keys and badges, and settle any cash.
3. Dan doesn't start anything new after **12:30**. He only finishes or hands over.
4. If PM isn't present by 13:15, the **Handoff Owner covers** and Dan still leaves at 13:30. The Handoff Owner phones PM at 13:00 if there's no sign of them.
5. After 13:30 Dan is **not** the fallback. If he said he can take a call (section 7 of the note), it's a courtesy, not coverage.

## Part 5: Safety net (afternoon)

- **14:00 – first check (Handoff Owner):** "PM, anything missing? Any gap in tools or contacts?" Ask this by name, aloud or by message.
- **15:00 – second check:** PM confirms they've seen the full channel history for the day and know where the incident log is.
- **16:30 – the old failure time:** the Handoff Owner confirms PM is receiving messages. They check that PM has posted at least once since 14:00.
- If PM has been silent for more than 60 minutes, the Handoff Owner finds them in person.

## Part 6: Failure fallbacks

| Failure | Response |
|---|---|
| PM can't open a tool at 13:15 | Handoff Owner fixes it or adds a workaround before sign-off. Sign-off waits. |
| PM not in channel | Add them immediately. Post the last 3 hours of key messages as a recap, and phone them. |
| Dan's note is late | Handoff Owner takes the verbal handoff at 13:00 and writes the note in his place. |
| PM no-show | Handoff Owner covers and tells the lead organizer. Dan still leaves at 13:30. |
| Dan reachable after leaving? | Only if recorded in section 7 of the note. Don't assume. |

## Part 7: After the event

- Within 48 hours the Handoff Owner collects one line from each person: "What did you lack at handoff?"
- Add any recurring gap to this checklist for next edition.
- Archive both handoff notes with the incident log.
