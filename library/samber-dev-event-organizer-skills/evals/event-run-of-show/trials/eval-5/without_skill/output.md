# Gridwave Hack: closing cue sheet

**Room:** Closing segment 17:00, room is ours until **19:00**. Nobody returns to the workshop rooms after, so all gear leaves with its owners from this room.

**Assumptions** (tell me if any is wrong):
- Judges deliberate away from the stage until about 17:40, so stage demos are a showcase and are not scored.
- Each team gets 3 minutes of demo plus a 1-minute changeover, which makes 8 slots of 4 minutes.
- There are 3 main prizes plus the sponsor's own prize.

## Stage rules

1. Publish the demo order before 17:00. Draw it or use reverse registration order. Put the riskiest hardware demos in the first block, while the room is fresh.
2. Show one on-screen countdown timer. Warn at 2:30 and cut at 3:00. The MC thanks the team and walks them off.
3. Every team gets a pre-recorded backup video on one shared laptop.
4. If a demo fails, the team gets 90 seconds to retry. After that the video plays and the clock keeps running.
5. Do all connection tests with the next team during the previous demo.
6. Sponsors get hard time caps in writing. Anything over time is cut by the MC with a smile and a thank-you.

## Roles

- **MC:** runs the script and owns the clock.
- **Timekeeper:** runs the timer and gives the 30-second signal.
- **Tech:** runs the screen switcher, audio and backup video.
- **Stage manager:** keeps the on-deck queue, pack-out and room sweep.
- **Judge liaison:** takes the results envelope, confirms 17:45 and tells the MC.
- **Sponsor liaison:** briefs the three sponsors on time and order.

## Contingencies

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Judges late by up to 10 min | Run a filler after the buffer: a 1-minute "one thing we learned" per team from the stage (8 min) or a live audience poll. |
| Judges late by more than 10 min | Push awards no later than 18:00. Cut the open floor to 25 minutes. Never cut the pack-out. |
| Judges early | Keep the demos. Judges wait at stage side. Sponsor C moves up. |
| A team no-shows or drops out | Close the gap with the buffer. Don't move other teams' slots. |
| Awards run past 18:10 | Cut the spokesperson to 1:00 and drop the group photo. |
| Audio or video failure | Fall back to the handheld mic and the backup video laptop. Keep the demo going. |
