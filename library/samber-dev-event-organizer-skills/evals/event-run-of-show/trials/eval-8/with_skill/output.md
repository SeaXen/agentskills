# Lunch flip, 12:30-13:00: how to put it in the day-of document

Give the flip its own named, timed rows, one per space, on the staff track of the cue sheet. The sheet is the cue sheet, not the published schedule. The staff track runs ahead of the attendee track, so each reset starts while the current segment is still running.

Names, headcount and the sponsor close time are placeholders. I haven't been given them, so I haven't invented any.

## 1. What goes wrong if you don't

- The lobby reset has no owner and no row. It eats into the 13:00 keynote or gets skipped.
- One person is assumed to be in the lobby, Room 2 and Room 3 at once.
- The 30 minutes are spent on three resets with no buffer, and nobody has said which reset yields if time runs out.

## 2. Roles for the flip

Name a person for each slot. "A volunteer" is not an owner.

| Slot | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lobby reset lead | `[name]` | Owns the 180 chairs, riser, sponsor tables out, and the plenary AV check. |
| Room 2 reset lead | `[name]` | Room lead for Room 2. Theatre rows out, workshop tables in. |
| Room 3 reset lead | `[name]` | Room lead for Room 3. Same as Room 2. |
| Tech owner, plenary | `[name]` | One machine, keynote deck already loaded. |
| Organizer on duty, 12:00-14:00 | `[name]` | Target of every escalation below. Cannot also be a reset lead. |
| Keynote introducer | `[name]` | A separate role from the MC. |

Check whether the venue's own staff move furniture. If they do, they need a named contact on the sheet, not just a mention.

## 3. The cue-sheet rows

Each row carries clock, room, owner, tech, what is said, what physically moves, and escalation.

**Pre-stage (staff track, before 12:30)**

> **11:45-12:30 · Lobby · Pre-stage plenary**
> Owner: `[lobby lead]`. Tech: `[plenary tech]`.
> Moves: stack the 180 chairs and stage the riser components at the lobby edge, outside sponsor sight lines. Load the keynote deck on the plenary machine.
> Said: nothing.
> Escalates to: `[on-duty]` if the chairs or riser aren't on site by 12:00.

> **12:15-12:30 · Lobby · Sponsor wind-down**
> Owner: `[sponsor liaison]`.
> Said, at 12:15: "Sponsor hours end at 12:30. Please start packing tables now." Repeat at 12:25.
> Escalates to: `[on-duty]` if any sponsor won't clear.

This row only works if sponsor hours really end by 12:30 and the sponsors know it. Confirm with the sponsor agreements before you print.

**The flip (all three start at 12:30)**

> **12:30-12:55 · Lobby · Reset to plenary**
> Owner: `[lobby lead]`. Tech: `[plenary tech]`.
> Moves: sponsor tables out, riser built, 180 chairs set, mic and screen checked.
> Said: nothing.
> Hard checkpoint 12:45: riser up and chairs at least half set. If not, call `[on-duty]`.
> Escalates to: `[on-duty]`.

> **12:30-12:55 · Room 2 · Theatre to workshop tables**
> Owner: `[Room 2 lead]`.
> Moves: chairs out, tables in.
> Escalates to: `[on-duty]` if not done by 12:50.

> **12:30-12:55 · Room 3 · Theatre to workshop tables**
> Owner: `[Room 3 lead]`. Same shape as Room 2.

**Close the flip**

> **12:55-13:00 · Lobby · Doors and seating**
> Owner: `[lobby lead]`.
> Said, from the stage: "Keynote starts in five minutes. Please take a seat."
> Escalates to: `[on-duty]` if the plenary isn't ready at 12:55.

> **13:00 · Lobby · Keynote**
> Owner: `[keynote introducer]`. Tech: `[plenary tech]`.

## 4. When the 30 minutes aren't enough

Don't decide this at 12:40. Quote the priority order from the schedule document into the sheet. The skill's default order is:

1. Protect the plenary or keynote first.
2. Protect the headline commitment the audience showed up for next.
3. Minimize how many other sessions the fix disturbs.
4. Lock the revised grid for the rest of the block.

Applied here, if the lobby slips, pull help from the Room 2 and Room 3 leads. If the lobby is still late, the on-duty organizer calls the revised keynote start and announces it in the room and on the org channel. Hold the flip as a short, announced delay of the plenary start, not a quiet reshuffle.

If the schedule document has no priority order, get one before you print.

## 5. Comms for the flip

Three simultaneous resets need a channel the reset leads can use without leaving their room. Use the org-only channel, kept separate from any attendee-facing one. Add a live channel (radio or phone group) if staff will be moving furniture with their hands full. Test it live at 12:15, not just check that people joined.

## 6. Questions that change this

1. When do the last sessions in Rooms 2 and 3 actually end? If they run to 12:30, the flip has no slack.
2. Is lunch served in the lobby? If so, attendees have nowhere to eat for the flip.
3. Who moves the furniture: your crew or the venue's? How many people?
4. Can the riser be pre-built or staged in the lobby before 12:30? It is probably the long pole.
5. Does the sponsor agreement allow sponsor hours to end at 12:30, or earlier?
6. Do Rooms 2 and 3 start their workshops at 13:00, or later?
