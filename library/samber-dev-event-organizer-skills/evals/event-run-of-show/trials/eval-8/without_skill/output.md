# Day-of document: how to write the 12:30–13:00 flip

Treat the flip as its own block, not a line in the schedule. The schedule gets one row. The block gets a page with a timeline, an owner per space, and a hard stop. Three spaces change at once in 30 minutes, so the crew split is the main thing to plan.

## 2. Changeover page

**Goal:** At 13:00, the lobby is a 180-seat plenary with a stage, and Rooms 2 and 3 are set as workshop rooms.

**Hard stop:** 12:55 is "doors ready". 13:00 is the keynote start, with no slack after it.

### Timeline (lobby is the critical path)

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| Before 12:30 | Pre-stage everything: chairs stacked at the edge, riser panels and skirting in a corridor, AV cases staged, workshop tables stacked outside Rooms 2 and 3. Tape the floor marks that can go down without disrupting sponsors. |
| 12:25 | Sponsors get a last call. Their own kit is boxed and ready to leave. |
| 12:30 | Go. Sponsor tables and banners go out first, to [storage location]. |
| 12:30–12:40 | Lobby: riser goes up. Rooms 2 and 3: chairs out, tables in. |
| 12:40–12:50 | Lobby: chair rows laid to the floor marks. Rooms 2 and 3: power strips, table signage. |
| 12:50 | Lobby: AV check, stage lighting, clicker, and mic check. |
| 12:55 | Lobby: the changeover lead walks the aisles and gives a go/no-go. Rooms 2 and 3: the leads confirm by radio. |
| 13:00 | Doors open. Keynote starts. |

Adjust the minutes to your crew size and measured build times. A do-a-dry-run timing for the riser and the chair rows beats any guess.

### Decisions to settle before writing the final version

1. **Where do attendees wait?** The lobby is closed to them during the flip, and Rooms 2 and 3 are being rebuilt. You need a lunch or holding area, with a person at each closed door and a sign saying the opening time.
2. **Where do chairs and tables go?** Rooms 2 and 3 free up theatre chairs while the lobby needs 180. If the chairs can be reused, plan the route. Otherwise plan separate storage and a separate labour pool.
3. **Do Rooms 2 and 3 session blocks really end at 12:30?** If either runs over, the flip loses minutes. Brief the session chairs to finish by 12:25.
4. **Sponsors:** Do they leave at 12:30, or remove their own kit? If they can't leave at 12:30, give them a written deadline and a named contact.
5. **Venue limits:** Check whether the venue allows the riser to be built or the floor to be marked during the morning.

### Risks and fallbacks

| Risk | Fallback |
|---|---|
| Lobby not ready at 12:55 | Hold the doors, announce a delayed start. Drop to fewer chairs (e.g. 150) before cutting the stage. |
| Rooms 2 and 3 late | The workshops start late. This never delays the keynote. |
| Crew shortage | Priority order: lobby, then Room 2, then Room 3. |
| A sponsor refuses to move | The changeover lead escalates to the organiser on duty. |

## 3. Formatting tips for the day-of document

- Put the changeover page on its own sheet, printed. People will carry it, not scroll for it.
- Use one table per space and one timeline, with no prose.
- Give every task one named owner and a clock time, never "the team".
- Repeat the 12:55 and 13:00 times in the header of the page.
- Give the changeover lead the right to call the delayed start. Settle that before the day.
