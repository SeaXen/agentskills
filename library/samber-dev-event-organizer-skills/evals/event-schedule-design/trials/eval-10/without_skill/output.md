## Schedule changes on the day

### Why this section exists

Last year a speaker no-showed at 14:00. We moved three sessions earlier, moved two back when he arrived at 15:10, and the published grid stayed wrong all afternoon. This section sets the rules in advance so nobody improvises under pressure.

Three principles:
- **One owner, one grid.** One person decides and one published grid exists.
- **Smallest change wins.** Change as little as possible, and never reverse a change.
- **Sessions never start earlier than published.** Attendees plan their walk between rooms around the printed time. A session that starts early is a session they miss.

### Roles

| Role | Who | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule Owner | [name], backup [name] | Only person who can change the grid. Decides within the time limits below. |
| Room leads (×4) | [names] | Report no-shows and delays to the Owner. Hold the room. They never change anything themselves. |
| Comms lead | [name] | Publishes every change on all channels using the template below. |

If the Owner is unreachable for 5 minutes, the backup decides.

### Before the day

1. **Standby item for every slot, in every room.** Each is a 20–40 minute filler that needs no preparation: lightning talks from pre-agreed volunteers, an organizer Q&A, a sponsor demo, or an open discussion. List them in the run sheet by room and time.
2. **Speaker check-in.** Every speaker confirms arrival the day before. On the day, they check in with a room lead at least 30 minutes before their session. Collect a mobile number and a travel plan for each speaker.
3. **One published grid** with a version number and an "updated at" time, for example `v1 – 08:30`. The website, the app, room signage, the printed program and the MC script all point to it. Print a "check the grid online" QR code on the program.

### When a speaker is missing

| Time | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| T–30 | Not checked in | Room lead calls the speaker and tells the Owner. |
| T–10 | Unreachable or no ETA | Session is **at risk**. Owner confirms the standby item is ready. Nothing is published yet. |
| T+0 | Not in the room | Owner decides. Do not wait past T+5. |

**Decision order.** Take the first option that works:
1. **Replace in place.** Run the standby item in the same room and slot. No other session moves. This is the default.
2. **Swap within the same room.** Swap only if the next session's speaker is on site and agrees. The next session starts later than published, never earlier.
3. **Cancel the slot.** Mark it "break / open space". Nothing else moves.

**Never:**
- Move any session earlier.
- Move more than one other session.
- Move a session to a different room or time slot than published.

If none of the three options works, option 3 applies.

### When the missing speaker turns up

The slot is gone and the grid does not revert. Last year's mistake was moving two sessions back to restore the original order.

The speaker can instead:
- take a standby slot later in the day, if one is free and unpublished changes are not needed;
- have the talk recorded;
- go into the next event.

The Schedule Owner tells them in person, not by message.

### Communication

The ops suggestion was 30 minutes for all changes. That is too slow for anything starting soon: at 14:00 a 30-minute delay means 300 people are already in the wrong rooms. Use two deadlines instead:

| Affected session starts | Publish within |
|---|---|
| In under 30 minutes | **5 minutes** |
| 30 minutes or later | **15 minutes** |

Publish on every channel at once, from the same text:
- the website and app grid, with a new version number;
- the screens in the foyer and at each room door;
- an announcement from the MC in every room;
- the attendee chat channel.

**Template:**
> ⚠️ Schedule change (grid v[N], [time])
> **[Session]** in **[Room]** now: **[standby item or cancelled]**.
> Everything else is unchanged.
> Latest grid: [URL]

Each room lead also puts a printed sign on the door within the same deadline. The change is complete only when all four channels match, and the Comms lead confirms that to the Owner.

### Freeze

The ops suggestion was a 4-hour freeze after any change. Keep the idea, with these rules:

- **After a change is published, the sessions affected are locked until the end of the day.** Nobody can reverse or re-move them. This covers the late-arrival case above.
- **A new, separate problem is handled by the same playbook**, using options 1 to 3 only. For example, a second no-show gets a standby item. The freeze never blocks this.
- **Sessions that were not touched stay as published.** Only a cancellation can change them.
- **Exceptions:** safety or venue emergencies (the Owner decides and informs everyone at once), and a wrong grid. If the published grid contradicts this section's rules, fix it and publish within 5 minutes.

A flat 4-hour freeze would expire at 18:00 for a 14:00 change, which is effectively the whole day. A flat freeze would also have forced a bad improvised answer to a second problem. This version locks what was changed and keeps the playbook open for new failures.

### Log and review

The Comms lead logs every change in a shared sheet: time, grid version, reason, option used, who decided. At the debrief, review the log and ask whether the playbook held and whether any standby item was missing.
