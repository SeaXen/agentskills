## Why this beats the two requested plans

The skill matches rooms on expected audience size, not on how much a track matters. Your own numbers decide it.

- **Beginner track in Minho fails.** Last edition's intro-to-k8s session drew 210 and still turned people away, so 210 is a floor. Minho seats 80, so at least 130 people per session would be turned away. This is the public failure the skill warns about.
- **Beginner track in Douro also fails.** It seats 150, so at least 60 people per session would be turned away. Only Tejo holds it.
- **Platform engineering in Tejo all day looks empty.** Its deep dives average 95, so 95 people in 420 seats is 23% full. The flagship would look like a failed track in photos and on the video stream. In Douro, 95 in 150 is about 63% full and looks busy, with 55 spare seats. An empty big room only looks quiet, but a flagship that looks quiet costs you something real.
- **Minho is the worst place for beginners.** It is a four-minute walk away, up a flight of stairs. Newcomers are the attendees least likely to find it, and the most likely to give up on the walk.

## What the flagship still gets

Two options, in my order of preference:

1. **Keynote and plenaries in Tejo.** Make the keynote platform-engineering themed, so the flagship opens and closes the day in the biggest room. It costs nothing.
2. **Swap a marquee session into Tejo.** Do this only for a session you expect above 130 people, and only if the beginner track has a slot you can move to Douro at that time. I would not do this without last edition's per-session counts.

## Assumptions to confirm

These are my estimates, not benchmarks. Please check them before the assignment is locked.

- **Tracks 3 and 4 are unknown.** I need last edition's per-session average for each. Minho only works if both fit in about 65 people, which is 80% of the seats. If either is bigger, the plan changes.
- **The 210 figure is censored.** I don't know how many people were turned away. If you have a door count, the Tejo estimate could be tighter or higher.
- **Plenary overflow.** 520 registered is more than Tejo's 420 seats. If you expect more than about 80% to show up, set up an overflow room with a video feed, probably Douro. Tell me your expected show rate.
- **Peak sizes for platform engineering.** The 95 is an average. If any single session is likely to pass 130, say which one.

## Consequences for the rest of the grid

- **Buffers for Minho.** Any session starting in Minho needs a published gap of at least 10 minutes from anything in Tejo or Douro. A 4-minute walk plus stairs doesn't fit in a tight changeover, and a zero-gap grid is not an option for this venue. Please check whether there is a step-free route to Minho.
- **Alternating tracks 3 and 4.** Put consecutive sessions of the same track together in Minho, so people can stay in the room. Swap which track opens the day at the midpoint, so neither always gets the later or lesser slots.
- **Staggered starts.** Start Minho 5 minutes after the other two rooms, so the stairs aren't jammed when a block ends.
- **Disruption order.** Protect the keynote first, then the platform-engineering sessions, then touch as few other sessions as possible. Lock the revised grid for the rest of the block.
