# PlatformDays Lisbon: room assignments

## Recommendation

| Room | Seats | Assignment | Expected load |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sala Tejo** | 420 | Getting Started with Kubernetes, all day. Also the opening keynote and the closing session. | 210+ (50%+) |
| **Sala Douro** | 150 | Platform engineering, all day, except the headline talk(s) below. | ~95 (63%) |
| **Sala Minho** | 80 | Tracks 3 and 4, alternating by time slot. | Under 80 each (needs checking) |

## Why this and not the original plan

- **Minho can't hold the beginner track.** Last year intro-to-k8s drew 210 and turned people away. That is 2.6× Minho's capacity, and the 210 is a floor, because the people turned away aren't in it.
- **Tejo is oversized for the flagship.** Platform-engineering deep dives averaged 95, so Tejo would sit 77% empty. Douro fits them at about 63% full, which is comfortable with room for spikes.
- **Minho is the wrong room for beginners anyway.** It is a four-minute walk away, up a flight of stairs. Newcomers are the people most likely to get lost or give up. The stairs may also be an accessibility problem.

## Giving the organizers the flagship treatment

"Flagship" is about prestige, not headcount, so give it prestige in Tejo:

1. **Opening keynote** in Tejo, on a platform-engineering theme.
2. **Closing session** in Tejo, with a platform-engineering panel or "state of" talk. This is the last thing everyone sees.
3. **One or two headline platform-eng talks** in Tejo, in slots where a k8s session can move to Douro. The 95 average is a mean, so some talks will run well above it.
4. **Signage and branding** put platform engineering first.

**Overflow rule:** any session with more than about 130 RSVPs two weeks out moves to Tejo, and the displaced session takes its slot in Douro.

## Tejo looks empty at 210

Rope off the back sections or reserve the front rows. Beginners in a half-empty hall can feel exposed, and a full front half reads as a success on camera.

## Minho and the walk

- Add a **10–15 minute changeover** for any session that starts in Minho, instead of the usual 5.
- Put the **smallest and most niche** sessions there. Don't put anyone with mobility needs there without a plan.
- Announce the stairs and the walk time in the schedule.

## Open question that decides the rest

I have no data on tracks 3 and 4, so the Minho row is an assumption. I need two numbers:

1. **What are tracks 3 and 4, and how many people do you expect in each?**
2. **Do you have per-session RSVPs?** The overflow rule needs them.

If either track expects more than 80, it can't go in Minho. In that case, move it to Douro in the slots where platform engineering has its smaller sessions.

## Risks

- **520 registered is not the same as 520 present.** Typical show rates are 70–85%, so plan around 360–440 on site. Beginner tracks tend to attract walk-ins, so they can run above forecast.
- **The two organizers may still object.** The strongest argument is that putting a 95-person average into a 420-seat room makes the flagship look unpopular, while turning people away from the beginner track repeats last year's failure.
