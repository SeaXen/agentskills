# Terrafold side events: application form and criteria

**Next action:** read the three timing and structure points below, then decide whether to accept them. The form and criteria follow.

## Three changes to your plan

1. **Don't open applications yet.** Nobody outside the four organizers knows Terrafold exists. A form with no public site, dates or schedule gets no applicants. Open it when the site is live with dates and venue. Until then, write the form and keep it ready.
2. **Make the guide digital first.** Side events need to be planned weeks ahead, and a guide handed out at check-in reaches people after they've made their evening plans. Publish a web page that updates as events are approved. Print a one-page card with a QR code and the top events for check-in.
3. **Use "listing" criteria, not "approval" criteria.** You aren't incorporated, and one person's account carries everything. If Terrafold approves events, attendees may read that as endorsement, and you may inherit liability. Frame it as: "we list events that meet these basics; we don't organize, insure or endorse them." Check the wording with someone who knows French association or event law before publishing. I'm not a lawyer.

## Published criteria

> **Terrafold fringe: how listing works**
>
> Terrafold is an independent conference for infrastructure engineers, held in Lyon on [dates]. Fringe events are gatherings that happen around the conference, such as meetups, dinners, workshops and hack sessions. They are organized by others, not by Terrafold.
>
> We list fringe events that meet all the criteria below. Listing is not endorsement. Terrafold doesn't organize, fund, insure or supervise listed events. The organizer is solely responsible for the venue, safety, insurance, permits, finances and legal compliance.
>
> **Required (all must be true)**
>
> 1. **Relevant.** The event is about infrastructure, cloud, platform, SRE, DevOps or adjacent engineering topics, or is a social gathering for people in that field.
> 2. **Named organizer.** A named person or company is accountable and can be reached by email before and during the event.
> 3. **Code of conduct.** The event follows the Terrafold Code of Conduct [link], or its own written equivalent that you link in the form.
> 4. **No clash with the program.** The event doesn't overlap official session hours [times to be published]. Times are provisional until the schedule is published. If it moves, you must update your listing.
> 5. **Venue confirmed.** You have a venue, or a signed or written agreement with one, and the venue allows the expected headcount.
> 6. **Honest description.** The listing states who it is for, its format, and any cost, sponsor or sales content. If it is mainly a sales pitch, it says so.
> 7. **Terrafold not misused.** You don't use the Terrafold name or logo as your own event name. "Terrafold Fringe: [your title]" and "for Terrafold attendees" are fine. Don't imply Terrafold organizes it or sells tickets.
> 8. **Free or ticketed by you.** Terrafold doesn't collect payments for fringe events. If you charge, you run ticketing and refunds yourself.
>
> **Reasons we may decline or remove a listing**
>
> - It doesn't meet a required criterion.
> - It mainly collects attendee data or contact lists with no stated purpose.
> - It excludes people on the basis of who they are.
> - A past event by the organizer led to a conduct complaint that wasn't resolved.
> - Another listed event has the same format, time and audience, and we ask you to coordinate with them first. This is not a ranking. Listing is not competitive.
>
> **What we don't promise**
>
> Attendance, promotion beyond the listing, venue help, sponsor introductions or badges for your attendees.
>
> **Decisions** come within 7 days of your application, from a named Terrafold contact. We may ask questions. Declined applicants get the specific criterion in writing and can reapply.

## Application form

Mark required fields with *. Keep it to one page. Nobody has applied yet, so don't add more.

**Organizer**
1. Name of the accountable person *
2. Organization or group, if any
3. Email *
4. Phone number (used only for day-of emergencies) *
5. Have you run a similar event before? If yes, give a link or a short description.

**Event**
6. Event title *
7. One-paragraph description: who it's for, what happens *
8. Format * (meetup / dinner / workshop / party / hack session / other)
9. Date *, start time *, end time *
10. Venue name and address * (or "pending" with the expected confirmation date)
11. Expected attendance *, and a hard cap if the venue has one
12. Language(s) *

**Cost and commercial content**
13. Is it free or paid? If paid, give the price and who sells tickets. *
14. Who funds it? Name any sponsors. *
15. Will there be product pitches, recruiting or lead capture? Describe briefly. *
16. What attendee data will you collect, and what will you do with it? *

**Safety and conduct**
17. Code of conduct: link to yours, or tick "we follow the Terrafold Code of Conduct." *
18. Who is the conduct contact at your event? *
19. Accessibility: is the venue step-free? Do you offer any accommodations (dietary, quiet space)?
20. Do you have liability insurance, or does the venue? (yes / no / don't know)

**Confirmations (tick all) ***
- [ ] I have read the listing criteria.
- [ ] I understand Terrafold doesn't organize, insure or endorse my event, and that I am responsible for it.
- [ ] I will update my listing if the date, time or venue changes.
- [ ] I agree to be listed on the Terrafold fringe page with the information above (title, description, time, venue, organizer name). Contact details stay private.
- [ ] I will tell Terrafold if the event is cancelled.

**Privacy notice, to put under the form:** "We use your answers only to review your application and publish your listing. Contact details are not published. We delete applications [N] months after the event. Contact [email] to see or delete your data." Confirm the retention period and the data controller. With no legal entity, that is probably one named organizer personally, so decide who it is before you publish.

## Open decisions for you

- Who is the named Terrafold contact for decisions? One person, with a backup.
- Is there a Code of Conduct yet? Criterion 3 and question 17 link to it.
- Which email address do applications go to? Use a shared mailbox, not a personal one.
- Should your fiscal setup change before you publish? Even though the form avoids money flows, listing events under an unincorporated group puts organizers at personal risk. Ask your treasurer or a lawyer before the public launch.
