# Halcyon Summit side-event application and refusal, tightened

I applied the vetted-application shape from `event-side-event-coordination`. Each field has to feed a criterion, so I cut anything that doesn't.

## 1. Application form

**Cut from your 14 fields**
- **Logo upload:** we don't grant logo use, and collecting logos invites co-branding requests.
- **VAT number:** nothing in the criteria uses it. Ask only if a paid slot is sold later.
- **Social handles:** nothing in the criteria uses them.
- **200-word and 150-word minimums:** word counts add friction and don't help us decide. The skill cites recruiting-funnel data, where each added minute of application complexity costs roughly a tenth of the applicants. That figure is an analogy, not an events benchmark.
- **"Why your event is a good fit":** an essay that rewards good writers. It's replaced by two direct questions: who it's for, and what they're asking from us.
- **Org website:** replaced by an optional event page URL, so we can see where our name already appears.

**Form (13 fields, all short)**

*Who is responsible*
1. Contact name. This must be a person, not a brand.
2. Mobile number reachable during the event.
3. Email.
4. Organization, if any.

*What it is*
5. Event name as you will publish it.
6. Format, in one line (meetup, dinner, workshop, party).
7. Who it is for: open to any attendee, invite-only, or restricted. If it is not open, say so here.
8. Expected headcount.
9. Ticketed or paid? If yes, who receives the money?

*When and where*
10. Date, start time, end time.
11. Venue address, and whether the venue is yours, theirs, or a third party's.

*Our name*
12. Where will "Halcyon Summit" appear (page, tickets, signage, social posts)? Does the logo appear anywhere? What are you asking from us: a listing, a mention, permission to use the name, or nothing?

*Conditions*
13. Accessibility (step-free, yes or no) and alcohol (served, and by whom), one line each. Also confirm three things:
    - You have a code of conduct, with a link and a named contact.
    - You will carry our non-affiliation sentence wherever you use our name.
    - You accept that we may withdraw the listing and naming permission.

**Before this form goes live**
- Publish the criteria first: overlap with our protected windows, audience, named responsible person, commercial shape, sponsor conflict, and our right to refuse without further reasons. Criteria written after the first application read as a verdict on that applicant.
- Check the sponsor-exclusivity clauses before approving anything.
- Pass the approved list to whoever owns the code of conduct, so its scope clause can name the approved satellites.
- Here is a draft non-affiliation sentence for you to validate. It is my wording, not the skill's:
  > *[Event] is organised by [organizer] and is not organised or staffed by Halcyon Summit, which does not endorse its content. Questions or complaints go to [organizer contact]. The organizer has agreed to follow a code of conduct.*

## 2. Refusal to Marek

> Hi Marek,
>
> We're not listing the Wednesday demo. It runs through our opening keynote, which is a protected window for us.
>
> If you move it clear of the keynote ([end time]), send us the new time and we'll review it again. Either way, please don't use the Halcyon Summit name or logo on the invite or ticket page.
>
> [Your name]

**What changed**
- It states one criterion (overlap), one decision (not listing), and one thing we can still offer (move it and we'll reassess).
- I removed the sponsor worry, the vague "how it might look", and the "circle back closer to the date". They turn a refusal into a negotiation, and Marek can't plan around them.
- The last line covers naming now, so he can't print materials against a silence.

**Check these before sending**
- **What Marek asked for.** I assumed a listing or promotion. If he only asked for permission to run it, change "not listing" to "we can't support it".
- **The offer to reassess.** Delete that sentence if you won't actually reassess.
- **The real reason.** If it is a sponsor-exclusivity conflict, name that criterion instead of the keynote. State one reason, not both.
- **The keynote time.** Fill in `[end time]` from the published grid.
