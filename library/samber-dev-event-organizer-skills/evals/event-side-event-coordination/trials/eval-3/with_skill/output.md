# Answer for the Lagos Go User Group

**Short version:** yes to the Tuesday meetup, no to the name, no to the mascot. The listing is a yes only after it passes criteria we write first. I've set the thresholds below myself. They aren't benchmarks, and nothing should be published until you've validated each section.

## 1. Approval posture: vetted application
- Someone has now asked, and you want them findable, so doing nothing is out.
- An open, ungated list would endorse events you never examined, so it's out too.
- A signed agreement is too heavy for a meetup.
- **Write the criteria before you reply**, not after. Criteria written in response to one request read as a judgment about that requester.
- This is a friendly group and your decider knows them. A written test protects the relationship, because a refusal then points at a criterion instead of at them.
- If nobody on your team can say no to them, fall back to an open listing with a non-endorsement line. Don't run a gate that never refuses.

## 2. Criteria (write once, reuse every edition)
- **Overlap:** does it run against a protected window of the published grid?
- **Audience:** is it aimed at attendees, or is your crowd a convenient audience for something unrelated?
- **Responsibility:** is there a named person reachable on the night?
- **Commercial shape:** are recruiting, product pitches and paid training each allowed or not?
- **Conflict:** does it break a sponsor's exclusivity?
- **Code of conduct:** do they adopt one and name a contact?
- You may refuse without giving reasons beyond these criteria.

## 3. The three asks

| Ask | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "GopherNaija Pre-Conference Night" | **No** | "Pre-conference" claims a relationship to your programme. Only "during" and plain dates are factual. |
| Our gopher on the poster | **No** | A logo grant needs a named purpose, an end date, and someone who clearly holds the marks. This isn't a sponsor slot or a partner track, so none of that applies. |
| Listed on our site | **Yes, if it passes the criteria** | A listing is an endorsement, so it carries the disclaimer and a "please support them" framing. |

- **Name:** the default rung is the mandatory disclaimer, and it permits the event's name. The name still has to be factual.
- **Suggested title:** "Lagos Go Night", with the line "the evening before GopherNaija".
- **Mascot:** they use their own artwork.
- **Who decides on the marks:** if a parent body holds your marks, that grant is theirs to make, not yours.
- **Fallback:** the strictest rung bans the name outright and hands out a substitute name. It's the honest answer if you won't review their materials.

## 4. The sentence they must carry
It goes on their page, tickets and posts, and you send it in the approval message.

> Lagos Go Night is organised by the Lagos Go User Group (contact: [name, email]). It is not organised, staffed or run by GopherNaija, which listed it against published criteria but does not vouch for its content. Questions or concerns go to the Lagos Go User Group. Attendees are expected to follow the Lagos Go User Group's code of conduct, which GopherNaija required as a condition of listing.

This covers the five components: who runs it, who doesn't, what you did not do, where complaints go, and what still applies.

Research on disclaimers is not encouraging. They often fail to correct the confusion they target. That is why the title change matters more than the sentence.

## 5. Calendar: published protected windows
- Tuesday evening is outside your published programme, but check three things first:
  - Is there a registration or early-arrival slot that evening?
  - Do you run your own evening event?
  - Is the grid published?
- If the grid isn't published yet, you can't promise windows. Hold the answer until it ships.
- Don't move any of your sessions to fit them.
- The 60 attendees aren't a problem in themselves. They are your audience criterion passing, as long as nothing on your side is scheduled against it.

## 6. The two cross-checks (run these before approving)
- **Kestrel Cloud exclusivity:**
  - Read the titanium agreement for exclusivity clauses.
  - Ask the Lagos Go User Group who sponsors the venue, food and speakers.
  - A competing cloud vendor's night, listed on your page, is inventory Kestrel paid for.
  - If a sponsor hosts it, the title must read "[Event] hosted by [Company]".
- **Code of conduct:**
  - Your published code reaches exactly as far as its scope clause says.
  - Make adoption of a code, with a named contact, a condition of approval.
  - Hand your conduct owner the approved satellite list so the scope clause can name it.

## 7. Liability: routed, not answered
I state no legal rules here. Each category below depends on jurisdiction.

| Exposure | Route to |
|---|---|
| Use of your name and marks by someone you don't control | Whoever holds the marks; counsel if a grant is made |
| Apparent endorsement from your listing | Counsel; mitigate with the framing and the sentence above |
| Harm to a person at their venue | Counsel; risk register if you track it |
| Alcohol served by them | Counsel |
| Their RSVP list and personal data | Whoever owns your privacy obligations |

- **Withdrawal:** keep a right to pull the listing and the naming permission at any time, separate from any breach. Printed materials stay their problem.
- **Expiry:** the approval covers this edition only.

## 8. What to send them
Send this once you've validated sections 1–7.

> Thank you, we would love to see this happen, and the Go community here is better for you. Three answers:
>
> 1. **Name:** we can't use "GopherNaija Pre-Conference Night". "Pre-conference" implies it's part of our programme. Please call it "Lagos Go Night" and say it's the evening before GopherNaija.
> 2. **Mascot:** please don't use our gopher or any of our logos. Use your own artwork.
> 3. **Listing:** we'll list it once you've sent us: a named contact for the night, venue, expected headcount, whether it's ticketed, who sponsors it, and your code of conduct with its contact. Please carry the sentence below on your page and tickets.
>
> [disclaimer sentence]
>
> We can withdraw the listing and naming permission if needed, and approval covers this edition only. Printed materials remain yours to reprint.

## 9. Before you publish
- **Publish the posture** on your site either way, even as "we list nothing". Silence reads as permission.
- **Five items must exist in writing with no blanks:**
  1. The posture and why.
  2. The criteria.
  3. The naming sentence.
  4. The calendar windows.
  5. The routed exposure categories, each with a named person.
- **Open questions that could change the answer:**
  - Is anyone already using your name in published materials? If yes, "do nothing" is off the table.
  - Who holds your marks?
  - Is the grid published?
  - Who on your team says no?
  - Will anyone maintain a list live through the week?
  - Do you plan to repeat this next edition?
  - Is there an existing sponsor clause or code scope that already covers this?
- **After the event:** record what ran, what collided, and anything that used your name without asking.
