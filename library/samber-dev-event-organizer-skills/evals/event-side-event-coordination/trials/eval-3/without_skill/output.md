**Why this is the middle option:**
- Declining outright would hurt a group that has spoken at your event twice.
- A full official event means you own the code of conduct, the sponsor conflicts, and the liability. That should be a deliberate decision, not a poster request.
- The listing and mascot give them the lift they want. The disclaimer and their own code of conduct keep your obligations clear.

**Decisions only you can make:**
- Whether Kestrel Cloud gets a courtesy heads-up or a formal sign-off. It depends on the contract.
- Whether you promote it to attendees beyond the listing.
