## 2. Criteria set (self-set, write before replying to anyone)

3. **Responsibility.** Is there a named person who is reachable that evening and who accepts the code-of-conduct condition?
4. **Commercial shape.** Recruiting event, product pitch or paid training? Each is judged on its own.
5. **Conflict.** Does it collide with a sponsor's exclusivity or paid inventory?
6. **Right to refuse.** We may refuse on these grounds alone, with no further reasons owed.

Rank refusals by which criterion failed, never by who asked. Publish the criteria, not the decisions.
