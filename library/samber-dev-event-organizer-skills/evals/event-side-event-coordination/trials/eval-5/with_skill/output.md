## ⚠️ Decide before publishing: Vaultrun

You asked for "no filtering, whoever submits gets listed." Vaultrun competes with Bastion, who bought your top tier, so those two things may conflict.

1. **Check Bastion's sponsorship agreement first.** Look for exclusivity or competitor clauses. The owner is whoever holds sponsor fulfillment.
2. **If the agreement is silent:** list Vaultrun like everyone else. Tell Bastion's contact before the page goes live, so they don't find out from an attendee.
3. **If the agreement covers it:** a fully open list isn't available. Add one published criterion that applies to every submitter, for example "events that conflict with a sponsor's exclusivity are not listed." Write it now.
4. **Don't wait to rule on Vaultrun after it submits.** A rule written in response to one applicant reads as a verdict on that applicant.

The draft below assumes case 2. The Vaultrun row is marked **HOLD** until the check is done.

## 1. Posture [self-set]

**Self-registration listing, framed as "please support these," not as programming.**

- **Why not a vetted application:** you asked for no gate, and nine parties already exist. A vetted rung would also need someone to say no, and that's a cost you didn't sign up for.
- **Cost of this choice:** it scores worst on containment, because you list things you haven't examined. The framing and the disclaimer below carry that weight. A list without the framing reads as an endorsement.
- **No ranking:** attendees asked which parties are worth going to. The page doesn't answer that, and says so.

## 2. Criteria [self-set]

There is no gate, and that is an explicit decision. Two things sit outside it:

- **Sponsor exclusivity** is unresolved (see above).
- **Withdrawal right:** you may take any listing down at any time, without giving reasons. It is separate from entry, so it doesn't contradict "whoever submits gets listed."

Open question on links: every row links to a page you don't control. I suggest the comms lead confirms each link loads before the row goes live. That is a mechanical check, not a content judgment.

## 3. Naming rule [self-set]

The rule is **mandatory unofficial-disclaimer wording**, with no logo and no licence.

- **Allowed:** "during Meridian Dev Summit" in a plain factual sentence.
- **Not allowed:** "official," "part of," "pre-conference," and any use of the logo or colours.
- **Required on the organizer's own page** wherever they name the summit:

> **[Event name] is organised by [Organizer]. It is not organised, run, staffed or vetted by Meridian Dev Summit, which is not responsible for it. Questions or concerns about this event go to [Organizer contact]. Meridian Dev Summit's code of conduct covers the summit only, not this event.**

The five components are covered in order: who runs it, who doesn't, what you didn't do, where to complain, and what still applies. The last sentence is honest only because you impose no code-of-conduct condition. If you want one, change it to "Organizer has confirmed it follows a code of conduct" and make adoption a form requirement. Decide that with whoever owns your code of conduct.

Disclaimers are weak at preventing confusion. The sentence reduces misattribution but doesn't eliminate it.

## 4. Calendar depth [self-set]

**Shared public calendar**, which is the /parties table.

- **Why it fits:** more than a couple of events are running, and your comms lead will maintain it live through the week.
- **If that stops being true:** an unmaintained calendar is worse than none. It sends people to cancelled parties.
- **Protected windows:** add one line to the page, `Please don't schedule against [keynotes / closing session / your evening programme]`. Include it only if the session grid is already published. If it isn't, drop the line.

## 5. Liability position

This is a routing table. I'm stating no legal rules, and every category is jurisdiction-specific.

| Exposure | Route to |
|---|---|
| Your name and marks used by someone you don't control | Whoever holds the marks (team or parent body), plus counsel if a misuse is challenged |
| Apparent endorsement from listing | Counsel. Mitigate now with the framing and disclaimer |
| What happens to a person at a third party's venue | Counsel |

## The submission form

One page, short. Required unless marked.

**Event**
1. Event name
2. Date, start time, end time
3. Venue name and address
4. Link to the event page
5. Who it's for: open to any attendee / ticketed / invite-only. Invite-only events are listed, but must say so.

**Responsible person** *(never published)*
6. Full name of a person who will answer on the night
7. Mobile number or email reachable during the summit
8. Organization (optional)

**Naming**
9. Where will "Meridian Dev Summit" appear in your materials? (page, tickets, signage, social)
10. ☐ I will not use the summit's logo or colours, and I will not call the event official, part of the summit, or pre-conference.
11. ☐ Wherever I name the summit, I will carry the disclaimer sentence above, unchanged.

**Optional, not published**
12. Accessibility in one line (step-free? seated?)
13. Alcohol in one line (bar-only?)

**Notice:** *We use your contact details only to reach you during the summit and will delete them by [date]. Listing is not endorsement, and we may remove any listing without giving a reason.* [Privacy owner to confirm wording and deletion date.]

Questions 12 and 13 feed no decision, so I made them optional. Every extra required field costs submissions.
