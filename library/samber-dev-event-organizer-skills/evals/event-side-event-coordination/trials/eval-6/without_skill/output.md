## Internal notes (don't send)

- **Send only what the lockup needs.** That means the wordmark, the lockup, and the colour values. Leave out source files, other marks, and the full brand kit.
- **Name.** If they push back on dropping "@ Ferrolink", the fallback is "Cobalt Night, during Ferrolink Conf". Don't give way on the "side event" and host wording.
- **Insurance.** If the agreement has no insurance clause, ask for a certificate of liability cover. A 150-person evening with alcohol under your name carries real exposure.
- **Legal read.** If you can, have counsel look at items 9 and 10 once before you reuse the sheet as a standard template for future sponsors.
- **Placeholders.** Fill in the bracketed items before sending: the names, the dates, the end time, and your legal entity name. I haven't seen the signed agreement, so the clause numbers and package terms are assumed.
