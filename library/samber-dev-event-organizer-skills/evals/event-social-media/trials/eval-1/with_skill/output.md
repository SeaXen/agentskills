# KernelConf 2027: social posting schedule (LinkedIn + Mastodon mirror)

**Assumptions to confirm** (I couldn't run the interview, so these are my defaults):
- **Year:** Doors 14 Oct is 2027, since 14 Oct 2026 has passed. All weekdays below are 2027.
- **Weight:** The 8% weight and the handed-down dates are fixed. Nothing here argues with them.
- **Governance:** The event is community-run, so no neutrality allow-list applies.
- **Message and voice:** I didn't have the positioning sentence or the tone matrix. The announcement post carries the positioning sentence verbatim, and the voice comes off the social column of the tone matrix.
- **Sponsors and partners:** I don't know whether sponsor handles are collected or partner deals are signed. Those rungs are conditional below.

## 1. Platform set and hashtags

**Platforms:** Home plus mirror. This is judged, and it fits one writer at about 2 h/week.
- **LinkedIn** is home. It reaches the managers who approve travel and training budgets.
- **Mastodon** is the mirror. It reaches practitioners.
- Adapt lightly. Never paste a LinkedIn post into Mastodon unchanged. The agenda release becomes a thread there.
- Go to two natively adapted accounts only if your audience answer shows the two platforms hold different people and you have spare hours.

**Hashtags** (3 max, fixed before the first post, because changing one mid-campaign splits the archive for good):

| Tag | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `#KernelConf2027` (proposed, check for collisions first) | Campaign measurement for this edition only |
| One topic tag, e.g. `#PlatformEngineering` | Reach beyond current followers |
| Optional categorization tag | A track or theme, only if the agenda has one |

Report the campaign tag's volume as the campaign's reach. Never report the topic tag's volume.

**Tracked links:** Create these before launch, because they can't be added afterwards.
- One per platform.
- One per organiser who posts from a personal account.
- One shared link for speaker posts.

The marketing plan reads them. You only create them.

**Rules for the schedule:**
- **Speaker slots:** Hand out the next free Tue/Thu slot at confirmation, and tell the speaker that date in the ask email. Hold the first card until the confirmed set isn't a thin or unrepresentative teaser. The 1 July start is the earliest, not a promise.
- **Overflow:** If confirmations outrun the slots, the extras roll into the agenda release.
- **Alternation:** Inside any multi-post phase, alternate "gives something" posts with plain informational ones.
- **Weekend deadlines:** CFP close (5 Jun) is a Saturday and early-bird close (5 Sep) is a Sunday. I scheduled posts on the weekend. If the plan owners prefer a Friday cutoff, that is their call to make, not mine.
- **Partner and meetup channels:** The plan ranks these above you. For each of 3 Mar, 10 Mar, 31 May, 20 Aug and 26 Aug, give partner communities, meetup organisers and the mailing list owner the date plus the ready-made assets. Don't negotiate deals, and don't move any dates.

## 3. Your 2 hours a week

You have about 64 h over 32 weeks, and the posts need roughly 15 to 20 h. Spend the slack on replies and speaker asks, not on filler content. A "tips" series would compete with your own announcements.

| When | Time | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Wk of 15 Feb | ~4 h | Tags, tracked links, accounts. Write and schedule posts 1–6 |
| Wk of 3 May | ~2 h | Write and schedule posts 7–13 |
| June | ~4 h | Build the kit. Prepare the speaker-ask email and the reveal slot sheet |
| Jul to mid-Aug | ~30 min per week | One card per confirmation, about 15 min each |
| Wk of 9 Aug | ~2 h | Posts 28–32 |
| Wk of 6 Sep | ~2 h | Posts 33–36 and the recap draft |

Re-check every scheduled batch against the plan's dates before each session and whenever a date moves. A scheduled post announcing a moved date is worse than none.

## 4. Amplification

**Default rung: ready-made kit plus per-speaker ask.**
- **Kit** (built once, in June): speaker cards, a 2–3 sentence copy block with the positioning sentence and tracked link, the hashtags labelled by purpose, one or two horizontal venue images, and a line on what not to do (don't announce a talk before the agenda is public).
- **Speaker ask:** Send it at confirmation, inside the email already going out. Attach the card and name the date the event's post goes out. Make the opt-out explicit ("if you'd rather not, nothing changes"). An ask without the asset attached is the usual cause of low take-up.
- **Organisers' personal accounts:** Ask each organiser whether they'll post, and record who agreed. Give them the same kit and their own tracked link. Keep the event page as the source of truth for practical details.
- **Sponsor thank-you:** Add it only if sponsor onboarding already collects a handle, and present it as goodwill. If an agreement contracts a mention, it becomes a fulfilment item for the sponsor-fulfilment owner. With no sponsors, delete this rung.
- **Coordinated partner push:** Supply dates and assets only where a deal already exists.

## 5. Live coverage

**Rung: single recap (#37).** You write the posts and presumably run the day, and one person can't do both.
- **Upgrade path:** To move to a roving poster, name a different volunteer in the run of show before the day.
- **Photos:** Name a photographer who drops files into one shared folder, named per session.
- **Before the day:** Settle these three things.
  - The badge-carried photo opt-out, owned by the accessibility owner.
  - The escalation path.
  - What the speaker agreements say about recording and quoting.
- **Skip the scheduled "doors are open" skeleton.** It produces nothing reusable.

## 6. Handover after the event

Pass the following to the content-repurposing owner, and stop there:
- The photo archive folder.
- The recordings.
- The quotable moments.
- The per-platform link data.
- The list of who amplified.
- A record of what was posted and how it did.

## 7. Measurement

No numeric engagement target, because any such figure would be false precision. Judge the channel on these self-set checks:
1. Every dated post shipped on its date.
2. The tracked links moved.
3. Personal-account links against the event page's link, to test whether personal accounts reach more.
4. How many speakers and partners posted, out of those asked.
5. The archive you end up with.

## Answers that would change this plan

1. **Photographer:** Who is a named, different person for photos and live posting?
2. **Edition:** Is this the first edition? That changes the warm-up posts and whether to build for compounding.
3. **Sponsors and partners:** Are there sponsors with handles, or signed partner deals?
4. **Platforms:** Do LinkedIn and Mastodon really hold different audiences for you?
