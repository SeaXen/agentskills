# KernelConf posting schedule: LinkedIn with a Mastodon mirror

**Assumption:** I used 2027 weekdays, because 14 October 2026 has already passed. If your year differs, the dates below shift. Under the 2027 calendar, CFP close (5 June) and early-bird close (5 September) fall on weekends, so those two closing posts are scheduled in advance.

## Rules

- **Volume:** about 41 posts in 32 weeks, mostly one a week. At 8% of the mix, social supports the mailing list, meetup network and partner communities. It doesn't compete with them.
- **Order:** send the mailing-list email first, then post the same day or the next morning. Subscribers hear it before the public.
- **Mirror:** post on LinkedIn, then paste the same text to Mastodon within the hour. Add 2–3 CamelCase hashtags on Mastodon (#KernelConf plus one or two topic tags).
- **Timing:** Tuesday to Thursday, 08:30–09:30 CET.
- **Reuse:** write every post so a meetup organiser or partner can forward it unchanged. Put a short copy-paste blurb in each CFP post for them. This is where social adds the most value.
- **Weekly 2 hours:**

| Task | Time |
|---|---|
| Write or finish the week's post | 60 min |
| Mirror and schedule | 15 min |
| Replies and DMs | 30 min |
| Look ahead | 15 min |

- **Banked weeks:** weeks marked "no post" are drafting time for the denser stretches later.

## Peak weeks

The densest weeks are 30 Aug–5 Sep (three posts) and the CFP deadline week (three posts). Both are drafted in the banked June weeks, so each stays within 2 hours.

Live coverage on 14 October doesn't fit the 2-hour budget. Schedule the doors-open post ahead and have someone else cover the day.

## Measuring it

Judge social by clicks to list signup and ticket pages, using UTM tags. Don't judge it by impressions. If a post type doesn't send people to the list or the ticket page, replace it with another type.
