**Verdict:** the tags aren't the main problem. Last year's sponsor numbers are, and you should fix them first.

## 1. Last year's reach numbers are probably wrong

`#observaday` is a standing community tag for the whole network. Roughly 30 independent cities use it, so most of its volume belongs to other cities. Reach pulled off that tag measures the network, not Lisbon.

- Tell your sponsor before you quote a new number. Say last year's figure came from the shared tag and included other cities' posts.
- Ask them what they want reported this year. This will be uncomfortable, but it's better than a sponsor finding out on their own.
- Never report the community tag's volume as your campaign's reach.

## 2. Your plan has no campaign tag

Federated events need two kinds of tag, and you only have one:

| Purpose | Tag | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Community signal (membership, finding the other cities) | `#observaday` | Keep it. Don't measure with it. |
| Edition measurement (this cycle, Lisbon only) | **missing** | Add one. |

A campaign tag would look like `#ObservaDayLisbon`. Before you fix it:

- Check the network's convention, because other cities may already use a pattern.
- Check that nobody else is using your pick.
- Lock it before the first post. Changing it mid-campaign splits the archive permanently, and you can't merge the two halves afterwards.

## 3. Eight extra tags is too many

Past about three tags per post, tags stop routing and start decorating. Here is how I'd cut your list:

- **Campaign tag:** `#ObservaDayLisbon`. It's the only one that gives you a Lisbon-only number.
- **Community tag:** `#observaday`.
- **One topic tag:** `#observability`. Swap in `#opentelemetry` on posts about an OTel talk or track.

Drop the rest:

- `#sre`, `#devops`, `#monitoring`, `#techevents` are huge, noisy and generic. Your posts vanish in them, and they give you nothing to measure.
- `#lisbon` and `#portugal` are a judgment call. Use `#lisbon` as your third tag on local-facing posts if you want local discovery, since you have no way to measure either. `#portugal` adds nothing.

These are judged defaults, not measured results. If your audience clusters on a specific tag, that answer outranks this list.

## 4. Get sponsor-grade reach from somewhere other than tag search

- **Per-platform tracked links.** Create them before launch, because they can't be added retroactively. Use one per platform and one per organizer's personal account.
- **Account analytics** for the event page's own impressions.
- **The campaign tag**, as a Lisbon-only post count.

Hashtag search is not a reach measurement on any platform. Don't promise a sponsor a number you can't pull cleanly.

## 5. Questions that could change this

I haven't asked you these, so the advice above rests on assumptions:

1. Which platforms does your audience actually use? That decides whether tags matter much. On some platforms they do little.
2. Does your network or the other cities have a tag convention?
3. Who posts, and how many hours a week do they have?
4. Does the sponsor agreement promise reach figures or a social mention? If it does, that's a deliverable, not a courtesy.
