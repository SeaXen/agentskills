**Short answer: add a few tags, but not all eight, and fix the measurement problem first.**

**1. Last year's reach numbers were probably network-wide.** ⚠️
- `#observaday` is shared by about 30 independently run events, so a number pulled off it includes every other city.
- If you gave your sponsor that number as Lisbon's reach, it was overstated.
- Decide whether to tell the sponsor before this year's report, or to label last year's figure clearly as "network-wide".

**2. Generic tags can't be measured.** 🎯
- `#devops`, `#monitoring`, `#techevents`, `#lisbon` and `#portugal` are flooded with unrelated posts.
- You can't separate your reach from everyone else's, so pulling numbers off them is meaningless.
- Extra tags can raise discovery but not attribution, so give each tag one job: discovery or measurement.

**3. Nine tags look like spam.**
- LinkedIn works best with about 3 to 5 tags.
- X works best with 1 or 2.
- Instagram tolerates more.

**Recommended set**

| Job | Tag | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Network identity | `#observaday` | Keep it. It's the shared brand. |
| Measurement | `#observadaylisbon` | Ours alone, so reach on it is truly yours. This one is new. |
| Discovery | `#observability` | Right topic, active audience |
| Discovery | `#opentelemetry` | Niche and on-target |
| Discovery (local) | `#lisbon` | Only on locally targeted posts |

**Skip these:**
- `#devops`, `#monitoring` and `#techevents` are too broad.
- `#sre` is a small audience and a maybe.
- `#portugal` is mostly tourism.

**4. Don't rely on tags for sponsor numbers.** ✅
- Use UTM links on every post and on the registration page.
- Add a "How did you hear about us?" field on registration.
- Use your own account analytics for impressions.
- Report registrations and attendance as the headline, and impressions as a secondary figure.

**5. Tell the network organizers.** ℹ️
- Ask whether other cities are also adding their own `#observaday<city>` tags.
- A shared convention helps everyone, and you avoid colliding with someone else's tag.
