# HackNorth social plan

**Verdict:** run one platform, your Discord. Don't post on the other four handles. You have about 3 hours a week, and this is a single-campus audience that already lives in one place.

## What I need from you first

The skill assumes the marketing plan has already fixed the dates, so I haven't invented any. I've used placeholders (`[T-x]`). Send me:

1. Doors date, signup deadline, and any other fixed dates (CFP, early-bird, agenda).
2. Whether you have sponsors, judges or mentors.
3. Whether you already reach students outside Discord, such as lecturers, class announcements or student-union mailings.
4. Whether HackNorth is one edition or meant to run yearly.

Until then, treat the dates below as a template.

## 1. Platform set

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Home platform | **Your Discord server** (judged) | Last year every signup came from it, and 1,900 members covers a 250-seat event many times over. |
| TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Facebook | **Deleted, not demoted** | Your audience is one campus on one server, so no second platform reaches a different audience. |

The "serious hackathons run five platforms" advice comes from MLH's campus-scale guide. That guide fits audiences spread across platforms, where no single one reaches even half of them. Yours is the opposite case.

At 3 hours a week, five accounts would turn into unattended profiles. An account whose last post is four months old subtracts credibility from the event.

**The five reserved handles:**
- Keep them. Reserving costs nothing and blocks squatters.
- Don't post on them.
- Put "Join us on Discord: [link]" in each bio, so anyone who searches finds the real home.
- Reopen one only if a named person volunteers to run it.

**Your real lever is conversion inside the server.** 1,900 members and 250 seats means the job is turning existing members into signups, not finding new reach.

## 2. Hashtag

- **One campaign tag**, for example `#HackNorth[year]`. Fix it before the first post, because it can't be merged later.
- You need no community tag and no topic tags.
- It matters mainly for the photo archive and any resharing on personal accounts.

## 3. Post plan (Discord, pinned to your dates)

One named person owns the account. The other approves.

| Phase | Post | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Announcement `[date]` | What it is, who it's for, date, signup link, and your positioning sentence. | `#announcements` pinned, plus a server-wide ping |
| Signup open | Myth-busting: answer the real reasons people don't sign up ("I don't have an idea", "I'm not good enough") with last year's real numbers. | `#general` |
| Mid-signup | A first-timer's arc, told hour by hour. | `#general` |
| Reveals (judges, mentors, prizes) | One card per person, each sent to that person first with the ask below. | `#announcements` |
| Deadline close | Countdown, one line, link only. | `#announcements` |
| Final push `[T-1 week]` | Practical details: what to bring, schedule shape, where to go. | `#announcements` |
| Wrap `[T+2 days]` | Thank-yous and the day's best photos. | `#announcements` |

- Write the whole batch in one sitting, about 2 hours. Re-check it whenever a date moves, because a scheduled post announcing an old date is worse than none.
- Alternate one post that gives something on its own with one plain announcement.
- **No posts-per-week target.** Every post hangs off a date.

## 4. Amplification asks

- **Default:** a ready-made kit (a card, a 2–3 sentence copy block, the hashtag and a tracked link), plus the per-speaker ask.
- The per-speaker ask is sent at confirmation, with the card attached, the date your post goes out, and an explicit opt-out.
- Each organiser reshares in their own channels. Ask each one and record who agreed; don't assume.
- **Delete the sponsor thank-you** if you have no sponsors.
- **Delete the partner push** if no partnership exists.

Speaker ask template:
> Your slot is confirmed, thank you. Attached: your card and a short copy block. We post our announcement on [date]. Sharing yours around then helps. If you'd rather not, nothing changes. Tag: [tag]. Link: [tracked link].

## 5. Day-of coverage

- **Default:** the single recap. This is one post after doors close with real photos and thank-yous, about 30 minutes.
- Move up to a roving poster only if a third person is named in the run of show.
- The reason is that both of you are running the day, and the person covering a venue problem can't also post.
- **Photo rules**, settled before the day:
  - A badge-carried opt-out.
  - What to do with a photo taken before the opt-out was noticed.
  - One person to escalate to.
- **Archive:** one shared folder, named per session. Hand it to the repurposing step afterwards; I won't cut clips.

## 6. Measurement

- **No engagement-rate target.** That would be false precision.
- **Every dated post shipped on its date.** This is the one that fails most often on volunteer teams.
- **Tracked signup source:**
  - Add one required "how did you hear about us?" field to the form.
  - Give each channel its own link: `#announcements`, personal accounts and any off-Discord channel.
- **Amplification take-up:** how many of the people you asked actually posted. A low rate usually means the ask arrived without a ready-made asset.
- **Archive produced:** photos and quotes you can reuse next edition.
