## 4. Amplification

**Default rung: ready-made kit plus per-speaker ask.**

**Speaker ask (send now, attach the card):**

> Your talk is confirmed, thank you. Attached are your speaker card and a short copy block you can use.
> We post your card on [date]. If you'd like to share yours around then, it genuinely helps. If you'd rather not, that's completely fine.
> Hashtag: [tag]. Link: [tracked link]. Please don't share an unpublished agenda.

- Name each speaker's own date in the email. The speaker's post can then ride the event's.
- It must carry an explicit opt-out and the attached asset. A missing asset is the usual reason take-up is low.
- If different internal owners manage different speakers, hand each of them their kit. Don't route everything through one queue.

**Organizer personal accounts.** Ask each organizer whether they will reshare, and record who agreed. Give them the same kit and their own tracked link. Keep the event page as the canonical home. [low confidence]
