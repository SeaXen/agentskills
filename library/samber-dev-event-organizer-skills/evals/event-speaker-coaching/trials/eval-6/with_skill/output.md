## 3. Q&A: give it a host

The 10 minutes of open floor with no host is the format where a long comment or a hostile challenge lands on the speaker alone. Yusuf dreads it, and the room has 300 seats, so I recommend written questions with a host.

- **Format:** cards or a form. The host reads them out, easiest first.
- **Host:** you or a briefed volunteer. They sit where Yusuf can see them.
- **Three moves, added to your run-of-show:**
  1. A comment that isn't a question: "Thanks. Is there a question for the speaker?" Then move on.
  2. A hostile or demeaning question: the host closes it under the code of conduct.
  3. Out of time: the host ends Q&A on time and says where Yusuf will be afterwards.
- **Fairness:** if you change the format for his slot, decide whether it applies to the whole hall or only to him. Tell any other speaker whose slot changes.
- **Don't spring this on him as a surprise.** Tell him the format before his dry run, so he rehearses the right one.

## 5. Nerves support, in order

1. A listened run early. Each one makes the real one less new.
2. The real room: the photo now, the walk-through on the day.
3. Naming what will happen: 300 seats, who introduces him, timing cards, what happens if the demo breaks, how Q&A works.
4. Optional reappraisal: saying "I'm excited" instead of "calm down". Present it as a cheap thing to try, not a promise.
5. The recovery move (above), rehearsed.
6. A friendly face: the host or mentor sits where he can see them.

**Don't offer:**

- "You'll be fine."
- Power poses.
- Memorising the talk.
- Any medication advice.
