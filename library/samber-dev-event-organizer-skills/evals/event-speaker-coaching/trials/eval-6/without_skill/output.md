## 1. The reply

**Subject: Re: your talk, and thank you for telling me**

Hi Yusuf,

Thank you for writing this. Telling me took some courage, and I'd much rather know now than find out on the day.

First, the talk is good. I heard the early run, and it works. You're not at risk of embarrassing yourself. The part that's hard is the stage, and that's a separate problem we can work on.

I'd change two things.

**1. The Q&A.** The 10 minutes of open-floor questions with nobody hosting is our gap, not your job. I'll put a host on stage with you. They will:
- introduce you
- take questions from the room and from a shared question list
- read each question aloud before you answer
- pick the questions, so you never face a silent room or a rambling one
- call time

I'll also plant a few easy questions so the first one is never cold. If you'd like the Q&A shorter, or moved to a side room after the talk, say so and I'll arrange it.

**2. Learning it word for word.** I'd steer you away from this one. A memorized script feels safe in the first week and fails the first time you lose a word on stage, because there is no way back in. People who prepare this way tend to get more anxious, not less. Here is what tends to work:
- Memorize only the first minute, the transitions between sections, and the last 30 seconds.
- Know the rest as 5 to 7 key points per section, and say it differently each time.
- Practice aloud, standing up, and record yourself once.
- Add two or three rehearsals with real listeners, a bit bigger each time.

It's your talk, so use whatever method you prefer. I only wanted to say this before you spend five weeks on the script.

On the sleep and the beta blockers: I can't advise on that, and it's your call. If you're considering it, please see a doctor or pharmacist soon, not the week before. Never try anything new for the first time on the day. If the sleeplessness keeps up, that's worth raising with a doctor whatever happens with the talk.

I'd also like you to have a real choice. If it still feels wrong in a couple of weeks, tell me. There are options, like a shorter slot, a co-presenter, or a recorded talk with live Q&A. I'd rather change the format than lose you, and there's no penalty. Let's check in on **Nov 9** and decide together then.

Could you do a 20-minute call this week? I'd like to hear what scares you most: the 25 minutes, the room, the questions, or something else. The fix is different for each.
