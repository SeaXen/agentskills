## Check these before sending

- **Name the co-speaker only if their confirmation is public or they agreed.** Otherwise write "a speaker who works on [problem]". An angle must be true, and naming someone without consent breaks that rule.
- **Use the exact budget line from the first email.** If travel and lodging were vague then, fix that now. A speaker outside the hub who sees a night left unpaid will decline without saying why.
- **Check the travel gate.** If she must travel, the 25th is inside the 4–6 week floor for arranging a trip and employer approval. The optional line names that constraint instead of letting her discover it. Keep it only if remote delivery or a shorter slot is on your negotiable list. Don't offer anything beyond that list.
- **Don't write the co-speaker as a threat.** Don't say "your slot may go to them." That is invented urgency, and it is deleted from the skill. The 25th is your only real deadline.
- **Run it through a humanizer pass**, and show it to whoever owns the event before it goes out.
