- **Day 7:** one close-out with a fresh angle (a narrowed ask, or the committee member with their consent), then stop.

- **Never bump.** Don't write "did you see my message?".
- **Switch to a long-runway cadence** (1–2 weeks between touches, capped at three) if she needs employer sign-off, or if she must travel and your answer date is inside 4–6 weeks. Say that constraint in the message.
- Treat silence past the cap as a no, and release the slot.
