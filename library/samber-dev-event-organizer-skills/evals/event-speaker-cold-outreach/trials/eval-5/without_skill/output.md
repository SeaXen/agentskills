## Steps

1. **Pick one sender.** Use a named organizer, not "the committee". Give a reply address on your conference domain, so she can answer by email if she wants.
2. **Send the contact-form message** (draft below).
3. **Send the Mastodon DM** right after. Say you used the form, so she doesn't read it as a duplicate.
4. **Wait 7 days.** Then send one follow-up on the same channel. Don't use other channels for it.
5. **Stop after 2 messages with no reply.** Silence is an answer. Don't escalate to Discord or GitHub.

## Tips

- Name the specific work you admire. A generic invitation gets ignored.
- State the logistics and money up front. This is the first thing a busy speaker checks.
- Give her an easy way to say no.
- Verify that the Mastodon account and the site belong to her before sending. Check that the site links to the Mastodon profile, or the reverse.
- Don't name the PC member in the first message. If she's open to it, mention the connection later.
