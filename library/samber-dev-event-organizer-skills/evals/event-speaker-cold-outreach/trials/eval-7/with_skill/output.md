## Plan once the packet is complete

- **Channel:** a referrer-relayed intro if your co-organizer knows him. Otherwise direct email to an address he published. Don't use a guessed address, and don't approach him inside a project forum or chat.
- **Personalization:** the sourced "why" as the default. Promote it to fresh-work personalization if he scores Hot.
- **Cadence:** three touches by default. Day 0, Day 3 only with new information, Day 7 with a fresh hook plus the close-out, then stop. Never send a bump.
- **Fee:** don't invent a rate. If he asks, answer from your budget line, and name who approves any fee and by when.
- **Negotiable list:** fix it before the first message. Choose from travel dates, slot length, remote delivery, co-speaker seat, companion ticket, recording terms, time of day and an introduction.
- **Close:** on a yes, confirm every term in writing the same day. On a no, record the reason and ask for a referral. After the cadence cap, treat silence as a no and release the slot.
