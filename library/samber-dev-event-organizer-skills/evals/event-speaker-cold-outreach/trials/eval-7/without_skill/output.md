## Message B: Reply to the platinum sponsor

Fill the `[brackets]` first. Check what the sponsor contract promises. If it lists a speaking slot, the offer below should match it.

**Subject:** [Conference name] 2027: speaking opportunities for [Company]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for platinum sponsoring [Conference name]. We're glad to have [Company] with us. Your EUR 12,000 directly funds [venue / travel grants / ticket fund: pick one that is true].
>
> We'd love to have [VP name] on stage. Here is how we can do it:
>
> 1. **Main stage, through our program committee.** [VP name] is warmly invited to submit a talk proposal by [CFP deadline]. The main stage is curated by volunteers on one criterion: what attendees will learn. Every talk goes through the same review, including sponsors'. A technical talk from [VP name] on [topic they know well, e.g. how you run X in production] would be a strong submission. We'll send you a direct link and can give feedback before the deadline.
> 2. **[Sponsor session, if your package includes one]:** a [20]-minute slot on [Track/room, not main stage], labeled "Sponsored session" in the program. [VP name] can present your product, roadmap, or customer story there with no review.
> 3. **Panel or fireside chat:** if [VP name] prefers a conversation to a solo talk, we can add them to a [topic] panel, subject to the same curation.
>
> We keep this separation because attendees come for unbiased content. It is also what makes your sponsorship worth more to you: a main-stage slot that is visibly earned carries more credibility than one that is visibly bought.
>
> Could we have a 15-minute call this week to pick the best option? I'm free [two time slots].
>
> Best,
> [Your name]
> [Conference name] organizing team

**Why I wrote it this way:**
- On a volunteer conference, selling the main stage damages the thing sponsors are paying for. Attendees notice.
- It doesn't refuse the sponsor. It gives them three concrete paths and a call.
- Option 2 is a commitment. Delete it if your sponsorship tiers don't include a session.
- 🎯 If the contract already promises "keynote" or "main stage", don't send this. Tell me and I'll rewrite it around honoring the contract.
