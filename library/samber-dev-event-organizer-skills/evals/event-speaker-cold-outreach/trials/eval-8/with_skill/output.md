# Aurélie said yes: what to do now

⏳ Send the written confirmation **today**. The skill says same-day, because a condition agreed on a call and missing from the confirmation is a condition nobody agreed. Nothing goes out until you approve the draft.

## 1. Check five things before sending

1. **Afternoon slot.** The skill says never promise what you don't control. Is an afternoon 25-minute slot free on the program? If not, find out now rather than after she has told her childcare people.
2. **Day of week.** 14 Feb 2027 is a **Sunday**. Check that this is intended. If it is, say so in the email, since weekend childcare may differ from what she assumed.
3. **Colleague.**
   - Who is the colleague, and which employer?
   - A sponsor can't buy a slot, so if the employer sponsors you, the colleague gets the same terms as anyone else.
   - This person was not vetted in sourcing. Who reviews the co-presenter, and in which lane? The packet's invited-review lane applies, and `event-talk-selection` needs that stated.
4. **What the event covers for the colleague.** Ticket only, or travel and lodging too? Ghent may need a night covered for both of them. The co-speaker seat costs you one ticket by default. Anything beyond that is your call, or someone else's if you lack authority (Q9). Name that person and a date if so.
5. **Recording.** You said you'd check, so check these before answering her:
   - Do you record sessions?
   - Do you publish them, where, and when?
   - Can a speaker opt out, or must she approve before publication?
   - Which license applies?
   - Does the employer need to sign off?

   Don't answer from memory or guess. If last edition's recordings are public, you can give her one exact figure and where to check it.
