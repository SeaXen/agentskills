# Baltic Rust Days speaker travel plan

**Recommendation:** don't use the treasurer's plan for everyone. Split speakers by cost and risk.

- **Hotels (all 9):** you book centrally.
- **Flights for the 4 EU speakers:** they self-book under a cap, and you reimburse fast.
- **Flights for India, Brazil and the US (5 speakers):** you book, or you pay the airline directly.

## Why not "everyone books, then claims back"

- The five long-haul tickets are about 60% of the budget. Asking a speaker from India or Brazil to float roughly 1,000 EUR for 4+ months is a real barrier. Some will decline or drop out, and others will book the wrong fare.
- Uncapped self-booking lets the total drift past 9,000 EUR. You only find out at claim time.
- International reimbursement means FX loss and SWIFT fees for the speaker, plus follow-up emails for your treasurer.
- Your call said "travel and two nights covered". Reimbursing only after the event is a weaker offer than speakers will assume you made.

The reserve is about 7%. It covers fare spikes, a third hotel night for someone who can't land in time, and bank fees. Meals and local transport are not included. Say so in writing.

## How to run it

1. **Confirm what "covered" means, in writing, this week (by 21 Oct).**
   - Send all 9 speakers one email with the policy, using the draft below.
   - It should state: economy flights, a cap per person, 2 hotel nights (the night before and the night of the talk), and what is excluded.
   - Decide now whether visa fees and travel insurance are covered. I'd cover them, because India speakers can't attend without them.
2. **Collect details by 28 Oct.** From each speaker you need the legal name exactly as on their passport, the home airport, and passport validity beyond 3 March plus 3 months. You also need to know whether they can float a ticket for 1–2 weeks.
3. **Start the India visas first.**
   - Send invitation letters now. The Schengen application window opens 6 months before travel, so it is already open.
   - Appointments are the bottleneck. Set an application deadline of 1 Dec.
   - Book the ticket within 5 working days of the visa being granted, and no later than 15 Jan.
   - If a visa is refused, fall back to a remote talk. Don't buy a non-refundable ticket before the visa.
4. **Book the hotel block now.**
   - Use one hotel near the venue, with one invoice paid by the treasurer.
   - That replaces 9 reimbursements with 1 payment, which is the admin saving she is after.
   - Ask for free cancellation until about 2 weeks out.
5. **Book the long-haul flights through the organisers.**
   - Use one card or one travel agent, with one invoice per speaker or one combined invoice.
   - Target mid-Nov to mid-Jan, depending on the visa.
   - If the treasurer insists on the claim model, make it "reimbursed within 7 days of ticket receipt". Never "after the event".
   - Offer it only to speakers who said they can float the money.
6. **EU speakers self-book under the cap.**
   - They send the receipt and IBAN, and you pay by SEPA within 14 days.
   - Any amount over the cap is theirs, or needs your approval before booking.
7. **Track spend in one sheet.** Keep a row per speaker with cap, booked amount and status, and review it at the end of every month. If the forecast exceeds 8,350 EUR, act early with one of these:
   - cheaper hotel
   - tighter caps on flights not yet booked
   - sponsor cover for one or two tickets
   - a remote talk for the most expensive trip
8. **Pay after travel.** Settle the final claims within 14 days of the event. Check with your accountant whether covering non-resident speakers has any Latvian tax or reporting implications.

## Pitch for the treasurer

Her plan means 9 claims, plus bank-transfer chasing across 5 countries. This plan means 1 hotel invoice, 5 airline bookings and 4 SEPA payments. If she doesn't want to hold the booking card, a travel agent billing once is still less work than 9 claims.

## Check before sending

- **Event dates and arrival day:** is it a single day on 3 March? If some long-haul speakers can't arrive by 2 March, plan a third night for them and use the reserve.
- **Latvia-based speakers:** are any of the 5 other speakers outside Riga, and do they expect travel too?
- **US and EU speakers:** check the current ETIAS and entry-rule status for 2026–27, because the rollout has slipped before.
