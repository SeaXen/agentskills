# Hollowmere AV and green room plan

**Main change:** stop checking at a desk right before the talk. Do the check in the actual room, at a time you assign, a day or half-day ahead. Keep the desk only as a help point.

## Why the desk plan fails

- It tests nothing real. The desk has no stage projector, no room audio and no stage cable run, so a pass there proves little.
- Every speaker shows up when they are most anxious and the schedule is tightest. Back-to-back talks across two tracks produce a queue at the desk.
- A failed check right before the slot leaves no time to fix it.
- One volunteer with an adapter bag is a single point of failure.

## 1. Collect slides and specs now (this week)

Send speakers a short form with a deadline of 5 days before the event. It should ask for:

- Slides as a PDF, plus the native file.
- Laptop OS and port type (USB-C, HDMI, none).
- Whether they have video, audio or live demos.
- Aspect ratio (16:9 or 4:3).
- Whether they need a clicker or a lapel mic.

You then know in advance which adapters you need. You also have a backup copy of every deck.

## 2. Run the check in the room

1. Book a 10-minute slot per speaker, in the room they will speak in.
2. Do it the evening before, or 08:00–10:00 on the day for afternoon speakers. 16 speakers at 10 minutes each is about 2h40 in total, and with both tracks running in parallel it is under 1h30 per room.
3. The check covers: connect, mirror versus extend, slide advance, audio or video playback, clicker, mic, and the timer or confidence monitor if there is one.
4. Send each speaker their slot time by email and add it to the speaker schedule.
5. A speaker who can't attend gets a 5-minute check in the changeover before their talk. They still go to the room, not to a desk.

## 3. Put a loaner laptop in each room

- One house laptop per room, with every PDF preloaded and tested.
- Fallback rule: if a speaker's laptop does not connect in 2 minutes, switch to the loaner and their PDF. Nobody debugs on stage.
- Speakers who want to present from the loaner can give you their deck in advance. This removes most adapter problems.

## 4. Replace the AV desk with room captains

- Assign 2 trained volunteers per room, so no single person is a point of failure.
- They stay in the room all day. They run checks, changeovers and the fallback rule.
- Split the adapter bag: one labeled kit per room.
- Inventory and test it this week against the real venue cables. A minimum kit:
  - USB-C to HDMI
  - Mini DisplayPort to HDMI
  - Spare HDMI cable
  - USB-C hub
  - Clicker with spare batteries
  - Power strip
  - Gaffer tape
- Buy what is missing. It is cheap compared with a failed talk.
- Keep one roaming AV lead, reachable by phone or radio, for escalation.

## 5. Fix the green room

The room has four uses that conflict. Give it one purpose, speakers, and move the rest.

| Use | Move to |
|---|---|
| Volunteer briefing, 08:00, 22 people | Main hall or a registration area, before doors. Finish by 08:45. |
| Organizer laptops | A lockable office or the organizer desk at registration. They are also a security risk for attendee data. |
| Lost-property box | Registration desk. |
| Speakers | Green room, kept quiet. |

If the venue gives you nowhere else, hold the briefing in the green room and clear it by 08:45. Before 08:45 it is not a green room. Put the lost-property box at registration regardless.

The green room should have: water, coffee, a table, power strips, a printed schedule, and a sign reading "Speakers only".

## 6. Day-of flow

1. The speaker checks in at registration and gets a badge marking them as a speaker.
2. A speaker host takes them to the green room.
3. The host walks them to the room 15 minutes before their slot.
4. The room captain does a final 2-minute check: deck open, display mode, mic, timer.
5. After the talk, the host returns them to the green room or to the hall.

## 7. Two-week timeline

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Now | Send the speaker form. Decide where the briefing, laptops and lost property move. |
| T-10 days | Audit the adapter bag and buy the missing items. Confirm the venue's projector inputs and audio in each room. |
| T-7 days | Slides are due. Load the loaner laptops. Send check-slot times. |
| T-3 days | Brief room captains. Run a dry run of one full check with a volunteer's laptop. |
| T-1 | Run the room checks. Set up the green room. Print the signs and schedule. |
| Event day | Briefing at 08:00, out of the green room by 08:45. |

## Questions that would change the plan

1. Can you access the rooms the evening before?
2. What inputs do the venue projectors have (HDMI, USB-C, VGA)?
3. Does each room have a house audio system and mics?
4. Is there radio or phone coverage for your AV lead across the warehouse?
