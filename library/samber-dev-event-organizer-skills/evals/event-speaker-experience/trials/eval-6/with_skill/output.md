**No, not as one email.** Four things need to change. The skill's post-event follow-up rules are the basis for each.

## What breaks

1. **Feedback and the "come back next year?" question can't share a message.** The rule is "never deliver feedback and a request in the same message." Scores sit next to an ask, and the speaker can't tell whether the ask depends on how they scored. Send the return question separately, later, once.
2. **One email to all 19 doesn't work.**
   - Scores are per session, so each speaker should see only their own.
   - A shared thread with everyone on it leaks addresses and scores. The skill deletes that option outright: use per-speaker threads or a mailing tool that merges fields.
   - A thank-you must be individual and name one specific thing about the talk. A generic blast to someone who prepared for a month reads as an obligation discharged.
3. **The playlist link is the wrong link.** Each speaker should get the direct link to their own session. The playlist can sit alongside it.
4. **Holding back the rough comments is the weakest part, though not the worst.** The skill says to pass on written comments in full, minus abuse, because filtering the negative ones "teaches a speaker nothing and they will assume you did it."
   - If the comments are abusive (personal attacks, slurs), cut those lines and tell the speaker you did.
   - If they are only blunt, send them, with your own read as context.
   - Only two speakers are affected. The other 17 get scores and comments as normal.
   - Whatever you remove, say that you removed it. Silently dropping comments is the path to avoid.

## Things to check before sending

- **Lateness.** The thank-you was due within days of 12 September. Feedback was due within a week or two. This is about four months late, so say so plainly in the first line. Don't imply it's on time.
- **"Tell them before it goes live."** The videos went up last Thursday, so that window has passed. Own it in the email. Don't hide it.
- **Consent record.** Before you send anyone a link, match the published videos to the per-session record: format, licence and opt-out answer. All 19 need a row. If a speaker opted out, or a condition was attached, find out now, because the email will point them straight at a live video.
- **Retention pass.** Delete leftover travel documents, ID scans and payment details this week, on the schedule you stated when you collected them. This is separate from the email, but it is overdue too.

## What to send instead

**Message 1, now, one per speaker.** It contains:
- a specific thank-you
- the apology for the delay
- their direct video link, plus the playlist
- where else the video lives, if anywhere
- the title as published and the thumbnail
- a line saying they may post it themselves
- their own scores
- their written comments (full, minus abuse)
- your read as host

It asks for nothing.

**Message 2, a week or two later, one per speaker.** A single specific ask: would you speak again, or refer a peer? Record each answer.
