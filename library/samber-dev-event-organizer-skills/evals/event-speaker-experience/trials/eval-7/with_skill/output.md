## How to stop this happening next year

The skill's rule is to pin each deadline to the last date the material is usable. Your printer date was the real constraint, but "last Friday" was only an arbitrary deadline.

1. **Work backward from print.** Ask the printer for lead time in September. Set the bio and headshot deadline at least a week before the print date, with buffer for stragglers.
2. **Collect bio and headshot at acceptance.** Put them in the "what we need next" line of the acceptance email. Fewer people are "chaotic" in the week they've just said yes.
3. **Name one speaker contact.** One person, one address, one stated reply window, from confirmation through the event.
4. **Send a speaker packet.** It is one document with the slot, the deadlines and the real reason for each, the recording policy, travel mechanics, and the contact.
5. **Use two reminders, each with new information.**
   - First reminder, at about half the remaining time: name the missing items, state the real reason for the date, and say everything else on their side is done.
   - Second reminder, on the deadline: state the actual consequence, and offer to renegotiate.
   - Never send a reminder that only repeats the previous one.
6. **Send per-speaker threads only.** With 12 speakers, that's the default. Use milestone messages for any first-time speaker.
7. **Keep a tracker with one row per speaker.** Record the date each item arrived and which reminder produced it. Next year's ladder is built from that record.
8. **Measure material completeness at the deadline.** This is the skill's gate: every accepted speaker has a bio, headshot, final title, A/V requirements and travel details by the date each becomes unusable. If it fails, the ladder is wrong.
9. **Record this edition's lateness.** Note which five were late and after which reminder. If the same people are late again, move them to the milestone cadence.
