## 🔴 Collected counsel flags (read first)

1. **No contracting entity.**
   - Who signs for the organizer, and who is personally liable if the event fails or a claim arises?
   - Volunteers who sign personally may carry that exposure themselves.
   - Ask counsel whether to form an association, or use a fiscal host or umbrella body, before signing. This is also covered by the team-structure skill in this collection.
2. **Payment into a personal bank account.**
   - Hexabyte's procurement will likely refuse a personal IBAN, and may refuse a counterparty with no legal entity at all.
   - Counsel and an accountant should address tax treatment of the receipt (VAT, income characterisation), invoice issuance without an entity, and whether the money is held on trust for the event.
   - Do not paper over this with a clause. Fix the structure first, or disclose it to Hexabyte plainly.
3. **Liability and indemnity scope** (§8), including whether any liability cap is enforceable in France.
4. **Whether the late-interest rate and the refund and de-branding mechanics** are enforceable under the governing law (§4, §17).

6. **Data protection.** Applies only if a lead-retrieval benefit exists; none was sold (§14).
7. **Insurance.** Whether the venue contract or any organizer cover applies, and the French equivalent of the CGL standard (§9).

## 4. Payment 🟢 / 🟠

- **Schedule:** 100% of 7,500 EUR, due within 15 days of invoice and before [date]. This is the default on real cards (SCCE, PyCon US 2020).
  - 🟠 Staged deposit and balance is the alternative. Offer it only if Hexabyte's procurement says it cannot release the full amount before a fiscal boundary.
  - Invoicing after the event is excluded. It removes your only leverage.
- **Late payment:** interest at 12% per annum from the due date. Verified published rates run 12-18% (TMS Awards 12%, AFCEA 18%). Interest is recoverable only if the contract says so. 🔴 Enforceability under French law.
- **Non-payment:**
  - The obligation survives the sponsor's non-attendance.
  - The organizer may withhold perks, including passes and table access, until payment.
  - The organizer may immediately remove the sponsor's branding from the website and event materials (AWMA).
- **Payee:** the account details depend on flag 2. **Do not state a personal IBAN here until counsel and your accountant advise.**

## 5. Sponsor withdrawal 🟢 (separate clause)

Two-tier cutoff, in writing, measured against the event start:
- Refund **50%** of the fee if written notice arrives at least **[60] days** before the event. 🟠 Figures are placeholders. Published examples: IBTTA 50% before a cutoff; PyCon US 2020 75% at 120 days, 50% at 80 days.
- Nothing refunded after that date.
- The sponsor remains responsible for removing its marks from materials already produced.

No discretionary refunds.

## 6. Organizer cancellation 🟢 (separate clause, narrower)

If the organizer cancels for a reason other than force majeure:
- It refunds the sponsor's proportionate share of fees received after deducting expenses already incurred, within 45 days of the cancellation notice (the PyCon US 2020 shape).
- Before the lanyard print lock the expenses deduction is small. After it, it is larger. 🔴 Counsel to decide the documentation standard for "expenses incurred."
- This is **not** a mirror of §5. The sponsor's own activation costs (travel, collateral) are not refunded. Expect Hexabyte's counsel to raise this.

## 7. Force majeure 🟢

**Triggers:** fire, flood, earthquake, natural disaster, war, terrorism, strike, epidemic, pandemic, government order or public-health directive, venue closure or destruction, sustained utility or network failure, and any similar cause beyond the organizer's reasonable control.

**Remedy for each branch, named in words** (courts read these clauses literally: *NetOne v. Panache*, D. Haw. 2020, refund denied on silent text; *Sanders v. Edison Ballroom*, N.Y. 2021, $45,219.28 refunded on explicit text):
- **Event cancelled:** pro-rata refund net of documented expenses already incurred, within 45 days. 🟢 Volunteer-run, no reserve to absorb a full refund.
- **Event postponed:** the sponsorship carries over to the new date. If the new date is more than **[90] days** later, the sponsor may terminate by written notice within **[14] days** of the announcement and receive the same pro-rata refund. 🟠
- **Event converted to virtual or reduced:** the sponsor may elect within [14] days to carry the sponsorship over to the changed format, or terminate and receive the same pro-rata refund. 🟠 No virtual deliverables are promised. If you want to offer any, add them in an amendment.
- **Duration:** either party may terminate if force majeure prevents performance for 30 days or more (Personal Finance Society).

Considered and not chosen: sponsor-elected credit toward the next edition (Linux Foundation 2021). It needs a confirmed next edition.

## 8. Liability and indemnification 🔴

Two real shapes exist. Present both and let counsel and the parties choose:
- **(a) One-sided, organizer-favouring (PyCon US 2020):** the sponsor indemnifies the organizer for claims from its participation, and the organizer's exposure is capped at the amount actually paid.
- **(b) Mutual (Template Republic):** each party indemnifies the other.

Sponsors generally ask for (b) with broad coverage. Which shape lands is a matter of bargaining power.

**Special concern here:** an unincorporated organizer has no liability shield, so any cap has to work for the individuals signing. 🔴

## 9. Insurance 🟠🔴

**Proposed rung:** **$1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate commercial general liability** (or the French/EUR equivalent 🔴), because the sponsor will staff a table.
- Organizer, and venue if its contract requires it, named as additional insured.
- Certificate due by **[date, tied to signature, not event day]**. The table is held until it arrives.
- A single-event policy is acceptable.

**Alternative:** no sponsor-carried insurance (small events rely on the venue's master policy). This is a real position, but it must be stated explicitly, not left silent.

Open questions:
- Does the venue contract require cover that flows down?
- Does the organizer have any cover for itself? An unincorporated group probably does not. 🔴

- A sponsor employee selected through the open call is not a breach. That slot is earned through the call and confers nothing on the sponsor.
- 🟠 If you cap sponsor-affiliated speakers per edition, state the number here. It is applied at selection.

## 16. Term, cure, termination, renewal 🟢
