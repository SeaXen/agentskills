# KernelDays Bordeaux: Gold sponsorship contract for Hexabyte Cloud

## Read before sending

⚠️ **The contract is drafted but has a structural problem you need to decide on first.** An unincorporated group has no legal personality in France. Whoever signs is personally liable, and money paid into a personal account is likely to be treated as that person's income. Hexabyte's procurement team will probably also reject an IBAN in a private person's name.

Your options, best first:

1. 🥇 **Incorporate as an association loi 1901 before Friday.** The online declaration is quick, and a récépissé gives you a SIREN and SIRET, so the association can open a bank account in its own name. This is not instant, so check whether Hexabyte will accept "in formation" status with the account details to follow.
2. **Use an existing association as fiscal host.** Ask a friendly association to sign and receive the money, with a written agreement between you.
3. **Sign now through the treasurer, with the safeguards below.** This works legally, but the treasurer carries the risk personally. The draft is built to make this work: Clause 1 states the situation openly, Clause 11 allows transfer to a future association, and Clause 5 lets Hexabyte pay into the treasurer's account.

Other points to settle:

- ℹ️ **VAT.** I assumed no VAT is charged (art. 293 B CGI, the small-business exemption). If the organiser is, or becomes, VAT-registered, change Clause 4 to 7,500 EUR plus VAT. Confirm with an accountant.
- ℹ️ **Placeholders.** Fill in everything in `[brackets]`: dates, venue, names and the notice address.
- ℹ️ **Legal review.** This is a solid working draft, not legal advice. Have a lawyer or your accountant skim it once, especially the personal-liability and tax points.

# SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT

**KernelDays Bordeaux [YEAR]**

**Between**

**[FULL NAME], Treasurer**, residing at [ADDRESS], acting in the name and on behalf of the volunteer organising committee of the conference "KernelDays Bordeaux" (the "**Organiser**"),

**and**

**Hexabyte Cloud**, [LEGAL FORM] with registered office at [ADDRESS], registered under number [SIREN / REGISTRATION NUMBER], VAT number [VAT ID], represented by [NAME, TITLE], duly authorised (the "**Sponsor**").

Each is a "Party" and together they are the "Parties".

## Preamble

KernelDays Bordeaux is a systems conference run by volunteers. It gathered about 320 attendees at its previous edition. The Sponsor wishes to support the [YEAR] edition (the "**Event**") at the Gold tier of the Organiser's published rate card. The Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Status of the Organiser

1.1 The Organiser is not, at the date of signature, a legal entity. It is an informal group of volunteers. The Treasurer signs on behalf of that group and receives funds on its behalf.

1.2 The Sponsor acknowledges this and accepts that payment is made to the bank account described in Clause 5.

1.3 The Organiser may incorporate (for example as an association under the French law of 1 July 1901) and transfer this Agreement under Clause 11.

1.4 Funds received under this Agreement are used solely to organise the Event and are accounted for separately from the Treasurer's personal funds in the Organiser's books.

## 2. Event

- Name: KernelDays Bordeaux [YEAR]
- Date(s): [DATES]
- Venue: [VENUE, CITY]
- Expected attendance: comparable to the previous edition (about 320). This is an estimate, not a guarantee.

## 3. Sponsorship benefits (Gold tier)

The Organiser grants the Sponsor the following (the "**Benefits**"):

1. **Website:** the Sponsor's logo and a link to the Sponsor's website on the Event's sponsor page, from [DATE] until [DATE, e.g. 12 months after the Event or the publication of the next edition].
2. **Lanyard:** the Sponsor's logo on the attendee lanyards. The Sponsor supplies print-ready artwork by [DATE]. Placement, size and print method are chosen by the Organiser, in consultation with the Sponsor on the proof.
3. **Hallway table:** one table of approximately 3 m × 2 m in the hallway, for the full duration of the Event. The Organiser provides the table and [power / chairs / other, if any]. The Sponsor staffs, equips and cleans its own space. Setup and teardown windows: [TIMES].
4. **Passes:** four (4) full conference passes, personal to the Sponsor's employees or guests. Passes may be reassigned to another person from the Sponsor's organisation or its guests, but not resold. Names are communicated by [DATE].

No other benefit is granted unless agreed in writing. The Sponsor has no exclusivity and no right of approval over other sponsors or the programme.

## 4. Price

4.1 The sponsorship fee is **EUR 7,500 (seven thousand five hundred euros)**, the price of the Gold tier on the Organiser's rate card.

4.2 [**Option A, default:** This amount is final. No VAT is charged (VAT not applicable, art. 293 B CGI).] [**Option B:** This amount is exclusive of VAT, which is added at the rate in force.]

4.3 No other fees apply. Each Party bears its own costs, including the Sponsor's travel, staff and materials.

## 5. Payment

5.1 The Organiser issues an invoice or payment request on signature. The Sponsor pays within **30 days of the invoice date**, and in any case before [DATE, at least 14 days before the Event].

5.2 Payment is by bank transfer to the following account, held by the Treasurer on behalf of the Organiser:

- Account holder: [FULL NAME OF TREASURER]
- IBAN: [IBAN]
- BIC: [BIC]
- Reference to quote: "KernelDays [YEAR] Gold – Hexabyte Cloud"

5.3 The Sponsor may verify the account details with the Organiser by phone on a number it already knows, before its first payment. The Organiser will confirm any change of account details only in a document signed by the Treasurer.

5.4 Late payment: as provided by French commercial law, late payments bear interest at three times the legal interest rate, plus a fixed recovery fee of EUR 40.

5.5 If the Organiser incorporates before payment, it may require payment to a new account in its own name by written notice, and the Sponsor will pay there.

## 6. Sponsor obligations

6.1 The Sponsor supplies logos (vector format) and all other materials by the dates given in Clause 3 and is responsible for their content.

6.2 The Sponsor, its staff and its guests comply with the Event's code of conduct, the venue's rules and applicable law.

6.3 The Sponsor does not sell products at the Event, or collect personal data from attendees, other than with the attendee's consent (for example, a scan or a form the attendee fills in voluntarily).

6.4 The Sponsor does not use the Event name, logo or attendees' details beyond what is stated here without the Organiser's written approval. Factual mentions such as "Gold sponsor of KernelDays Bordeaux [YEAR]" are approved.

## 7. Organiser obligations

7.1 The Organiser delivers the Benefits with reasonable care and in good faith.

7.2 The Organiser shares no attendee list or personal data with the Sponsor. Any lead-sharing mechanism is opt-in by the attendee and subject to a separate notice.

7.3 The Organiser may make reasonable changes to the programme, schedule and floor plan. If the table location changes, the replacement will be of comparable visibility.

## 8. Intellectual property

Each Party keeps all rights in its names, logos and materials. Each grants the other a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free licence to reproduce them for the purposes of this Agreement, during the term and for the Event's promotion. The Sponsor's licence ends with the removal period in Clause 3.1, except for past printed or published materials.

## 9. Cancellation, change and force majeure

9.1 **Event cancelled by the Organiser.** If the Event is cancelled and not rescheduled within [12] months, the Organiser refunds the fee in full within 30 days.

9.2 **Event rescheduled.** If the Event moves to a new date, this Agreement applies to the new date. If the Sponsor cannot attend on the new date, it may terminate and receive a full refund, by notifying the Organiser within 15 days of the new date being announced.

9.3 **Partial failure.** If a Benefit cannot be delivered for reasons within the Organiser's control, the Organiser offers an equivalent substitute. If none is acceptable to the Sponsor, the Sponsor receives a pro-rata refund for that Benefit, based on the agreed value in Clause 9.5.

9.4 **Force majeure** (events beyond a Party's reasonable control, such as a pandemic, strikes, severe weather, venue closure, or a public-authority order) suspends affected obligations without liability. Clause 9.1 applies if the Event cannot take place.

9.5 For Clause 9.3 only, the Parties value the Benefits as follows: website EUR [ ], lanyard EUR [ ], table EUR [ ], passes EUR [ ] (total EUR 7,500).

9.6 **Withdrawal by the Sponsor.** If the Sponsor cancels for a reason other than those above:
- more than 60 days before the Event: 50% of the fee is refunded
- within 60 days of the Event: no refund

The Organiser has committed costs for printing and logistics.

## 10. Liability and insurance

10.1 Each Party is liable for damage it causes to the other through its fault, under ordinary law.

10.2 The Organiser's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the amount of the fee paid, except for fraud, wilful misconduct, or bodily injury.

10.3 Neither Party is liable for indirect loss (such as lost leads, profits, or reputation).

10.4 The Sponsor is responsible for its own equipment and staff at the Event. The Organiser is not liable for loss or theft of the Sponsor's property, unless caused by the Organiser's gross negligence.

10.5 The Organiser will inform the Sponsor if the venue or the Event is covered by liability insurance, and the Sponsor will inform the Organiser of its own cover for its staff and equipment.

10.6 The Sponsor agrees that the individual volunteers of the Organiser, including its Treasurer, are not personally liable under this Agreement, except in cases of fraud, wilful misconduct, or misappropriation of the funds paid. Claims lie against the Organiser's assets, including the funds received. The Treasurer signs solely as representative of the Organiser.

## 11. Transfer to a legal entity

11.1 The Sponsor consents in advance to the transfer of this Agreement, with all rights and obligations, to a legal entity created to carry on the Event (for example, an association loi 1901 set up by the Organiser's volunteers). Transfer takes effect when the Organiser notifies the Sponsor in writing with the new entity's name, registration number and bank details.

11.2 From then on, the new entity alone is bound as Organiser, and Clause 10.6 ceases to be needed. The Treasurer is released from the Agreement and any funds held on its behalf are transferred to the entity.

11.3 Other assignments need the other Party's written consent.

## 12. Compliance, data protection and confidentiality

12.1 Each Party complies with applicable law, including anti-corruption law. Neither will offer or accept improper advantages in connection with this Agreement.

12.2 Each Party processes the other's contact data (names, work emails of the people named in this Agreement and for pass registration) only for performing this Agreement, as an independent controller, in accordance with the GDPR.

12.3 The financial terms of this Agreement are confidential, except where disclosure is needed for advisers, accountants, authorities, or the Organiser's own account reporting. The Organiser may publish the Sponsor's name and tier.

## 13. Term and termination

13.1 The Agreement takes effect on signature and ends when the Benefits are delivered and the removal period in Clause 3.1 has expired.

13.2 Either Party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not remedied within 15 days of written notice (or 48 hours during the Event itself). Refunds follow Clause 9.3 and 9.6 as applicable.

13.3 The Organiser may terminate with immediate effect, with a refund of the fee less costs already incurred, if the Sponsor or its staff seriously breach the code of conduct or if the Sponsor's conduct or materials would harm the Event's reputation.

## 14. General

14.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, including the rate card tier as described in Clause 3, replaces all prior discussions. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

14.2 **Independence.** The Parties are independent. This is not a partnership, agency (except as stated in Clause 1), or employment.

14.3 **Severability.** If any provision is invalid, the others remain in force.

14.4 **Notices** are sent by email with a read receipt, or by registered post, to:
- Organiser: [NAME, EMAIL, ADDRESS]
- Sponsor: [NAME, EMAIL, ADDRESS]

14.5 **Counterparts and e-signature.** This Agreement may be signed electronically, in counterparts.

14.6 **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by French law. The Parties first try to settle disputes amicably. Failing that, the competent courts of **Bordeaux** have exclusive jurisdiction.
