# PyFrame Meetup: Silver sponsor, attendee-list request

**⚠️ This is a term sheet excerpt for review by a qualified lawyer. It is not a contract and not legal advice. Do not sign it.**

## Decision: I did not draft the attendee-list clause

The skill says a data clause is draftable only where a consented lead-retrieval benefit was genuinely sold. PyFrame is community-run and publishes a no-attendee-contact-details rule, and Silver doesn't mention data. So a delivery clause doesn't exist for this event. The sponsor agreeing to have it written in doesn't change that, for three reasons:

1. **🔴 Attendees' contact details aren't the sponsor's to waive.** Their consent to a written deliverable is irrelevant. The published rule binds PyFrame toward attendees.
2. **🔴 The exhibit may only list what was sold.** A perk absent from the rate card and prospectus does not enter the contract. Adding this one would be inventing a benefit.
3. **🔴 One breach costs every future edition.** The fulfillment skill names "bulk-list leakage" as a failure mode. A volunteer who exports the list for an insistent sponsor breaks the published rule for good. A contractual deliverable would make that export an obligation.

The skill's rule for an insistent sponsor is that they get the aggregate-demographics document or no deal.

## Term sheet excerpt

**Legend:** 🟢 Decided · 🟠 Open · 🔴 Counsel

### §14. Attendee data 🟢/🔴

**Position (drafted):** no attendee-data or lead-list deliverable exists under this agreement.

> **14.1 No attendee personal data.** The Organizer does not provide, sell, license, transfer or otherwise disclose to the Sponsor any attendee's name, email address, telephone number, employer-identifying contact details, registration record, badge scan or other personal data, in any form, before, during or after the Event. This applies to every sponsorship tier. It is consistent with the Organizer's published rules, which form part of the Event terms and are incorporated by reference.
>
> **14.2 Aggregate audience information.** Within 7 days after the Event, the Organizer will deliver to the Sponsor a document of aggregate audience information, as listed in Exhibit A. The document contains no names and no information that identifies or could reasonably be used to identify an individual attendee. Where a category would hold fewer than [N] attendees, the Organizer may merge or withhold it.
>
> **14.3 Sponsor conduct.** The Sponsor will not ask Event staff, volunteers or attendees' registration systems for attendee lists, and will not collect attendees' personal data at the Event except as an attendee freely gives it to the Sponsor directly in conversation. A request in breach of this clause may be refused without liability and is not a failure to deliver.
>
> **14.4 No remedy.** The absence of an attendee list or other attendee personal data is not a breach by the Organizer. It gives the Sponsor no right to a refund, credit or termination.
>
> **14.5 Precedence.** If the Sponsor's own terms, purchase order or order form contains a data-delivery term, that term is excluded. This section prevails over it.

**Why this rung:**
- It is the default for a community-run event.
- 14.1 states the published rule in the contract instead of leaving it to the prospectus.
- 14.4 follows the pattern the skill uses elsewhere: the consequence is named in the same clause, as with the code of conduct and editorial independence.
- 14.5 is there because, at the community pole, the sponsor's own paper often arrives instead.

### Exhibit A: deliverables row to add

| Perk | Specification | Surface | Hard deadline | Responsible | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aggregate audience demographics | Attendance count; role, seniority and company-size mix; no names, no list | Document | Event + 7 days | Organizer | 🟠 Confirm it is in the Silver rate card |

If it isn't in the rate card, it doesn't go in the exhibit. The same rule that blocks the list blocks anything not sold. Then either add it to the card for all Silver sponsors first, or offer it as a recap-email item that is not contractual. The skill's default recap email already carries aggregate attendance and demographics, so the sponsor loses nothing.

## What to tell the sponsor

> "Silver includes no attendee data, and PyFrame's published rules mean we can't add it to any tier. What we can commit to in writing is an aggregate audience report within 7 days after the Event: role, seniority and company-size mix, with no names. We've drafted that as a deliverable."

If the sponsor's goal is pipeline or recruiting and that was part of the pitch, the only other route is a consented raffle where attendees volunteer their details directly to the sponsor. That is a separate perk. It needs its own pricing decision and a compliance review, and it must not be folded into Silver at this stage.
