I did not draft the clause as asked. A clause that makes the attendee list a deliverable would put the contract in direct conflict with your published rule that attendee contact details are never shared with anyone. The sponsor's consent doesn't change that, because the attendees never gave it. I drafted two substitutes instead. Both give the sponsor something concrete and keep the rule intact.

## Clause A: aggregate report and no personal data (recommended)

> **Section X. Attendee Information**
>
> **X.1 No disclosure of personal data.** PyFrame Meetup (the "Organizer") does not disclose, sell, rent, or transfer to any sponsor, including Sponsor, the name, email address, phone number, employer, social media handle, or any other contact detail of any attendee, registrant, or speaker (together, "Attendee Contact Data"). This applies to every sponsorship tier, and no provision of this Agreement, including the Silver package description, shall be read to require such disclosure.
>
> **X.2 Aggregate attendance report.** Within fourteen (14) days after the Event, Organizer will deliver to Sponsor a written report containing only aggregated, non-identifying information, namely:
> (a) total registrations and total check-ins;
> (b) a breakdown by self-reported role or seniority band;
> (c) a breakdown by self-reported primary use case or industry, where collected;
> (d) the number of attendees who opted in under Section X.3.
>
> Organizer will omit or merge any category containing fewer than five (5) attendees, so that no individual can be identified.
>
> **X.3 Attendee-initiated contact.** Sponsor may, at the Event, (a) host a sign-up form, QR code, or raffle that attendees choose to complete on their own initiative, and (b) display its materials. Organizer will announce these opportunities. Any data attendees provide this way is collected by Sponsor directly and under Sponsor's own privacy notice. Organizer does not collect or forward it, and Sponsor is the sole controller of that data.
>
> **X.4 Sponsor undertakings.** Sponsor will not (a) attempt to obtain Attendee Contact Data from Organizer, its volunteers, or its platforms by any other means, or (b) scrape, harvest, or re-identify attendees from badges, name lists, or registration systems.
>
> **X.5 Acknowledgment.** Sponsor acknowledges that the restrictions above are a published policy of the Organizer and are a material term of this Agreement. Sponsor waives any claim that the absence of Attendee Contact Data is a failure of delivery under any package.

## Clause B: opt-in introductions (only if the sponsor needs contacts)

Use this in place of X.3 if the sponsor insists on receiving contacts. Each attendee decides for themselves.

> **X.3 Opt-in introductions.** At registration and at check-in, Organizer will offer each attendee a separate, unticked, clearly worded choice: "Share my name and work email with [Sponsor] so they can contact me about their products." Organizer will deliver to Sponsor, within fourteen (14) days after the Event, only the contact details of attendees who affirmatively selected that choice, together with the date and wording of their consent. Sponsor will use that data only to contact those attendees about its products, will honor any opt-out request promptly, and is the sole controller of the data from the moment of delivery. Organizer makes no commitment about the number of attendees who will opt in.

Clause B only works if you change the registration form first. The consent has to be collected before the Event, and it has to name this sponsor.

## Before you send it

- **Silver description:** because Silver mentions no data, the sponsor's expectation comes from the sales conversation. Tell them Silver includes the X.2 report, so they are not surprised later.
- **Published rules:** if you adopt Clause B, update them, because "never shared with anyone" would no longer be accurate. Clause A needs no change.
- **GDPR:** if you operate in the EU or UK, have counsel check Clause B's consent wording. I'm not a lawyer, and this is a draft.
- **Thresholds:** the 14-day delivery window and the 5-attendee minimum cell size are my placeholders. Adjust them to what you can actually do.
