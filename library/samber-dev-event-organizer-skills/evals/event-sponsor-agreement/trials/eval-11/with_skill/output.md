## Q1: Does the sponsor's booth staff have to follow our code of conduct?

**Yes, but only if the contract says so.** Your published code of conduct binds attendees. Nothing in it automatically binds a sponsor's employees. The draft needs a clause that does.

The skill treats this clause as a default, not an option. Published contracts from PyCon US, Write the Docs, MLH and others carry it. Four elements should sit in one clause:

1. **Sponsor responsibility.** The sponsor and all its representatives (booth staff, speakers, contractors, volunteers) must follow the SchemaFest code of conduct at all times. Name the document and its version or URL.
2. **Booth-specific conduct.** Optional, but real contracts add it, for example no aggressive recruiting, no invasive marketing, and no sexualised costumes at the booth.
3. **Ejection right.** The organizer may remove any sponsor representative, or the whole sponsor, at its discretion.
4. **No-refund consequence, stated in the same sentence.** Every published example does this. If you leave refunds to the cancellation section, the sponsor can argue an ejection is a cancellation.

Example shape, not signable language, adapted from PyCon US 2020:

> Sponsor and all of its representatives shall conduct themselves at all times in accordance with the SchemaFest Code of Conduct. SchemaFest may eject from the event any representative, or Sponsor itself, violating that code. Ejection is not a breach by SchemaFest and entitles Sponsor to no refund of any part of the sponsorship fee.

- 🟠 **Open:** is ejection of one staffer enough, or can you also de-brand the sponsor? Decide which sanctions you want.
- 🔴 **For counsel:** whether a no-refund ejection could be struck locally as a penalty. Also how the clause interacts with your cancellation clauses.
- **Related drafting point:** the code of conduct has to be published and reachable before signature. Link it in the agreement.

## Q2: What happens to logo use after the event?

**As drafted, I can't tell.** Your draft grants a right "to use the sponsor's logo in our promotion". If that's all it says, three gaps follow from the skill's reference examples:

- **The grant looks one-sided.** The skill's default is reciprocal: a limited, non-exclusive, promotion-only licence each way. The sponsor will probably want to say it was at SchemaFest. Decide whether you are offering that.
- **No end date.** A licence with no expiry reads as continuing indefinitely. Courts read these clauses literally, so whoever hoped for more or less than the text says loses.
- **No ceiling.** The Eclipse Foundation example states "No other use of Sponsor's logo is authorized." Your draft should state the same limit.

The recommended shape has four qualifiers:

1. **Limited, non-exclusive and non-transferable.**
2. **Promotion of the SchemaFest sponsorship only.** It does not cover other uses, and the draft should bar implying endorsement of the sponsor or of you.
3. **Subject to the sponsor's brand guidelines.** Prior approval of new uses is optional, with "not unreasonably withheld" language as in PyCon US.
4. **Automatic expiry** at the end of the agreement or a named date, unless extended in writing (the SponsorVista wording): "All use of the Sponsor's brand ends once the agreement expires, unless otherwise agreed in writing."

You also need to decide what happens to material that already exists. AFCEA FedID requires each party to "cease using the other party's Intellectual Property" on termination. CLOC makes the sponsor liable for the cost of removing its marks. For SchemaFest the open decisions are:

- 🟠 **Cut-off date.** Pick a fixed date after the event, such as 30 or 60 days, or the day of the last deliverable.
- 🟠 **Archive carve-out.** Do you want to keep the logo on past-event pages, recap videos and published photos? If yes, write that carve-out in explicitly. Otherwise automatic expiry technically forces you to remove it. Expect a sponsor's counsel to push back either way.
- 🟠 **Removal timeline.** For example, remove from the website and social channels within X days. Printed material already distributed is normally exempt.
- 🔴 **For counsel:** trademark-licence and quality-control wording, and whether archive use survives expiry in your jurisdiction.
