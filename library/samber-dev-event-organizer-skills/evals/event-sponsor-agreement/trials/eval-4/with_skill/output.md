3. 🟠 **Settle what the 4,000 USD buys.**

   - If 4,000 covers all three editions, it is about 11% per edition. That is below even the 15% low-contention floor.

4. 🟠 **Pick the three-edition structure.** The skill's default is a flat grant bounded to the paid term, plus a right of first refusal on renewal. A flat three-edition lock gives away category inventory for editions the sponsor hasn't bought.

| Option | Shape | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| A | Edition 1 only, plus right of first refusal for Editions 2 and 3 | Safest default |
| **B (drafted)** | Lock covers up to three editions, but each edition's lock exists only if NimbusTrace renews Gold and pays that edition's fee | Closest to "locked for three editions" without free inventory |

## 13. Category exclusivity 🟠

*Example language for counsel, not signable. No published contract in the skill's library carries an exclusivity clause (PyCon US 2020 has none). The wording is original drafting from the skill's three-element rule, not verified market practice.*

**13.1 Definitions.**
- "**Observability Product**" means a commercial software product or hosted service whose primary advertised purpose is to collect, store, query, alert on or visualise telemetry data (metrics, logs, traces, profiles) so customers can monitor and diagnose running software. This includes application performance monitoring and distributed tracing.
- The following are **not** Observability Products, even if they include monitoring features. 🟠 Each exclusion is an organizer business decision, and counsel will expect NimbusTrace to push on (a) and (c):
  - (a) general-purpose cloud, hosting or infrastructure platforms where monitoring is one feature among many;
  - (b) security-event and other security products;
  - (c) incident-management and on-call products;
  - (d) CI/CD, source hosting, orchestration, databases and message brokers;
  - (e) open-source projects and non-profit foundations that sell no commercial offering.
- "**Placement**" means any sponsorship benefit on an Organizer-Controlled Surface: logo or name, booth or exhibit space, sponsored session, workshop or demo slot, signage, badge or lanyard, party, swag or giveaway, and branded content.
- "**Competing Placement**" means a Placement that promotes an Observability Product to anyone other than Sponsor. 🟠 This is a product-level test. An entity-level test ("any company that sells one") is wider and will be requested.
- Schedule 13-A lists example competitors by name. It is illustrative and non-exhaustive, and is replaced with each edition's exhibit.

**13.2 Grant.** During each Locked Edition, Organizer will not sell, grant or deliver a Competing Placement.

**13.3 Channel scope.**
- "Organizer-Controlled Surfaces" are:
  - the venue areas Organizer controls;
  - the event website and app;
  - Organizer newsletters and attendee emails;
  - Organizer social accounts;
  - livestreams and recordings;
  - printed and digital collateral.
- Not covered: venue-operated concessions and signage, third-party content, and media or community partners listed in Schedule 13-B.
- 🟠 Organizer will bar Competitor co-branding in other sponsors' standard terms and will remove it on notice. Organizer gives no stronger promise about sponsor-controlled booths.

**13.4 Published caps are unchanged. 🟢**
- Sponsor is one of at most three (3) Gold sponsors per Edition, as published in the prospectus.
- Nothing in this Section makes Sponsor the sole or a limited-number Gold sponsor.
- Nothing changes the published Silver cap (6) or Bronze cap (20).
- Nothing restricts sales of any tier to companies outside the Category. This Section limits what may be promoted, not how many places exist.
- Caps are maximums, not guaranteed sales.

**13.5 Duration.**
- Locked Editions are Edition 1 [name, dates] and, only under §13.8, Edition 2 and Edition 3.
- For each, exclusivity starts when Organizer receives that Edition's Exclusivity Fee. It ends at the close of the Edition, or earlier under §13.10.
- Exclusivity exists for an Edition only if Sponsor is a Gold sponsor for that Edition and has paid its Exclusivity Fee.
- A cancelled Edition does not count toward the three. 🟠 Add a longstop date so the lock cannot run indefinitely.

**13.6 Exclusivity Fee.**
- [4,000 USD per Locked Edition] 🟠, invoiced and payable with the Sponsorship Fee under Section 4. Late-payment interest and the non-payment remedies apply. Exclusivity is suspended while payment is overdue.
- For Editions 2 and 3, the fee is the then-current rate card figure in that Edition's exhibit. 🟠 Decide whether to cap it. No Gold price lock is granted.
- **Refunds follow the Sponsorship Fee, with no separate regime:**
  - Sponsor withdrawal: the dated schedule in Section 5.
  - Organizer cancellation: the narrower remedy in Section 6.
  - Force majeure: the Section 7 remedy, applied as if the Exclusivity Fee were part of the Sponsorship Fee.
- 🟢 Each branch names its remedy in words (the *NetOne* / *Sanders* point).

**13.7 Existing arrangements.**
- Organizer represents that, at the Effective Date, no Competing Placement has been sold or agreed for any Locked Edition, except those in Schedule 13-B. Those continue and are not a breach.
- 🔴 This representation is a liability for Organizer. Complete the audit in step 2 first.

**13.8 Renewal into Editions 2 and 3 (right of first refusal on renewal).**
1. Within [30] days after each Locked Edition closes, Organizer sends a renewal exhibit offering Gold and exclusivity at the then-current rate card.
2. Sponsor has [30] days from receipt to approve it in writing. 🟠 Silence means lapse, not renewal.
3. During that window, Organizer holds one Gold place for Sponsor within the cap of 3 and sells no Competing Placement for that Edition.
4. If Sponsor approves and pays, exclusivity continues for that Edition. If it lapses, Organizer may sell the Category freely.
5. Sponsor has no obligation to renew.

**13.9 Breach and remedy.**
1. On written notice of a Competing Placement, Organizer removes or withdraws it, as far as it controls the surface, within [5] business days. During the event, the period is [24 hours]. 🟠
2. If it can't be removed (already printed, sent or on-site) or isn't removed in time, Organizer refunds [100%] of the Exclusivity Fee for that Edition within [30] days. 🟠
3. That refund is Sponsor's sole monetary remedy for the breach. The Gold sponsorship continues.
4. 🔴 Enforceability of a fixed refund as liquidated damages is local law. Sponsor's counsel will probably ask to also recover part of the Gold fee.

**13.10 Automatic lapse.** Exclusivity ends when:
- Sponsor is in uncured payment default;
- Sponsor withdraws or is terminated for cause;
- Sponsor stops marketing an Observability Product;
- Sponsor drops below Gold.

If a sponsor that was not a Competitor at signing later starts marketing an Observability Product, Organizer may deliver that sponsor's already-contracted Placements for that Edition. It will not renew or expand them in the Category.

**13.11 Preserved rights. Section 11 (editorial independence) prevails over this Section. 🟢**

None of the following is a Competing Placement or a breach:
- a talk by a Competitor's employee chosen through the open call;
- Competitor attendance or ticket purchase;
- Organizer-run observability sessions or tracks;
- venue sales;
- partners listed in Schedule 13-B.

Sponsor gets no say over speakers, topics or content.

**13.12 Disclosure.** Organizer may tell any prospect the Category is unavailable. It may not disclose price.

**Three-element check 🟢.** Category definition with exclusions is in §13.1. Duration and channels are in §§13.3 and 13.5. A named breach remedy is in §13.9. All three are present.
