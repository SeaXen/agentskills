# NimbusTrace category exclusivity clause (draft)

⚠️ This is a drafting aid, not legal advice. Have counsel review it before it goes out.

## Decisions to make first

1. **Fee basis.** Your "extra 4,000 USD" doesn't say whether it is one-time or per edition. The draft below assumes **4,000 USD per edition**, payable with each year's Gold fee. A one-time fee would mean you sell three years of exclusivity for about 1,333 USD a year.
2. **Existing sponsors.** Check your current and past sponsor list for observability vendors, including Silver and Bronze. If any are signed for a coming edition, grandfather them (Section 3(a)) or don't sell this.
3. **Prospectus.** The cap of 3 Gold, 6 Silver and 20 Bronze stays intact, because this clause removes no slots. It does reduce who is eligible to fill them. Update the prospectus to say that "category exclusivity is available to Gold sponsors on request". Otherwise later observability prospects are quoted a deal you can no longer sell them.
4. **Three-edition lock.** The draft ties exclusivity to NimbusTrace renewing at Gold each edition. Without that condition, they could lock out competitors at the lowest cost for three years.

## Clause

**[X]. Category Exclusivity**

**[X].1 Grant.** Subject to this Section [X], for each Edition in the Exclusivity Term, TraceCon will not accept as a Sponsor, at any tier, any Competitor of Sponsor in the Exclusive Category.

**[X].2 Definitions.**
- "**Edition**" means each annual TraceCon conference, including its associated side events that carry the TraceCon name.
- "**Exclusivity Term**" means the three (3) consecutive Editions beginning with [Edition name/year].
- "**Exclusive Category**" means commercial observability products and services, being [application performance monitoring, distributed tracing, log management, metrics monitoring, and observability platforms]. [Counsel: finalize this list. A narrow, enumerated definition is safer than "observability" alone.]
- "**Competitor of Sponsor**" means any entity whose principal commercial offering falls within the Exclusive Category. The entities listed in Schedule [Y] are Competitors for the Exclusivity Term. TraceCon may add an entity only by mutual written agreement.
- "**Sponsor**" means any entity that purchases a sponsorship package from TraceCon (Gold, Silver, Bronze or any other tier) for an Edition.

**[X].3 Exceptions.** This Section [X] does not restrict TraceCon from:
- (a) honoring sponsorship agreements signed before [Effective Date], as listed in Schedule [Z], for the Edition each covers;
- (b) accepting talks, workshops or speakers selected through the open call for proposals on editorial merit, provided the speaker receives no sponsored placement, branding or booth space;
- (c) permitting attendance by any person or company, including Competitors of Sponsor, at standard ticket prices;
- (d) acknowledging non-commercial open-source projects and foundations, whether or not they overlap with the Exclusive Category, where they receive no paid sponsor benefits;
- (e) using venues, media, infrastructure or service providers whose products are incidental to running the Edition;
- (f) accepting a sponsor that is not a Competitor of Sponsor at the time of signing but later acquires or merges into one. TraceCon will not renew that sponsor's package in a way that gives it visibility in the Exclusive Category.

**[X].4 Fee.** Sponsor will pay an Exclusivity Fee of USD 4,000 per Edition, in addition to the Gold sponsorship fee of USD 12,000, due on the same date as that fee.

**[X].5 Conditions.** Exclusivity for an Edition applies only if Sponsor:
- (a) has signed the Gold sponsorship agreement for that Edition by [date, e.g. the renewal deadline], and
- (b) has paid the Gold fee and the Exclusivity Fee in full when due.

If Sponsor does not renew at Gold or later downgrades, exclusivity ends immediately for that Edition and every later one. Fees already paid for a completed Edition are non-refundable.

**[X].6 Renewal and lapse.** TraceCon will send Sponsor a written renewal notice at least [90] days before each Edition's sponsor sales open. If Sponsor does not confirm within [30] days, TraceCon may offer the Exclusive Category to others for that Edition without breaching this clause.

**[X].7 Remedies.** If TraceCon breaches Section [X].1:
- (a) Sponsor will notify TraceCon in writing, and TraceCon will have [15] business days to cure by ending the offending sponsorship, with any refund to that sponsor at TraceCon's cost;
- (b) if the breach is not cured, Sponsor's sole remedy is a refund of the Exclusivity Fee paid for the affected Edition, plus [25]% of the Gold fee for that Edition as liquidated damages. Both parties agree this is a reasonable estimate of loss and not a penalty;
- (c) neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

**[X].8 No other rights.** Exclusivity covers sponsorship status only. It gives Sponsor no control over agenda, speaker selection, attendee lists or editorial content, and no right to be consulted on any Competitor's attendance.

**[X].9 Tier caps unaffected.** Nothing in this Section [X] changes the number of sponsorships TraceCon may sell at any tier. TraceCon's published tier caps remain in force, and TraceCon may fill any unsold slot with a sponsor outside the Exclusive Category.

**[X].10 Confidentiality.** Sponsor may state publicly that it is the "Exclusive Observability Sponsor of TraceCon" for the Edition. The fee terms of this Section [X] are confidential.

**[X].11 Termination.** Either party may terminate this Section [X] on written notice if the other commits a material breach of the Agreement that remains uncured 30 days after notice. On termination, TraceCon will refund the Exclusivity Fee for any Edition that has not yet taken place.

## Why the key choices are there

- **Per-edition fee and renewal condition:** this stops a three-year lock-out sold at a one-time price.
- **Enumerated category list:** this keeps "observability" from expanding to cover every vendor with a dashboard.
- **Carve-outs (b) to (d):** they protect your CFP independence and open-source goodwill. Without them, a competitor's maintainer could be barred from speaking.
- **Liquidated damages plus a sole-remedy cap:** this prices your risk up front, so a breach can't turn into an open-ended claim.
- **Section [X].9:** it ties back to the prospectus caps, so nobody can argue you changed the published tier structure.
