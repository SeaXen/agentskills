### 9.3 Cocktail sponsor (Thursday evening) 🟢 / 🔴

Add to the baseline:
- **Liquor liability.** The skill names this cover but gives no limit. 🟠 Leave the limit blank for counsel and the venue.
- **Workers' compensation and employer's liability, $500K.**

Questions for counsel and the venue before this sponsor is signed: 🔴
- Who may legally serve alcohol in the hall? Venues often require their own licensed caterer or bar, or a licensed bartender.
- Does the venue's licence or contract allow outside alcohol at all?
- Does the sponsor's CGL exclude liquor? Many policies do, which is why liquor cover is a separate line.
- Does the organizer need its own host-liquor cover?
- Are there rules on serving hours, age checks and a drink cap? Does the code of conduct need a sentence on this?

### 9.4 Timing and gate 🟢

- **Due at contract execution.** Tie the certificate deadline to signature, not event-day arrival. Precedent: the Bank of America Shamrock Shuffle requires certificates "upon execution of contract." The skill's reason: a missing or wrongly worded additional-insured endorsement is discovered only after an incident, when it can't be fixed.
- **Check the venue's deadline.** If it is earlier than signature, the venue's date governs. I haven't seen it. 🟠
- **Gate.** Booth and activation are held until the certificate and endorsement arrive. Someone must check the endorsement wording against the venue contract.
- **Ask for a draft endorsement before signing**, from Voltway and the cocktail sponsor in particular. Auto and liquor cover usually need broker involvement, so they take longer than a CGL certificate.
- **Single-event policies exist.** A sponsor who says year-round cover is disproportionate can buy a compliant one. ESP Specialty publishes $79 for a $1M/$2M single-event booth CGL policy. Treat that as an order of magnitude, and note it covers CGL only, not auto or liquor.
- **Deleted option:** an insurance clause with no deadline and no gate. A requirement nobody collects against creates a false belief that the exposure was transferred.

### 9.5 Consequences 🟠

- **Non-compliance by the deadline.** Draft the remedy in words: the booth and activation are withheld, no refund is given, and the payment and withdrawal clauses apply. Counsel decides the exact wording.
- **Exhibit rows.** Add one row per sponsor: "Insurance certificate and endorsement: due [signature date], responsible: Sponsor." Add the van and cocktail-activation rows from 9.2 and 9.3.
- **Indemnification (§8).** Insurance does not replace it. Present counsel with both real shapes: one-sided in the organizer's favour (PyCon US 2020) and mutual (Template Republic). 🔴
