# Sponsor agreement: insurance section for DriveStack Summit

**Assumptions:**
- US/Canada-style policies. If you're in the UK or EU, "CGL" becomes public liability, and the other terms need local equivalents.
- I haven't seen the venue contract. Where it says more than "1M per occurrence", it overrides my numbers.
- Have your broker or counsel review the wording before it goes out.

## What to require

### A. All exhibitors (base clause)

| Coverage | Minimum | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial general liability (CGL) | $1M per occurrence, $2M general aggregate | The venue sets $1M per occurrence. The aggregate is my recommendation. An umbrella or excess policy can fill the gap. |
| Workers' compensation | Statutory limits | Covers booth staff. |
| Employer's liability | $1M | |
| Additional insured | The convention centre (exact legal name from the venue contract) and DriveStack Summit's organizing entity, plus any other party the venue contract names | Required on the CGL, and on the auto and liquor policies below where those apply. |
| Policy terms | Primary and non-contributory, with waiver of subrogation | The sponsor's insurer pays first. |
| Insurer | AM Best A- VII or better | |
| Evidence | Certificate of insurance (COI) and the additional-insured endorsement page | A COI alone doesn't prove additional-insured status. Require the endorsement (for example ISO CG 20 11 or equivalent). |

### B. Voltway (van in the hall)

The base coverage is not enough, because a standard CGL excludes auto liability. A van indoors may fall outside it, so add the following.

1. **Commercial auto liability:** $1M combined single limit, with the convention centre and DriveStack as additional insureds. Require written confirmation from the insurer that coverage applies while the van is parked and displayed.
2. **Pollution or environmental liability:** $1M. This covers battery, coolant and fluid leaks, and fire or thermal-runaway cleanup. If the insurer won't write it, require the CGL to carry no total-pollution exclusion for this use.
3. **Property damage to the venue:** state that Voltway is liable for floor and structural damage, including floor loading and tire marks, regardless of fault.
4. **Voltway's own van:** they insure it, and they waive subrogation against us and the venue.

**Non-insurance conditions** (put these in the agreement or an activation schedule):
- Written venue and fire marshal approval of the van display before contract signature or countersignature.
- No charging in the hall, with the battery state-of-charge cap and disconnection rules set by the venue or fire marshal.
- No fuel or open fluids.
- Floor protection under the tires.
- A staffed vehicle at all times, with keys controlled.

### C. Cocktail sponsor (Thursday evening)

1. **Liquor liability:** $1M per occurrence minimum. Name the convention centre and DriveStack as additional insureds.
   - If the drinks are free, the CGL's host liquor coverage may suffice, but require written confirmation of that.
   - If the drinks are sold, or service is outsourced, full liquor liability is mandatory.
2. **Licensing:** a temporary alcohol permit if the jurisdiction requires one. Check the venue contract first, because many venues require their exclusive caterer or bar operator to serve alcohol. If so, the sponsor's activation runs through that caterer, and the caterer carries the liquor policy.
3. **Operating conditions:**
   - Trained, certified servers.
   - ID checks and no service to minors or intoxicated guests.
   - Defined service hours and a cut-off time.
   - A drink-count cap or ticket system, if you want one.
4. **Fallback:** if the sponsor can't buy liquor liability, use a licensed third-party bartending firm that carries it and names the venue.

## When we need the paperwork

Set our deadlines earlier than the venue's. We must hand the venue the documents before its deadline, and sponsor insurers often take 1 to 3 weeks to issue endorsements.

| Milestone | Standard sponsors | Voltway and the cocktail sponsor |
|---|---|---|
| **At signing** | Sponsor confirms it holds the required coverage. | Activation plan, plus a letter from the insurer or broker confirming the activity is covered. Venue and fire marshal approval comes first for the van. Without it, the activation isn't confirmed. |
| **Certificate and endorsements delivered** | **45 days before load-in** (or 14 days before the venue's own deadline, whichever is earlier). | **60 days before load-in.** Both need revisions and back-and-forth. |
| **Policy renewal** | If a policy expires before or during the event, renewal evidence is due 10 business days before expiry. | Same. |
| **Load-in** | No compliant COI and endorsement means no load-in. | The van is not admitted and the bar does not open. |

**Our action:** read the venue contract's deadline, and use whichever date is earlier. Then pick the final calendar dates from the event date.

## Draft clause

> **Insurance.** Sponsor shall maintain, at its own expense, during the Event (including load-in and load-out): (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least USD 1,000,000 per occurrence and USD 2,000,000 general aggregate; (b) workers' compensation at statutory limits and employer's liability of USD 1,000,000; and (c) any additional coverage required by Schedule [X] for Sponsor's activation (including, as applicable, commercial auto liability of USD 1,000,000 combined single limit, pollution liability of USD 1,000,000, and liquor liability of USD 1,000,000 per occurrence). Each liability policy shall name [Convention Centre legal name] and [Organizer legal name], and their respective officers, employees and agents, as additional insureds; shall be primary and non-contributory; and shall include a waiver of subrogation in their favor. Insurers must be rated A- VII or better by AM Best. Sponsor shall deliver certificates of insurance and additional-insured endorsements no later than [45/60] days before the Event, and renewal evidence before any policy expires. Failure to deliver compliant evidence entitles Organizer to refuse access to the exhibit hall, without refund of sponsorship fees. Sponsor is liable for all damage to the venue caused by it, its personnel, vehicles or contractors, whether or not insured, and shall indemnify Organizer and [Convention Centre] against third-party claims arising from its activation.

## Open questions (they change the numbers)

1. What are the venue contract's exact additional-insured entity names and its COI deadline?
2. Does the venue require its exclusive caterer for alcohol?
3. What does the venue or fire marshal allow for an EV (state-of-charge cap, charging, floor loading)?
4. Which jurisdiction are you in? That sets the liquor permit and dram-shop exposure.
