# Payment Terms

**Sponsor:** Praxis Data ("Sponsor")
**Package:** Platinum Sponsorship ("Package")
**Fee:** USD 25,000 (twenty-five thousand US dollars), exclusive of any applicable taxes

## 1. Fee

1.1 The Sponsor shall pay the Organizer a fixed fee of USD 25,000 for the Package.

1.2 No payment is due on signature. The Organizer invoices the Fee after the Event, as set out in Section 2.

## 2. Invoicing

2.1 The Organizer shall issue one invoice for the full Fee. It will be issued after the Event has taken place, within seven (7) calendar days after its last day (the "Invoice Date"). For example, if the Event ends on 14 May, the invoice is issued no later than 21 May.

2.2 The Organizer shall send the invoice by email to the Sponsor's accounts-payable contact. The Sponsor names that contact in Section 2.4.

2.3 The invoice shall state:
- the Sponsor's legal name and address
- the Sponsor's purchase order number, if the Sponsor has given one before the Event
- the Fee
- any applicable taxes, shown separately
- the Invoice Date and the due date
- the Organizer's bank details

2.4 The Sponsor shall give the Organizer, before the Event, the accounts-payable email address and any purchase order number it needs on the invoice. The Organizer is not responsible for delays caused by missing or wrong details the Sponsor supplied.

2.5 If the Event is cancelled or postponed, the Organizer will not issue an invoice for the Fee before the Event takes place, except as the parties agree in writing.

## 3. Payment due date

3.1 The Sponsor shall pay the invoice within forty-five (45) calendar days of the Invoice Date ("Due Date").

3.2 Payment is made by bank transfer in US dollars to the account on the invoice. The Sponsor shall cite the invoice number as the payment reference.

3.3 The Sponsor bears its own bank charges. The Organizer must receive the full invoiced amount, with no deduction for transfer or currency conversion fees.

3.4 Payment counts as made on the day the funds reach the Organizer's account.

## 4. Taxes

4.1 The Fee excludes VAT, sales tax and similar taxes. The Organizer adds them to the invoice where the law requires.

4.2 Where the Sponsor must withhold tax by law, the Sponsor shall tell the Organizer before the Event and give the Organizer the official withholding receipt within 30 days of payment.

## 5. Invoice disputes

5.1 The Sponsor shall tell the Organizer in writing of any dispute about the invoice within ten (10) days of receiving it, giving its reasons.

5.2 The Sponsor shall pay any undisputed part by the Due Date. The parties will work in good faith to settle the disputed part.

## 6. Late payment

6.1 If the Organizer has not received payment by the Due Date, it will send a written reminder to the Sponsor's accounts-payable contact. It may send further reminders.

6.2 **[Option A, recommended if the Organizer is established in France: use as written]**
Late payment carries a penalty at three times the legal interest rate in force, accruing daily from the day after the Due Date until full payment. A fixed recovery fee of EUR 40 is also due. The Organizer may waive these amounts in whole or in part, and a waiver on one occasion does not waive them on any other.

6.2 **[Option B, only if French law does not apply: use as written]**
The Organizer does not charge interest on late payment. Failure to pay by the Due Date remains a breach of this agreement. The Organizer may claim its reasonable costs of recovery, and sums unpaid stay due in full until paid.

6.3 The Organizer's reminders and any delay in chasing payment do not waive its right to payment or to any remedy.

## Notes for you (remove before sending)

1. **Dates.** I assumed the Event is in May 2027 and wrote no calendar dates. With a last Event day of 14 May, the invoice date falls by 21 May and the due date by 4 July. Fill in real dates once the schedule is final.

2. **Late payment and French law.** This is the one point I would not leave as it stands.
   - If the Organizer is French (Naomakers, Nantes), Article L441-10 of the Code de commerce requires B2B terms to state the late-payment penalty rate. The rate must be at least three times the legal interest rate.
   - Terms must also state the EUR 40 recovery fee. Omitting them is an administrative offence with fines, and the EUR 40 is owed by law anyway.
   - Net 45 from the invoice date is inside the 60-day legal cap, so the term is fine.
   - Not having charged interest in the past does not change this. Option A lets the treasurer keep chasing by email, since the clause permits a waiver.
   - If a non-French entity signs, use Option B. Ask your lawyer to confirm which law governs. I am not a lawyer, and this is a drafting judgement.

3. **Cash-flow exposure.** All 25,000 USD arrives roughly 50 to 60 days after the Event, so you finance the Package's delivery costs up front. If that is a problem, ask for a deposit of 20 to 30 percent on signature as a counter-offer. The AP rule only blocks payment before delivery, and a deposit invoiced at signature breaks it. That is likely why they refused the deposit.

4. **USD and exchange rate.** If your bank account is in euros, the Sponsor pays in USD and you absorb the conversion. Section 3.3 covers the Sponsor's bank fees but not the exchange rate. Decide whether you accept that risk.

5. **Cancellation.** This section covers payment only. Add a clause on what the Sponsor owes if it withdraws between signature and Event, since no money is held in the meantime.
