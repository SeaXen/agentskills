## Order to work through it

### 1. Today: matrix and payment column (self-set: ~1 hour)
- Build the tier-by-perk matrix from the signed agreements, not the prospectus. Each perk moves through promised → scheduled → delivered → evidenced.
- Add invoiced-date and paid-date columns for every sponsor.
- Check the lanyard against the exclusive-surface cap: it must be one sponsor only.

### 2. Today: Cindercloud, the real emergency
The conflict is that lanyard art is due in 6 days, but the invoice is open and nothing ships before payment in full. A printed logo can't be undone. An unpaid sponsor with a printed logo has no reason left to pay.

1. Message their liaison today. Say the lanyard logo and placements are queued behind the invoice ($2,500).
2. Give a payment date before the art deadline. I suggest **Mon 10-19**, which leaves 2 days of buffer (self-set).
3. Ask for the vector logo and print-spec assets now. Collecting assets isn't delivering, so this doesn't break the gate.
4. Ask the printer what a late-art or revised-art option costs. This is my judgment, not from the skill.
5. Decide the fallback before 10-19, not on 10-21:
   - **Recommended:** if unpaid by the cutoff, send art without their logo and tell them in advance. A make-good is cheaper than a lanyard printed for a non-payer.
   - **Your call, not the skill's:** accept a written payment commitment such as a PO with a date. That means printing on trust.

### 3. Today or tomorrow: Tesserly, welcome and checklist
Tell them: "Yes, social posts are on the plan. We schedule printed surfaces first, then digital."

- Send the checklist email from their matrix column, listing every asset owed, its format, and its hard deadline.
- Collect their vector logo and blurb now.
- If their agreement includes a swag-bag insert, the 11-05 print date applies to it. Collect that insert this week or next.
- If their agreement includes a program logo, the 11-19 lock applies to it.
- Social posts are a digital perk with no printer date. "Plenty of time" is the trap: the 10 weeks to the event isn't their clock. Schedule the posts for a fixed later week, once the printed assets are in.
- Their paid status clears them to ship. Check the contract before you promise any specific perk.

### 4. By ~10-29: swag bag
Chase every swag-bag sponsor's assets and inserts, and validate formats on receipt, not at the printer. Only paid sponsors get placed.

### 5. By ~11-12 for 11-19 lock: program
Collect logos and blurbs from all sponsors, and gate the program logos on payment.

### 6. Tuesdays 10-20 onward: newsletter
- Don't put Cindercloud in an issue until they've paid.
- Spread paid sponsors' mentions across the ~9 weekly sends.
- Capture evidence each time.

### 7. Then: Tesserly social posts and other digital perks
- Schedule the social posts, then capture a screenshot or link for each cell at delivery time.
- Do ticket codes, event-page logos, and slide logos here too.

### 8. Week of the event
Send the day-of brief about a week out, covering setup and teardown, shipping, power, WiFi, A/V, booth, and slots. After the event, send the recap inside 72 hours.
