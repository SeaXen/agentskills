# Steelharbor Dev Days: sponsor onboarding plan

**Short answer: no.** Nine 45-minute kickoff calls is the wrong instrument for seven of the nine sponsors. Use calls for two sponsors, and send a form plus an email to the rest.

## Why not nine calls

- **Call time:** 9 × 45 min is 6.75 h. Add prep and follow-up notes, and it lands around 10–12 h. That figure is my estimate, not a measured one. You have about 8 h/week combined, and the calls would use all of it.
- **What gets squeezed:** the calls would crowd out the work with hard deadlines (printer dates, the perk matrix, payment chasing).
- **Where calls pay off:** the skill ranks the kickoff call as the highest-value but highest-effort instrument. It promotes a call only for a top-tier sponsor, a first-time sponsor, a compounding relationship, or a perk that needs live coordination.
- **Silvers:** seven experienced Silvers meet none of those conditions. A form and an email give them the same assets without using your hours.

## Instrument per sponsor

| Sponsor | Onboarding | Why | Post-event report |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marrowgate (Platinum, first time) | **Kickoff call, 45 min** | Top tier and first-time. They don't know the drill, and Platinum perks (speech, premium placement, pitch) need live coordination. | Full metrics report |
| Pellucid Labs (4 editions) | **Short call, about 30 min (self-set)** | A compounding relationship promotes the call. Make it a planning conversation: what they want to change this year and what success looks like. Skip the basics. | Full metrics report |
| 7 Silvers | **Intake form + one checklist email** | Veterans with standard perks. Nine sponsors is past the roughly-five mark where the skill says a form is worth building once. | Recap email |

The intake form validates logo formats and blurb length on submission, so you aren't chasing malformed files one by one. Generate each welcome email from that sponsor's matrix column. Tie it to specific program moments (which slot, which time block, which reception) instead of a flat benefits list.

## Corrections to the plan

1. **Pellucid saying "we're in for next year" is a verbal signal, not a contract.** Use it to decide how to run the call. Re-signing belongs to the outreach motion, not this one. Don't skip their report on the assumption they will renew.
2. **Check payment before any onboarding perk ships.** No logo, ticket, or booth goes out until fees are paid in full. Tell sponsors that early, through your liaison. Nine sponsors means nine rows in the tracker with invoiced and paid dates.
3. **Start from the earliest printer deadline, not the event date.** Lanyards, swag, and signage set the real clock. Collect those assets first from every sponsor, Silvers included.
4. **Build the matrix from the signed agreements, not your prospectus.** Marrowgate's first-time Platinum deal may carry negotiated differences that exist only in the contract. Honor any published per-tier caps on exclusive surfaces. With nine sponsors versus four last year, check that you haven't sold the same exclusive surface twice.
5. **Name one liaison per sponsor.** One of you takes roughly four or five sponsors.
6. **Reports go out within 72 hours of the event.** Marrowgate and Pellucid get the full report, scored against the success metric each named. The seven Silvers get one shared recap email (photos, aggregate attendance, a delivered-perk summary for each). Each report ends with a renew, renegotiate, or drop read for outreach.

## What would change this plan

- **Two Silvers turn out to have a perk needing live coordination** (a pitch slot, a workshop, a sponsored session). Those two get a short call. Review the actual content of any sponsored session before you confirm it.
- **A Silver is new to your event or sells pipeline or recruiting.** Promote that one to a call or a full report.
- **You only have a few hours before the first printer date.** Send the checklist email to everyone now and run the calls second.

## Open questions (they change the ranking)

1. **Community-run or company-run?** It decides which lead-capture options exist. At a community-run event, no attendee lists and no badge scanning. Offer a consented raffle or aggregate demographics instead.
2. **What are your earliest hard deadlines** (printer dates, program lock)?
3. **What payment status does each sponsor have?**
4. **What success metric did each sponsor name,** especially Marrowgate and Pellucid?
5. **When does next edition's outreach start?** If it's soon, the recap email moves up in priority for everyone except those two.

Answer those and I'll build the matrix skeleton, the welcome email, and the intake form fields.
