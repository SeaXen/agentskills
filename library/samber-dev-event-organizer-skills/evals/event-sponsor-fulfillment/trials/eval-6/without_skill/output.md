**Not quite. Book calls for Marrowgate and Pellucid, and use one group call plus a written brief for the seven Silvers.**

**Why not nine calls**
- Nine 45-minute calls is 6.75 hours, before prep, notes and follow-up. Realistically that's 10 hours or more.
- You have about 8 hours a week combined, so this would eat over a week of your joint capacity before you've done any actual logistics.
- Calls don't catch what slips through the cracks. A tracked deliverable list with owners and dates does. A call can produce one, but a call without it won't.

**Suggested split**

| Sponsor | Format | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Marrowgate (Platinum, first time) | Full 45-minute kickoff, plus a written recap | 45 min |
| Pellucid Labs (4 editions, in for next year) | 15-minute check-in, or async | 15 min |
| 7 Silvers (repeat, know the drill) | One group kickoff plus the written brief | 30–45 min |

That comes to about 1.5–2 hours of calls instead of 6.75.

**Marrowgate**
- They have the highest stakes and no history with you, so this is where a real conversation pays off.
- Ask what success looks like for them and who their day-to-day contact is.
- Walk through every Platinum deliverable and deadline. Confirm decision-makers and who signs off on assets.
- Send a written recap within 24 hours so it can be checked later.

**Pellucid Labs**
- They need no onboarding. Use the call to thank them and ask what they'd change.
- Your best move is to get next year's commitment in writing (contract or email) while it's fresh. A verbal "we're in" is easy to lose when their contact or budget owner changes.
- Ask whether they'd give a short testimonial, or a quick tip to Marrowgate. A peer's word carries weight with a first-time sponsor.

**The seven Silvers**
- Send a one-page brief to all of them:
  - Deliverables and deadlines.
  - Asset specs and the submission link.
  - Logistics: booth or table, passes, load-in.
  - One named contact, with a reply-by date.
- Hold one group call, or two optional office-hour slots. Questions asked in front of the group get answered once for everyone.
- Handle changes by exception. Follow up one-to-one only if a Silver changes contact, scope or deliverables, or misses the first deadline.

**Track it simply**
- Keep one shared sheet with a row per sponsor and a column per deliverable, with owner and due date.
- Check it weekly. A missed date is your trigger to reach out personally.

**Split between the two of you**
- One of you owns Marrowgate and the other owns Pellucid and the Silver group. That's two people on the relationship that carries the most risk, and the other seven stay cheap.

**One thing to confirm**
- Check whether any Silver has a new contact or changed terms this year. If so, that one deserves a short call too. "Done it before" is only reliable if the people are the same.
