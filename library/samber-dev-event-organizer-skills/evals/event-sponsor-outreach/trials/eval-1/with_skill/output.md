## 7. Discovery call (20 minutes)

1. Say what KestrelConf is, in two sentences, and why the team can deliver.
2. Ask what they need from events this year: hiring, product feedback, brand, selling to the audience, or community goodwill.
3. Ask what they spent on past sponsorships and which metric they used.

5. Book the next call before hanging up.

Only pitch activities your program really contains. If a need can't be met, say no politely. Don't stretch a package to fit.

## 8. Objections

- **"Budget is committed."** Ask which month next year's budget is set, and get on that radar.
- **"Can we get a speaking slot?"** No. Point to the CFP.
- **"Can we scan badges or get the attendee list?"** No. Offer aggregate audience data and conversations attendees choose.
- **"Too expensive" or "what do we get?"** Walk through their stated metric, not the perk list.
- **"We don't see the value."** This is a discovery signal. Ask what they would need to walk away with.
- Prepare the fixed answers before outreach: attendance, audience mix in aggregate, per-level benefits, and logistics.
- Log every objection with the sponsor's segment. A recurring one points to a weak value statement, not a sales problem.
