## Cadence after that

- Send at most 3 follow-ups in total, 1-2 weeks apart.
- Tighten to 3-4 days only close to the event.
- For the last reminder, use a real production date, such as a merch print cutoff or program lock.
- Remove her immediately if she opts out, even mid-sequence.
