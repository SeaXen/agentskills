Skill formula: goal ÷ average package = sponsors needed. Sponsors needed ÷ close rate = prospects to pitch. Prospects to pitch ÷ first-touch-to-call rate = list size.

1. €60,000 ÷ €5,000 = **12 sponsors**.
2. 12 ÷ 25% close = **48 prospects pitched** (a call held and a proposal sent).
3. 48 ÷ 33% first-touch-to-call = **about 145 companies on the list**.
