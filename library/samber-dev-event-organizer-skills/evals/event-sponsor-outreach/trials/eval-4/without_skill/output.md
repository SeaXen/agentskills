# Fernweh Devfest sponsor list sizing

**Short answer:** build a list of about **170 companies**, tiered by warmth. Plan for **14 signed deals** to land 12. This only works if you have about 25 weeks or more to sell. If you have less, shrink the cold list and raise package prices (see Time check).

All conversion rates below are planning assumptions typical for event sponsorship. They are not measured benchmarks. Replace them with your own numbers from the last two editions as soon as you can.

## 1. Target deals

1. EUR 60,000 ÷ EUR 5,000 = **12 deals**.
2. Add about 15% for discounts, in-kind deals, slips and late drops. That gives **14 deals** (about EUR 70k nominal).

## 2. List size by segment

Don't use one blended rate. Past sponsors and cold companies convert very differently.

| Segment | Plan rate (list → signed) | Deals | Companies needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Past sponsors from the last two editions | 40% (renewals typically run 40–60%; plan low) | 5 | **12** |
| Warm: speakers' employers, attendees' employers, partners, community intros | 15% | 3 | **20** |
| Cold, fit-scored (hiring devs in your region, sponsor similar events) | 4% | 6 | **~140** |
| **Total** | | **14** | **~170** |

- **Fewer than 12 past sponsors:** move the shortfall to warm and cold. Each missing renewal costs about 25 extra cold companies.
- **Cold is the expensive part.** It is 80% of the list but fewer than half the deals.

## 3. Funnel rates to plan on

Use these for the whole pipeline, and track each stage separately:

| Stage | Cold | Warm / past |
|---|---|---|
| Contacted → reply | 10–12% | 50–60% |
| Reply → call | 50% | 80% |
| Call → proposal | 70% | 85% |
| Proposal → signed | 35% | 55% |

- **Cold check:** 140 → 15 replies → 8 calls → 5–6 proposals → about 2 signed at these stage rates. The 4% figure above is the optimistic end.
- **Cold, honest version:** at the stage rates above, plan cold at **2–4%**. That is why the cold list is large and why the warm segments matter most.
- **Past and warm check:** 32 companies → about 17 replies → 14 calls → 12 proposals → 6–7 signed at these stage rates. That is more than the 8 deals (5 renewals + 3 warm) the table assumes, so treat the table as the conservative case.

## 4. Time check (4 h/week)

Rough effort for the 170-company plan:

| Work | Hours |
|---|---|
| Cold research + first email (140 × ~12 min) | 28 |
| Two follow-ups | 14 |
| Warm and past outreach (32 × ~25 min) | 13 |
| Calls with prep (about 30) | 22 |
| Proposals (about 18) | 18 |
| Contracts, invoices, onboarding (14 × ~1.5 h) | 21 |
| **Total** | **~115 h, about 29 weeks** |

Templates and reuse could bring this down to about 90–100 h, or 23–25 weeks. If you have fewer weeks, pull these levers in this order:

1. **Raise the average package.** Three tiers at EUR 10k plus six at EUR 5k gives 9 deals and EUR 60k. That cuts the list to about 110 and the hours to about 65.
2. **Cut cold, never past or warm.** Past and warm yield about 4x the deals per hour.
3. **Get a third person for fulfilment** (contracts, invoices, logistics). It is 20% of your hours and doesn't need to be you.

## 5. Halfway checkpoint

Halfway means the midpoint of your selling window, from first email to the date you want the sponsor book closed. Closings lag, so judge on pipeline, not just cash:

| Metric at midpoint | On track | Warning | Act now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Companies contacted | 100% of the list | 80–99% | below 80% |
| Signed revenue | **≥ EUR 20k (4 deals)** | EUR 12–20k | below EUR 12k |
| Proposals out, plus signed | ≥ 12 | 8–11 | below 8 |
| Open proposals' value | ≥ 2x the remaining gap | 1–2x | below 1x |
| Cold reply rate | ≥ 8% | 5–8% | below 5% |
| Past-sponsor renewal rate (decided so far) | ≥ 40% | 25–40% | below 25% |

**Early alarm at 25% of the window:** after the first 40 cold emails, a reply rate below 5% means the message or targeting is wrong. Fix it before you send the other 100.

**If you are behind at midpoint, in this order:**
1. Compare signed revenue plus open proposals to EUR 60k. If the total is under 1.5x the goal, you will miss.
2. Open a new warm channel, such as speaker and attendee employers, then add a higher-priced anchor tier.
3. Change the offer (a cheaper entry tier) only after those two, because it lowers revenue per deal.
