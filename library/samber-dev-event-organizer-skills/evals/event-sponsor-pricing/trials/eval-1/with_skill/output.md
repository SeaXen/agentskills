# PipelineDays Rotterdam 2027: sponsor rate card

I followed the skill's workflow but skipped its interview and filled the gaps with the assumptions below. If any assumption is wrong, say so and I'll re-run the affected numbers.

## Assumptions

- **Pole:** community-run and CFP-driven, with sponsorship as cost recovery.
- **Disclosure:** a published ladder. Six volunteers cannot absorb quote-gated selling, so that rung is deleted.
- **Format:** a one-off annual conference, so there is no annual-series tier. Attendees pay, so there is no free-event shortcut.
- **Comps:** none in hand, so the tier prices lean on transposed ratios from the skill's reference ladders, not live comps. Collect 2-3 dated local comps before publishing.
- **Hiring market (Q9):** unknown. The card hedges with a staff-free tier.
- **Event date:** unknown. Deadlines are given as offsets from it.

| Line | Amount |

| Sponsorship share | 78% |
| **Sponsorship target** | **EUR 31,980** |
| Ticket target (remainder) | EUR 9,020 |

The card must raise the EUR 31,980 in total. It is not split evenly across tiers.

## 3. Ladder (published, 3 paid tiers, market-comp ratios)

Gating uses only the four physical axes: passes, floor presence, branding and organizer attention. The top-to-floor ratio is 5:1, inside the community range of 2:1 to 7.5:1.

| | **Gold** (anchor) | **Silver** (best value) | **Digital** (staff-free) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | **EUR 6,000** | **EUR 3,000** | **EUR 1,200** |
| Cap | 2 | 6 | 6 |
| Floor table | Yes, first pick of the 8 positions | Yes, standard position | **None** |
| Passes | 6 | 3 | 1 |
| Branding | Largest logo class on site, website, and printed program | Mid logo class on site, website, and printed program | Logo on website and the between-session screen loop |
| Organizer attention | Named organizer contact and a pre-event intro call | Named contact | Shared inbox |
| Stage time | **None** | **None** | **None** |

- **Tables:** Gold's 2 plus Silver's 6 fill all 8. Tables exist only at Gold and Silver (booth price floor), so there is no cheap route onto the floor.
- **Digital tier:** it is the first line that survives a hiring slowdown, because a booth costs the sponsor staff days on top of the fee.
- **Free Community category:** logo and one pass, no table, no pitch, capped at 3. It is for non-commercial local organizations only and is not a funnel. It carries no revenue and is not counted in the target.
- **Silver is the default the card points at.** Present Gold first as the anchor.

## 4. Add-ons (priced independently of the ladder)

None of these re-sells a table or a stage minute, so none cannibalizes a tier.

| Add-on | Price | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Lunch presented by | EUR 3,000 | 1 |
| Lanyards and badges | EUR 2,500 | 1 |
| Coffee and breaks | EUR 1,500 | 1 |
| Evening or networking event | Contact us (custom) | 1 |

- The evening event is a "contact us" line because it is genuinely custom.
- Add-ons are sold to any paid tier, and no add-on buys program influence.
- A sold-out add-on stays on the page, struck through. A sellout means next edition's price goes up, never down.
- A "captioning presented by" line is only possible if captioning is already budgeted. It is funded whether or not it sells, and the sponsor buys visibility only. Say so if you want it added.

## 5. Capacity check against the target

| Scenario | Revenue |
|---|---|
| 2 Gold (12,000) + 6 Silver (18,000) + 6 Digital (7,200) | 37,200 |
| Three priced add-ons sold | 7,000 |
| **Full sellout** | **44,200** (138% of target) |
| **Break-even fill** | **about 72%** of the card |

- Full sellout covers the target 1.4 times, so the card can under-fill and still land.
- **Early-bird:** Gold is EUR 5,500 until T-150 days, a dated price on the top tier only. If both Gold slots go early, the card loses EUR 1,000 (the sellout total drops to 43,200).
- **Pass cost:** a full sellout gives away 36 passes (12 Gold + 18 Silver + 6 Digital). Check that the EUR 9,020 ticket line was not computed on 320 paid seats. With 36 sponsor seats, the target needs about 284 paid tickets at roughly EUR 32 average. Reconcile this with `event-ticket-pricing` before publishing.

## 6. In-kind terms

- Value an in-kind deal at the budget line it displaces, never at the donor's retail value.
- The displaced line must be real: it exists in your budget and would otherwise be paid in cash this edition.
- The deal buys the tier its displaced-cost value would buy in cash.
- **Gold is cash-only (or cash-majority),** because venue, insurance and catering deposits are paid in cash.
- Write the displaced-cost value, the deliverable and the delivery date into the sponsor agreement. An undelivered venue or catering deal is a breach, not a discount.

## 7. Negotiation and discount policy (write this before the first call)

- **Negotiable, value-neutral only:**
  - Payment schedule, such as split invoices or a different fiscal year.
  - Equal-value benefit swaps, such as extra passes for a benefit the sponsor won't use.
  - Bundling add-ons into a tier at the sum of their card prices.
- **Never negotiable:**
  - Published tier prices.
  - **Talk slots, judging, CFP or program influence.**
  - Exclusivity not on the card.
  - The early-bird deadline.
- **No returning-sponsor discount.** Renewal is earned with the post-event report and an early-bird date, or a price lock or right of first refusal on the same tier.
- **Approval:** any deviation needs two of the six organizers. The person closing the deal never approves it alone.
- **Floor:** there is no price cut. When pushed, add value (a benefit) rather than cut the number.
- **Deviation log:** record the sponsor, tier, what moved, why and who approved. A rising frequency means the card is mispriced or the policy is too loose.

## 9. Repricing triggers (set these now)

- **Target coverage at T-90:** below 100% of EUR 31,980 means a pricing, ladder or pipeline problem. Split it with `event-sponsor-outreach` before touching the card.
- **Tier fill pattern:** Gold selling out fast means raise it next edition. Digital filling while Silver sits empty means the Silver step-up shows no visible value.
- **Add-on sellouts:** raise the price next edition.
- **Booth versus Digital sell-through:** booths lagging while Digital sells is a market signal, not a ladder bug.
