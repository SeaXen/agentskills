# PipelineDays Rotterdam 2027: Sponsor Rate Card

All prices are in EUR, excluding VAT. Dutch VAT applies, and EU businesses with a valid VAT ID can use reverse charge.

## Target

| Item | EUR |
|---|---|
| Budget | 41,000 |
| Sponsorship target (78%) | **31,980** |
| Tickets (22%) | 9,020 |

## Packages

The venue has 8 tables. Tables are sold only in Headline, Gold and Silver. Supporter has no table.

| Package | Qty | Price | Table | Included passes | Recognition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Headline Partner** | 1 | 8,000 | Best position (entrance or main hall) | 6 | Top logo on site, badge, stage screens between talks, and the website. Welcome note from one of your people (2 min, no pitch, no talk slot). |
| **Gold** | 3 | 5,000 | Prime position | 4 | Logo on the website, stage screens between talks, and the printed program. Your own page in the attendee guide. |
| **Silver** | 4 | 3,000 | Standard table | 2 | Logo on the website and the sponsor wall. Listing in the attendee guide. |
| **Community Supporter** | 6 | 1,000 | None | 1 | Name and logo on the sponsor wall and website. Your flyer or swag on the shared sponsor table. |

Headline, Gold and Silver use up all 8 tables.

## Add-ons (open to any sponsor)

Each add-on is exclusive. It adds logo placement only, never program content.

| Add-on | Qty | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Lanyard | 1 | 2,000 |
| Coffee and lunch stations | 1 | 2,500 |
| After-party drinks | 1 | 3,500 |
| Wi-Fi | 1 | 1,500 |
| Attendee scholarship fund (free tickets and travel for underrepresented attendees) | open | from 500 |

## Program integrity (applies to every package)

- ❌ **No talk slot is for sale.** This covers keynotes, sessions, workshops, lightning talks and sponsored "sponsor stages".
- ❌ No sponsor sees, reviews or influences CFP submissions or the schedule.
- ✅ Sponsor employees may submit to the CFP like anyone else. Their talks go through the same blind review, with no discount or priority. Accepted sponsor talks do not count toward the package.
- ✅ Lead scanning is opt-in only (attendee consent per scan, GDPR). No attendee list is shared.
- ✅ Sponsor swag and tables follow the code of conduct, so no booth staff or materials that breach it.

## Checking the numbers

| Scenario | Revenue |
|---|---|
| Ceiling: all tables, all supporters, no add-ons | 35,000 + 6,000 = 41,000 |
| Ceiling with all add-ons at list price | about 51,000 (add-ons are 10,000 plus the scholarship fund) |
| **Target hit with a minimal mix:** Headline + 3 Gold + 2 Silver + 3 Supporters | 8,000 + 15,000 + 6,000 + 3,000 = **32,000** ✅ |
| Weaker mix: Headline + 2 Gold + 3 Silver + 2 Supporters + 1 add-on | 8,000 + 10,000 + 9,000 + 2,000 + 2,000 = 31,000 (⚠️ about 1,000 short) |

You need about 63% of the ceiling revenue (31,980 of 51,000). The table tiers alone, if fully sold, give 35,000, so the target does not depend on selling add-ons.

## Things to check before publishing

- ⚠️ **Passes versus ticket revenue.** The card gives sponsors up to 6 + 12 + 8 + 6 = 32 passes at full sell-out. If these come out of the 320 capacity, paid tickets drop to about 288. The 9,020 ticket target then becomes about 31.3 per ticket, not 28.2. Either cap sponsor passes outside the 320, or keep the ticket price at about 32 or more.
- ⚠️ **Gold and Silver count.** If you expect fewer than 4 Silver buyers, move the unsold tables to a "second Gold-lite" tier rather than discounting. Discounts break the target faster than an empty table does.
- ℹ️ I assumed the 8-table layout has one clearly best position, one prime tier and standard tables. Adjust the position labels to your floor plan.
- ℹ️ I did not set an early-bird discount, because the 31,980 target has little slack. If you want one, raise the list prices by the same percentage so the net stays unchanged.
- ℹ️ Add-on prices are my estimates, not quotes. Check them against your real catering, Wi-Fi and lanyard costs before publishing.
