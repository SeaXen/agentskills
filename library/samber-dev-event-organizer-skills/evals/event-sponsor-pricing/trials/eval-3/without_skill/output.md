# Recommendation

**Cloud vendor: don't give Platinum. Counter at $9,000 cash.**
**Caterer: give a tier worth about $4,200, with lunch branding.** Their offer is real money.

## 1. Cloud vendor

**Value to you**
- $45,000 is their list price, not your savings. You'd never buy it, so it saves you $0.
- Credits that expire or lock you in can cost you time.
- The only real value is what you'd use. Count nothing for it unless you can name a use, such as site hosting, streaming, or a CFP tool.
- The cash on offer is $2,000, against $9,000 for Platinum.

**What to say**
1. Platinum is $9,000 cash. Credits don't count toward it.
2. Credits are welcome as a bonus. They don't buy a tier.
3. Their offer shows a real cash budget. Ask them to pay the $9,000.
4. If they can't, offer the tier that $2,000 cash buys. Check your price list for the right one.

**Conditions on any credits**
- No exclusivity.
- No requirement to use their product in talks or on stage.
- Written terms covering the expiry date, any lock-in, and who owns the account.
- Nothing from them touches the CFP, speaker selection, or content.

A cloud vendor in front of 400 infrastructure people probably values Platinum above $9,000, so you don't need to discount.

## 2. Caterer

**Value to you**
- Free lunch saves you $4,200 of real budget. It's worth full face value, unlike the credits.

**What to give**
1. Offer a tier at about $4,200. If you have no tier near that price, create a named "Lunch Partner" package.
2. Include these perks:
   - Logo on lunch signage and tables.
   - A thank-you from the stage before lunch.
   - A listing on the sponsors page and in the program.
3. Leave out the booth, attendee data, and Platinum perks. Cash sponsors paid for those.

**Confirm in writing before you accept**
- "Free" covers 400 people for all days, including service, staff, delivery, and VAT.
- Drinks, coffee, and breaks are either included or clearly excluded.
- Dietary and allergen options are covered, including vegan, halal, and gluten-free.
- You have a headcount-change deadline and a no-show policy.
- If they cancel or miss delivery, they cover a replacement.
- Liability and food-safety certifications are in the agreement.

## Why the two offers differ

- **Caterer:** $4,200 saved in cash, so they get about $4,200 of perks.
- **Cloud vendor:** about $2,000 of real value, so they get about $2,000 of perks. Platinum stays at $9,000 cash.
