# What to tell FrontendCamp Lyon's biggest sponsor

**Recommendation:** Answer today, but don't say a flat yes to 15%. Reply with a warm "yes to Gold, and here's how we reward loyalty." Make the discount depend on a commitment from them. Your sponsorship lead is right that you shouldn't go quiet, but replying today doesn't require accepting the number today.

## Why not just say yes

- **A bare 15% is $900 for nothing.** It would be $5,100 instead of $6,000. You get no extra commitment, and it lowers your price for next year too.
- **The deadline is their pressure, not a real one.** A sponsor of three editions isn't walking over a same-day reply that includes a real offer. Answering today with a counter respects their clock without letting it set your price.
- **It sets a precedent.** If other sponsors paid list, they will hear about it. Once "loyalty" means a 15% discount, you can't reverse it.

## What to offer

Trade the discount for something you value, such as certainty or cash timing:

| Option | Their commitment | Our price |
|---|---|---|
| **A (default)** | One edition, Gold | $6,000 list, plus loyalty extras (see below) |
| **B (the real trade)** | Two editions signed now (2027 and 2028), invoice paid within 30 days | 10% off, so $5,400 per edition |
| **Floor** | Same as B, and they push back | Up to 15% off, so $5,100 per edition |

**Loyalty extras for A** (cheap for us, visible to them):
- Priority pick of add-on surfaces and the best booth location.
- Extra staff passes.
- A written post-event report with audience data.
- A price lock for the next edition.

Check one thing before you send this: what Gold actually costs you to deliver. If passes, booth and catering eat most of the margin, set the floor higher than 15% off. I don't have your cost numbers, so treat the 10% and 15% as placeholders for your lead to confirm.

## What to tell your sponsorship lead

- **Agree:** Reply today, and reply warmly. Silence is the real risk.
- **Disagree:** Don't concede the discount before you know what you get for it. If they hold firm on 15% for one edition, make the multi-year version your last move.
- **Next time:** Publish a standing returning-sponsor policy. A 5% discount for a two-edition commitment would do. Then nobody has to negotiate this from scratch.
