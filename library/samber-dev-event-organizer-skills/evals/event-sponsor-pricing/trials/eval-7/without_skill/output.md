**Verdict: the core card is fine, but the Lightning Talk add-on is wrong and should not go live. The Exhibitor Only line is a sound idea that needs two fixes.**

## ❌ Lightning Talk ($3,000): remove it

- **It undercuts Platinum.** Platinum is $8,000, and this sells main-stage time to anyone for $3,000. A buyer who wants the stage will skip Platinum, so you lose $5,000 of revenue per sale. It also gives Gold and Silver sponsors the same stage access.
- **It sells editorial content.** Your attendees are 900 customers and prospects at your own conference, and the main stage is where they decide whether the program is worth their time. Pay-to-play talks are the best-known way to damage a user conference. Developers read a paid slot as a statement about your company, and they punish it.
- **"For anyone who missed Platinum" is the wrong reason to sell it.** It treats the stage as leftover inventory. The stage should be the thing that makes Platinum worth $8,000.

**Alternatives:**
1. Make a 5-minute "sponsor spotlight" a Platinum-only inclusion. This keeps the value in the top tier.
2. Sell a clearly labeled sponsor session on a side track or in a demo theatre, never on the main stage. Cap how many there are.
3. Keep customer and community talks on the main stage, chosen through your CFP. A sponsor can propose a talk to the CFP like anyone else, but the sponsorship buys no slot.

## ⚠️ Exhibitor Only ($1,200): keep it, with changes

The goal is sound. It gets smaller companies onto the floor, and the price sits sensibly under Silver at $2,000. Two fixes:

1. **Define the gap to Silver.** Write down exactly what Silver includes beyond a shared table (logo, passes, booth size). Otherwise Silver at $2,000 looks overpriced next to $1,200.
2. **Resolve "no logo anywhere."** A company paying you has to appear in the exhibitor directory and on the floor map, or attendees can't find them. Say "no logo on slides, signage or the website; name listed in the exhibitor directory." If you mean literally nowhere, say that on the card.

Also state these terms:
- A cap on shared tables.
- Whether the sponsor gets any passes, and how many.
- Whether you or the sponsor hold badge-scan or lead rights. Lead capture should be opt-in only.
- A competitor-adjacency rule, since tables are shared.
- Exclusion from the add-ons, or eligibility only for the cheap ones.

## ✅ Core tiers and add-ons: mostly fine

- **The tier ladder is coherent.** The steps ($2,000, then $4,500, then $8,000) are reasonable. Check that Gold is clearly worth more than 2.25× Silver. The card is incomplete without a benefit matrix per tier (booth size, passes, logo placement, speaking, lead data).
- **Add-ons are sensible.** Lanyards ($5,000), Coffee cart ($2,500) and Happy Hour ($4,000) are the usual branded surfaces. Lanyards at $5,000 is the highest-visibility item at 900 attendees, and sits only $3,000 under Platinum. Decide whether add-ons need a minimum tier. Without one, a Silver sponsor can out-brand a Platinum one.
- **Add exclusivity and caps.** State "one per event" for each add-on (lanyards, for example).

## Other gaps before it goes live

1. **Attendee data.** State what sponsors receive: opt-in scans, or nothing. Never promise an attendee list.
2. **Deadlines.** Add a contract deadline and a date after which add-ons are released. Branded surfaces sell first.
3. **Audience evidence.** Publish the role mix, company sizes and customer-versus-prospect split. Don't lead with "900 attendees" alone.
4. **Conflict rules.** Cover competitor exclusivity (or the lack of it) and sponsor conduct, and state that sponsorship buys no program influence.
5. **Your own conflict of interest.** At a vendor-run event, say what sponsors cannot do: pitch your competitors' products from the stage, or demo against yours.
6. **Revenue sanity check.** Fill the grid with realistic counts (for example 2 Platinum, 4 Gold, 6 Silver, 5 Exhibitor Only). If the exhibitor line would bring in more sponsors than the paid tiers combined, it is cannibalizing them.

## Recommended edits, in order

1. Drop Lightning Talk, or turn it into a Platinum inclusion.
2. Rewrite Exhibitor Only with defined terms and a directory listing.
3. Add the tier benefit matrix, caps and exclusivity.
4. Add the data, deadline and conduct clauses.
