# KubeNord 2027 sponsorship kit: draft

## Three changes to your brief (read first)

1. ❌ **Badge scanning and attendee-list export (Gold perk): not in the kit.**
   - KubeNord is community-run, so no attendee list is given or sold. A scanning row would contradict that rule, and any sponsor who reads your own published terms would catch it.
   - **Replacement:** aggregate audience demographics (page 4) and a "what you do not get" box (page 7). Gold's difference comes from visibility and stage presence instead (page 6).
   - If KubeNord does sell attendee data, or attendees opt in to scanning, tell me. The pole changes, and the row becomes printable with consent wording.
2. ❌ **"We guarantee at least 40 qualified leads": not in the kit.**
   - No guarantee clause of any kind is printed. Community events can't promise lead value. If you miss 40, the sponsor has a written promise to hold against you.
   - Use verified facts instead: attendee count, role mix, and past-sponsor quotes. Page 5 carries the proof.
3. 🟠 **"Lead with the pricing page": price moves up but not to the front.**
   - Proof comes before price: cover, photos, facts, value, testimonials, then tiers on page 6 of 9.
   - To keep the price easy to find, the cover and the contents line say "Packages from EUR 1,800, page 6".
   - Placeholders in `[brackets]` are things I can't know. Don't print anything until each is filled with a real, verified value.
