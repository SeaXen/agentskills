# KubeNord 2027 Sponsorship Kit (draft)

## Two changes to your co-organizer's asks

1. **I replaced the "40 qualified leads" guarantee.** You can't control it, and she'd be promising it in writing to every sponsor.
   - "Qualified" is undefined, and a sponsor can argue any scan isn't qualified.
   - Forty qualified leads is about 11% of a 350-person event for each sponsor.
   - A written guarantee invites refund claims. Sponsors who miss it will hold you to it.
   - The kit instead promises what you control: scanned opt-in contacts, access, and a post-event export date. Add a lead number only once you have real data from past editions.
   - If she insists, offer a defined remedy, such as a credit toward the 2028 edition if opt-in scans fall below a number you can afford. Write that into the contract, not the headline.
2. **Gold gets badge scanning plus an opt-in lead export, not the full attendee list.** KubeNord is an EU event, and sharing the full list with sponsors needs each attendee's explicit consent under GDPR.
   - Attendees opt in at registration, or by letting a sponsor scan their badge.
   - Each sponsor receives only the contacts who opted in to them.
   - It's still a strong perk: consented contacts are the ones a sponsor can actually email.
   - Have your lawyer or a data-protection adviser review the data clause (§5) before this goes out.

## PAGE 3: Gold perk in detail: badge scanning and lead export

**How it works**

1. **At registration,** attendees choose whether to share their contact details with sponsors. Every attendee sees the same clear, unticked-by-default question.
2. **At the event,** your team scans attendee badges with the KubeNord scanner app [or: your own device]. A scan records that the attendee agreed to be contacted by you.
3. **After the event,** within [10] business days, you receive a CSV export of the contacts you scanned and the attendees who opted in to share with all sponsors.

**What the export contains:** name, email, company, role, and [any other fields attendees agree to share]. It carries no data from attendees who declined.

**What you do not receive:** the full attendee list, or contacts who didn't opt in.

**Your responsibilities:** use the data for follow-up on KubeNord-related offers only. Include an unsubscribe in every message. Delete or manage the data under your own privacy policy (see Terms, §5).

**Why this matters:** you get contacts who said yes to hearing from you, so your follow-up lands in an inbox that expects it.
