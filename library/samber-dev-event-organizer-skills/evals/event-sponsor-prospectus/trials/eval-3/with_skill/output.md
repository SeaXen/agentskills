## The four things you flagged

### 1. Red banner on slide 6: "Only 2 Gold spots left!" 🔴
- **Delete it.** It is a stale scarcity counter, and the skill names it as manufactured scarcity.
- Don't replace it with a new "X left" banner.
- Scarcity is only printable as a cap you can prove, such as booth floor space or a single exclusive surface. Put it in a "Max. available" row of the tier table.
- Re-verify each cap against 2027 inventory before it goes in. Gold may no longer be 2 or 3 spots.
- If you print no cap, print nothing.

### 2. Testimonials from 2025 attendees 🔴
- **Replace them.** Last year's voices can't be presented as current.
- Your deck is last year's deck, so these quotes are probably two editions old.
- Sources for fresh quotes, fastest first:
  1. Sponsors that came to the last edition, especially the 7 returning ones.
  2. Public posts from last edition's attendees. Get permission before you screenshot them.
  3. Last edition's feedback survey or post-event sponsor report, if you have one.
- Each quote needs a name or role and the edition it comes from.
- If you can't get at least 2-3 real quotes by next week, cut the slide to what you have. Do not keep the 2025 quotes unlabelled.
- Keep the proof slide before the price slide. If testimonials sit after pricing in your deck, move them up.

### 3. Logo wall: 11 sponsors, 4 not returning 🟠
- **Remove the 4.** Logos of sponsors who didn't return are a named failure mode.
- That leaves 7 logos. A wall of 7 that is true beats a wall of 11 that is padded.
- Decide how to caption it:
  - Label it "2026 sponsors" if it shows who sponsored last edition.
  - Label it "Returning for 2027" only for sponsors who have actually confirmed.
  - Don't use an unlabelled wall.
- Confirm each remaining sponsor is fine with their logo appearing. Check that the files are current brand versions.
- If you have signed 2027 sponsors, add their logos only with their consent.

### 4. Prices up about 15%, not reconciled 🔴
"About 15%" is not a number, so the deck can't be updated from it. Prices come from your rate card, which this deck presents but doesn't set.
1. Get the exact 2027 rate card: tiers, à-la-carte add-ons, any custom-tier note.
2. Go through the deck line by line and update every price cell. Check every slide, not only the tier table:
   - the tier table price row (it must stay the first row)
   - add-on cards
   - any "from €X" mention in the intro, value-prop, or contact slides
   - the discount on extra tickets, and ticket and booth-staff counts, since these change when prices change
3. Check that each tier's perks still match what you will deliver at the new price. If a tier gets more perks, add them. If a perk was dropped, remove it. Never print a perk you can't deliver.
4. Do not round or guess. Don't multiply last year's numbers by 1.15 without checking the rate card.
5. Do not add price-protection language, a "price rises soon" line, or any guarantee.

## What you didn't mention but should check

Edition facts (check every slide):
- Dates, venue, city, and the website URL.
- Any "2026" or "last year" wording left in the copy.
- Dead links, old social handles, old contact emails.

Audience evidence:
- Update it from the latest registration data, with the same fields as before (role, industry, company size).
- Print only splits whose numbers match their categories exactly. If a table can't be verified, cut it down to a verified one-liner.
- Don't carry last year's percentages forward unless they are labelled as last edition's.

Photos and team:
- Replace or confirm the photos. They should come from the latest edition, not stock images.
- Check the organizer names, headshots, and contacts on the final slide.

Honest-content sweep:
- Remove any guarantee wording and any promise of leads or attendee lists.
- A community-run event can't deliver attendee-list or badge-scan perks. If the deck lists any, remove them and say what sponsors will not get.
- Check that every add-on is still available and still exclusive where it says so (lanyard, party, coffee, and so on).

Document anatomy (check order): cover → brand imagery → event facts → value proposition → proof (logos, quotes) → tier table → add-ons → logistics → named contacts.
- Keep it to 10-12 slides at most.
- If a logistics FAQ is missing (booth set-up, shipping, power, wifi, staff count), add one slide, because it saves a reply-to-ask email per sponsor.
