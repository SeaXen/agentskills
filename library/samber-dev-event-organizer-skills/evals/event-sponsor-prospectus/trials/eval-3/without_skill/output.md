# Changes needed before the 2027 sponsor deck goes out

## 0. Before you edit
- ⚠️ **Save a copy** as `RustFest Bratislava 2027 - Sponsor Packages` and edit the copy. Editing "in place" destroys last year's deck, which you'll want for reference and for returning sponsors who ask "what did we get last time?".
- Retitle the deck and the cover slide with the year.

## 1. Slide 6: red banner "Only 2 Gold spots left!"
- ❌ Delete it. It's a February 2026 claim about the 2026 edition, so it's false for 2027.
- Add a new scarcity line only if it's true on send day. Count the Gold spots actually open, then write "N Gold spots remaining" or "Gold: limited to N".
- If you don't know the count yet, leave the banner out. A fake scarcity claim sent to sponsors who talk to each other costs more than it gains.
- Search every other slide for similar urgency or availability text ("sold out", "early bird until…", "limited").

## 2. Logo wall (11 sponsors, 4 not returning)
- Remove the 4 who aren't coming back.
- ⚠️ For the remaining 7, check whether each has confirmed for 2027. If not, relabel the wall "2026 sponsors" or "Past sponsors". A bare logo wall in a 2027 deck implies they're signed.
- Make sure you may still show each remaining logo. Some sponsors' permissions are tied to the year they sponsored.
- Use current logo files, since brands rebrand.
- With 7 logos the grid will look sparse, so re-space the layout. Don't pad it with prospects.

## 3. Testimonials (from 2025 attendees)
- They're two editions old by the time the 2027 edition runs. Either replace them with 2026 attendee or sponsor quotes, or keep them and label them "RustFest Bratislava 2025".
- Ask for 2–3 fresh quotes from 2026 attendees or sponsors. A sponsor quote is stronger in a sponsor deck than an attendee quote.
- Confirm you have permission to reuse each quote, and check that each person's name, title and employer are still correct.
- Remove any quote that mentions a feature, venue or format that has changed for 2027.

## 4. Prices: the 15% increase
Don't multiply by 1.15. Copy every number from the rate card, because the rounded real prices won't equal "+15%". Walk the deck against the card line by line:
- Every package price (all tiers, not just Gold).
- Add-ons and à-la-carte items (extra tickets, booth upgrades, job board, swag insert).
- Currency and VAT treatment ("excl. VAT" vs. "incl.").
- Early-bird or returning-sponsor discounts, and their deadlines.
- Payment terms, invoice dates and cancellation or refund terms.
- Any derived numbers, such as "cost per attendee", "value of package", or comparison tables and totals.
- Whether inclusions changed with the price (ticket counts, booth size, speaking slot, logo placement). A 15% rise with unchanged benefits needs a one-line justification, and a changed benefit must show on the slide.
- ⚠️ Prices may also appear in speaker notes, footers, a pricing summary slide, and the email or PDF filename. Check all of them.

## 5. Other things that will be stale (you didn't list these)
- **Event facts:** 2027 dates, venue, city, tracks, expected attendance. Replace last year's attendance and demographic stats with 2026 actuals or 2027 targets, labeled as such.
- **Dates and deadlines:** sponsorship signing deadline, logo-submission deadline, any "2026" in footers, headers and copyright lines.
- **Photos and speakers:** drop last year's photos or speaker lineup unless captioned "2026".
- **Contact details:** name, email, booking link and any form URL. Click every link.
- **Terms:** code of conduct link, sponsor terms and conditions, and any exclusivity or category-limit wording.
- **Hidden content:** speaker notes, hidden slides, comments, and embedded chart data (right-click → edit data).
- **Metadata:** document author/title properties and the PDF filename.

## 6. Order of work (about a day or two of effort)
1. Duplicate the deck and retitle it.
2. Reconcile all prices against the rate card (the highest-risk item).
3. Resolve the Gold-spot count, then fix or delete the banner.
4. Fix the logo wall and its label.
5. Replace or relabel the testimonials. Request new quotes early, since replies take days.
6. Run a find-and-replace for `2026`, `2025` and `Feb` across slides and notes.
7. Export to PDF, check the metadata, and do a full read-through on a second device.
