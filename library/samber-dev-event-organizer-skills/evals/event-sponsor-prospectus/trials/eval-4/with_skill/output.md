## What was wrong

1. **Three of the five "tiers" are not tiers.** The lanyard, coffee cart and after-party are each one branded surface, and only one sponsor can buy each. They belong in the add-on cards, not in the tier grid. Each add-on card states its own price, so the "what does the coffee thing cost" emails stop.
2. **The stars hide the items.** "3-star premium branding package" says nothing a budget holder can route internally, so it would produce more "what's in it?" emails. A star score also reads like a rating from a third party when it is only your own label. The skill prints plain marks for included items, and it uses numbers only for real quantities such as tickets or discount percentages.
3. **The caps were unmarked.** Only the three exclusive surfaces have a real cap (1 each). Platinum and Gold are unlimited, so no "max available" number is printed for them. A cap printed on an unlimited tier would be invented scarcity.
