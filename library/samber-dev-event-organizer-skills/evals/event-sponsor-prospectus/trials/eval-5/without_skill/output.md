**Notes before the packet:**
- ⚠️ **Resumes:** I wrote "every participant who opts in," not "all participating students." Under GDPR you can't hand over students' resumes and contact details without their explicit, specific consent. A packet that promises "all" invites a complaint to the university or the data regulator. Registration needs a separate, unticked recruiting-consent box. Sponsors will get a list of the students who tick it.
- ℹ️ **Workshop slot:** I kept it, as requested. It sits at the end of the final morning, before submissions close. Hackers will be coding at that point, so I made attendance optional and told sponsors to expect a smaller room.
- ℹ️ **Prize:** I kept it as specified. The packet states that the prize is sponsor-funded and that the track is judged separately from the grand prize. A EUR 10,000 prize beside a EUR 2,000 grand prize may draw questions from hackers.
- ℹ️ **Placeholders:** Items in [brackets] are things you didn't give me (dates, prices, venue, contact). I left tier prices out so I didn't invent them.
- ℹ️ **Length:** There's no hard 2-3 page rule, but this packet fits that length.

## Sponsor-run workshops

Platinum sponsors get one 45-minute workshop on the **final morning, just before submissions close**.

This slot reaches hackers who are finishing their projects. It is also when they are most pressed for time. Sessions work best when they are:
- **Practical:** a working walkthrough of your API or tool that attendees can use in their project in the next hour
- **Short:** about 30 minutes of content and 15 for questions
- **Optional:** attendance is voluntary, so expect a smaller room than an evening session would draw

We will announce the slot in advance so teams can plan around it. Gold sponsors run their workshop earlier in the weekend, when teams are still choosing what to build.

## Platinum prize track

**Best Vaultly-powered invoicing tool for small retailers: EUR 10,000**

Vaultly, our platinum sponsor, funds and defines this track in full. Teams must build an invoicing tool for small retailers using Vaultly. The track is open to every team.

- **Prize:** EUR 10,000, paid by the sponsor. HackAtlantic's own grand prize is EUR 2,000.
- **Judging:** a separate panel scores the track, with Vaultly representatives and at least one HackAtlantic organizer. The grand prize and track prizes are judged independently, and a team can win both.
- **Criteria** (published to all hackers in advance): real use of Vaultly, fit for small retailers, working demo, and quality of the invoicing flow.
- **Support:** Vaultly credits, documentation, and mentors available for the full 48 hours.

Other tiers can sponsor smaller tracks. Ask us.
