## Why sponsor GoWest

Sponsors get two things from us: **visibility with working Go engineers** and **a way to talk to them about jobs**. We offer nothing beyond these.

### Brand: be known by the people who choose the tools

Go developers choose their own libraries, cloud providers, databases and observability stacks, and they recommend them to their teams. A sponsor name seen across two days at a talk-led, community-run conference reaches them in a setting they trust. Our attendees are there to learn, not to be sold to, and they notice who funded that.

- Your logo is on the stage banner, slides, website and newsletter, according to your tier.
- Your staff talk with attendees at the booth, over coffee and at the party. A good conversation with an engineer does more for your brand than a banner does.
- Attendees see you supporting a community event that stayed free for two editions. That reflects well on you.

### Hiring: meet engineers in a conversation, not a CV database

Go engineers are hard to hire. At GoWest you can meet them in person, talk about real engineering problems, and let them see how your team works.

- **Recruiting corner:** a dedicated space for open conversations about roles, with no scanning and no collected lists. [Confirm you will provide it.]
- **Job board:** your open Go roles are listed on the GoWest site and in the attendee email. [Confirm you will provide it.]
- **Your engineers on the booth:** candidates judge a company by the people they meet. Send the engineers they would work with.
- **Your talks:** sponsors' employees can submit to the open CFP like anyone else. A talk accepted on merit is the strongest hiring signal you can get. Sponsorship does not buy a slot.

### What sponsors do not get

- No attendee list, contact data or badge-scan leads. Attendees keep control of their own data.
- No purchasable speaking slots or influence over the programme. The CFP review decides the talks.
- No guaranteed number of leads, hires or conversations. We sell visibility and access, not outcomes.

## Why these prices

- **Anchor:** DevOpsDays Graz (about 300 people) charges €4,000 / €2,500 / €1,500 at the three tiers. GoWest is a third larger, so Gold lands at €4,500 and Silver at €2,500.
- **Bronze at €1,200:** you have no sponsor track record, so the entry price should be easy for a small Go shop to approve without a long budget process.
- **Spacing:** Gold costs about 1.8× Silver and Silver about 2× Bronze, so each step up has a visible difference in perks.
- **Revenue ceiling:** if every printed cap sold, the total would be about €45,900. Treat that as a ceiling, not a forecast. A first commercial edition should plan for far fewer sales.
- **Review after edition 1:** Gold is the tier most likely to be mispriced, because it has no sales history yet. Use this edition's sales and the questions prospects ask to adjust next year.
