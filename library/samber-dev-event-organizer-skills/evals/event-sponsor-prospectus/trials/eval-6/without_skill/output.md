## 1. Why sponsor GoWest

GoWest is a Go conference for working Go engineers. It is a single-track-friendly, community-run event, not a trade show. Two free editions built the audience. This is the first edition with sponsors, so early partners get founding-sponsor terms and visibility they cannot get later.

### Brand: be known by the people who choose the tools

- **Go engineers decide what gets built and bought.** At a Go conference, the people in the room choose or influence their team's infrastructure, libraries, cloud and tooling. Few events put 400 of them together.
- **Credibility comes from helping.** Developers distrust ad-driven booths. They remember companies that answered a hard question, ran a good workshop or paid for something the community needed. Our packages are built around that.
- **Founding-sponsor status.** The first commercial edition sets the order for later years. Founding sponsors are named as such on the website, in the program and on the opening slides, for every future edition.
- **Reach beyond the room.** Sponsors get recognition on the website, in our newsletter and social posts, and in the talk recordings [CONFIRM: recorded and published?]. Recordings keep working for years after the event.

### Hiring: reach engineers who are hard to find

- **Go talent is scarce and rarely on the market.** Most engineers at GoWest aren't job hunting. They are the engineers every hiring manager wants, and they respond to conversations, not job ads.
- **Europe's Go community in one place.** Attendees come from [X] countries [FILL FROM LAST EDITION'S DATA]. If you hire in Europe, whether remote, hybrid or Iberian, this is where to meet them.
- **Porto is part of the pitch.** Porto and Portugal have a growing tech scene, direct flights from most European hubs, and prices well below other European conference cities. Sponsors with a Portuguese or nearshore team can show that off.
- **Real conversations, not résumés.** Hiring sponsors get dedicated time and space to talk with engineers: a recruiting table, a hiring lightning talk, or a hosted dinner. We pass on contact details **only for attendees who opt in**, so the leads are people who wanted to hear from you.
- **Your engineers are your recruiters.** The strongest employer brand at a developer event is a sponsor's engineer giving a good talk or fixing a stranger's bug at the booth. Every tier includes passes for your technical staff.
