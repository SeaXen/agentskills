## Is it good?

**Benchmark.** The "62% average" circulates but traces to no published study, so don't compare against it and never quote it to a sponsor. The nearest measured figure is trade-show exhibitor company retention: 73% for small shows, 78% for midsize and 69% for the largest (CEIR 2026 organizer survey, sample size not published). It is a weak proxy. By that yardstick 86% on 7 sponsors is above range. The final figure could still fall to 67%.

**Verdict.** The signal is healthy. Revenue per surviving sponsor grew, and two sponsors moved up a tier despite the price increase. Treat your own trend as the real benchmark: this is edition 1 of the series, so keep this cohort as the baseline.
