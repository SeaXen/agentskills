# Ridgecombe API Summit: renewal offers (from `event-sponsor-renewal`, as of Oct 26)

I read `SKILL.md` and both references. I treated Ridgecombe as a company-run event, because you have paid staff, consented scans and sponsored talks. That makes talk slots, scan hand-offs, category exclusivity and multi-year terms legitimate renewal currency. I did not run the interview, so I made the assumptions below.

## Check these before any ask goes out

1. **Report items.** Each Oct 20 report needs the five items. The Vexmoor report must name the missing logo as a miss, with its make-good.
2. **Recording views.** If a report row says "pending, sent by [date]", bring that figure to the call.
3. **Unpaid invoices.** Hold any ask on a sponsor with an unpaid invoice.
4. **Red flags.** Unanswered reports count as flag 5 from **Nov 3** (10 business days after Oct 20). Alderquay refusing to give a lock date is not a flag, because an unobservable flag counts as absent. Ask once more on the call.

## 1. Vexmoor (Platinum, anchor)

**Contact.** Hold an executive meeting, the promoted rung for an anchor sponsor with a multi-edition deal on the table. Their lock is about 11 weeks away, so the override for a lock under a month away does not apply. Book it for the week of Nov 9 and run the debrief call first if it hasn't happened. Ask who signs besides the champion and who sits in procurement.

**Offer.**

| Item | Offer |
| --- | --- |
| Package | Platinum for next edition. The date and venue are "confirmed by [February date]". |
| Price | The rate-card price from `event-sponsor-pricing`. If that isn't set, quote it as indicative with a confirmation date. |
| Term | A three-year agreement, which is reasonable for a company-run event because they asked. Year one is fixed. Later years carry an escalator (CPI or 3-5%, both judged, not a measured norm). It has a yearly review point and a cancellation-refund clause drafted by counsel. |
| Hold | A Platinum hold (a right of first negotiation) until **Fri Dec 18**. That is the 60-day edge of the 30-60 day window. |
| Intent | Their lock falls after the hold window, so ask for **written intent by Dec 18**. The slot stays held only on that intent, and it converts at their mid-January lock. |
| Incentive | First pick of capped surfaces: sponsored talk slot, category exclusivity at its card premium within the published cap, and booth placement. |
| Date risk | The agreement must say what happens if the February date or venue moves. Counsel and `event-sponsor-agreement` own that clause. |

**Formal ask.** Send the written proposal and agreement draft by about **Dec 1**. Procurement and counsel need weeks, so the real deadline is the mid-January lock, but don't use it. Hand a yes to `event-sponsor-agreement`.

**Right of first refusal with matching rights: decline.** The skill deletes it. A competitor won't bid on a slot someone else can match, and one organizer-side law firm advises dropping it for that reason. Offer the time-boxed hold above instead. Procurement gets what it needs, which is certainty for its January budget.

**The 2k and the 20%: don't do either as proposed.**

- **20% early-renewal discount: no.** The skill deletes loyalty discounts. On a sponsor worth about half your revenue, it becomes a permanently discounted anchor, and a three-year term compounds it. If the rate card has an early-bird, offer it to every sponsor on the same terms. Otherwise, the first-pick surfaces and, if a price increase exists, the published price-change date (see the Price increase paragraph below) are the incentives.
- **The 2k:** the logo miss is a make-good, which is compensation, not an incentive.
  - Keep it in kind. Add the logo to the recordings or give an equivalent placement, and deliver it before the formal ask, because the gate is delivered, not promised.
  - If cash is the only way to make good, it is a credit toward next edition. That goes through pricing's deviation log, kept apart from the renewal price and never stacked with another reduction.
- **Price increase:** if one applies, tell them privately at the meeting, tied to a documented reason, with the published price-change date.

## 2. Alderquay (Gold) and Brinewood (Silver): the entrance booth

**Who gets it.** The rule is higher tier first, then whoever confirmed first. Alderquay (Gold) gets first pick. Brinewood asking a week earlier doesn't count, because an ask is not a confirmation. Tell both sponsors the rule. Don't let the order of emails decide it.

**Alderquay.**
- Offer the entrance booth, held until **Fri Dec 4**. That is 45 days after the report, inside the 30-60 day window, and it is a short hold because the contest delays Brinewood's answer.
- Their lock is unknown, so ask once more on the call. If they still won't say, the hold date stands. Ask only for written intent by Dec 4, not a signature.
- Without written intent by then, the booth opens.

**Brinewood.**
- Tell them now that Alderquay has first pick and why.
- Offer the best other booth, held until **Dec 18**. If Alderquay gives no intent by Dec 4 and Brinewood has given written intent, the entrance booth goes to Brinewood.
- Their August lock falls after the hold window, so ask for **written intent by the hold date** and convert at the August lock. The surface stays held only on that intent.
- Ask which year the August budget funds. If it funds the edition after next, say so now.
- Send the formal ask by about early July, before the August lock.
- If a price increase applies, tell them privately. The published price-change date covers the gap before their lock.
