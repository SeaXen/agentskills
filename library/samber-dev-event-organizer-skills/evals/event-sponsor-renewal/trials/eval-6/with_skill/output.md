   - Every Kestrelbank invoice is paid. An unpaid invoice holds the ask.
   - Their survey verdict. A "no" means a same-day call and the rescue path.
   - Red flags: a changed champion, silence, unused perks.
   Two or more flags put them on the rescue path first, with no price talk until the reason is answered.
2. **By Wed Sept 16:** ask the champion for 30 minutes by phone. Open with a specific result and one new idea. Don't write "are you coming back?".
3. **Before the call:** send a short-form report early, only for Kestrelbank, with the five required items.

   If you can't build it in time, use last year's report plus Sept 12 headline numbers.
4. **On the call:** ask what their team reports to whom, who else signs off, what exactly must be in the budget by the lock (often a figure and a package, not a contract), and the true cutoff date. Then make the soft ask: one package, with a hold date of Thu Sept 17 or Fri Sept 18.
5. **By Fri Sept 18:** send the formal ask. It names one package and a price. If the date or venue is unset, state "confirmed by [date]". Add first pick of capped surfaces (booth location, workshop room, prize track).
6. **If a price increase exists:** tell them privately on the call, with a documented reason. Quote it as indicative until your pricing is set, and publish the price-change date.
7. **A yes** goes to the sponsor-agreement step.
