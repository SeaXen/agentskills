# Marrowgate JS Conf renewals: what to send

**Don't give either sponsor the attendee email list.** Attendees agreed to hear from Marrowgate. They didn't agree to be passed to third parties, so sharing the list is a GDPR risk and a trust problem for a community event. It also wouldn't fix anything. Ledgerwick's issue is audience fit, and Pinecrest's proposal doesn't need a sweetener. If you want a sponsor-reach perk, offer a message sent by you to attendees who opted in to sponsor messages. Add that opt-in checkbox to next year's registration.

## 1. Pinecrest Analytics (Gold): send the proposal, built around the workshop

Their booth was quiet and the workshop had a waiting list, so shape the proposal around the workshop. Keep the pending recording views out of the pitch. Don't quote a number you don't have. Send the figure by Nov 5 as promised, or earlier if you can.

## 2. Ledgerwick Compliance: don't upsell, decline the keynote, and say why

Zero conversations at the booth means the audience is wrong. A bigger booth would fail more expensively, and they'd blame you. A keynote is the other problem. Selling a keynote slot to a sponsor makes the main stage pay-to-play, and attendees notice. Keep keynotes editorial.

Recommended: be honest, don't renew at a bigger tier, and leave one door open through the normal CFP.

**Subject:** Re: Marrowgate JS Conf [year]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for the kind words, and for wanting to come back.

I'd rather be direct than sell you something that won't work. Our attendees are frontend developers, and your booth had very few conversations. A bigger booth wouldn't change who walks past it, so I don't recommend upgrading.

On the keynote: our keynote slots aren't part of sponsorship packages. They go through our editorial selection, so I can't offer one.

Here's what I can offer:
- **Talk via CFP:** if you have a topic frontend developers care about, such as handling sensitive financial data in the browser or compliance constraints on UI work, submit it to our CFP like everyone else. Speakers get selected on content.
- **Same-tier booth** at [price], only if you have a frontend-relevant angle to lead with. Otherwise I'd suggest skipping this edition and finding events where fintech and compliance buyers are in the room. I'm happy to point to a few.

I'd like to stay in touch either way.

Best,
[Name]
