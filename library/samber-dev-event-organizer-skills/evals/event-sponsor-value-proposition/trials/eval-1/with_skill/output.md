## Next action

Don't promise the attendee list or the keynote. Neither is a bargaining chip, and agreeing would break the rules a community-run, CFP-driven event stands on. Offer the honest package below, and say plainly what's not in it.

## Not offered (state this first)

Sponsors get this before they hear the benefits.

- ❌ **No attendee list.** Nobody gets contact data, at any tier. Aggregate demographics are shared instead. A contact moves only when an attendee volunteers it, face to face.
- ❌ **No purchased keynote or speaking slot.** Money buys placement and access, never program content. Kestrel's CTO can submit a talk to the CFP like any other speaker. It's judged on merit, and the outcome is not tied to sponsoring.
- ❌ **No influence over the program, speaker selection or roadmap.**
- ❌ **No lead-generation or pipeline promise.** At a community pole the event can't deliver one, and a diligent sponsor who has read your published rules will see that.

ℹ️ The skill says to delete the pipeline category, not park it "for later". A parked promise tends to reappear in the deck as scope.

## Value-proposition statement: Kestrel Cloud, brand buyer

### Who

Kestrel Cloud's marketing director, who owns developer awareness and perception. The CTO is a participant, not a second buyer.

### Why

Their stated need is to reach the attendees directly (the list) and put their CTO on stage in front of them. The job underneath is to be known, and trusted, by Hamburg-area engineers. A list and a keynote are the two tactics they picked for that.

### What before

I'm assuming these. Please correct them from your call with her.

- Paid developer media that engineers block or distrust.
- Booths at larger conferences, where the logo is one of many.
- Cold outreach to the same audience.

### How

Each claim carries its status.

1. **Named top-tier association with a community-run, CFP-driven conference.**
   - Value: sponsoring signals that Kestrel backs the community's own program, and it's visible to the whole room.
   - Status: *evidenced*, if the no-purchased-slots rule is published, which makes the program structurally independent. Publish it before you send anything.
2. **Visibility inventory** (logo, signage, mentions, digital).
   - Status: *evidenced*, since you control the inventory.
   - Staff-free: this route needs no Kestrel staff on site.
3. **A staffed table or space where attendees choose to talk.**
   - Value: Kestrel engineers meet attendees who opt in. Contact details move only if the attendee volunteers them.
   - Status: *aspirational*, because it depends on Kestrel sending engineers (open item 3). Budget two engineers, not only a marketing rep.
4. **An activity nobody else in Hamburg runs, held outside the CFP program and labelled as sponsor-led.**
   - Example: a hands-on session or CTO office hours.
   - Status: *aspirational*. I haven't seen your program or the Hamburg event landscape, so this is a placeholder. You choose the format.
5. **Audience at edition 2: 320 attendees at edition 1.**
   - Status: *evidenced*, the one hard number I have.
   - Missing: role, seniority and company-size mix.
   - Do not write "senior engineers" or "decision-makers" without that data.
6. **A post-event sponsor report** (reach, attendance, aggregate audience mix, activity participation).
   - Status: *aspirational*, as a checkable commitment.
   - Say what it will contain, and don't present it as history.

### What after

- **Lands on site:** conversations with attendees who chose to talk, and direct hallway feedback from a technical audience.
- **Compounds across editions:** recognition in a community that paid channels can't reach credibly, and standing as a backer of the community.
- **Not promised:** leads, a conversion rate, or any number I can't source.

### Alternatives

| Option | What it gives Kestrel | Where this event differs |
|---|---|---|
| Larger vendor conference | Reach, with the logo one of forty | Smaller room, community trust, more room for conversation |
| Developer-media ads | Impressions the audience may block | Association with a program Kestrel can't buy into |
| Their own user event | Existing users only | An outside audience |

Be honest if it's not their best channel. An event with one prior edition won't beat a large conference on reach.

### Sponsor's cost (name it before they do)

Fee, booth or space build, Hamburg travel, and staff days. A booth costs staff days on top of the fee. That's why the staff-free visibility route exists.

## Open items

2. Is the no-slots and no-lists rule published? If not, the "evidenced" label on claim 1 doesn't hold.

4. Which sponsor-led activity, if any, can you run outside the CFP program?

6. Is the top tier a presenting or co-branded sponsorship? If so, hold the line on control.
7. Is Kestrel's hiring active? That's the signal for whether recruiting could join as co-lead. It probably doesn't matter for a marketing buyer.
