# Ollavant sponsor reply

**I can't write the 400 and 4x claims.** Both would be false or unsupported, and a fintech's due diligence is likely to catch them.

- **400 developers:** you have 140 meetup members and 210 waitlist sign-ups. Those groups may overlap, and not everyone on a waitlist shows up. Calling that 400 attendees is a number you can't defend on the day.
- **"Sponsors typically see 4x":** I know of no source for this, and this is your first hackathon, so you have no track record to cite. A fintech will ask where it comes from.

Honest numbers can still make a strong pitch. This is a draft you can send, with placeholders in brackets.

---

**Subject: Brackenhall Build – what Ollavant would get, and how we'd measure it**

Hi [Name],

Thanks for asking about return. I'd rather answer with what we can measure than quote a multiple, since Brackenhall Build is our first hackathon and we have no prior sponsor results to cite.

**What we have today**
- A monthly developer meetup that draws about 140 people.
- 210 people on the hackathon waitlist, 5 weeks before the event [adjust to the real timing].
- [Optional: the share of the waitlist working in fintech, payments or backend roles, if you can pull it from sign-up data.]

**What Ollavant gets** [edit to the package you're actually offering]
- [Challenge track, e.g. "Best use of Ollavant's API"], with your team writing the brief.
- [Mentor and judge seats], giving your engineers hands-on time with builders.
- [Logo placement, a demo slot, and the project list with each team's consent.]

**How we'd measure return**
We'd agree on three or four numbers with you before the event and send you a report within [two weeks] afterward:
1. Teams that built on Ollavant's API or product, out of total teams.
2. Developer accounts or API keys created during the event, and how many are still active 30 days later.
3. Qualified conversations between your team and participants, and any hires or partnership leads that come out of them.
4. Actual attendance, which we'll report as the real figure.

This puts the return on the numbers that matter to you, such as activations and conversations. It also lets you judge the first edition against your own cost per developer reached, rather than against an industry figure we can't verify.

**Proposal:** a 20-minute call this week to choose the metrics and fit the package to your goals. As a first-edition partner, you'd also get [founding-sponsor pricing / first refusal on next year / a say in the challenge design].

Best,
[Name]
