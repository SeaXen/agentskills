# Evidence to collect for Calderhaus Conf edition 2

**Next action (5 min):** send the sponsor emails (item 2) and the attendee survey (item 4) first. Both wait on other people, and everything else you can do while they reply.

The budget is about 3.5 hours, with 30 minutes spare to assemble a one-pager. I haven't seen your data, so every number below is yours to fill in. If a number doesn't exist, write "not measured" and don't estimate.

Sponsors buy qualified conversations with specific people. Headcount, impressions and press mentions don't answer that, so skip them.

## The five items, in order

**1. Last year's sponsors (60 min, highest value)**
- From the roster, mark who returned, who left, and who is new in edition 2.
- Email 5 or 6 past sponsors, including at least 2 who didn't stay. Ask three questions:
  - What did your team do on site?
  - How many conversations continued afterward, and did any become a trial, hire, partner or deal?
  - What would you change in the package?
- Ask permission to quote them. One named quote with a real outcome is worth more than any attendance figure.
- Churned sponsors tell you what to fix before a prospect asks about it.

**2. Attendee composition from edition-one registration data (45 min)**
- Pull role or job function, seniority, company size, country, and ticket type.
- Pull registered versus attended, and the share who attended more than once.
- Pull any buying-influence field, if you collected one.
- Check your consent wording first. Publish aggregates only.
- Map each sponsor category on last year's roster to the share of attendees it wants to reach, e.g. "x% platform or infra engineers at companies over 200 staff."

**3. Attendee pulse survey (send now, 15 min to write, review in 15)**
- Send three questions to last year's attendees:
  - Which sponsors do you remember or talk to?
  - Did you act on any of it (try, buy, apply, introduce)?
  - Would you come back?
- Sponsor recall plus action is the closest thing to evidence of sponsor value you can get in three weeks.
- Send it today to get most replies before registration opens. Report the response count alongside the percentages.

**4. Program audit from the published program (30 min)**
- Count technical sessions versus sponsor-authored sessions.
- Group sessions by topic or ecosystem, and count distinct speaker companies.
- Add room counts or video views per session if you have them.
- This shows sponsors the editorial balance. It also shows which topics draw an audience and which sponsor category should sit next to them.

**5. Edition-two momentum and package facts (45 min)**
- Gather any leading indicator that exists: waitlist or interest-list size, CFP submissions, confirmed speakers, venue capacity versus edition one, early commitments from last year's sponsors.
- Write down exactly what each package delivers: passes, booth, workshop slot, and what contact data the sponsor gets under what consent.
- Note which categories were absent from last year's roster, so you can show competitor-free space.

## What to hold back

- Make no claim without a number behind it.
- Don't sell an attendee list you don't have consent to share.
- Don't promise leads. Promise access, and show past sponsors' own outcomes.
- If the registered-versus-attended gap is large, say so and show what you're doing about it. Sponsors will ask.

## Finish: one page (30 min)

Assemble the one-pager in this order:
1. Audience table (item 2).
2. Two or three sponsor quotes with outcomes (item 1).
3. Survey recall and action rates (item 3).
4. Program balance (item 4).
5. Edition-two growth signals and package line items (item 5).

Items 3 and 5 may be thin by the time you start sponsor conversations. If so, lead with items 1 and 2.
