# Thornwick Rust Days: travel footprint

**Number for the deck: about 400 t CO2e (396 t), attendee travel only.**

That is roughly 0.7 t per person. It is not a total event footprint, because venue energy, catering, accommodation and materials are not included. Don't label it "total footprint" in the deck. Use "travel footprint" or "estimated CO2e from attendee travel".

## Assumptions

I picked the distances and the 575-person headcount. Please check them.

- **Headcount:** 575 travellers (500 attendees + 30 speakers + 25 volunteers + 20 sponsor staff), each travelling round trip.
- **Distances:** average one-way distance from Zurich, plus your 10% detour, doubled for the return trip.
- **Switzerland:** I assumed ground travel, not flights. I used 0.05 kg/pkm, a rail and car mix, because your flight factor would overstate short domestic trips.
- **Everyone else:** your 0.25 kg/pkm flight factor, applied to all of them.

## Caveats

- **Long-haul dominates.** The 10% of people coming from the US and Asia produce about 68% of the total. Expect questions about this in the sponsor Q&A.
- **Your factor is on the high side.** 0.25 kg/pkm is at the upper end of published figures, which often sit around 0.15 to 0.20 for long-haul. If you use the lower end for long-haul, the total drops noticeably. Keep 0.25 if you want a conservative number, and say so in the deck.
- **Headcount.** If your 500 attendees already include speakers, volunteers and sponsor staff, the total is about 345 t.
- **Rail.** Many European attendees from Germany, Austria and northern Italy will take the train. Treating them all as flights overstates their share.

You can quote "about 400 t CO2e from travel (estimate, assumes all non-Swiss travel by air)" without defending it line by line. If you tell me the real headcount and likely rail share, I can tighten the figure before next week.
