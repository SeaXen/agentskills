**Plan 1, this edition**

1. **Booked-travel policy** (one written page): economy by default, direct flights where available, rail or coach where the journey is short, regional keynote speakers where the programme allows. Publish it only if you book or pay for trips. Access exceptions are owned by `event-accessibility-inclusion` and always override the policy. Never rank people by their flight footprint.
2. **Remote speaking option:** offered, never imposed. Same slot, same billing. It never replaces a travel grant.
3. **Train-times page:** mostly deleted. Few flyers have a rail option into Singapore. Add a short "arriving overland from Malaysia" note only if it adds real value.
4. **Plant-based catering default,** alternatives on request. This is small (about 1–3% of travel at one modelled conference) but cheap and visible. Check it against halal and other dietary commitments with `event-hospitality`.
5. **Venue energy ask:** metered kWh, and whether supply is a renewable contract or unbundled certificates.
6. **Waste actions,** alongside the above and never as the headline:
   - Weigh food waste and donate surplus. First confirm the venue's exclusive caterer and local food-safety rules allow donation.
   - Reusable lanyards, collected at the exit.
   - Refill stations, with bendable straws available without asking.
   - No swag bag.

## 8. Claim rules

I'm not a lawyer. Have someone qualified review the page before it goes live.

- **EU (25% of your audience).** Directive 2024/825 has applied since 27 September 2026. Offset-based neutrality claims, generic claims such as "green", and self-made labels are blacklisted. Numeric future targets need a detailed plan verified by an independent expert. I don't know whether you sell tickets to EU individuals, so I applied the rule anyway.
- **UK and US (California).** The CMA Green Claims Code (UK) and California AB 1305 apply if you market there and make neutrality or reduction claims. This page makes none.
- **Singapore.** I haven't verified Singapore-specific environmental-claim rules. I defaulted to the strictest rule: no offset-based neutrality, no generic labels, targets only with a plan.
- **ISO 20121.** Say nothing about it unless the management-system cycle (policy, issue evaluation, supplier requirements, post-event review) actually runs. Then write "self-declared".
- **Numeric targets** stay internal. This page publishes action commitments only.
