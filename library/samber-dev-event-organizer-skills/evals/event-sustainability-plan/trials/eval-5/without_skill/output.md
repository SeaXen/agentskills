# Pelton Cloud Days 2026: 9.8 tonnes CO2e from venue, food and waste

*Travel not yet measured. This figure is not comparable with last year's 41 t.*

Pelton Cloud Days 2026, our third edition, took place in Manchester with 700 attendees. This report covers what we measured on site: venue electricity, food and waste. It does not cover attendee travel, which we did not measure.

## Headline figures

| | |
|---|---|
| Measured footprint (venue, food, waste) | **9.8 t CO2e** |
| Per attendee | about 14 kg CO2e |
| Scope | Venue electricity, catering, waste |
| Not included | Attendee travel, accommodation, speaker and staff travel, event production and materials |
| Emission factors | Latest edition of the UK government greenhouse gas conversion factors |

## What we measured

| Area | Activity data | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Venue electricity | 18,400 kWh | Metered |
| Food | 2,100 meals served (3 per attendee) | Caterer count |
| Food waste | 310 kg weighed | Weighed on site |
| Surplus food donated | 95 kg | Recorded at hand-over |
| General waste | 1.2 t (1.7 kg per attendee) | Waste contractor |

## What we did not measure, and why

We did not ask attendees how they travelled when they registered. That was our mistake. For most conferences travel is the largest part of the footprint, so the 9.8 t is a floor, not the total event footprint.

We hold two partial sources, neither good enough to publish a figure from:

- **Billing country** for every attendee. It shows where an invoice goes, not where someone travelled from or how far.
- **A post-event survey** sent to 120 people. 64 answered with their travel mode, which is 9% of attendees, and respondents chose themselves.

We will not extrapolate from these.

## Comparison with last year

Last year we reported 41 t, including travel from a registration question. That figure used an older edition of the government factors. The two years differ in scope and in factor edition, so we are not publishing a percentage change.

## What we will do next year

1. Ask travel origin, mode and return-trip plans at registration.
2. Report travel as its own line, with the method stated.
3. Restate this year's and last year's figures on the same scope and factor edition, so the comparison is fair.
4. Keep weighing food waste and metering electricity.
