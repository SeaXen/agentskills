# Cindermoor DevCon speaker travel policy

**Status:** draft for team sign-off. Three of the team's proposals changed. The reasons come first, then the policy text.

## What changed from the team's proposals, and why

| Proposal | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Distance penalty in CFP scoring | ❌ Rejected | It systematically ranks down the speakers the grant program exists to bring in. It also rewards location instead of talk quality, and the saving is tiny. |
| Replace travel grants with free remote tickets | ❌ Rejected | The grant is access to the room: hallway track, hiring, networking, visibility. A remote ticket removes that. It also cuts the program's reach. Remote tickets become an optional addition, not a replacement. |
| All flights economy, no exceptions | ⚠️ Modified | Economy is the default. A "no exceptions" rule fails on accessibility and medical needs, and on very long itineraries. Exceptions are narrow, written, and approved by one named owner. |

**Footprint context.** About 37 funded flights (25 speakers plus 12 grantees) is a small share of the travel behind an 800-person event. Attendee travel is usually the largest part. The policy cuts speaker and grantee emissions where that costs nothing in fairness, and the team should measure the rest before claiming a result.

## Policy

### 1. Principles

1. Talk quality decides selection. Location does not.
2. Funded travel is for access, so the grant program stays whole.
3. Cut emissions through itinerary choices and measurement, not by excluding people.
4. Every exception is written, approved by one owner, and logged.

### 2. CFP selection

- Review submissions blind to speaker location where the tooling allows.
- Never use distance, country, or flight cost as a scoring input, a tie-breaker, or a filter.
- Ask speakers for a travel preference at acceptance, not at submission. Options are "I will travel", "I prefer to speak remotely", and "either".
- Treat remote talks as full-status slots. Pre-record or live-stream them. They have the same stage time, billing, and Q&A as in-person talks.
- Keep selection committee notes on diversity of topic and speaker background. Do not trade them off against footprint.

### 3. Travel grants

- Keep **12 grants**, reserved for underrepresented developers. Prioritize applicants from outside Europe.
- A grant covers economy round-trip airfare, accommodation for the event nights plus travel days, visa fees, and a daily allowance. Fix the allowance amount before applications open. `[amount: TBD]`
- Pay or book directly where possible. Where reimbursement is unavoidable, pay within `[N: TBD]` days of receipts. Grantees often cannot front long-haul fares.
- Open applications early enough for visa processing. Give a date for decisions that leaves grantees a visa lead time of at least `[N weeks: TBD]`. Offer invitation letters on request.
- Selection criteria are published. They cover need and background, plus a short statement of interest. They never consider distance or flight cost.
- **Remote ticket option:** an applicant who cannot or prefers not to travel may take a free remote ticket instead, and the grant passes to the next applicant. Declined grants are never banked as savings.
- Offer grantees mentoring and introductions, as a courtesy, not as a condition.

### 4. Flights

**Default:** economy fare, booked by the organizers or reimbursed within the cap. `[cap per itinerary: TBD]`

**Exceptions** (premium economy or business class), approved in writing by `[travel owner: TBD]`:

1. **Accessibility or medical need.** The speaker states the need. No medical documentation is required unless the airline demands it.
2. **Long-haul with a short turnaround.** A single leg over `[8–9 hours: TBD]`, or total journey time over `[20 hours: TBD]`, when the speaker presents within 24 hours of arrival. Premium economy first, business only if premium economy is unavailable on the route.
3. **Safety or disruption.** Rebooking after cancellation, or an itinerary change needed for personal safety.

Log every exception with the reason, the extra cost, and the approver. Review the log after the event.

**Itinerary rules (apply to everyone):**

- Prefer direct flights and the fewest connections.
- Use rail instead of a flight for trips of about `[6 hours: TBD]` or less by train.
- Book one trip per speaker. Where a speaker is already in Europe for another engagement, build the itinerary around it when they agree.
- Book the lower-emission option when fares are within `[X%: TBD]` of each other.
- Never route through an extra continent to save money.

### 5. Footprint measurement

- Estimate emissions per funded itinerary at booking, using one stated calculator, and record them.
- Add an attendee travel question to registration (origin region, mode) so the next policy rests on real data.
- Publish a short post-event summary: funded flights, estimated emissions, exceptions granted, grants awarded by region.
- Carbon offsets are optional and never a substitute for the steps above. If used, buy verified removal or high-quality credits and say so.
