## Leadership's three claims

None of the three can go on the page as worded.

| Requested wording | Verdict | Why | What the page says instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| "ISO 20121 compliant" | ❌ Not as written | ISO 20121 is a management-system standard, not a carbon method. Reading it and holding a policy document is not conformity. "Certified" needs a named certification body. "Self-declared conformity" needs the cycle to actually run: written policy, evaluation of significant issues, supplier requirements, objectives, and a post-event review. | Say nothing about it until that cycle runs. Wording for later is in the "ISO 20121" section of the page below. |
| "Net zero by 2030" | ❌ Remove | No baseline, no implementation plan, and no independent verifier. The EU rule requires all three, and the UK ASA discourages unqualified net-zero claims. California's AB 1305 requires disclosures from entities in California that claim net zero. FTC Green Guides also apply. | Action commitments and a measured result, with no target year (see "What we will change next edition"). Keep any numeric target internal. |
| "Powered by 100% renewable energy" | ❌ Not as written | The venue's claim rests on renewable energy credits. That is market-based accounting, not a change in the power the venue draws. Also, it's the venue's claim, not yours. | Attribute it to the venue and state the mechanism. Report the location-based figure first. |
