# Larkspur Go Meetup: sustainability plan and what to say

I followed `event-sustainability-plan`, including its three references. I couldn't ask the interview questions, so the gaps are listed as assumptions. I didn't fetch emission factors. The skill forbids typing uncited ones, so every figure below is a placeholder you fill in.

## Verdict

- ❌ **Train-times page:** skip. It only moves people who fly and have a rail option, and nobody here does.
- ❌ **Rail partnership:** skip. It takes weeks of back-and-forth and has no audience to serve.
- ❌ **Offsets:** don't buy them to claim anything. Since 27 Sep 2026 the EU blacklists "neutral/compensated" claims based on offsets. Offsets also reduce nothing.
- ✅ **Publish a footprint:** do this, as a range with its method. Your venue's metro access is already the best travel lever, so say that.
- ✅ **Cheap levers:** a pizza and beer ask to the sponsor, a food-waste weigh-in, and a venue energy question.

## 4. Lever plans

The profile is none of the skill's three. It is the "audience mostly local, arrives by transit" re-rank, where catering and venue energy move up. Travel-side levers have almost nothing to move.

| Plan | What it is | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Measure once, act cheaply (recommended)** | Screening estimate, 2 form fields, 3 asks, one honest page. Fits your 2 hours. | Estimate-grade figures. |
| B. Full inventory every month | Report skeleton filled after each meetup. | Roughly 30–45 min every month (my estimate), too heavy for a volunteer team. |
| C. "The usual stuff" | Train page, rail deal, offsets. | Zero effect on your footprint, and the offset claim is illegal wording in the EU. |

**Plan A, this edition:**
1. **Pizza and beer ask:** a plant-based default with alternatives on request, and order quantities based on your last no-show rate. This is the main food lever.
2. **Food-waste weigh-in:** weigh leftovers with a kitchen scale and donate if the venue and food-safety rules allow it. Publish the kilograms.
3. **Venue energy ask:** the supply contract type (renewable contract or unbundled certificates) and an estimated kWh for your room.
4. **Travel policy, if you pay for any speaker trips:** economy, direct, and rail when the journey is short. Offer a remote slot, never impose one.
5. **Protect the travel advantage:** if you ever change venue, metro access is a hard criterion.

**Next edition:** the commitment is to report the measured result. Don't publish numeric reduction targets, because the EU wants a verified plan behind them.

> - We order from our no-show rate, weigh leftovers and publish the kilograms.
> - We ask the venue for its energy contract and usage.
>
> **What we will change next edition**
> - We will report the measured result here.
> - We will keep metro access as a requirement for any venue.
>
> **How you can help**
> Tell us how you travelled when you RSVP. That is how we measure.
>
> **What we do not claim**
> We do not call this meetup carbon neutral, and we do not buy offsets to claim it.

Wording rules:
- Don't write "eco-friendly", "green" or "carbon neutral".
- Don't add ISO 20121, since no management cycle runs.
- Say "almost everyone" until the form confirms it.
- If you add a contribution later, put it on its own line with a stated rule and a named project, with the words "This does not cancel our emissions." Never put it in the same sentence as the total.
- Translate the page to French, keeping the claim wording.

⚠️ Not legal advice. A free meetup may fall outside the EU consumer rule, but a named sponsor makes it look commercial, so apply the strict rule anyway. France also has its own carbon-neutral advertising rule, which the skill doesn't cover. Have someone qualified check the page before publishing if the sponsor is named in any claim.
