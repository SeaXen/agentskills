**Do this: publish a one-page footprint estimate and a "getting here" line. Skip the train page, the rail partnership and offsets.** Total time is about 1h45.

## Why

- **Train times and rail partnership:** everyone arrives by metro, bike or on foot, so there is no long-distance travel to improve. These items suit conferences with traveling attendees.
- **Offsets:** a meetup footprint is a few tonnes per year, so the money would be tiny and the claim weak. Offsets also invite "carbon neutral" wording, which is a legal and credibility risk. Since 27 Sep 2026 the EU rules on offset-based environmental claims got stricter. Verify the exact wording before you use any neutrality claim.
- **Food is the real footprint:** the pizza and beer, not travel. A report that says so is more useful than the usual stuff.
