Why this mix:
- **Scottish UK-registry units** fit an Edinburgh event. They are domestic, audited, and have a story you can tell.
- **Biochar** is a durable removal. Trees and peat can burn or dry out, so durability is the weak point of the nature-based slice.
- **"Verified" vs "validated":** Woodland Carbon Code and Peatland Code sell both. Validated units are future promises. Buy only verified units, which are already delivered.

Screening checklist for each seller:
1. Registry serial numbers, retired in your name.
2. A third-party rating (BeZero, Sylvera) or an ICVCM Core Carbon Principles label.
3. Matches the Oxford Offsetting Principles: removal over avoidance, with durability disclosed.
4. A written quote and a retirement certificate.

## 4. Cheaper avoidance credits: no

Avoidance credits cost GBP 3 to 10 per tonne. At that price, GBP 8,000 would nominally cover 800 to 2,500 t.

I recommend against them for four reasons:
- Avoidance does not remove a tonne. It claims that a tonne was not emitted somewhere else.
- Quality is the weak spot. Studies of **cookstove** and **REDD+** credits found large over-crediting, and renewable-energy credits are mostly non-additional now.
- A badge claiming neutrality built on these credits is the one most likely to draw criticism or a regulator's attention.
- More tonnes at low quality does not beat fewer tonnes at high quality. Your footprint is about 120 t, so you do not need 800 t.

Cheaper only makes sense in one case: you cannot afford to cover the full footprint. In that case, cover part of it with quality credits and say so.

## 5. Do not put up a "carbon neutral" badge

- **UK:** The CMA's Green Claims Code and the new DMCC Act enforcement powers apply. A flat "carbon neutral" claim must be backed with evidence and caveats.
- **EU:** Directive 2024/825 has applied since 27 September 2026. It bans claims that a product or service is "neutral" based on offsets. It is aimed at consumer marketing, and your attendees are professionals. Still, 15% of them are in the EU, and the direction of regulation is clear.
- **Reputation:** An audience of observability engineers will check your numbers.

Safer wording:

> "We measured our event footprint (about X tCO2e, methodology linked) and funded Y tonnes of verified carbon removal. Here are the retirement certificates."

Publish the methodology page and the certificate links. Keep "neutral" off the page.
