**There is no standard organizer-to-attendee ratio for tech conferences.** Don't put one in the deck as an industry norm. The research behind this skill found no rigorous, generalizable ratio. It has one documented real-world data point.

**The data point:** JSConf EU 2019 ran about 1,600 attendees with 6 core organizers, 8 MCs and 17 volunteers. That is roughly 1 core organizer per 270 attendees. The team's own closing statement said this exceeded "what can be responsibly handled by our volunteer team", and the conference ended after that edition.

**What that gives you for 2,000 attendees:**
- At 1 per 270, you get about **7 core organizers**. Treat that as a floor, not a target, because the team behind that ratio burned out.
- Your 700 → 2,000 jump also moves you toward the point where comparable events add paid operations staff. That point sits at roughly low-thousands of attendees and/or a six-figure budget. The first hire was an event coordinator or manager in every documented case except one, where a larger organization hired a senior executive director.
