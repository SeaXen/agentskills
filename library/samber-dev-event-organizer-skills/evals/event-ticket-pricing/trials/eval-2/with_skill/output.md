## ⚠️ One change to your brief

You asked for four time tiers. The skill's published evidence says no event runs more than two public price points. A third phase works only as a late surcharge, never as another discount step. A stack of discounts teaches buyers to wait for the next one.

- **Super Early Bird is merged into Early Bird.** Both were discounts, so one discount step is enough.
- **Last Chance is kept**, as a surcharge on top of Regular. It is the same mechanic as EuroPython's published late-bird step.
- **Result:** three price points per persona: Early Bird → Regular → Last Chance. This is one more than any sourced event runs. If the sale tracks, Last Chance is the one to cut.

## 5. Bulk and access rungs

- **Group discount:** 10% off for 5 or more tickets in the same order, on the **Regular tier only**. It never stacks on Early Bird or Last Chance, because that would discount the same seat twice. Self-set, one threshold and one percentage.
- **Pay-by-invoice:** at full price, on request, and described as a payment rail, never as a discount. Offer it only if your entity can issue invoices and reconcile payment. Without that capacity, drop this rung.
- **Scholarship (need-gated, reviewed by a human):** unpriced and by application, never a public cheap tier. Publish it only once you have named a reviewer, a reply time, a closing date, and what the applicant receives. These tickets are non-transferable. An unanswered application is worse than no route.
- **Supporter tier:** skip it until a named aid program exists for it to point at.

## 6. Refund and transfer policy (self-set, to publish beside the prices)

Dates are placeholders. The skill's four examples share only a single dated cutoff, so every other line below is my proposal for you to decide.

- **Refund deadline [D3]:** tied to the catering headcount and badge print run, and written as a calendar date. After it, no refund (RustConf's form).
- **Fee:** 15 EUR administrative fee, 10 EUR for Student. Waived, with a later deadline, for visa, health and travel problems.
- **Transfers:** free within the same organization at any time. To another person before [D3], with a fee if the two ticket prices differ, so a Corporate ticket can't become a resold Individual one. Student and scholarship tickets can't be transferred.
- **If the event is cancelled by us:** this needs contract and insurance review. It is not legal advice, so route it to your sponsor-agreement and risk work.
