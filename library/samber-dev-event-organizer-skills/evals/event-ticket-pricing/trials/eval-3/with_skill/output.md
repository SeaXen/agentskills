**Short answer for the logistics company:** no stacking, yes to the invoice, and no extra 5%.

## 1. Group discount on top of early bird: no

- Apply the group discount only to the standard tier (310 EUR), never on top of early bird. Stacking discounts the same seat twice.
- The exclusion is sourced practice: GopherCon applies its group discount to standard tickets only, not to early or late tiers. Your own rule should say this on the page.
- The numbers favor the buyer anyway:

| Option | Per seat | 12 seats |
|---|---|---|
| Early bird (until 12 March) | 240 EUR | 2,880 EUR |
| Standard with 10% group discount (after 12 March) | 279 EUR | 3,348 EUR |

- Tell them that if they order before 12 March, early bird at 240 EUR already beats the group price. After 12 March they get 10% off 310 EUR.

## 2. Pay by purchase order, 30-day invoice: yes, at full price

- Invoicing is a payment rail, not a discount. A company that can't pay by card buys nothing without it, so offering it is worth the handling cost.
- Your nonprofit status and bookkeeper cover the capacity requirement. The invoice terms below are the only part to settle first.
- Settle these with your bookkeeper before you reply:
  - **Invoice issuing.** Confirm they can issue the invoice and reconcile the payment.
  - **VAT.** Confirm how VAT is shown on the invoice. I haven't assumed a treatment.
- Set these terms in the reply:
  - **Price lock.** Is the early-bird price locked on the invoice date or on the payment date? I'd lock it on the invoice date, which is a business decision for you. The 30-day term could otherwise run past 12 March.
  - **Seat release.** Release named tickets on payment, or on a signed PO. Pick one.
  - **Due date.** Make it fall before the event.

## 3. Treasurer's extra 5%: don't offer it

- Corporate buyers already pay the top rung. Discounting the invoice rail gives them a second reduction for a problem that was never about money.
- Their problem is the card limit. The invoice solves it, so the extra 5% isn't needed.
- If the treasurer insists, make it a separate decision about the group rate, not a reward for invoicing.
