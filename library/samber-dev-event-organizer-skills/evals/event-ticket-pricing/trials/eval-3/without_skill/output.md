## Terms to pin down

- **Seat hold:** seats are held at the quoted price once you receive the signed PO. Unpaid seats are released after the due date.
- **Names:** attendee names are due by a set date. Name changes are free.
- **VAT:** ask your bookkeeper about VAT treatment as a nonprofit, and about their VAT number and billing entity.
