## 1. Is there an industry standard?

No. For community technical events there is only one shared practice: a single dated cutoff, after which refunds stop. All four events I have policies for (PyCon US, EuroPython, RustConf, KubeCon NA, all 2026) do this. None uses a sliding scale by days remaining.

Everything else differs between them:

| Term | What the four do |
|---|---|
| Cutoff | From 2 weeks before the event (KubeCon) to about 15 days (RustConf) to a month or more (PyCon US, EuroPython) |
| Fee | $50, or $25 for students (PyCon US). 6% of price (KubeCon). None (EuroPython, RustConf before its cutoff) |
| Hardship | Fee waived for health and visa reasons (PyCon US). Later deadline for visa and travel issues (RustConf). Nothing (EuroPython, KubeCon) |
| Transfers | Free and self-service (KubeCon). Allowed, with a fee if the ticket prices differ (PyCon US). Generally not allowed, case by case (RustConf). Not mentioned (EuroPython) |

So there is nothing to "just apply". Any fee, hardship rule or transfer rule you choose is your own decision, and you should present it that way.

## 3. The policy: decide it now, publish it, and don't apply it retroactively

The 190 existing buyers bought without any terms, so you can't impose a stricter cutoff on them. Write the policy for the event's remaining sales and for future editions.

The checklist below has the decisions you have to make. I've put a suggested default after each one, but they are your choices, not norms.

1. **Refund deadline:** one calendar date, tied to your catering or badge headcount deadline. Not "X days before".
2. **Fee:** flat amount, percentage or none. Suggested: none or small, since none of the sample events charge a graduated fee.
3. **Hardship:** name the reasons that waive the fee or extend the deadline, and say who decides.
4. **Late requests:** refused, or converted into a transfer.
5. **Transfers to another person:** allowed or not. If allowed, state the rule when ticket prices differ.
6. **Transfers within a company:** usually looser.
7. **Hardship and comp tickets:** transferable or not.
8. **Event cancelled by you:** what attendees get back.
9. **Where it's published:** on the registration page next to the price, not only in the confirmation email.

**Publishable wording (fill the brackets):**

> **Refunds and transfers.** Full refunds until [DATE]. After that, no refunds. [A fee of X applies / No fee applies.] [Hardship reason, e.g. health or travel problems: fee waived / deadline extended to DATE; email us.] Tickets may be transferred to another person until [DATE]; write to [contact] with the new attendee's name. [Hardship tickets are not transferable.] If we cancel the event, we refund in full.

Put it on the registration page, repeat it in the confirmation email, and email it to the 190 existing buyers as informational. Don't present it as new terms against them.
