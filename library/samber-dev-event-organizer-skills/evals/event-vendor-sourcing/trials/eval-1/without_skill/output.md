# DeployConf Lisbon: caterer note and selection method

## Before you send (10 min)

1. **Check the date.** If you mean 14 March 2027, that is a Sunday. Ask for weekend surcharges, and confirm each caterer is free that day.
2. **Ask the venue** whether outside caterers are allowed. Also ask about kitchen or prep space, power, a loading dock, and any exclusivity or corkage fee. This can disqualify a caterer on its own.
3. **Fill the `[brackets]`** in the note: times, dietary split, deadline.
4. **Send the same note to all three** on the same day, so the quotes are comparable.

## Budget reality check

- 4,200 EUR ÷ 260 people ≈ **16.15 EUR per head** for one lunch and two coffee breaks.
- A rough split is about 3 EUR per coffee break (two of them) and about 10 EUR for lunch. That is tight but doable for a buffet or finger-food lunch. A plated lunch will not fit.
- Fixed fees (staff, equipment, delivery, service) eat into that quickly, so ask for them as separate lines.
- **Confirm whether 4,200 includes VAT.** If it doesn't, your real ceiling is lower.
- I suggest **not telling them the budget**. Ask for the spec and one lean variant instead (see the note below).

## The note

**Subject:** Quote request – DeployConf Lisbon, 14 March, ~260 guests (reply by [date])

Hello [name],

I'm organising DeployConf Lisbon, a one-day technical conference for software engineers. I'm asking three caterers for quotes and would like yours by **[date, e.g. Friday 24 Oct]**.

**Event**
- Date: 14 March [2027], [start time]–[end time]
- Venue: main auditorium, Instituto Nova Tecnologia, Lisbon
- Guests: about 260 (final number confirmed by [date]; please quote at 230, 260 and 290)

**What we need**
- 2 coffee breaks, around [time] and [time]: coffee, tea, water, juice, and light pastries or fruit
- 1 lunch, around [time], for 260 people, served in about 45–60 minutes. A buffet or finger-food format is fine.
- Dietary needs: expect about [25]% vegetarian, [10]% vegan, plus gluten-free, lactose-free and nut-allergy requests. Please say how you label and separate these.
- No alcohol needed.

**Please include in your quote**
1. A **per-person price** for each of the three services, with a sample menu.
2. **Every other charge as its own line**: staff, equipment and crockery, delivery, set-up and clean-up, service fee, weekend surcharge, disposables.
3. Whether prices are **with or without VAT**. Please give the total with VAT.
4. A **lean option** of roughly **15 EUR per person all-in**, and what you'd cut to get there.
5. How many staff you'd bring, how many service points, and how you'd keep the lunch queue short.
6. What you need from the venue (kitchen, power, water, access), and whether you have worked at Instituto Nova Tecnologia before.
7. **Terms:** deposit and payment schedule, the deadline for the final headcount, how much the headcount can drop, the cancellation policy, and how long the quote stays valid.
8. Two references from events of 150 or more people, and your food-safety certification and liability insurance.
9. Whether a tasting is possible before we sign.

Please reply with a single itemised quote. Call or email me if anything is unclear.

Thank you,
[Name] · [role] · DeployConf Lisbon · [phone] · [email]

## How to pick

### Step 1: pass/fail gates

Drop any caterer that fails one of these:
- Not allowed by the venue, or not free on the date.
- Can't show food-safety certification and insurance.
- Can't serve 260 people in about an hour.
- All-in quote above 4,200 EUR at 260 people, with no lean option that fits.

### Step 2: compare on all-in cost

Build one row per caterer, with **everything included** (fixed fees, VAT, surcharges) at 230, 260 and 290 guests. The per-head price alone is misleading, because a low headcount price with high fixed fees can lose to a higher price with no fees.

### Step 3: score the survivors (100 points)

| Criterion | Weight | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| All-in cost at 260, and at 230/290 | 35 | Fits 4,200 EUR with some margin |
| Menu and dietary handling | 25 | Real vegetarian and vegan dishes, labelled allergens, not an afterthought |
| Reliability and references | 20 | Comparable events, references who answer |
| Service logistics | 15 | Enough staff and service points, short queues, venue-ready |
| Terms and flexibility | 5 | Late headcount changes allowed, fair deposit |

### Step 4: tie-break

If two are within about 5 points, **taste both** and call a reference. Keep the loser warm as a backup until you've signed.

### Red flags

- Vague quotes that bundle everything into one number.
- No reaction to the dietary section.
- Pressure to sign or pay a large deposit before the tasting.
- Fees "to be confirmed".
