**Partly fair. Spend should set how hard you negotiate, not the order you start in.** Order by dependencies and lead time instead, and start the cheap, slow items now.

## Why spend-order is risky

- **Insurance** is the smallest cheque but often gates other things. Venues commonly want a certificate before they sign, and cancellation cover only protects you from the day you buy it. Leaving it last means weeks of exposure and possibly a stalled venue contract.
- **Security** depends on venue rules, floor plan and licensed-staff ratios. Good providers also fill up for popular dates.
- **AV** may be locked to a venue's in-house supplier. If so, your "RFP" is really a quote negotiation.
- **Catering** also depends on the venue (kitchen access, exclusive caterer, curfews). Check this before you collect bids.
- **Shirts** are cheap and late. They need a size census from registrations and about 4-6 weeks of production. Starting early only means locking a design too soon.

## Proposed order

1. **This week, before any supplier call:** read the venue contract for exclusive suppliers, insurance requirements, security rules, load-in times, curfew and kitchen limits. Any of these can void a quote. ⚠️
2. **This week, in parallel:** send the catering RFP (your plan holds here, and it is the biggest cheque). Send the insurance enquiry to a broker, since it is a 30-minute email with a long lead time. Send AV scoping to the venue's in-house supplier plus 2 outside quotes.
3. **After the floor plan is firm:** security quotes.
4. **About 10-12 weeks out:** shirt design, then order after the size census.

## Where spend does decide

- **Catering:** 3+ quotes, per-head price breakdown, dietary and allergen handling, a minimum-guarantee clause and a headcount-adjustment window.
- **AV:** 2-3 quotes, plus a check of what is already included in the venue fee.
- **Security, shirts, insurance:** 1-2 quotes each. Don't over-invest effort on small cheques.

Sending requests costs almost nothing. Your attention is the scarce resource, so spend it where the money is, but start every slow clock now.

## Two questions that change the plan

1. Is the venue already signed? If not, it comes before all of this.
2. Does the venue have exclusive caterer or AV rules?
