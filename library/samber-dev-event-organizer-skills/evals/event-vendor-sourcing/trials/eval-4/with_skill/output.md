# ForgeCon AV contract with Halcyon Stage: what it should say

I read the `event-vendor-sourcing` skill and its three references and followed them. I did not look up Halcyon Stage, so nothing below is a claim about them. I give no prices, because no public benchmark exists for AV day rates at a recorded 400-seat tech conference. Only quotes on one identical brief give you a number you can defend.

## 1. Sign this month only if four things exist first

Four things don't exist yet. Signing before they do is the main risk, not any single clause.

1. **The venue's answer in writing.** Does the venue supply AV, mandate its own supplier, permit an outside one, or charge a fee to allow one? Also ask for load-in and load-out windows, overnight secure storage, power and rigging limits, and the rules for outside deliveries. If the venue mandates AV, Halcyon is moot.
2. **The technical spec.** `event-production` writes what the supplier must deliver, and this contract points at that spec. If Halcyon's site visit produces the package, Halcyon defines the spec. Every later change then becomes a variation they price.
3. **A written brief that a second supplier also answers.** AV is your largest line for a recorded conference, so it earns more than a single referral. The skill's sourced process is to shortlist, then send the full RFP to two finalists on identical scope. Put a budget range in the RFP, because withholding it widens the spread between bids. Halcyon's site visit is fine, but it works from your brief and not theirs.
4. **A reference check.** A friend's recommendation tells you they turned up once. Call one event they recorded and ask what happened to the recordings. Recording failure is the only AV failure you can't see until the event is over.

"This month" is a self-imposed deadline. The skill sets no standard AV lead time, so I won't invent one. What you need to know is your binding date. Is "October" October 2027? If the event is this month or next, tell me and the plan changes.

## 2. What the contract should say

This is a checklist, not legal advice. A commitment this size deserves a real reviewer.

**Scope and deliverables**
- Attach the `event-production` spec unchanged. State that it governs over Halcyon's proposal wherever the two differ.
- List cameras, audio capture and slide or screen capture, with equipment named by model or class.
- State the crew roles and headcount per day, and name the lead engineer.
- Cover all 18 talks across both days.
- List the deliverables per talk: file format, resolution, audio spec, raw versus edited, and the delivery date.
- A backup recording runs simultaneously, on a separate recorder, from the same feed.
- The raw media is handed over on site before the crew leaves, with a copy verified on your own drive.

**Rights and data**
- You own the recordings, or hold a licence you can transfer to speakers and sponsors.
- Halcyon gets no reuse rights, or only rights you grant in writing.
- Speaker consent and releases stay your job. Say so in the contract.
- Add confidentiality over unreleased talk content and attendee or speaker data.

**Price, with nothing hiding outside it**
- Itemise equipment, crew, transport, rehearsal, post-production, deliverables and taxes.
- State the crew window, when it starts, and the named overtime rate.
- State what is excluded, by name.
- Fix a not-to-exceed total. Any change needs written approval before it is priced.

**Rehearsal**
- A tech check or rehearsal is in the quote, with a date.
- The contract says who from Halcyon attends.

**Remedy for failure** (the clause most supplier drafts omit)
- Late or missing crew or equipment: a replacement within a stated time, plus a credit against the invoice.
- Lost or unusable recording: a per-talk refund. A re-record is impossible, so money is the only remedy. The cap on liability should not sit below the fee.
- No-show: refund of deposit plus a stated contribution to the cost of a replacement supplier.
- Check these terms against the cost of failure. If the remedy is worth plainly less than 18 lost talks, that is the finding. It also makes the reference call worth making.

**Substitution**
- No equipment downgrade and no change to the named lead without your consent, except for an equivalent or better replacement.

**Payment**
- Stage payments against milestones: spec sign-off, rehearsal, delivery of verified files.
- Hold the final balance through an acceptance period in which you can check the files.
- State the deposit amount, due date and refundability.

**Cancellation, both directions**
- Yours: write out the schedule of what you owe as the date approaches, not "fees may apply".
- Theirs: what happens if Halcyon cancels. This is usually much weaker, so name it.
- Force majeure: what happens to your money if neither side can perform.

**Insurance**
- Halcyon provides a certificate of insurance before move-in and names you, and the venue if required, as additional insured.
- Get the required limits from the venue contract, not from me. They flow down to suppliers.
- At 400 seats over two days, expect the venue to ask for more than a small meetup would. Confirm with `event-risk-management` and the venue.
- Liability and indemnity should be mutual. Their first draft will favour them.

**Venue rules and logistics**
- Halcyon complies with the venue's rules: load-in and load-out windows, fire code, power, rigging, and any in-house crew or union requirement.
- Give an exact delivery location, an arrival window, a named primary contact on each side, and a second phone number.
- Equipment cases and staging have an agreed pickup time and a named responsible person.

**Site visit and quote**
- Say whether the visit is free or credited against the fee.
- Fix the validity date of the quote and the price hold.

**Credentials (optional)**
- Ask whether the crew hold CTS or the local equivalent. The skill labels this a working default, not established practice.

1. Which October, and is the venue contract signed?
2. Has the venue said, in writing, whether it includes AV or permits an outside supplier?

4. Are you willing to get one second AV quote on the same brief?
