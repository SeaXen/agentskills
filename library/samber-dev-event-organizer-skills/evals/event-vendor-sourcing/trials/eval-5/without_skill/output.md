# Kernelcamp supplier ballparks (350 people, 2 days, Kraków)

All figures are my estimates from general knowledge of Polish event pricing. I didn't check live quotes. Treat them as ±30%. Rates are excl. VAT (23% in Poland, mostly recoverable if the entity is VAT-registered). Conversion used: €1 ≈ 4.25 PLN.

## Working numbers

| Line | Low | Working | High | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lunch (buffet or boxed) | €22 | **€32** | €45 | per head per meal |
| Coffee break (coffee, pastries, fruit) | €6 | **€9** | €13 | per head per break |
| Breakfast, if offered | €10 | **€15** | €22 | per head |
| Evening reception or dinner, if offered | €40 | **€60** | €90 | per head |
| AV crew only (4–6 techs) | €1,000 | **€1,600** | €2,500 | per day |
| AV crew plus gear (stage, sound, LED or projection, cameras, stream) | €4,500 | **€8,000** | €14,000 | per day |
| Security guard (licensed, via agency) | €10 | **€13** | €17 | per guard-hour |
| Event liability insurance (OC) | €500 | **€1,200** | €2,500 | whole event |
| Add cancellation cover | €1,500 | **€3,000** | €6,000 | whole event |

## Notes per line

1. **Catering**
   - Budget on about 90% of registrations as guaranteed headcount, but add 5–10% for speakers, sponsors, and staff.
   - Dietary needs (vegan, gluten-free, halal) usually run 15–25% of a tech crowd. They rarely cost extra but must be in the contract.
   - Venue in-house catering is often mandatory and 10–20% above independent caterers. Check this before you compare quotes.
   - A service charge of 10–15% and a per-head minimum are common.

2. **AV**
   - The gap between crew-only and full package is the gear. If the venue has built-in AV, you may only pay for the crew.
   - Add a setup day at 50–100% of a full day rate. Budget 3 billable days for a 2-day event.
   - Extras that add up fast: livestream or recording (+€1.5–4k/day), second room (+€2–5k/day), translation booths.
   - Day rates assume 10–12 hour days. Overtime is usually 1.5×.

3. **Security**
   - Agencies bill minimum shifts, typically 4–8 hours, and charge more for nights and public holidays.
   - Rough staffing for 350 people: 6–8 guards in peak hours, 2–3 for setup, teardown, and overnight if the venue is not locked.
   - Example: 8 guards × 10 h × 2 days = 160 h ≈ €2,100. With overnight cover, about €3–4k.
   - Some venues require their own security. Ask before booking an outside agency.

4. **Insurance**
   - Liability premiums scale with attendance, venue, and any alcohol or physical activities. 350 people at a conference venue is a low-risk class.
   - Many Kraków venues require proof of OC with a minimum coverage, often PLN 1–5M. Ask for their minimum.
   - Cancellation cover is a separate product, roughly 1–3% of insured costs. It is optional, but the funders may ask about it.

## Sample total at working numbers

| Line | Calculation | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Catering | (€32 lunch + 2 × €9 breaks) × 350 × 2 days | ~€35,000 |
| AV | 3 days × €8,000 | ~€24,000 |
| Security | 160 h plus setup and overnight | ~€3,500 |
| Insurance | OC plus cancellation | ~€4,200 |
| **Total** | | **~€67,000** |

That is about €190 per attendee on these four lines alone. A plausible range is €45k to €100k.

## Next steps

1. Get 3 quotes each for catering and AV once you have a shortlisted venue, because venue rules decide a lot of this.
2. Ask the venue three questions before anything else: mandatory caterer, mandatory AV, mandatory security.
3. Decide now whether you offer breakfast and an evening event. Together they can add €25–30k.
4. Tell the funders the ±30% margin up front and add a 10–15% contingency line.
