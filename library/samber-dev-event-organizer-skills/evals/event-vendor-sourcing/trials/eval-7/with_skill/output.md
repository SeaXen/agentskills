## What it leaves out

The template covers insurance but misses the terms that decide whether a lunch arrives right:

- **Scope in units.** What she delivers, how many covers, and at what time.
- **Headcount change.** Until when, in which direction, and at what cost. Monthly meetup numbers move, so this matters most.
- **Cancellation, both directions.** What you owe her, and what happens if she cancels.
- **Late or short delivery.** The actual remedy. Most small-supplier contracts say nothing here, and the skill treats that absence as the finding.
- **Substitution.** What she may swap without telling you.
- **Deposit.** Whether you need one at all, given a year of track record.
- **Delivery logistics.** The exact drop location, a named on-site contact plus a backup number, and who collects any returnable trays.
- **Who serves and who sets up.** Decide this before the day.
- **Venue rules.** Load-in window and any restrictions on outside food.
