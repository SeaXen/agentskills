**Not as is.** Don't send it yet. The template is written for a corporate vendor, not for a two-person shop that has fed you well for a year.

## What's wrong

1. **$2M is probably too high for Marta.** Small food businesses commonly carry about $1M per incident and $2M aggregate. If the template says "$2M" without saying which, clarify. Raising her limit could mean a premium increase she'd have to absorb on a sandwich order for 60 people.
2. **"Additional insured" is the right idea but needs an endorsement.** A certificate alone doesn't grant it. Some policies include it automatically (a "blanket" endorsement), and others charge extra or refuse. Ask her insurer before you require it.
3. **"30 days before move-in" doesn't fit.** That's construction or long-lease wording. For a monthly recurring order, "before the first delivery, then once a year at renewal" works better. Marta may already have a valid certificate, since she's been supplying you for a year.
4. **You may be asking for the wrong thing.** The co-working space is your host and probably has its own vendor or event insurance rules. Ask them what they require, and whether they want to be the additional insured instead of you or alongside you. Match their requirement and don't invent your own.
5. **Jurisdiction matters.** I don't know where you are. "Additional insured" and "certificate of insurance" are US-style terms. In France, for example, the equivalent is an *attestation d'assurance RC pro*, and additional-insured endorsements are uncommon.

## What's missing

For food, a few things matter more than the liability limit:

- Proof of food-business registration and hygiene compliance, as required locally.
- Allergen labeling for your attendees.
- Who is responsible if someone gets sick.

## What I'd do

1. Ask the co-working space what vendor insurance they require.
2. Ask Marta what she currently carries. A short, friendly message is enough:
   > "Hi Marta, our host asked us to collect proof of insurance from anyone serving food at the meetup. Could you send me a copy of your liability certificate? If you'd like to ask your insurer about listing us as additional insured, tell me what it would cost and we'll sort it out."
3. If you still want a written agreement, cut it to one page: what she delivers, price, cancellation terms, insurance certificate on file, allergen handling.
