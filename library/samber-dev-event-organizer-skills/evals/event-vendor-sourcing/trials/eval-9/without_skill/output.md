## Six-week schedule (6h each)

1. **Week 1:** Venue answers and contract read. Write one shared brief per category: 300 people, one day, date, venue spec, dietary and AV needs, and the same line items every supplier must price. Send catering and AV briefs.

3. **Week 3:** Calls and site questions. Build one comparison sheet per category: price, what's included, exclusions, cancellation terms, references.
4. **Week 4:** Decide catering and AV. Book tastings or a tech walkthrough only for the finalist.

6. **Week 6:** Submit the supplier list by 30 Sept, a few days early. Keep a backup supplier for catering and AV.

## Rules to protect your hours

- **One brief per category, same for everyone.** Otherwise you can't compare prices.
- **Set a reply deadline of 7 days.** Drop the quotes that miss it.
- **Save one comparison sheet per category.** That is your proof of value.
- **Delegate.** Ask one volunteer or a past organizer to chase quotes or do the shirts. Even 2 hours a week helps.
- **Prefer suppliers the venue already works with.** They know the building and often cost less.
