# Venue line for the Meridian Systems Day sponsorship deck

These are planning estimates from my general knowledge of the Dutch conference market. I did not pull live quotes, so check them against 2–3 real Rotterdam quotes before the deck goes out.

## Paste-ready deck line

> **Venue and catering: €35,000–€55,000 for two days, 180 attendees (about €195–€305 per attendee).**
> This covers the main hall, breakout rooms, AV and technical crew, and two days of catering (coffee breaks, lunch, drinks).

## Numbers behind it

| Item | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Venue hire, 2 days | €10,000–€25,000 | Main room for 180, 2–3 breakout rooms, foyer for sponsor booths. Converted industrial spaces sit at the low end. Congress centres sit at the high end. |
| AV and technical crew | €3,000–€8,000 | Often quoted separately: stage, mics, projection, streaming, house technician. |
| Catering per head | **€65–€95 per person per day** | Delegate-day-rate style package: coffee breaks, lunch, afternoon snack, soft drinks. |
| Catering, 2 days × 180 | **€23,000–€34,000** | About €130–€190 per head for the two days. |
| Evening dinner or party (if any) | €45–€90 per head | Not in the figures above. |

- **Total:** venue, AV and catering come to roughly €36,000–€67,000, which I rounded to €35,000–€55,000 for the deck. The upper end assumes a central congress centre, so state a ceiling only if you have one in mind.
- **VAT:** quotes are usually excl. VAT. Dutch VAT is 21% on room hire and AV, and 9% on food. Make sure the deck says whether the line includes VAT.
- **Per-head catering for the deck:** say **"€65–€95 per person per day"**. Quote a daily rate, because it is how venues price and how sponsors will compare it.

## Expected discount from the first quote

- **Typical:** 5–15% off the room hire and AV lines.
- **Catering:** 0–5%. Margins are thin and tied to guest counts, so venues move less here.
- **April and May are peak season** for Dutch conferences, and Tuesday to Thursday dates are the busiest. Expect the lower half of that range, around 5–10%, unless you shift to a Monday or Friday or commit early.
- **Better than a headline percentage:** ask for value-adds, such as a free breakout room, AV included, a lower minimum-guest guarantee with an attrition window, or free sponsor-booth space.
- **Leverage:** a community conference with a track record, an early commitment and a competing quote give you the most room.

## Deck tips

- Show the venue as a range, not the first quote. Sponsors will assume you negotiated.
- Present the per-head figure as a daily rate next to the venue line.
- If you want a safer headline, use the midpoint, about €45,000. Keep the range in the footnote.
