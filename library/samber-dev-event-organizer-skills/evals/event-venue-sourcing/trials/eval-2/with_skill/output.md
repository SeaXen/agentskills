**Short answer: I can't give you bandwidth or camera numbers, and you shouldn't send any.** The venue-sourcing skill rules out inventing them. A made-up figure can be wrong in either direction. Too low, and the centre confirms something that fails on the day. Too high, and they say no or add a fee. The venue has to state in writing what it can guarantee, and your AV supplier then checks that number against the stream. So the reply to the centre is a **short fact sheet plus written questions**.

⚠️ **Before you send:** if the contract isn't signed yet, this email is your leverage. Ask for the answers before you sign. A reply that says "we can't guarantee that" is only useful while you can still change venue or date. If it's already signed, send it anyway, and expect to adapt around whatever they answer.

## Draft reply to the conference centre

**Subject: Northwind Data Summit – technical requirements (written answers requested by [date])**

Hello [name],

Thank you for asking. We run Northwind Data Summit as a hybrid event. Below is what we are planning, followed by the questions we need you to answer in writing. We are not prescribing equipment. We want to know what you can guarantee.

**Our plan**
- 250 on-site attendees, plus a remote audience that watches live.
- Rooms streamed live: [main room / which others]
- Remote audience size expected: [your estimate and its source]
- Remote audience can ask questions live: [yes / no]
- Dates and setup access: [event dates], team access from [time] the day before.
- We may bring our own AV supplier: [yes / not decided].

**Attendee internet**
1. What is the usable WiFi capacity in each room we'd use, and can it be raised?
2. Please plan for up to about 4 devices per attendee, around 1,000 devices. That is an upper bound borrowed from hackathon rules of thumb, and our real figure is likely lower. Can you support it?
3. How many power outlets are available at attendee seating?

**Streaming connection (separate from attendee WiFi)**
4. What upstream bandwidth can you guarantee to the room we stream from? Please give a guaranteed number, not best-effort or shared. Is a dedicated or reserved line available, and at what cost?
5. Is there a wired connection at the production position, or only wireless?
6. Is there a second, independent path to the internet, such as another provider or cellular fallback?
7. Who administers the network on the day, and are they reachable during our hours?
8. Are any ports, protocols or destinations blocked by default? Who lifts the blocks?

**Room, cameras and rigging**
9. What is already installed in each room: projection, screens, sound system, mixing desk, microphones, lectern, confidence monitor?
10. Where are the rigging points, what load do they take, and who may rig: us, you, or a nominated contractor only?
11. Can cameras go at the back and side of each room without blocking a fire route? Can cables be taped across those routes?
12. Can we take an audio feed from your sound system, and in what form?
13. What stage lighting exists, and can it be controlled separately from the house lights?

**Space, access and power**
14. Is there a space in or next to each room for a production position or control room?
15. What are the load-in times, access route, lift dimensions and storage between days?
16. What power is available at the stage and the production position, and on how many circuits?
17. Is a house technician included? For which hours, and responsible for what?

**Permissions and costs**
18. Are there any restrictions or fees on recording or streaming here, such as a house AV exclusivity?
19. May we bring our own AV supplier, or must we use yours?
20. Are any of the items above charged separately? Please list them.

Please answer each numbered point individually. A "yes, no problem" answer won't help us. We'd like to schedule a technical walkthrough with your AV lead and ours before [date].

Thank you,
[name, organization]

## What to fill in before Friday

The placeholders are facts only you can supply. Each one comes from a decision that sits outside venue sourcing:
- **Which rooms stream.** This is the capture posture per room, from `event-production`.
- **Remote audience size and whether it's two-way.** These come from `event-format-selection`. Two-way interaction raises the connection requirements more than anything else.
- **Own supplier or venue's.** Question 19 decides who writes the supplier brief, so ask it early.
