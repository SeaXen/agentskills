# Recommendation: don't sign it as proposed. Counter-offer this week.

The block is probably about twice what you can fill, and the 80% guarantee turns that gap into a bill. Taking a block is reasonable, but only a smaller, shaped one with softer terms.

## ⚠️ Check the dates first

2-3 October is a Thursday and Friday in 2025, not in 2026. Today is 2026-10-15. Confirm the year before anything else, because the whole analysis assumes the event is still ahead.

## Why 60 rooms across 3 nights is too much

Assumptions (replace them with your own data):

| Step | Number | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Attendees | 320 | given |
| Out of town | about 107 | one third |
| Need a hotel, not staying with friends or family | about 90 | 85% |
| Book at *this* hotel | about 55-65 | 60-70% capture if the rate is competitive |

Room-nights by night:

| Night | Realistic rooms | Block |
|---|---|---|
| Wed | 15-25 | 60 |
| Thu | 50-60 | 60 |
| Fri | 8-15 | 60 |
| **Total** | **about 75-100** | **180** |

- The block is 180 room-nights, and 80% of that is **144 you must pay for**.
- My estimate is 75-100 room-nights, so the shortfall is about 45-70.
- At an assumed 180 per night, that is roughly **8,000-12,000 of attrition fees**. Use the real rate.
- Only Thursday night fits the block. Wednesday is for early arrivals and speakers. Friday is for people who skip the trip home after a Friday finish.
- Attendees are not forced to book through you. If the group rate isn't clearly better than the public rate, your pickup falls further.

## Counter-proposal

1. **Shape the block:** Wed 20, Thu 55, Fri 10, about 85 room-nights.
2. **Soften the guarantee:** ask for 70% or lower. Say you want 60-70% of the block, counted *cumulatively* across the three nights, not night by night.
3. **Add a no-penalty walk-down:** release up to 20% of rooms without charge until 30-45 days before arrival.
4. **Credit attrition against other spend:** let any shortfall fee offset your F&B or meeting-space spend instead of being cash on top.
5. **Count every booking toward pickup:** staff, speakers, and anyone who books through the group code, including by phone or an online agent.
6. **Ask for the meeting-space price without the block.** You need this number to know what the block is really buying you.

If they refuse everything, there are two other options:

- **Courtesy block:** a no-attrition hold at the group rate, with the meeting space priced separately.
- **Other venues:** a conference centre or university space with no room-block requirement. Get one competing quote before the deadline, even if you expect to stay.

## Decision rule

Sign only if both are true:

- **Worst-case attrition fee** = (guaranteed room-nights − your pessimistic pickup) × rate
- That fee is **less than the meeting-space discount** from taking the block.

If the fee is higher, you are paying extra for a "discount".
