Get it in writing this week. A verbal OK from an office manager is not a venue commitment.

**Do first (about 20 min):**
1. Email your co-organizer to ask for a written confirmation. Ask them to get it from someone with authority over the space, such as facilities, the site lead, or whoever owns the lease. The office manager may not have that authority. The email needs date, hours, floor, and capacity.

**Then the rest, ranked:**

1. **Fire and occupancy capacity (blocker).**
   - Check that the third floor is legally rated for 140 people plus staff, caterers and speakers.
   - Offices are often rated well below that.
   - If it isn't, you need a lower headcount or a different space.
2. **Insurance (blocker).**
   - Castellan's landlord or insurer may require event liability cover.
   - Many landlords require a certificate naming them as additional insured.
   - Expect roughly a few hundred euros for a one-day policy. Confirm the figure with a quote.
3. **Building access and security (blocker).**
   - 140 unknown visitors need badges or an escorted list, and probably a name list given to reception ahead of time.
   - Check the lift capacity and whether a single entrance would cause a queue.
   - Plan for the hours the building is open, including setup and teardown.
   - Confirm the rest of the building, and the other tenants, are not affected.
4. **Why "free" and who pays what.** Ask directly: is there any cost for cleaning, security staff, after-hours HVAC, or the AV room? Hidden costs often replace the venue line. Also ask whether Castellan wants anything in return, such as logo, a talk slot or recruiting presence. Settle that now so it doesn't change your event's neutrality later.
5. **Facilities check (visit in person).**
   - Toilets for 140
   - Wi-Fi capacity (guest network, not staff)
   - Projector, screen and sound
   - Power
   - Accessibility (step-free, accessible toilets)
   - Space for catering
   - Cloakroom
   - Whether you may bring in food and alcohol
6. **Date hold.** Get the exact date blocked in their shared calendar. Ask what happens if they need the floor back. Agree a cut-off date, and keep a backup venue shortlisted until the written confirmation arrives.
7. **Sponsor and neutrality optics.** If other sponsors exist, or Castellan competes with them, decide how the venue is credited.

**Budget:** don't delete the venue line yet. Move it to a small contingency of about 10 to 15% of the old amount, until items 1 to 4 are confirmed.
