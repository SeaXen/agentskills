**Short answer:** it's not as good a deal as it sounds. The walkthrough comes after you sign, so it can't inform the decision. You'd lock the date and commit before you've seen the space.

**Do this first (today, you have until Friday):**
1. Ask for a site visit or video walkthrough before Friday. If they refuse, that tells you something.
2. If they can't do that, ask for a free or refundable hold for 1-2 weeks. Venues often grant this.
3. Ask for a 48-72 hour extension. A Friday deadline set tomorrow (if Friday is 16 Oct) is a pressure tactic until proven otherwise.
4. Ask for the full contract and floor plans now, then read them before the walkthrough.

**What to check in the contract**
- **Cancellation and deposit:** How much do you pay now, is it refundable, and what's the schedule of penalties as the date nears? The key question is whether you can walk away after the January walkthrough if the space doesn't fit, and what that costs.
- **Attrition and minimum spend:** Do you owe a minimum on catering, bar or room hire, even if attendance falls short?
- **Force majeure and venue default:** What happens if you or they must cancel or postpone? Is there a refund, a re-date, or a credit?
- **Dates and access:** The dates are 12-13 March (Fri-Sat). Do they include setup and teardown time? What are the load-in and load-out hours, and are there noise or curfew limits?
- **What's included:** Rooms, capacity per room (check the standing, theatre and banquet numbers), AV, Wi-Fi bandwidth, furniture, staff, security and cleaning. Get each item in writing with its price.
- **Exclusivity:** Is catering, AV or sponsor activation limited to their in-house suppliers? Is there a corkage or outside-vendor fee?
- **Right to change space:** Can they move you to a different room or hall, or double-book neighbouring areas? Ask for the specific spaces to be named in the contract.
- **Price escalation:** Are rates fixed, or can they rise (service charge, tax, utilities, overtime)?
- **Insurance and liability:** Do you need event liability cover? What are you responsible for if there's damage?
- **Accessibility and logistics:** Step-free access, lifts, toilets, parking, nearby transport, deliveries, and signage rules.
- **Branding and sponsors:** Can you hang banners, place sponsor booths or sell tickets on site? Are there any restrictions?
- **Payment schedule and who signs:** Who is the contracting party, and does it match your organisation's legal entity?

**Questions for them**
- Who else holds or has asked about those dates?
- Will the walkthrough let us change the layout, or is the layout fixed by the contract?
- What's the latest date we can reduce room count or headcount without penalty?

**Before you sign**
- Check you have budget and authority to commit.
- Confirm the date works for key speakers, partners and other events that weekend.
- Have someone with contract experience read the cancellation and liability clauses.
- Get any verbal promises, like "the walkthrough will be free" or "we'll include X", written into the contract.

**My view:** sign only if the contract lets you cancel at low cost after the January walkthrough, or if you've already seen the space. Otherwise push for a hold or a short extension first. A venue that wants your business will usually agree.
