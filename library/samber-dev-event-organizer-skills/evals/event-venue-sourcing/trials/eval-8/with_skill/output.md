❌ **No. Don't send it Monday until you've read the contract.** Read it today or tomorrow, not "next week".

**Why**

- The skill lists "quoting sponsors before reading the venue's insurance requirement" as a named failure mode.
- Venue insurance is often a condition of use, often with the venue named as additional insured. What the venue requires of you shapes what you can require of sponsors.
- The contracts person already mentioned insurance. That makes it a known unknown, not a hypothetical one.
- The prospectus goes to 40 companies and can't be recalled. Any tier, perk, or sponsor obligation that conflicts with the contract becomes a retraction or a renegotiation in front of all 40.

**Reading time:** about 30–60 minutes if you target the clauses below. That fits before Monday morning.

**Read these clauses first, in this order**

1. **Insurance.**
   - Which coverages and amounts does the university require of you?
   - Is the university named as additional insured?
   - Must sponsors and exhibitors carry their own cover?
   - Write down the exact wording and amounts.
2. **Sponsor and exhibitor restrictions.**
   - A university is a hosted space, so it substitutes restrictions for fees.
   - Look for limits on which companies or categories may sponsor, on branding, on exclusivity, and on selling or promoting on campus.
   - This directly affects what your five tiers can promise. Check it before selling any category.
3. **Cancellation-fee schedule.**
   - Capture the dates and percentages verbatim.
   - Hand them to `event-risk-management`.
   - It matters if sponsors withdraw after a scope cut.
4. **Access and setup windows.**
   - Is setup-day access guaranteed?
   - Do shipping and receiving rules cover sponsor equipment, and is there secure storage?
   - Any tier that includes a booth or shipped hardware depends on these.
5. **Catering and separately charged staff.**
   - Check for a mandated caterer, outside-food rules, and charges for security or janitorial staff.
   - These affect what tiers can include, such as sponsored coffee or meals.
6. **Overnight permission.** Check this only if the event runs overnight. Ask separately about working and sleeping.
7. **Force majeure.** Send this clause to a lawyer. Don't self-assess it.

**Decision rule after reading**

- ✅ **No conflict:** send Monday as planned. Add the insurance line to the sponsor terms if the university requires sponsor cover.
- ⚠️ **Insurance applies to sponsors, or a category restriction exists:** edit the affected tiers and terms first, then send. Edit the prospectus, don't delay it, unless the changes are large.
- ❌ **Clause you can't interpret:** hold the send and get the clause answered in writing by the contracts person or a lawyer. Ask them: "What insurance is required of us and of sponsors, and are there restrictions on who may sponsor?"
