# Script to get about 20% off the Quayside Centre quote

**Next action:** Email 1 today. Email forces you to put the full case in writing, and that favors you. Because replies take about 3 days, **every email must stand alone, make one clear ask, and give a reply-by date**. Plan for 2 to 3 weeks.

Fill the `[brackets]` before sending. Don't mention a competing venue unless you really have one.

## Prep (30 min, before email 1)

1. Get the quote **itemized**: room hire, AV, F&B per head, staffing, Wi-Fi, and any service charge.
2. Find your levers:
   - Date flexibility, especially off-peak or midweek.
   - Deposit timing (paying earlier helps them).
   - A multi-year or repeat booking.
   - A big F&B spend.
   - Bundling an overnight or hotel block.
3. Get one or two competing quotes, even rough ones. This is your strongest lever.
4. Set your numbers:
   - Open by asking for about 28% off.
   - Settle at 20% or more.
   - Know your walk-away: `[X]`.

## Email 1: Anchor and unbundle (day 0)

**Subject:** Quote [ref] – [Event name], 220 guests, [date]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for the quote for [Event], [date], 220 delegates. We'd like to hold Quayside Centre as our first choice.
>
> We are comparing it against [one or two] other venues, and the total is currently [X]% above our budget of [£/€ amount]. To close that gap, could you send:
>
> 1. An itemized breakdown of the quote.
> 2. A revised proposal at [28% below quote total], or your best offer in that range.
>
> For context: we can [commit to / pay a deposit by / be flexible on dates within ___ ], and we're open to [multi-year or repeat booking, if true].
>
> We'd like to decide by [date, about 10 days out]. Could you reply by [date, 3 days out] so we can plan around it?
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]

**Why:**
- It gives them a reason to discount (competition and budget).
- It names a number, which anchors the talks.
- It offers trades.
- It sets a deadline.

## Email 2: Trade, don't beg (day 3 to 4, after their reply)

**If they offer 5 to 10%:**

> Thanks, [Name]. That's a step in the right direction, but it doesn't close the gap. We'd need to be around [20% off total].
>
> We can offer [specific trade, e.g. a signed contract and 30% deposit within 7 days / a shift to Tuesday / a commitment to our 2027 event]. Could you check whether that gets us to [target number]?
>
> If the room hire is fixed, we're happy to look at AV, staffing, and F&B instead. Which line items have the most flexibility?

**If they refuse a discount:** ask for value instead.

> Understood on the headline rate. Could you add [free AV package / extra breakout room / reduced F&B minimum / waived service charge / free setup day]? Together those would bridge most of the gap for us.

**If they don't answer a question:** repeat it as a single numbered question at the top of your next email. Never stack open questions.

## Email 3: Escalate (day 7 to 8, if stuck)

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'd like to keep this simple. Our maximum for Quayside Centre is [number, about 20% below the quote], with [your trades]. If you can approve that, we can sign this week.
>
> If it needs someone senior to approve, could you pass this to them and let me know who decides? I'm happy to write to them directly.
>
> We need an answer by [date] to hold our other option.

## Email 4: Close or walk (final)

**If they hit about 20%:**

> Great, thank you. Please send the revised contract showing [total], [included items], and [payment terms]. We'll return it signed by [date].

**If they land at 12 to 15%:** ask for the gap in extras, not cash.

> We're close. If you can add [item worth the remaining 5%], we'll accept and sign by [date].

**If they won't move past your walk-away:**

> Thank you for your time. We've decided to proceed with another venue. If your situation changes, we'd welcome a revised offer before [date].

Send this one for real. A polite walk-away often produces the best offer within a day or two.

## Rules for the whole thread

- **Always set a reply-by date**, and keep it 3 or more days out. It stops a silent 3-day stall.
- **Ask for one thing per email.** Bundled asks get half-answered.
- **Never accept their first counter.** Ask "is that your best?" in the same email, so you don't lose another 3 days.
- **Put every concession in writing.** Get the final deal in the contract, not just in the email chain.
- **Give a concession only in exchange for one.** Say "if you do X, we do Y."
- **Move on dates:** if they go silent past your deadline, send a one-line nudge ("Following up on [ref], we need an answer by [date]"), then move on to the competing venue.
- **Don't bluff** about a competing quote or a budget cap you can't back up. Venues know the market.

## Realistic outcome

- 20% off headline price is ambitious. Expect 8 to 12% cash off, plus extras (AV, F&B, staffing, setup day) worth another 8 to 12%.
- The best leverage, in order: a competing quote, a flexible date, and a quick deposit.
