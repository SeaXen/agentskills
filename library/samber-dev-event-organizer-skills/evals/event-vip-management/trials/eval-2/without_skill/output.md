## The core rule

Keep her visit off every public surface, and keep the circle of people who know small. A leak is far more likely from a volunteer chat, a spreadsheet or a calendar export than from the website.

Three tiers:

| Tier | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Nothing about her visit. No "special guest", no "TBC", no gap in the programme | Everyone |
| Need-to-know | Visit exists, arrival 08:40, the three teams, route | You, venue manager, floor lead, safety lead. Max 4-5 people |
| Restricted | Departure time 09:35 | You and the floor lead only |

Treat arrival time and route as sensitive too. The cabinet only named departure, but ask them (Step 1).

### Step 6: the three teams

- Prepare one 2-minute pitch per team and one named contact person per team.
