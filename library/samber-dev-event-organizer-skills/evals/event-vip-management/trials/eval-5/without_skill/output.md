4. **Seat them with no marking.**
   - Keep your normal seating. Unmarked seats on an aisle in the first third of the hall give easy access and a good view.

## Rules for the day

- **Same rules as everyone.** They queue for coffee and sit on the same chairs.
- **Quiet hospitality is fine.** Water, a quiet corner to take a call, and a reliable host cost nothing in hierarchy.
- **Check your current practice.** If speakers, sponsors, or volunteers already have a distinct badge, lounge, or seats, give the guests the same standard as the closest existing category. Do not invent a new tier.
- **No stage mention** unless the run of show already thanks funders or partners. Thank them in the closing by name only if you already do that for others. Ask the guests first.
- **Funders talk to people.** Plan one unscripted 20-minute conversation with a couple of organizers or attendees. The trustees see the community, which is your pitch.
