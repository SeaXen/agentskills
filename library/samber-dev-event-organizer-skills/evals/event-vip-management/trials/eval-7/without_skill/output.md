# Guest process

Version 1.0, written 2026-10-15. Owner: the guest-list lead (named in §3). Review once a year, in May.

## 1. What a "guest" is

A guest is someone we invite for who they are, not for what they bought or present. They need hosting beyond a normal ticket.

- Examples: grant officers, city or regional officials, partner-organization leads, funders' representatives.
- Not guests: speakers, sponsors, volunteers, press. They have their own lists.
- Expected volume: 4–5 a year, so the list stays small.

## 2. Who goes on the list

A person qualifies if at least one of these holds:

1. They fund us, or decide on funding, now or in the next 12 months.
2. They hold a public role that gives us permission to operate, such as venue, city, or region.
3. They are a peer organization lead where a visit builds a real relationship.

Rules:

- One organizer proposes. A second approves. Record both.
- Every guest needs a named host, an organizer who greets them and stays their contact.
- Do not add someone only because they are senior.
- Funding discussions live in the fundraising tracker, not here. The guest list only says "funder" or "official."

## 3. Who sees what

| Role | Sees | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Guest-list lead | Full sheet | 1, plus 1 deputy |
| Hosts | Only their own guests' rows | 1 per guest |
| Rest of the orga team (23) | Day-of run sheet only (§5) | everyone else |

- Today the full sheet reaches all 23 people. Cut that down.
- Name the lead and the deputy in the sheet's first tab.

## 4. What we record

| Field | Content | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Full name | Welcome, badge |
| Organization and role | As on their public profile | Greeting, signage |
| Category | Funder / Official / Peer | Replaces "Why they matter" |
| Reason invited | One factual sentence | Audit trail |
| Host | Organizer's name | Accountability |
| Invited by / approved by | Two names | Process check |
| Status | Proposed / invited / confirmed / declined / attended | Tracking |
| Welcome level | Host greets / lead organizer greets / desk | Replaces "Seniority" |
| Logistics | Arrival time, parking, accessibility needs, driver or escort | Day-of |
| Gift or hospitality offered | What and its estimated value | Compliance (§7) |
| Follow-up | Thank-you sent, next step, owner | Closes the loop |

Write every cell as if the guest might read it.

- Cut subjective notes such as "tough negotiator" or "cares about X." Those are opinions about a named person.
- If someone holds a view we need to remember, store it in the fundraising tracker, in factual form, with its own access limits.
- Collect dietary or health information only if the guest volunteers it. Record it as "requirement: X," never the reason.
- Record the driver as "driver, 1 vehicle." Add their name only if site access requires it.

## 5. Where it lives

1. **Master sheet:** one Google Sheet in a restricted folder. Sharing is off for "anyone in the org." Access is the lead, the deputy, and the hosts for their rows. Use a filtered view or a separate tab per host.
2. **Run sheet:** a separate sheet for event day, built a week before. It holds name, organization, welcome level, arrival time, host and host phone, and the logistics line. Share it with the whole team. Do not copy the master's other columns into it.
3. **Naming:** `guests-YYYY-event` for the master, `runsheet-YYYY-event` for the run sheet. Never duplicate the master as "copy of."

## 6. Calendar

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| T–10 weeks | Lead opens the new master from the blank template, never from last year's copy |
| T–8 weeks | Propose, approve, assign hosts |
| T–6 weeks | Send invitations |
| T–2 weeks | Collect logistics and check gift rules (§7) |
| T–1 week | Build the run sheet and brief the hosts |
| Event day | Hosts update status to "attended" |
| T+1 week | Send thank-yous and log follow-ups |
| T+30 days | Delete or archive (§8) |

## 7. Officials and grant officers

- Many public bodies and funders limit gifts and hospitality. Ask the guest or their organization before the event, not at the door.
- Offer the same hospitality every attendee gets, unless the guest's organization has confirmed more is acceptable. Log anything extra in the gift column.
- A grant officer on the list must not be a route to influence a pending decision. If a decision is open, the approver states in the sheet that the invitation is general, not tied to it.
- A driver or escort gets a short, agreed access plan, such as parking and a waiting area. Decide it in advance with the venue.

## 8. Retention and deletion

- **At T+30 days:** delete the run sheet and the master's logistics and gift columns. Keep a minimal archive of year, name, organization, category, and attended yes or no.
- **Why the archive:** it stops us re-deriving the list every June. It is the only part we keep, and it holds no notes.
- **Never keep:** dietary or health requirements, driver details, or free-text opinions.
- **Right to delete:** if a guest asks what we hold or asks us to delete it, the lead answers within 30 days. Remove the archive row too.
- **Immediate cleanup:** last year's copy is still in the shared drive. Move it to the restricted folder now, strip it down to the archive fields, and delete the original. Check that the sharing link is off, and look for other copies in team members' drives.

## 9. Template

Create these tabs:

1. **Read me:** lead, deputy, retention date, link to this document.
2. **Master:** the §4 columns, with data validation on Category, Status, and Welcome level.
3. **Archive:** year, name, organization, category, attended.
4. **Run sheet:** built from Master via a one-off export, not a live link.

## 10. Open questions for you

- ⚠️ Does your country's data-protection law require a record of processing for this list? Check with whoever advises the organization. Shared-drive access to a list of named officials, with opinions about them, is the weakest point today.
- Who is the lead and who is the deputy this year?
- Do your funders or the city publish a gift or hospitality policy? If so, link it from §7.
