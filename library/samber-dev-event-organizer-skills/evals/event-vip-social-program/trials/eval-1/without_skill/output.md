# Terrafest Friday dinner: setup

## 1. One decision before you send anything

The dinner isn't a problem by itself. You turned down reserved front rows because "who gets the good seat" shouldn't be decided by status. A free back room for two maintainers and a well-cited researcher is the same thing in a different room. Attendees will read it that way if they hear about it. They will hear about it, because 400 volunteers and attendees in one venue talk.

Proposed fix: keep the dinner small, but change how it's set up.

- **Everyone pays their own share**, org members included, split evenly. The room is free, but nobody is treated.
- **The room isn't a perk.** Put a sign-up sheet by registration so any attendee can claim the back room for a table on Thursday or Saturday evening. Your six are one booking among others.
- **Hosts, not VIPs.** The three of you are hosts. Your comms lead's pitch is "help them meet each other". That is a good reason, and it needs no ranking.
- **Say it openly.** If asked, the answer is: "Six people who've only met online are having dinner. The back room is open to any group."

If you'd rather not do the sign-up sheet, the minimum is the pay-your-own-share rule plus an honest answer when asked. If you want a fully private, org-paid dinner, that is a different decision. It would contradict the reserved-seating stance, so decide it deliberately.

## 3. Invitation (send as individual messages, not a group thread)

> **Subject: Dinner Friday in Porto, after Terrafest day one**
>
> Hi [name],
>
> A few of us are having a small dinner on Friday evening: you, two other maintainers or researchers whose work we all lean on, and three of us from the Terrafest team. You've mostly met online, and it felt overdue to put you at the same table.
>
> - **When:** Friday [date], 20:00 (doors 19:45)
> - **Where:** back room at [venue], a short walk from the conference
> - **Cost:** we split the bill evenly, organisers included, same as at any Terrafest table
> - **Format:** one long table, no talks, no agenda
>
> Could you tell me by [date] if you can come, and whether you have any dietary needs?
>
> No pressure either way. If you'd rather keep your evening free after a full day, we'll understand.
>
> [Your name]

Notes on the wording:

- It doesn't say "special guests" or "exclusive".
- It states the cost split up front, so nobody has to ask.
- It makes declining easy.
- Send each invitation separately, so nobody sees who else was or wasn't asked before they reply.
