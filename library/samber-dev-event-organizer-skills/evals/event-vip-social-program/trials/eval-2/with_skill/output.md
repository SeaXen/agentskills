## 4. Invitation and door

**Named door list.**

- **Invitations:** individual and private, from the list holder only. Never use a group thread.
- **What each message says:** what the lunch is in plain words, where and how to enter, who else attends in shape not by name, what they'd miss on the public programme, how to decline easily, and one contact.
- **Wording:** say "partner lunch", which is Aurelio's contract wording. Never say "VIP" or "founders' table" to guests or in the team's vocabulary. The word becomes the tier.
- **The door:** one person, one printed page, names alphabetical and nothing else. Anyone not on it gets taken by name to the owner, never turned away or argued with. To curious attendees: "It's a private meeting, it'll be done shortly." The page goes back to the owner afterwards.
- **No credential and no schedule listing,** unless a guest asked for discretion. Even then, a printed listing is a decision with its own conditions.
