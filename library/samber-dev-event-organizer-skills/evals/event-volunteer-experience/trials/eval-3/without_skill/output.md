## Before you publish (4 gaps)

1. **Check that all 28 agreed to be named in public.**
   - A volunteer sign-up sheet is not consent to a public name list.
   - Some people may not want their full name tied to the event on the blog or socials, for privacy or employer reasons.
   - Fix: send a one-line "OK to name you?" message first, or use first names only.
   - Do not tag social handles unless people have said yes.
   - You're in France, so GDPR applies.
2. **Don't treat the crew leads' thanks as your thanks.**
   - Thanks at the end of a shift reached only the people who were there at that moment.
   - Anyone who left early, worked another slot, or signed up but couldn't come may have missed it.
   - The post and email are your thanks as organizer. Keep them.
3. **Handle the Marta and Kwesi shout-out with care.**
   - Tell them privately before the post goes live.
   - "Ran the cloakroom on their own" says the cloakroom was understaffed. Other cloakroom volunteers may feel erased, and attendees may read it as a planning failure.
   - Say what they did, such as "handled X coats over Y hours", and credit the rest of the cloakroom team.
   - Put a staffing question in the survey so you can fix it next year.
4. **Reconcile the list against reality.**
   - The sheet shows who signed up, not who showed.
   - Add people who helped without signing up, and remove no-shows.
   - Check name spellings and accents.
