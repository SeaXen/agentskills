### Questions to bring to your insurer and counsel

**Insurer**

1. Does our event policy cover volunteers? Does that include minors?
2. Does it cover people on site under another company's employment?
3. Who is liable if one of them is injured, or injures someone?
4. What do we need on file, such as signed waivers, guardian consent or a sponsor letter?

**Counsel**

1. What are the rules for minors at our venue, in our jurisdiction? Think supervision, hours, consent and any alcohol on site.
2. What documents should the coding club's guardians have signed?
3. Does a sponsor-supplied volunteer need a written agreement with the sponsor?
