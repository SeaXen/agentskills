## Why not 1 per 40

I can't give you a standard ratio, because the skill I followed rules out sizing from one. I also can't trace your 1-per-40 figure to a source.

The only ratio in circulation is JSConf EU 2019: 17 volunteers for about 1,600 attendees, roughly 1 per 94. That was the event's final edition, and its closing statement said the load was beyond what the volunteer team could responsibly handle. It is a floor observed at failure, not a target.

For 700 attendees, 1 per 40 gives about 18 people. That only works if every volunteer takes three shifts, and it silently drops to half coverage if they take two.

## What the number does not include

- **Code-of-conduct response team.** These are named, identifiable people who need to be reachable all day. I haven't counted them, so tell me whether they are organizers or volunteers.
- **Registration and cloakroom staff.** Registration handles attendee records and the cloakroom handles belongings. Give each a named person, not an open claim, and run a short just-in-time check before their first shift.
- **AV.** I assumed it is staffed by a vendor or by organizers, not volunteers.
- **Named backups.** Every post needs one. The floaters cover surprises, but they don't replace backups.
