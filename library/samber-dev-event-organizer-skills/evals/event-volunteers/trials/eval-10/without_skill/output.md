**Short version:** keep the thank-you page, but make it opt-in. Drop the cash. Keep the t-shirts. Cover lunch.

## 1. Cash at EUR 50 per day: don't

- ⚠️ **Legal.** Portugal's volunteer law (Lei 71/98) lets volunteers be reimbursed for expenses, not paid. A flat EUR 50 per day looks like remuneration. That risks reclassifying them as undeclared workers, with tax and social-security exposure. It could also break volunteer insurance cover. I'm not a lawyer, so check this with Portuguese counsel or your organizing entity before deciding.
- ⚠️ **Operational.** Paying cash to many people means cash handling, signed receipts, and no audit trail. It also invites no-shows who just want the money.
- ℹ️ **Perception.** EUR 50 for a 4-hour shift reads as a wage. It can attract the wrong motivation and make unpaid contributors, such as speakers and organizers, feel the gap.

## 2. Skip the t-shirts: reverse this

- ❌ **Attendees need to spot volunteers instantly.** With 600 people over 2 days, an identifiable volunteer is a basic operational need.
- ✅ **A shirt is cheap.** It costs roughly EUR 6–10 each, a fraction of one cash payout. People also tend to value the keepsake and wear it afterwards.

## 3. Lunch on their own: fix this first

- A 4-hour shift that spans lunch with no food provided will feel stingy.
- ✅ **Cheapest high-value perk:** give volunteers a meal voucher or access to the attendee catering line, on the day they work.

## 4. Name and photo thank-you page: opt-in only

- 🔐 **GDPR applies.** Publishing names and photos needs explicit, informed, withdrawable consent. Collect it at sign-up with a separate checkbox, not buried in the terms.
- Offer three options: name and photo, name only, or not listed. Some people avoid public exposure for privacy or employer reasons.
- Photos should be ones the volunteer chose or approved. Don't pull them from event shots without consent.
- Plan for takedown requests after publication.
- "Morning after" is fine, as long as consent was collected beforehand.

## Suggested replacement package

Per volunteer, per 2-day event. These are rough estimates, so replace them with real quotes.

| Item | Est. cost |
|---|---|
| T-shirt | EUR 8 |
| Lunch voucher or catering access, 1 per shift | EUR 12 to 15 per meal |
| Thank-you dinner or drinks for all volunteers | EUR 25 |
| Travel reimbursement against receipts, capped | Variable |
| Opt-in thank-you page | Free |

For 50 volunteers working 2 shifts each, your cash plan costs about EUR 5,000. The package above is about EUR 3,000–3,500. It also looks like gratitude rather than payment.
