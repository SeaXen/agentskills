# Volunteers to recruit for DataMesh Day Rotterdam

**Answer: recruit 24 volunteers.** That is 20 shift slots, each filled by a different person, plus a buffer for no-shows. The count covers only the three posts you listed.

## Shift slots

| Post | Shift | People | Slots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Registration | 08:00-11:00 | 4 | 4 |
| Registration | 11:00-14:00 | 2 | 2 |
| Registration | 14:00-16:00 (short, 2 h) | 2 | 2 |
| Info point | 09:00-12:00 | 2 | 2 |
| Info point | 12:00-15:00 | 2 | 2 |
| Info point | 15:00-18:00 | 2 | 2 |
| Teardown | 18:00-20:00 (short, 2 h) | 6 | 6 |
| **Total** | | | **20** |

- Registration from 11:00 to 16:00 is 5 hours, which doesn't divide into 3-hour shifts. I used one full shift and one 2-hour shift. You could instead run 14:00-17:00 and keep one extra person on hand.
- The info point is exactly 9 hours, so it splits into 3 clean shifts.
- Peak load is 6 people at once: 09:00-11:00 (registration 4 plus info 2) and 18:00-20:00 (teardown).

## Why 24

- **Base:** 20 people, each working one shift.
- **Buffer:** add about 15-20% for no-shows, which is 3-4 people. That gives 23-24, and I rounded up to 24.
- **Fewer people if some work two shifts:** a morning registration volunteer could take an afternoon info shift, which cuts the base toward 17-18. I wouldn't plan on it. It gets tiring, and the 08:00 start and 20:00 finish make a single-shift plan easier to recruit for.
- **Teardown:** the 15:00-18:00 info volunteers could stay on for teardown. Treat that as a bonus and recruit 6 separate people anyway.

## Ratio check

About 500 attendees over 4 registration desk staff gives roughly 125 check-ins per person during the 08:00-11:00 window. That is workable. If most people arrive 08:30-09:30, add 1-2 floaters for that hour.

## Not included

You didn't list room monitors, speaker support, or a first-aid/floor lead. With 3 parallel tracks, room monitors are the usual gap:

- **One monitor per room:** 3 rooms × 3 shifts = 9 more slots, bringing the total to about 29 slots and about 33 volunteers with the buffer.

Do you want the room monitors included, or is that covered by the venue or speaker team?
