## 1. Core design

Volunteers do not run everything. Three tiers:

| Tier | Who | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Paid pros | 1 AV lead + 4 AV techs (one per room pair), 2 registration pros | Anything that breaks, needs skill, or carries liability |
| Volunteer captains (7) | Experienced volunteers or Northwind staff from other teams | Lead 8–10 volunteers each. Handle first escalation. |
| Volunteers (~64 working slots) | Customers, community, UT Austin students, internal staff | Greeting, badge pickup, room monitoring, mic running, wayfinding |

- **AV rule:** volunteers do first-line only (clicker, mic handoff, laptop switch, timer cards). They never troubleshoot. They call the tech.
- **Your team:** 3 FTE do not staff positions. Each owns one area:
  1. Registration and badging
  2. Rooms and AV liaison
  3. Volunteer program (recruiting, scheduling, comms)
- During the event all three are escalation points and run the volunteer HQ.

## 2. Staffing math (assumptions, adjust to your agenda)

**Registration**
- Assume ~800 arrivals/hour on Day 1 morning.
- Use QR self-print kiosks at ~100/hour each with a helper. That needs 8 kiosks.
- Plus 2 greeters, 2 problem-lane staff, and 1 captain.

| Window | Staff |
|---|---|
| Day 0 evening pre-reg (4 hrs) | 6 |
| Day 1 7:00–10:30 | 14 |
| Day 1 10:30–14:00 | 5 |
| Day 1 afternoon | 3 |
| Day 2 7:30–10:00 | 6 |
| Day 2 rest of day | 3 |

Total is about 140 person-hours. The 2 paid pros anchor the problem lane. Volunteers handle kiosks and greeting.

**Room monitors**
- 8 rooms × 1 monitor × 9 hrs × 2 days = 144 hrs.
- Add 2 floaters × 9 hrs × 2 days = 36 hrs.
- Add 2nd monitor at transitions if room turnover is tight.
- Total is about 180 hrs.

**Headcount**
- ~320 volunteer hours ÷ ~5 hrs per person = ~64 volunteers.
- Cap each volunteer at one shift of about 5 hrs. They get the rest of the day as attendees. This is the main recruiting hook.
- Overbook 20% for no-shows. Recruit **~80**, keep a 10-person standby list.

## 4. Recruiting

Sources, in order of fit:
1. **Customers and community.** They love inside access. Best room monitors, since they know the product.
2. **Northwind employees from other teams.** Free, brand-aware, reliable. Get manager sign-off early. Use them as captains.
3. **UT Austin students** (events, hospitality, CS). Good for registration. Give them a recommendation letter or LinkedIn endorsement.
4. **Local event or tech community groups.**

Offer:
- A free conference pass, if your policy allows it, since marginal cost is zero.
- A meal, a shirt, and a volunteer lounge.
- A named credit and a LinkedIn recommendation.
- The rest of the day to attend sessions.

Do not pay volunteers cash. It changes the legal status (wage and labor rules). Ask legal if you want a stipend.

**Application form:** name, availability by half-day, role preference, T-shirt size, prior event experience, and consent to a privacy briefing. Pick captains from past experience and reliability.

## 5. Training and operations

**Training (3 touchpoints):**
1. 60-min virtual session at T-1 week: roles, escalation ladder, privacy, dress code.
2. One-page role cheat sheets, sent with confirmation.
3. 45-min on-site walkthrough on Day 0 evening: kiosks, rooms, radios, AV tech introductions.

**Room monitor checklist:**
- Arrive 20 min before the session.
- Do a mic and slides check with the tech.
- Count headcount against fire capacity.
- Hold timer cards at 5 and 1 minute.
- Run the Q&A mic.
- Reset the room after the session.

**Escalation ladder:** volunteer → captain → AV tech or reg pro → your team lead. Use one radio channel for AV and one for registration. Fix target: 5 minutes.

**Privacy:** registration volunteers see attendee data. Have them sign a short confidentiality note. Print no attendee lists. Keep competitors out of registration roles.

## 7. Risks

- **No-shows (15–25% typical):** overbook 20%, standby list, two reminders. Back critical posts (registration lane, AV) with paid staff.
- **Day 1 peak at registration:** pre-email QR codes at T-3 days. Add a separate lane for problem and walk-up cases. Open Day 0 evening to pull 20–30% of pickups forward.
- **Day 2 attrition:** assign different people to Day 2. Do not rely on Day 1 volunteers returning.
- **AV failure:** every room gets a named tech and a backup contact. Keep spare mics, clickers, and a loaner laptop in the volunteer HQ.

## 8. Success metrics

- Volunteer fill rate ≥ 95% and no-show rate ≤ 15%.
- Peak registration wait under 5 minutes.
- Room incidents resolved in 5 minutes or less.
- Volunteer satisfaction score and return rate (aim for 50%+ re-signing next year).
