# GoNorth Conf: Volunteer Briefing

Two days, four rooms, about 40 volunteers. Read this once, then keep it on your phone.

## Before you start

- Arrive at **[time]** on both days for check-in at **[location]**.
- Pick up your badge, shirt, and radio at the volunteer desk.
- Keep your radio on **channel [N]** and your phone charged.
- Eat and drink at your break. Breaks are mandatory.

## Radio basics

- Keep calls short: who you are, where you are, what you need.
- Example: "Room B door, need a mic runner."
- Say "break, break" only for emergencies.
- Move long conversations off the radio.

## Who to radio

| Situation | Radio |
|---|---|
| Schedule change, speaker late or missing | Floor Lead **[name]** |
| AV, mic, slides, or projector fault | AV Lead **[name]** |
| Room full, overflow, or crowd flow | Room Lead for that room |
| Lost property, registration problem, badge issue | Registration Desk |
| Catering, spills, or venue facilities | Venue Contact **[name]** |
| Medical emergency | Call **[local emergency number]**, then radio Floor Lead |
| Anything you can't classify | Floor Lead |

If someone reports harassment or a code-of-conduct concern, or you see one yourself, don't handle it. Radio **"CoC, [location]"** (or send the person to a CoC badge holder) and stay calm and polite. The code-of-conduct team takes it from there.

## Posts and tasks

### Registration desk (**[N]** volunteers)
- Scan badges and hand out lanyards.
- Look up names for attendees without a QR code.
- Send badge reprints and ticket problems to the Registration Lead.

### Room hosts (4 rooms, **[N]** per room)
- Open the doors **15 minutes** before the first session.
- Count heads against the room cap and close the doors when it's full.
- Keep the aisles and exits clear.
- Introduce speakers only if the Room Lead asks you to.
- Reset the room (water, chairs, trash) between sessions.

### Timekeepers (one per room)
- Hold up the time cards at **10, 5, and 1** minutes remaining.
- Hold Q&A to the schedule.
- Radio the Room Lead if a talk runs more than **2 minutes** over.

### Mic runners
- Bring the mic to the questioner. Don't hand over the mic stand.
- Stay in your room's aisle during Q&A.
- Report a dead battery or a feedback problem to the AV Lead.

### Wayfinding and floor
- Stand at your assigned junction and point people to rooms, toilets, and food.
- Learn the map and the schedule at a glance.
- Don't leave your spot until a replacement arrives or the Floor Lead releases you.

### Catering and breaks support
- Refill and clear the stations on the break schedule.
- Check dietary labels before pointing people to a dish.
- Radio the Venue Contact about spills or shortages.
