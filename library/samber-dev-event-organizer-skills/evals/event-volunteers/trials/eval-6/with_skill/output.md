## Do now (13:40 to 13:45)

1. **Post in the volunteer coordination channel.** Use FOSDEM's format: name, task, time slot, absent or late. Example: "Info desk 14:00 to [end of slot] on day one: [his name] is stuck at the airport and will not make any shift today. Need a named replacement by 13:55. Mira is on until handover." Post it in the channel, not in a DM.

3. **Tell Mira two things.** She is not abandoning the desk. And she is not the fallback: you will confirm the handover or cover it yourselves.

## Fill the 14:00 slot (13:45 to 13:55)

Work down this list and stop at the first person who says yes:

1. **A floater.** Floaters exist for this. Pulling one leaves a hole in the floater post, so tell the floaters who covers that hole.
2. **A volunteer on a break or off shift.** Ask them by name, not with a general call.
3. **An organizer from the standing team.** This is the guaranteed fallback. The coordinator stands the desk personally if nobody else can.

Whoever takes the slot gets the following:

- **A handover starting at 13:45.** The replacement shadows Mira for 15 minutes. That is the arrival buffer, and it is part of the shift.
- **A just-in-time gate if the desk handles cash or registration records.** If it runs point of sale or touches attendee data, show them the point-of-sale system before they handle a transaction. This takes about five minutes. Do not put an unvetted person alone at that desk.
- **A quick conduct briefing.** The info desk is where code-of-conduct reports land. Give them the five-step escalation script: make safety the first step, don't confront anyone, report, document, support.
- **Your named referent for the shift.** One person gives them live direction, so they don't take conflicting instructions.
