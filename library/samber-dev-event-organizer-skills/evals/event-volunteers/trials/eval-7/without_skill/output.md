**Mostly right. Three changes would make it work better.**

**1. Split Runner (the biggest issue)**
- "Anything that comes up" and "get speakers to rooms on time" pull in opposite directions. A runner who is fixing a lost badge can't also be at the green room 10 minutes before a talk.
- Make **Speaker Liaison** its own role. It owns the green room, speaker arrival and check-in, and walking speakers to their room before each slot. One person can cover this at first, with a deputy for changeovers.
- Keep **Runner** as the floater who handles whatever comes up.

**2. Add roles that are missing**
- **Volunteer Lead / Ops Lead:** one person with the schedule, a phone, and authority to reassign people. Without this, every problem lands on you.
- **AV / Tech per room:** mics, slides, laptops, and recording or streaming. Room Hosts shouldn't be fixing HDMI mid-intro. If Setup Crew covers this, name it explicitly and make sure those people stay on site during sessions, not just before them.
- **Code of Conduct contact:** named, visible, and not doubling as another role. Attendees need to know who to find. This is standard for Rust events.
- **Teardown:** fold it into Setup Crew and rename it "Setup & Teardown" so the end-of-day shift gets staffed.

**3. Tweak the existing roles**
- **Registration + Info Desk:** at 350 people, registration is busy for roughly the first 45 minutes and quiet after that. Staff Registration heavily early, then fold it into the Info Desk once the rush is over. Two separate permanent desks would waste people.
- **Room Host:** the role is fine. Add a rule for who ends a talk that overruns, and use time cards for speakers. For Q&A, specify whether the host repeats questions for the room and the recording, and who holds the mic.
- **Room Host cover:** you need a relief person, because hosts want breaks and lunch. Use a Runner or a spare volunteer.

**Revised list**
1. Ops Lead
2. Registration (folds into Info Desk after the morning rush)
3. Info Desk
4. Room Host (one per track, plus relief)
5. AV / Tech (one per room)
6. Speaker Liaison
7. Runner (floater)
8. Setup & Teardown Crew
9. Code of Conduct contact

**Rough headcount (my estimates, so adjust to your venue)**
- About 20–25 volunteers in total.
- Peak split: 4 on Registration, 2 on Info Desk, 3–4 Room Hosts, 3 AV, 1–2 Speaker Liaison, 2–3 Runners, 4–6 Setup & Teardown, 1 Ops Lead, and 2 CoC contacts.

**Questions that would let me refine this**
1. Is the venue's own AV staff included, or are you bringing your own?
2. Is the day recorded or streamed?
3. Are there sponsors who need a liaison?
