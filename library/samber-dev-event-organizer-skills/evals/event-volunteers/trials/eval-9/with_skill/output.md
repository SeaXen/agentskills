## Step 5: Shift grid and perks

- **Arrival buffers go inside the published shift time.**
  - Standard: arrive 15 minutes early.
  - First-timers: 30 minutes.
  - Room leads: in the room 10 minutes before the first talk.
- **Perks:**
  - Default: free access to the talks they want, plus food at the post. Ask on the form what each person wants from the day, and match posts to it.
  - Add proper meals for shifts longer than 3 hours or covering lunch.
  - A t-shirt serves wayfinding, but only if the lead time fits. Otherwise use a colored lanyard.
  - ❌ No cash stipend.

## Step 6: Briefing content (all three items are required for everyone)

1. **Post choreography.** A numbered sequence with times given as minutes before the thing it precedes.
2. **Escalation channels.** Name who to contact, on which channel. Use one referent per shift to direct volunteers live.
3. **CoC escalation script** (the skill's five steps):
   1. Ensure immediate safety, for the affected person first and then yourself.
   2. Ask the behaviour to stop, but only if it is safe. Say out loud that volunteers are not required to confront anyone.
   3. Report immediately, in person or via the anonymous path your policy provides.
   4. Document what happened while it's fresh: what, when, where, who was present.
   5. Support the affected person until the response team arrives.

Brief the script exactly as your published CoC states it. Do not rewrite the policy.

## Step 7: Coverage protocol (publish with the grid)

1. If you can't make your shift, post in the volunteer channel: your name, post, slot, and late or absent.
2. If your relief hasn't arrived after 10 minutes, contact the coordinator by chat, phone or in person.
3. **Do not leave your post until a replacement is confirmed.**
4. The named coordinator acknowledges every no-show report and finds the replacement. Floaters are the first reserve, then organizers.

Use three channels:
- Real-time chat during the event.
- Email for announcements before and after.
- A phone number for emergencies.

## Step 8: Measure coverage, not sign-ups

- Post-hours covered as a share of post-hours required, computed **per hour**.
- Share of posts with a named backup.
- No-show rate, and how many replacements were confirmed before the post was left.
- Share of volunteers briefed on the CoC script before their first shift. Target: 100%.
- Share of shifts that started on time.

**Pass threshold:** every post-hour covered by a named person with a named backup, and everyone briefed.
