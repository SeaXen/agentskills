# ElixirDays Krakow: volunteer recovery plan

**Situation:** 16 days out (Sunday 1 Nov, if the event date is what I computed). You have 0 confirmed volunteers against last year's 22. Treat the four sign-ups as leads, not staff. Waiting for the user group on Tuesday is too slow to be the only plan.

## Do today (about 2 hours)

1. **DM each of the four sign-ups personally.** Ask for a yes/no, their availability (full day or half), and a role preference. Assign one named person to own this. Right now nobody does, and that's the real failure.
2. **Pull last year's 22 volunteers and message each one individually.** They already know the job, so they're the cheapest source. Skip the Slack broadcast and send a direct message: "We're short. Can you do a half-day shift on 1 Nov? Reply yes/no by Tuesday."
3. **Write the roster of roles and shifts** (below) so every ask is concrete. "Help at the event" gets ignored. "Registration desk, 08:00–12:00" gets answered.

## Size the real need

These are my estimates, not your data. Adjust them to your venue.

- **Minimum viable:** about 12 people, with the key roles covered all day.
- **Target:** 18–20, using two half-day shifts and expecting a 20–30% drop-out.
- A volunteer who works only half a day is still useful. Offer shifts, not whole days.

## Recruit in this order

1. **Direct DMs** to the four sign-ups and last year's 22 (today).
2. **Speakers, sponsors and organizers' own contacts.** Ask each sponsor for 1–2 staff to cover registration or a room. Ask accepted speakers for a friend.
3. **Slack re-post, rewritten.** Open with the specifics: roles, shift hours, what volunteers get, and a one-click signup with a deadline. Don't re-post the old call.
4. **User group on Tuesday 20 Oct.** Ask for 5 minutes at the start. Bring a QR code to the signup form and a printed shift sheet. Collect names and phone numbers in the room, because in-person asks convert far better than Slack. Treat this as a top-up, not the plan.
5. **Local university or bootcamp communities** (Kraków dev clubs). Offer a free ticket and a certificate.

## Make the offer worth saying yes to

- Free ticket, including for the half-day not worked.
- Meals, a T-shirt, and a thank-you spot at the speaker or sponsor party if you have one.
- Fixed shifts, so a volunteer can still attend talks.
- A 30-minute briefing the week before (online is fine).

## Fallbacks if you're under 12 on 27 Oct

- Stagger registration with a self-service QR check-in, so you need fewer desk staff.
- Drop low-value roles first: cloakroom, extra floaters, welcome greeters.
- Ask the venue for its own staff for doors and AV, and ask sponsors for extra staffing.
- Use a paid temp agency for registration only. This is the most expensive option, so decide it by 27 Oct.
- Pull speakers and organizers into room-host slots, since they're already on site.

## Why did this fail?

Three weeks of a broadcast ask produced four sign-ups, and nobody followed up. Last year's 22 probably came from personal asks. After the event, keep a volunteer list with contacts and a named owner, and ask for next year's sign-ups at the closing talk.
