# Challenge

## Build something for your city.

That's the whole brief.

Pick a problem, a place, or a group of people. Make something for them in 24 hours. Choose any tool, any size, any angle: an app, a map, a bot, a dataset, a hardware hack, a service, a game.

Bring a project you've been chewing on, or arrive with nothing and join someone else's. On Friday night, pitch your idea to the room. Teams form around the ideas that get traction. You can work solo, join a team, or switch teams early on. Nobody assigns you to anything.

## Open Data Prize

[Consultancy name], a local consultancy, is awarding a prize for **best use of open data**.

- It's optional. Any project can enter, and the main challenge is unchanged.
- Use any open dataset: city, regional, national, or community-maintained. Say which one you used when you demo.
- Judges look at how well the data does its job. They'll ask whether it changes what the project can do or show, and whether it's used with care. Data that is only decoration won't win.
- Prize: [prize details].

## Format

- 24 hours, 70 or so people.
- Friday night: pitches, then teams form.
- Final demos: [time]. Show what you've built, and keep it short.

Everything else is up to you.
