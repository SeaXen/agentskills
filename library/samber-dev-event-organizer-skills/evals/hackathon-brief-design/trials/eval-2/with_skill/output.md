# Copperline Hack: Submission Guidelines

## What judging looks like

Judging is in person at expo tables. Six judges walk the room on Sunday morning for two hours, starting at [Sunday 09:00]. Your live demo at your table is what gets judged. Everything below exists to make that demo easy to find, run and verify.

## What to submit

Every team submits **one project**. Submit once, through [submission form / link]. You may enter that one project in several challenge tracks. Entering a sponsor's challenge track never removes you from the general prize.

**Required**

1. **Code link.** Your code must be publicly available, ideally in a git repository. It must **stay public after the event** to remain prize-eligible. If you make it private on Monday, new winners may be selected. [If your event cannot honour public code, replace this line and say what replaces it.]
2. **Readme in the repository**, containing:
   - what the project does, in two sentences
   - how to run it
   - what is not finished
   - every library and framework you used
   - every AI tool you used (see "AI-tool disclosure")
3. **One-paragraph description**, in your own words: what it does and who it is for. Judges read this before they reach your table.
4. **Table number and presenter name**, filled in on the submission form.
5. **API keys removed** from everything you share. Check the repository history too, not just the latest commit.

**Optional**

- **Demo video, 2 minutes or less.** It is not judged and not required. If you make one as a fallback in case your live demo fails, record it during the event, say "Copperline Hack" at the start, and keep it public.

**Not required, not judged**

- A slide deck. Do not make one.

**At your table**

- At least one team member must be at the table for the whole judging window, [09:00-11:00]. Judges walk in any order, so an empty table cannot be scored.
- Bring your own laptop, power adapter and any cables. Your demo must run from your own machine. [Wi-Fi/power details from venue.]

## Deadline

**Submissions close Sunday [date] at [08:30] Europe/Berlin time.** This is the deadline on the clock, not "when hacking ends".

- Judging starts at [09:00]. The half hour in between is for setting up your table. Judges receive the list of submissions at the deadline.
- "Submitted" means the form is complete and the link opens for someone who is not logged in. A private repository has not been submitted.
- **Late policy:** a submission that arrives after [08:30] is not entered. There is no grace window. [Alternative if you choose one: a late team is judged but is ineligible for prizes.]
- **After the deadline** you may debug and make small fixes of a few lines. New features and large changes are not allowed. Stop development at the deadline.

## AI-tool disclosure

You may use AI tools: code completion, code generation, image generation and similar. You must say which ones, in your readme and when a judge or organizer asks you at your table. A project that is a reskin of an existing AI tool is not eligible. Undisclosed AI use is grounds for disqualification.
