# Submission Guidelines

*Copperline Hack, Berlin. In-person teams only.*

## Changes from your plan

1. **I dropped the slide deck.** Judges walk the room and visit tables, so no team presents from a stage. A deck would go unread, and teams would lose an hour of build time making it.
2. **I kept the video, but as a fallback.** Judges won't watch 2-minute videos while walking the expo. The video protects a team whose live demo breaks (Wi-Fi, a crashed laptop) and gives judges something to check afterwards. The section says so, so teams don't over-invest in it.
3. **"When hacking ends" is now a fixed clock time.** Teams will argue about the end if it isn't written down. The section also adds a short gap between the deadline and the expo, so teams can set up tables. Fill in the bracketed times.

---

## 1. What every team submits

Submit all four items by the deadline in section 2.

1. **Repository link.** Public, or private with access granted to `[judging account]`. It must contain the code written during the event.
2. **Project page.** One page, either the repo README or a form entry, with:
   - project name and team members;
   - the problem, in two sentences;
   - what it does, in two sentences;
   - how to run it, or the URL of the deployed demo;
   - the tools, APIs, and pre-existing code you used.
3. **Demo video, 2 minutes maximum.** A screen recording with voice-over is fine. It shows the working product end to end. It is your backup if the live demo fails and is not scored separately. Link it from the project page, and make sure it plays without a login.
4. **Table number.** You get it at check-in. Judges' routes are built from submissions, so a team without a submission may be skipped.

**No slide deck is required or accepted.** Judges score what is on your table, so spend the time on the demo.

## 2. Deadline

| Milestone | Time |
|---|---|
| Hacking ends and **submissions close** | `[Sunday, HH:MM]` |
| Table setup (no code changes) | `[HH:MM–HH:MM]` |
| Expo opens, judges walk the room (2 hours) | `[Sunday, HH:MM–HH:MM]` |

- The organizers' clock is the official clock. It is displayed in the venue and announced at `[HH:MM]`, `[HH:MM]`, and 15 minutes before close.
- **Code freeze.** The repo's last commit must be timestamped before the deadline. Judges may check commit history, and commits after the deadline are ignored.
- **Fixes during setup.** You may fix your table setup, such as cables, laptop, and demo data. You may not change the code you demo.
- **Late submissions.** You have a `[10]`-minute grace period. After that, the team is not scored but may still show at the expo.
- **Resubmission.** Edit your entry as often as you like before the deadline. The latest version at the deadline counts.

## 3. What judges see

- **Live demo at your table.** Be ready to give a 3-minute walkthrough, then answer questions. Judges visit every table at least once during the 2 hours, and some more than once.
- **Stay at your table.** At least one team member must be present at all times during the expo. Take breaks in shifts.
- **Bring your own power and a working setup.** The venue provides a table, power, and Wi-Fi, but the Wi-Fi is shared by 120 people. Your demo must not depend on it, or the video must cover for it.
- **Show real work.** Judges will ask to see the product running, not a mockup. Anything you did not build during the event must be listed on the project page.

## 4. Eligibility and rules

- All code for the project must be written during the event. Existing libraries, APIs, and templates are allowed if disclosed on the project page.
- Team size: `[1–4]` people. Everyone on the team must be on site.
- One submission per team, and one team per person.
- Organizers may disqualify a submission that breaks these rules or the event's code of conduct.

## 5. Checklist (print and bring)

- [ ] Repo link works for the judging account
- [ ] Project page is complete, including disclosure of pre-existing code
- [ ] Demo video is 2 minutes or less and plays without login
- [ ] Table number is on the submission
- [ ] Last commit is before the deadline
- [ ] Demo works offline, or the video is ready as backup
