**Short answer: no, 8 is too many. Use 4 tracks, 2 of them tied to the sponsors.**

## Why 8 breaks

1. **The 50 per track assumes all 400 show up.** Free hackathon registrations usually convert at 50–70%. That leaves about 200–280 people on Saturday.
2. **Teams, not people, are what get judged.** At 4 per team and a 70% submission rate, 240 attendees means about 60 teams and about 42 submissions. Across 8 tracks that's about 5 submissions each, and uneven picking makes it worse. The popular track gets 12 and the niche ones get 2 or 3. A track with 2 entries isn't a contest.
3. **6 judges can't cover 8 tracks.** That's fewer judges than tracks. With 3 judges per track you'd need 24 judge-slots, so each judge would cover 4 tracks. Per-track expertise disappears.
4. **2 sponsors can't fund 8 prize tracks.** Six tracks would have no prize owner or budget. Prizes that sponsors fund usually need a track named for them.
5. **Friday's 3 session tracks don't inform Saturday's count.** They're a separate format with a different audience, so 3 doesn't lead to 8.

## Recommended structure

| Track | Funded by | Expected submissions (at 240 attendees) |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsor A challenge | Sponsor A | ~10 |
| Sponsor B challenge | Sponsor B | ~10 |
| Open / best overall | Organizers | ~12 |
| Wildcard (community choice or best newcomer) | Organizers | ~10 |

Teams can enter one sponsor track and also the open track.

**Judging:** run 2 panels of 3 judges. Panel 1 takes Sponsor A and the Wildcard. Panel 2 takes Sponsor B and the Open track. Each panel sees about 20 submissions, so 5-minute demos take under 2 hours. Hold a short joint session for the final ranking.

## Numbers to confirm

- **Show-up rate:** I assumed 60%. Check your past events. If you have an RSVP reminder step, ask for a reconfirmation 3 days out and count only those who reply.
- **Team size:** I assumed 4. If you allow solos or cap teams at 3, submissions go up.
- **Sponsor terms:** ask each sponsor whether they require a named track, their own judge, or a prize minimum.
- **Judge expertise:** with 6 judges, check that at least one per sponsor panel knows that sponsor's product.

## If you still want 8

Only do it if the tracks are themes inside the 4 prize categories, not separate contests. For example, the Open track could have 5 theme tags used for browsing and no separate prizes. That gives participants the variety without splitting the judging.

**Decision for you:** are the sponsors' prizes tied to named tracks, or do they just fund a shared pool? If it's a shared pool, 3 tracks also works.
