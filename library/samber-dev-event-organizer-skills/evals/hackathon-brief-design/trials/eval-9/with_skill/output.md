## Problems beyond the seven

1. **Minimal rules are off the table.** Two sponsors fund prizes, so a rules set with only a team-size cap is not allowed. You need at least the adopted standard rule set. Nothing in your summary shows it. The fields the brief must carry are:
   - work only inside the window
   - pre-existing code
   - crossposting
   - AI-tool disclosure
   - disqualification grounds
   - code-of-conduct link and reporting contact
2. **The cross-track rule is missing.** The brief must say "one project, may enter several challenge tracks, still eligible for the general prize." Without it, teams assume that entering a sponsor track forfeits the general prize, and fewer teams enter.
3. **Sponsor tracks are unvetted.**
   - Which of AI and Climate are the sponsor tracks? Open can't be one, because a sponsor prize needs a stated criterion.
   - Has someone on your team built something small, end to end, against each sponsor's API or data?
   - Does each sponsor prompt state a capability rather than a brand instruction, the access path (where keys come from, the rate limit, who to ask), and a **fallback sentence** for when the sponsor platform fails?
   - Default: make the sponsor product an optional bonus path, not required. Promote it to required only if the signed agreement names the track and your team has built against it.
4. **The Open track needs a pitch mechanic.** That means a problem-not-solution pitch, an evidence requirement, and a minimum before a team may start building. Hand those to `hackathon-team-formation`.
5. **Minors and personal data are gates.** If minors may attend or the submission collects personal data, someone qualified reviews the rules before publication. That is counsel, or whoever owns data protection for your organization. Neither of us writes that text.
6. **No intellectual-property clause.** I won't write one. If a sponsor asks for IP terms, route that to counsel through `event-sponsor-agreement`.
7. **The checklist is incomplete for any format.**
   - Public repo, and it **stays public** after the event to remain prize-eligible.
   - API keys removed.
   - Frameworks listed in the readme.
   - AI-tool use disclosed.
   - A one-paragraph description.
   - Ask for consent to contact teams afterward.
   - If the demo format is expo or stage, say whether a video is optional. Don't require one nobody watches.
8. **The evaluation section is incomplete.**
   - Say who weights the categories and when the weights get published. Print no weights, scale or totals.
   - Print the elimination criteria in full.
   - Add a "not judged" list, each exclusion with a reason, so teams stop polishing slides. You decide the content for your event.
   - Write a description of what a judge looks for in each of the three categories.
9. **Registration closes Thursday, and freeze starts there.** Publish before it closes, then change nothing. Any later rule re-scores teams that already built against the published text.
