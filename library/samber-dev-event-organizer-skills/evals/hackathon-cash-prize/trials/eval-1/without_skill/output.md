Keep 20,000 / 12,000 / 8,000. Don't weight the top more.

**Why**
- 120 people is about 25 to 35 teams. Top 3 is about 10% of teams winning. That is already a tight bar.
- Judging a 36-hour build is noisy. A flatter split means the judges' coin-flip between 1st and 2nd costs less.
- Teams split the prize. At 4 people, 1st is 5,000 NOK each, 2nd 3,000, 3rd 2,000. Cutting 3rd to make 1st bigger drops 3rd below the point where people care.
- Heavy top weighting rewards risky demos over finished work. It also makes losing teams leave early.
- 50/30/20 is a normal, defensible shape. Nobody will argue with it.

**Optional tweak, only if you want more winners**
- 16,000 / 10,000 / 6,000 for places 1 to 3 (32,000 total).
- 2 × 4,000 for category prizes, for example "best beginner team" and "best use of the theme" (8,000 total).
- Total stays at 40,000. Winners go from 3 teams to 5, which suits 120 people. Top prize drops by 4,000.
