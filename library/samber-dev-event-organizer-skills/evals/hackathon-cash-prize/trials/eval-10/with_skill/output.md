## 1. Moving Vantwell's second place close to first: ❌

## 2. Raffle winner paid from the prize pool: ❌, with a better source

- **A raffle isn't a competition prize.** It is an attendee incentive open to anyone. It belongs to the sponsor-fulfillment side and is funded by the booth sponsor.

Options, in order:

1. **Have the booth sponsor fund it.** That is what a booth raffle is.
2. **Use a leftover unit.** If any winning team is smaller than the team-size cap, a gear or credits unit is left over. Raffling it is a published, accepted use. It only works if the brief said what happens to leftovers. If it didn't, hold or return the unit.
