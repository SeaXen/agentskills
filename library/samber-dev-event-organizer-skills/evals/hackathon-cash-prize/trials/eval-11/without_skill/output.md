# Prizes

Oakcrest Devfest Hackathon awards **3,000 EUR** in prizes, given as hardware kits. There is no cash.

## Sponsor tracks

Two sponsors fund the prizes. Both have signed and paid.

| Track | Sponsor | Prize budget |
|---|---|---|
| Ferrowyn track | Ferrowyn | 1,500 EUR |
| Stilbeck track | Stilbeck | 1,500 EUR |

Each track awards one hardware kit to its winning team. The kit's retail value is up to 1,500 EUR. Each sponsor sets its own track challenge and picks its winner. The sponsor's challenge brief is published before the event.

## Rules

- **Prizes are hardware kits only.** No cash alternative and no substitution.
- **Teams have at most 4 members.** A winning team receives one kit per track won, not one per member. The team decides how to share it, and it must name one contact person for delivery.
- **A team can win only one track.** If it ranks first in both, it takes the higher-ranked track. The next team in the other track takes that kit.
- **Participants under 18 may compete and win.** A minor needs written consent from a parent or legal guardian at registration. A kit won by a minor is delivered to, or confirmed by, that guardian.
- **Prize details and delivery.** The organizer buys the kits from the sponsor-funded prize budget. The nonprofit's treasurer approves the purchase and signs for it. Kits are handed over at the closing ceremony or shipped within a reasonable time after it. Winners must provide a delivery address in the EU.
- **Disqualification.** A team that breaks the code of conduct or the event rules is not eligible for prizes.

## Transparency

The organizer is a registered nonprofit. The full prize budget comes from the two sponsor payments above. The treasurer holds the funds and keeps the records.
