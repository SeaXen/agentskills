## First place: team with the 16-year-old

Under Irish law a person under 18 is a minor, so a minor's signature alone is weak.

1. Get written consent from a parent or legal guardian. It should cover accepting the prize and say where to pay it.
2. Get the guardian's ID and a short proof of relationship, such as a birth certificate or a signed declaration. Check which your accountant wants.
3. Pay to one of these:
   - the minor's own bank account, with the guardian's written approval
   - the guardian's account, with the guardian's written receipt for the minor's share
4. Get the guardian's consent before you use the minor's photo, name or data publicly.
5. Check whether the guardian already signed the hackathon's under-18 consent. If not, get it now.

Also check the other members' ages. If any are under 18, the same steps apply to them.
