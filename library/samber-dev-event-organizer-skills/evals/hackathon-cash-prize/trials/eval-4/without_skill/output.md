**No, don't use a 50/30/20 split.** Cash divides into any proportion, but a kit is one set per person. The fair unit is **one full set per person on the submission**. Percentages would leave some people with no soldering station or multimeter.

**First place (3 people)**
- Give them 3 sets, one for each person on the submission.
- The friend who isn't on the submission doesn't get a set. The cap of 4 is a limit, not an entitlement.
- This leaves 1 spare set from the 4 you ordered for first.

**Stock**
- You have 8 sets in total.
- 3 go to first place, so 5 are left for second and third.

**Second and third: count the people on each submission**

| Second place size | Third place size | Sets used | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 1 | 5 | Exactly enough |
| 4 | 2 or more | 6 or more | Short by the third-place headcount minus 1 |
| 3 | 2 | 5 | Exactly enough |
| 3 | 3 or 4 | 6 or 7 | Short by 1 or 2 |
| 2 | 3 | 5 | Exactly enough |

Second place always gets full sets for everyone on its submission.

**If third place comes up short**
1. Order the missing sets. You have time.
2. Give third place a lesser prize and keep the ranking visible. For example, multimeters only, or a gift card.
3. Give third place whatever is left. Say clearly that third gets "remaining stock", so it doesn't read as a mistake later.

I'd pick option 1 if the shortfall is 1 or 2 sets. Otherwise pick option 2.
