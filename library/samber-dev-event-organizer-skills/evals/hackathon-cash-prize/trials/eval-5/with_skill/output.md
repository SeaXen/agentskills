## 2. Merribell: 5 seats as the grand prize

**Do not publish a 12,000 grand prize.** Six problems:

1. **The figure is the donor's list price, not a value you verified.** Don't price an in-kind prize at retail. A prize may also displace spending you would never have made. Check what the seats are worth to a winning team.
2. **Five seats count in whole units, not cash.** Team-size cap vs 5 units:
   - A team of 3 leaves 2 units.
   - A team of 6 is short by 1.
   
   Decide before publishing: return, raffle or hold the leftovers, and whether the award follows the submitted roster or the people on stage.
3. **It is a credit-type prize.** Publish the expiry, redemption region and account requirement for the seats. Redeemable codes carry the same reporting considerations as cash, so route that to your fiscal host or counsel.
4. **A headline sized at nominal value is a flagship pool.** That rung needs two things:
   - a Prizes line in the event budget that is set and large enough, and
   - an intent to buy attention before the event rather than reward winners afterward.
   
   If the Prizes line doesn't exist yet, the flagship rung is deleted. I'd also compare publicly "12,000 of seats" against Halcyon's real cash with care. Nominal list price next to cash misleads, and Halcyon may notice.
5. **"Push Merribell's own track to third" mixes two things.** The rank belongs to `hackathon-judging`, and I never move a placement or let a prize influence who won it. What I can decide is the split shape: a podium of overall places plus sponsor tracks as horizontal awards. A sponsor's own track shouldn't sit as the overall third place. Give Merribell a named track award, and let the overall podium be judged on its own.
6. **Seats in the grand prize favour teams that use Merribell's platform.** The teams most worth keeping may not use it. A grand prize no winner wants is a weak headline. Check utility to a recipient, or put the seats on Merribell's track and use another prize as the headline.

My default split is a graduated podium (1st, 2nd, 3rd) plus one named track award per sponsor. That is only possible once the sponsors are confirmed. It needs your Q2 answer on what the published brief already promised.
