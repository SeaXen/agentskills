# Bramblewick Hack #3: prize structure (draft for your validation)

✅ **Ready to publish after 5 answers from you.** Nothing below is published yet. Publication is the point of no return.

⚠️ **I can't give you one winner and one big award.** The three sponsors each bought a named track award, and the brief already published a general best overall category. A single grand prize would delete those awards. That means re-negotiating three signed deals, and the sponsors would lose the visibility they paid for.

🎯 **The nearest simple structure is 4 funded awards.** That's one overall award plus one award per sponsor track. The ceremony stays short: the three track winners go in one batch, and the overall winner goes last as the headline. Second and third place on the overall are optional and cost nothing to pay out (see 3).

## Who owns what

- **Ranking** belongs to `hackathon-judging`. I turn its ranked outcome into awards and never re-open a placement. Prize size never influences who won.
- **The published rules** belong to `hackathon-brief-design`. That skill owns who may *compete*. I own who may *receive* an award once a rules-eligible team has won. I hand it the text in section 4.
- **The ceiling** belongs to `event-budget`, which sets the Prizes line. I size inside it.
- **Sponsor-funded prize shape.** The sponsorship skills price and deliver the add-on, but none of them says anything about a competition prize. I'm asserting this boundary into empty space, not repeating an agreement.
- **A booth raffle is not a competition prize.** Raffles belong to `event-sponsor-fulfillment`. These awards are judged-rank awards.

## 1. Medium

**Decided by your answers to Q3, Q4 and Q5. Cash is conditional.**

- **Delete cash** if any winner could be a minor or outside your country, or if you have no legal entity and no fiscal host. Cash is the only rung that moves through a payment rail to a named person, and that rail triggers the tax, cross-border and minor-payout categories.
- **Credits and vouchers** are the likely sponsor medium. They count as cash-equivalent for compliance. Publish each credit's expiry, redemption region and required account, or you'll hear about it months later.
- **Gear:** skip it for track awards. It has the longest lead time, because items get backordered. That's a poor fit with a near publication date (Q9).
- **Opportunity** (a ticket, an introduction) is only offerable if the external party has committed in writing. Otherwise delete it. Its legal treatment is an open question.
- **Overall award, if the Prizes line is zero or unset:** use recognition only, such as a trophy passed from cohort to cohort. It's a legitimate rung, it needs no legal entity, and it still gives you a headline award.

## 2. Pool

- **Sponsor-funded part:** 3 × 5k, one per track. It scales with what was actually signed and draws nothing from your Prizes line.
  - ❌ **It can't be pooled into the overall.** Each 5k is tied to its sponsor's track.
  - ⚠️ **Don't announce a track prize before its money has landed or been committed in writing.** Otherwise you owe the gap personally.
- **Organizer baseline** (the overall award): **I can't size it.** You haven't told me what the Prizes line carries, and I won't size against nothing.
  - Size it inside whatever `event-budget` sets.
  - If that line isn't set, flag it and use the recognition-only fallback above.
- **Rejected:** a flagship pool. It only fits an attention-before-the-event goal with a large, set Prizes line. You want simplicity, so it's out.

## 3. Split and award count

**Shape: graduated podium on the overall, plus the three sponsor-bought track awards.**

- **Winner-take-most is deleted by the rules** as soon as any sponsor funds a track. That is why last year's single grand prize doesn't carry over.
- **Flat across many tracks is deleted** by your low effort ceiling.
- **Track-only is out** because it contradicts the overall category already in the published brief.

| Award | Funded by | Medium |
|---|---|---|
| Overall 1st | Organizer (Prizes line) | Per section 1 |
| Track A, B, C (1 each) | The matching sponsor | Per Q3 |
| Overall 2nd and 3rd (optional) | None | Recognition only |

- **4 funded awards**, or **6 total** with the optional 2nd and 3rd.
- Dropping 2nd and 3rd gives you your simplest version. I'd keep them because they give more teams something to finish for, and they add no payout or eligibility check.
- If credits or gear are counted in units per person, the team-size cap in the brief sets the unit count. Publish the leftover-unit rule: returned, raffled or held. Cash needs no such rule.

## 5. Payout mechanic

**Default: standard-form payout.** That means published eligibility text, one claim step, and identity collection appropriate to the medium. You write it once before the event, while a mistake is still free.

**Simplest for two part-timers: administered payout for the 3 track awards.**
- Each sponsor pays or delivers its own track winner under its own process.
- You only facilitate, and you run the standard-form payout for the overall award alone.
- Get the division of responsibility in writing before announcing. "The sponsor is handling it" is not a plan.
- This also removes the money-lands-late risk for the track awards.

**Written payout plan (6 lines, fill before publishing):**
1. Medium, plus expiry, region and account requirement for any credit.
2. Handover: who hands over or transfers each award.
3. Claim: what the winner provides, as a category and not a document name.
4. Finality: when the award becomes final.
5. Lapse: what happens to an unclaimed award, and by when.
6. Routing: see section 6.

## 6. Exposure categories and routing

I state no threshold, form name or currency amount. The specifics are jurisdiction-specific and change over time.

| Category | Status | Route to |
|---|---|---|
| Contest-law classification | Live, as it is for any prize contest. Don't break ties with a coin flip or random draw (the judging skill avoids that for this reason). | Counsel, or fiscal host |
| Payer-side tax reporting and withholding | Live if any award is cash or cash-equivalent | Fiscal host, else counsel. Track awards: each sponsor's legal team if it pays directly |
| Cross-border payment | Unknown (Q4) | Your bank or platform for rail reach. Counsel for the rules |
| Minors receiving an award | Unknown (Q4) | Counsel or fiscal host, before announcing |
| Sponsor employees on their own track | Live. Answer it in the sponsorship agreement | Each sponsor's legal team, then counsel |
| Organizers, volunteers, mentors, judges | Settled by published practice: may build, may not win | Adopted in section 4 |

🔐 **Three answers are gates, not trade-offs.** These are a minor winner, a winner outside your country, and a sponsor employee eligible for their own track. If any is live, counsel or the fiscal host reviews the payout **before** you announce. Simplicity does not get a vote on that.
