# Sableford Hack closing ceremony: plan and reply

## Changes to your plan

1. **Don't project the full sheet with judge names and raw marks.**
   - Every team that didn't win sees its weakest marks on a big screen, and every judge becomes a target for "why did you give us a 4?"
   - The full ranking appears before you announce it, which kills the reveal.
   - Judge identity plus raw marks is personal data. Judges agreed to judge, not to be publicly attributed. Check with them first.
   - The sponsor-track marks aren't final, so you can't show a complete sheet anyway.
   - **Instead:** publish the rubric and process before Sunday. After the ceremony, send each team its own marks per criterion with judges shown as "Judge A–F". Publish the final top-five table (team, total, per-criterion) within 24 hours. Name the judging panel, but don't link names to marks.
2. **Don't present the top-five demos as a filler.**
   - Showing "the top five" before the reveal leaks the result. Teams 6 and below also read it as a verdict.
   - Live demos fail on stage (wifi, crashes).
   - **Instead:** call them "finalist showcases" and say clearly they are not scored. Give each 4 minutes, with a recorded fallback ready. Better, finish sponsor judging before the ceremony.
3. **Make the sponsor judges hit a deadline.** Set a hard cutoff of 30 minutes before doors, with scores locked. If they miss it, announce main-track winners first and the sponsor prizes last, as a planned segment rather than filler.
4. **Don't announce from loose notes.** Lock the sheet and print one copy. A second person checks the sums and the ordering against it. Winners go on sealed cards in announcement order.

## Run-of-show

Draft times, adjust to your slot.

1. **T-3 days (now):**
   - Send judges the consent question.
   - Send teams the written rubric, the appeal rules and the cutoff time.
2. **T-1 day:**
   - Confirm the sponsor judges' cutoff in writing.
   - Name your second checker.
   - Test the demo laptop and the fallback recordings.
3. **T-30 min:**
   - Scores locked and timestamped.
   - Totals checked against the printed sheet.
   - Winner cards sealed.
4. **Ceremony, 5 min:** Welcome. Explain the scoring and appeal process in 60 seconds.
5. **Showcases, up to 20 min:** Unscored, 4 minutes each, with a strict timekeeper.
6. **Winners, 15 min:**
   - Announce from the sealed cards, third place first.
   - Show only a slide per winner with the team name and total.
   - Sponsor prizes follow, from their locked scores.
7. **Close:** Repeat the appeal rule and the deadline.
8. **Within 24 hours:** Send per-team marks, publish the top-five table, and open the appeal window.

## Appeal rules (publish before Sunday)

- **Grounds:** a calculation or data-entry error, a breach of the published rules, or an undisclosed conflict of interest. Disagreement with a judge's opinion is not a ground.
- **Window:** written appeal to you within 48 hours of receiving the team's marks.
- **Review:** someone who judged neither track and has no stake in the result. Not you alone, if you scored anything.
- **Outcome:** the reviewer answers within 5 days. Errors get corrected and the correction is published. Otherwise the result stands, and the answer is final.
