## Judge brief: Ferrowood Hack, Sunday scoring

**Your job:** Score each project you are eligible to score, using only what you see in the Sunday demo. Scoring takes about 2 hours.

### Non-negotiables

1. **Never score a team you mentored.** Your sheet has those rows crossed out. Leave them blank. Don't discuss those teams with other judges either.
2. **If you entered a project in the open track, you don't score the open track at all.** This covers all open-track projects, not only your own. You're competing with them.
3. **Score the demo, not the weekend.** You know what Saturday looked like. Ignore who struggled, who you helped, and who you like. If it isn't visible in the demo or the Q&A, it doesn't count.
4. **Score alone and don't compare scores until the end.** Write your numbers before you hear anyone else's reaction.

### Criteria (1–5 each, whole numbers)

| Criterion | Weight | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Working demo | ×3 | Core feature works live, with no manual fixes. |
| Idea and usefulness | ×2 | A real problem, and a clear reason someone would use it. |
| Craft | ×2 | Sensible design and finish for a weekend of work. |
| Presentation | ×1 | Clear in 3 minutes, and the team handles questions well. |

*Replace these with your own criteria if you have a rubric. Keep the weights.*

Anchors:
- **1:** Not shown or broken.
- **3:** Works, with rough edges.
- **5:** Impressive for a weekend.

Use the full range. If every score is a 4, the ranking is meaningless.

### How to score

1. Watch the full demo and Q&A first, then write your scores. Don't write them while the demo is running.
2. Fill in the sheet within 2 minutes of the demo ending, before the next one starts.
3. Weighted total = sum of (score × weight), out of 40.
4. Add one sentence of reason per project, in case of a tie or a question.

### What organizers do with your sheet

- Each project's final score is the **average** of the eligible judges' totals. The average is used because judges score different numbers of projects.
- Ties go to the higher *Working demo* average, then to a short judges' discussion.
- Only the organizers see individual sheets. Teams get the winners, not your scores.

### Questions during judging

Ask an organizer, not another judge. If you realize mid-demo that you have a conflict we missed, leave that row blank and tell an organizer afterward.
