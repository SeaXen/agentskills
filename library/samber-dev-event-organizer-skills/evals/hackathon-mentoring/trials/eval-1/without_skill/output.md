# Riverbend Build Days: mentor headcount

**Answer: about 50 confirmed mentors, with about 12 on the floor at peak.** The plausible range is 40 to 60, depending mainly on how long your mentors' shifts are.

## How I got there

1. **Expected attendees.** 340 registered × ~70% show rate ≈ **240 people**. Free events usually see 60–75% turn up. Replace this with your own past show rate if you have one.
2. **Peak ratio.** I used 1 mentor per 20 attendees: 240 ÷ 20 = **12 on the floor at peak**.
   - Use 1:15 (16 mentors) if you want faster help.
   - Your open theme means every project has a different stack, so I wouldn't go looser than 1:20.
   - No sponsor is supplying technology, so no vendor engineers will cover for you. All mentors come from your own recruiting.
3. **Coverage curve.** Demand is not flat across the 44 hours. Floor mentors per window:

| Window | Hours | Mentors on floor | Mentor-hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fri 18:00–23:00 (kickoff, team forming, setup problems) | 5 | 8 | 40 |
| Fri 23:00–Sat 09:00 (overnight) | 10 | 2 (on-call) | 20 |
| Sat 09:00–19:00 (peak building) | 10 | 12 | 120 |
| Sat 19:00–Sun 01:00 (things break) | 6 | 6 | 36 |
| Sun 01:00–08:00 (overnight) | 7 | 2 (on-call) | 14 |
| Sun 08:00–14:00 (deploys, demo prep) | 6 | 10 | 60 |
| **Total** | **44** | | **~290** |

4. **Convert hours to people.** 290 mentor-hours ÷ ~6 hours per mentor ≈ **48**, so I rounded to 50 confirmed.
   - Most volunteers give one block of 4–6 hours, so the shift length you can get sets the total.
   - With 8-hour shifts you need about 36 mentors. With 4-hour shifts you need about 72.
   - Recruit a few extra, about 10–15%, because some will cancel.

## Make sure the roster covers the right skills

Open theme means you can't pick mentors by sponsor tech. Cover these domains in every peak window, with at least 2 generalists on top:
- Web front end and back end
- Mobile
- ML/AI and data
- Cloud and deployment
- Hardware or embedded, if anyone is likely to bring it
- Design and UX
- Product and pitch coaching, weighted toward Sunday morning

Put 2–3 overnight mentors on call. Solo hackers lack teammates to unblock them, so give them an easy way to ask: a help board or a mentor request queue.

## What would change the number

- **Show rate:** 280 attendees instead of 240 gives 14 on the floor and about 58 on the roster.
- **Shift length:** this is the biggest lever, as shown above.
- **Project count:** 18 teams plus solo hackers means maybe 100–150 projects. A high project count raises demand more than raw attendee count does.

## Questions for you

1. What show rate did past events have?
2. What shift length can you realistically ask of mentors?
3. Will the overnight stretch be staffed by on-call mentors, or does someone need to be physically in the room?
