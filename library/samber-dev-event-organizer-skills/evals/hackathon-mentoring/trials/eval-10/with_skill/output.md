**Verdict:** Half the plan is wrong. Keep the roaming and fix how it is coordinated. Don't double the headcount, and don't go request-only.

## Why the plan misses

1. **Your three failures are coordination gaps, not depth gaps.**
   - The re-explaining, the midnight-to-6am dead zone, and the three visits in ten minutes all share one cause. Nobody can see who has visited whom.
   - Doubling mentors adds more overlapping visitors. The re-explaining gets worse.
   - The skill says a coverage-depth change cannot fix this. Only a shared view of who has been where can.
2. **Request-only throws away what teams loved.**
   - Roaming works because nobody has to notice they are stuck first. Several teams said they loved it.
   - Request-only favors the confident teams. Quiet or exhausted teams at 3am stop getting help.
   - The skill says to keep push coverage and rate-limit it. The two approaches solve different problems.
3. **"Double it" is a headcount, and the skill refuses headcount sizing.**
   - It rejects invented mentor-to-participant ratios because nothing on the day contradicts them.
   - The scarce thing is the right expertise in the right hours, not mentor-hours in aggregate.
   - Size coverage against the brief's tracks and the hours each track is worked.

## What to do instead

1. **Rate-limit roaming** with four mechanics from the skill:
   - A per-visit time cap with a timer the team can see. Relax it only for hands-on work that can't be boxed.
   - A "do not disturb" signal the team controls, visible from across the room.
   - A published list of what each mentor actually knows, so a team can ask for one by name.
   - A shared live view of which teams are blocked and on what, plus who visited whom. An idle mentor goes to the next blocked team instead of re-visiting a team that is fine.
2. **Stop the re-explaining.** The skill doesn't prescribe this, so treat it as my extension. Put a one-line "where we are, what's stuck" note per team in the shared view, and have each visiting mentor read it and append to it before walking up.
3. **Add a request mechanic on top of roaming, not instead of it.**
   - Use identifiable mentors (marker plus a named role) as the default.
   - Add a request channel with organizer routing if the event is digital or hybrid, spans several rooms, or has a sponsor track that needs one named person.
   - Any routing needs at least two people who can work it. Never use one organizer's DMs, because that person sleeps at the hour with the most blocked teams.
   - Office hours are a supplement only, never the sole route on an overnight event.
4. **Decide the overnight hours on purpose.** Staffed, thinly staffed, or published as unstaffed. Publish whichever you pick. Add a visit cadence per mentor for their assigned teams, and stop scheduled visits before the final stretch.
5. **Spend extra recruiting effort on tags, not bodies.**
   - Tag each mentor by the brief's tracks and by sponsor technology by name.
   - Record depth as either "has built with it" or "has read the docs".
   - If you add mentors, add them where a track or hour has no tagged mentor.
