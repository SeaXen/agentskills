## Why the 09:50 lock fails

1. **It cancels Ilkka's job.** He walks the room checking that people have something to work on. The skill ranks that "floor sweep" as the default safety net. Its first pass is the moment the pitch round ends, and it can only place someone on a team that is short-handed or on a stocked project. If membership froze at 09:50, the team with room for them is no longer allowed to take them. With 180 signups and a pitch round, some people will be unteamed at 09:50. A freeze leaves them to the room, or to working solo.
2. **A roster snapshot would stop the ringer; a lock isn't needed.** The ringer problem happens late in the day. A lock at 09:50 and a lock at 13:30 both stop someone walking in at 20:00. Only the later cut-off leaves time for placement.
3. **Ilkka alone is thin for 180 people.** The sweep needs two inputs nobody produces automatically:
   - Written rosters, so Ilkka knows which teams have room. Capture them when the round ends, at 09:50.
   - A stocked backlog of projects that someone can join and start on in under 20 minutes.

   If Ilkka is the only free person in the window, the escalation step drops out. Say so now rather than pretending a rota exists.

## What I'd recommend instead

1. **Size rule:** publish "Teams can be 1-4 people". It states the cap and answers the solo question in the same line. The 4 is one organizer network's observation, not a study, so you can change it. Check it against how your prizes divide.

   - Rule it before it opens: nobody pitches a project they won't build.

3. **Safety net:** Ilkka runs the floor sweep, with a backlog behind it.

4. **Change policy:** open until a published cut-off, then frozen. Take the 09:50 snapshot as the baseline roster. After that, any change goes through Ilkka and gets written down on the roster. That is your audit trail against a late ringer.
