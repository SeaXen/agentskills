**Partly. The anti-ringer goal is sound, but locking at 09:50 sharp breaks team formation and strands people.**

## What breaks

1. **Pitches end at 09:50, so teams haven't formed yet.**
   - Pitches are how people pick a project. Nobody can choose a team until they've heard all of them.
   - With a lock at 09:50, either the lock is fiction or teams were fixed before the pitches. Then the pitches pick nothing.
   - Teams realistically need 30 to 45 minutes to form.

2. **A 14-hour day produces legitimate roster changes.**
   - Some people will arrive late, and with 180 signups, expect no-shows too.
   - Some teams will have a member quit at hour 4, and some will realise their idea is dead.
   - Some solo hackers will find no team. A hard lock pins every one of them to their state at 09:50.

3. **Ilkka can't fix what he finds.**
   - He can spot someone with nothing to work on. Under a hard lock, the only fix is breaking the rule.
   - Make that exception a defined path, with a named owner other than Ilkka. Otherwise it happens ad hoc and unfairly.

4. **"The second the pitch round ends" is hard to run.**
   - 180 people are in motion in one room. You'd need a roster form or desk already open and a rule for what counts as locked.

5. **A lock doesn't stop the real cheat.**
   - Someone can still do all the work for a team they're nominally in.
   - A late-joining ringer is a small risk if joins are visible and capped.

## What I'd do instead

1. **09:50 to 11:00: team formation.** Run a roster form or desk that records team name, members, and project. Cap team size at 4 or 5.
2. **11:00: soft lock.** Rosters are published. Changes after this go through one named organizer, not Ilkka.
3. **Late joins are allowed until about 14:00.**
   - They need the organizer's approval and must appear on the roster.
   - After 14:00, rosters are frozen except for removals. This keeps "ringer at 18:00" impossible without stranding anyone.
4. **Final roster check at about 18:00.** Each team confirms who is actually present. This drops no-shows and makes the judging list real.
5. **Add judging-time checks, which are the real defence against ringers.**
   - Every listed member must be present at the demo.
   - Judges can ask any member what they built.
   - Optionally, require that the repo has no commits from before 09:50.
