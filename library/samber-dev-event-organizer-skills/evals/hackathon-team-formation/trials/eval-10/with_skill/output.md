**Your plan changes in two places.** Fill all four slots and you lose a slot to a role that doesn't exist at your event, and you shut out solo hackers.

- **No fabricator.** The four-role list comes from a hardware-hackathon guide. Fabricator means wiring, 3D printing and laser cutting, which Meridian Hack doesn't have. Only the structure carries over: a few named functions plus one shared duty. The guide says keep functions and headcount separate. That way a three-person team can cover everything, and a four-person team can leave a function thin on purpose.
- **A cap of 4 is not four slots.** The "4" comes from one organizer network's observation, with no study behind it. The wording to copy is "Teams can be 1-4 people". The "1" answers the solo question in the same sentence. "One person per slot" would turn away solo entrants and make a team of 3 look incomplete.

## Teams

### Team size

**Teams can be 1–4 people.** Working alone is fine. Working in a team of two, three or four is fine. Each team submits one project.

Organizers, volunteers, judges and sponsors can build for fun but are not eligible for prizes.

### What a good team covers

A team of four is a ceiling. Nobody has to fill four seats. What matters is that these three jobs get done by someone, and one job is shared by everyone.

| Function | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Writing the web or mobile app: front end, back end, integrations, deployment. |
| **Design** | How the app looks and feels: screens, user flow, visual polish. |
| **Direction** | Keeping the scope realistic, tracking the clock, and preparing the demo and pitch. |
| **Shared by everyone: testing and debugging** | Someone is always trying to break the app before the judges do. Nobody owns this alone. |

How this works in practice:

- **Solo or two people.** Cover the three functions between you. Cut scope rather than skip design or the demo.
- **Three people.** Often one person per function, with everyone testing.
- **Four people.** Add a second builder (a web or mobile split works well) or a second designer. You don't need a fourth function.
- **Missing a function?** Say so when you join a team or pitch. Teams that need a designer, or designers who need a team, are matched at [mechanic, time and place: to be filled in].

You don't need to call yourself a "hacker" to be useful. Designers, writers, product thinkers and people new to coding are all welcome. A team of only programmers usually has no design and no demo.
