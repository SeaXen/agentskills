## Rule text for the brief

**Students Cup track**

The Students Cup is open to any registered team. Teams don't apply separately, and a team stays eligible for every other track.

**Eligibility.** A team is eligible if at least half of its members are currently enrolled students. Half is counted at check-in:

| Team size | Students needed |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 2 |
| 4 | 2 |

If a team ends up with an odd number of members, round up. Extend the table if your size rule allows teams larger than 4.

**Who counts as a student.** A member counts if they are currently enrolled at a school, college or university, on the day of the event. Graduates and people who start a course after the event don't count.

**How we verify.** Each member who claims student status shows a valid student card at check-in. Organizers tick a "student" box on the roster. We don't copy, photograph or store the card or any number on it. The tick box is the only record.

**Fixed at check-in.** Eligibility is set from the roster at [check-in close time]. Teams can't add the Students Cup claim after this point or fix a missing proof later.

**If membership changes.** Your team's membership can change until [change cut-off time, wall clock]. If a member leaves or joins before the cut-off, the team stays eligible only if at least half of the new roster is ticked student. Any new member who claims student status must show a student card to an organizer before the cut-off. After the cut-off, the roster is frozen and the Students Cup status is final.

**Organizers and judges.** Organizers, volunteers, judges and sponsors can't compete for the Students Cup.
