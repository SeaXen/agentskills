**Students Cup**

**What it is.** The Students Cup is a separate track at Riverstone Hack. Teams that meet the eligibility rule below compete for it alongside the main competition.

**Eligibility.** A team is eligible if at least half of its members are currently enrolled students. In numbers, twice the number of student members must be at least the team size. A team of 2 or 4 needs 1 or 2 students, and a team of 3 needs 2. A team of 5 needs 3.

**Who counts as a student.** A member counts if they are enrolled at a school, college, or university on the day of check-in. The institution must be recognized in its country. Graduates, alumni, and people on a break between enrolments do not count.

**Verification.**
1. Each member who claims student status shows a valid student card at check-in.
2. The card must show the member's name and a validity date that has not passed. An enrolment certificate dated this academic year is accepted if the member has no card.
3. A check-in volunteer ticks the "student" box next to that member's name on the roster. The tick is the only record. We do not copy, photograph, or store card details.
4. A member without a valid document at check-in is not counted as a student. Eligibility is not checked again later.

**Team composition.**
- Eligibility is assessed once, on the roster as it stands at check-in.
- Adding or swapping members after check-in requires organizer approval. The new member must show a card, and the team must still meet the half-or-more rule.
- A team that drops below the threshold after approval loses its Students Cup eligibility.

**Judging and prize.**
- Eligible teams are judged on the same criteria as all other teams, and a team that meets the eligibility rule is not an automatic winner.
- The Students Cup goes to the highest-ranked eligible team.
- An eligible team stays in the main competition and can also win main prizes.
