**Verdict:** the rule is publishable, but it leaves several holes. Fix the five blockers below before you push it live.

## Blockers

1. **Team membership has no lock date.**
   - Kits go per member, so a team that wins can add friends after the result to claim more kits.
   - Fix: the roster is final at the submission deadline, and kits go only to people on that roster.

2. **One person can sit on several teams.**
   - Nothing stops someone joining two teams, or a group of 8 splitting into two teams of 4 that submit near-identical projects.
   - Fix: "Each person belongs to exactly one team."

3. **"Track winner" is undefined.**
   - What happens on a tie?
   - Can one team win several tracks and collect several kits each?
   - Is there an overall winner on top of the track winners?
   - Fix: "One winning team per track. Each person receives at most one kit, even if the team wins several tracks."

4. **Your kit exposure is unknown, so "buy in advance" is risky.**
   - Maximum kits = number of tracks × 4. With 6 tracks, that is 24 kits.
   - There is no minimum team size, so solo and duo winners leave kits unused.
   - Fix: buy for the worst case only if one SKU is cheap enough to hold as leftover swag. Otherwise buy for a likely case (for example 3 per team) and state a fallback. The fallback can be a spare-kit reserve or a published substitute prize.

5. **"People" is ambiguous.**
   - Do mentors, remote participants, and sponsor staff count toward the 4?
   - Are sponsor staff or judges eligible to win?
   - Fix: define a team member as a registered participant. Exclude organizers, judges, and sponsor staff from prizes.

## Worth deciding

- **Judging load.** There is no minimum size and 240 people are expected. That means 60 teams at the cap and over 100 if many go solo or in pairs. Decide on a submission cap or a judging plan, or set a minimum of 2.
- **Shipping and eligibility.** If winners can be remote or abroad, say who pays for shipping and customs, or restrict kits to on-site pickup.
- **Published rules.** Put the team size, prize and eligibility terms in one "official rules" page. Since you're in France, check whether the prize terms need a formal règlement.

## Suggested wording

> Teams have 1 to 4 members. Each participant joins exactly one team. Team rosters are final at the submission deadline. Each track has one winning team. Every roster member of a winning team receives one hardware kit, with a maximum of one kit per person across all tracks. Organizers, judges, and sponsor staff are not eligible.
