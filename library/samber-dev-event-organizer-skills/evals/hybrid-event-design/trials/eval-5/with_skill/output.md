## 5. Two-audience disruption rule (proposed, unsigned)

Documented defaults:
- Room keeps going. Fix a dead feed in parallel. Do not pause on reflex.
- The call to give up on the feed sits in the technical chain and escalates to a senior organizer. A volunteer is never alone with it.
- On failure, post in channel: what broke, what still works, where to watch.

Two items have no documented answer, so you must decide them:
- **Same technician needed by the room lead and the remote advocate.** Proposal: the room wins while a session is live, and the feed wins at gaps. Decided by: `<name>`.
- **A session moved to fix an in-room problem.** Proposal: it keeps its remote slot only if it stays in the streamed room. Otherwise remote gets a holding card plus a channel line. Decided by: `<name>`.
