Not good enough. Skeleton is right, but it has no hard limits, no hybrid rule, and no decision owner. Run the day on the revised version below.

## Gaps

1. **No hard constraints.** Safety, room capacity, accessibility, interpretation and speaker availability must sit above everything. Today a keynote fix could break any of them.
2. **Nothing about the two streamed rooms.**
   - A move in a streamed room hits remote viewers, time zones, captions, recording and stream crew.
   - Crew and encoders can't be in two places.
   - The order treats a streamed session like an in-room one.
3. **"Headline commitment" is undefined.**
   - Who decides which session it is?
   - Sponsor-paid slots, speaker travel limits and contracts are not ranked.
   - Decided live, this becomes an argument.
4. **"Minimize sessions disturbed" counts sessions, not people.** Moving one streamed session with many remote viewers costs more than moving three small in-room ones. A count also ties often and has no tiebreak.
5. **"Lock the grid" is missing steps.**
   - No owner and no deadline.
   - No comms path to the app, signage, stream platform, speakers and volunteers.
   - No rule for a second disruption after the lock.
6. **No decision owner or time limit.** Who can say yes in the room, and within how many minutes?
7. **No cascade limit.** Fixes can spill into breaks, lunch, catering and the next block.

## Revised order

0. **Hard limits.** Safety, capacity, accessibility, interpretation, speaker availability. Never traded away.
1. **Plenary or keynote.** Protect for both audiences, in-room and remote.
2. **Named commitments.** Use a list fixed before the day (headline session, sponsor-paid slots, contracts). No live debate.
3. **Streamed-room integrity.** Prefer swaps inside the same room. Move a streamed session only with a stream-crew check and a remote notice.
4. **Least disruption, weighted by people.** Rank by in-room plus remote audience affected, not by session count. Prefer swaps over cascades. Cap the fix at one block.
5. **Lock and announce.** One owner, time-boxed (suggest 10 minutes). Push to app, signage, stream platform, speakers and volunteers at once. Timestamp the grid version. Any new disruption after the lock reopens only steps 0–1.

- Write the commitment list for step 2.
- Get from the stream crew a list of what each streamed room can and can't change.
