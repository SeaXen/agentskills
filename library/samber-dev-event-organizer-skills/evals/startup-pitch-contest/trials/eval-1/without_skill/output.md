**Startup Stage numbers for the announcement**

| Item | Number |
|---|---|
| Pitch length | 5 min, hard stop |
| Q&A per company | 3 min |
| Changeover | 1 min |
| Finalists on stage | 6 |
| Judges | 3 |
| Prize budget | €10,000 total |

**Timing, 90 minutes**

1. Opening and rules: 4 min.
2. Six slots of 9 min (5 pitch, 3 Q&A, 1 changeover): 54 min.
3. Judges deliberate while a sponsor or host fills the gap: 8 min.
4. Awards: 5 min.
5. Buffer: 19 min.

The buffer is larger than usual on purpose. Founders overrun, and a main stage with 600 people has slow walk-ons and AV problems. If you want it tighter, 7 finalists fit (63 min, 12 min buffer). I'd still announce 6, because you can add a 7th later but you can't cut one after announcing.

**Judges: 3**
- Three judges give you a majority vote with no tie-breaker.
- With only 3 min of Q&A, five judges can't all ask a question.
- Pick one investor, one infrastructure practitioner (a buyer) and one operator or founder. Avoid sponsors' own staff, so the result looks clean.
- Optionally add a 10% audience vote through the event app, so the room feels involved. Don't let it decide the result.

**Prize budget: about €10,000**
- 1st place: €5,000.
- 2nd place: €2,500.
- 3rd place: €1,000.
- Remaining €1,500: trophies, a finalist travel contribution, and the awards moment itself.

For early-stage infrastructure founders, in-kind prizes often matter more than cash. These cost you nothing:
- A free booth at next year's Northbound.
- Intros to your sponsors.
- Cloud credits.

Ask a sponsor to fund the cash, and consider naming the prize after them.

**Caveats**
- I assumed a fixed 90-minute window with no slack before or after it. If the stage needs a reset or a sponsor video, take that out of the buffer.
- The prize figures are my judgment, not an industry standard. Confirm the budget with marketing before you publish amounts. I'd announce "cash prizes plus in-kind support" if the funding isn't signed yet.
- Fix the finalist selection now. With 6 slots, you need an application deadline and a shortlist date before the announcement goes out.
