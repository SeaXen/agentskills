**Short answer:** Don't open a call you won't judge. You can fill the slate from your own list, but not behind a public "open to any early-stage company" promise.

## Why not

- ❌ **Applicants are misled.** They spend time on pitches, decks, and maybe travel for a decision that was made in advance.
- ❌ **"The bar was high this year" is a false statement.** It's a specific claim you'd make to many people, and it will be sent as rejection emails.
- ⚠️ **It leaks.** Six picks from your network will be recognizable, and founders compare notes. One of the six could mention how they got in. The cost is your credibility with the whole startup community, long after this slate.
- ⚠️ **You promised something publicly.** The site copy is a standing commitment. Quietly ignoring it is the part that does the damage.

## What you can do instead

1. 🎯 **Curate openly (recommended).** Change the page to "The Ashgrove startup stage is curated by the organizing team. Want to be considered? Write to us." Then invite your six. This is common, honest, and costs you nothing but one edit. Do it before announcing the slate, and don't pretend the old text never existed.
2. **Run a real call with reserved slots.** Say so up front: "Six slots are curated, N slots come from open applications." You keep your six, and you get a pipeline of new names.
3. **Run a short, real call.** Two weeks, a simple form, and a clear criterion. Your six apply or are fast-tracked, and you judge everyone on the stated criteria. If your six win, fine. If someone else is better, you'll be glad you looked.

## If you've already received applications

Reply to them honestly. Something like: "We've decided to curate this year's slate directly and won't be selecting from open applications. Thank you for applying." Don't cite a quality bar you didn't apply.

## Suggested wording if you go with option 1

> **Update:** The Ashgrove startup stage is curated by the organizing team this year. We're not running an open call. If you'd like us to consider your company for a future edition, contact us at [email].

## Bottom line

You don't need to run a full open call. You do need to either run one for real or stop advertising one. Everything between those two is the deception.
