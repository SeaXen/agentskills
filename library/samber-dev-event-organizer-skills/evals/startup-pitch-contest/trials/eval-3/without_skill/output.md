**Verdict:** the weights add up to 100, so the arithmetic works. Three things need changing before you publish.

**1. Traction at 35 is too heavy for what you collect.**
- The form asks for name, website, and 300 characters. That gives judges nothing to check traction against, so they will score it on whatever founders claim on stage.
- If any applicants are pre-revenue, a 35% weight sinks them whatever else is strong.
- Fix: cut it to 25, and define it relative to stage (see point 3).

**2. Market size at 20 is the weakest signal.**
- It is the easiest number to inflate in a pitch, and judges can't verify it live.
- Fix: cut it to 10.

**3. You have no product or problem criterion.**
- Nothing scores whether the solution is real, differentiated, or solves a sharp problem. Wow factor (10) is a poor stand-in.
- Fix: add "Problem and product" at 20.

**Proposed weights (sum 100):**

|---|---|---|
| Traction | 35 | 25 |
| Problem and product | – | 20 |
| Team | 20 | 20 |
| Delivery and storytelling | 15 | 15 |
| Market size | 20 | 10 |
| Wow factor | 10 | 10 |

**Other fixes to make before publishing:**
1. **Write anchors for 0, 5, and 10 on each criterion.** Without them, a "7" means something different to each judge. For traction, define it per stage, for example "paying customers", "active pilots", or "waitlist with evidence".
2. **State the formula.** For example, score ÷ 10 × weight, summed to a total out of 100.
3. **Normalize per judge, or drop each criterion's high and low scores.** On a 0–10 scale, one harsh or generous judge can decide the result.
4. **Pick a tie-break rule**, such as the higher Traction score or a judges' re-vote.
5. **Match the form to the rubric.** Add one optional field for a traction metric, or tell applicants the 300-character description is the only screening input. Right now the form can't support a traction-led rubric.
6. **Check the rubric against the Ferrolith Conf audience.** I don't know the conf's theme, so I couldn't check whether the criteria fit it.

**One question that could change the weights:** what stage are the applicants? If most are pre-revenue, drop Traction to 20 and move 5 points to Team or Problem and product.
