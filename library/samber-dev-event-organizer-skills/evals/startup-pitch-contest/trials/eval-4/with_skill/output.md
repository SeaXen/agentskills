Tighten these points when you fill it in:

- **Name the entity**, not "Halberd Ventures" in general.
- **Publish the terms or link to them.** Otherwise applicants are deciding on an award they can't read.
- **"Convertible" needs no other adjectives.** Don't add "investment", "funding", or "grant" unless counsel agrees.

**Option B: if the written commitment doesn't arrive by Monday.** Don't mention capital. Either delay the call, or go live with the contest, criteria, weights, and route, and publish the prize later:
