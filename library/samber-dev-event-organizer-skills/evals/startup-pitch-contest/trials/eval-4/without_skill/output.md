## Wording problems

Your draft: *"Winner receives a 25k EUR convertible from Halberd Ventures"*

1. **"Receives" overpromises.** A convertible is an investment instrument, not a prize. It usually needs a signed note, due diligence, and a company that can legally take it. The winner may be unincorporated, outside the EU, or not interested.
2. **"25k EUR" reads as cash.** Applicants will assume a check. The headline should say it's an investment offer.
3. **The terms are missing.** Cap, discount, maturity, and conversion trigger are all unspecified. If Halberd hasn't set them, say "on standard terms to be agreed" and don't imply they're fixed.
4. **"Winner" is ambiguous.** Is it one winner? What if the winner is ineligible or declines? Does the offer have conditions?
5. **"Convertible" may be unclear.** Some applicants won't know it means a convertible note or SAFE. Spell out the instrument once.
6. **Format.** Write "€25,000" or "EUR 25,000", not "25k EUR".
