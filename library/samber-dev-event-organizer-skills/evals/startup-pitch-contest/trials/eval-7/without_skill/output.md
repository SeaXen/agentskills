**Short answer: no. The application window is the problem.**

**Dates checked (today is Fri 16 Oct 2026)**
- Mon 19 Oct: publish the call.
- Tue 20 Oct: close applications. That is **one day** open. If you meant 20 Nov, it falls after the event.
- Tue 27 Oct: notify the selected companies. This leaves a one-week review, as you planned.
- Sat 14 Nov: the summit. That is 29 days from today, so four weeks, not five.

**What breaks**
1. **A one-day call gets almost no applicants.** Companies need time to find the call, get sign-off, and write the application. You would only get people already watching you.
2. **Selected companies get 18 days to prepare.** That is fine for a pitch, but not if travel, a contract, or a deck approval is involved.
3. **There is no buffer.** If a selected company drops out, you have no time to replace them.

**Proposed schedule (keeps the 14 Nov date)**

| Date | Step |
|---|---|
| Sat 17 – Sun 18 Oct | Write the rules, eligibility, judging rubric, and prize or terms. |
| Mon 19 Oct | Publish the call and personally invite 10–20 target companies. |
| Thu 29 Oct | Close applications. That is 10 days open, the shortest I'd go. |
| Fri 30 Oct – Mon 2 Nov | Review. Two reviewers each score every application against the rubric. |
| Tue 3 Nov | Notify the selected companies and 1–2 alternates. |
| Wed 4 – Thu 12 Nov | Prep calls, deck check, and a tech check. |
| Sat 14 Nov | Pitch contest. |

This gives you a 10-day application window, 4 days for review, and 11 days of prep for the finalists.

**Things to lock before Monday**
- **Judging rubric.** Write it first so review is fast and defensible.
- **Judges.** Confirm them before you publish, since the call will mention them.
- **Terms.** Cover prize, eligibility, IP, and who pays travel. The board approved the contest only yesterday, so check whether these were decided.
- **Number of slots and pitch length.** These set how many companies you select.
- **Alternates.** Name 1–2 so a dropout doesn't break the schedule.

**Questions for you**
1. Did you mean 20 Oct or 20 Nov for the close? I assumed 20 Oct from the "week to review" gap.
2. Are the terms, prize, and judges already settled?
3. How many pitch slots do you have?

If you can't extend the call, the minimum viable version is to close Wed 28 Oct and notify Mon 2 Nov. That only works if you invite target companies directly on Monday.
