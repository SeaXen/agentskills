# Verdict: ❌ Not fine. Don't announce it in the WhatsApp group the morning of.

The stream and clips can still happen. They need a written notice and a yes from each pitching company first.

## Why the WhatsApp plan fails

- **This is a hard gate, not a preference.** Recording or streaming that applicants weren't told about before applying can't be fixed afterwards. Either the room isn't recorded, or every applicant is told and re-consents before the block.
- **Founders control the distribution of what they say on a pitch stage only once.** Pitches include traction claims they would not publish elsewhere. A YouTube clip is permanent, and a company can't take back what it said.
- **A morning-of message isn't consent.** By then the founders have rearranged their month and travelled. Declining means losing the slot, so "fine by everyone" would be forced agreement.
- **The form is silent, so this changes the terms.** Applicants entered under terms with no recording in them. Treat the livestream and clips as an amendment to those terms.
- **There is no time to backfill.** The published floor for people to arrange travel or time off is at least 4-6 weeks. You are already inside it. A company that declines in week 3 can't be replaced, so decide the decline path now.

## What to do, in order

1. **Pause the commitment to the newsroom.** Say yes in principle and tell them it depends on each company's consent. Don't confirm the date or the clip list yet.
2. **Check where the slate stands.** Have accepted companies been notified?
   - **If not yet notified,** put the recording terms in the acceptance notice. Companies then accept with full knowledge.
   - **If already notified,** send a separate amendment that asks for a fresh, explicit reply.
3. **Fix the newsroom terms in writing before you ask anyone.** These are your terms, not facts from the skill:
   - What is streamed: the whole pitch block, or only some slots.
   - Who edits the clips, and who picks which companies get one.
   - Whether a founder can ask for a clip to be pulled afterwards, and how fast.
   - How long the footage is kept.
   - A clause that the newsroom has no say in who pitches or who wins, and receives no slot in exchange for coverage. A stage slot in front of investors is a speaking slot, and it is not for sale.
4. **Send each pitching company a written notice.** Use email or the application channel, not the group chat. State the facts: what is streamed, who the outlet is, where the clips will live, and that clips may stay online. Ask for an explicit yes or no by a set date.
5. **Decide the "no" path before you ask.** A company that declines must not lose its slot or score. Two options:
   - **It pitches off-stream.** Check with the newsroom first. The stream must be able to cut away for that slot, and the audience camera must not catch the stage.
   - **It withdraws without penalty.** Pick this option only if the cut-away is not possible.

   Publish the choice in the notice so nobody has to guess.
6. **Brief the judges in writing, before they see any application.**
   - Say that the session is recorded, so judges and their remarks will be on camera.
   - Ask each investor judge to confirm in writing that their institution allows them to be filmed.
   - Don't interpret their institution's rules yourself.
7. **Handle the audience separately.** Signage at the entrance and an announcement before the block. Send the legal side to whoever holds your data obligations, or to counsel. If nobody holds that role, that is a finding to report. I state no legal rule here.
8. **Only after the replies are in,** confirm the date and the clip plan with the newsroom. Then use the WhatsApp group for a reminder only. It carries no consent and no news.

If you say no, you keep your slot and your score: you pitch off-stream [or: you may withdraw with no consequence]. The newsroom has no say in who pitches or who wins.

3. Will clips cover every pitcher or only some? If only some, who chooses?
4. Does the newsroom get anything from you in exchange for the coverage?
5. Will investor judges be on camera?
