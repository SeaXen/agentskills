**No, that's not fine.** Tell speakers now, in writing, and let them opt out. A WhatsApp message the morning of the event doesn't work, for four reasons.

- **They never agreed to this.** They applied to pitch to a room. A livestream plus permanent YouTube clips is a bigger, different use of their image, voice, and pitch.
- **The harm can't be undone.** Once clips are online, you can't pull them back. A founder with unannounced pricing, customers, or fundraising details in the deck can't fix that afterwards.
- **A morning-of notice gives people no real choice.** They've already travelled, prepared, and slotted in, so "opt out" means dropping out of the event. That is pressure, not consent.
- **You carry the risk, not the newsroom.** If the venue is in the EU, as I assume (you're in Nantes), image and voice are personal data and need prior information. For online publication, explicit consent is safest. I'm not a lawyer, so confirm with whoever holds the legal role for the event.

You can still get this coverage. You have three weeks, which is enough time to do it properly.

**Plan**

1. **Today:** Ask the newsroom for a short filming and publishing agreement. It should cover these points:
   - Speakers who decline won't be filmed or shown.
   - They will remove a clip on request.
   - They won't edit clips in a misleading way.
   - They will credit the speakers.
2. **This week:** Email every speaker. WhatsApp is not enough, because not everyone reads it and it isn't a record. Use the draft below.
3. **Opt-in or opt-out:** Ask for an explicit yes or no by a deadline (T-10 days). Treat silence as a no for the stream and clips until you hear back.
4. **For speakers who decline:** Offer a non-streamed slot or camera-off pitch, or "stream yes, clips no". Make sure declining never costs them their place.
5. **T-7 days:** Send a reminder to non-responders, plus the final list of who is filmed.
6. **Day before:** Brief the newsroom on the no-film list. Put signage at the door and on stage, and tell the audience too.
7. **Morning of:** Use WhatsApp only as a reminder ("streaming starts at X, the no-film list is respected").
8. **Update the page:** Add a recording notice to the event page and the speaker terms for next time.
