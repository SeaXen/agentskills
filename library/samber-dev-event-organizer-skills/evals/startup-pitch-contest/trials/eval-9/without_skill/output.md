**Verdict: it mostly holds together. The morning is fine, but the gap between 12:00 and 14:00 and the room and tech setup are underspecified.** ⚠️

## What works
- ✅ Mentoring before pitching is the right order.
- ✅ A half-hour buffer after arrival is sensible.
- ✅ Six mentors for 2.5 hours is a good ratio if you have about 6 to 10 startups. I don't know your number, so check it (see the questions).

## What doesn't hang together
1. **Mentors can't get a demo working.** They can sharpen a deck, but a working demo needs the venue's Wi-Fi, the projector, adapters, power and the stage laptop. Nothing in the plan covers a stage tech check.
2. **There's no rehearsal on the real stage.** Startups pitch at 14:00 without having stood on it. Add a 10-minute slot per startup, with slides loaded and the clicker tested.
3. **Breakout room and prep room are unclear.** Office hours use the breakout room, so the prep room has no job. The prep room should be where teams fix decks and demos between mentor sessions. Without it, teams stay in the mentoring room, which blocks mentors and prevents hands-on work.
4. **Mentoring has no structure.** 150 minutes with no rounds means some teams get three mentors and others none. Use fixed slots (below).
5. **12:00 to 14:00 is empty.** There's no lunch, no final rehearsal, and no buffer if the morning runs late.
6. **The pitch format is missing.** Pitch length, Q&A, order, who judges, and who keeps time are all undefined. 14:00 is also the post-lunch slump, and 400 attendees may be elsewhere. Decide whether this is a main-stage slot or a side track.
7. **Wi-Fi is the biggest risk.** A 400-person conference network will fail live demos. Each startup needs a recorded fallback video or an offline build.

## Suggested revision
| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 09:00 | Arrival, badges, meet the organizer |
| 09:15 | Mentor and startup briefing: goals, format, rules (15 min) |
| 09:30–11:30 | Mentor rounds: 4 slots of 25 min, 5 min to switch. Teams rotate between the breakout room and the prep room. |
| 11:30–12:15 | Hands-on fixing in the prep room, with an AV/tech volunteer on hand |
| 12:15–13:15 | Lunch (mentors join startups) |
| 13:15–13:50 | Stage tech check, 5 to 10 min per startup, in pitch order |
| 14:00 | Pitches |
| After | Judging, awards, networking with attendees |

For six startups and six mentors, you can run six 25-minute rounds instead. That takes the full 150 minutes with no building time, so I prefer four rounds plus a fix block.
