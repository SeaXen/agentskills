## 1. Existence posture and owner

**Recommended posture: archive-only feed, launched in April, with no stated rhythm.** This comes from the skill's default for a volunteer team with no named quiet-month owner.

| Candidate shape | Quiet-month cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Fortnightly original podcast** (the vote's proposal) | Highest. Someone books, vets and publishes a guest every two weeks, for twelve months. | ❌ Rejected. It is a standing duty with no owner. The skill deletes both original rungs when no one is named. |
| **Low-cadence original** (slower, tied to the edition cycle) | Medium. One person must find, book and vet a guest every cycle. | ⏳ Not available yet. It opens when one person has recurring quiet-month hours and you agree the show should outlive the current team. |
| **Archive-only feed** | Near zero. You already produce the recordings and transcripts. | ✅ Recommended. A named feed with a purpose line, publishing what you already make, as it's finished. |
| **No channel** | Zero. | ⚠️ The fallback if nobody takes the owner role *and* the audio cut has nowhere to land. |

**Why this honours the vote.** The April start still happens. The March recordings reach Vimeo within a month, so the feed can open in April with audio cuts of those talks. The part we hold back is the promise of a rhythm and original episodes.

**To answer the dissenting voter:** this shape has no booking calendar and no cadence promise. It is the least a "no" vote can object to, and the cheapest to reverse.

**Owner for March to next March.** None named yet. This is the blocking item:

- [ ] Their recurring quiet-month hours: ______ (their number, not a guess from me)

Edition-week hours don't count. Ask the owner what the weeks right after an edition look like, since that's when everyone recovers.

**Rungs rejected, for the record:**

## 2. Cadence commitment

Publish only a sentence the owner has agreed to. A template:

> "We publish the audio of our talks as they are finished after each March edition. Hosted by **[owner]**. If a release slips, we say so in the feed."

The sentence names what the show publishes, its rhythm (tied to the edition), who owns it, and what happens on a miss. "New episodes every other week!" has no owner, no agreed rhythm and no missed-cycle behaviour.

**For the two organizers who want the fortnightly rhythm on the website.** Their wish is the rung the skill deletes. The route to get there:
1. Run the archive-only feed through the spring and summer.
2. Count what you actually published between editions.
3. The owner then decides whether a rhythm is holdable.
4. Only then does the website change.

The skill sets no sourced interval, so I won't invent one. A miss that repeats is a signal to demote the posture, not a discipline problem.

**If you later add original episodes, start with the cheapest format.**
1. Unedited live stream, audio reused for the feed. This skips editing, but nothing can be cut afterwards, so the release must cover live publication and the audio use. It still needs a named publisher.
2. Short clips from recordings.
3. Produced podcast, last. Organizers call it a very hard step.

## 3. Guest pipeline

The archive-only feed has **no guests to book**, so this section is dormant until you move to an original rung. Prepare it now so the owner doesn't start from zero.

**Default source: your own speakers.** Past and confirmed-upcoming DevFest speakers already have a relationship, a register match and prior vetting.

Build the list with these columns:
- speaker name
- date last spoke
- topic
- **allowed to contact for something other than the event? (yes/no)**

Teams skip the last column, and it decides whether this source is usable. Permission to email about their talk is not permission to invite them onto a show. I don't know your pool size yet. If it can't support the cadence you pick, the next rung is an open pitch, but only once a vetting owner exists.

**Vetting owner:** ______ (one named person)

**Vetting bar** (the skill's own construction, not an observed standard):
1. Does the guest have something specific to say on a topic your audience recognises?
2. Is there anything in the public record you'd be answering for after publishing?
3. Has anyone on the team worked with them, and what did they say?
4. Does the guest understand the recording will be published, and how?

Your code of conduct binds the show exactly as it binds the room. Route the conduct questions to `event-code-of-conduct`.

**Rejected:**
- Guest slots given because a sponsor paid. That is a sponsorship decision, disclosed as one, not a sourcing rung.
- Open pitch with no vetting owner.
- Cold outbound. It needs a compounding intent and hours that return nothing for several cycles.

## 4. Release instrument

This is the item most likely to hurt you if skipped.

- **A stage consent does not cover an episode.** Your speakers agreed to be recorded delivering a talk, in a stated format. Whether that covers an audio-only cut on a podcast feed is a question for your consent record, not an assumption.
- Before April, check what your existing consent record actually covers. If it doesn't clearly cover audio publication on a standing feed, don't publish those cuts until it's resolved. Route the wording to your own counsel.
- For any original episode: collect a **separate episode-specific release** from every participant, hosts included, before recording. It must cover the use the episode makes of the recording, including live publication for streams.
- **Release custodian:** ______ (a named person, written into the handover below)

I name no jurisdiction and quote no retention period. Wording, music licensing for intro or outro audio, platform terms and hosting retention are for counsel.

## 5. Feed relationship

The skill requires one line per path, marked *exists* or *aspirational*.

```
conference -> channel   EXISTS       talk recordings arrive within a month, with transcripts on the blog
conference -> channel   EXISTS       speakers are the booking pool, subject to the contact-permission column
channel    -> conference ASPIRATIONAL show notes link to the ticket page; nobody has measured whether anyone follows
channel    -> speakers   ASPIRATIONAL a visible channel makes prospective speakers want to come; practitioner hypothesis, untested
channel    -> swap       EXISTS      episodes can serve as inventory in a cross-promotion with another organizer
```

- **Don't promise the podcast will fill seats.** Nothing in the skill's record supports a conversion figure. Argue reach and relationship value instead.
- **Test the speaker loop cheaply.** Add one optional question to your next call for speakers: "Did our published videos play a part in your decision?" Count the yes answers, don't compute a rate. Keep that path *aspirational* until your own answers support it.
- **Your existing Vimeo page is a flagship-only pattern risk.** Publishing only around the March edition leaves the quiet months empty. The archive-only feed is a legitimate answer to that, as long as nobody promised more.

**Checklist:**
1. Complete and date the inventory.
2. Move every account off any individual's personal ownership, with a second named holder on each. Adding a second admin while a personal account stays owner of record is the failure this prevents.
3. Name the release custodian and write the retention decision beside the records.
4. Confirm every episode still resolves at its own address after any transfer.
5. Record what the show has promised publicly.
6. Set a re-check date: **______** (not "the next resignation").

**Recommended handover rung:** documented ownership and access transfer. It's an afternoon's work and prevents the failure that orphans a show. The other rungs:
- A named successor with overlap would fit if a departing organizer's last month is known.
- Folding into a closed archive is the exit if nobody will own the quiet months going forward, *and* the back-catalogue is worth keeping. In that case post a dated closing note with the final location.
- Never let the feed lapse silently.

## 7. Gates and self-set measures

The plan is not done unless all three gates pass, with no exceptions.

| Gate | Pass condition | Status today |
|---|---|---|
| **Release coverage** | Every published episode has a release (or confirmed stage-consent coverage) from every participant, on file with a named holder | ⏳ Open: consent coverage unchecked, custodian unnamed |
| **Named quiet-month owner** | The posture has an owner, and the public cadence is what that person agreed to | ❌ Fails: nobody named |
| **Handover readiness** | The handover document exists, is dated, and names living holders | ⏳ Draft only: holders blank |

**Self-set measures** (the skill sets no numeric thresholds; write yours down before the cycle starts):
- **Cadence kept**: committed versus published, per cycle. A repeated miss means demote the posture.
- **Quiet-month publishing**: at least one item between editions, yes or no per gap.
- **Pipeline depth**: guests named and agreed ahead of the next publish date. Report the count, not a rate.
- **Back-catalogue reachability**: every episode still resolves after any account change.
- **Speaker-loop answers**: the count of speakers who said the videos played a part.
