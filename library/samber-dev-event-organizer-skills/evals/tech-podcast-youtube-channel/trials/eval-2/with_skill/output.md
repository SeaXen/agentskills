## On not going back to your lawyer

The skill routes release wording, music licensing, platform terms and retention to your own counsel. It deliberately names no jurisdiction and quotes no retention period. I can't certify wording for you.

You can keep the cost small:

- **Ask for one review of a one-page release template, once.** You then reuse it for every future episode. That is a single small ask, not one per episode.
- **Don't ask her to draft from scratch.** Bring her the decision list below so the review is short.
- If you skip her entirely, you are guessing at the wording. The risk is yours, and nothing here can tell you the wording is sound.

**Decisions the release must settle** (my construction, as a checklist and not as clause text):

1. The recording is published on your standing show, as video and as an audio feed, under the event's name.
2. Which derivatives are included: clips, audio-only, promotional cuts.
3. Whether the guest can withdraw, and what happens to published episodes if they do.
4. Who holds the signed record and for how long. Counsel decides the period.
5. Any music or intro material, and the terms you obtained it under.
6. Platform terms on your hosting account.

   - If you never asked, route there first. Four recorded episodes don't answer it.

## Posture and cadence

You recorded original interviews, so you are at the **low-cadence original** rung, not archive-only. That rung needs:

- **A named owner for the quiet months.** That is probably you, with recurring hours when nothing is being produced.
- **A compounding intent.** This is meant to outlive the current team.
- **A public promise no bigger than your owner agreed to.** For example: "We publish episodes around each edition, hosted by [name]. If a cycle slips we say so in the feed." Don't write "new episode every X" until the owner has agreed to it.

If you can't name the owner, the skill's answer is archive-only or no channel, by decision. An unowned original show with your event's name on it is worse than none. I don't recommend any interval, because none is sourced.
