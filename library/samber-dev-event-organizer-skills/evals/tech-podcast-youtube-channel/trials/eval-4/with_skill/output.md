# ShipIt Conf YouTube channel: board summary

**Bottom line.** The channel is a growing reach asset: 12,000 subscribers, with views up 40% year over year. We have not measured whether it sells tickets. We are setting up the measurement now so the next board update can say.

**What we know**

- 12,000 subscribers.
- Views are up 40% year over year.
- The ticket page is linked in every video description, so a path from channel to ticket page exists.

**What we do not know**

- How many viewers click the ticket link.
- How many of those clicks become ticket purchases.

So the channel-to-ticket path is **marked aspirational**. A link in every description shows the path is open. It does not show anyone walks it.

**What the channel does for the conference today** (these are the reasons to keep it, none depends on ticket conversion)

1. **Reach beyond the room.** Talks reach people who never attend.
2. **A durable home for recordings.** Every edition's talks stay searchable after the event.
3. **Speaker value.** A channel that visibly publishes gives prospective speakers a reason to come. This is a hypothesis we have not measured, so it is not a claim.
4. **Sponsor and partner inventory.** Episodes and videos give sponsors and cross-promotion partners something to attach to. [Confirm this applies to our sponsor packages.]

**How we will measure it** (self-set, declared before the next cycle)

- Add a tagged ticket link, such as a UTM parameter, to every video description, so clicks and purchases from YouTube are separable from other traffic.
- Report three counts at the next board update: ticket-page visits from YouTube, ticket orders from those visits, and the share of total orders they represent.
- Add one optional question at purchase: "How did you hear about ShipIt Conf?"
- Add one optional question to the speaker invitation: "Did our published videos play a part in your decision to speak?" Report it as a count, not a rate.

**Decision requested**

Approve continued investment in the channel on its reach and archive value. Treat ticket-sales attribution as a question the next cycle's data will answer.

**Risk to name.** Any channel can lapse if nobody owns it in the months between editions. Owner for the quiet months: [name]. Cadence we commit to publicly: [the one this person agreed to].

**Notes for you (not for the board)**

1. **"Top-of-funnel for ticket sales" is not defensible yet.** The skill's rule is not to promise the channel fills seats, because no sourced figure ties channel activity to registrations. A link in every description shows the path exists, not that it converts. A board member can ask, "what share of tickets came from YouTube?", and you would have no number. If you want the line, earn it with the tagged link and the purchase question above. After one edition's data you can write it, or drop it, with evidence.

2. **Do not cite the agency deck.** Millions of added views and 30,000 new subscribers are channel-growth numbers for a different conference. They sit next to a ticket-sales goal with no ticket or registration figure attached. That is the skill's named example of a reach claim presented as a sales claim. Using it would invite the same challenge, and it says nothing about ShipIt's conversion. The skill also says not to invent a benchmark, and I added none.

3. **"Views up 40%" needs a footnote.** Check what it covers (all videos, or only new uploads) and the period. A single edition's talk uploads can drive the whole number. I left it as you gave it.

4. **Brackets to fill.** I need the quiet-month owner and the cadence they agreed to. The skill treats a cadence stated without a named owner as a lapse nobody decided. I also left the sponsor-inventory line in brackets because I don't know if sponsors get anything from the channel today.

5. **Questions the skill would ask before a fuller channel plan**: who owns the show between editions, how large the speaker pool is, and whether the release records are in order. These are not needed for this page, but they matter if the board asks about cadence or risk.
