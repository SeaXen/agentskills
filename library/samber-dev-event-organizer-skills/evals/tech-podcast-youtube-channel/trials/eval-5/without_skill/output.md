**First action this week: do not make the volunteer an admin on her personal account. Move the assets to an account the team owns.**

## Why the second-admin plan fails

- **Ownership stays personal.** After she leaves, the show lives on an ex-colleague's personal Google account, tied to her card. The card expires or she closes it, and billing breaks.
- **Her identity stays attached.** Recovery email, phone and 2FA are hers. If Google flags the account, only she can unlock it.
- **Access is not capability.** Nobody has ever published an episode. A second admin can log in and still not know how to ship.
- **Whoever volunteers may not be the right owner.** Pick the owner by criteria (below).

You can still add a second admin in week 1 as a temporary safety net. Treat it as a stopgap, not the endpoint.

## Check first: your cadence

61 episodes in 4 years is about 1 every 3.4 weeks. That leaves roughly **3 episodes before she leaves**. The whole handover has to fit in those 3 episodes, so plan around them.

## Plan: 11 weeks

### Weeks 1–2: decide and secure

1. **Decide whether the show continues.** Options are continue, pause, or end with a final episode. All three are valid. Do not drift into continuing by default.
2. **Name the successor by criteria.** Look for time to spare (likely 3–5 hours per episode), some audio and video comfort, and a willingness to commit for a year or more. Also name a **backup** person.
3. **Pick the owning identity.** Use a shared team account such as a Workspace group or `podcast@yourdomain`, not one person's login. At least two humans must be able to recover it.
4. **Inventory everything she holds.** Ask her to list it all in a single doc, with no passwords in it:
   - YouTube channel and the monetization/AdSense setup
   - Podcast host account and its billing
   - Apple Podcasts Connect and Spotify listings
   - Domain, email, and artwork files
   - Raw recordings and project files
   - Recording tools, guest contact list, sponsors, and invoices

### Weeks 2–4: transfer ownership

- **YouTube.** In Studio, open Settings → Permissions. If an Owner role is offered, assign it to the team identity. If not, move the channel to a Brand Account (Settings → Channel → Advanced). That keeps subscribers and videos, and a Brand Account can have its primary owner changed. Verify the exact options in her Studio before relying on this.
- **Monetization.** AdSense is usually tied to the person. Check whether payments go to her personal profile. If they do, set up a team payout profile and expect some re-verification. Do this early, because payouts are the part most likely to break.
- **Podcast host.** Check whether it supports an organization account or an ownership transfer.
  - If it does, transfer it and put the team card on it.
  - If it doesn't, plan a migration to a new host. Keep the RSS feed working by setting a 301 redirect from the old feed, so Apple and Spotify keep your subscribers.
- **Directory listings.** Apple Podcasts Connect and Spotify are tied to the email on the feed. Change that email to the team address.
- **Billing.** Move every subscription to a team card or invoice.
- **Download everything.** Export an archive of all 61 episodes (audio, video, show notes) to team storage.

### Weeks 3–9: shadow, then reverse-shadow

Run the 3 remaining episodes like this:

| Episode | Who publishes | Who watches |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maroua | Successor and backup shadow every step, and **screen-record** it |
| 2 | Successor | Maroua sits alongside and corrects |
| 3 | Successor, solo | Maroua reviews the result afterward |

- Turn the screen recordings and notes into a **runbook**: recording, editing, upload, titles and thumbnails, show notes, publishing to both platforms, promotion.
- If cadence is slower, record ahead. Record two episodes in advance so the successor can practice on real material before she leaves.
- Hosting is a separate job from publishing. Decide who hosts. If the answer is "unknown", start auditioning co-hosts or guest hosts now, using these episodes.

### Weeks 10–11: cut the cord

1. **Test the new setup without her.** Ask someone else to log in and recover access using only the team identity.
2. **Remove her personal access.** Remove her from every account, and remove her card from every billing profile.
3. **Check what's left.** Make sure nothing still sends mail to her personal inbox or depends on her phone for 2FA.
4. **Record her farewell.** She could appear as a guest in the final episode.
5. **Keep a way to reach her.** Ask for a quick-question channel for the first 3 months after she leaves.

## Done criteria

- ✅ Team identity owns every account and pays every bill.
- ✅ Successor has published at least 1 episode without help.
- ✅ Runbook exists and was followed by someone other than its author.
- ✅ Two people can recover each account.
- ✅ Her personal account and card are not tied to anything.
