## 2. Posture

- **Rung:** low-cadence original channel.
- **Why:** Tomas has about 3 h/week in the quiet months, which is a named owner with recurring hours.
- **Rejected:** heavy-cadence, because it needs a funded owner and an in-house editor or an existing host. Archive-only and no channel stay available as fallbacks.
- **Format:** start with unedited live streams. A produced podcast is the hardest format. Climb to it only after a cadence has held.
- **Release:** the release must cover live publication before going live. Tomas must also be the named publisher.
- **Two busy months around the conference:** promise no original episodes. Publish only what the repurposing pipeline already makes.

## 3. Public commitment (the cadence sentence)

> "Between Editions publishes at least one new episode in every gap between our conferences, hosted by Tomas. If a gap passes without one, we say so in the feed."

- **Tomas must agree to this wording before it goes live.** If he won't, shrink the promise until he will.
- The sentence is a rhythm tied to the event cycle, with no calendar interval, because none is sourced.
- It never says "every week" or "every other week". An announced cadence nobody has agreed to hold is the failure the skill deletes outright.

## 5. Your co-founder's three asks

### Subscriber growth targets: keep as a private, self-set count

- The skill sets no figure and I won't invent one.
- Record the subscriber count at launch and at each episode.
- Write the target as a count, before the cycle starts, after you've seen your own baseline.
- Don't publish it. Reach is real, but it is a different quantity from the next two asks.

### Listener-to-ticket conversion rate: cut it as a KPI

- No sourced conversion figure exists between channel activity and registrations. `event-portfolio-strategy` says so itself.
- The GOTO Conferences case shows the failure. The stated goal was ticket sales, the reported results were views and subscribers, and no ticket figure was ever attached.
- Mark the feed path `channel -> conference registration` as **ASPIRATIONAL**. Marking it exists because a link exists is the failure.
- If your co-founder wants evidence, add one optional question at registration: "did Between Editions play a part?" Report the count, never a rate.
- Don't promise the show fills seats, publicly or internally.

### Drop after three missed episodes in a row: keep, but reshape

- The skill sets no count, so "three" is your co-founder's own number. A repeated miss is a signal to demote the rung, not a discipline problem.
- With one episode per gap, three consecutive misses means roughly three conferences of silence. The show would read as abandoned long before the rule fires.
- Proposed ladder, all of it self-set:
  1. **First miss:** say so in the feed, as the cadence sentence promises.
  2. **Repeated miss:** demote to archive-only, which keeps the home for the recordings and promises no rhythm.
  3. **Third miss in a row (your co-founder's number):** fold into a closed archive.
- Never let the feed lapse silently. Publish a dated closing note instead. Closing deliberately costs one post and makes the show read as finished rather than dead.
- The closing note says the show has ended, the date, where the catalogue stays online, who holds the account, and where the audience should go instead.

## 7. Guests, release, handover

- **Guest sourcing:** speaker-pool only to start. Check you may contact speakers about something other than the event. Promote to pool plus community nomination only once you have a community space that surfaces candidates.
- **Release:** collect an episode-specific release from every participant before recording, including Tomas and any third voice. Stage consent does not cover an episode. Route the wording to counsel; I name no jurisdiction or retention period.
- **Handover:** write it now, not when someone leaves. Don't leave the account in Tomas's personal name. List:
  - account and recovery contact
  - feed control
  - admin roles
  - master-file location
  - release records and their custodian, with a retention decision
  - guest contact list
  - the public cadence sentence, so a successor inherits the promise
