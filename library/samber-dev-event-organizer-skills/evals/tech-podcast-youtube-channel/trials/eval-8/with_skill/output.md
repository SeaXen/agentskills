# Three asks, one rule: none is this skill's job

`tech-podcast-youtube-channel` runs your own standing show. Each of these three asks belongs to a sibling skill. Below, each ask goes to its owner, plus the one thing this side supplies or guards.

I did not run the interview, so everything below assumes your show already exists and has a decided place in your portfolio.

## 1. Platform Weekly swap (they plug the show, your lead organizer guests on their podcast)

- ℹ️ **Owner:** `event-media-partnerships`. Its podcast-exchange rung is "an organizer or speaker guests on the partner's podcast". It is someone else's show, traded for visibility.
- ℹ️ **Mechanics:** `cross-event-promotion` states that newsletter-swap mechanics apply when the counterparty runs a media property. Platform Weekly is one.
- ❌ **Not here:** this skill never brokers the barter.
- ✅ **What this side supplies:**
  - One concrete episode, or a date, for them to plug. The `channel -> swap` path is `EXISTS`: episodes are swap inventory.
  - That episode must already be published, with an episode-specific release from every participant on file.
- ⚠️ **Guard rails for the plug:**
  - Do not agree to a plug schedule tied to a cadence nobody owns. A cadence with no named owner is a lapse nobody decided.
  - Do not promise Platform Weekly seats or conversions. No sourced figure ties channel activity to registrations, so keep the `channel -> conference` path `ASPIRATIONAL`.
- ℹ️ **Your organizer's guest spot:** prep for someone else's show is `samber/developer-relations-skills@tech-podcast-interview-prep` (a recommendation, not a dependency). Booking the appearance is `event-media-partnerships`.

## 2. RustFest Iberia (audience for audience, you negotiate terms)

- ℹ️ **Owner:** `cross-event-promotion`. It brokers swaps with other organizers.
- ❌ **Not here:** this skill supplies the episode or date for a swap and never negotiates it. I won't draft terms.
- ✅ **What this side supplies:** the same inventory as above. List which published episodes, or which upcoming episode date, you can offer.
- ⚠️ **Guard rails:**
  - Offer only episodes whose releases you hold.
  - Do not quote listener counts as a promise. Promise publication, nothing more.
  - Do not commit to a new episode just to fit their calendar unless the quiet-month owner agreed to that date.

## 3. Discord for listeners, moderated by "whoever is around"

- ℹ️ **Owner:** `event-community-building`. This is the sharpest seam in the skill. A space members belong to and post in, with a member list and a retention duty on it, is that skill's object. A published show is not.
- ⚠️ **Your plan collides with that skill.** Listeners emailing questions is demand for a space. "Whoever is around" is not a moderation owner.
  - The event's code of conduct binds anything under your name (`event-code-of-conduct`).
  - A space nobody answers dies visibly, which is the same failure I'd warn about for the show.
  - Decide the Discord there, with a named owner, before opening it.
- ❌ **Failure mode:** "letting the show become the community". If the plan needs a member list, replies or moderation, it is no longer the show.
- ✅ **What crosses the seam:** a new-episode notice is content that skill can push into its space.
- ✅ **What stays on this side:** guests or topics surfaced in Discord are input only.
  - You still source and vet them. Vetting needs a named owner and a written bar.
  - Each guest signs an episode-specific release before recording.
  - A Discord post or stage consent is not a release.
- ⚠️ **Don't promote the nomination rung yet.** "Pool plus community nomination" requires a community space that already surfaces guest candidates, and yours doesn't exist. Stay on speaker-pool only until it has run for a while.
- ℹ️ **Interim for the emails:** the existing listener questions can feed episode topics. That is a topic list, not a space.

2. Who owns the show in the months with no edition: one named person, a rotating role, or nobody?
3. Do you already have a community space or an editor, or neither?

Then two decisions are yours:
