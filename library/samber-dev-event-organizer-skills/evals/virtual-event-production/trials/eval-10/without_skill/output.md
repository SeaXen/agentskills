# Sable Infra Summit: KPIs and Board Targets

## Caveats

- I had no budget, ticket price, past-edition data or business goal, so these targets are not calibrated to Sable.
- The rates are rule-of-thumb figures for free or low-cost live virtual events. I didn't verify them against a source in this session.
- Recalibrate the rates before the board sees them. Use any past in-person or webinar data you have, and adjust the first-edition rates if your audience is warmer or colder than typical.

## Setup

- Base: **900 registered, 4 sessions**.
- Three tiers per KPI:
  - **Commit:** the number the board holds us to. We should hit it in all but a bad scenario.
  - **Goal:** the plan.
  - **Stretch:** a good outcome.
- Definitions are frozen before launch.
- We report at T+7 days and T+30 days.

## Board scorecard (7 KPIs)

| # | KPI | Definition | Commit | Goal | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Registrations | Unique, deduplicated registrants at close | 900 | 1,000 | 1,200 |
| 2 | ICP share of registrants | Registrants matching the agreed target profile, by job title and company type | 60% (540) | 70% | 80% |
| 3 | Unique live attendees | Registrants who attended at least 1 session live | 40% (360) | 45% (405) | 55% (495) |
| 4 | Average live attendance per session | Mean of the four sessions' live-attendee counts | 25% (225) | 30% (270) | 40% (360) |
| 5 | Engaged attendees | Attendees who watched at least 50% of any one session | 70% of attendees (about 250) | 75% (about 305) | 80% (about 395) |
| 6 | Total reach at 30 days | Unique viewers, live plus on-demand | 55% (495) | 65% (585) | 75% (675) |
| 7 | Post-event action rate | Share of unique attendees who take a defined next step within 30 days | 12% (about 43) | 18% (about 73) | 25% (about 124) |

**Defined next step for KPI 7.** Pick one before launch and write it down. Examples are a trial or sandbox signup, a demo or call request, a docs or community join, or an opt-in to the next edition. Count each person once.

| KPI | Commit | Goal | Stretch |

| Survey response rate | 15% | 20% | 30% |
| Overall CSAT (1-5) | 4.0 | 4.3 | 4.6 |

## Operational KPIs (floor, not stretch)

- Stream uptime during scheduled minutes: ≥99% commit, ≥99.5% goal, ≥99.9% stretch.
- Zero session interrupted for more than 2 minutes.
- Speaker no-shows: 0, backed by a pre-recorded fallback for every session.
- Recordings published: within 72h commit, 48h goal, 24h stretch.
- Follow-up email to attendees and no-shows: within 48h commit, 24h goal, same day stretch.
- Join success (clicked join, then in the stream): ≥95%.

## Cost and pipeline (cannot be set yet)

Fill these in once budget and goal are known.

- **Cost per engaged attendee** = total spend ÷ KPI 5. The commit denominator is about 250. Board rule: commit to a ceiling ≤ the figure the board approves.
- **Cost per post-event action** = total spend ÷ KPI 7 count.
- **If the goal is pipeline:**
  - Agree one attribution rule before the event: influenced pipeline, a fixed lookback window, and no retroactive reclassification.
  - Set the dollar target as spend × the ROI multiple the board requires.
  - Do not commit to a revenue number for a first-edition event.
- **If the goal is community or brand:** KPIs 6 and 7 plus the quality block become the headline.

## Leading indicators (so a miss shows up early)

- Registration pace against a curve. Virtual events typically collect a large share of registrations in the final 2 weeks, so measure against that curve rather than a straight line.
- Registration is the only lever before the event: if registration falls below about 55% of commit at T-14 days, trigger the promotion fallback plan.
- Reminder-email open rate. Reminders at T-7 days, T-1 day and T-1 hour drive live attendance.
- Session-1 attendance is the strongest predictor of the rest: below 20% of registrants means the series commit is at risk.

## Rules for holding us to it

1. Freeze definitions, tools and data sources before launch. Use one registration and streaming platform as the source of truth.
2. A miss on any commit KPI triggers a written post-mortem within 14 days, not a quiet redefinition.
3. Dedupe by email. Exclude staff, speakers and test accounts from attendee counts.
4. Report ICP share and engagement, not just totals. Raw registrations are the easiest number to inflate.
5. Do not use raw platform "views" or "impressions" as a KPI. They have no stable definition.

## Decisions needed before committing

1. What is the primary goal: pipeline, community or brand?
2. What are the total spend and the board's ROI test?
3. Is the event free or paid? Paid events typically show up at much higher rates.
4. What is the ICP definition, and what is the one next step for KPI 7?
5. Is there any baseline from past editions or webinars? If so, replace my rates with it.
