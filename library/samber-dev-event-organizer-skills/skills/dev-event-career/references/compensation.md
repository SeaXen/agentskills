# Compensation

Contents: PCMA's survey · BLS occupational data · Glassdoor's tech-industry premium · named tech-company and geographic examples.

Carry every figure with its source and edition - none of this is industry-standard, and it will drift. No dev/tech-conference-specific salary survey exists; the Glassdoor tech-industry premium data is the closest tech-specific cut available.

## PCMA Convene 2025 Salary Survey (275 event professionals, primarily North America)

The most-cited industry benchmark found, by planner level:

- Coordinator: $77,321
- Manager: $95,077
- Director: $133,147
- Executive/VP: $176,217

## US Bureau of Labor Statistics: "Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners" (occupation code 13-1121)

Median $59,440 to $101,310 - a census-scale figure blending every seniority level and every industry. Industry sources describe meeting-planner salary data generally as "highly fragmented across the industry, making accurate benchmarking difficult" - the honest state of this domain's pay data.

## Tech-industry premium (Glassdoor, by title)

- **Event Manager**: national average $85,244-$89,104/yr (25th pct $70,855, 75th pct $113,560, 90th pct $140,243). Information Technology is the single highest-paying industry for this title, median total pay $114,016 - ahead of Financial Services. Named top-paying IT employers: Amazon Web Services, Google, Meta.
- **Event Marketing Manager**: national average $100,135/yr (25th pct $79,716, 75th pct $127,521, 90th pct $157,358). Information Technology again leads, median total pay $175,690 - the widest tech premium of any title sourced. Named top-paying IT employers: Amazon Web Services, Amazon, Microsoft.
- **Events Manager** (broader title): Information Technology leads at median total pay $157,737, Amazon named as top payer.
- **Event Technology Manager**: national average $116,910/yr, 90th pct $189,139.

## Named tech-company example

**Google, Event Manager** (Glassdoor estimate): average $145,583/yr - 40% above the general average of $87,339 - with a stated range from $103,538 at entry up to $383,791 at the most senior level sourced under that title. Treat the top end as reflecting a senior/director-scope role carrying the same title, consistent with the title-inconsistency finding.

## Geographic cuts

- **San Francisco tech hub**: roughly $83,000-$115,000 base, often exceeding $130,000-$150,000 total comp with bonus/equity. At named top-tier tech employers (Meta, Anthropic, OpenAI, Salesforce cited) experienced professionals reportedly reach $130,000-$200,000+. This figure comes from a single self-published practitioner blog post, not a survey - weight it as one data point, not a benchmark.
- **New York City**: Event Manager average $91,303/yr (8% above the US national average); top-paying local industries again led by Information Technology at median $130,306, ahead of Hotels & Travel ($78,003) and Education ($72,873).

## UK agency-side bands (dated, general B2B conference producing, not tech-specific)

Entry-level ~£18,000/yr; established Producer £23,000-£35,000/yr; Manager/Director level £35,000-£45,000/yr, with event-success-linked bonuses supplementing base "significantly." No publication date could be confirmed - treat as a real named source with an unconfirmed edition year, likely stale given typical UK salary inflation, and flag that explicitly.

## What's missing

No comp data specific to volunteer-community-run tech events' first paid hires (devopsdays/PyCon/FOSDEM-style organizations), and no comp data for a standing for-profit conference business, were found. Say so plainly rather than extrapolating from the figures above.
