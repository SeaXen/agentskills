# Practitioner quotes

Contents: named, sourced quotes on what separates strong event producers from median ones.

All quotes below are attributed and sourced from named individuals; none are invented. Most speak to corporate/experiential event production broadly rather than developer conferences specifically - the scope is flagged per quote, except where noted otherwise.

## On focus and audience-centering

**Amanda Matuk**, Executive Producer at Google (led scaling of Google's own developer conference, Google I/O): "Focus on the user and all else will follow." Matuk's framing of I/O's growth: the team "didn't just scale for scale's sake" - growth decisions were driven by attendee feedback rather than by an ambition for scale itself. This is the single most directly dev-conference-relevant quote sourced in this field.

## On the emotional core of the job

**Yifat Oren**, Founder and Creative Director, Oren Cove Productions: "In order to be good at producing events, you have to enjoy taking care of people."

## On going beyond the default playbook

**Khaleed Juma**, Creative Director, Mosaic: challenges the industry's reflexive reach for "surprise and delight" as the only emotional register worth designing for - "We feel more than 'surprise and delight.'" His argument: surprise-and-delight is "easy to do: it's positive, it's universal, and it's safe," but a stronger producer designs across a wider range of human emotion rather than defaulting to the safe choice.

## On authenticity as differentiation

**Catherine Simmons**, VP of Strategic Events, Salesforce Dreamforce: "Being authentic helps cut through the clutter."

## On anticipating the full experience arc

**Audrea Hooper**, Head of "Fungineering," Zappos: "Anticipate how somebody's going to experience your event from start to finish."

## On perspective under pressure

**Jeremy Bernard**, former White House Social Secretary: "Sometimes you get wrapped up in bureaucracy or drama, and you need [to take a] step back and realize that this is really fun."

**Rachel Mueller-Lust**, event planner: "It always seems impossible until it's done."

**Eddie Ross**, author/editor and producer: "Good planning I've always thought is in the details... Plan it out, make it interesting, and you're sure to pull it off!"

## On what a recruiter looks for at the senior end

**Diana Dixon**, quoted via Regan & Dean (UK events recruitment): experience at reputable, established companies is highly valued; recruiters prize professionals who can manage senior-level contacts confidently and demonstrate transferable production skills across sectors. Her "duality" framing - research-focused introversion paired with networking extroversion, meticulous detail paired with strategic vision - doubles as a differentiator: candidates who present only one half read as under-developed for senior rungs.
