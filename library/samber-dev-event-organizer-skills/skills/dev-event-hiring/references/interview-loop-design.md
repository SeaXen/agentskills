# Interview loop design

Contents: the general event-planner sequence · CMX's five-competency loop, from the hiring side · work-sample design by role shape · scoring mechanics.

No single, fully-documented tech/developer-conference hiring loop exists published anywhere - this design is assembled from general event-industry hiring-guide sources plus one much more specific named source for the community-manager-adjacent slice of this role family. State this to the user rather than presenting it as one pre-existing tech-event loop.

## The general event-planner sequence (producer/manager-shaped hires)

1. **Portfolio and past-events review** - ask for work showing the style, scale, and quality level appropriate for this event, plus client or stakeholder testimonials.
2. **Concrete metrics request** - attendee satisfaction scores, percentage of events delivered on-budget, repeat-client/repeat-attendee rate. Score for whether the candidate volunteers these unprompted.
3. **Scenario-based questions** - a last-minute venue change, a vendor no-show, a weather contingency. Score for real-time troubleshooting, not memorized process.
4. **Budget-management questions** - staying within budget while meeting or exceeding expectations.
5. **Client/vendor relations questions** - communication style, handling a difficult stakeholder relationship.
6. **Live reference calls, not written recommendations** - a candidate's past clients describing the working relationship reveals soft skills no resume captures.

## CMX's five-competency loop (community-manager-shaped hires)

- **Growth mindset**: "What would you like to achieve in this role within a year?" / "What motivates you to do your job well?"
- **Data-driven approach**: score whether the candidate volunteers results and metrics unprompted when describing past projects.
- **Empathy**: "What would your current or past colleagues say about working with you?"
- **Leadership**: "What are three things you would change about our current community?" / "If the community wants one thing and your boss wants something else, what would you do?" - score for a concrete, specific answer over a diplomatic non-answer.
- **Communication**: assessed through the depth and quality of follow-up conversation, not a single scripted question.

## Work-sample design, by role shape

| Role shape                                       | Work sample                                                                            | Never                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Community-manager-shaped, senior/final candidate | A paid mini-audit of the existing community - a real, bounded, compensated deliverable | An unpaid, open-ended audit                                              |
| Community-manager-shaped, junior/moderator-track | A sample-thread-response exercise in a shared document                                 | Live, public-facing moderation as a "trial"                              |
| Producer/manager-shaped                          | A scenario walkthrough (the venue-change or vendor-no-show question, scored live)      | An unpaid mock full-event plan requiring real hours of client-style work |

## Scoring mechanics

Score independently per interviewer with a written evidence note before any debrief, then combine mechanically against the scorecard's pre-set competency weights - a general employment-selection finding, not specific to event organizing, but it transfers directly.
