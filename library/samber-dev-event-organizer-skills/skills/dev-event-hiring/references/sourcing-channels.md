# Sourcing channels

Contents: the general event-industry boards · the CMX community-manager channel · what to say about the tech-specific gap.

No dev/tech-conference-specific job board or candidate community exists. Every channel below serves the general corporate-events/meetings/community-management industry; the closest thing to a tech-specific channel is CMX's community-management board.

## General event-industry job boards

- **PCMA Career Center** (careers.pcma.org) - run by the Professional Convention Management Association, serving the business-events industry broadly.
- **MPI Career Center** (careers.mpi.org) - run by Meeting Professionals International, similarly broad, not tech-specific.
- **BizBash** - a publication for corporate event and meeting professionals, running a searchable vendor directory alongside its job board.
- **MeetingJobs.com** - merged with Cadre in 2020 to add short-term contract-planner and on-site event-staff placement alongside permanent roles.
- **Event Careers** (via Event Marketer) - North America-focused; employers pay to post jobs or purchase resume access.
- **Event Industry News** - a European event-tech and industry-news publication; job/career content is secondary to news coverage.

## The community-manager-adjacent channel

**CMX Job Board** (cmxhub.com/job-board) - connects to 7,000+ community professionals, the single most relevant named channel for the community-manager-shaped slice of dev-event-organizing roles (meetup organizers, developer-community-events hybrids). CMX also runs a Slack community explicitly recommended as a discovery channel for both sides of a hire.

## What to say about the gap

Tech/developer-event hiring currently borrows general event-industry channels rather than having its own - say this plainly rather than inventing a tech-specific board. The community-manager overlap with CMX is the closest exception. An informal channel like the "#eventprofs" hashtag on X/LinkedIn is widely known in the general industry but was not independently verified for current activity in this field's research - name it as informal and unverified if mentioned, never with invented member counts or activity levels.
