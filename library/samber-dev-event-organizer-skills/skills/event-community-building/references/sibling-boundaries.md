# Sibling boundaries, in full

Contents: the four boundaries each sibling already draws from its own side; the three drawn from one side only.

SKILL.md carries a one-line, actionable version of each. This file carries each sibling's own wording and the fuller mirrored reasoning, for anyone auditing where a boundary comes from.

## Written from the other side

- **`samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-comms-channels` owns the same platforms for a different job.** Its scope is one edition's channel lifecycle; yours is the gap after wind-down decides a channel persists.
  - Its own words: _"`event-community-building` owns the standing community between editions"_. Its wind-down menu's hand-off rung tells the reader to coordinate with this skill, naming it the receiving side.
  - Mirrored: take the space at the moment its edition-specific job ends, and hand it back with a live member list when the next edition's channels open. Never redesign the roster, the routing rule or the wind-down.
- **`samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-volunteer-experience` owns the volunteer alumni loop, and this is the mirror it asked for.** It states: _"The alumni loop is yours, and `samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-community-building` must mirror that when it is built."_
  - Mirrored: the past-volunteer list, the volunteer thank-you and the volunteer candidate flag are that skill's, not yours. Your audience is every community member who never worked a shift: attendees, repeat speakers, people who only ever read the archive.
  - A standout member gets flagged to `samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-team-structure` as a candidate the same way that skill flags one; you do not build a second nomination path for volunteers.
  - That skill also carries a standing alumni channel as a menu rung, and its own interview asks whose standing space already exists before it opens one. Where your surface exists, the alumni loop runs as a segment inside it rather than as a second space: the segment stays that skill's to fill, the surface stays yours to run.
- **`samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-portfolio-strategy` gates the surface's existence.** Its own words: _"Animating it is `samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-community-building`'s job, not this skill's; this skill only decides whether it belongs in the portfolio."_
  - It also owns any _event_ touchpoint: a quarterly meetup feeding the flagship is its low-cadence-small-format rung, not a community tactic.
  - The moment a touchpoint you designed needs a venue, a schedule or a speaker roster, it stops being yours and routes there and through the collection's event-planning skills.
- **`samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@cross-event-promotion` faces outward.** It routes inward-facing community animation between editions to this skill, and keeps its own calendar tactic outward-facing. Mirrored: a partner event announced inside your space is content you deliver; the deal behind it is brokered there.

## Written from one side only

Say so when you lean on these - the sibling has not written its half of the mirror.

- **`samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-cultural-identity` sets the voice and you execute it.** Its own description gives it the register - hacker, corporate, design, business - and that register's consistent expression across the event's touchpoints. It also settles the temptation to invent a quieter off-season voice: _"The identity is fixed; only its expression modulates"_. Read the register off it; never originate one.
- **`samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-growth-strategy` owns whether the event scales.** You never recommend a growth lever. The one real interface runs the other way: a healthy carry rate across the gap is evidence you hand to `samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-market-fit` and through it to growth, never a recommendation you make.
- **`samber/dev-event-organizer-skills@event-code-of-conduct` binds your space exactly as it binds the room.** A standing space is a standing conduct duty; you name the owner and route the policy and reporting path there.
