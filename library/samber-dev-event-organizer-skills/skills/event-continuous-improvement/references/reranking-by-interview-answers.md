# Re-ranking each menu against the interview answers

Every promotion, demotion and deletion below is this skill's own construction, not a published standard. Say which answer moved which option, per menu, rather than silently reordering.

- **Q1, editions of records held.**
  - Persistence: one edition deletes the two upper rungs outright; four or more promotes the structured entry record to default.
  - Detection: two editions deletes the written trend review and caps every claim at "candidate".
  - Audit: fewer than two deletes the closure pass and the standing owner.
- **Q3, team turnover.**
  - Persistence: most-turned-over or full rotation promotes the structured entry record, because nothing survives in anyone's memory to compensate.
  - Detection: the same answer promotes the written trend review, since the deciders did not witness the evidence.
  - Audit: a rotation _this_ cycle promotes the standing owner, because the log's trustworthiness is due at handover, not at kickoff.
- **Q5, when planning starts.**
  - Persistence: barely moves, and say so - reshaping the record is not on the critical path, and a rushed schema is worse than an appended heading.
  - Detection: a start date within weeks demotes the written trend review and leaves theme grouping as the default.
  - Audit: a near date promotes the kickoff pass to happen now rather than being scheduled.
- **Q6, one-off or compounding.**
  - Persistence: a closing edition collapses the menu to whatever the successor can read; a compounding mandate with several editions left promotes the structured entry record.
  - Detection: a compounding mandate promotes the written trend review, jointly with Q7.
  - Audit: a closing edition deletes everything above audit on read.
- **Q7, effort ceiling.**
  - Persistence: a low ceiling holds the default and forbids the retrofit.
  - Detection: a low ceiling demotes both upper rungs and leaves the default untouched, which is the point of a default costing one clustering pass.
  - Audit: an existing between-edition job promotes the standing owner; no ceiling at all leaves it starved.
- **Q8, who re-reads the log.**
  - Detection: no named person promotes the written trend review, the only rung that pushes the log at someone instead of waiting to be opened.
  - Persistence and audit: unchanged, and say so - a better-structured record does not make anyone read it.
