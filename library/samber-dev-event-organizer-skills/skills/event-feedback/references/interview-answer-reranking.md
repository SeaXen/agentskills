# Re-ranking the three menus against the interview answers

Say which answer moved which option, per menu, rather than silently reordering. This walks all three menus (collection channel, instrument depth, closing the loop) against each relevant interview question.

- **Q1, format and whether the day is still open.** Channel: already over deletes in-room capture outright; a hackathon demo day promotes it hard, since the captive moment is louder there than at any other format. Depth: hackathon promotes the full instrument to default. Loop: unchanged - format does not change what respondents deserve back.
- **Q2, which roles exist.** Depth: no sponsors and no speaker slate deletes both upper rungs. Channel and loop unchanged.
- **Q3, the decision waiting.** Depth: a role-owned decision falling due promotes the role module. Channel: a decision due within days promotes in-room capture where still available, since email plus reminders takes a week to finish arriving. Loop unchanged.
- **Q4, anonymity.** Channel: an anonymity requirement deletes the incentive. Depth: ordering unchanged, but it changes what the instrument may ask by name. Loop: an anonymous instrument with no publication consent deletes the public post.
- **Q6, the deadline.** Channel: a near deadline demotes the reminder cadence's later sends - one landing after the debrief collects data nobody will use. Depth: a near deadline demotes both upper rungs, since per-role analysis is where the hours go. Loop: barely moves; a loop-back note is not on anyone's critical path.
- **Q7, one-off or compounding.** Loop: a one-off deletes every rung above the acknowledgement - no next edition to buy a response rate for, and the thank-you is simply owed. Compounding promotes the note and, with a recap already planned, the public post. Channel: a prior edition's poor rate promotes the incentive.
- **Q8, the effort ceiling.** Depth: a low ceiling demotes both upper rungs and leaves the default untouched. Channel: a ceiling excluding day-of coordination deletes in-room capture, as firmly as an event already over. Loop: a low ceiling holds the default, since the note's cost is drafting rather than analysis.
