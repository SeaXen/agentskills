## Fix before launch to make the release defensible

- **Run a quick hygiene pass on the 412.** Check dead links, abandoned apps, missing privacy policies or contact info, and unmaintained OAuth scopes. A one- to two-week automated sweep is realistic, and you can delist or flag the worst offenders.

- **Prepare Q&A for press and customers:**

- **Make sure the support and security team know the launch date** and have a path for incoming reports.
