## 1. Blog copy: not safe

- **"49% install lift in 14 days"** is Shopify's Built for Shopify figure. Shopify publishes it ("an average increase of 49% new installs in just 14 days of achieving status"), so it is operator-published. Hollowmere has no badged-app data of its own.
  - Reusing it as a Hollowmere result is a false claim.
  - Shopify's gates are the same 50 installs and 5 reviews. That does not make the number transfer to a 240-app marketplace with different traffic.
- **"Rank higher in search"** is contested. One developer tracked keyword rank with SASI after earning the badge and reported "nothing happened". The skill reads the badge as buying eligibility, trust and an install bump, not a rank boost.
  - Partners run their own rank trackers and will check.
  - Say "badged apps get preferential search placement" only if Hollowmere builds that, and state it as a design rule, not an outcome.
- **"Verified"** implies vetting. The four-part checklist is a real gate, so the claim is fine if the post names what the checklist covers. I don't know your categories. Shopify's are security, performance, UX and listing accuracy.
  - Claim only what the review checks.
  - I don't know if Hollowmere has a live review gate for all listings. If it has none, do not call the badged group "curated" or "vetted" beyond the checklist itself.

## 2. Does the badge fix long-tail discovery? No

- The gate is 50 net paid installs plus 5 reviews. Apps without traction cannot reach it.
- Any search or featuring bonus goes to apps that already have installs. Long-tail developers, and most of the ~15 new apps a month, start below the floor. The badge widens the gap.
- The complainers are, by definition, under the floor. The badge gives them a goal, not discovery.

## 3. What fixes it

Run the badge as the traction and trust layer. Add a rotation for small apps.

1. **Weekly editorial picks (the Shopify Staff Picks model).**
   - Selection is not based on install base, which is the opposite of the badge's gate.
   - Shippo reported 3x normal signups in its pick week. That is a vendor-reported anecdote, so label it as one if you cite it.
   - Cost: standing editorial labor, roughly a few hours a week. Confirm what your app team can commit.
2. **Collections and category pages.** These are platform-wide demand generation. They cost the other partners nothing, so fund them for everyone.
3. **A "Rising" tier or badge for newer apps.** This is Atlassian's Rising Star idea, designed by you. I would not cut the badge's thresholds to include them.
4. **Publish the rules.** Publish the gates, the re-audit cycle, and the rule that losing the badge drops the endorsement, not the listing.
