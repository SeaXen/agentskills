# Quillane partner marketing allocation, $900K

## Recommendation

Don't divide the money evenly. Equalize the process instead: published tiers, published scoring, and one intake calendar that applies to all 130 partners. Partners will accept unequal outcomes if the rules are visible and the same for everyone. Their complaint was that nobody could tell why one partner got a yes and another got a no.

## Why the even split fails

- **The math leaves no room to run the program.** $6,900 × 130 = $897K, which leaves $3K for ops.
- **It funds nothing meaningful.** $6,900 buys about one webinar. Partners who could use $50K get $6,900, and partners who ignore Quillane get the same.
- **It adds no co-investment or accountability.** A partner who does nothing with the money loses nothing.
- **Your team can't execute it.** 130 simultaneous co-marketing projects is more than any partner team can run.
- **It doesn't fix the complaint.** A partner who wants a case study and gets a check for a webinar will still say you played favorites.

## Budget split

| Bucket | Amount | Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 Strategic MDF (about 10 partners × $25K cap) | $250K | 28% | Largest co-investments |
| Tier 2 Growth MDF (about 30 × $8K cap) | $240K | 27% | Mid-size co-investments |
| Tier 3 Active MDF (about 50 × $2K cap) | $100K | 11% | Small co-funded activities |
| Shared programs for all tiers, including Tier 4 | $130K | 14% | Group webinars, case study production, showcase series |
| Reserve | $90K | 10% | Mid-year reallocation, new partners, overperformers |
| Program ops | $90K | 10% | Webinar platform, case study production tooling, tracking |
| **Total** | **$900K** | **100%** | |

Tier counts are placeholders. The scoring below sets the real counts, and the caps scale down pro rata if too many partners qualify.

## What every partner gets, equally

- Marketplace listing and a self-serve co-marketing kit.
- Entry into the quarterly "Partner Showcase" webinar series, where 4 to 5 partners co-present. Slots rotate by a published schedule.
- A case study request form. Production is funded from the shared pool and prioritized by the same scoring.
- The same application form, rubric, timeline, and written decision as every other partner.

## Tiering rules

Score each partner on four published criteria:

| Criterion | Weight | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| Marketplace-sourced results | 40% | Active mutual accounts or revenue from installs, trailing 12 months |
| Integration quality | 20% | Certification status, support SLA, open critical tickets |
| Customer fit | 20% | Overlap with Quillane's target customer profile |
| Delivery track record | 20% | Share of past co-marketing commitments completed on time |

- **New partners** start in Tier 3 and can move up after 2 quarters.
- **Tier changes** happen twice a year, with one quarter's notice before any demotion.
- **Emerging partners:** a portion of the reserve funds pilots for partners with low history but high fit. Pure revenue ranking would otherwise lock in today's big partners.

## How the money is released

Tier caps are ceilings. They are not entitlements.

1. **Quarterly windows.** Applications open in Jan, Apr, Jul, and Oct. Off-cycle requests come only from the reserve and are logged.
2. **Rate card.** Pre-set unit costs make requests comparable. For example: webinar $3K, case study $2.5K, co-branded content asset $1.5K. These figures are illustrative until you check real vendor costs.
3. **Match requirement.** The partner co-invests: 1:1 for Tiers 1 and 2, and 50% for Tier 3. Quillane reimburses after proof of execution.
4. **Required goal.** Each activity states a measurable outcome, such as registrations, qualified leads, or installs.
5. **Quarterly tranche.** Each quarter gets 25% of the pool. Unclaimed funds at quarter end return to the reserve.
6. **Result tracking.** Measure installs and pipeline at 90 days. Results feed the next scoring round.

## Decision process

- **Decision-makers.** A panel of 3 (partner manager, sales, product marketing) scores each request against the rubric. Anyone with a conflict of interest recuses.
- **Turnaround.** Decisions come within 10 business days, with a written reason.
- **Appeals.** One appeal per quarter, reviewed by someone outside the panel.
- **Exceptions.** Only from the reserve, and each one is logged with its reason.
- **Transparency.** Publish the tiers, rubric, calendar, and a quarterly summary of total spend by tier. Don't name partners.

## Handling the LinkedIn critic

- Contact them privately with the criteria and the next application window.
- Don't argue in public.
- If they qualify, treat them like any other applicant.

- Does Finance require the money to be spent within the calendar year, or can unspent funds roll over?
- How many people can run partner programs? That caps how many Tier 1 and Tier 2 activities you can support at once.
