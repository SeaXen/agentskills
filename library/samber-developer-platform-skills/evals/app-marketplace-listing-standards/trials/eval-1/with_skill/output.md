# Loomside Integrations Directory: Media Requirements

**Your premise is wrong, so the numbers below are ours, not "the industry's".** Across the eight benchmarked marketplaces there is no standard screenshot size and no standard video length. Screenshots ship at 16:9 (Shopify), 8:5 (Slack), and roughly 3:2 (Salesforce). Zoom and HubSpot publish no pixel rule at all. Video ceilings run from 30 seconds (Atlassian) to 2–3 minutes (Shopify). Copying one vendor's figures and calling them standard would be false, and nobody checking our page would expect them to match.

Video length tracks who evaluates the listing. Your installs go through customer IT admins and, at two large accounts, a vendor-review committee. That puts you at the strict end: short, function-only video. Everything below is set from that.

The benchmark figures come from the skill's reference file. I did not re-check them against the live operator docs.

## What to submit

| Asset | Required? | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Screenshots | Required | 3 to 6 |
| App icon | Required | 1 |
| Demo video | Optional | 0 or 1 |

## Screenshots

- **Size:** exactly 1600 × 900 px (16:9). Any other ratio is rejected.
- **Format and weight:** PNG or JPG, under 2 MB each.
- **Content:**
  - At least 1 screenshot must show the real app UI.
  - At least 1 screenshot must show the app working inside Loomside, such as the app's step in a workflow. Admins evaluate what it does to their tenant, not the vendor's own product.
- **Captions:** every screenshot needs a caption of 220 characters or fewer. State what the screen shows and which data it touches.
- **Alt text:** every screenshot needs descriptive alt text of 140 characters or fewer.
- **Legibility:** text and UI controls must have at least 4.5:1 contrast (WCAG 2.2 AA).

## Icon

- **Size and format:** 1200 × 1200 px (1:1), PNG.
- **Content:** no text, no Loomside logo or trademark, no pricing or ratings.

## Demo video (optional)

- **Length:** 30 seconds maximum. Longer videos are rejected.
- **Content:** show the live app doing its job in a realistic environment, in this order: connect, configure, run. Marketing footage, animated intros, logos-and-music sequences, and ads are banned.
- **Captions:** required. A video without captions is rejected.
- **Hosting:** a public link that works without login.

## Rules for every asset

- No personal data: no real names, emails, customer records, or tokens. Use sample data.
- No claims baked into images: no pricing, review scores, or "#1" statements.
- No Loomside trademark in any asset, including the icon. This follows the naming-order rule used by Atlassian, Slack, and Zoom.
- Assets must match the current version of the app. Out-of-date media is a rejectable violation. Outdated or non-matching screenshots are rejected at review, and again if found later.

## Named rejection triggers

Each of these can be checked before you submit:

1. Screenshot is not 1600 × 900.
2. Fewer than 3 or more than 6 screenshots.
3. No screenshot shows actual app UI.
4. A caption or alt text is missing, or over its limit.
5. Icon is not 1200 × 1200, or contains text or a trademark.
6. Video is longer than 30 seconds, uncaptioned, behind a login, or contains marketing footage.
7. Any asset contains personal data, pricing, or review claims.
8. Media does not match the current app.

Media rejection is about content only. Security and functionality review run separately, and a listing can pass one and fail the other.

## Decisions to confirm before publishing

1. **The 30-second ceiling.** It is derived from your admin and committee audience. If you'd rather allow longer, 90 seconds (Slack's upper end) is the next defensible step. Don't average the benchmarks.
2. **1600 × 900 and 1200 × 1200.** These are our picks. 1600 × 900 matches Shopify's spec, which makes cross-listing easier for vendors who already have assets. Say so if you'd prefer another ratio.
3. **Optional video, or required for committee-facing listings?** Since two big accounts run formal reviews, you could require video for any listing you mark as committee-ready. I left it optional, as you asked.
4. **Notice runway.** At launch there are no existing listings, so no runway is needed. Once the catalog exists, give vendors a notice period before any new media requirement applies to listings already live.
