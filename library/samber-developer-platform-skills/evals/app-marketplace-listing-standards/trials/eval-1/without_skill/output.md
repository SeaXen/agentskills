# Media Requirements for Integration Listings

Your listing's media is the first thing customers and their IT teams see. Admins and vendor-review committees use it to judge your app before approving an install. Show the real product, and be precise about what the app does.

## Summary

| Asset | Required | Format | Size | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| App icon | Yes | PNG | 512 × 512 px, square | 2 MB |
| Screenshots | Yes, 3 to 8 | PNG or JPG | 1600 × 900 px (16:9) | 5 MB each |
| Demo video | Optional | MP4 (H.264) | 1920 × 1080 px (16:9) | 60 to 120 seconds, 200 MB |

## App icon

- Use a 512 × 512 px PNG with a transparent or solid background.
- Keep the artwork within the central 80%. We display it at sizes down to 32 px and in circular crops.
- Use your own logo. Don't include Loomside's name or logo, since that implies endorsement.
- Don't put text in the icon beyond a wordmark that stays legible at 32 px.

## Screenshots

**Specs**
- Use 1600 × 900 px (16:9). Smaller images must be at least 1280 × 720 px and the same ratio. We don't upscale them.
- Upload 3 to 8 images in PNG or JPG, each 5 MB or less.
- The first screenshot is your listing's preview image, so make it the strongest one.

**Content requirements**
1. **Real product only.** Capture the app running inside Loomside. Mockups, concept art and stock imagery are rejected.
2. **No real customer data.** Use sample data, and blur or remove names, emails, tokens, API keys and internal URLs.
3. **Include a permissions screenshot.** One image must show the authorization or consent screen, or the settings page that lists the data and scopes your app requests. This is the screenshot IT reviewers look at first.
4. **Show setup.** At least one image should show how a workspace admin connects and configures the app.
5. **Keep text legible at 50% scale.** Captions and callouts should be readable in a thumbnail.
6. **Claim only what you ship.** Don't show features that aren't generally available. Label beta features "Beta".
7. **Keep it clean.** Don't show browser chrome with personal bookmarks, other tabs, notifications or developer tools.

## Demo video (optional)

**Specs**
- Use MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio, at 1920 × 1080 px (16:9) and 30 fps.
- Aim for 60 to 90 seconds. The maximum is 120 seconds and 200 MB.
- Add captions or subtitles in English. Many reviewers watch muted.
- Upload the file directly. Third-party hosted links aren't accepted, because reviewers need the video to stay unchanged after approval.

**Content requirements**
1. **Lead with the problem.** Spend the first 10 seconds on what the app solves, then show it working.
2. **Use the production product.** Record a real flow, not an animation of one.
3. **Show the permission and connection step.** Include the install or authorization moment, so reviewers see what access the app requests.
4. **Don't expose credentials.** Never show passwords, API keys, tokens or real customer records, even briefly.
5. **Keep claims verifiable.** Don't state security certifications, compliance standards, uptime or customer counts in the video unless you've documented them in your listing's security section.
6. **No pricing or promotions** that may change. Put those in the listing text.
7. **Avoid hype.** Keep the tone factual. Marketing-heavy videos tend to raise reviewer scrutiny.

## Review and approval

- We review all media before publication, and a listing can't go live until it passes.
- Your listing may be re-reviewed if your app's permissions or features change significantly. Update your media when that happens, so screenshots always match the shipped product.
- Customers' IT admins and vendor-review committees may inspect your media independently. Anything that misrepresents the product or its data access can lead to removal from the directory.

## Checklist before you submit

- [ ] Icon is 512 × 512 px PNG, with the artwork inside the central 80%
- [ ] 3 to 8 screenshots at 1600 × 900 px
- [ ] One screenshot shows permissions or scopes
- [ ] One screenshot shows setup
- [ ] No real customer data, credentials or internal URLs in any asset
- [ ] Video (if included) is 60 to 120 seconds, captioned, 1080p
- [ ] Every feature shown is generally available or labelled Beta
