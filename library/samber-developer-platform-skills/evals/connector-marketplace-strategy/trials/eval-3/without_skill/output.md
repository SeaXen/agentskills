**Recommendation:** announce one flat 15% all-in rate, with a 10% rate locked for 24 months for the 12 founding partners. Drop the listing fee. Turn the security review fee into a credit against first-year revenue share. Don't ship the 25% + $2,500 + $400 stack.

## Why not the CFO's structure

1. **Apple's 30% doesn't transfer.**
   - Apple sells access to a captive installed base that partners can't reach any other way.
   - Your partners can sell direct and integrate via API. That is their exit.
   - Apple's rate is also falling: it offers 15% to small developers.

2. **Enterprise B2B marketplace rates sit well below 25%.**
   - From memory, not checked live: AWS and Microsoft marketplaces are around 3%, Shopify is 0% on early revenue then 15%, and Salesforce AppExchange is around 15%.
   - **Verify these before citing them to the CFO.**
   - Your marketplace is cold-start with no buyer-base track record. It shouldn't price above those comps.

3. **The 25% applies to revenue that doesn't exist yet.** None of the 12 apps charges today. Your real risk is partners deciding not to start charging through you, not leaving a rate on the table.

4. **Fixed fees are regressive and barely pay.**
   - A partner earning $10k/yr would pay 25% plus $2,900 in year one, about 54% of revenue. At $120k/yr the same stack is about 27%.
   - Fixed fees across 12 partners bring in about $34.8k in year one, a rounding error next to the signal they send.

5. **Break-even math for the CFO.** Assume 12 partners at an average $100k/yr, so $1.2M GMV:
   - 25% yields $300k and 15% yields $180k, a $120k gap.
   - 25% only wins if it costs you fewer than 40% of that GMV (180 ÷ 300 = 0.6).
   - With nobody charging yet, losing 40% of GMV to partners who delay or sell direct is plausible. These inputs are my assumptions, so swap in real ones.

## Rate card

| Item | Founding 12 | Everyone else |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue share (all-in, incl. payment processing) | 10%, locked 24 months from launch | 15% |
| Security review | Free | Cost-recovery fee ($1,000–2,500, set from real review hours), credited against first-year revenue share |
| Listing fee | $0 | $0 |

- **Founding rate:** it gives you room to rise to 15% without breaking a promise.
- **All-in:** you absorb the roughly 3% processing cost, which beats a quoted 25% plus processing.
- **Review fee as deposit:** serious partners recoup it, so it stops working as a toll.
- **Revisit:** review the rate at month 12 after launch, with at least 6 months' written notice of any change.

## Terms to settle with Finance before announcing

- Revenue base: net of refunds and taxes.
- Payout cadence, for example monthly, net 30.
- Bypass rule: customers first acquired via the marketplace stay on the share for 12 months even if they move to direct billing.
- Which partners count as founding (all 12, or only those who sign by a date).
