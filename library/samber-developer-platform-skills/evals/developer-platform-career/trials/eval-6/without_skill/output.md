**Take Ardent Pay, unless the checks below fail.** Glenview is the real alternative. I'd drop the third conversation from this decision.

## Why Ardent Pay

- **The API is the whole product.** Reliability, versioning, and developer experience are what the company is judged on, so the work is well funded and well regarded.
- **Staff is a level up.** Staff on a nine-person platform team probably carries more scope, pay growth, and hiring leverage than Senior. A Staff title also travels to your next job. It's hard to get later from a Senior role at a company where the API is a side feature.
- **You'd work with eight peers who build APIs for a living.** You'd learn what mature API governance, deprecation, SDKs, and incident handling look like at a payments company. Glenview can't teach that this year.

## Why not Glenview

"You'll own the API end to end" is attractive but carries three risks:
- It's a first public API at a company whose product is freight software. The API competes with the core roadmap for attention.
- You'd be one of six backend engineers with no platform team. "Own" can mean you're the only person who cares about it, plus on call for it.
- The title is Senior. If the API succeeds, you do Staff-level work without the title or pay. If it fails, you spent a year on a stalled side project.

Choose Glenview over Ardent only if you want greenfield design authority more than depth and scale. That means you'd rather decide the API's shape yourself than work inside a mature one.

## The third conversation

An enterprise partner engineering team that supports ISVs is closer to developer-facing support and enablement than to building the API. Keep it going only if that direction appeals to you.

## Checks before you accept Ardent

1. **Staff scope.** Ask what the last Staff hire owned in their first six months. If the answer sounds like senior ticket work, the title is hollow.
2. **Total compensation.** Base is within 8%, so equity, refresh grants, and the vesting and liquidity terms decide it. Compare the Staff and Senior equity bands.
3. **On-call.** Ask for the payments-grade rotation, and for pages per week.
4. **Company health.** Check runway, the funding round, and customer concentration. A payments API company is only as safe as its volume.

## Checks that would flip it to Glenview

- Glenview confirms the API is a funded top-two company priority, with a named exec sponsor.
- Glenview will level you to Staff, or give a written promotion path within 12 months.
- Ardent's Staff scope turns out thin, or its equity is weaker by more than the base gap.
