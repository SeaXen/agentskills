# Trailhaus Shopify listing: name, subtitle and search terms

I couldn't ask the skill's interview questions, so I made assumptions, listed at the end. Your proposal needs three changes before it's usable.

## What's wrong with the proposal

1. **The title order is backwards for Shopify.** Shopify's naming guidance is brand-first ("QTeck Announcement Bar", not "Announcement Bar QTeck"). That is the opposite of mobile-ASO advice. `Bundle Discounts & Volume Pricing - Trailhaus` puts keywords first and the brand last. *Source tier: Shopify's own guidance, per the skill's benchmark file.*
2. **The name is too long.** It's 45 characters. Shopify's app-name cap is 30 characters, from my memory of the docs. The skill says to check current limits, and I couldn't browse, so confirm in the listing editor. The subtitle cap I use below (62 characters) is also from memory.
3. **Your search terms break Shopify's term rule.** Terms must be complete words with one concept each. "email marketing" is valid and "email marketing for leads" is not.
   - `bundle discounts for shopify stores` packs in extra words and "shopify" is redundant.
   - `volume pricing app` and `increase average order value` also carry filler words.

## "Name and subtitle are the whole ranking game"

This is only partly right.
- The "≈80% of keyword weight" figure is **one tracker's estimate**. It is practitioner inference, not Shopify-official. Don't promise leadership a magnitude. The direction is plausible, so the rewrite is still the best use of your hours.
- The only **Shopify-official** ranking statement is that Built for Shopify apps rank higher. At 140 installs you probably don't qualify yet. Check the current criteria before planning for it.
- **Practitioner inference** also points to review recency and velocity, install velocity, view-to-install conversion and uninstall rate. A keyword rewrite won't fix a weak gallery, few reviews or churn.

## Before you publish

1. **Record your baseline first.** Note the last 30 days of listing views, installs and view-to-install conversion from the partner dashboard. If you haven't wired GA4 or the Meta Pixel under Distribution → Manage listing → Tracking, do that now. Without it you can't tell whether the rename helped.
2. **Judge the result against noise.** Your traffic is low. Treat a lift under 2% as noise and don't call a win in under a few weeks. This threshold comes from consumer-app-store analogy, so use it only as a generic rule.
3. **Check the first three gallery images.** Shopify now rejects duplicate and logo-only images. Order them hero, then differentiator, then most-loved feature, all in-UI and captioned. Don't bake pricing or review claims into the images.
4. **Set up review asks on sanctioned triggers only.** That means the Reviews API, an in-product prompt at a natural pause, or an ask after a positive support interaction. Never offer discounts or feature unlocks for reviews. Shopify's penalties run up to Partner-account termination.
5. **Add a 30–60 day refresh cycle with a named owner.** Update recency is a freshness signal on Shopify.
