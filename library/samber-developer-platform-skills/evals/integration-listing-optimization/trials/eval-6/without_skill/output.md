# Fernlight upload checklist: Atlassian Marketplace and Shopify App Store

I wrote this from memory and did not check live docs. Items marked 🔎 are exact specs that change often, so confirm them in each marketplace's current listing guidelines before you export anything.

## ❌ Fix the kit first: five assets won't pass as designed

| Asset | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Image 1, marketing homepage | Both stores want screenshots of the product UI, not a website capture | Replace with a product hero view. Keep the homepage as a social or launch asset. |
| Image 2, logo on gradient | A logo is not a screenshot | Upload it in the logo or icon slot (square crop), not the gallery. |
| Images 3–6, 5-star badge overlay | A self-awarded rating is a misleading claim. Both stores show their own reviews. | Remove the badge from all four. |
| Video closing card "from $19/mo" | Each store sets pricing through its own pricing fields. Atlassian pricing is per tier, and Shopify pricing is per plan. A hard-coded price goes stale and conflicts with the listing. | Cut the card, or replace it with a CTA such as "Try Fernlight free" (see the one-per-store tradeoff below). |
| Video customer logos | You need written permission for each logo, and some customers may restrict use | Get sign-off per customer, or remove the logos. |

⚠️ After removing the badge and swapping images 1–2, you are down to four valid screenshots. Both stores want about 3–6, so four is enough, but you can add more product views.

## Shared asset plan

| Asset | Same file on both stores? |
|---|---|
| Promo video, 2:40, voiceover | Yes, once the pricing card and logos are cleared. 🔎 Check each store's length guidance, since 2:40 may run long. |
| Product screenshots (3–6 views) | Partly. The same 1600x900 views can work, but 🔎 Atlassian's gallery size may differ (I recall about 1840x900), so re-export at that size. |
| Logo or icon | No. The two stores need different sizes, so export per store. |

⚠️ **Tradeoff:** a single kit is only safe if it contains no store-specific wording. A screenshot or voiceover that says "Jira" or "Shopify" belongs to one store only. Check the voiceover script for this.

## Atlassian Marketplace checklist

1. ⏳ Confirm the vendor account, the app listing, and the app key are ready.
2. Upload the logo at the required size 🔎 (I recall 144x144).
3. Upload the banner image if the listing needs one 🔎.
4. Add the promo video as a **YouTube or Vimeo link**. I believe it is not a file upload 🔎.
5. Upload the gallery screenshots at the required size 🔎, each with a caption.
6. Add the listing text and the product category.
7. Set pricing in the Marketplace pricing fields. Do not put prices in the media.
8. Complete the support, privacy, and security fields.
9. Submit for review. 🔎 Check the review turnaround.

## Shopify App Store checklist

1. Upload the app icon at **1200x1200**, JPEG or PNG 🔎.
2. Add the promo video as a **YouTube or Vimeo URL** 🔎 (supports the voiceover, but check the length cap).
3. Upload the feature media image at **1600x900**, 16:9, if you are not using the video 🔎.
4. Upload **3–6 desktop screenshots** at 1600x900. They must show the real app UI, not the marketing site.
5. Check every screenshot:
   - No ratings or testimonials.
   - No pricing.
   - No real merchant or customer data.
   - No claims such as "best" or "#1".
6. Fill in the listing copy, the pricing plans in the pricing section, and the support contact.
7. Complete the privacy, compliance, and review-submission steps.
8. Submit for review.

## Same-day launch plan

- 🔴 Don't plan on both going live exactly next month. Shopify review and Atlassian review run on their own schedules, and neither is on your clock.
- Submit both at least 2–3 weeks early 🔎.
- Prepare both listings in draft and approve them separately.
- Time the announcement to whichever store is approved last.
