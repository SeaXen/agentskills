## 4. Certifications

- **Join none in year one.** You have no step-1 evidence that any certification platform reaches a segment you've named.
- **Before joining anything**, name a renewal owner and budget the recurring cost as standing engineering headcount. For example:
  - Slack's Works with Slack requires maintenance modules each release.
  - Salesforce's AppExchange security review charges per submission, and roughly half of first submissions fail.

## 5. Attribution, built before scale

Your empty partner-source field is the biggest blocker to proving any of this. Fix it first:

1. **Make the field required at opportunity creation.**
   - Use a picklist of partners, not free text.
   - Each claim gets a dated ID that predates the outcome.
   - Never reverse-engineer attribution at close.
2. **Report two separate capped numbers.**
   - Sourced: the deal would not exist without the partner. One sourced partner per deal.
   - Influenced: the deal existed and the partner advanced it. Split by role.
   - Set credit caps before the quarter starts.
   - Never blend them into one number.
3. **Claim window: about 14 days from deal creation.**
4. **Compensation:** don't let AEs earn less on partner deals. If you can't change comp now, state it as a known risk.
5. **Forecast:** partner pipeline joins the normal forecast review under the same exit criteria.
6. **Motion-specific rules:** referral, co-sell and OEM get different attribution rules. Year one needs only the referral and co-sell rules.
7. **Last quarter's win/loss notes** are evidence for step 1. They are not attributable revenue, so don't back-fill them as partner-sourced.

**Executive KPI set**, added to dashboards leadership already checks daily:
- Sourced vs. influenced revenue.
- Activation rate.
- Time to first deal.
- Partner CAC vs. direct CAC.

**Baseline** the first two quarters. A common working benchmark is partner CAC under ~1.5x direct CAC, but it's vendor-published and directional. Don't promise it to the CFO.

## 6. Org placement

- **Staffing:** you plus a part-time integration engineer, per the assumed effort ceiling. Add an integration PM later if the Gusto build slips.
