| # | Language | Why it's on the list |
|---|----------|----------------------|
| 1 | **TypeScript / JavaScript** | Most common for integrations and web or Node automation. Typed, so it's a good fit for an API-heavy product. |
| 2 | **Python** | The default language for ops, data, and scripting teams. Likely your core buyer persona. |
| 3 | **Go** | Common among platform, infra, and DevOps teams. Easy to ship as a single binary. |

| 5 | **C# / .NET** | Common in Microsoft-centric enterprises and IT ops shops. |
| 6 | **Ruby** | Many older SaaS integrations are Ruby. **PHP** is the usual alternative for this slot. |

The sixth slot is the least certain. Ruby versus PHP versus Kotlin or Swift depends on your customer base.
