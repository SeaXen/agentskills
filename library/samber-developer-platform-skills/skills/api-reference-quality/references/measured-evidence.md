## Try-it / time-to-first-call effects - vendor-produced, not isolated

| Figure                                                                                                                                                                                            | Source                                                                            | Evidence quality                                                                             | Caveats to state when citing                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Forking a ready-to-run collection cut time-to-first-call from 17 to 10 minutes (1.7x) for one publisher; across the full sample, developers were 1.7x-56x faster; "20x" is the headline aggregate | Joyce Lin, "Improve Your Time to First API Call by 20x", Postman Blog, April 2023 | Vendor experiment (Postman has a direct commercial interest in the collection/console story) | Not a controlled, independent result; measures a ready-to-run collection, not an embedded try-it console in isolation. |
| "At least 10x, maybe more" faster time-to-first-call and a 225% rise in developer registrations after launching interactive docs with sandbox testing                                             | Postman, Moneris customer story, 2025                                             | Vendor customer story                                                                        | Conflates the console with sandbox testing and better onboarding generally. Cite as a named case study only.           |

Both figures above bundle the try-it console with other changes, so neither isolates its effect. Recommend try-it on the strength of the Stripe/GitBook practitioner consensus (GitBook grades interactive try-it as the differentiator between good and great reference docs), and measure your own before/after time-to-first-call rather than reusing any multiplier above.

## Benchmark attribution

Stripe and Twilio as the paired gold standard is well-corroborated practitioner consensus (independently named by Mintlify, DEV Community, Apidog, and others) - not a controlled comparison against unnamed competitors. Compare against their checkable mechanisms (see the audit-rubric file's checklist), never their reputation.
