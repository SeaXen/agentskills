# Sourcing channels

Contents: apidays as the confirmed venue, the PlatformCon near-miss, and why this pool overlaps more with senior backend sourcing than DevRel sourcing.

## The one directly confirmed, named venue for this exact audience

**apidays** (apidays.global) is the clearest sourced answer to "where do API-platform and API-product people gather." It explicitly names its audience as "API platform engineers," "API product managers," and "decision-makers shaping API-first strategies" - the two named roles landing squarely on this skill's tracks. Confirmed scale: 100,000+ past attendees, past speakers including Docker's creator, Twilio's CEO, and AsyncAPI's creator; structured tracks including the OpenAPI Initiative's own OAI track; a running "apiMasters" certification program (see the skill body for the caveat on its hiring weight). This is a legitimate sourcing and community channel to use.

## The confirmed near-miss: PlatformCon serves a different population

PlatformCon (platformcon.com) is real, large (280,000+ practitioners in its community, a six-city world tour, hundreds of speakers), and easy to mistake for the same audience because it also uses the word "platform." It explicitly serves **internal developer platform (IDP)** practitioners - "platform engineers, platform architects, and infrastructure teams... responsible for building and maintaining internal platforms at scale," covering IDPs, agentic development platforms, and organizational scaling for enterprise infrastructure teams. **Do not list PlatformCon as a sourcing channel for external/public API-platform hires without this caveat** - a recruiter fishing there for a Stripe/Twilio-style API platform engineer or an API product manager will draw from the wrong specialization.

## Why this pool overlaps more with senior backend sourcing than DevRel sourcing

The target candidates for platform-engineer and integration/partner-engineer roles are screened on system design and API design judgment - the same axis general senior backend-engineering hiring already screens on, just with an API-design specialization layered in. Stripe's own posting explicitly welcomes candidates "whether they've spent their entire career in infrastructure or are looking to apply their skills... for the first time," implying the sourcing pool is general senior/staff backend engineers, not a separately credentialed specialist pool. DevRel sourcing, by contrast, draws from public content and community output (talks given, blog posts, community roles) as the primary signal - a different pool almost by construction, since the deliverable being screened for is different.

Practical implication: general senior-backend-engineering sourcing channels (engineering referral networks, senior-IC hiring pipelines, open-source contribution history as a screen, per GitLab's explicit requirement) will surface more relevant platform-engineer and integration-engineer candidates than DevRel-adjacent channels (developer-advocate job boards, DevRel Slack or Discord communities, conference speaker circuits) will. The API Product Manager track sources more like a technical product-management hire - general PM pipelines filtered for API or developer-facing product experience - than like either backend engineering or DevRel.

## What was not confirmed

No dedicated, named job board or community specifically for "API Product Manager" hiring, distinct from general technical-PM communities, was found or verified. Treat general product-management sourcing channels, filtered for developer-facing/API product experience, as the working answer until a more specific channel is confirmed. A "Postman certification" community or channel was referenced in passing but could not be verified live - do not cite it as a sourcing channel without re-confirming.
