# Partner-onboarding archetypes: five sourced platforms

Partner-program structures churn frequently - one platform renamed its entire tier model in July 2024, another added identity verification in 2025 - so re-verify any specific against the platform's current official pages before citing it in a deliverable.

## Shopify - self-service, speed-first

- **Entry:** self-service signup form, no application gate, no fee or minimum revenue requirement. The program markets speed itself: "from idea to launch in days, not weeks."
- **Provisioning:** unlimited free development stores with no time limit, including a fake payment gateway for testing payment flows.
- **Education:** Shopify Academy is optional - a three-course App Development Fundamentals path ending in a Verified Skills assessment, positioned as merchant-facing credibility, never a submission prerequisite.
- **Support:** the heaviest onboarding-stage support of the self-service platforms studied - an official 35,000+-member partner Slack community, Partner Townhalls, a developers Discord, and a 100,000+-member forum.
- **Tiers:** "Registered" is a label assigned at signup, not a graded gate; real tiers are post-launch performance ladders.
- **Review stage:** 5-10 business days officially, but community threads describe 2-3 rejection cycles before approval, clustering on UI-standard compliance, GDPR webhook requirements, billing-API compliance, and broken support contacts.

## Atlassian - ungated instance, staged guide, split commercial track

- **Entry:** immediate, form-free provisioning - "No request forms needed here" - of a free cloud developer instance. Since a 2025 marketplace-security update, the program splits "Developers" (build freely, no agreement) from "Marketplace Partners" (commercial sellers, who sign an agreement and pass KYC/KYB identity verification).
- **Provisioning:** the free dev instance ships with working products (project tracker, wiki, service desk seats) at signup; the first-party app platform provides hosting and API auth, lowering the technical floor.
- **Education:** continuous, non-gating track; since late 2025 a free 45-minute platform-fundamentals certificate framed as a career credential, not a submission gate.
- **Stage model:** the official onboarding guide names four phases - **Plan, Build, Launch, Grow** - with enterprise-trust research placed in Plan and the monetization choice (Free / Paid-via-Atlassian / Paid-via-Vendor) placed at Launch, as the last gate before listing.
- **Tiers:** none at entry; partner-program levels are reviewed automatically each July and require at least one paid live app first.
- **Review stage:** 10-15 business days to approval, with 5-10 business days just to start review - among the heavier self-service gates studied.

## Slack - fastest first app, thinnest support, hidden pre-review wall

- **Entry:** zero forms before code - a four-command CLI sequence (install, login, create, run) puts a working app in the developer's own workspace, which doubles as the sandbox.
- **Provisioning:** the free Developer Program layers provisioned enterprise-grade sandboxes on top - quota'd (up to 10 per 30 days, 2 active, 6-month lifetime), with a card required for identity verification only.
- **Education:** no developer certification exists at any stage.
- **Support:** the thinnest of the five - docs and community channels, no partner-manager layer for individual developers.
- **Tiers:** none; Developer Program membership is a binary free enrollment.
- **Review stage:** preliminary review up to ~10 business days, then a functional review that can run up to ~10 weeks for new submissions. Timings have varied across doc versions. Since August 2025 the review requires ≥5 active-workspace installs before it can start (lowered from ≥10) - a documented pre-review eligibility wall partners hit after believing onboarding was complete.

## HubSpot - free entry, education loop, post-launch certification wall

- **Entry:** self-service developer-account signup, no payment information required at any point.
- **Provisioning:** free app test accounts (~90-day renewable) included; full production-mirror sandboxes gated behind a paid enterprise subscription.
- **Education:** the Academy's cyclical "Learn. Build. Certify. Repeat." loop - ongoing lessons, certifications, developer blog and newsletter - positioned as continuous enablement, not an entry checkbox.
- **Support:** named Partner Development Managers for commercial/solutions partners, plus a developer Slack community, forums, and docs.
- **Tiers:** four technology-partner tiers, all post-launch - a developer holds no tier until their app is approved and live; tiers upgrade quarterly on trailing-12-month performance.
- **Review stage:** initial check within 10 business days, whole process capped at 60 days, $0 fee. App certification, however, opens only after ≥6 months listed and ≥60 active unaffiliated installs - a post-launch wall, not a submission prerequisite.

## Salesforce - the application-gated outlier

- **Entry:** registration itself is a reviewed gate - an application to the partner community with a days-long review, and a company-level Partner Program Agreement that blocks every user at the company until an authorized agent accepts it.
- **Provisioning:** post-acceptance, a free Partner Business Org is auto-provisioned with license-management and revenue-reporting tooling; partners then create dev, test, and demo orgs from an environment hub - a genuinely complex multi-org model.
- **Stage model:** a seven-milestone official onboarding guide, and the one true pre-launch stage ladder studied. Registered → Exploration → Build precede listing, while Select and Summit are post-launch (platform-documented). The ladder replaced a score-based tier model in July 2024 - program churn is frequent, re-verify.
- **Education:** continuous and non-gating (gamified learning trails, partner learning camp); role certifications are career credentials, not listing prerequisites.
- **Support:** the richest studied - dedicated partner support, Partner Account Managers for qualifying partners, biweekly office hours, onboarding Q&A groups, and case-based security-review office hours.
- **Review stage:** the mandatory security review is the hard gate - $999 per submission attempt for paid apps ($0 free), roughly 1-2 weeks initial verification plus 3-4 weeks testing, 6-9 weeks end-to-end with queue time, and a ~50% first-time fail rate per Salesforce's own estimate as relayed by third parties, not an official published figure. Budget guidance from the same research: 2-3+ months from build to listing once realistic resubmission is included.

## Cross-platform synthesis

- Four of five programs have **no entry gate beyond a form** and **no entry tier that gates anything**; Salesforce's application gate and pre-launch stages are justified by weeks-to-months build complexity, not a norm.
- All five provision **build access before any human review**; review effort concentrates at the submission gate.
- All five keep **education non-gating**; certification is a credibility signal or a post-launch quality badge, never a submission prerequisite.
- Support depth tracks partner commercial value, not partner count: named managers appear only for commercial partners.
- Friction concentrates at two points - before the first API call, and at the review gate - see the funnel reference file.
