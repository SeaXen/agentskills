Short version: **don't send identical text to all five.** Same underlying story, but each channel needs its own framing — as written, this plan burns your best shot on Hacker News and underperforms on two others.

**✅ X — post as drafted.** Home base, your audience already has context (pinned roadmap, prior numbers, support threads). No changes needed.

**⚠️ Newsletter — same facts, more depth, don't reuse the social copy verbatim.** Subscribers opted into depth; a tweet-length paragraph under-delivers here. Expand: what "40" actually means (MRR? logos? both?), one specific customer story, what's next. This is your only fully-owned channel — treat it as the anchor piece, not a copy of the tweet.

**⚠️ LinkedIn — reformat, don't paste.** LinkedIn's algorithm buries plain announcements and link-shaped posts; it rewards a hook + line breaks + personal narrative. "Hit 40 customers" as a flat statement reads as corporate filler. Restructure: 1-line hook → the numbers → one hard lesson → what's next. Strip any X-native hashtags/@mentions.

**❌ Hacker News — reconsider entirely, this is the real risk.** "We hit 40 customers" is neither a Show HN (nothing new to try) nor a Launch HN (no major launch) — it's a plain milestone announcement, which is exactly what HN's culture flags as self-promotion. Best case it dies at 0 points; worst case it gets flagged, which taints the account for a future genuine Show HN. Two real options:
1. Skip HN this round.
2. Rewrite as a genuinely technical post ("what we learned building privacy-first analytics to 40 paying customers" — real architecture/tradeoff content, not a numbers flex), posted as a regular submission, not "Show HN."

Before either: do you have any existing HN karma or comment history? A brand-new account posting a company milestone is close to a guaranteed flag or dead-on-arrival post.

**⚠️ r/SaaS — check the sub's self-promo rule first, then rewrite the register.** Look for a "Self-Promotion" flair/day or a stated helpful-post ratio. If you've never commented there, a polished milestone post reads as drive-by marketing. r/SaaS rewards vulnerability and specifics ("here's exactly what got us from 20 to 40, including what didn't work"), not press-release tone.

**Timing:** don't blast same-day. Owned channels first (newsletter + X, early week), LinkedIn a day or two later with its own edit, Reddit/HN last and only once each has a bespoke version — treat those two as separate go/no-go decisions, not part of the batch.

**The underlying issue:** your entire public presence lives on X — that's exactly why the identical draft works there and on the newsletter (people already know your voice) and exactly why it'll feel off everywhere you have zero prior presence. The risk isn't the milestone, it's channel-voice mismatch.
