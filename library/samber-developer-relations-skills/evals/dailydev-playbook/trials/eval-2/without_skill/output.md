## Why

1. **The upvote ring is fake engagement.**
   - Most platforms ban it, and I expect daily.dev does too. I did not check its current terms. Read them before you reply.
   - Likely result: removed posts or banned accounts. Your brand is attached to the posts.
   - Screenshots of a rig like this travel fast on HN and Reddit.
2. **Trust is your product.** You sell a payments API. Developers pick one on reliability and honesty. A staged-hype story hurts you more than missing signups.
3. **The pin is a paid endorsement.**
   - Squad members think the admin chose it. You paid for it.
   - Hidden material connections break FTC endorsement rules in the US and similar rules in the EU and UK. The agency and your company can both be liable.
   - Disclosed sponsorship is fine. Hidden is not.
4. **Referral links are fine only if disclosed.**
   - A tutorial full of partner-program links reads as affiliate spam.
   - Use UTM-tagged links to your docs and signup in the tutorial. Keep partner referral codes for a disclosed "sponsored" line.
5. **Upvotes do not make signups.** They push the post up the feed for a day. A 30k-member Squad is reach, not readers. Expect a small fraction to see the pin, and fewer to click.
6. **Money looks off.** The pin is $300 of a $4k budget. Ask where the rest goes. Likely most is agency margin.
