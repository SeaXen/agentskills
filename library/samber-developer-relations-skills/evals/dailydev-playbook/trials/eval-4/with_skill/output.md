## 3. Entry path

1. **Source suggestion: no.** Your blog is a company blog, and you'd also need 250 reputation and a review of up to 30 days. [platform]
2. **Already on an indexed publication or DEV?** If yes, claim authorship by adding `<meta name="twitter:creator" content="@handle">` to the post head. Add the same X handle under Account details on daily.dev, and email support for posts picked up before the claim. You get the listing notice, an author badge and analytics. Being picked guarantees a feed slot, not traffic. If the posts are only on your own blog, skip this and don't cross-post to DEV. DEV content may rank lower right now while daily.dev prioritizes niche sources. [platform]
3. **New Post** to the poster's profile ("Everyone") plus at most 3 Squads in total, your own Squad included. Docs disagree on whether New Post needs 250 reputation, so check the composer. If it blocks you, post inside your own Squad, where admins and moderators are never gated. [platform]

  - Add affiliate links.
  - Publish AI-drafted text. daily.dev screens for it, so write the post and commentary yourself.

## 7. Paid decision
