## 3. Entry path

Ferrule's project blog counts as a company or personal blog, so it **cannot be a source** [platform]. Use this route:

1. Publish through **New Post** to your profile plus at most 3 Squads in total, your own Squad included.
2. If a post is already on an indexed publication or on DEV, claim authorship. Add `<meta name="twitter:creator" content="@handle">` to the post head, add the same X handle in Account details on daily.dev, and email support for posts picked up earlier. You get the listing notice, an author badge and analytics.
3. The docs disagree on whether New Post needs 250 reputation. Check the composer. If it blocks you, post inside your own Squad, where admins are never gated.

Skip the Verified Company Squad. It is sales-assisted with a quarterly commitment, and it fits teams that publish weekly and plan to stay at least two quarters.

## 8. Paid decision

**No paid spend now.** Conditions that would change that:

- **Post Boost:** only for a post that earned organic upvotes in its first 24 hours [self-set].
- **Squad Boost:** only after the Squad has 10+ quality posts [self-set]. Otherwise new members land on an empty Squad and leave.
- **Verified Company Squad or Ads plan:** only if you publish weekly and want auto-posting for at least two quarters. daily.dev publishes no rate card, so price from a written quote.
- **Never:** paying anyone to post, upvote or pin. That risks demotion, removal or suspension [platform].
