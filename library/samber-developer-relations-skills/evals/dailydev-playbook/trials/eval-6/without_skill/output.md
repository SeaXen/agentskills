**Short answer:** about half of it. Two parts of your plan work against your goal, and the goal itself isn't in your control. I'm going from memory of daily.dev's Squads, with no live check, so verify the settings below in the Squad admin UI.

## What I'd change

**1. Private start: fine for setup, but keep it to a few days.**
- A private Squad is invite-only and won't appear in the directory. You can't be Featured while it's private.
- Use private for avatar, description, rules, and the first seed posts. Then go public.

**2. "Let anyone post without review so it feels active": drop this.**
- Open posting on a public Squad attracts spam, off-topic links, and low-effort self-promo. That is the opposite of a Squad worth featuring.
- Real activity comes from you. Post on a schedule yourself, with maintainers and contributors helping.
- Turn **post moderation on** at launch. Loosen it later for trusted members once the norms are set.
- Don't use sockpuppet accounts or ask people to mass-upvote. It's detectable and would hurt your chance of being featured.

**3. Invite Discord when live: yes, but seed it first.**
- Have 10–15 good posts up before the invite, so the first joiners see a living Squad.
- Good seed posts for a Rust framework:
  - release notes
  - a "how X works in Ferrule" deep dive
  - benchmarks
  - migration guides
  - contributor spotlights
  - good RFC or issue threads
- Invite in waves: Discord announcement, pinned message, README badge, docs site link. Ask people to join and post. Don't ask them to upvote.

## The Featured goal

- As far as I know, Featured is curated by the daily.dev team. I don't know of published criteria and no application form, so I can't promise it, least of all within 2 weeks.
- 4k stars is a respectable signal, but it isn't a guarantee.
- Set goals you control: member count, posts per week, comment rate, and the share of members who aren't maintainers.
- Once you have two weeks of real activity, contact daily.dev through their official channel. Ask what Featured requires and whether your Squad qualifies.

## Suggested 2-week timeline

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Days 1–3 | Set up the Squad privately: name, description, rules, banner, moderators. Draft 10–15 posts. |
| Day 4 | Switch to public with moderation on. Publish the first 5 posts. |
| Days 5–7 | One post per day. Invite maintainers and contributors first. |
| Day 8 | Announce on Discord, README, and docs. |
| Days 9–14 | Reply to every thread. Track members, posts, and comments. |
| Day 14 | Contact daily.dev with the numbers and ask about Featured. |

Two questions would sharpen this. Who besides you can moderate and post regularly? And can you spare time daily, or only a few times a week?
