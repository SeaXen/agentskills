## 3. Replacement plan

**Before the show (the 80%)**
1. Build a target list of 40–60 named accounts and contacts. Use AWS co-sell and customer lists, plus attendees who will be on site.
2. Book 15-minute meetings 2–3 weeks ahead. Use cold email and LinkedIn.
3. Promote each theater session with a named topic and a time slot, by email and social. This replaces the flyer walkers.

**On the booth**
1. **Layout:** a demo station with headphones or a directional speaker, and a small theater area with seats for [N] people. Add a meeting corner.
2. **Sessions:** [N] per day on a fixed schedule, 15–20 minutes each. End each with a specific call to action, such as a booked follow-up.
3. **Audio:** a line-array or directional speaker set to under the hall's dB cap. Use silent-disco headsets for the demo if the cap is strict.
4. **Giveaway:** use an item that works as a qualifying step. Give it only after a 2-minute conversation or a booked meeting. Pick something people keep using, such as a power bank, a notebook, or a docs-themed item.
5. **Scanning:**
   - Use official lead retrieval for badges.
   - Add a 3-field qualifier on every scan: role, AWS use case, next step.
   - Sync to HubSpot daily with an `event=reinvent-2026` source tag.
   - Use your own app only for non-badge capture, such as QR forms at the theater.
6. **Staffing:** staff in shifts of 2–3 hours. Put 4–5 people on the floor at once: 1 greeter, 2 demo, 1 meeting host.

**Hiring (15 engineers)**
1. Don't run it from the booth.
2. Run a hiring breakfast or dinner for 8–12 invited engineers, or put an engineering lead on a 1-hour "ask us anything" slot in the theater.
3. Put a QR code to the jobs page on one booth sign. Staff who are engineers answer hiring questions casually.

**After the show**
1. Within 24–48 hours, send a personal note to every hot conversation. Reference what you discussed and propose a specific next step.
2. Warm contacts get one asset tied to their stated problem.
3. Scans with no conversation get one light touch or none.
4. Report cost per qualified meeting and pipeline influenced. Ignore scan counts.
