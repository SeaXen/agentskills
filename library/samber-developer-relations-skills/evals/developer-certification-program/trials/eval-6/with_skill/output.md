# Orvalon Certified Integrator: training and exam-delivery partner model

I couldn't run the interview, so every input I lacked is an assumption in section 0. Correct any of them and the affected section changes. Every number below is self-set unless marked sourced.

## 0. Your proposal against the program rules

Four of the five parts break a rule the certification-program skill sets. Each is replaced below.

| Your proposal | Verdict | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Training firms teach the official prep course | ✅ Keep | Authorised training partners, with published criteria (§3) |
| Training firms run exam sittings for their own students | ❌ Cut | A body that trains and proctors has a reason to pass its own students. Use an independent exam-delivery partner (§4) |
| Pay 40% of the exam fee for every student who passes | ❌ Cut | A revenue share on exam vouchers sold is allowed. A share of passes is not. Pay on vouchers sold, whatever the result (§5) |
| Tomasz, lead item writer, writes the official prep course | ❌ Cut | Item writers never author prep courses. Tomasz keeps the item bank. A different author writes the course (§3) |
| Move the real exam into the MOOC quiz engine | ❌ Cut | Course-platform quizzes are unproctored and only the vendor awards the credential. The engine hosts the free practice quiz instead (§4) |
| Give the training firms the current item bank | ❌ Cut | Live items never go to a training partner. Publish the blueprint, domain weights, retired sample items and practice environments (§3) |

The cuts also protect the 200 current holders. A pass share, a trainer-run exam, a quiz-engine exam and a shared bank each give a path to a credential that means less than it did. Together they would devalue the credential the 200 hold.

**Assumptions, none confirmed:**

- **A1.** Clients of freelancers and integration firms use the badge to shortlist them, through referrals, CVs or tenders. I haven't seen evidence. §8 makes this the first check.
- **A2.** The badge is a skill badge (a bounded claim, assessed tasks). If you market it as a "certification" with job-level claims, §1 applies.
- **A3.** The current exam is item-based and may be multiple-choice. If so it is already weak against leaks and AI assistants. Moving it to partners multiplies that exposure.
- **A4.** Orvalon has a standing owner for the item bank, and Tomasz is not the only person who knows it.
- **A5.** The exam fee, per-sitting cost and yearly volume are unknown. §5 gives the formula and leaves the figures blank.

## 1. Gate verdict

- **Outside consumer:** clients and procurement teams that choose an integrator by credential (A1, **unverified**).
- **Action to confirm:** shortlisting, tender scoring, or browsing a "find a certified expert" page.
- **Evidence needed before scaling:** a count of the last year's engagements, tenders or inbound leads that cited the badge. Without it, the three partners just sell a badge nobody reads.
- **Ceiling:** skill badge. Role certification needs all three of: a buyer contract or job posts naming it, two years of funded renewal and re-alignment, and a standing item-bank owner.
- **Marketing consequence:** if the badge is a skill badge, reword partner materials from "certified" to "holds the Orvalon Certified Integrator badge". Don't present it as a certification to a buyer's contract.
- **Alternative if the evidence is missing:** don't scale the delivery model. Run the directory and fix the consumer side first.

## 2. Roles and the firewall

| Role | Who | May | May not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certifying body | Orvalon | Own the bank, the cut score and the award, revocation and appeals | Delegate the award |
| Item writers | Tomasz and the item team | Write, review and pilot items | Author prep courses or teach partners |
| Course author | A person outside the item team | Write the prep course from the public blueprint | See live items |
| Training partners | Poland, Brazil, India firms | Teach and sell courses, sell vouchers | Proctor, see live items, write items, award, promise a pass |
| Exam-delivery partner | Independent proctoring service or test-centre network | Run sittings as a contracted processor | Hold the item bank, set the cut score, decide the award |
| Learning platform | Your MOOC | Host the prep course and the free practice quiz | Host the assessed exam |

A training firm can sell and teach but never touches the live exam. A trainer who sits for the badge does so through the independent route like any candidate.

## 3. Training partners

**Authorisation criteria (all required):**

1. Every teaching trainer holds the credential.
2. Each trainer passes a teach-back session run by Orvalon.
3. The firm teaches from the official blueprint only.
4. The firm signs the partner agreement: no pass guarantees, no live-item possession, no proctoring of its own students.

**Operations:**

- Orvalon audits a sample of sessions each year.
- Orvalon publishes the partner list.
- Orvalon revokes authorisation for pass guarantees or leaked items.
- The agreement names who decides on revocation and how a partner appeals.

**The prep course:**

- A person outside the item team writes it, from the blueprint and retired sample items.
- It is hosted on your MOOC platform. A public marketplace brings learners already browsing for the skill. Your own hosted instance keeps pricing, data and branding yours. If services firms will enrol staff, choose hosted.
- It ships with a free practice quiz on the platform's quiz engine, which is the right use for that engine. It links out to exam booking.
- Training partners license or teach the same course. They add local-language delivery, labs and classroom time, not different exam content.

**What the partners get instead of the bank:**

- the published blueprint with domain weights
- retired sample items
- practice environments, if the exam is hands-on

**Tomasz.** Keep him on items and ask him to review the public blueprint's accuracy. He doesn't draft lessons, and his knowledge of live items doesn't leave the item team.

**If the bank was ever shared.** I assume it was not. If any item went to a partner, treat it as leaked: pull it, rotate, and check pass rates for a jump.

## 4. Exam delivery

**Where the exam runs.** The assessed exam stays off the MOOC platform. The student finishes the course, books a sitting through Orvalon's exam system, and Orvalon awards the result. "One place" is a seamless link, not one quiz engine. The course platform can show the credential after award through a verified link, with no exam content on it.

**Who proctors:**

- **Default:** live online proctoring by an independent service, contracted as a processor. Orvalon keeps the bank, cut score and award.
- **Where candidates' connections fail or a buyer requires supervision:** an independent test-centre network.
- **Training-firm premises:** they can't host their own students' sittings. If the premises are used as a test centre at all, the proctor must be a staff member of the independent delivery partner, and the room must carry no training-firm staff presence. I don't recommend this at launch.

**Identity:**

- No automated face processing at launch.
- A human proctor can watch the webcam.
- Run spot live verification on a sample of passes, so a strong engineer can't sit for colleagues.

**Privacy review.** Do it before contracting a proctoring service. Candidates sit in Poland (GDPR), Brazil (LGPD) and India (DPDP Act). Have counsel confirm retention, processor contracts and cross-border transfer for each. This is a flag, not legal advice.

**AI policy.** Write one into the candidate agreement:

- **Allowed** is credible only if the job uses assistants. Then the claim becomes "competent with their own tools".
- **Banned** is enforceable only under live proctoring with screen capture.

**Accommodations.** Offer extra time, assistive technology and breaks, with a published request process. Keep accommodation records away from the partner and the candidate record.

## 5. Economics

**What each side earns:**

- Training partners earn from course fees.
- Orvalon earns from exam fees.
- A revenue share on vouchers sold through a trainer is allowed.

**Voucher share rule:**

- Pay a percentage of the voucher price when the voucher is sold, and claw back if it's refunded.
- It pays identically whether the student passes, fails or never sits.
- Pay on retake vouchers the same way.
- Track each partner's vouchers by code and report them separately from organic demand.
- Vouchers have an expiry, are single-use, and can't be resold.

**Is 40% safe?** Check it, don't assume it. With exam fee F, share rate s, per-attempt delivery cost c, and yearly fixed and operating costs C over N expected attempts, the share is affordable only if:

`F × (1 − s) ≥ c + C / N`

On a partner-sold voucher you keep F(1−s). That must still cover the delivery partner's per-sitting cost plus your item and re-alignment cost. If it doesn't, either the share is smaller or the fee rises. Fill F, c, C and N before signing (A5). Don't set the fee from a competitor's price.

**Fee floor.** A fee one engagement repays is the right level for freelancers paying from their own pocket. Offer seat bundles for firms.

**Other money flows:**

- VAT or GST, withholding and invoicing on each partner payment differ by country. Have finance and counsel confirm them before the first payout.
- Sanctions and payment restrictions apply to any country where vouchers are sold.

## 6. Verification, revocation, renewal

- **Credential:** signed, portable, on Open Badges 3.0. It carries a version stamp and a status entry.
- **Revocation** updates the status entry, not only the web page. The page shows "not valid" with no reason.
- **Verification:** by credential ID or a holder-shared link. A third party can verify without contacting Orvalon.
- **Directory:** a "find a certified expert" page for freelancers and firms, opt-in only. It lists name, firm, credential, region, languages and contact. For this audience it is the main return on the exam fee.
- **Version binding:** bind the badge to the supported major or API version. For a new version, add a short delta task. Quote other vendors' rules only as their choices.
- **Retirement:**
  - Exam version: announce it, honour holders until their expiry, and offer a path to the replacement.
  - Program: honour every unexpired credential, keep verification live for a stated period, and refund or honour sold vouchers.
- **Candidate agreement:** conduct rules, AI policy, item confidentiality, grounds for revocation, and an appeals path with a named decision-maker.

## 7. Operating model

- **Owner:** one named person with recurring weekly hours. **Successor:** one named person. If neither exists, delete every assessed tier and issue a completion badge only (A4).
- **Course author:** outside the item team (§3).
- **Partner manager:** one person for the three partners, with audit time.
- **Rollout (self-set):** pilot with one partner before opening the other two. Choose the one with the best audit readiness. Widen after one clean audit cycle and one stable pass-rate period.
- **Languages:** if the exam is offered in Polish or Portuguese, translated items need a fairness check (does one language group fail an item unusually?) and equating to the English forms. Until you have that, keep the exam in English and teach in the local language.
- **Budget lines:** delivery partner per-sitting cost, voucher share, audit time, course authoring.

## 8. Measurement, go/no-go, kill rule

- **Outcome metric (outside consumer):** engagements holders won citing the badge, directory contact requests from clients, tenders naming it.
- **Health signals:**
  - pass rate per form, per item and **per partner cohort**. A cohort that passes far above the others points to a leak or collusion.
  - voucher redemption rate
  - appeals and revocations
  - re-alignment time per release
  - audit findings
- **Not headlines:** holder count and pass rate.
- **Go/no-go for each new country:** a dated readiness check a few weeks ahead. It covers the partner audit, a signed processor contract, the privacy review, a payout path, and green checkers or item review. The fallback is the course and practice quiz without a local sitting.
- **Kill rule (self-set, label it as yours):** no outcome-metric movement within 18 months, or an unresolved leak across two partners, ends the partner model and returns to Orvalon-run delivery.
