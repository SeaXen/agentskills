A few real risks in this exact shape, flagged before the copy so you can adjust fast:
- **15% recurring + content quota = classic affiliate/ambassador hybrid, not MLM** (no recruiting-of-recruiters). Fine to ship. But pure cash-for-post reads mercenary unless wrapped in real status — so the copy below leans hard on identity/access, not just the check.
- **Disclosure is a legal requirement, not a nice-to-have.** Anyone paid per signup must say so in every post (FTC-style rule, most consumer-protection regimes mirror it). Baked into the terms below.
- **Keep it invite-only for round one.** You hand-picked 6 out of 340 — don't undercut that by making it look like an open form. Copy below reflects that.
- **This is a plain-English deal outline, not a contract.** Route it through a lawyer before money moves — commission agreements are exactly the kind of document that's cheap to fix now and expensive to fix after a payout dispute.

# 4. Deal Terms

### Commission

- **Rate:** 15% of net subscription revenue (after refunds/taxes), recurring monthly.
- **Duration:** For as long as the referred account stays subscribed, provided the Ambassador remains in good standing (see Term below).
- **Exclusions:** Self-referrals, existing paying customers moved onto a link retroactively, and free/trial signups that never convert don't count.

### Attribution & Tracking

- Unique referral link per Ambassador (e.g., klaxon.dev/r/[handle]), tracked via a Stripe-native affiliate tool (Rewardful or Tolt — both plug into Stripe billing directly, so commission math isn't a spreadsheet you maintain by hand).
- **Cookie window:** 90 days, last-touch.
- Referred customer must sign up as new and reach a paid plan within that window.

### Payout

- Monthly, net-15 after month close.
- **Minimum payout threshold:** $50; balances under that roll to the next month.
- Method: Stripe Connect or PayPal, Ambassador's choice.
- **Tax:** Ambassador is responsible for their own tax reporting. US-based Ambassadors submit a W-9 before first payout (1099 issued if annual total exceeds $600); non-US Ambassadors submit a W-8BEN.

### Content Obligation

- One post per month, minimum ~600 words, about something real (a use case, an integration, a debugging story) — not a thin "check out Klaxon" post.
- Published wherever the Ambassador wants (their own blog, dev.to, Klaxon's blog as a guest post) — their choice, their byline.
- **Must include a disclosure line** stating the commission relationship (e.g., "I'm a Klaxon Ambassador and earn a commission if you subscribe through my link") — this isn't optional, it's a legal requirement wherever they publish.
- Klaxon can reshare and link to it; editing requires the Ambassador's sign-off. They keep copyright.

### Missed Months

- 1 missed month: friendly check-in, no penalty.
- 2 consecutive missed months: Ambassador status paused — badge and channel access pause, but commission on referrals already generated keeps paying out.
- Reinstated on request once posting resumes, or removed if no response after 30 days.

### Term & Exit

- Rolling, no fixed end date; reviewed quarterly.
- Either side can end the arrangement with 30 days' notice.
- **On voluntary or good-standing exit:** commission on referrals already made continues (grandfathered); no new referral commissions accrue after exit.
- **On removal for cause** (fraud, abuse, TOS violation): all commission — past and future — stops immediately.

### IP & Usage

- Klaxon can use the Ambassador's name, photo, and quotes on the roster page and in marketing materials referencing the program, while they remain an active Ambassador.
- Remove this on request within 5 business days of them leaving the program.
