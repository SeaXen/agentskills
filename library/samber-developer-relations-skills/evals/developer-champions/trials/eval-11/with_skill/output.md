# Quillrun Navigators

**The people who help other developers survive a schema migration.**

You've run the risky migration at 2am. You've answered the question in Discord before we woke up. You've filed the issue with a repro instead of just a complaint. Quillrun Navigators is how we say thanks — with access, not swag.

## What you get

- **A seat at the table.** A private Slack channel straight to the Quillrun engineering team — not support, the people writing the migration engine.
- **See it before anyone else.** Early access to every release candidate, with a direct line to shape it before it ships.
- **The annual summit.** One trip, one room, every Navigator and the whole team, on us.

## What we're looking for

Any two of the following gets you considered:

- You've shipped real migrations with Quillrun in production.
- You answer other people's questions in our community — Discord, GitHub, Stack Overflow, wherever.
- You've written or spoken about Quillrun — a post, a talk, a stream.
- You've filed sharp, well-reproduced bug reports or feature requests.

We're not scoring follower counts or conference badges. We're scoring whether you make the next person's migration easier.

## What we ask

- Give us honest feedback — including the criticism.
- Be decent to people who are newer at this than you.
- Keep release-candidate details under embargo until we ship.

## What we won't ask

- You don't speak for Quillrun, and you don't promise our roadmap.
- You're not unpaid support. Redirect escalations to us.
- No quotas, ever. This is recognition, not a job.

## Terms

Navigator status runs for 12 months and renews on activity, not tenure — we'll check in before your term ends, using the same criteria above. Life happens: tell us if you need to pause. When your term ends without renewal, you keep the title as **Navigator Alumni**, not a deletion.

Free access and product carry a material connection — if you post about Quillrun as a Navigator, say so.
