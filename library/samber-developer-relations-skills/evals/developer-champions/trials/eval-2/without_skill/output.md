# Harbourline Champions Program — Recommended Shape

**Bottom line:** flat, invite-only, capped cohort (20-25 people), one-year term that renews every year at the conference itself. No portal, no dashboard, no points system — one spreadsheet, your existing forum/Slack, and the conference you already run. Ongoing cost: ~8 hrs/month, plus one concentrated week of extra work per year around the event.

## Why this shape, not another one

- Tiered (bronze/silver/gold) needs a system to track progression. You have none. ❌
- Open, rolling-admission membership grows without limit — by year three it eats your whole month. ❌
- "Champion for life" has the same flaw: headcount only grows, your time budget doesn't. ❌
- Flat + capped + annual-term is the only shape whose admin cost stays constant regardless of program age. ✅

## Structure

- **Size:** cap at 20-25 (roughly the top 1.5% of your 1,400 90-day actives). Small enough for one person to know everyone by name.
- **Term:** one year, conference-to-conference. Champions are named or renewed live on stage every year — this is what makes "runs forever" free, because the conference is already your annual forcing function. Don't invent a second one.
- **Tier:** none. Everyone gets the same benefits. Levels mean tracking progression, which means a system you don't have.

## Finding champions — zero build

1. Pull your forum's own built-in stats. Most platforms (Discourse, Circle, similar) already compute top posters, solutions marked, trust level, likes received, at no engineering cost — that's your shortlist for free.
2. Open a two-week nomination window with a plain Google Form. Staff and peers nominate. Form plus spreadsheet is the entire "system."
3. Score everyone in that same spreadsheet: activity signal + nominations + do they already help other users. Pick the top 20-25.
4. Invite personally, one email each, not a mass blast. Don't announce who wasn't picked — just don't oversell how many spots exist.

## Benefits — all zero or near-zero ongoing admin

| Benefit | Why it costs you almost nothing |
|---|---|
| Forum badge/flair + one pinned "Champions" page | Native forum feature, no build |
| Private channel with product/eng | Reuse a channel staff already read |
| Comped conference ticket + a champions meetup at the event | Rides the event you're already running |
| One annual swag drop | Single yearly bulk order, not recurring logistics |
| Quarterly 30-45 min group call with product | Group format costs the same regardless of cohort size |
| Early access to release notes, or beta flag if one exists | A BCC list, not new tooling |

Don't offer: content quotas, tiers, cash or commission referrals, anything that needs a dashboard to run.

## The "system" is one spreadsheet

Columns: name, company, cohort year, contact, forum handle, renewal status, notes. That is the entire infrastructure — no CRM, no custom code.

## Time budget — 8 hrs/month, 96 hrs/year

- Private-channel presence, light engagement: 2 hrs/month × 12 = 24 hrs
- Monthly public spotlight post: 1 hr/month × 12 = 12 hrs
- Quarterly group call, prep + run: 2 hrs × 4 = 8 hrs
- Swag logistics, one annual burst: 8 hrs
- Annual selection cycle (nominate, score, invite): ~20 hrs, concentrated in the run-up to the conference
- Conference-week logistics (meetup, briefing, stage list): ~12 hrs
- Buffer: ~12 hrs

Tight, but it only works because nothing here requires building or maintaining software.

## Six-week runway to the stage date

1. **Week 1** — Lock name, benefit list, cohort size, leadership sign-off. Confirm budget for tickets and swag now; that's the one real hard cost.
2. **Week 2** — Pull the forum activity shortlist. Open the nomination form for 5-7 days.
3. **Week 3** — Score, pick the first cohort, send personal invites.
4. **Week 4** — Stand up the private channel, brief the cohort quietly. They should know before the stage moment, not learn it live.
5. **Week 5** — Build the one static page, write the stage script, confirm who's in the room to stand up when named.
6. **Week 6 (conference)** — Announce the program and this year's cohort together. Open next year's nomination form on the same page, same day.

## Why this repeats forever without more headcount

The conference does double duty: announcement moment and annual renewal clock in one. Every year the loop is identical — nominate, select, invite, brief, announce, reopen nominations — so there's no redesign work in year two, three, or ten. The cap keeps cohort size, and therefore your monthly time cost, flat forever. Without that cap, this quietly becomes a second job by year three.

## What to tell leadership now

- ⚠️ A public portal, gamified points, or leaderboards would make a bigger stage story, but none of it is operable at zero engineering headcount. Don't announce a feature you can't run solo.
- ⚠️ The real dollar cost is conference tickets and swag, not your time. Get that budget line signed off before the stage date, not after.
- Undersell scale on stage ("our first champions"), oversell exclusivity. A small, real cohort reads better long-term than a big promise you can't staff.
