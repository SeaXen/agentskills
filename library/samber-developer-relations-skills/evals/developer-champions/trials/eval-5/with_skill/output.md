**Start here: audit the 61, don't recruit 20.** Manager's plan adds people to a broken lifecycle and upgrades the weakest perk tier. Both are named failure modes in a working champions-program playbook. Fix the plumbing first — takes about 2 weeks of part-time work, not months.

## 1. Why the manager's plan makes it worse

- **Recruiting 20 more onto a program with no term, no renewal, no removal path just grows the graveyard faster.** Your active share is 8/61 = 13%. Healthy bar is ≥60%. Adding 20 without fixing intake drops that share further, not up.
- **Swag-first is backwards.** Access (roadmap input, engineer time, early builds) is the highest-value, lowest-cost perk. Status objects — jackets, tumblers, badges — rank *last*. "Perks were front-loaded swag, access never materialized" is the textbook cause of champions going silent by month three. A nicer jacket doesn't fix that; it's the same mistake with better packaging.

Bring this to your manager as the actual finding, not a pushback: *13% active share on a 5-year-old roster nobody's ever pruned. Recruiting into that roster before fixing it wastes the 20 new people too.*

## 2. Audit the 61 (do this first, ~1 week)

Contact everyone not in the "active last quarter" 8. Sort into:

1. **Confirmed moved to unrelated field** (the dozen, maybe more once you check) → move to alumni now. Not a punishment — a status. Keep their dated title, drop them from active obligations.
2. **Reachable, silent, still in-field** → this is your real "at risk" group. They're candidates for renewal against real criteria, not automatic cuts.

Expect the true active-and-reachable pool to land well under 20. That number, not 61, is what you're actually managing.

## 4. The redesign, once the audit lands

**Archetype: move to a published application cohort with a grandfather re-qualification window.** Not a quiet purge — a legitimate re-qualification: publish two-or-more criteria, run every current member's evidence against them, invite renewal for those who clear the bar, move everyone else to alumni. This is what makes 40+ non-renewals defensible instead of arbitrary, since the roster's been public for 5 years.

**Criteria (any two, published):**
1. Direct product contribution (stated floor).
2. Answers other people's questions — weight this highest, it's the least visible, most valuable signal.
3. Teaching in public (talks, workshops, streams).
4. Mentoring newcomers.
5. Non-paywalled content.

**New lifecycle rules (the actual fix):**
- **Term:** 12 months, fixed dates.
- **Renewal:** judged on the last term's published criteria only, activity record sent 90 days ahead.
- **Inactivity clause:** no visible activity/contact for 6 months → ineligible for renewal → alumni.
- **Alumni status:** dated title kept ("Otterlane Advocate, 2024–2026"), low-frequency channel, role in reviewing next cohort. This is where the dozen career-changers go — a status, not a deletion.
- **Removal for conduct:** routed through moderation, never the program owner alone (you need a conduct baseline first — gate 5 above).

**Perk reorder** — access > platform > skill-building > funding > status objects. Put the welcome-kit upgrade last. If your manager wants a visible "we invested" moment, spend it on one recurring access beat (quarterly roadmap briefing, a private engineer channel) — that's what keeps people past month three; a Yeti tumbler doesn't.

## 5. Charter (fill this before any public relaunch — mark what's still open)

1. **Purpose** — OPEN. Ask: product feedback, content volume, regional reach, or retention of people you'd lose otherwise? Pick one.
2. **Room** — OPEN. Confirm audience: employer-approved developers, independent, students, mixed.
3. **Archetype** — application cohort with a one-time grandfather re-qualification window (recommended above).
4. **Size** — self-set: target cohort = your real audited active-and-reachable count, not 61 and not 61+20. Pipeline should run 2–3× that.
5. **Criteria** — the five listed above; adapt to your product.
6. **Obligations** — code of conduct, constructive with newcomers, honest feedback, embargo respect. May not: speak for the company, promise roadmap, act as unpaid support.
7. **Perks** — access first (recurring roadmap/engineer beat), platform, skill-building, funding, then the welcome kit last.
8. **Term** — self-set: 12 months, fixed start/end.
9. **Exit** — 6-month inactivity clause → alumni; conduct removal via moderation, not you alone.
10. **Compliance** — OPEN: employer approval, gift limits, disclosure wording, NDA scope. Needs a pass before relaunch.
11. **Operations** — OPEN: your hours/month, budget/member/year. Get both from your manager before setting cohort size — this is the actual constraint the recruit-20 plan ignores.
12. **Bar** — self-set starting point: ≥60% quarterly active share, ≥70% renewal acceptance, ≤5 business days response time, ≥1 traceable feedback outcome/quarter. Current state (13% active) is a kill-criteria trigger, not a growth signal.
