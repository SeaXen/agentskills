**Core call: title and perks are two different things. Split them. Both of you are right — you're just answering two different questions.**

## Recognition vs active perks

Co-founder's argument ("reward for past work, expiry insults it") is correct — but only for **recognition**. Applies to:

- Insider badge/name on site
- "Insider since 2026" status
- Public credit, contributor page listing

None of that costs you anything ongoing. No reason to ever revoke it. Give lifetime, no debate.

**Active perks** are different animal. Not reward for past work — investment in current relationship. Examples:

- Early build access
- Private Slack/Discord channel with team
- Roadmap input calls
- Conference ticket sponsorship

These cost you real resources (support time, seats, budget) every month someone holds them, active or not. Founder's "reward for work done" logic does not apply here — nobody argues a free trial should stay free forever because user liked month one.

## Structure

Two layers, one program:

1. **Insider (permanent)** — title, badge, public recognition. Granted once, never expires, removed only for cause (below).
2. **Active Insider (rolling)** — perks layer. Requires light ongoing signal (posted in community, used product, opened feedback last ~90-180 days). No signal → perks taper. Title untouched.

Frame the taper as graduation, not demotion: "Insider Emeritus" or similar, not "downgraded." Person keeps everything symbolic, loses only the resource-heavy stuff nobody misses if they've drifted away.

## Removal for cause — define it now, in writing

Founder's "only genuinely bad" exit door is fine, but "genuinely bad" undefined today becomes an argument later. Write the actual list before launch:

- ToS violation / abuse of other members
- Fraud (fake referrals, stolen license keys)
- Public harassment tied to your brand

Nothing vaguer than that. If it's not on the list, it's not grounds for removal. This protects you from "well HE thought it was bad enough" disputes down the line.

## Mistakes to dodge

- Title with zero benefits attached → feels hollow, people don't want it (this is your founder's actual fear — solved by giving real perks at the active layer, not by making title permanent).
- Mandatory activity quotas to KEEP the title → feels like work, kills goodwill. Quotas (if any) gate only the perks layer, never the badge.
- Revoking recognition for inactivity → breaks the promise, exactly what founder is worried about. Don't do this. Ever.

## Rollout

Start invite-only, small (5-10 people). Expand once you've watched perk-taper play out on real humans and confirmed nobody feels burned.
