3x claim correct in math, risky in framing. Retention chart design has 2 bugs. Fix both before board deck.

**"Community up 3x" — real number, wrong story**

312/96 = 3.25x members. 2,900/1,050 = 2.76x posts. 44/12 = 3.67x first-time posters. Math checks out.

Problem: v2.0 ship (Mar 4) + HN front page (Mar 5) = 2 days out of 31. If most of March's 312 signups cluster on those 2 days, "community up 3x" reads to board as sustained growth trend — real story is "one launch spike, durability unknown."

Board-safe framing if spike confirmed: "3x new members in March, driven by v2.0 launch + HN front page. April trend line will show if it sticks." Honest, still a good slide.

**Retention chart — cohort-per-day breaks 3 ways**

1. Low N per day. Feb ~3/day, March ~10/day (outside spike). One member joining or leaving swings a day's retention 33% to 100%. Curve reads as noise, not signal.
2. Right-censoring. Cohort from Mar 30 has 1 day observed. Cohort from Mar 1 has 30+ days observed. Plotted raw, late cohorts look like they churn fast — they just haven't had time yet.
3. HN-spike cohort (~Mar 5) is drive-by traffic, different intent than organic-day signups. Averaged into daily cohorts, it distorts neighbor days without being labeled as a separate population.

Fix:
1. Bucket cohorts by week, not day — smooths N.
2. Only compare cohorts with equal elapsed time (e.g. all cohorts ≥14 days old) — kills right-censoring bias.
3. Pull the Mar 5 (HN) cohort into its own labeled line, separate from organic baseline.
