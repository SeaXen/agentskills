Publishing usernames with case details isn't the fix here — it trades one problem for three new ones. The community-building skill's own CoC template is explicit on this: "Doxxing or privacy violations" sits in the Don'ts list, right next to "All reports are confidential." Breaking that confidentiality now — even for cases already closed — tells every future reporter that "private" is provisional, and that the person they report about might get named later if the mod team needs to prove a point. It also exposes the nine people (including six who got a warning and moved on) to a pile-on in a thread that's already hostile. Detail specific enough to prove the case is often detail specific enough to re-identify the person even without the username attached.
