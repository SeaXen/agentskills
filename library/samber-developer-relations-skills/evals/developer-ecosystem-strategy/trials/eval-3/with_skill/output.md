## Bottom line

CFO model has 2 real flaws. Not a minor tuning issue — fix before pitch, not after.

1. **20% + $600/yr fee, on zero live partners, violates the core rule of complementor economics: tax kills supply before market exists.** Right move: take-rate stays 0% til liquidity real (some integrations actually live, moving real revenue). Charge now = charge on nothing.
2. **20% revenue share fits ISV, not agencies.** Agencies bill implementation hours, no recurring product revenue to skim. "20% of anything sold through us" doesn't map to their business at all — that's likely root of "what's in it for us." You pitched wrong currency to wrong builder type.

## Fix — recommended structure

1. **Take-rate: 0% for founding cohort**, revisit only after a real liquidity signal (e.g. 5 live integrations, or first $X moved through partner-built work). Write the revisit trigger down now, not "someday."
2. **$600 listing fee: waive for founding partners.** "Keep directory clean" solved by review/listing standard (you approve what's listed), not a cash toll — toll filters for who can afford $600, not who's good.
3. **Split the offer by builder type** — this is the actual fix to "what's in it for us":
   - **Agencies** (×3): referral flow (real leads from your 180 accounts) + certification/badge + priority support line. Not revenue share — they don't have recurring revenue to share.
   - **ISV** (×1): revenue share can make sense here — it's their actual product, actual recurring revenue. Still start at 0% or a low intro rate for first 6-12 months, step up once volume proves out.
4. Revenue share language on the ISV side only — strike "recurring revenue share on anything a partner sells" as universal, rewrite scoped to ISV product revenue.
