Notes on the drafting choices, since you'll want to adjust before sending:

- The bundle discount is the headline ask, not a footnote — anchoring on the bigger number first makes the single-event 20% look like the easy fallback, not a fresh negotiation.
- "Unproven event risk" is framed as leverage for you, not an apology — you're compensating for their lack of track record, not asking them to overlook yours.
- The stage-time topic is a placeholder; fill in something concrete once you know their agenda gaps — a named topic is harder to say no to than "some talk."
- If you have logos of other $40k+ ACV customers, consider offering to loop them into future promo — first-edition organizers often value that more than money, and it costs you nothing.
