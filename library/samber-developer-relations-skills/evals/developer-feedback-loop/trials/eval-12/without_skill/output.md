## Problems with the current framing

- **Offline mode:**
  - The 12 votes are your baseline. The 140 came overnight from a different population, and nobody has checked yet whether they use your SDK or build payments products.
  - The surge is a real reach signal, but it doesn't measure demand from your users.
  - Check before the meeting: voter identity (how many are existing accounts or known users), referrer and vote timestamps, and whether the 12 were already trending up before the link.
- **Onboarding friction:**
  - Six audit findings are not six mentions. They're one reviewer's observations, so they have no vote-like weight.
  - The two that match Discord are your strongest evidence on the board, because two independent channels agree.
  - Count the Discord complaints per friction point and add them as their own column. I don't have that number.
- **Multi-currency:**
  - "Enterprise: 3 customers" is wrong. They're prospects, not customers, and the AE's wording will be read as installed-base demand.
  - Log it as "3 prospects asked" and attach deal size and stage for each.
  - Confirm they are three distinct companies, that each raised it unprompted, and that all three are enterprise.

## Suggested agenda order

Rank by evidence quality and business link, not raw count:

1. **Multi-currency settlement:** the only item tied to named pipeline. Bring deal values and stages.
2. **The two corroborated onboarding frictions:** two channels agree, and they affect every new user. The other four findings can be a short list.
3. **Offline mode:** a time-boxed slot to decide how to validate the 140 (voter identity, a short survey of voters, and the check against your existing users). Don't decide whether to build it until that's done.

If you still want one ranked list, use a rubric that scores each item separately on:
- segment fit
- number of independent channels
- revenue link
- severity

Then show the raw counts next to the score.
