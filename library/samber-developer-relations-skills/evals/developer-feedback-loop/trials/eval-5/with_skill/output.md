## What I changed and why

- **Title.** "Add GraphQL support!!!" names a solution, which asks Lior for a yes/no vote on one design. A problem title lets product compare options, and that decision is theirs.
- **Revenue and renewal.** I moved Brightwater's name, $120k and the March renewal into the private block. Account value is a legitimate input for product, but it doesn't belong in the public record.
- **Mentions vs. people.** "23 mentions" counts messages, so it rewards whoever repeats themselves. I replaced it with distinct requesters, counted once across channels. You need to count those people before sending.
- **Reactions.** The +31 is a separate signal. It sits beside the requester count and isn't added to it.
- **Priority.** I dropped "HIGH, top of the list" and the "!!!". Product sets priority, and a self-assigned rank makes Lior discount the whole record.
- **Missing evidence.** The draft has no problem, workaround or severity, so I left those fields open instead of guessing.

## Why there is no score

Lior ranks it, not you. Product weighs the inputs, and one number from DevRel hides the segment mix and the severity that Lior needs. The record gives those inputs separately, so Lior can rank it with its context. If you need a ranking aid, add the segment mix and representation (requesters ÷ active developers in that segment), and leave representation blank if you have no usage data.

## Before you send

1. **Split the forum requests into a second record.** The 3 forum people want GraphQL for the typed schema. That is a different problem from whatever the Discord users need, and it may be solvable without GraphQL, for example with a published schema or typed SDKs. Treat the two as separate records.
2. **Ask the Discord requesters one question:** "What were you trying to do when you needed this?" Use the answers to fill Problem, Workaround and Severity. Without them the record only tells Lior that people said "GraphQL".
3. **Add the real links** to the Discord thread, the forum thread and the call notes. Quote one developer verbatim if you can.
