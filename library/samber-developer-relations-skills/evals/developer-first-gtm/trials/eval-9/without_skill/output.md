103% NRR not bad by itself. Investor's "120%+" rule wrong benchmark for this business. Question under it matters more: what's driving 103%.

**Why 120%+ doesn't apply here**

That number comes from enterprise / usage-based / land-and-expand SaaS (large accounts, many seats or usage units to grow into, sales-led expansion). Stavemill: bottom-up, small teams, card-pay, self-serve. Public SaaS benchmark surveys (OpenView, KeyBanc, ChartMogul) consistently show NRR scales with ACV and account size — SMB/self-serve cohorts cluster near 95-110%, enterprise/usage-based cohorts pull the "120%+" headline number up. Small teams have few seats to expand into and higher structural logo churn (businesses close, cut tools, downsize). Ceiling is mechanically lower here, not a performance failure.

**103% alone doesn't tell you what to fix**

NRR = gross revenue retention (GRR) + expansion. Same 103% can come from two opposite situations:

- GRR ~85%, expansion +18pts → churn problem. Upsell motion pours water into leaking bucket.
- GRR ~96%, expansion +7pts → expansion-constrained. Upsell motion is right lever.

Building upsell motion this quarter off one investor comment, without decomposing GRR vs expansion, risks solving wrong problem. Get that split before committing roadmap.

**1,900 seats metric is broken**

"Every seat ever provisioned" only goes up. Never reflects churn, downgrades, or seat removal. Company could be shrinking and this number still climbs. It's a vanity/cumulative metric — same category as "total signups ever" instead of active users. Report current active/paid seats instead, trended with net adds vs removed.

**Standing board metrics miss retention entirely**

Signups, MAU, ARR, NPS — none of these show revenue health under the hood:

- ARR: aggregate, lagging. Hides whether growth = new logos, expansion, or churn being masked by new bookings.
- MAU: usage, not paying-seat health. Free/trial activity can inflate it while paid seats shrink.
- NPS: attitudinal, self-selected, weak tie to actual renewal behavior.
- Signups: top-of-funnel, meaningless without activation/conversion attached.

Missing: NRR, GRR, logo churn rate, quick ratio (new+expansion MRR / churned+contracted MRR). Telling fact: NRR wasn't a standing metric — someone ran it once, reactively, after investor pushback. Board has been steering on vanity/lagging numbers while the two that show real health were absent till now.

Next: pull GRR/expansion split and logo churn rate for trailing 12 months before deciding on upsell motion.
