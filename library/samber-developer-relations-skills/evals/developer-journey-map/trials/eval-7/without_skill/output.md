# Gravelock Developer Journey Map — v1 Draft

**Segment:** platform engineers · **End state:** production usage · **Stage model:** as given by CX (Awareness of need → Value prop → Confidence → Commit → Champion)

This is a hypothesis-driven first pass, built from known platform-engineer/DevOps buying patterns in the self-hosted observability category — not from Gravelock-specific interviews yet. Treat pain points and opportunities as testable claims; validate against 5-10 real platform-engineer conversations before this drives roadmap or copy decisions. Emotion scores are directional design inputs, not measured data.

| Stage | Customer Goal | Emotion (0–10) | Pain Points | Opportunities |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Developer becomes aware of need** | Pin down why current visibility failed and whether fixing it is worth a project. | **3** — anxious, defensive | Blind spot found the hard way: incident, audit, or bill shock exposed the gap.<br>Alert fatigue has already eroded trust in the current stack.<br>No budget or mandate yet — this is a hunch, not a project.<br>Compliance/data-residency rules quietly rule out SaaS options. | Show up where the trigger gets discussed: incident postmortems, cost-blowup threads (r/devops, r/kubernetes, HN).<br>Publish content that names the trigger directly ("SaaS observability bill audit," "self-hosted for data residency").<br>SEO on the trigger, not the product name. |
| **Developer understands the value proposition** | Decide whether Gravelock's model (self-hosted, OTel-native, no data egress) actually fits their stack and constraints. | **5** — curious, guarded | "Self-hosted" fatigue from past tools that promised control and delivered ops burden.<br>Pricing/licensing unclear at first glance.<br>No honest comparison against the DIY stack they half-run already (Prometheus + Grafana + Loki).<br>Marketing claims read as unverifiable until proven. | One-page architecture diagram, no signup wall.<br>Direct "vs DIY stack" and "vs [SaaS vendor]" comparisons.<br>Publish the agent's own resource footprint up front.<br>Lead with OTel-native positioning — existing instrumentation should just work. |
| **Developer feels confident in the product** | Prove it works in their environment, at their scale, with no surprises, before it touches anything real. | **4 → 7** — swings hard on setup friction, recovers if the trial goes clean | Docs cover the happy path, not their distro (bare metal, EKS, on-prem, air-gapped).<br>Agent overhead untested at their node count until they test it themselves.<br>One missing exporter blocks the whole evaluation.<br>Security review of the agent's own permissions stalls the trial.<br>No sandbox — first real test lands on infra someone cares about. | Quickstart showing real telemetry in under 15 minutes.<br>Published overhead benchmarks by node size, not marketing claims.<br>Reference architectures per environment (EKS, bare metal, air-gapped).<br>Security whitepaper that pre-answers the AppSec review.<br>Live Discord/Slack with fast technical answers, not a ticket queue. |
| **Developer commits** | Get sign-off, cut over to production without an incident, and be able to defend the choice in six months. | **5 → 6** — tense through cutover, relief once stable | Selling the migration internally to SRE, security, and finance.<br>Risk of losing historical data/dashboards during cutover.<br>Double-running cost while trust is still being built.<br>On-call not yet fluent in the new tool during the riskiest window.<br>No rollback plan if something breaks at 2am. | Migration playbook with a tested rollback path.<br>Parallel-run support so old and new coexist during validation.<br>ROI/TCO calculator to build the internal business case.<br>Dashboards-as-code import from their existing stack.<br>Named onboarding contact for the production rollout window. |
| **Developer champions** | Get credit for the call, spread it to other teams, and stop being the single point of failure for it. | **8** — proud, invested | Win is invisible outside their own team unless they push it.<br>No easy way to hand dashboards/config to other teams without redoing the work.<br>Some employers restrict public vendor advocacy, capping their upside.<br>No feedback loop into the roadmap for the edge cases they hit.<br>Being the only expert means pager duty for life with no bus factor. | Champion program: early access, direct roadmap input, named recognition.<br>One-click template sharing for dashboards/config across teams.<br>Case study co-authored and credited by name.<br>Conference/meetup speaking slots, contributor path.<br>"Add a teammate" flow that removes the bus-factor risk they're carrying. |

**Next step:** re-read this map after 5-10 real platform-engineer interviews (trigger events, actual overhead numbers, real internal-approval friction) and swap any hypothesis row for verified language once you have it.
