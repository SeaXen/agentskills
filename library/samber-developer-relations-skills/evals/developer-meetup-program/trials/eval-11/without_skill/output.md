# [Project] Lisbon Meetup — 12-Month Programme (Oct 2026 – Sep 2027)

**Format:** Monthly, in-person, third Thursday, 18:30–21:00 (talks + networking + drinks). Consistency of day/time is deliberate — recurring cadence is what turns a launch event into a community.

**Location:** Lisbon, fixed venue for the year (candidates: Heden Lisboa, Second Home Lisboa, Fábrica Braço de Prata — confirm one and keep it stable rather than moving venue each month).

**Placeholders to swap before this goes external:** `[Project]` = the OSS project name. Guest speakers marked "TBC" are target profiles, not confirmed bookings — a year of external speakers can't be locked today; they get confirmed 4–6 weeks out per edition. Sponsor names are draft target accounts (fictional placeholders here), not signed deals.

**Sponsorship model:** One Title Sponsor anchors Edition #1 (launch) and Edition #12 (anniversary) for maximum visibility. Every other edition has one Host Sponsor (covers venue/catering/swag, gets logo placement + 3-minute slot + attendee list opt-in). Repeat sponsors across the year is a feature, not an oversight — it signals renewal, which is the actual pitch to future sponsors.

## Notes for the pitch

- **Topic arc is deliberate:** launch → architecture → community → roadmap → production proof → hands-on → performance → security → enterprise validation → contributor pipeline → open feedback → anniversary. Every topic is something the audience would attend even if they never adopt [Project] — that's what makes it a community draw and not a thinly veiled product demo.
- **Content capture:** record every talk from Edition #1 onward. Recap post + clips per edition, published within 48h. By month 12 this is a 12-video library and a dozen recap posts — durable content, not just a night of pizza.
- **Success metric:** track qualified conversations and contributor sign-ups per edition, not just headcount. Judge the series on the trend across editions 1–3, not on edition 1 alone — first editions of anything underperform steady state.
- **Budget ask:** venue + catering for 12 editions, sponsor outreach kicks off 8 weeks before Edition #1 (October) to lock Title + first three Host sponsors before the launch date is public.
- **Next steps:** confirm venue, confirm `[Project]` naming for external comms, start outreach for Edition #1's Title Sponsor and Edition #5's guest speaker (longest lead time — needs an external production user willing to speak).
