### Reminders

| When | Content | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Now / tomorrow | Announcement (Option A or B above) | Reach people who plan evenings ahead |
| 1 week out | Same, + "still a few seats" / "waitlist open" | Second wave; waitlist signals demand |
| 2 days out | Practical only: exact address, door code/entry instructions, start time, food | The reminder that actually moves attendance |
| 2 hours out | One line: "see you tonight, doors 18:30" + "can't make it? release your spot for the waitlist" | Frees seats, gives you an accurate headcount |
| Day after | Thanks, slides/recording if any, save-the-date for next edition | Turns an attendee into a returning one |

Frame the "can't make it" ask as freeing a seat for someone else, never as a guilt trip — the skill is explicit that the rebuke framing produces silent no-shows instead of released seats.
