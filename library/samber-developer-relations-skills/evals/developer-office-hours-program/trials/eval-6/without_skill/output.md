# Office hours options for Ossifer, ranked

## Verdict

Your proposal optimizes for **reach**. Your stated goal is **inbound product feedback**. A panel broadcasts to users and collects little back, so I rank it last. I'd also fix the forum backlog before launching anything public.

## Ranking

| Rank | Option | Feedback value | Eng cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🥇 1 | **Backlog sprint: clear the 30 open forum questions** (one-time, then a weekly 30-min triage) | High. Each question is already a real user pain point. | About 1 week, split across the 4 engineers and 2 DevRel | Low |
| 🥈 2 | **Weekly small-group video office hours**: 1 engineer + 1 DevRel, 5–8 users per slot, sign-up in advance, product attends or reads the notes | Highest per engineer-hour. Users talk, and engineers see their screens and problems. | About 1.5 eng-hours per week | Low. Attendance may start small. |
| 🥉 3 | **Discord office-hours channel** with DevRel as first responder and a softer SLA (see below) | Medium. It captures many small items, but the content is mostly support questions. | DevRel-heavy, engineers on a rotation day | Medium |
| 4 | **Livestream panel**, but monthly, 1 engineer + 1 DevRel host, questions collected beforehand | Low. It's one-to-many, and chat is thin. | Your version: 3 engineers every 2 weeks, about 6+ eng-hours per cycle with prep. Smaller version: about 2 eng-hours per month. | High if launched now |

## Why your two proposals rank lower

**Livestream panel (rank 4)**
- It uses 3 of your 4 engineers, which is 75% of the rotation pool, every two weeks.
- Panels produce answers, not feedback. Viewers mostly watch.
- If the backlog is still open, the first comment will be "answer my forum post".
- Revisit it after about 8 weeks. By then you'll have real questions and stories to feature, and a smaller format works.

**Discord with a 1-hour SLA (rank 3)**
- A one-hour answer promise means someone is always on duty. With 4 engineers, that is roughly a quarter of engineering capacity, permanently.
- A missed public SLA damages trust more than having no SLA.
- It attracts support questions, not product insight, unless you add tagging.
- Safer promise: **DevRel acknowledges within 1 business hour. An engineer answers within 1 business day.** Name the engineer on duty each day.

## Make any option produce feedback

Without this, none of the options solves "product is flying blind":
1. Add a **feedback** tag in Discord and the forum. DevRel applies it.
2. Use a fixed note template: user, use case, pain, what they tried, and quote.
3. Send a weekly digest, top 5 themes with quotes, to product.
4. Invite one PM to each office hours session, or have them read the notes.

## Suggested sequence

1. **Weeks 1–2:** Backlog sprint. Announce "we cleared the backlog" on the forum.
2. **Week 3 onward:** Weekly small-group office hours, plus the Discord channel with the softer SLA.
3. **Week 8:** Check three numbers: feedback items logged per week, how many became product tickets, and median first-response time.
4. **After week 8:** Add the monthly livestream if those numbers justify it.

## Assumptions to check

- I assumed the community is small enough for a 5–8 person session to matter. If you have thousands of active users, the livestream moves up to rank 3.
- I assumed product will actually read the digest. If they won't, nothing here fixes that.
