**Middle, the gate:**
- A workshop is granted only with an open qualified opportunity, a named champion, one use case from discovery, a decision maker in the room, and a next step agreed before the date is set.
- It's signed by [sales owner] and [DevRel/SE owner].
- Everything else gets a self-paced lab or a one-hour session.

**Right, the ask:**
- [N] field workshop engineers, cost $[ ].
- Capacity: about 2–3 delivery days per guided half-day workshop (baseline). That gives [N × hours] ÷ 2.5 days ≈ [X] workshops per quarter.
- One gated quarter to set baselines.
- Quarter two review, with this pass threshold:
  - Next-step execution rate holds at or above the first quarter's.
  - No environment-caused lab failures.
  - Every instructor is certified.
  - Delivery hours fit capacity.

**Bottom line:** We'll report workshop impact against matched accounts without a workshop, as "influenced" pipeline. If qualified workshops produce no next step, we stop.

## Speaker notes

- **Open:** "Workshop accounts closed $4.1M against $1.9M. I won't claim workshops caused all of that."
- **Selection bias:** Reps pick accounts with momentum, so the raw gap overstates the effect. That's why the ask is a gated pilot with a matched comparison group.
- **If the CRO asks for industry benchmarks:** No independent study of workshop-to-POC conversion exists. Lab-platform vendors publish figures, but they come from marketing or studies they commissioned. Our own data is the only defensible baseline.
- **If asked what happens if it fails:** The pass threshold is written down, and we stop if qualified workshops don't produce next steps.

## Before this goes to the CRO

1. **Matched comparison.** Replace "no workshop" with accounts matched on stage, ACV and segment at the workshop date. Without it, the $4.1M vs $1.9M gap mixes workshop effect with account selection. Add capacity-deferred qualified requests as a few more comparison accounts.
2. **Per-account numbers.** If the groups differ in size, the totals mislead. Show the average per account and the account counts.
3. **Grant-rule score.** Score this year's workshops against the grant rule. Report conversion on the qualified subset only, because goodwill workshops dilute the full-population rate.
4. **Attribution.** Check that each workshop was logged against its opportunity on the day it was granted. If not, say so on the slide.
