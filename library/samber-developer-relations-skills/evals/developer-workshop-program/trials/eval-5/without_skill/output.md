**Speaker note (say this before the CRO asks):** "The $4.1M vs $1.9M gap is not proof that workshops caused the difference. We may have given workshops to accounts that were already more likely to close. So the ask includes a matched-control test at two quarters, and the break-even needs only [N] extra deals."

## What to fix before presenting

- **Selection bias is the main risk.** Compare like with like: same segment, deal size, and stage when the workshop happened. If workshops went to accounts already in late stage, the gap partly reflects that. Also say whether $4.1M and $1.9M are totals or per-account, because a total depends on how many accounts are in each group.
- **Forrester.** Forrester TEI studies are commissioned by a specific vendor and measure that vendor's product. Name the study. If it's about a lab-platform vendor and not workshops as a practice, the footnote should say that. If you can't find the source, cut the line. The slide still works without it.
- **If you want a learning-science stat,** cite something you can source, such as a peer-reviewed study on active learning. I haven't verified one, so I haven't put one in.
