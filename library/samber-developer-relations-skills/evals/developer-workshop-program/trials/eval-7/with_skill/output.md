   - The agreement has three parts:
     - A success criterion. For example: "3 attendees reach checkpoint 4 on their own schema."
     - The step if the criterion is met, with a date and an owner on each side. For example: POC scope or an evaluation plan.

2. **Pull checkpoint results per module from the lab check scripts.** Include the 7 who stopped.
