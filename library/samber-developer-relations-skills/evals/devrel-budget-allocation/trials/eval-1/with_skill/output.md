**B — Content-compounding, single pole (~50% content).** Over-invests in publishing volume; docs, events and community all drop to maintenance. Three largest lines: content pipeline, docs maintenance, one event. Starves: time-to-first-success work — traffic arrives and bounces at the product, not just at the blog. **C — Field-and-events heavy, i.e., repeat last year's 3-conference pattern.** Over-invests in events (50–60% of spend, archetype default). Three largest lines: 3× conference presence, light content, light docs. Starves: the self-serve path itself and everything that compounds. The 2024 DevRel.Agency survey (n=47, shares sum to 104.5%, two cycles old) puts events at a mean 41.1% of program budget — that's where the field's money has historically gone, not evidence it fits a self-serve motion. Needs ~1,900 h/year, mostly burst travel. Earliest signal: per-event, fast, but doesn't compound. Wrong when the motion is individual/self-serve — which is exactly what's stated here, so this is the shape being deliberately not chosen, not a neutral option.

Community and events get funded at token level, not zero, because Discord already exists and one event still buys credibility and content raw material — but neither gets to compete with the two poles for hours.
