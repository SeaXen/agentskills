## Halcyon Metrics — DevRel Budget Split, FY (€180,000)

**Funded driver: self-serve developer adoption.** Every allocation below is weighted toward channels that compound without paid headcount — docs and content lead, events get trimmed and re-aimed, community stays lean because it's early and time-constrained, not money-constrained.

### Headline split

| Category | Amount | % | Logic |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Docs & tooling | €52,000 | 29% | Highest-leverage spend for self-serve — docs *are* the product experience for observability buyers |
| Content | €45,000 | 25% | Compounding SEO asset; scales your writer's 30h/week without adding headcount |
| Events | €36,000 | 20% | Down-weighted and re-aimed: fewer, better events instead of 3 lower-focus ones |
| Community | €27,000 | 15% | Discord is 1 year old — bottleneck is your time, not budget; spend buys back time |
| Education | €20,000 | 11% | Smallest bucket by design — webinars/workshops repurpose content and event assets rather than starting from zero |
| **Total** | **€180,000** | **100%** | |

Your team is ~1.5 FTE-equivalent total (you at 0.63 FTE net of support rotation, writer at 0.75 FTE, designer at 0.15 FTE). This budget doesn't buy more people — it buys contractor hours and tools that extend what those 1.5 FTEs can cover. Every line below is chosen with that constraint in mind, not as a wishlist.

---

### 1. Docs & tooling — €52,000

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Docs platform + search (Mintlify/ReadMe-tier + Algolia DocSearch) | €6,000 |
| Contract technical writer hours — clear the SDK/integration reference backlog (Go, Python, Node, OTel exporters) your 30h/week writer can't reach alone | €18,000 |
| Interactive API sandbox / "try it live" quickstart environment (contractor build) | €12,000 |
| Doc-testing / code-sample CI (stop quickstarts from rotting silently) | €4,000 |
| Quickstart UX audit + time-to-first-dashboard optimization (one-off contractor engagement) | €6,000 |
| Docs diagrams/illustrations (overflow beyond your 6h/week designer) | €6,000 |

**Why it's the biggest bucket:** for a devtool, the quickstart and API reference *are* the self-serve funnel. This is also where a contractor writer buys the most incremental adoption per euro, since your in-house writer is your scarcest resource.

### 2. Content — €45,000

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Freelance writer/editor pool — scale from ~14 to ~24–30 posts/year (comparison pages, troubleshooting guides, SRE/observability topics) | €24,000 |
| Video/screencast production for quickstart walkthroughs (dual-purpose: feeds Education too) | €8,000 |
| Distribution: newsletter sponsorships, light paid boosting of top-performing posts | €5,000 |
| SME/guest contributor honoraria | €4,000 |
| SEO tooling (keyword/backlink tracking) | €4,000 |

**Why:** blog output was almost certainly capacity-capped last year, not idea-capped. This buys volume without touching the headcount line the VP Marketing owns.

### 3. Events — €36,000

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| 2 targeted conferences, sponsor + speaking slot (not 3 diluted ones) | €24,000 |
| 1 lean "attend only" event + adjacent dinner/side-event (cheapest, often highest meetings-per-euro format) | €4,000 |
| Local Lisbon/EU meetup hosting, 2–3x/year | €4,000 |
| Talk recording/production for repurposing into content | €2,000 |
| Booth/swag collateral | €2,000 |

**Why cut from 3 to 2 hosted-tier events:** you attended/ran 3 last year on the same team capacity that now also owns Discord. Events are the highest-effort, lowest-compounding channel per euro for a self-serve-funded motion — trim here, redeploy into docs and content, which don't evaporate the week after.

### 4. Community — €27,000

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Part-time contract Discord moderator (buys back your time, the real constraint) | €9,000 |
| Ambassador/power-user program (perks, early access swag, small stipends) | €9,000 |
| Community tooling (moderation bots, engagement analytics — Orbit/Common Room-tier) | €4,000 |
| AMA/community event production | €3,000 |
| Recognition swag | €2,000 |

**Why it's not bigger:** a 1-year-old Discord needs founder/lead attention (welcoming people by name, seeding threads) more than it needs a budget. The one line worth funding heavily here is a contractor moderator — that directly returns hours to you.

### 5. Education — €20,000

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Quarterly webinar series (production + promotion) | €8,000 |
| Hands-on workshop content, reused at the 2 conferences above | €6,000 |
| Pilot self-serve course ("Observability with Halcyon" — instrumentation walkthrough) | €6,000 |

**Why smallest:** education leans hardest on assets you're already paying for elsewhere (event workshops, quickstart videos). Keep it a repurposing layer this year, not a new production line — revisit sizing once you see whether the pilot course drives signups.

---

### Two things to flag, not gloss over

- **This split is capacity-based, not ROI-based.** You don't yet have per-channel CAC or signup attribution (which channel actually drives self-serve activation). Treat this as the starting allocation, not gospel — pull a quarterly read on where signups originate and shift the Q3/Q4 tranche of Content vs. Events accordingly. Events is the easiest bucket to claw back from if it underperforms; docs/content are the buy-and-hold assets to protect.
- **Don't let "3 conferences → 2" read as retreat.** Reframe it externally as sponsor+speak at the 2 that actually contain your ICP, plus a cheap side-event at a third — that's usually a *higher* qualified-conversation count than spreading thin across 3 booths, and it's the only way this budget and your 25h/week survive the year together.
