# Sablecourt DevRel — 2027 Success Criteria

## The core problem, stated plainly

None of the four lines below has a baseline: analytics went live in August 2026, nothing was tracked before that, and no one is named as the owner of any number. Under that condition, a hard numeric target is a guess wearing a target's clothes — it gets hit or missed by luck, and next year's review becomes a political argument instead of an evidence-based one. **Labeling key** (used throughout): **Sourced** = published external figure, name/date attached. **Baseline** = this program's own prior data. **Self-set** = a starting point being proposed here; no data backs the exact number, so move it and say why.

## Proposed 2027 lines

### 1. Discord community
- **Draft target:** 10,000 members.
- **Problem:** raw member count is an input metric, not an outcome — it counts joins, not health, and inflates easily (bot joins, one-time drive-bys from a launch post).
- **Reframe:** outcome unit = % weekly-active members and median time-to-first-response on questions. This period is an experiment: *"We will know our real weekly-active rate and response-time baseline by end of Q1 2027."* No numeric threshold before then. Keep 10,000 total members as an aspirational reach marker for the VP deck — track it, don't defend it in a review.
- **Owner:** community lead — **TBD, name before kickoff.**
- **Review:** quarterly.
- **If it misses:** N/A this year (experiment window). From Q2 2027, missing the weekly-active % two quarters running triggers a cut to paid community tooling/events.

### 2. Blog / SEO ("event-driven architecture")
- **Draft target:** #1 Google result for "event-driven architecture."
- **Problem:** this is a broad, non-branded head term already held by Martin Fowler's site, AWS, Confluent, Red Hat and similar category-defining publishers. Outranking that field organically in one year is a domain-authority problem, not a budget problem — set as a committed threshold, it's set up to fail regardless of execution quality.
- **Reframe:** outcome unit = cost per published-and-reviewed piece, plus organic sessions and MQLs from blog content. This period is an experiment: *"We will know our organic-traffic and MQL baseline from blog content by end of Q2 2027."* [Self-set] stretch marker for the VP: top-10 (page-1) on "event-driven architecture." [Self-set] real, winnable target: top-3 on 3–5 named mid-tail/branded terms Sablecourt actually controls (e.g. "[product] event-driven architecture pattern").
- **Owner:** content/SEO lead — **TBD.**
- **Review:** quarterly.
- **If it misses:** cut agency spend / halve commissioned volume before cutting in-house authorship — in-house content compounds, agency pipelines don't, once the cost structure is actually known.

### 3. Conference booths
- **Draft target:** "generate strong pipeline."
- **Problem:** not a number. Can't be reviewed, can't be defended, can't be cut against — "strong" gets decided after the fact by whoever wants it to have worked.
- **Reframe:** outcome unit = cost per qualified conversation (define "qualified" before the first event — a scheduled follow-up call with a named account, not a badge scan). This period, one event at a time: *"We will know cost-per-qualified-conversation for [named event] 2 weeks after it runs."* No fixed pipeline-value target this year — any such number right now would be invented precision.
- **Owner:** events lead — **TBD.**
- **Review:** 2 weeks post-event, rolled up quarterly.
- **If it misses:** renegotiate the next booth to a smaller/workshop tier before cutting the line — booths at least leave behind material (talks, contacts) the program keeps.

### 4. Docs freshness
- **Draft target:** "stay current."
- **Problem:** freshness decays in one of two ways — invisible, or actively wrong (a broken sample, a stale version number) — and "stay current" doesn't fund the maintenance hours that prevent the actively-wrong case. Unfunded maintenance isn't current, it's just not-yet-noticed-as-broken.
- **Reframe:** outcome unit = % of docs pages reviewed within a trailing 90 days, plus count of open broken-sample/broken-link reports. [Self-set] SLA, startable today with no new tooling: **≥90% of published pages reviewed in the last 90 days; 0 open "broken sample" reports older than 2 weeks.**
- This is the one line that can carry a real committed threshold now — "reviewed" and "open reports" are checkable today, it doesn't need to wait on the visibility fix the other three do.
- **Owner:** docs lead — **TBD.**
- **Review:** monthly — decay outruns quarterly review on this line.
- **If it misses:** foundational-maintenance lines get cut last regardless of ranking; pull hours from the lowest-ranked experiment above to cover it instead.
