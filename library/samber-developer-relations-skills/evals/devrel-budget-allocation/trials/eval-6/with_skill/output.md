## Step 3 — two shapes, brainstormed before choosing

**Shape A — Barbell: launch-moment reach + a compounding bet signed now, foundation protected (recommended)**
- Three largest lines: content-agency retainer, newsletter sponsorships, quickstart refresh.
- Starves: certification, community, any owned-events line.
- Hours: ~150h charged pre-launch, well under the ~350h ceiling.
- Earliest signal: newsletter CTR/signups within days of each placement.
- Wrong when: you can't get a vetted content agency under contract within 3-4 weeks — signing a stranger under launch-week time pressure is its own risk.

**Shape B — Rented-reach sprint: spend it all on the launch moment, sign nothing new**
- Three largest lines: expanded newsletter/paid-social placements, quickstart refresh (with more scope — translations, video), a larger flex reserve.
- Starves: anything that outlives launch week — the whole $120K evaporates once the moment passes.
- Hours: lower, no vendor-onboarding load.
- Earliest signal: same as Shape A, days.
- Wrong when: you have room to build something that outlasts the launch and 10 weeks is enough runway to vet a vendor properly — which, per the shortlist you brought, it is.

**Recommendation: Shape A.** The FY-lock is what tips it — this is exactly the kind of money that shouldn't just get blown on one loud week when a real asset can be committed instead. Fallback: if you can't get an agency contracted in the next 3-4 weeks, switch to Shape B and redirect that money into more reach + quickstart depth before the clock runs out.
