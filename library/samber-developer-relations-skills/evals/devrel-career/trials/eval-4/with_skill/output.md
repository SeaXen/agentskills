# Skill-gap roadmap: mid → senior developer advocate, Karrowin (data-pipeline tooling)

Three years in, longest-serving of four, developer advocate at a 200-person data-pipeline tooling company — generalist/all-rounder profile (writing, speaking, community, events), which is normal for a team this size even though the company itself is past early-stage. ## Assessment

| Dimension | Senior bar | What you have | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Execution | quality + strategic scope, not volume | 14 posts, 6 talks, 2 booths — all counts, zero outcomes attached | Every one of these needs a "so what": which post fixed a specific confusion, which talk changed a specific behavior, measured |
| Independence | scopes and defends work without being asked | unstated whether any of the above was self-initiated vs. assigned | Name one initiative *you* proposed and defended, not one you executed on request |
| Collaboration | org-wide, strategic level, outside the DevRel seat | booth work implies sales/marketing contact; nothing stated with product or engineering | Needs one story where you pulled another function into a decision or a fix, with their acknowledgement |
| Competence | command of practice + the technical speciality, design-level not just delivery-level | 3 years of content in the data-pipeline space implies real depth | Needs one artefact showing you *shaped* something (API feedback, docs architecture, a reviewed integration) rather than only communicated it |
| Strategic influence | ratio of strategic to tactical work; whose decisions moved | none stated | Needs one decision — content strategy, event picks, community structure — that changed *because of you*, evidenced by a before/after |

Tenure ("longest-serving of four") and raw counts don't appear in this table on purpose — they're not dimension evidence, and including them in the actual case is what sinks it.
