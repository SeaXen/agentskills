## What to do next

1. **Don't tune the CV to the bullet list.** Optimising to keyword-match a gatekeeper posting is spec effort into a signal that the company hasn't decided what it's hiring for — this field hires on public work and referrals, not ATS matching, and over-tailoring here specifically rewards the pattern you should be naming instead. *(no artefact — a decision, not a task)*
2. **If you apply, apply with your existing portfolio as-is** and treat the application itself as the low-cost move — the filter to actually invest effort in is the interview, not the resume. *(artefact: application sent, unmodified from your standing materials)*
3. **If you get a call, open with the reconciliation question, not your pitch:** "The title reads Developer Advocate I, the requirements read staff-level — what level does this actually map to internally, and does the comp band change with it?" This is Mikhaleva's diligence point turned into a direct ask. *(artefact: their answer, written down verbatim — vague or defensive answers are the real result)*
4. **Ask the reporting-line and driver questions directly:** what does success look like in 12 months and who measures it; why Growth Marketing rather than product/engineering; is there a written career matrix for "I → II → III." *(artefact: three answers on record before you invest more prep time)*
5. **Confirm the coding-depth expectation against the work, not the title:** ask what the team's last three shipped things were and who wrote them — this tells you whether "8+ years across 4 languages" is a real screen or padding. *(artefact: named examples, or the absence of any)*

## Review point

After the first call (or after two weeks of silence post-application, whichever comes first): if the reconciliation question gets a real answer — a level, a comp band tied to it, a stated driver — treat it as a normal Series A negotiation from here. If it gets deflection, a restated laundry list, or "we're still figuring that out," skip: that's the gatekeeper's root cause (the company doesn't understand DevRel) confirmed live, not just inferred from the text.
