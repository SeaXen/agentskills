Straight answer first, then the two things that will move the number more than anything I can tell you.

**Ask for €115k–€140k base** (or $125k–$155k if they pay in USD), plus equity, opening near the top of that. Below is why that band and not something else — and one caveat: I don't have live web access in this session (a permission prompt for it wasn't granted), so this is a directional estimate built from stage/geography/level signals, not a sourced, dated figure. Treat it as a floor to walk in with, not a number to defend line-by-line.

| Factor | What it does to the number |
|---|---|
| **Series B, infra/devtool** | Past seed scrappiness, real equity, not yet big-tech bands. Comp structure at this stage is typically "mid cash plus equity," not high cash alone. |
| **Remote-friendly, Berlin** | This is the swing factor. If they benchmark to the German market, you're at the low-mid end of the range above. If they benchmark to a global/US remote band (common for VC-backed infra companies hiring EU remote), you're mid-to-high end or above it. |
| **Senior IC** | Anchors you above generalist/mid pay — but only if the loop actually evidences senior-dimension work (org-wide collaboration, strategic scope), not tenure. If they're really hiring a mid-level advocate at a senior title, the number won't hold either. |

**One question is worth more than the number:** ask early on the call which market they benchmark against — German payroll or a global remote band — and who the role reports to (sales/marketing/engineering changes what you're measured on and what the role is really worth to them). That answer tells you which end of the range to push, and it costs you nothing to ask.

**Before the call, if you have 10 minutes:** check Levels.fyi filtered to Germany/DevRel-adjacent roles, kununu.de, and Glassdoor Berlin for "developer advocate" — those will give you something dated and sourced, which is what you actually want walking in rather than my estimate. If nothing current turns up, ask the recruiter for the band outright before you name a number; many EU postings disclose it now, and it's a free way to avoid anchoring low.
