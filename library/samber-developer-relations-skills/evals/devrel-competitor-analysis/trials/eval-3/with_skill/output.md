## What's needed before this becomes a scorecard row

Pull twelve months, monthly, for all four, with launches/conferences/major ships marked on the calendar. Then read:

- **Baseline rate, not peak month.** Beacontree's non-launch-month average is what answers "are they investing more" — not the 19.
- **Larkspur's trend line.** Flat-low all year is a real signal (deprioritized content); one low month between normal ones is noise. Only twelve months tells these apart.
- **Format and evergreen mix.** If Beacontree's 19 is mostly release notes, it resets to zero next cycle and compounds nothing; if it's tutorials and deep technical posts, it keeps earning traffic long after launch week. Same count, very different asset.
- **Posts per named DevRel human** (SKILL.md § Content, § Normalization). A 19-vs-4 gap restates headcount if Beacontree runs a bigger content team — it says nothing about effort or skill until divided.
