# Cindershift Q3 Plan — DevRel & Content

**Spine of the quarter:** launch → proof → stage → harvest. Weeks 1–5 build to v5 GA, weeks 6–9 turn the launch into public proof (benchmarks) and load Raoul's ObserveCon talk, weeks 10–13 harvest and set up Q4. The agency feeds both peaks, one beat at a time.

## Locked anchors

| Week | Event |
|---|---|
| **5** | **v5 GA** + launch post live (agency, publish day-of-GA) |
| **8** | Benchmark post live (agency) — my placement, see judgment call #1 |
| **9** | **Raoul's talk @ ObserveCon** |
| ~4, ~8–9, ~13 | Community office hours (last Thursday of each month) |
| 2 | Agency starts executing (briefed pre-quarter) |

## Phase 1 — Set up and build to GA (weeks 1–5)

- **W1:** Finalize agency review workflow (reviewer, SLA, claims sign-off). Hand them beta access + v5 messaging doc. Pin real calendar dates and **resolve the office-hours/ObserveCon collision risk now** (see risks).
- **W2:** Agency kickoff. Launch-post outline due end of week — approve immediately so drafting starts W3 with maximum runway.
- **W3:** v5 RC / feature freeze (post writes against frozen scope). Agency full draft of launch post. Eng starts migration guide + changelog, DevRel edits.
- **W4:** Launch post tech-reviewed, final, staged for publish — hard lock EOD W4. **Office hours #1: "v5 preview + Q&A"** (doubles as live rehearsal of launch messaging; fall back to roadmap-only if pre-announce is off-limits).
- **W5:** **GA.** Post live, social + newsletter + community shares inside 48h. Support/forum watch. Friday debrief: capture adoption blockers.

## Phase 2 — Prove it and load the conference (weeks 6–9)

- **W6:** Run benchmarks on the GA build (reproducible scripts, written methodology). Agency check-in: launch-post performance + benchmark-post outline. Raoul talk outline starts.
- **W7:** Benchmark results validated — engineering sign-off, no unowned numbers. Agency drafts benchmark post. First internal talk dry run. Sales/support get a v5 + benchmark one-pager.
- **W8:** **Benchmark post live early in the week** (~1 week before the talk). Newsletter, social, community amplification. **Office hours #2: "v5 GA Q&A + how we benchmark"** — gives Raoul live Q&A reps on the same material. Slides locked end of week.
- **W9:** **ObserveCon.** Talk cites the fresh benchmark post ("published last week — deep dive here"). Booth/social/lead capture. Recording + slides queued for sharing. Nothing else ships this week.

## Phase 3 — Harvest and reset (weeks 10–13)

- **W10:** Talk recap post (short: slides, recording, takeaways). Conference lead follow-up. Second-wave amplification of the benchmark post — it's the quarter's evergreen asset, keep pushing it.
- **W11:** v5 adoption read (telemetry, support themes, office-hours question log) → feeds content backlog and Q4 roadmap. Scope a design-partner case study for Q4.
- **W12:** Agency performance review: two posts, on-time, quality, revision rounds. Community metrics roll-up (office hours, conference, benchmark post). Draft Q4 skeleton.
- **W13:** **Office hours #3: "v5 in production — learnings + what's next"** (harvests Q4 topics live). Close the quarter, lock Q4 calendar around its anchors.

## Judgment calls I made

1. **Benchmark post = week 8, not attached to launch.** It must postdate GA (benchmarks run on the GA build, W5–6) and should precede ObserveCon (W9) so Raoul walks in with a citable asset and the talk drives readers back to it. Earlier = pre-GA numbers nobody can reproduce; later = the talk points at nothing new. It also gives the new agency a real runway on their second piece instead of stacking both posts into the launch window.
2. **Office hours track the quarter arc for free:** preview (W4) → Q&A + benchmarks (W8) → production learnings (W13). Each session reuses material that already exists, so the recurring program adds no prep peaks.
3. **Agency cadence:** outline-first, one content beat at a time, mid-quarter check-in at W6. Two posts is light for a retainer — confirm whether distribution variants (social cuts, newsletter versions, the talk recap) are in scope at the W6 check-in.

## Risks

- ⚠️ **Tightest chain: new agency's first deliverable is the most date-critical asset with a 3-week runway.** Outline W2, draft W3, hard lock EOD W4. Name an internal fallback writer now.
- ⚠️ **Feature-freeze slippage breaks the W4 content lock.** Define "frozen scope" for the post; anything later goes to the changelog, not the post.
- ⚠️ **Benchmark credibility.** Reproducible harness, methodology section, engineering sign-off at W7, care with competitor comparisons — a pulled benchmark post hurts more than a late one.
- ⚠️ **Office hours #2 may land on ObserveCon week** depending on the real calendar. If it does: run it hybrid from the conference (community upside) or pull it to W8. Decide W1, not W8.
- ⚠️ **Raoul is a single point of failure.** Rehearse W7 and W8, lock slides end of W8.

Assumptions: 13-week quarter (weeks fiscal, not calendar); office hours land ~W4, ~W8/9, ~W13 — pin to actual dates in W1; small team, so no week carries more than one major content beat.
