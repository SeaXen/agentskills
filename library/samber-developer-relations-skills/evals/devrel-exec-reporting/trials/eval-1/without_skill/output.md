## Why I didn't use your headline

**"DevRel generated $380k in revenue, a 5x return" won't hold up in the room.**

- **It isn't revenue.** The figure is influenced pipeline. Calling it revenue is a factual error, and a CMO or finance partner will catch it.
- **The 5x doesn't follow from your numbers.** $380k ÷ $210k is 1.8x. Reaching 5x would take about $1.05M, and I can't derive that from what you gave me.
- **Sales also counts these deals.** If sales presents the same deals as theirs, the "generated" claim is contradicted in the same meeting. That would damage the credibility of everything else on the page.

The headline above still leads with revenue-adjacent numbers, which is what your boss wants. It is a claim you can defend line by line.

**Two things would let you make a stronger claim:**
- **Win rate.** Pipeline × historical win rate gives an expected-revenue figure, labeled as an estimate. Do you have it?
- **Closed-won deals from the influenced set.** If any have closed, that is real revenue and you can state it plainly.

If either number is strong, I'll rework the headline around it.
