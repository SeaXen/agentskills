# Case to the CFO: DevRel 40% cut

## Two claims to drop or fix first

**1. "Support would drown without us."** Don't say it unless you can measure it. A CFO will ask for the number, and an unsupported claim costs you credibility on everything else.

- Forum views and answered threads are activity, not tickets avoided.
- Pull the real figures from the last 6-12 months in 10 days or less: how many support tickets link to or quote a forum answer, how many forum threads staff answered, and how many of those would otherwise have been tickets.
- Get your support lead's cost per ticket and a written statement of what happens if the forum goes quiet.
- If the numbers are weak, say so and defend other value. If they are strong, this is your best argument.

**2. "Industry surveys say companies our size spend more."** Don't lead with it. I can't verify any specific figure from here, so I haven't put one in this draft.

- Spend is not return, and a CFO will say "they may be wasting it too."
- DevRel surveys are self-selected and mostly cover firms with developer-first products. They rarely control for company size.
- Use a benchmark only as a footnote. It needs a named source, a size-matched peer set and the survey's own caveats.
- Never use it as the argument.

## The numbers

- 40% of $540k = **$216k** to cut.
- Your ranked list totals $299k (55%).

| Cut | Cumulative | % of $540k |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Tier-2 sponsorships ($70k) | $70k | 13% |
| + 2. Swag and travel ($35k) | $105k | 19% |
| + 3. Community platform ($24k) | $129k | 24% |
| + 4. Video producer ($60k) | $189k | 35% |
| + 5. Community manager ($110k) | $299k | 55% |

- Cuts 1-4 save **$189k (35%)**, with $27k still short of the target.
- Cut 5 overshoots by $83k, so there is no clean path to exactly 40%.
- This is where you negotiate. Cuts 1-4 leave the people and the forum intact.

## Recommended position

Offer cuts 1-4 now, for $189k (35%). Hold the community manager role.

- It is the only item with a plausible link to support cost, which you have yet to measure.
- Offer a review of the remaining $27k gap at a set date, such as mid-year. Tie it to the support data and the metrics below.
- If the CFO insists on exactly 40%, find $27k inside the remaining $351k first, or propose reducing the community manager role (hours, contract or scope) rather than removing it. Decide that with the data in hand.

## 10-day plan

1. **Day 1-2:** Ask support for ticket data and cost per ticket. Export forum stats (staff answers, threads, links to docs).
2. **Day 3-4:** Estimate deflection conservatively. Show a low and a base case, and state the assumptions.
3. **Day 5:** Ask the support lead for a short written statement.
4. **Day 6:** Find one or two other outcomes you can defend with data, such as signups or activation from developer content. Tie them to the cuts only if the link holds.
5. **Day 7:** Fill in the memo and have someone outside DevRel check the numbers.
6. **Day 8-9:** Pre-brief the CFO's finance partner, then the CFO.
7. **Day 10:** Plan lock.
