## Why I did not keep it green or keep the old highlights format

- **Green is not supportable.** The primary metric is 42 below its threshold and fell from last quarter. A green status would be read as my judgment. If it turns red later, that costs far more trust than red now.
- **The prior report needs a correction.** Last quarter's figure of 210 was also under 230, yet the report said GREEN and left the metric out. If the threshold was 230 then, marketing will see the mismatch the moment they compare. I would say so in one line to the Head of Marketing rather than let them find it. Check the threshold history first.
- **The highlights format hides the outcome.** An activity list answers "were you busy", not "what did it move". I kept the activity numbers in the appendix, so marketing can still compare against the old format, but they no longer lead the page.
- **Telling them early is calmer than a surprise.** The ask is small and dated, which gives them something to approve instead of something to worry about.
