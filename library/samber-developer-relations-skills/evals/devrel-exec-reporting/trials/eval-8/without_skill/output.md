# DevRel half-year update for the COO

**Headline:** Developer activation rose 56% (900 to 1,400 first successful API calls per month) after the DevRel program and the v3 SDK launched. Program spend was $180k, about $60 per added activation before salaries.

## Why not your headline

I changed the headline because the original wouldn't survive a COO's questions.

- **"DevRel drove" claims cause.** The v3 SDK shipped in April, one month after the program started, and a better SDK plausibly lifts first API calls on its own. The numbers can't separate the two.
- **$60 vs $95 isn't a like-for-like comparison.**
  - $60 excludes DevRel salaries. The $95 includes the growth team's salaries.
  - Add DevRel salaries and the $60 rises. For example, $225k of salaries (my illustration, not your figure) gives $135.
  - The $60 also divides by the *increase* (about 3,000 over six months), while the $95 is probably cost per *all* paid-attributed activations.
- **The math is slightly off.** 900 to 1,400 is +55.6%, which rounds to 56%.

## Update

**Results**
- Monthly activated developers, company-wide: 900 before March, 1,400 now (+500/month, +56%).
- Over six months at the new run rate, that is about 3,000 added activations. This assumes the lift was immediate; if it ramped, the true added total is lower and the cost per activation is higher.
- DevRel spend was $180k, excluding salaries. That works out to about $60 per added activation, on the "all of the lift is DevRel" assumption.

**What we can and can't say**
- ✅ Activation grew 56% while the program ran.
- ✅ Program spend, excluding salaries, was $180k.
- ⚠️ The lift can't yet be split between DevRel and the v3 SDK. If DevRel gets half the credit, the cost is about $120 per activation.
- ⚠️ We don't yet have a comparison with paid ads. Growth's $95 includes salaries and our $60 doesn't. We should compare on one basis, either both with salaries or both without.

**Cost per added activation under each assumption** (cost ÷ 3,000 added activations)

| DevRel's share of the lift | Excluding salaries ($180k) | Including salaries |
|---|---|---|
| 100% | $60 | ($180k + salaries) ÷ 3,000 |
| 50% | $120 | ($180k + salaries) ÷ 1,500 |

**Next steps (with owners and dates)**
1. Tag activations by source (DevRel content, docs, events, community) to measure DevRel-touched activation directly. *Owner: DevRel and data, by [date].*
2. Compare activations by SDK version (v3 vs older) to size the SDK effect. *Owner: product analytics, by [date].*
3. Get DevRel's fully loaded cost and restate the $60 on the same basis as growth's $95. *Owner: finance, by [date].*
4. Report the comparison to paid ads again next half with one agreed method.

**Bottom line:** Activation is up and the program is a plausible contributor. We recommend continuing it while we put attribution in place, and not claiming a cost advantage over paid ads until the comparison is like-for-like.
