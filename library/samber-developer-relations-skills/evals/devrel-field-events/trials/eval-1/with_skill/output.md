## 6. Scanning badges and Salesforce

The skill's rule is to take guest data from your own RSVP only. Scanning badges at the door with the conference's scanner is out; the organizer keeps those for the expo floor. Your own event app, checking people in against your RSVP list, is fine.

An RSVP is not consent to a newsletter or a nurture sequence. In Salesforce:

- Log attendance as a Campaign Member and record the next step agreed in the room.
- Follow-up is one person writing to one person within 48 hours.
- Add a guest to a sequence only when they ask.
- Say in the invitation that you collect name and company for the event only.

**Compliance flags.** Read these before the invitation goes out, never at the door.

- **US federal employees** can accept $20 per occasion and $50 per year from one source. They get coffee, or they pay their own way.
- **Healthcare professionals:** use the stricter of the guest's employer policy and the health-industry code caps. Skip activities.
- **Public hospitals, universities, foreign-government and state-owned guests:** ask their policy first.
- **Banks and insurers** often cap vendor entertainment.
- **Everyone:** record each guest's hospitality value and offer an alcohol-free option.

This step supplements your legal review and never replaces it.

## 8. Follow-up and verdict

- **Within 48 hours:** the host who spoke with the guest sends a personal note naming something they said and offering the step agreed in the room. If the account owner wasn't at the table, the host copies them and the owner carries the step.
- **No agreed next step:** thank-you only.
- **Customers** go to customer success. **Community guests** go to DevRel.
- **30 days:** show rate, guests with an agreed next step, steps completed. Compare with the threshold.
- **90 days:** stage movement of attendee opportunities against accounts nominated but not seated. Waitlisted accounts are the comparison group. If nominations exceed seats, choose among equally ranked ones by lot. Report counts and label the comparison directional.
- **Verdict:** repeat the format, change the guest-list method, or drop it. A miss means changing the method, not finding a friendlier metric.
