# KubeCon NA exec dinner: tightened plan

**Verdict:** the sponsor-dinner instinct is right, but three parts of the plan would work against Dana's goal. Those are the headcount math, the CEO walkthrough, and the seating.

## What to change

| Dana's plan | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Exactly 15 invites | Cold prospects accept at roughly 30-45%, customers at 80%+. You'd seat about 9. KubeCon Tuesday night is also crowded with vendor dinners. | Invite about 24-26 in two waves to fill 15 seats (see Invite waves). |
| Subject "KubeCon VIP Dinner - Exclusive Evening with Lumenrail" | Reads as mass vendor email. Platform directors get dozens of these that week. | Name the audience and topic, not the vendor (see Invite copy). |
| Sent from events@ | A stranger gets a no-reply-style address from a vendor they barely know. | The prospect's AE or Dana sends a personal note. events@ handles only confirmations and logistics. |
| CEO 10-min product walkthrough between courses | Directors came for peers. A demo turns a dinner into a captive pitch, and the 9 prospects are the ones most likely to resent it. | CEO gives a 3-min welcome and no slides. Offer the walkthrough at the booth or as a follow-up. |
| AEs sit with their own accounts to "work the table" | Guests feel hovered over. Customers get sold to in front of prospects. | Hosts sit with guests they don't own. AE owners brief the host beforehand and debrief afterward. |
| 6 customers + 9 prospects | The ratio is fine, but customers are the best part of the evening only if they aren't used as props. | Seat customers across from prospects and brief them lightly. Ask for honest conversation, not endorsement. |

## Seating and headcount

- **Decision for Dana:** is "15" guests or total seats?
- **Default:** book 20 seats, either two tables of 10 or one long table. That gives 15 guests plus 5 hosts.
- **Hosts:** CEO, Dana, and up to 3 AEs or solutions engineers. No more hosts than that.
- **Each table:** 7-8 guests and 2-3 hosts. Put customers and prospects at both tables, and keep no more than 2 guests from the same company together.
- **Table topic:** one conversation prompt per table, such as "what did your platform team stop building this year?" Hosts ask and listen.

## Invite waves

1. **Wave 1 (send now):** all 6 customers and the 9 warmest prospects. Set the RSVP deadline at 5 business days.
2. **Wave 2 (day 4):** about 8-10 backups, sent only if Wave 1 acceptances are under 15.
3. **Hold a waitlist.** Expect 2-3 no-shows on the day, so keep a backup list you can call Monday.
4. **Prospects we barely know:** the AE sends a short personal message first, then Dana or the AE sends the invite.

## Invite copy

**Subject:** Dinner for platform engineering leaders, Tue 7pm, Salt Lake City

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm hosting a small dinner for platform engineering leaders during KubeCon NA, Tuesday at 7pm in Salt Lake City. About 15 people, one long conversation about [topic: e.g. platform adoption, golden paths, what to retire].
>
> Lumenrail is hosting, but this isn't a pitch evening. Our CEO will say hello, and the rest is conversation. We'll be done by 9:30.
>
> If you can join, reply here and I'll confirm the details. If not, no worries.
>
> [Sender name]

Add a line for customers: "Would love your honest take on [topic]." Add a line for prospects with a specific reason, such as a talk they gave or a problem they've mentioned. Include a note on dietary needs and one that employer gift rules are on us to respect: "If your company has a gift policy, tell me and we'll work with it."

## Run of show

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 7:00 | Arrivals, drinks. Hosts introduce guests to each other, not to Lumenrail. |
| 7:20 | Seated. CEO welcome, 3 min, no slides. |
| 7:25-9:00 | Courses. Table prompt for the starter, free conversation after. |
| 9:00 | Optional: CEO takes questions for 10 min, only if guests ask. |
| 9:30 | Close. Each host jots 2 notes per guest within the hour. |

## After the dinner

- **Within 48 hours:** a personal follow-up from the AE who owns the account, referencing something said at the table. No automated sequence.
- **Goal:** next meetings booked, not "pipeline attributed". Count meetings from the 9 prospects.
- **Customers:** thank them, and ask about references only later.

## Check before sending

1. **Schedule clash:** confirm 7pm Tuesday doesn't overlap the official sponsor reception or booth crawl. Gold sponsors usually have obligations then.
2. **Dates:** I believe KubeCon NA is Nov 9-12, so Tuesday is Nov 10, only about 10 days out. Verify the date. If it's right, invites go out today.
3. **Venue:** a private room in Salt Lake City, with a rough per-head budget. Utah alcohol rules can limit what the restaurant can serve, so ask the venue.
4. **Open question for Dana:** are the 9 prospects named and routed to AEs yet? If not, that's the critical path.
