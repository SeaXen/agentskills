**Yes to both, kept small.** Cap the spend at about €2k for a one-off. Ask the founder or CEO for that today, since there's no budget line.

**Do these today (about 30 minutes):**
1. Sales lead checks the 6 prospects against your ICP and confirms each is attending. Drop any who aren't.
2. Sales lead sends 6 invites by tomorrow. Make them personal, one line, no pitch.
3. DevRel picks 3 plugin authors, ideally ones prospects would want to meet. Invite them to the same dinner.
4. Someone books a restaurant for 10 to 12 people near the venue. Book now, since 12 days out is already tight.

**Dinner**
- It has 6 prospects and 3 plugin authors. Add DevRel, 1 AE and 1 founder or exec, so no more than 11 or 12 seats.
- Don't call it VIP. Call it a dinner for people building on API gateways.
- Don't pitch. Plugin authors talking to prospects does the selling.
- Keep staff to roughly 1 per 3 guests, or prospects feel hunted.
- Cost is about €1,000 to €1,500, assuming €80 to €120 a head with drinks.
- Success target is at least 4 of the 6 prospects attending. Measure it by next-step meetings booked within 7 days, not by attendance.

**Plugin author event**
- The dinner already covers 3 authors. For the rest of your community, host a 45-minute coffee or breakfast meetup on day 2 near the venue.
- It costs about €150 to €300 and DevRel can run it alone.
- The AEs skip it. It's community, not pipeline, and an AE there will make authors wary.

**Why not drop one**
- Dropping the dinner leaves the 6 known prospects with no way to meet you.
- Dropping the author event wastes the community and the content it would produce.
- Combining 3 authors into the dinner costs almost nothing extra and makes both better.

**Why not go bigger**
- 25 people and 12 days is not enough to run a large event well.
- The AEs also need to book hallway meetings with other attendees. The dinner shouldn't use up their time.

**Follow-up (the part that decides ROI)**
1. The same night, each host notes who said what and any agreed next step in the CRM.
2. Within 48 hours, the AE sends a personal note to each prospect that references their conversation.
3. Within 7 days, review: number of meetings booked, and cost per meeting.

**Open question:** are the plugin authors also prospects or customers? If they're mostly end-users of your gateway, the mixed table works. If they're competitors' users, keep them out of the dinner and give them the coffee meetup only.
