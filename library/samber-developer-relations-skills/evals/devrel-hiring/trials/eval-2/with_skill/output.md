Quick flag before the rewrite: this draft is close to a textbook "gatekeeper" posting (Joe Nash's term) — it stacks a senior, multi-language production-engineering résumé, a CNCF contribution history, a published writing portfolio, event-organizing experience, and a 10k+ audience requirement, all at an Associate title and $72k. No one candidate clears that bar at that pay, and the ones who could clear it wouldn't apply at that level. I right-sized the requirements to match "Associate" and warmed the tone at the same time — both were needed, not just the wording.

---

**Developer Advocate (Associate) — $72,000**

Helix Relay builds tooling for event-stream systems — we help developers run their pipelines without losing a weekend to them. We're hiring our first Developer Advocate: the friendly, technical bridge between our engineering team and the people building on our tools.

**What you'll do**

- Write posts and docs that get developers unstuck faster — we'll track this against support-ticket volume on the topics you cover
- Run our Discord: answer questions, and turn the ones that repeat into docs fixes or product feedback
- Speak at a few meetups or events a year — we'll help you find them and prep
- Join sales calls when a prospect needs a technical translator, and bring back what you hear to the product team

**What we're looking for**

- Comfortable reading code and writing simple examples. Bonus if you've touched a message broker or event-streaming system (Kafka, NATS, Pulsar, or similar) — we'll teach you the rest
- You enjoy explaining technical things in plain language, and you don't mind a first draft getting edited
- Not fazed by a Discord full of strangers, some with a real bug, some just having a bad day
- Nice to have, not required: something you've written or built in public — a blog, a side project, talk notes, a community you've moderated. No follower count needed; we just want to see how you communicate

**What we're not asking for**

- Years of production engineering across multiple languages
- Open-source contributions to a specific foundation project
- A conference-organizing track record
- Any particular social following

We're a small team, so this role is broad by design — you'll help shape it, not just fill it. First 90 days: use the product like a real developer would, keep a running list of everything that trips you up, and ship your first fix from that list.

**Compensation:** $72,000 + [equity/benefits — fill in].
