# Hiring funnel: Community Manager (Discord/forum, 35k members)

Order below fits 3 hrs (180 min) interviewer time per candidate, assuming 1 interviewer per live round. Panel of 2+ on a round multiplies that round's cost against the cap — trim elsewhere if you panel any round.

Budget only hits full 180 min for candidates who reach founder round (your final 2-3). Earlier rejects cost 10-45 min, not 180.

## Order

1. **Technical self-rating questionnaire** — async, 0 interviewer min. Pure filter, cuts mismatches before any human time spent. Cover: SQL/DB basics, git/GitHub workflow, open-source licensing basics, moderation-tooling familiarity.
2. **Content take-home** — async (candidate time), review 10 min. Writing/communication is core skill for this role, test before coding. Gate step 3 on this: only pass-content candidates get sent coding take-home — saves review hours across whole pool, not just this candidate.
3. **Coding take-home** — async, review 10 min. Technical fluency is a floor for this role, not the main skill. Keep light.
4. **Conversational round 1 (screen)** — live, 1 interviewer, 25 min. Motivation, logistics, comms style, basic fit. Cheapest live filter before deeper spend.
5. **Presentation round** — live, 1 interviewer, 30 min (15 present + 15 Q&A). This IS the job (AMAs, community calls). High signal, mid-funnel — weak presenters exit before founder time spent.
6. **Conversational round 2 (scenario/judgment)** — live, 1 interviewer, 30 min. Give anonymized real transcript from your licence-change threads. Ask how they'd moderate/respond live. Highest-value round given your context — test against YOUR actual fire, not generic scenarios.
7. **DevRel-opinion round** — live, 1 interviewer, ideally a maintainer/dev, not founder, 25 min. Tests technical-community fluency, whether devs trust this person's day-to-day judgment.
8. **Conversational round 3 (team fit)** — live, 1-2 future peers, 25 min. Last non-founder round — people they work with daily sign off before founder round.
9. **Founder round** — live, founder, 25 min. Final. Close/sell + gut check. By now: writing, technical floor, presentation, judgment, dev-trust, peer-fit all validated — this round is not a screen.

## Budget check

10 + 10 + 25 + 30 + 30 + 25 + 25 + 25 = 180 min. Exact fit.

## Why this order, not another

- Content before coding: writing predicts CM success better, and it's cheaper for candidate to produce (no dev env).
- Presentation mid-funnel, not last: direct job simulation, cheapest way to cut weak performers before spending founder time.
- Licence-change scenario round placed after presentation, not first: judgment call needs basic fit confirmed first, but must land before DevRel/team rounds so those rounds can react to how candidate actually handled it.
- DevRel round before team-fit, not after: dev trust is a harder gate than peer chemistry — cut on it before spending peers' time.
- Founder last always: scarcest interviewer time, best spent closing a validated finalist, not screening an unknown.
