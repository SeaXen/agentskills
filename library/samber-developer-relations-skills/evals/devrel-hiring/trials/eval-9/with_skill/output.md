Assuming unpaid-or-stipend model here, standard for this role type — confirm if wrong, changes everything below.

**What's wrong, line by line:**

1. **4+ years experience** — wrong filter. Screen on contribution evidence (PRs, talks given, forum/issue triage history, a DevStats-style score), not years.
2. **Own the conference schedule for their region** — fine as activity, not as owned deliverable. No budget/employment backing "ownership."
3. **Respond within 24h** — no SLA on volunteers. Reframe as guideline, not requirement.
4. **Weekly 1:1, reports to you** — biggest issue. 8-10 people × weekly 1:1 = unsustainable for you, and it makes unpaid contributors look like direct reports. Some jurisdictions read sustained one-on-one control over unpaid people as an employment-classification risk. Replace with monthly group call + async channel.

**Rewrite the ask as:**

- Contribution threshold to apply (talks given, PRs merged, community answers, whatever this project already tracks)
- Regional meetup/conference presence as expected activity, not owned KPI
- Community support as best-effort, not SLA-backed
