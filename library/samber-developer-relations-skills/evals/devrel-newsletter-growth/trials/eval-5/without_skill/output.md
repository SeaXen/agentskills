The FTC and EU rules (you're in France) expect it to be disclosed, and readers who find out later trust you less. A short, friendly disclosure is enough. It doesn't need the word "sponsored".

## Is it free growth?

- **Audience fit is weak.** Her readers are designers and illustrators. Ours write Go backends. Few will care about Go, so expect low signups from her side.
- **The swap is lopsided.** She reaches 14k people and we reach 3k. Our readers get a mention they may find off-topic, and her readers get one that is mostly irrelevant to them.
- **The cost is trust.** If your subscribers feel an unlabeled ad slipped in, you lose more than the swap gains.

If you still want to do it, the blurb above is fair to both lists. If she insists on no disclosure at all, I'd decline politely and offer a swap with a clear "we trade mentions" line instead. A better growth partner would be another backend or developer newsletter.
