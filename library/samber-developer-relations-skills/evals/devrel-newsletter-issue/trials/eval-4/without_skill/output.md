**What I changed and why**
- **Subject:** I replaced "October Edition 🚀" with the lead story. The story gives readers a reason to open the email, and the month is already in the intro. I also dropped the emoji, which signalled enthusiasm rather than information. If you like it, add it back at the end.
- **Intro:** The draft opened with filler ("busy month", "tons of exciting updates", "grab a coffee") and never named the news. The new version leads with 5.0. It also warns readers about the two breaking changes, since ESM-only and Node 18 are the parts that can break someone's build.
