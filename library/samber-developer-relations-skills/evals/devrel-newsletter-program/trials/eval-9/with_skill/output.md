Provisional. The skill says to ask about supply, owner hours and backup before picking a shape. You skipped those, so the gate below is unproven, not passed. The digest is the safest pick to make blind.

Why the digest wins:
- A curated newsletter is a standing job. Its curator's judgment is hard to hand over, and a startup with no named backup loses it fast.
- The digest is an afternoon per issue. Any teammate can fill a fixed template from the changelog, blog and docs.
- The skill's upgrade rule: move to curated only after three monthly digests that each carried one trial external section whose items drew clicks.

## Shape
Own-surface digest. Deleted: personal letter (ends when the person leaves) and community-edited (no contributor base known). Deferred: curated ecosystem (see the upgrade rule above).

## Cadence and anchor
Monthly. Send on a fixed weekday right after your release or changelog cut. If releases are irregular, fix a calendar day and cover everything shipped since the last issue. Slow down after 2 thin issues. Speed up only after 3 full ones. Never raise cadence to fix engagement.

## Curation rule (house rule, no industry ratio exists)
- Own content only for issues 1-3, plus one trial external section ("Elsewhere in GraphQL", 1-2 links).
- Include: anything a reader can use this month.
- Exclude: gated content, announcements with no usage example, named-competitor comparisons, unannounced features, named customers. Add your own off-limits items.
- Paid or partner items: none at launch.

## Measurement

- Review quarterly against your own trend. Issues 1-3 set the baseline; label thresholds as house rules.

## Pause and sunset

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Owner leaves, backup can't take over within one cycle | Hiatus note, then handoff checklist or downgrade to feed only |
| 2 consecutive thin issues | Drop to a slower cadence or merge into the changelog |
| Clicks and replies fall 2 quarters in a row | Re-run the gate |
| A forum, chat or feed now does the job | Final issue pointing to the replacement |
| No budget line for running cost | Close or downgrade before anyone pays personally |
| List silent more than 6 months | Re-permission issue, suppress non-responders |

Announce every pause. Never skip an issue silently.
