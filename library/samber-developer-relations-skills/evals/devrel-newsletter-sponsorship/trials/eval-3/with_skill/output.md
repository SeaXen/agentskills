# Should The Lantern take the $300 hosting-startup sponsorship?

**Answer: not yet, and not at $300 as offered.** Say "yes, after two fixes" to the sponsor, and you can still book a paid pilot for the issue after next.

- **Gate:** it fails on "readers told" and on measured engagement.
- **Price:** $300 is about 2× what your evidence supports.
- **Next issue:** use the next issue to fix both problems. Run the sponsor one issue later.
- **CI costs:** one $300 booking won't fix them, and a bad first booking costs you the list's trust.

## Refused asks

| Ask | Status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| "Sponsored section" if it includes a review, pick or news-section mention | Decline | Trust and house rule: editorial coverage is never part of the price. |
| "Presented by" or "Sponsored by" as the only label | Decline | Regulator guidance (US, UK) and German case law treat these as funding wording, not advertising. |
| Any click guarantee or subscriber-list sharing | Decline if asked | Gateway scanners meet raw-click guarantees. Sharing clicker identities has no lawful basis in the EU or UK. |

**Check first:** the email says "sponsored section", and I haven't seen it. If it means one labelled ad box, we can sell it. If it means editorial space, that part is refused. Ask the sponsor to say which.

## Gate result

All four conditions must hold. Two fail, and one of those blocks every sale.

| Condition | Evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Measured engagement | 4 issues sent, no click tracking, no bot filter. The gate needs 6 issues of per-link data with a named filter. | **Fails** |
| Describable reader | You say "contributors to an open-source static site generator". The form asks no role or stack question. | **Fails** (cheap fix) |
| Seller with hours | No named seller, hours or invoicing entity (project, fiscal host?). | **Blocked**, needs your answer |
| Readers told | The form says only "Get monthly project news". It doesn't mention sponsor content. | **Fails**, and blocks every sale until fixed |

**Cheapest fixes, in order:**

1. **Readers told (blocks everything).**
   - Change the form to something like "Monthly project news. Issues may carry labelled sponsor content."
   - Announce sponsor content in the next issue, before any paid one, with the unsubscribe link prominent.
2. **Engagement.**
   - Turn on per-link click tracking and the platform's bot-click filter now.
   - Gateways click links on arrival, so unfiltered counts are machine noise.
   - If your platform kept click data for issues 1–4, export it.
3. **Reader description.** Add one optional signup question, such as role (maintainer, contributor, user) and which site generators or hosting they use.
4. **Seller.** Name the person who answers sponsors, approves copy and sends reports. Name the entity that invoices.

Four issues, one a month, means the six-issue evidence threshold lands around two issues after tracking starts. You don't have to wait that long, because the skill allows a pilot.

**Pilot booking (allowed once "readers told" and "seller" pass):** one issue, at the cost-recovery floor, with the missing evidence disclosed to the sponsor in writing. Its report becomes your first data point.

## Acceptance policy

Written now, before any cheque. Proposed defaults are marked, for your approval:

- **Fit:** products that serve contributors to a static site generator. Claims must be ones you could check.
- **Category vetoes (proposed):** token sales, gambling, and products that fork or rebrand the project.
- **Competitor rule:** this applies if you sell a product or hosting yourself. Decide whether the project has a recommended or partner host. If it does, a rival host is a conflict; decide that before answering this sponsor.
- **Editorial firewall:** the slot buys a position and a label, never coverage, ranking or a review. Sponsors see only their own unit before send.
- **Edit rights:** you may require edits or reject copy, with a reason, up to the copy deadline.
- **Links:** one link per unit, to the sponsor's own domain. No downloads, no unreadable shorteners.
- **Refusal log:** date, sponsor, rule applied, who decided.

The hosting startup is **not judged yet**. Judging it needs the veto list and the competitor decision.

## Sales route

Ranked by efficiency, recommend one: direct primary slot, network listing, classifieds, bundles, dedicated sends.

- **Recommended: one direct labelled primary slot per issue.**
  - It is the route that scales with a few hours a month, and renewals compound only where you own the relationship.
- **Network listing:** 1,380 subscribers barely clears the lowest stated floors (about 1,000). You'd give up 10–40% of revenue and some copy control. Not worth it at this size.
- **Dedicated sends:** deleted. Readers consented to "project news", not partner marketing.
- **Bundles and secondary slots:** later.

## Inventory and caps

- One primary slot per issue. Cap it at the charter's limit, or one primary slot plus classifieds if there's no charter. Check whether a charter exists and what it says.
- Spec to fix: position 2 (after the lead item), up to 60 words, one link, no image.
- No exclusivity by default. Sell it only as a priced add-on, limited to the booked issues.

## Rate card

Provisional. Questions 2, 3 and 6 are still open, so this is a band, not a quote.

| Method | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Cost-recovery floor | (sending platform cost + seller hours × hourly value) ÷ slots, grossed up for any fiscal-host share | **Blocked.** Needs your platform cost, seller hours, hourly value and host share. |
| Comparable band | Published primary-slot ratios run about 20–73 per 1,000 gross subscribers (Q2 2024 cards, lists of 16k–180k). 1.38 × (20 to 73) | about **$28–$101** |
| Click value | No sponsor-unit clicks and no tracked editorial clicks | **Cannot compute.** Until the first report, quote the band's bottom. |

- **Ceiling:** before the first sponsor report, cap click value at the band's top plus a third, about **$135**. Never go under the floor.
- **$300 is about 2.2× that ceiling.** It is about $217 per 1,000 subscribers against a published range of 20–73.
- **Weak evidence:** the band comes from lists 12–130× your size, so treat it as a frame, not proof.
- **Sanity check only:** a cross-genre figure (unverified) of about 2% ad click-through on a small list gives roughly 28 clicks, so $300 would be about $10 per click against about $5 in that analysis. It says nothing certain for your list.
- **Why it matters:** a sponsor who overpays and sees tiny click numbers doesn't rebook. If they know that, pay what the evidence supports and let the first report set the next price.
- **Price for the pilot:** quote the cost-recovery floor, once you give me the inputs. It will almost certainly land under the ~$135 ceiling.
- **If they still want to pay $300** after reading the disclosure below, that is your call. Treat it as one-off, make no guarantee, and note that it breaks the "price traces to floor, band or measured clicks" rule.

**The hosting startup's CPC:** if they quote a search or social cost per click, label it buyer-supplied and discount it. Newsletter clicks carry less intent than search clicks.

**For CI costs:** if the startup wants to fund the project, not buy an ad slot, that's a different deal. Offering CI credits or a standing sponsorship fits `oss-sponsors-brand-strategy`. Ask them directly.

## Booking terms

Confirm every line in writing before the invoice:

- **Slot and date:** one named slot in one named issue. Same price for any sponsor on the same date.
- **Hold:** released if not confirmed in 7 days.
- **Copy deadline:** 2 full working days before send.
- **Late copy:** runs as sponsor name, one neutral line and the link, or moves to a later issue. You never write promotional copy for them.
- **Edit rights:** you may require edits or reject copy, with a reason.
- **Label:** fixed house label, not negotiable.
- **Links:** one link to their own domain. Their tracking parameters are fine.
- **Cancellation:** at least 1 week's notice. Prepaid fees refunded for issues more than a week out.
- **Pause clause:** if you skip an issue or the programme pauses, the slot moves to the next issue or is refunded in full.
- **Make-good:** a failed send, broken link or missing label reruns in the next comparable slot, at no charge.
- **Payment:** before the copy deadline, through whichever entity invoices. Check VAT or sales-tax treatment for a cross-border sponsor, and whether the fiscal host accepts advertising income and how much it keeps.
- **Warranty:** the sponsor warrants its claims and that the link stays live and safe. You may pull a unit whose claims or destination change.
- **Performance pay:** none, unless added on top of a flat fee above the floor.
- **Data:** aggregate report only. No list, no clicker identities.
- **Exclusivity:** none unless bought.
- **Disclosure to the sponsor (pilot only):** in writing, tell them you have 4 sent issues, no historical click tracking, and no measured human-click median. Tracking starts now.
