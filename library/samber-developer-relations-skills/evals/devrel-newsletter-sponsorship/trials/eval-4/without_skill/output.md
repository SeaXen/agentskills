# Velora sponsorship: draft reply and terms

## Decisions for you (and my assumptions)

1. **Paid review.** I treated it as a sponsored review and labeled it as one. Calling it "honest" doesn't remove the need to disclose payment. FTC rules, French law and the EU Unfair Commercial Practices rules all require it. A labeled review also reads as more credible to 21k subscribers than a hidden one.
2. **Review scope.** Their message doesn't say whether the double rate applies to all 6 issues or to one review. I assumed one review issue at $1,800 plus 5 standard slots at $900, for **$6,300** total. If they want a review in all 6, the total is $10,800, but I'd push back because it would turn the newsletter into a Velora channel.
3. **Click guarantee.** I replaced it with a make-good. A guarantee of 300 clicks can't be promised honestly, because clicks depend on their copy, offer and landing page. I also excluded the review issue from it. A click target on a review creates pressure to write it as an ad.
4. **Click floor.** I set it at 200 unique clicks. That is roughly 1% of your list, so adjust it to your own sponsor-click history. If your typical sponsor slot gets fewer than 200, raise or lower it before sending.

## Terms

2. **Fees.** Review issue: $1,800. Standard slots: $900 each. Total: $6,300.
3. **Payment.** 50% on booking, 50% before the final issue runs. Net 14 days. [Add your currency and VAT or tax handling.]
4. **Schedule.** Dates are agreed in writing within 7 days of booking. Moving a date needs 7 days' notice.
5. **Disclosure.** The review is labeled "Sponsored review" and states that Velora paid. Standard slots are labeled "Sponsor".
6. **Editorial independence.** The author writes the review and keeps full control of its content and verdict. Velora may fact-check and request corrections of factual errors only. Praise is not a condition of payment.
7. **Access.** Velora provides free full access to the library at least 7 days before the review issue.
8. **Withdrawal.** Velora may withdraw the review before publication. A 50% kill fee applies to the review slot. After publication, the review stays up.
9. **Ad materials.** Velora supplies copy, links and creative at least 5 business days before each issue. If materials are late, the author may run a short default text or move the slot, with no refund.
10. **Tracking.** Every link carries a UTM parameter. "Unique clicks" are counted by the author's email platform, with bot and duplicate filtering on.
11. **Click make-good.** This applies to standard slots only, not the review issue. If a slot gets under 200 unique tracked clicks within 7 days of sending, the author reruns it once in a later issue at no charge. It applies only if:
    - the landing page is live and working;
    - the supplied copy and link were not changed after approval;
    - Velora delivered materials on time.

    The make-good is the sole remedy. No refunds.
12. **Exclusivity.** The author won't run a competing component library in the same issue. No wider exclusivity.
13. **Content standards.** The author may decline copy that is misleading, makes unverifiable claims or breaks subscriber trust.
14. **Termination.** Either party may end the agreement with 14 days' written notice. Issues already published are paid in full.
15. **Governing law.** [Your jurisdiction, e.g. France.]

Have a lawyer check the contract-law items (15, VAT and kill fee) before signing if the deal grows beyond this size.
