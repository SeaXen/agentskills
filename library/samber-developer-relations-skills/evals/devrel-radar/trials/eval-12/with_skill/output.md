## Why market stats won't save the headcount

"X million developers, Y% use AI tools" is true of every company. It doesn't show that your team earns its seats.

The number that matters is yours: about 120 developers hit your API weekly. Ferrocast's audience is small, so a "normal team size" benchmark may argue against you. Leadership will ask what the team produces for that audience.
- The stronger slide is outcomes. Use how many of the 120 are new or retained, what DevRel changed in activation, and the cost per active developer.
- The `devrel-metrics` skill builds that framework, with attribution rules and baselines.
