## Before you build the slide

- **Your own number works against industry share.** 120 weekly API developers is small. Any "market is huge, so staff up" argument invites the question of why you reach only 120.
- **Industry stats won't save headcount. Your funnel will.** Show what your 120 weekly developers do after they arrive: activation, retention, and revenue or pipeline they influence. Add what happens to that if the team shrinks.
- **If leadership wants a benchmark,** ask peers directly. The DevRel Collective Slack and your own network will give you real numbers for companies your size.
