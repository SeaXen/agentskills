# DevRel radar for a technical writer who just inherited the community

**This is a curated reading list, not a monitoring service.** Nothing here alerts you when a source dies. Re-check it quarterly, and the sources have to be re-checked by hand this time (see the verification note).

**Profile:** technical writer at Ostley (payments API), now also owning community. **Pillars:** enablement (docs) and community. **Budget:** 25 min/week, text only. **Entries are ordered by value per attention-minute, highest first.**

## Do this first (about 5 min, once)

1. Subscribe to the DeveloperRelations.com newsletter (free).
2. Subscribe to the DevRel Foundation community mailing list (free, no application).
3. Put a 25-minute block in your calendar called "DevRel radar".

## Is DevRel Weekly still a thing?

Not as a weekly. The skill's last audit found that the curator, Mary Thengvall, posted her own announcement on the site that the strict weekly format is ending. Twitter/X changes had broken the RSS workflow it ran on. It is replaced by an occasional "DevRel Thoughts Shared Randomly" format.

- The site was returning 525 errors during that audit, which fits a site mid-transition rather than a dead one.
- Subscribe if you like. It's free and costs little.
- Don't give it weekly minutes. It sits in the rotating slot below and gets read only when an issue lands.
- I could not re-check it today (see the verification note), so confirm it by opening the site.

**Annual: one concentrated read**

- **CMX Community Industry Report** (cmxhub.com): budgets, team sizes and measurement benchmarks for community teams. It is free but gated. Read it once, the next time someone asks what a community team should look like after the layoffs. It is the cheapest source of numbers for a headcount conversation. Cite its edition and date, never the bare number. The 2026 edition is the seventh.
- **Diátaxis** (diataxis.fr) is an evergreen docs framework you probably know already. It is a one-time reference, not a weekly item, and it carries no dates.

**Bench (verified substitutes, only if a slot opens)**

- DevRel Collective Slack (devrelcollective.fun): the peer Slack, free, application-gated. I'd swap it in for the mailing list if you want to ask questions rather than read. Its activity can't be observed from outside.
- Open Source Guides (opensource.guide): evergreen maintainer-side community guidance. It fits a closed-source API only partly.
- CMX community (cmxhub.com): the community-profession hub.

**Totals:** 10 weekly + about 15 rotating = 25 of 25 min. That is 2 weekly entries, 4 rotating, 2 annual and 3 bench.

**Pillar coverage:** every owned pillar has a verified-active source: yes. Enablement is covered by Write the Docs and Diátaxis. Community is covered by the DRF list, DeveloperRelations.com and CMX. Marketing is not owned, so I left it empty.

## Dropped, with the reason

- **All podcasts** (Community Pulse, Scaling DevTools, Open Source Ready, The Business of Open Source, the Heavybit network, DevRel Roundtable): audio is ruled out, since you can't listen at your desk and have no commute.
- **Video, including the DevRelCon talk archive and any channel:** video is ruled out.
- **Marketing-pillar newsletters** (Console.dev, Draft.dev, EveryDeveloper, Tessa Kriesel): you don't own that pillar. They are left out rather than padded in.

## Gaps to know about

- **Payments or fintech:** the skill's source data has nothing payments-specific. I won't invent a source. Your own support queue and changelog are the best ecosystem signal you have.
- **Segment:** this skill's data skews to bottom-up B2B devtools. If Ostley sells top-down to enterprises, tell me and I'll re-rank.
- **People worth following (text surfaces):** Mary Thengvall (curator of DevRel Weekly, community; sells consulting) and Matthew Revell (founder of the DeveloperRelations.com hub; his agency has closed, the hub has not).
