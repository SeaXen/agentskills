**Scope:** Few big channels teach DevRel as a craft. This list is channels a GraphQL gateway advocate should watch: big dev-culture channels, API and gateway channels, and GraphQL channels.

## Ranked by subscribers

1. **Fireship**, ~3.5M, **15 min/week**. Watch 2 short videos, like "100 Seconds" or "Code Report". Use it to learn how to make dense technical content that stays short.
2. **ByteByteGo**, ~1M, **10 min/week**. Watch 1 video. API design, gateways, and caching explained for a broad audience.
3. **GOTO Conferences**, ~1M, **20 min/week**. Watch 1 conference talk, 20 minutes. Pick API, architecture, or developer-experience talks.
4. **Theo (t3.gg)**, ~500k, **15 min/week**. Watch 1 video. See how developers react to API tools and launches. Unfiltered feedback on your market.
5. **Hussein Nasser**, ~400k, **15 min/week**. Watch 1 video. Proxies, gateways, HTTP, and protocols in depth. Your technical credibility comes from here.
6. **Postman**, ~150–200k, **10 min/week**. Watch 1 video. A large API company's DevRel at scale. Copy the format, not the product.
7. **CNCF**, ~150k, **10 min/week**. Skim 1 talk. Search "API gateway" or "Envoy". Shows what the cloud-native audience expects from a gateway.
8. **Apollo GraphQL**, ~30k, **15 min/week**. Watch 1 video. The closest peer: Federation, supergraph, and router content. Know their story to tell yours.
9. **GraphQL Foundation**, ~10k, **10 min/week**. Watch 1 GraphQLConf talk, trimmed to 10 minutes. Your community's own conversation.

**Total: 120 min/week.** Ranking by size does not mean priority. Channels 8 and 9 are small but closest to your job. Do not cut them first.
