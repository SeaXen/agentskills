## Session 1 (2h): learn what the job is

1. Read *The Business Value of Developer Relations* (Mary Thengvall). Skim, don't finish. Target: chapters on advocacy vs. community vs. enablement. 60 min.
2. Watch Adam DuVander's "Developer Marketing Does Not Exist" talk. Learn why devs distrust marketing. 30 min.
3. Join the DevRel Collective Slack. Read the intro and jobs channels only. 15 min.
4. Write 5 lines: "advocacy means X for me, enablement means Y." You'll revise on day 10. 15 min.

## Session 2 (2h): become Hollick's customer

1. Do the Hollick quickstart cold. Use a fresh account, no insider help. 60 min.
2. Log every stumble in a doc: step, error, time lost. Your platform-engineer eyes are your best asset, and they dull after week 2 on the job. 30 min.
3. Read Hollick's docs, changelog, and last 10 GitHub issues or forum threads. Note the top 3 complaints. 30 min.

## Session 3 (2h): learn the field and the people

1. Read the Orbit model write-up (Orbit, community-led growth) and *People Powered* (Jono Bacon), first third. 45 min.
2. Find 3 competitors' DevRel people. Follow them. Note their content cadence and formats. 30 min.
3. Find 5 Hollick users in public: X, LinkedIn, GitHub, Discord. Read what they say about the product. Do not DM yet. 30 min.
4. Pick 1 small public thing to ship in week 2 (a short tutorial from your Session 2 log). Draft the outline. 15 min.

## Day-one asks (send before Session 1)

1. Who is the target developer, and what does success look like at 90 days?
2. Which numbers does leadership watch: signups, activation, docs traffic, pipeline?
3. Who owns docs, community channels, and social accounts? Do you get access on day one?
4. What content or events exist already? What is already committed?
5. Who in product and engineering will answer your questions fast?

## Free and remote-friendly fits (no travel budget)

- Virtual talks: apply to CFPs for online events. Conference submissions often open months ahead.
- Meetups: join local or online ones as an attendee first.
- Livestreams and recorded screencasts need no budget.
- Written tutorials and a public sample repo need no budget.

## Skip until you have 30 days of context

- Metrics frameworks and dashboards.
- Big campaigns, swag, or event plans.
- Hiring or strategy decks.
