**Outsource the blog. Don't hire a community manager — not yet.**

Reasoning, short version:

- **There's no community to manage.** No Discord/Slack/forum, no user base with critical mass. A "community manager" title only makes sense once a community exists. Right now you'd be hiring someone to build one from zero — that's a founder-led job, not a CM job, and at 4h/week you don't have the bandwidth to direct that build.
- **The actual gap is output, not engagement.** Two engineers post "when they feel like it" — that's a cadence problem. An agency solves cadence directly: scoped brief, fixed deliverables, fixed monthly cost.
- **Reversibility favors the agency.** Cancel a retainer in 30 days if it's not working. Undo a hire is expensive — severance, sunk ramp time, morale hit.
- **The finance number is the tell.** You don't know if it includes your salary. That ambiguity is itself the answer: an agency retainer is a clean cash number you can compare against budget today. A hire is salary + benefits + tools + the management time you don't have — you can't even price that side of the comparison yet. Don't commit to the harder-to-reverse, harder-to-price option while the cheaper, clearer one is sitting right there.
- **Management overhead.** A CM needs a manager with real bandwidth. You have 4h/week. An agency needs a monthly review call, not a manager.

**Mitigate the one real risk of outsourcing** (agency content reading generic, not developer-authentic): have the agency work from short interviews/outlines with your two backend engineers each month, so posts still carry their voice and technical specifics — just with someone else doing the writing and shipping on schedule.

**Revisit the community-manager hire when:** you have an actual place users gather (support channel, forum, Slack) with organic activity, and someone above 4h/week can own directing that hire. Until then, it's a role with nothing to manage.

One action before either: **ask finance directly whether their number is fully-loaded (includes salary) or agency-only** — that answer alone may make this moot.
