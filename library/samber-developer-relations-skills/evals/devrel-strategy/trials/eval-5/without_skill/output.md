## Ormont Labs — H1 DevRel Plan, tightened

**Biggest problem first, because it invalidates the rest until fixed:** this is an H1 plan (Q1–Q2), but the hires land in Q3 — after H1 is over. As written, two blog posts a month, four conference talks, a Discord launch, a docs refresh, a monthly livestream, two hackathons, and a swag store all run with **zero dedicated DevRel headcount for the entire plan period.** That's not an aggressive plan, it's a plan for a role that doesn't exist yet. Fix this before anything else — either pull the dev advocate hire into Q1, or cut H1 scope to what founders/eng can sustain part-time and treat Q3 as when the *scaled* program starts, not when it begins.

I've assumed the second (no budget to move the hire) and cut accordingly. If the hire can move to Q1, say so and I'll restore the deferred items.

### Goals — ranked, not listed

Six co-equal goals isn't a plan. Two of these are outcomes, two are inputs that serve those outcomes, and two are byproducts you shouldn't chase directly yet.

- **Primary: awareness, sales support.** These are the outcomes leadership will ask about.
- **Supporting infrastructure: docs, product feedback.** Not separate initiatives — they're what makes the primary goals credible (bad docs kill sales-assisted trials; no feedback loop means the runtime doesn't improve).
- **Deferred to H2: community growth, employer brand.** Both need a staffed program to do well. Running them unstaffed produces the worst version of each — a dead Discord server, a "we have a Discord" line on a careers page nobody can point to results from.

### Metrics — three of your five stated goals currently have no metric at all

Blog views, Discord members, conference attendees, GitHub stars, and social followers are reach numbers — they tell you who was exposed, not whether the goal was met. Mapped against your own goals: awareness and community have metrics; **sales support, docs, and product feedback have none.** That gap is worth fixing before the reach numbers.

| Goal | Drop / demote | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Keep blog views, stars, followers — but as a trend line only, never a target | Referral traffic to signup, branded search volume |
| Sales support | *(nothing currently measures this)* | # sales-cited docs/blog pieces; deals with self-reported "found via [content/talk]" |
| Docs | *(nothing currently measures this)* | Time-to-first-deploy on quickstart; support-ticket deflection rate |
| Product feedback | *(nothing currently measures this)* | # actionable items logged to backlog from Discord/talks; triage/close rate |
| Community (H2) | Discord member count | 7-day new-member retention, WAU/member ratio — a small active server beats a large silent one |
| Conferences | Attendee headcount | Qualified conversations / meetings booked per event — headcount just means people were in the room |

Employer brand gets no H1 metric — it's a byproduct of the above, not a targeted line item until you're actually running a hiring push around it.

### Net effect

H1: 1 blog post/month, one scoped docs fix, 2 conference talks (CFPs submitted immediately), a quiet Discord seed. Everything requiring dedicated staff — livestream, hackathons, swag, public community push — starts in Q3 alongside the hires, not before them. Metrics get three new owners (sales support, docs, feedback) instead of five reach numbers standing in for six goals.
