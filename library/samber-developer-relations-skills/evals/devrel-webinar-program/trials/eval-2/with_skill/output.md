## 1. Diagnosis

- **Live attendance:** 70 of 240 registrants is 29%. That sits inside the 20–35% band for a small host with a mixed registrant list (assumed). No developer-specific benchmark exists. After two episodes, compare against your own median instead.
- **The 38% MQL figure:** this skill refuses it. It traces to an agency blog with no methodology, misattributed to other vendors. Neither of us should quote it.
- **What the 70 are worth:** no one has measured it yet. This season's episodes will produce Brightfold's own attendee-to-opportunity rate, which replaces the 38%.
- **"They gave a work email, so they expect a call":** registration alone doesn't create that expectation. The form's notice and each country's rules do. Calls after a technical session also push developers to stop registering. Pushing all 70 would mostly create leads that never asked for contact.
- **Form problems:**
  - The marketing box is **ticked by default**. A pre-ticked box is not valid consent. Count every existing registrant as "box not ticked".
  - About a third of registrants are in Germany and France. Germany requires prior express consent for marketing email, B2B included, and B2B phone calls need at least presumed consent. France allows B2B prospecting only when the message relates to the recipient's profession, they were informed at collection, and objecting is simple.
  - The form collects phone, company size, job title and use case. Each field needs a stated use.

## 2. The handoff rule

Route on behaviour, not on attendance alone.

| Tier | Signal | Route | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A – hand-raise** | Asked for a demo, pricing or contact. Clicked a pricing or demo call to action. | SDR queue, with the episode, the question asked and the link clicked. | Within 1 business day |
| **B – engaged, open account** | Engaged attendee or registered replay viewer at an account with an open opportunity. | Account owner gets a note. No cold call or sequence. | Next working day |
| **C – engaged** | Engaged attendee who ticked the **unticked** marketing box. | Product activation nurture, owned by DevRel or marketing. | Episode follow-up |
| **D – registered** | Everyone else, including no-shows. | The replay email they registered for. | Episode follow-up |

- **Engaged:** the top third of attendees by share of session watched, questions asked and resources clicked (baseline). Set the thresholds from the first two episodes.
- **Never routed to SDR:** Tiers C and D, unless they later meet Tier A.
- **Anonymous replay viewers:** they enter no tier. Count them through tagged links only.
- **Past registrants:** route them only under the notice they saw. Registrants never told that sales may contact them get the replay and the new notice.
- **Illustrative volume for 70 attendees** (the skill's worked example scaled, not a forecast): roughly 3–5 Tier A, 5–8 Tier B, about 23 Tier C eligible for nurture, the rest Tier D. SDR receives named hand-raises with context, not 70 cold leads. Real counts come from the first two episodes.

**Acceptance and review**
- Sales accepts at least 50% of Tier A handoffs (baseline, adjustable by agreement). DevRel reads acceptance back after every episode.
- If acceptance stays under the bar for a season, Tier A tightens.
- If acceptance is at or above the bar and sales wants more volume, the parties may add signals to Tier A by written amendment. Any added signal must show intent and fit the notice.

## 3. Form and consent requirements

These are conditions of the rule, to be live before the next episode:

1. **Marketing box:** unticked, separate from registration, shown to every registrant. It's the only basis valid in every EU state, so no country lookup is needed.
2. **Notice on the form:** who hosts. That the session is recorded and where the recording is published. That attendance, watch time, questions and clicks are recorded and used to decide follow-up. That Brightfold's sales team may contact them about the topic. How to opt out. How long data is kept. A link to the privacy notice.
3. **Fields:** name, work email and at most one qualifying field (job title or company size).
   - Drop phone. If sales wants it, make it optional with its own stated use. Phone calls to Germany still need consent.
   - Keep work email only because Tier B matches accounts on it.
4. **Legitimate-interest assessment** for sales calls and account notes based on webinar behaviour. Recorded before the first Tier A or B handoff: `<link, date>`.
5. **Opt-out:** honoured across sales and marketing tools within 5 business days (baseline).
6. **Retention:** registrant and engagement data deleted 12 months after the season ends (baseline).
7. **Same legal entity:** the SDR team must sit in the same legal entity as the host. A sales team in another group company counts as a disclosure and needs its own review.
8. **Co-hosts and sponsors:** no list goes to a partner unless the partner is named on the form and has its own unticked box. That needs a signed joint arrangement.

## 4. Season measures

- **Reported every episode:** live attendance, 30-day total reach, returning-attendee share, Tier A handoffs and acceptance.
- **Job metric:** attendee-to-opportunity rate under Brightfold's own opportunity definition. The season-one median becomes the target.
- **Never reported alone:** registrations.
