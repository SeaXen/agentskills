## 2. Why the 180% cannot go in the deck

- **The definition changed between your two months.** From 24 August 2026, all formats count a view "the moment a video starts to play" [YouTube].
  - July sits before the change and September sits after it.
  - Any lift in your report mixes real growth with a looser counting rule, and I cannot split the two from the report.
- **A pre-change comparison has a second problem.** If your Shorts-heavy views were counted under the engaged-view rule before the change, the gap grows. Shorts already counted every start or replay from 31 March 2025 [YouTube], so check which count your report used.
- **Views are not the outcome even under a stable definition.** Subscribers and public views are "not outcomes" in this framework. Subscribers are not viewers [YouTube].
- **A CMO who cites 180% invites the obvious challenge.** Someone asks how many signups came from it, and you have no answer yet. That hurts Marketing more than a smaller, defensible claim does.

**What to put in the deck instead:**

1. Watch time, which is the safe series across the redefinition [YouTube]. Compare July and September on watch time.
2. YouTube-search watch time on topics Lumenkey customers search for.
3. Signups from tagged links, plus signups that name YouTube in a "how did you hear" field.
4. If signups carry no source today, say plainly that the channel cannot be judged until they do. Add the source field first.

**Suggested wording for the CMO:** "Views were redefined in August, so September vs July is not like-for-like. Watch time is up [X%]. ## 3. Library diagnosis (you have a channel and a September report)

Run this before any new upload. The first step takes about an hour in Studio.

1. **Ownership.** Check whether the channel lives on someone's personal Google account or on a Brand Account. Personal accounts leave with the person. Roles cannot transfer ownership [YouTube].
   - If it is personal, move it to a Brand Account first and add a company-administered Google account as a second owner.
   - Allow three weeks. The new owner needs 7 days as owner before becoming primary owner [YouTube].
2. **Traffic sources.** Little YouTube-search watch time with high External and Notifications means the channel serves people who already know Lumenkey, not discovery.
3. **Top videos.** Rank the top five by watch time. Add a tagged link to each description and pinned comment now. Both need advanced features (phone verification plus channel history, or ID verification, which "isn't available to all creators" [YouTube]).
4. **Weak videos.** Leave them up. One weak video does not hurt the channel [YouTube].
5. **Baselines.** Capture the monthly audience split (new, casual, regular), watch time and search share before the first new upload.

- **Backup.** Name someone besides the main presenter who ships at least one video a quarter. The format and account must not depend on one person.
- **Cadence formula.** `(bench hours per week × 4) ÷ team hours per finished video, excluding editing`, rounded down, capped by editor capacity. Subtract about an hour a week for comment replies.

## 7. Shorts rule

- Cut Shorts from a long-form, never the reverse.
- Each Short's Related video points at the long-form it came from, and that long-form carries the signup link.
- **Short bridge.** Last line spoken and on screen: "Full fix, with the rollback case, is the [topic] video. Tap the related video link on this Short."
- **Don't speak the URL on screen as a fix.** The external links policy covers spoken and on-screen URLs too [YouTube].
- **Judge Shorts on the long-form views they send.** Use Studio's traffic sources, or the long-form's tagged link. Never judge them on public views.

## 8. Engagement recipe: dated cadence

Dates assume you start today, 2026-10-31. Shift them if the ownership move comes first.

1. **Weeks -4 to 0 (to about 2026-11-28):**
   - Ownership move, roles, phone verification, ID verification request.
   - Profile links (up to 14) and a channel trailer.
   - Comment moderation: held-for-review, blocked words, a named moderator.
   - **Build 6-10 Help videos before announcing anything.** If cadence allows fewer, build four weeks' worth.
2. **Weeks 1-12:**
   - Publish at the derived cadence.
   - Reply to every comment in the first 48 hours.
   - Post a poll only when you have a real question, and vary the wording.
3. **From week 8:** A/B test titles and thumbnails on the oldest videos first. Long-form only, up to 3 variants, up to 2 weeks [YouTube]. Winner is picked by watch time share, not click-through rate.
4. **From week 12:** invite creators who already cover your category, found in Studio's "Channels your audience watches", to a Collaboration. Never comment-promote on their channels.
5. **Quarterly:** prune pillars by watch time per production hour. Refresh titles on decaying Help videos.

**Binding limits for this plan:**

- Shorts links are dead text, and there is one Related video link per Short [YouTube].
- Pinned comments, clickable long-form description links, chapters and A/B tests need advanced features. Plan the launch so it works without them.
- Uploads stop at 15 minutes until phone verification [YouTube].
- 3 spam strikes in 90 days can terminate the channel [YouTube].

**Templates (long-form):**

Pinned comment:

```
Code and steps: [tagged link]. Stuck on [the step people ask about most]? Reply with your [error / version] and we answer here.
```

Tagged link: `lumenkey.dev/start?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=[video-slug]`. Check the parameter names against whatever your analytics already uses. The same link goes in the top three description lines.
