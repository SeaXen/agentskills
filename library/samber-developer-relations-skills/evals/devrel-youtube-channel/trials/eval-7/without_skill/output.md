## Core call

- Channel is a library, not a launch engine. New channel has 0 subscribers, so YouTube will not drive launch day. Email, event audience, X, LinkedIn, HN, and partners drive it.
- YouTube pays off after launch. Search-driven problem/solution videos compound for months.
- Bottleneck is the advocate. They own docs and the demo. Protect them.
- Reuse the launch demo as the first video. Zero extra filming.

## Launch (6 weeks)

Ship 4 assets, no more:

1. **Launch event as YouTube Live.** Attendees become first subscribers. Recording becomes the chaptered replay.
2. **Clean demo, 8-12 min.** Re-record after the live one; the live one has errors. Title is a problem, not a feature: "Find a cold-start regression in edge functions".
3. **Quickstart, 3-5 min.** Install to first trace. Pair with the docs quickstart.
4. **Brightloom v2 overview, 2-3 min.** For decision makers. Product screens plus voiceover. No facecam needed.

Plus 3-5 Shorts cut from the demo and event.

## After launch

Cadence the advocate can sustain: **2 long videos per month, capped at about 4 hours per week for YouTube.** Editor does the rest. Shorts are cut from long videos, never separate shoots.

Five content lanes, ranked:

1. **Problem/solution, search-led.** "Why is my edge function slow", "debug cold starts", "trace across edge regions". Check autocomplete before each title. Main growth lane.
2. **Integration tutorials.** One per edge platform your users run.
3. **Monthly "what's new", 3 min.** Cheap. Reuses changelog.
4. **Conference and customer talk recordings.** Only talks that already exist.
5. **Live Q&A, quarterly.** Not weekly.

Skip for now: daily Shorts, weekly streams, a branded series, paid promotion.

## Measure

- Launch: live attendees, replay watch time, UTM clicks from video descriptions to signup, quickstart completions.
- After: search-sourced views, signups attributed by UTM, watch time per lane.
- Set targets after 8 weeks of data. I have no baseline, so any number now is a guess. Judge the channel at 6 months, not week 3. Subscriber count is vanity here.

## Risks

- ⚠️ Advocate overload. Docs and demo slip. Fix: editor hired, hard 4h/week cap, cut lane 4-5 first.
- ⚠️ Live demo fails on stream. Fix: rehearsal in week 4, clean re-record is the durable asset.
- ⚠️ Launch slips. Fix: assets 1-3 are independent. Ship the quickstart even if the overview slips.
