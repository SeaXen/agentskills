## Leaderboard rules (scoring)

- **Activated invite** = invitee stays at least 14 days and posts at least 1 message outside `#introductions`.
- **Eligibility floor:** at least 3 activated invites in the month to win. Otherwise month 1 pays out 10 prizes for 10 single invites.
- **Exclusions:** staff, bots, and accounts under 30 days old, plus any invitee who shares a server join pattern with their inviter (same-minute joins).
- **Ban on:** DM invite spam and posting invite links in other servers. Violation means disqualification.
- **Prizes:** top 10 get a hoodie and $50 cloud credits. If fewer than 10 people clear the floor, fewer people win.
- **Tooling:** an invite-tracking bot (Invite Tracker or similar). Check what it stores. It holds member data, so keep it out of anything public.
- **Budget estimate per month:** up to $500 in credits (internal cost is lower). Hoodies are roughly $30–50 each plus shipping, so about $300–500. Worst case is about $1,000 per month, or $3,000 over the quarter. Confirm that figure with whoever owns the budget.
- **Logistics:** collect shipping addresses by DM only after a winner is announced, and decide upfront whether you ship worldwide.

## Go/no-go gates

- **Dec 14:** with the Discovery size threshold at about 1,000, you need roughly +360 members. At the pace you've shown (about 640 in two months, so about 75 per week at best, and slowing), that is about 5–8 weeks. If you're under about 800 on Dec 14, **Jan 31 is unlikely**. Move the target to Mar 31 instead of buying volume.
- **Jan 11:** if retention or activity is red on the checklist, hold the Discovery application. A rejected application can block you for a while.
- **Mar 30:** if activated invitees retain worse than organic joins, stop the program.

## Risks

- Reward gaming. Mitigation: the floor, the 14-day rule, and a manual audit of winners.
- Discovery rejection if activity looks bought.
- Verified may not be available to an open-source SDK. Plan B: stay unverified and use the website links for credibility.
- The holiday period (Dec 19 – Jan 2) is quiet, so a Jan 4 launch lands on a slow start. If January starts weak, extend month 1 to Feb 7.
