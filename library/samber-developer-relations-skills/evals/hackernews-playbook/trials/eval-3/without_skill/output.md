# Rescuing a 3-point Show HN

**Don't delete and repost tonight. Don't have your cofounder upvote or vouch.** Do these instead, in order.

## Do now (about 15 min)

1. **Check your first comment.** If you haven't posted a top-level "Hey HN, I'm X, I built this because…" comment, post one now. Cover the backstory, why Rust, and what's technically interesting (the architecture, how it differs from yazi, ranger and nnn). It's the biggest lever you control.
2. **Answer the one comment** fast and in depth, and keep checking back for more.
3. **Check the "Show HN" requirements.** A reader must be able to try it with no signup. That means a one-line install (`cargo install`, brew, or a prebuilt binary), a GIF or asciinema recording at the top of the README, and no waitlist.
4. **Fix the title if it's vague.** Use `Show HN: <Name> – <what it does, concretely>`. You can edit it for a limited time after posting. If it's still editable, state the differentiator (for example, "async previews", "plugin API in Rust") instead of "a terminal file manager".

## Email the moderators (about 5 min)

Write to **hn@ycombinator.com**. Give the link, a two-line description, and say you think it was under-seen. Ask politely whether it can go into the second-chance pool. This is the sanctioned way to get a second shot. It costs nothing, and it doesn't burn your chance to repost.

## Why not delete and repost

- HN lets you delete only within a short window, and I believe only while the post has no comments. I haven't verified the current rules. Yours has 1 comment, so the option may already be gone.
- Reposting the same project from the same account soon after can read as gaming. Moderators may also merge or kill the duplicate.
- Tonight's slot is likely worse, not better. Midnight UTC is Friday 5pm PT and 8pm ET, when the US audience is leaving. Most guidance favours about 6–9am PT on weekdays.
- Some people pick weekends for less competition, but traffic is lower too. I have no data showing 00:00 UTC beats your original slot.

## The cofounder question

- **Vouching** only applies to dead or flagged items. It does nothing for a live post.
- **Upvoting** from a "karma-rich old account" does nothing special, because votes aren't weighted by karma. It also risks a vote-ring penalty if the voting patterns look coordinated: same network, the same minutes, or a link sent to him just so he'd vote.
- ❌ **Don't ask anyone to upvote**, and don't put the HN link in a Slack, Discord or X message with "go upvote". HN detects this. The penalty can be a killed post or a banned account, and that's much worse than 3 points.
- ✅ **What he can do:** read it and comment if he has something real to say. If he happens to upvote it unprompted, that's fine. Don't make it a plan.

## If the mod email fails: repost properly

- Wait at least 24 hours, ideally a few days.
- Retitle it, and fix whatever the checklist above turned up (demo GIF, install path, first comment).
- Post once, on a weekday morning PT, and stay online for 2–3 hours.
- Expect "once or twice" to be the limit before it looks like spam.

## Meanwhile, get real traffic

HN is one channel. Post the project link, not the HN link, where terminal-tool users already gather: r/rust, r/commandline, lobste.rs, This Week in Rust (submit to the newsletter), and the Rust Discord and Mastodon. Traffic that arrives by your own channels doesn't count as vote solicitation.
