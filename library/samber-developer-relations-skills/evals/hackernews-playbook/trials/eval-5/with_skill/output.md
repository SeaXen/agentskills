## Account readiness

- A Northbeam employee, not the agency, owns the account and posts under their own username.
- That person watches replies for the whole month. Replying is a condition of the post [HN].
- I don't have the account's age or history. The skill gives no threshold for hiring comments, so check it before the posting day.
- Staff must not upvote or vouch for the post, and the agency must not either. Solicitation can penalize the post, the account and the domain [HN].

## What I'd do instead

1. **Surface:** a single comment in the Who is hiring thread. That is the one hiring surface the skill supports.
2. **Timing:** the October thread is past its two-week comment window, so wait for November. The thread opens the first weekday of the month [HN]. That is **Monday 2026-11-02**, at about 11:00 US Eastern [practitioner], which is about 17:00 CET.
3. **Author:** one engineer or hiring manager at Northbeam writes the comment from scratch, in their own words. Imperfect English is fine, and a human proofreader is allowed. No LLM polish.
4. **Content the post must have** [HN]:
   - One post covering all four roles: 2 backend Go, 1 SRE, 1 frontend.
   - Location plus `REMOTE`, with the region (EU).
   - What Northbeam does, in plain words.
   - A reply promise you will keep.
   - A salary range, if you publish one.
5. **Honesty:** the post must match the real job. See `tech-employer-branding`.
6. **Roles:** the sentence about what each role works on should be specific. "Go backend" alone tells a reader nothing.
7. **Shape to copy:** `references/templates.md` has a Fernwood Labs example, a fictional feature-flag company that is close to yours. Treat it as a shape to rewrite, not text to adapt. Don't paste your details into it.
8. **During the month:** reply to every applicant. Remove or update the post if a role closes. Post only while you are actively hiring [HN].
9. **Next month:** if roles are still open, post again in the new thread, not before.

## If you want more reach than one comment

Hiring visibility doesn't come from extra posts. Two options fit the skill's ranking:

- **Comment in other threads** about feature flags, rollouts or Go, where an engineer knows the topic cold. Disclose "I work on Northbeam" when you mention the product, and only when it answers the question. This builds account history, and it is the skill's top-value activity.
- **Submit an engineering post** your team wrote, if it teaches something. Keep own submissions a minority of the account's activity.

Neither belongs to the agency, and neither should be framed as recruiting.
