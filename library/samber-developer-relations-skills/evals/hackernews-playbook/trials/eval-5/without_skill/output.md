**Short answer:** change the plan. The agency's HN tactics break HN's rules and will probably hurt Northbeam. I'm working from my knowledge of HN's rules, not from the live thread, so check the exact wording in the November thread before posting.

## What to change

### 1. A company employee posts, not the agency
- The "Who is hiring?" thread rules say, in substance: only post if you personally are part of the hiring company, with no recruiting firms or job boards, and one post per company.
- An agency account posting for Northbeam breaks that rule. Commenters check this, and a flagged post costs you the thread.
- Use an account owned by someone at Northbeam, ideally an engineering lead or the CTO. The person who answers replies and emails should be the one who posted.
- The agency can still draft the text and screen the inbound applicants.

### 2. One top-level comment, not four
- The convention is one comment per company that lists all the roles. Four separate comments look like spam and risk flags.
- Put all four roles in one comment, with a short line per role.
- Suggested shape (the pay range and email are placeholders to fill in):

```
Northbeam (https://northbeam.example) | Remote (EU) | Full-time
Northbeam is a feature-flag platform. [2 concrete sentences: scale, stack, what is hard.]

- Backend Engineer, Go (x2): [what they own, e.g. flag evaluation path, latency, consistency]
- Site Reliability Engineer (x1): [what they own, e.g. infra, SLOs, on-call model]
- Frontend Engineer (x1): [stack, what they own]

Remote within the EU: [state the time-zone overlap needed, and whether you hire as employee or contractor].
Pay: [range per role].
Apply: jobs@northbeam.example (a human reads it)
```

- State the pay range. Many EU hiring rules now require pay transparency, and HN readers expect it. Check the current rules for the countries you hire in.
- Be concrete about the work. HN readers respond to specifics, not "fast-growing and passionate".

### 3. Drop the "Northbeam is hiring" story
- HN has no way to post job ads as ordinary stories. Posts like that get flagged and killed, and the account and domain can be penalized.
- Paid job listings go through YC's own jobs section. That is the only sanctioned route for a front-page-style listing.
- Any attempt to push a hiring story with votes or flags falls under vote manipulation, which HN treats as a bannable offense.

### 4. Drop the weekly reposts
- Reposting the same story every week is the clearest spam pattern. It risks a domain-wide penalty that would also hit Northbeam's product posts later.
- The "Who is hiring?" thread is monthly. Post once per thread. If a role is still open next month, post again in that month's thread, ideally with refreshed text.

### 5. Time it right
- Today is 30 Oct, near the end of the October thread, where new comments get almost no views.
- Wait for the November thread. It is usually posted on the first weekday of the month, so check around Mon 2 Nov.
- Post early in that thread, ideally in the first hours.

### 6. Add tracking and a reply plan
- Use a dedicated apply address so you can tell HN applicants from other sources.
- Someone from the company should answer questions on the thread for the first day or two.
- Keep the reply tone plain and honest, with no recruiter-style language.

## Better ways to get visibility
- Post a "Show HN" or a technical post about something real. Examples: how Northbeam evaluates flags at low latency in Go, or an SRE postmortem. A good post makes engineers curious about the team.
  - Have an engineer submit it, not the agency.
  - Don't ask anyone to upvote it.
  - Don't tie it to the hiring pitch beyond a short note in a profile or on the site.
- The monthly "Who wants to be hired?" thread is where candidates post themselves. Read it and contact people directly, within the thread's rules.
- Keep the agency for sourcing outside HN: LinkedIn outreach, Go community channels, and EU remote job boards.

## Questions for the agency
- Which account will they post from, and will it be a Northbeam employee?
- Do they understand that the story and weekly reposts are off the table?
- Will they accept a single monthly comment that the Northbeam lead owns?

If they insist on the story and the weekly reposts, I'd decline that part and keep only the monthly comment.
