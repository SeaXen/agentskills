**What I changed**
- "the most fast" became "the fastest". English uses "-est" for short adjectives.
- "encoder 2x faster" became "a 2x faster encoder". English needs the article and the adjective before the noun.
- I added "and" before the last item in the list ("new hooks, and bug fixes"). This is optional, but it is standard.
