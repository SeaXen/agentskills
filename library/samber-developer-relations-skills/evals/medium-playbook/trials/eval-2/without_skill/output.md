## Revenue model

Medium pays per story from member reading time. Non-members and bounced reads earn nothing. The inputs below are my estimates for a new company account with few followers. They are not Medium data.

| Scenario | Per story, per month | 40 stories, per month | Year 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low (no publication boost, mostly non-members) | $0.50 | $20 | about $200 |
| Base | $3 | $120 | about $1,000 to $1,500 after ramp-up |
| High (publication distribution lands) | $15 | $600 | about $5,000 to $7,000 |

- **Base case:** each tutorial gets about 100 to 400 views a month, and about 10% of those are member reads. Earnings usually come from a few winners, so the median story earns almost nothing.
- **High case:** only a handful of stories do well in a big publication. Don't plan on it.

## Four risks that can take it to $0

1. **AI-drafted tutorials.** As I understand Medium's policy, AI-generated writing is not eligible for the Partner Program or wide distribution. AI-assisted writing needs disclosure and often gets network-only reach. Many publications reject it outright. Ask which of the 40 were AI-drafted and how heavily humans rewrote them. Account-level enforcement can also forfeit earnings for the whole account, not just one story.
2. **Company account.** The Partner Program pays an individual through Stripe. It may not accept a brand account, or the earnings may land with one person. Confirm with Medium and with Legal.
3. **Product tutorials.** Medium discourages primarily promotional content. Tutorials that end in a Kiln call to action may be flagged or get no distribution. Duplicates of content already free on your site may also be a problem.
4. **Eligibility claim.** I can't confirm "6 stories and 3 months, no paid membership." The older rule needed a paid membership plus a follower threshold. Read the current terms yourself.

## The cost you haven't priced

- **Lost funnel:** tutorials exist to feed the free tier and paid Teams signups. A paywall hides them from non-members, search and AI answers. Searches for "GitHub Actions caching tutorial" will no longer reach your content.
- **Break-even:** a $1,000 to $1,500 yearly forecast is erased by one lost paid team. Compare it with the Teams ACV (annual contract value).
- **Publication that only takes paywalled stories:** you give up control of distribution, and you pay with the funnel.

## Recommendation

1. **Run a 6-story pilot, not 40.** Pick human-written or heavily rewritten tutorials. Paywall them for 90 days.
2. **Track per story:** Partner Program earnings, referral clicks to Kiln, signups, and Teams conversions. Compare against 6 matched free tutorials.
3. **Present the CFO an influenced-pipeline number** (signups and Teams attributed to tutorials) next to the Medium earnings. That is the stronger "pays for itself" case.
4. **Check these first:** the AI policy, company account eligibility, and which tutorials were AI-drafted. Takes about 30 minutes.
