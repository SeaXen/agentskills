When a questionnaire explicitly asks "are you certified," answering "compatible" isn't diplomatic — it reads as a non-answer to anyone doing real due diligence, and HR analytics buyers doing security review on employee data are exactly the audience that reads carefully.

Three deals this quarter named it in a procurement questionnaire. That's counted, named demand — not "everyone probably wants this." The CTO's objection ("bureaucratic theatre") is a judgment about the spec's merit. That judgment is irrelevant to whether the mark gates deals. Those are different axes — he can be completely right about the spec and still be wrong about the business call, because procurement isn't buying his opinion of the spec, it's checking a box.
