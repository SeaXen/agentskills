One quick flag before the drafts: HN's ranking system is specifically tuned to detect a burst of upvotes/comments arriving in a short window from one source — internal pushes like this are exactly the pattern it looks for, even with zero bad intent. Keeping the ask soft (as you're already planning) and framed around genuine reactions rather than "go upvote" reduces that risk, but it won't eliminate it. Here are your drafts:
