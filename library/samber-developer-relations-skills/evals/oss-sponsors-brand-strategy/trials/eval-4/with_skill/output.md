Switching a GitHub Sponsors organization to invoiced billing **cancels every sponsorship currently on the account**. Your 23 maintainers each get an automated cancellation email. The good news: your timing is actually right. You have three weeks before the $150k program has to be live, and none of that money should move until invoiced billing is in place anyway. Doing the switch now, ahead of both the controller's deadline and the announcement, is the correct order — you're not backing into it late.
