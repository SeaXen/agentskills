## Six flags before you publish this

**⚠️ GitHub won't let you edit a live tier's price.** Once a sponsor is on the $50 tier, Sponsors blocks changing that tier's dollar amount out from under them. Practical path: create the new $250 tier fresh, retire (unpublish, don't delete) the old $50 one. Delete only once it has zero active sponsors.

**⚠️ Message your existing $50 sponsors before they notice on their own.** A short personal note — "raising this tier to $250 with X added, you're grandfathered at $50 until you choose to move" — turns a surprise into a loyalty moment. Costs you five minutes, protects the relationship.

**⚠️ Ship stickers once, not monthly.** A recurring $15/mo tier that mails stickers every cycle eats your margin and your evenings. Trigger the mailing on first payment only; state that explicitly in the perk description so nobody expects a monthly restock.

**$25 → $250 is a 10x jump with nothing between.** Someone priced out of $250 but wanting more than "priority answers" has no landing spot — that gap is where you lose upgrades, not gain them. Two options, your call:
- Leave it. A steep top tier reads as a deliberate anchor/prestige option (makes $25 look reasonable by contrast) and doesn't need to convert often to be worth having.
- Add a $75 or $100 bridge tier (e.g., "quarterly instead of monthly call," or "async priority support, no live call") to catch the middle.

**Reframe $250 for companies, not individuals.** Asking one person for $250/mo out of goodwill is a hard sell; asking a company that ships zodiac-rs in production for $250/mo of informal support is not. Rewrite the tier description to speak to a team lead with a budget line, not a solo dev — "for teams running zodiac-rs in production" beats "all that, plus a call."

**Cap "faster answers" and the 1:1 call before they scale against you.** At $180/mo total revenue this isn't urgent, but write the promise with headroom now (e.g., "priority within N business days," "one 30-min call/month") so a future traffic bump in sponsors doesn't turn into an unpaid part-time support job.
