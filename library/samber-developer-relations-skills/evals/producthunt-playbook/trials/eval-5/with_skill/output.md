# Product Hunt plan for Querybook AI, 3 hours a month between launches

Product Hunt will not send meaningful traffic between launches, and a Product of the Day win doesn't change that. Its audience is makers, founders and product people, and Hacker News sent roughly 3x or more of the traffic in published devtool comparisons [practitioner]. So spend the 3 hours building assets that compound: followers, reviews, forum threads and a good branded page. Don't aim for visits.

Labels: **[PH]** means Product Hunt's help center, launch guide or staff state it. **[practitioner]** means datasets and reverse-engineering. I did not re-read the live help center for this answer, so treat the rules as last published. The relaunch gap and posting flow have changed within the past year.

## Assumptions

You gave me one pass, so I assumed the following. Tell me which are wrong.

- Anyone can try Querybook AI today, with no waitlist.
- One maker with a personal account (real name, own photo, bio) does the Product Hunt work. The account is older than a week, and the company doesn't post.
- You want a compounding presence, not a deadline spike.
- Your last launch was in July, and you have no paid-ads budget.

## 1. Fit verdict

Querybook AI is a hosted and desktop app that people can try today, so Product Hunt is a legitimate channel for it. The July win means the page has followers, and you can start from the forum instead of waiting on a launch.

## 2. Account readiness (a 10-minute check, once)

- Post only from a personal account. Company accounts cannot post, vote or comment, and a domain-named username counts as a company [PH].
- Complete the profile. Incomplete profiles get reviewed and their activity limited [PH].
- Make sure the Product Page is claimed and every builder is listed as a maker.

## 3. Moves refused

- **Upvote requests:** don't ask the team, Slack, Discord or a newsletter to upvote or boost a thread. Asking or incentivizing votes drops rank or disqualifies the launch [PH]. Review requests are different. Asking real users to review is invited [PH].
- **Vote swaps, paid hunters, "top spot" services:** don't join them or pay for them [PH].
- **Relaunching the same build:** repeated submissions of the same product count as spam [PH]. A relaunch needs a significant update, covered below.
- **Ads:** I'm not recommending them. You have no stated budget, and Promoted Products exclude brand-new launches [PH]. They only make sense later, around a relaunch.
- **AI-written comments:** I won't write your comments or replies. Product Hunt's guideline asks for no LLMs, and a public Likely AI badge marks such comments [PH]. I give empty skeletons and review your drafts instead.

## 4. What to do with the 3 hours

These are ranked by value per hour for a product that has followers but no launch coming up [PH, practitioner].

| Rank | Surface | Monthly time | What it buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product forum thread (`/p/<slug>`) | 60 min | Every thread notifies all product followers, and titles are searchable [PH]. This is your only way to reach the July followers. |
| 2 | Reviews on the Product Page | 45 min | They compound and feed the quarterly Orbit Awards per category [PH]. |
| 3 | Comments on others' launches | 45 min | The maker byline carries Querybook AI. Self-promotion inside a comment is removed [PH]. |
| 4 | Shoutouts | 15 min | Credit on other launches' pages [PH]. |
| 5 | Relaunch gate check and metrics | 15 min | See sections 6 and 7. |

### Forum changelog (60 min)

Post once a month. Use the Self-Promotion topic for what you shipped. Link to your real release notes, since the `changelog-writing` skill covers writing them. Don't just drop links.

Title patterns from the templates, which you fill in yourself:

```
What we shipped in Querybook AI in <Month>: <headline feature>
How we cut <Querybook AI thing> from <X> to <Y>
```

Use a General-topic thread when you have a guide or a real question for analysts. Ask one specific question, such as which SQL dialect or warehouse people most need supported next. Thread replies are your best measure of whether this works. Answer every one yourself, in your own words.

Empty skeleton for the thread body (you fill the slots):

1. `<what shipped, in one plain sentence>`
2. `<why analysts asked for it, or where you hit the problem>`
3. `<what it does not do yet>`
4. `<how to try it today>`
5. `<one specific question for readers>`

### Reviews (45 min)

1. Pick 5 to 10 real, active users each month. Choose people with Product Hunt accounts, such as analysts who gave feedback or sent a support ticket.
2. Ask each one personally, by email or in-app, for an honest review. Never tie it to a discount, freebie or vote [PH].
3. Reply to each review in your own words, using the reply skeleton below.
4. Flag any false or competitor review, then send support the evidence [PH].

Reply skeleton:

1. `<the part that is right, or the need behind the question>`
2. `<the fact, trade-off or workaround that answers it>`
3. `<what you will change, or why not>`

### Comments on others' launches (45 min)

- Pick 4 to 6 launches a month from analyst, data, SQL and developer tooling. Try the product first.
- Write comments that add something: a concrete observation, a question, or a workaround. Skip generic "congrats" and mass commenting [PH].
- Don't mention Querybook AI in the comment text. The byline does that [PH].
- This also builds the track record you'll want if you relaunch.

### Shoutouts (15 min)

Give one a month to a tool in your own stack. Use this format: `<Tool> - <what it does in your stack, one concrete line>`.

## 5. Monthly schedule

| Week | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forum changelog thread, and replies the same week | 60 min |
| 2 | Review asks, and replies to new reviews | 45 min |
| 3 | Comments on others' launches | 45 min |
| 4 | Shoutout, relaunch gate check, metrics row | 30 min |

## 6. Relaunch gate (a decision test, not a to-do)

You said not to just relaunch, and the plan above respects that. The gate is a test you run each quarter:

- The 2-month gap since July has passed [PH].
- A relaunch needs a significant update. A new app, or a redesign with new functionality, counts. New UI or a pricing change does not [PH].
- If the update is borderline, email hello@producthunt.com before building the post [PH]. Use the "Relaunch request" template for that.
- Launch only when a hard date or a real new product (for example a major platform or mode) justifies it. Until then, the forum and reviews are the work.

## 7. Binding limits

| Rule | Kind |
|---|---|
| Personal accounts only; domain-named usernames count as companies | enforced [PH] |
| No vote requests, incentives, mass DMs or coordinated pushes | enforced by detection and moderators [PH] |
| No self-promotion inside comments | removed by moderators [PH] |
| Forum promotion only under the Self-Promotion topic; no link-dropping | removed by moderators [PH] |
| 2 months between launches per product, company or root domain, plus a significant update | enforced by review [PH] |
| AI-written comments allowed but discouraged | labelled Likely AI, not removed [PH] |

## 8. Metrics

Review these monthly in the week-4 slot.

- **Product followers gained** and the forum thread reply count per month.
- **Reviews received.**
- **Reply speed:** the share of comments and reviews answered within a day.
- **Referral signups or installs from Product Hunt**, against a pre-launch baseline. Published figures vary too much to serve as targets [practitioner].

The Product Page's outbound links are `ugc`, so its search value is the branded page, not link equity [practitioner]. Don't count it as an SEO channel.

## 9. What would change this plan

- You become active on Product Hunt, with a following. The forum can then lead.
- A real significant update ships. Run the relaunch gate.
- Product Hunt traffic stays near zero for two more quarters. Drop to roughly 1 hour a month: one forum thread and review replies. Move the other 2 hours to Hacker News or your docs, since Product Hunt isn't where analysts and developers look for tools.
