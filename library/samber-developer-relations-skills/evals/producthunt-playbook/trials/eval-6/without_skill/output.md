## Bigger problems

1. **Wrong goal.** Upvotes are a vanity metric for a dev tool. What counts is installs, activated users (first webhook relayed) and GitHub stars or newsletter signups. Pick one target and set it from your own baseline.
2. **Wrong channel mix.** Local-dev tooling gets better traffic from Hacker News (Show HN), Reddit, GitHub and dev newsletters than from Product Hunt. Product Hunt can still be one day in a wider launch.
3. **Capacity.** Three people can't hold a 24 h comment desk, onboard users and fix bugs at once. Plan shifts and a bug owner.
4. **Product must work first.** A relay tool fails visibly: tunnels drop, signatures mismatch. Do a dry run with 10 outside developers before launch day.
5. **Incentive.** An agency that promises upvote counts is selling the metric it can control. Ask what they do if the launch gets 500 upvotes and 0 activated users.

## Better plan

- **Maker-written replies.** Your own team answers in the first 3 h. The agency can draft FAQs and flag comments, but humans post.
- **Real network, no asks for votes.** Ask friends, users and your waitlist to try it and leave honest feedback. Product Hunt discourages direct vote requests.
- **Free-tier launch offer** for Product Hunt visitors, plus a 60-second demo GIF of a webhook arriving on localhost.
- **Staggered launches.** Product Hunt on day 1, Show HN a few days later (not the same day, so you can staff each one).
- **Success metric:** activated users and installs within 7 days, with a number you set from your waitlist size.
