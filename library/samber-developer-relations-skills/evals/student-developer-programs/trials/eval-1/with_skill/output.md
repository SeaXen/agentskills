## Program shapes

I recommend the first.

1. **Classroom-first.** The student offer ships first. A faculty kit follows for autumn 2027. It needs about 5 h/week. The first result is the offer's early read in early 2027, and the kit's first result lands in autumn 2027.
2. **Campus-network-first.** This is the CEO's version: champions at many campuses. It needs far more than 5 h/week, shows slowly (about a term), and champions are the least durable component because their leaders graduate.
3. **Talent-first.** The gate removes it for now (no headcount).

I recommend classroom-first with a small champion pilot because you asked for ambassadors by name. Under the adoption job the pilot sits third in the order, so it is limited to campuses that already show demand.

## Components

Ranked for adoption:

1. **Student offer with graduation bridge.** Kept. It is the fastest, cheapest, and most durable component.
2. **Faculty classroom kit.** Pending. It waits on the free-path fact. A spring-2027 pitch is also too late for most materials deadlines, so the earliest realistic term is autumn 2027.

4. **Internship funnel.** Deleted for now. The gate shows no headcount, and the plan promises no hiring.

## Component rules applied

**Student offer**

- Give what the free tier lacks, such as more CI minutes or Pro features. Cap usage credits per student. Never issue resellable keys or vouchers.
- Verify by documents or an existing verified-student service. A GitHub Education-style verification is the cheapest control to piggyback. Audience is post-secondary students; anyone under 18 goes through a separate minors design.
- Expire at the verified end date and re-verify yearly. Before expiry, ask the student to add a personal or work email.
- **Watch for farming.** Free CI runners attract mass accounts and unrelated workloads such as crypto mining. Revoke on abuse.
- Bridge: a discount for a fixed window after graduation. The level is **blocked** until the pricing owner confirms it. JetBrains' 40% for two years is a reference point, not a target.
- Faculty and staff holding `.edu` or `.ac.uk` addresses are not students. Route them to the kit when it exists.

**Campus champions (pilot)**

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Age floor | 18+. |
| Time left | More than a year before graduating. |
| Structure | Two co-leads from different years per campus, a written handover before the last term, alumni status after. |
| Intake | Selective cohort with training (the GitHub Campus Experts shape), because you have few hours and few campuses. |
| Milestones | Reward what the student learns and ships. Never reward reach or signup counts, because those are the banned quota renamed. |

| Visa | F-1 and any non-US students: the role stays a student-led club activity with access perks. Ask each school's international office. Check UK rules with the school too. |

| Perks | Credits, training, a reference letter. A certification voucher only if you run a certification. A guaranteed intern interview only once headcount exists. |

## Metrics and kill rules

**Leading indicator (adoption):** share of verified students still active 6 and 12 months after their benefit ends. Whoever graduates in June 2027 gives the first 6-month reading around December 2027. The first target is self-set: above the 6-month activity of other free signups.

**Long-term outcome (hypothesis):** graduates bringing Pipewright into employers. Label it a hypothesis. No published source measures the lag.

**Until the 6-month reading exists,** report first-month activity of verified students, labelled **early read**.

**Activity log (not results):** campuses, students verified, events held, champions enrolled.

**Kill rules**

- Bridge retention no better than other free signups after two cohorts → stop spending above the offer.
- A campus without a named successor at term end → retire it to alumni status.
- Kit: no course re-adopts it for a second term → stop authoring new modules.
