## Champions rules

- **Age floor:** 18 or older.
- **Time left:** more than a year before graduation. Leaders in their final year can be alumni advisers only.
- **Structure:** two co-leads from different years per campus, and a written handover before the last term.
- **Pay rule:** no cash, no stipend, no per-signup or per-event payment. No quotas or assigned tasks. Students choose their own activities, such as a talk, a club session, or a doc fix they pick.
- **Perks:** access and learning only. Credits, training, a non-transferable certification voucher, and a reference letter. No promised interview or hire while headcount fails.
- **Visa rule:** the 3 F-1 students join only after their DSO clears the role. Ask the DSO about credits or swag too, since they may count as material value.
- **Public docs:** a student may contribute to public docs on their own initiative. Tracewell must not assign it, track it per person, or review it on a schedule.
- **Support tickets:** keep them off the student role. They involve customer data and are assigned work.
- **Club naming:** check the university's student-organization rules before branding any club.
