# Student program renewal case: Flagstaff

## Read this first: the claim as written won't survive the CFO

"Students become enterprise buyers in 5-10 years, so this is pipeline" has three problems:

1. **The lag exceeds the budget cycle.** A 5-10 year payback can't be checked at next year's renewal. A CFO will read it as unfalsifiable.
2. **The buyer is not the student.** Enterprise feature-flag purchases are made by engineering leaders and platform teams, usually 10-15+ years into a career. A new graduate is rarely the buyer. They are an *early adopter or champion* inside a team that buys.
3. **Your four numbers are all outputs.** Universities, registrations, events, and hoodies show what you spent effort on. None shows that a student used the product.

I don't have your retention, activation, or alumni data, so I have not invented any conversion statistic. Pull the numbers marked **[pull]** below before the meeting.

The stronger case is the one the CFO can test within 12 months:

> We are buying cheap, early product usage and a hiring and champion channel. We will prove it with leading indicators this year and track the long-dated outcome as an option, not a forecast.

## Speaker notes: likely CFO questions

**"Why not just call it pipeline?"**
Pipeline means opportunities with a named buyer. Students aren't that, and claiming it would be easy to disprove. We call it long-dated sourcing and keep it out of this year's return.

**"What do we get this year?"**
- Activated student users, with cost per activated user.
- Graduates and interns appearing at companies in our ICP, with a Flagstaff account moving from a student email to a work email.
- Optionally, a count of leads or opportunities where a former student plan user is a named contact, tracked as influence and not as sourced revenue.

**"What does the free plan cost us?"**
State COGS per student account **[pull]**. If it is near zero for inactive accounts, the real cost is the events and merch, and that can be cut by activation.

**"Why 41 universities?"**
Don't defend the count. Show activation by university **[pull]** and propose concentrating on the top performers. Cutting a long tail is a more credible story than defending breadth.

**"Why hoodies?"**
They are a cost line and not a result. Next year, gate merch on an action (see below) so every hoodie is tied to a product event.

## Next year's metrics

Report these in tiers. The CFO should judge the program on tiers 2-4. Tier 1 is context.

### Tier 1: reach (report, don't justify)
| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Universities active | Schools with ≥1 activated student, not schools with an event |
| Events | Count, with cost per event |
| Registrations | Student-plan signups |

### Tier 2: activation (the main target for this year)
| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Activation rate** | % of registrants who create a flag *and* evaluate it from an SDK within 14 days | Set after the **[pull]** baseline. Aim to beat it by a margin you commit to now |
| **Cost per activated student** | Total program spend ÷ activated students | Fix a ceiling, e.g. 15% below last year's figure |
| **Activation by source** | Event vs. course vs. open-source vs. organic | Shift spend toward the highest-activating source |

### Tier 3: retention and depth
| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **90-day retention** | % of activated students with flag evaluations in days 61-90 | Baseline **[pull]**, then set |
| **Multi-flag or multi-environment use** | Students using ≥2 environments or ≥5 flags | Directional proxy for "real project" use |
| **Team projects** | Student accounts inviting a collaborator | Shows peer spread |

### Tier 4: bridge to buying (the evidence for the long-dated bet)
| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Student-to-work transitions** | Student-plan emails matched to a later work-domain signup, via the same person or SSO or a user-provided graduation flow | Count only. Set a baseline in year one |
| **Alumni at ICP companies** | Transitions where the employer matches your ICP list | Count, plus list for sales |
| **Influenced opportunities** | Opportunities where a contact was previously a student-plan user. Use a stated lookback and don't retrofit attribution after the fact | Report separately. Never present as sourced pipeline |

### Merch rule
Send a hoodie only after an activation event, such as the first flag evaluation. Report cost per hoodie claimed and the share of recipients who are still active at 90 days.
