1. **Internship funnel: held, not committed.** The gate fails on headcount.
   - Rules when it opens: paid roles only, opened with the hiring manager before anything is promised.

   - Source candidates from consenting program participants.
   - Below about ten program-sourced interns a cycle, report counts and read them case by case. Rates and kill rules start above that.
2. **Campus champions, Lyon pilot: kept.** Your club tie moves it up one rung.
   - **Eligibility:** leaders must be 18 or older with more than a year left before graduating. Her stage means she is probably in her final year, and she would likely fail this rule. Check the classmates too. If none of the three qualifies, find a younger co-lead from the club.

   - **Perks:** access and learning only, such as training, a reference letter, and a guaranteed interview where headcount exists. No pay, no quotas, no assigned company work.
   - **The club:** it belongs to the university. Check INSA's student-organization rules on names, sponsors and money before branding it.
3. **Faculty kit: deleted** for this job.
4. **Student offer: deleted** for this job.
