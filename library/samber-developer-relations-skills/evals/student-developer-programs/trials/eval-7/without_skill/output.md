# Shieldpath internship pipeline: the unpaid 6-month plan can't go ahead

⚠️ **An unpaid 6-month stage is illegal in France.** Any stage longer than 2 consecutive months (more than 44 days or 308 hours on site) must carry a legal minimum *gratification*. The CFO's constraint can't be met as stated. This is the figure and the legal basis to bring to the CFO.

## 1. The legal floor

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Threshold | Stage over 2 months: gratification is mandatory. At 2 months or less it is optional. |
| Minimum rate | 15% of the hourly social security ceiling. This was €4.35/h in 2025 and should be about **€4.50/h in 2026**. ℹ️ Confirm the current figure on urssaf.fr before quoting it. |
| 6 months, full time | About €690/month, so **about €4,100 total**. |
| Social charges | Exempt up to that floor. Employer cost is roughly the gratification itself. |
| Other duties | Reimburse 50% of the TCL transport pass. Give the same canteen or meal-voucher access as employees. |
| Risk of non-compliance | URSSAF reclassification as a salaried job, back pay, social charges and fines. INSA may also refuse to sign the convention. |

The ceiling and rate are set by decree and move each January.

## 2. Role scoping: adjust it even once it is paid

A stage must be educational. It cannot fill a permanent post.

- ❌ "Run the developer support inbox" is an operational job. Owning it alone looks like an unpaid hire.
- ✅ Make support **shadowed and tutored** and put the stage's main output in the SDK docs:
  1. Months 1–2: read-only support triage with a tutor. She drafts replies and a senior engineer sends them.
  2. Months 2–6: write SDK docs, and turn recurring support questions into docs and troubleshooting pages.
  3. Months 4–6: take first-line replies on low-risk tickets with sampling review.
- 🔐 As an API security vendor, apply least privilege. She gets a scoped helpdesk seat with no production or customer-secret access. She signs an NDA, and the convention includes a confidentiality clause.
- Name one **tutor** (maximum 3 interns per tutor). Hold a weekly 1:1 and write learning objectives into the convention.

## 3. Capacity check

For a headcount of 25, the cap is 15%, rounded down, so **3 interns at the same time**. Her spring stage and the two summer stages fit if they don't all overlap on one tutor. Record every intern in the internship register (*registre des stagiaires*).

## 4. The two summer placements

| Option | Gratification | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 2 months | Optional | Legal unpaid. Scope as a project with a deliverable, such as a docs section or a demo app. |
| Over 2 months | Mandatory | Same floor as above. |

My recommendation is to pay all three at the floor anyway. A paid or capped-scope offer is fairer and keeps the pipeline defensible. Don't pay the campus-club students less than the spring student for the same length, because a gap reads as an unfair offer.

## 5. Pipeline setup

1. **Budget line.** One intern for 6 months costs about €4,100. Two summer interns of 2–3 months cost about €1,400–2,100 each. Total ask: **about €7,000–8,500 per year**.
2. **Convention de stage.** Use INSA's template. It records dates, hours, gratification, tutor, missions and confidentiality. Sign before day 1.
3. **Intern spec, one page per role.** Include the mission, learning objectives, tutor, tools and access level, and deliverables.
4. **Selection.** Run the same short process for all three: CV, a 30-minute technical chat, and a small docs or support exercise. Don't skip it because they come from the campus club. Document the criteria.
5. **Onboarding checklist.** Cover accounts with least privilege, NDA, security basics, the docs style guide, a first-week plan and a 30/60/90-day review.
6. **Repeatable cycle.** Publish the offer around October–November for spring and January–February for summer. Contact INSA's career office, and keep a tracker with candidate, school, dates, tutor and gratification.
7. **Convert or end well.** Hold a final review, give a reference letter, and decide on a job offer or an *alternance* in the last month.
