## Before this goes to Thursday's board

Two of the four original bullets are weak under scrutiny, and I couldn't verify them live this session (no web access here) — flagging rather than guessing:

- **"Gartner: 69% turnover cut"** — this exact figure circulates everywhere in HR content but rarely traces to a locatable Gartner report. I swapped in **28%**, which is the more commonly and traceably cited figure from Gartner's CEB/Corporate Leadership Council employer-brand research. Have your team pull the actual citation before printing either number — a board member who subscribes to Gartner may ask.
- **"72% check blog and GitHub"** — plausible, but this number is often confused with a different, real LinkedIn stat ("72% of talent leaders say employer brand significantly impacts hiring") which is about *recruiters' opinions*, not *developer behavior*. Confirm which stat you're actually citing before it goes up.
- **Vendor 55% cost-per-hire** — keep it, but name the vendor and label it a case study on the slide itself. An unattributed "55%" reads as a plant; a named case study reads as evidence.
- The two additions (Glassdoor 86%, LinkedIn "2x faster") are well-established, frequently-published figures, but pull the current-year report to confirm the exact number before it's in the deck — these get refreshed annually and the digit drifts a few points year to year.

One unsourced stat on this slide is a rounding error. Two is a pattern a sharp board member will notice — and it undercuts the two stats that *are* solid. Worth the 20 minutes to nail down citations before Thursday.
