## Diagnosis first

Your funnel data already tells you where the problem is: 81% offer-accept means candidates who reach an offer love what they hear. The problem is entirely upstream — nobody qualified is entering the pipe. Five months, three Principal Engineer reqs, near-zero qualified applicants is not a conversion problem. It's a *reach* problem.

The original $60k plan is built for the wrong bottleneck:

- **$35k job boards/promoted LinkedIn** — reaches active job seekers. Principal-level engineers in fintech infra are rarely active job seekers; they're recruited out of their current role, not browsing listings.
- **$15k careers-page redesign** — improves conversion once someone lands on the page. You have no senior traffic to convert. Optimizing a page nobody with the right profile visits is solving stage 3 when you're stuck at stage 1.
- **$10k agency to write role ads** — better copy doesn't fix channel mismatch. A beautifully written job ad on a channel your candidates don't use still gets zero qualified views.

All three line items assume the constraint is *quality of the ask*. Five months of silence says the constraint is *who sees the ask at all*. Reallocate toward finding and reaching passive candidates directly, not toward polishing a listing.

**Total: $60k.** Nothing goes to careers-page redesign or ad-copywriting this cycle — cut both, they don't move the constraint you actually have.

## Two things to check before spending, at zero cost

1. **Comp band.** Zero qualified flow for five months can also mean the Principal comp band is below market for your metro/remote policy, and word has already gotten around via recruiters and levels-style comp sites. No amount of sourcing spend fixes an uncompetitive number — verify this in parallel, this week, before you commit the $30k search-firm line.
2. **The req itself.** A role open five months with no qualified applicants sometimes has a leveling problem — requirements written for a mythical candidate (10+ years in 4 niche technologies simultaneously) that filters out realistic Principal-level hires before a human ever sees the profile. Have whoever wrote the JD sanity-check it against your last 2-3 actual Principal hires' real backgrounds, not an idealized one.

## Sequencing

Move this week: launch the referral bonus (internal announcement, zero lead time) and get search-firm quotes out. Both can be live inside 5-7 days. The sourcer/LinkedIn seat is the next fastest to stand up (1-2 weeks to onboard). The narrowed job-board spend is a background constant, not something to sequence.

Skip the careers-page redesign entirely for this cycle — if you still have budget appetite for it next year once the funnel is actually producing senior traffic worth converting, revisit it then.
