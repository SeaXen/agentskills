## Add this to job postings and the interview loop (not the hero page)

Same honesty, more specific, where candidates expect detail:

- On-call: SCADA connector rotation, ~2 pages/month, rotation size and comp stated per role.
- Leveling: no formal ladder or promo committee yet — say what exists today (title, scope, comp band), not what's planned unless a date is attached.
- Pay: state the actual band and name the 10-15% Berlin gap if a candidate asks directly. Getting caught softening it costs more than the gap itself.

## Guardrails — don't let marketing drift past this

Never claim "flexible/low-key on-call," "clear career growth," or "competitive comp" — all three get contradicted by a candidate's own interview loop or by Kununu/Glassdoor within one review cycle, and a caught lie costs more than the honest version. "Ownership" and the simulator claim are real — the only two claims safe to lean on hard.
