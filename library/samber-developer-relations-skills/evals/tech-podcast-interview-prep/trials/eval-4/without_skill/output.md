Sure — so I've been in distributed systems for about fourteen years now, which still catches me off guard a little every time I say it out loud. I started at a telco, and that's actually a great place to learn distributed systems the hard way: you're moving billions of tiny events across a network that's not allowed to go down, and nobody congratulates you for uptime — they only notice the moment it breaks. From there I spent three years at a payments startup, which was a different flavor of the same pressure, except now there's real money attached to every single request, and a much smaller team standing behind it. These days I lead the storage team at Halcyon Grid, so I ended up one layer further down — the part underneath everything else, the part people assume just works until it doesn't. Before any of that I did a master's in CS at Delft, and somewhere along the way I started getting out and talking about this stuff at conferences, which is one of my favorite parts of the job — this one included.
