## Why press won't carry 2,000 alone

Rough order-of-magnitude, per channel bucket, launch window:

| Channel | Realistic signups | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tier-1 funding press (TechCrunch, The Information, Axios) | 10–100 | Readers are investors/journalists/founders, not your ICP. High brand value, low direct conversion. |
| Dev/infra trade press (The New Stack, InfoQ) | 50–300 | Smaller reach, real ICP overlap. |
| Postgres-specific newsletters (Planet PostgreSQL, PostgreSQL Weekly, Changelog, console.dev) | 100–400 | Small audience, highest intent. |
| Hacker News front page | 500–3,000+ | Biggest single-day lever if it lands. Not plannable, only attemptable. |
| Existing 900 teams (email/in-app/referral ask) | 200–500 | Owned channel, controllable, ignored by most funding-PR plans. |
| Paid retargeting during launch window | Budget-dependent | Controllable lever to close the gap. |

Stacked total: 1,000–2,500 is realistic. As "press alone": no.
