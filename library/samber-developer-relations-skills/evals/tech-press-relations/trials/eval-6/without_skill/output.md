**Pushback on the assumption:** (a) isn't tier 1. Reach without topical fit is the AVE trap — a number that looks good in a recap but doesn't convert. 4.1M readers who care about consumer hardware are not your ICP; one dev-tooling piece in March (6 months old, no recurring beat) is a coincidence, not a signal they'll cover storage engines now.

### Tier 1 — (b) R. Lindqvist
Small list, perfect audience: 3,800 infra/platform engineers is close to 100% ICP overlap. 4 of the last 5 issues on storage engines means this is an active, current beat, not a one-off. Replying to every reader email signals a real relationship with an engaged audience — coverage here reads as credible, not as an ad. ### Tier 3 (opportunistic, not a pillar) — (a) J. Okonjo
Pitch if it's free to do so — the reach is real if it lands — but don't build launch timing around it and don't expect a hit rate. Frame it as a trend piece ("hardware/storage crossing into consumer relevance") rather than a developer-tooling pitch, since that's the beat they actually write. Budget zero follow-up effort.

### Unclassified — (d) S. Abara
