## Reputation table

| Activity                       | Points |
| ------------------------------ | ------ |
| Starting score                 | 10     |
| Post upvoted                   | +10    |
| Post downvoted                 | -10    |
| Post banned or removed         | -100   |
| Comment upvoted                | +50    |
| Comment downvoted              | -50    |

Floor 0. The author-claiming page says +1 per upvote on claimed articles; the reputation page says +10 per post upvote. Five comment upvotes from established members take a new account from 10 to 260.

## Limits per Squad

Each Squad sets its own gate; the composer shows it ("You need 1,000 reputation to post").

| Squad type                     | Who can post                                                            | Self-promotion                                      | Kind     |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Your own Squad                 | you choose: anyone, approval, minimum reputation, moderators only       | your rules, up to 10                                | enforced |
| Public Squad, default settings | all members; admins and moderators approve each post; edits re-approved | moderators decline against the rules card           | policed  |
| Public Squad, reputation gate  | members above the threshold (default 250), no review                    | rules card still applies; reports reach daily.dev   | enforced |
| Moderators-only Squad          | admins and moderators                                                   | outsiders cannot post; comment instead              | enforced |
| Private Squad                  | invite only                                                             | members only; not a reach surface                   | enforced |
| Verified Company Squad (yours) | as your own Squad                                                       | same content guidelines; posts are organic, not ads | enforced |

- Read the Squad's rules card and recent posts before the first post. A Squad full of tutorials and no product posts tells you its moderators' line.
- Public status is revocable for guideline breaches; a Squad can be suspended.
- Featuring in the directory is an editorial pick; ask at advocacy@daily.dev only after weeks of steady posting.

## Choosing paid over organic

All four rules are [self-set] except the last.

- **Boost** one post that already earns organic upvotes; boosting a weak post buys impressions on a post readers downvote.
- **Squad Boost** only after the Squad has 10+ quality posts; new members who land on an empty Squad leave.
- **Verified Company Squad** when you publish weekly and want auto-posting and day-one discovery for at least two quarters.
- **Not a fit** (daily.dev's own words): products developers do not use, a one-week test, a team with no technical content.
