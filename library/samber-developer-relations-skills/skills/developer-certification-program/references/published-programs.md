# Published vendor programs

Facts each vendor publishes about its own program, with the read date. Vendors change prices, windows and policies often: re-read the vendor's page before quoting a figure to anyone, and quote it with its date. These are choices other vendors made, never an industry standard.

CKA's published domain weights show a task-derived blueprint: troubleshooting 30%, cluster architecture and installation 25%, services and networking 20%, workloads and scheduling 15%, storage 10% (read 2026-08-28).

## Validity and renewal

| Program | Validity | Renewal path | Cost to holder |
|---|---|---|---|
| CKA | 2 years (3 years for credentials earned before April 1, 2024) | Retake | Full price |
| HashiCorp | 2 years | Pass same-level or higher exam; window opens 6 months before expiry | Full price |
| AWS | 3 years by retake, or 1 year by maintenance activities | Retake, higher-level exam, or maintenance activities within 90 days of expiry | 50% discount voucher, a benefit of holding the credential, usable for the renewal exam |
| Microsoft role-based and specialty | 1 year | Free renewal assessment in a 6-month window; passing adds 1 year from expiry | Free |
| Microsoft Fundamentals and Applied Skills | No expiry | None needed | - |

The spread is a strategy choice:

- Free short renewal maximises the number of current holders.
- Paid retake maximises revenue per credential and shrinks the holder base.
- CKA shortened its validity from three years to two for new holders only, which shows terms can change without touching existing credentials.

CKA re-aligns its exam with each Kubernetes minor release "within approximately 4 to 8 weeks" (read 2026-08-28). That window is a cost promise set by the product's release cadence.

## Exam and credential retirement

| Program | Rule | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft | May retire exams and credentials at any time; a retired credential stays active until it expires, or for two years after retirement if it never expires; holders whose expiry falls after retirement renew before the retirement date; an expired credential cannot be renewed, only re-earned | 2026-10-21 |
| Google Associate Android Developer | Retired, no new registrations; existing credentials stay valid through their expiry date | 2026-10-21 |

Both converge on one rule: retirement stops new issuance and never revokes what holders already earned.

## Portability

Open Badges 3.0 (1EdTech) is the current open format: each credential is signed by its issuer as a Verifiable Credential compatible with the W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0, and holders can store it in a wallet or post it to profiles the vendor does not own (read 2026-10-21). Adoption figures once circulated for Open Badges do not appear on 1EdTech's page; keep them out of a business case.

## How to use these figures

```
// ✓ Good - names who chose it, when it was read, and the trade-off bought
"We propose two years with a paid retake, as CKA and HashiCorp chose (read 2026-10-21),
and reject Microsoft's free annual renewal because partner fees must fund the program."

// ✗ Bad - three vendors' choices presented as a norm
"Industry standard is two years with a paid retake."
```
