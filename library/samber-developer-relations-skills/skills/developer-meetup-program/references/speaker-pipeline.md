# Speaker pipeline

Contents: sourcing channels · pipeline board · invitation examples · first-time speaker support · the pitch filter · speaker care.

## Sourcing channels, ranked by yield

1. **The room itself.** Regulars who built something. Ask directly and name the slot length - an open call reaches only people who already identify as speakers.
2. **Standing call for proposals.** A permanent form linked from every announcement and every event page. Fields: name, title, one-paragraph summary, format, takeaway, months available. Keep it under two minutes to fill.
3. **Regional conference speakers.** A talk given at a conference can be repeated locally with no new preparation. Ask during or right after the conference.
4. **Neighboring groups.** Trade speakers with the group one city or one technology over; cross-promote both audiences.
5. **Open-source maintainers of tools the room uses.** Remote slot if travel is impossible - a 20-minute video talk with live Q&A beats a cancelled edition.
6. **Local companies solving the group's problem.** The engineer who ran the migration, not the person who owns the product.
7. **Sponsors** - only through the same filter as anyone else, and never as a condition of sponsorship.

## Pipeline board

Keep one visible table.

- Two confirmed editions ahead is the health line.
- One confirmed edition ahead is a warning.
- Zero confirmed editions ahead is a fire.

| Date   | Format             | Speaker 1                                                  | Speaker 2                     | Status                      | Slides received |
| ------ | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------- |
| 12 May | Anchor + lightning | Confirmed - A. Rossi, "Postgres upgrades without downtime" | 2 lightning slots open        | Venue confirmed             | Yes             |
| 9 Jun  | Two-talk           | Provisional - M. Chen                                      | Prospect - maintainer, remote | Venue confirmed             | No              |
| 14 Jul | Study group        | n/a                                                        | n/a                           | Fallback, no speaker needed | n/a             |

Keeping one no-speaker-needed format in the plan (study group, demo night, open space) means a cancellation costs a format change, not an edition.

## Invitation examples

**Weak:**

> Hi! We run a monthly meetup and we'd love to have you speak sometime. Let me know if you're interested!

No date, no slot length, no audience, no reason it is this person. It asks the recipient to design the offer.

**Strong:**

> Hi Ana - I organize the Lyon Postgres meetup, around 40 backend engineers, mostly working on production databases. I saw your PGConf talk on zero-downtime major upgrades. Would you give a shortened version, 25 minutes plus questions, on Tuesday 12 May at 19:00? Venue is Acme's office near Part-Dieu, we cover dinner, and we record if you're comfortable with it. If 12 May doesn't work, 9 June is open.

Specific date, slot, audience, effort ("shortened version" - no new preparation), what is offered, one alternative date.

## First-time speaker support

Most of a healthy group's future speakers are sitting in the room, convinced they have nothing to say. Lower the barrier deliberately:

- Offer a 5-minute lightning slot rather than a full talk.
- Say explicitly that slides are optional and a terminal is fine.
- Offer a rehearsal with an organizer a week before, and offer to co-present the first time.
- Let them opt out of recording without explaining why.
- Tell them what the audience already knows, so they neither over-explain nor lose the room.
- Send a short written thank-you and the recording link afterwards - it is their portfolio piece, and it is what makes them say yes again.

## The pitch filter

Ask every speaker for the title, the one-line takeaway and the slides in advance. Review the slides against one test: **could this talk be given by someone who does not sell this product?** If not, it is a pitch.

Repairs, in order of preference:

1. Reframe around the problem and keep the product as the setting.
2. Move the product part into a 5-minute sponsor slot.
3. Decline.

Declining is cheaper than losing the regulars, who will not tell you why they stopped coming.

## Speaker care

- Confirm logistics in writing a week before: address, door code, arrival time, adapter, projector resolution, who meets them.
- Feed them.
- Introduce them to two people before the talk so they are not the stranger in the room.
- Send the recording and photos afterwards, and ask them who else should speak. The best sourcing channel is the speaker who just enjoyed the evening.
