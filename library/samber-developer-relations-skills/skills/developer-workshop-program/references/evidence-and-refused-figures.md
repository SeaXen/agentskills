No vendor publishes its internal grant criteria, a hands-on-to-lecture ratio, a shadow or reverse-shadow certification ladder, show-rate or completion benchmarks for account workshops, or a reminder cadence. Where the skill sets one, it is marked baseline or "this skill's construction".

## Evaluation practice

- Kirkpatrick's four levels: reaction, learning, behaviour, results.
- ATD, Effective Evaluation (2019, 779 talent-development professionals): 83% evaluate level 1, 80% level 2, 54% level 3, 38% level 4, 16% ROI; 40% judged their evaluation effective for business goals.
