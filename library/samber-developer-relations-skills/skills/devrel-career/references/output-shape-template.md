# Output shape template

Every deliverable is a short markdown document the user can act on the same week. Keep the skeleton below and drop sections the goal did not ask for.

```markdown
# {Portfolio audit | Skill-gap roadmap | Interview plan | Posting read | Offer scorecard}: {name}, targeting {sub-role} in {niche}

## Where you are

2-3 sentences: current seat, evidence in hand, the constraint that actually binds.

## Assessment

A table, one row per scored item - six signals, five ladder dimensions,
offer components, or posting symptoms - with score, the evidence link, and the gap.

## What to do next

3-5 steps, each with: the artefact or event that proves it, an owner-set date,
and the reason it is next rather than later.

## Review point

Date, plus the condition that means change course rather than push harder.

## Open questions

Anything you could not verify - pay figures, the reporting line, the format list -
and who to ask.
```

Never present a step whose completion cannot be observed from outside the user's head. Keep the assessment table shorter than the action list.
