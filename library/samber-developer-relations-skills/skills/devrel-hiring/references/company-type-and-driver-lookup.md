# Company type and funding driver lookup

Contents: the company-type bar · the four pillars · the seven funding drivers · per-sub-role hiring requirements.

Source: "A review of DevRel job titles and career progression" (Hoopy, May 2023, sponsored by Common Room); Matthew Revell, "The four pillars of developer relations" (developerrelations.com, 2018, updated 2025).

## Company-type bar

The same title means a different job across these columns. Tatiana Mikhaleva (Docker Captain, CNCF Ambassador): "'DevRel' and 'Developer Advocate' mean something different at every company." Recalibrate the whole scorecard against this table before reusing any prior posting.

| Dimension           | Early startup (pre-seed-A), team of one             | Growth / scale-up (B-D)                        | Big-tech enterprise                                     | OSS-first / foundation                                         |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hires for           | generalist across all four pillars, self-directed   | one pillar deep, collaborative                 | specialised; separate advocacy/education/community orgs | contributor-community credibility, maintainer instincts        |
| Portfolio must show | breadth plus one end-to-end outcome owned alone     | depth in the pillar, cross-functional evidence | sustained high-quality output at scale, in one lane     | real contribution history, triage and governance participation |
| "Senior" means      | founding generalist                                 | specialist plus team lead                      | anchored to the engineering ladder                      | deep upstream contributor, vendor-neutral                      |
| Coding depth        | high - builds the demos and the docs                | medium-high                                    | high - sample code, client libraries, API review        | very high - PRs, CVE fixes, working-group membership           |
| Content and travel  | very high volume, one person covers every channel   | structured, shared across a team               | large budgets, heavy conference presence                | community-led events and meetups                               |
| Success measured by | signups, early adoption, founder proximity          | activation and funnel metrics                  | attribution and product influence                       | contributor growth and project velocity                        |
| Comp structure      | low cash, meaningful equity, tiny conference budget | mid cash plus equity                           | high base plus stock, large travel budget               | foundation salary, or unpaid ambassador plus stipend           |

Foundation ambassador programs are frequently unpaid and gated on measurable contribution rather than communication skill - the CNCF Ambassador FAQ states the program is not a paid position and selects on a minimum DevStats contribution score. Foundations also hire salaried, vendor-neutral advocates; treat the two as different postings entirely, never the same template.

## The four pillars

| Pillar               | Definition                                                                   | Representative activities                                                                               |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Developer advocacy   | two-way bridge: developer needs into the company, guidance out to developers | speaking, sample apps and reference implementations, forum engagement, carrying feedback to product     |
| Developer marketing  | surfacing the technology where developers already are, without a sales push  | technical content marketing, technical SEO, developer social, event sponsorship, newsletters            |
| Developer enablement | removing friction once a developer tries the product                         | docs strategy, SDKs and tooling, getting-started guides, code samples, training and certification       |
| Developer community  | peer-to-peer learning and long-term engagement                               | community platforms, champions/ambassador programs, events and hackathons, recognising OSS contribution |

Boundaries are fuzzy on purpose - the same blog post can be marketing, advocacy and enablement at once. Ask which pillar the posting's outcomes actually sit in before writing the requirement list.

## The seven funding drivers

Developer adoption · sales enablement · developer enablement · product input · ecosystem and partnerships · contributor community · employer branding.

The driver determines the pillar mix, which determines what the role is measured on, whatever the title says:

- Early-stage companies fund enablement plus marketing.
- OSS projects fund community plus advocacy.
- Enterprise platforms fund enablement plus advocacy.
- Growth-stage companies fund marketing plus community.

Name the driver in the scorecard's mission line. A posting that cannot state its own driver is a posting nobody has decided the purpose of yet.

## Per-sub-role hiring requirements

| Sub-role                  | Screened on                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Outreach-focused advocate | some developer background, content creation, public speaking, networking, empathy, written communication                                                                                                        |
| Product-focused advocate  | a _recent_ developer background (stricter than outreach), content creation, networking, empathy                                                                                                                 |
| Community manager         | experience in an adjacent people field (support, social work), coaching and conflict-resolution communication, networking, empathy; less hands-on code except where community work includes OSS issue/PR triage |
| Developer educator        | pedagogy or teaching background, learner-journey design, ability to read code and instrument where learners get stuck                                                                                           |
| DX engineer               | strong engineering background, prior experience consuming similar products, technical writing                                                                                                                   |

A posting screening for all five profiles at once is screening for none of them - decide the sub-role before writing the requirement list.
