# Candidate sourcing channels

Contents: ranked channels · why generic job boards underperform here.

DevRel hiring skews toward channels that surface people already doing the work in public, not toward posting-and-waiting on a generic board - the same public-artefact logic that runs through the whole field's hiring practice.

## Ranked by what practitioners report actually working

- **Niche DevRel job boards** (e.g. devreljob.com) over generic boards - listings pre-filtered to companies already running a DevRel function.
- **Community scouting inside the product's own Slack or Discord** - identify already-engaged power users and ask directly. A documented pattern: a product's most active community member becomes its first developer advocate hire. Retention argument: someone who joined a community because they liked the product tends to stay past the hire.
- **Organic-content scouting** - watch blogs, YouTube, conference-speaker lists, and technical forums (Stack Overflow, Reddit, Hacker News) for people already producing the exact artefact this role needs, then approach directly.
- **Referrals from the existing DevRel network** - practitioners report referrals outperforming cold applications; visibility comes from commenting on job posts and engaging publicly in the same threads the team already reads.
- **Technology-specific Slack/Discord communities** - post in a dedicated `#jobs` channel, respecting each community's own rules; most forbid unsolicited direct messages to members.
- **DevRel-specific conferences** - in-person hiring-manager networking, lower volume, high fit.
- **Content-agency partner networks** - some agencies maintain vetted technical writers or engineers doing contract DevRel-adjacent work, giving a pipeline to convert a contractor to a hire.

## Why generic job boards and cold outreach underperform

The pool of people who both code credibly and communicate publicly is small and already visible through the channels above. A generic posting reaches a much larger pool of people who match the words in the ad but not the publicly-provable work sample this role actually needs - which is also why Artefact 3 scores shipped public work over resume claims.
