# Platform facts for a developer live show

Contents: YouTube Live; Twitch; LinkedIn Live; disclosure law; X; pages to re-read.

Labels: **[platform]** = the platform's own help, legal or developer pages; **[law]** = a regulator's own rules; **[practitioner]** = streamers, tool vendors, blogs. Each limit is marked **enforced** (the platform applies it) or **policed** (the community or channel owner punishes it).

## YouTube Live

| Limit or rule                                                                                                                                                                              | Kind                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| Live needs a verified channel and no live restriction in the past 90 days; hosts 16+ [platform]                                                                                            | enforced                |
| 10 active streams per channel, 3 per stream key; a daily live-creation cap with no published number [platform]                                                                             | enforced                |
| A strike blocks live streaming (the live intro page says 14 days; the strikes page gives one week for a first strike); a restricted account may not stream from another channel [platform] | enforced                |
| A stream matching another copyrighted live broadcast is restricted: no rebroadcast conference feeds or music [platform]                                                                    | enforced                |
| Streams under 12 h auto-archive; longer ones may not be captured at all [platform]                                                                                                         | enforced                |
| Dual stream on by default from Live Control Room; the vertical copy runs in the Shorts feed with no clickable links and cannot be added after start [platform]                             | enforced                |
| Mobile live has its own subscriber threshold, commonly quoted as 50 [practitioner]                                                                                                         | enforced                |
| Paid product placement and endorsement rules apply to live [platform]                                                                                                                      | enforced                |
| Product links in other creators' live chats                                                                                                                                                | policed by each channel |

Moderation toolset [platform]: standard and managing moderators; blocked words; hold potentially inappropriate messages (None, Basic, Strict); slow mode; subscribers-only chat with a minimum subscription age; members-only chat; live commentary mode (approved users only); delete, time out, hide user; Channel Activity panel; AI chat summary that moderators can turn off per stream.

Clips [platform]: viewers clip 5-60 seconds, on by default; hidden users cannot clip; blocked words apply to clip titles; clips from a live need DVR; the owner can turn a whole clip into a Short.

## Twitch

| Limit or rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Kind                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Live channels get priority over offline ones on shelves, left nav, Browse and search [platform]                                                                                                                                                                                                     | enforced (ranking)              |
| Simulcasting allowed for every channel; Affiliates and Partners regained it in October 2023 unless under an exclusivity deal; Twitch experience no worse than elsewhere, chat engagement included; nothing sending Twitch viewers to another streaming platform [platform, Simulcasting Guidelines] | enforced, warning first         |
| No third-party service combining other platforms' activity on the Twitch stream, merged chat named as the example [platform]; Twitch's CEO said in February 2026 enforcement would pause [practitioner reports]                                                                                     | written rule stands: follow it  |
| Past broadcasts off by default [practitioner]; kept 7 days, 14 for Affiliates, 60 for most Partners and Prime or Turbo users; Twitch support cannot recover a broadcast that was not saved [practitioner quoting Twitch help]                                                                       | enforced                        |
| 100-hour cap across Highlights and uploads; least-viewed Highlights deleted first when over [practitioner quoting Twitch]                                                                                                                                                                           | enforced                        |
| Raids can fail: blocked, target refuses raids from you, raiding party too large [platform, API docs]                                                                                                                                                                                                | enforced                        |
| Branded Content box required for paid or sponsored content, exempt for "your own products and services"; an employee streaming the employer's product is unclear, so tick it; no discovery penalty [platform, Branded Content Guidelines]                                                           | enforced                        |
| Affiliate thresholds, commonly quoted: 50 followers, 500 minutes on 7 days in 30, 3 average viewers [practitioner]                                                                                                                                                                                  | enforced                        |
| Raid only into channels that welcome it; thank and raid back, never demand reciprocity                                                                                                                                                                                                              | policed                         |
| Self-promotion in other channels' chats                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | policed by each channel's rules |

Moderation toolset [practitioner summaries of Twitch help]: AutoMod 0-4 holding messages for approval; blocked and permitted terms (blocked terms work with AutoMod off); Shield Mode, a saved preset switched on in one click (followers- or subscribers-only, verified chat, no first-time chatters, stricter AutoMod, bulk ban by phrase); phone- or email-verified chat by account age or follow age; raid settings (everyone, friends and followed channels, nobody) and a recent-raids review.

Discovery [platform]: category and tags drive Browse; "channels also watched by viewers" appears on channel pages; the mobile app has a clips feed.

## LinkedIn Live

| Limit or rule                                                                                                                                                            | Kind                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| More than 150 followers or connections, account or Page at least 30 days old, good standing, not mainland China [platform]                                               | enforced                   |
| No in-app camera: a broadcast tool or custom RTMP stream is required [platform]                                                                                          | enforced                   |
| Since 22 June 2026, every live is a scheduled event; can be scheduled minutes ahead, but LinkedIn advises at least two weeks to gather attendees [platform]              | enforced                   |
| Notifications at start go to event attendees (plus a subset of likely-viewing followers); attendees get reminders 3 days and 15 minutes before [platform]                | enforced                   |
| One destination per stream: event, profile or Page; members cannot stream to a Page [platform]                                                                           | enforced                   |
| Maximum 4 hours; recommended at least 10-15 minutes [platform]                                                                                                           | enforced / guideline       |
| Links in live comments render as plain text [platform]                                                                                                                   | enforced                   |
| No selling or promotional streams, no pre-recorded content, small sponsor logos, starting screen under 1-2 minutes, no meta streams about LinkedIn [platform guidelines] | policed by LinkedIn review |
| Pages lose the LinkedIn registration form on events created in a third-party tool [platform]                                                                             | enforced                   |
| Auto captions English only, enabled in the tool before going live [platform]                                                                                             | enforced                   |

Moderation [platform]: delete, report, mute commenters (mute covers that live and its replays); comment as the Page; LinkedIn recommends at least one dedicated moderator on a separate device.

## Disclosure law

- US: the FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR 255) require clear disclosure of a material connection, employment included, in the content itself; platform ticks supplement it [law].
- UK and EU teams follow their own advertising and unfair-commercial-practice rules; check with counsel before paying a streamer.
