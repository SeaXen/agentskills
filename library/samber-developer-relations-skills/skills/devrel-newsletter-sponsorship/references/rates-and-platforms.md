## 3. What the ratios show

- **Small niche lists cost more per subscriber.** On one card, the 16,000-subscriber database title costs about 3.6 times the 180,000-subscriber JavaScript title per thousand subscribers. Open rates also rise as lists shrink, so the gap narrows per opener but does not close.
- **Secondary units** price at roughly a third to a half of the primary; classified and job lines at about a twentieth to a sixth.
- **Bundles:** published discounts sit in the mid-teens percent, and only for multi-week bookings.
- **Flat per issue dominates.** One founder moved from CPM to a flat rate near 50,000 subscribers. Pay-per-click lives mainly inside networks; no developer title publishes a click guarantee.
- **Cross-genre averages** (unverified blog analysis): primary CPM in the mid-thirties of dollars, cost per click around five dollars, ad click-through near 2% on smaller primaries. Use only as a sanity check on a click-value price.

|---|---|---|---|

| beehiiv Ad Network | has ranged from 1,000 active subscribers to none on paid plans | not published | built into the beehiiv platform |
| Swapstack (beehiiv) | "most brands are looking for newsletters with at least 1,000 subscribers" | 10% plus card fees, charged to the brand | |
| Passionfroot | none stated | varies by booking route (low single digits to 15%) | creator storefront plus brand network |
| EthicalAds | 50,000 pageviews per month | about 2.50 per 1,000 pageviews to the publisher | web and docs ads, not email; filters known bots, hosting, VPN and proxy traffic |

Floors and fees change often; confirm on the platform before quoting. A network trades a tenth to two fifths of revenue, and some control over copy style, for demand without a sales cycle.

## 5. Reporting practice

- Large publishers promise click-through data after each campaign; one adds the job titles and companies of readers who engaged, an identity-level enrichment a small publisher should not copy without a privacy notice that covers it.
- One curator does not track opens at all, only link clicks, and so never reports opens.
- No developer publisher documents filtering security-scanner clicks in sponsor reports. Web ad networks do filter bots, hosting ranges and proxies; apply the same idea to email clicks and name the filter.
- Mail-privacy features have opened mail automatically since September 2021, so open-based figures in a media kit overstate reading.
- Tracked links fail when the sending platform's redirect breaks; treat a broken redirect on a sponsor link as a make-good.
