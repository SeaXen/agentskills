# Booking terms, acceptance policy and sponsor report templates

## Booking terms checklist

Confirm every line in writing before the invoice. The default column comes from published practice of a large developer newsletter publisher; adjust to your cadence.

| Term | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Slot and issue date | one named slot, one named issue | prevents "any issue this month" disputes |
| Price and currency | the card price for that slot | same price for every sponsor on the same date |
| Hold | released if not confirmed within 7 days | stops phantom bookings blocking real ones |
| Copy deadline | 2 full working days before send, or the Friday before a weekly send | leaves time for edits and the label check |
| Late copy | runs as sponsor name, one neutral line and the link, or moves to a later issue | the editor never writes promotional copy |
| Edit rights | publisher may require edits or reject copy, with a reason | the acceptance policy has teeth |
| Label | fixed house label, not negotiable | the sponsor cannot buy a softer label |
| Links | one link to the sponsor's own domain, sponsor's tracking parameters allowed | link safety and honest click counts |
| Changes and cancellation by sponsor | at least one week's notice; prepaid fees refunded for issues more than a week out | protects both sides near the send |
| Pause by publisher | if an issue is skipped or the programme pauses, move the slot to the next issue or refund in full | the charter's pause rule outranks a booking |
| Make-good | failed send, broken link or missing label rerun in the next comparable slot | the remedy is a slot, not a negotiation |
| Payment | large publishers: one invoice per booking, net 30. Solo and small publishers: paid before the copy deadline | names the invoicing entity or fiscal host; a small publisher cannot carry a bad debt |
| Warranty | sponsor warrants its claims and that the link stays live and safe; publisher may pull a unit whose claims or destination change before send | regulators can pursue both sides of a misleading ad |
| Performance pay | only on top of a flat fee above the floor; sponsor's tracking named; disputes settled on the publisher's filtered click count | the publisher cannot audit the sponsor's conversion data |
| Tax | invoice from the entity that books the revenue; check VAT or sales-tax treatment for cross-border sponsors, and whether a fiscal host accepts advertising income | advertising income is often treated differently from donations |
| Data | aggregate report only; no subscriber list, no clicker identities | consent and trust |
| Exclusivity | none unless bought, limited to the booked issue | exclusivity is inventory, priced separately |

## Acceptance policy template

```
We accept sponsors whose product serves [readers: role, stack].
We do not accept: [category list, e.g. gambling, token sales, products competing with ours].
Sponsors buy a labelled slot. They never buy editorial coverage, ranking or a review,
and they do not see the issue before it sends.
We may require edits or decline copy, with a reason, up to the copy deadline.
One link per unit, to the sponsor's own domain. No downloads, no unreadable shorteners.
Questions: [address].
```

Keep an internal refusal log beside it: date, sponsor, rule that applied, who decided.

## Sponsor report template

Send within a week of the issue.

```
Issue: #142, sent 2026-03-04 to 8,410 delivered addresses
Slot: primary, position 1, label "Sponsored"
Link: https://sponsor.example/start?utm_source=newsletter
Unique human clicks: 186   (filter: platform bot-click filter on; clicks within
                            10 s of delivery and all-links-clicked sends removed)
Total clicks recorded:  412 (before filtering)
Opens: directional only, inflated by mail-privacy features; not reported as reach
Median unique human clicks, last six issues' primary slots: 171
What we need back: signups or trials you attributed to this link
```

Never add subscriber names, emails or companies. If the privacy notice already allows company-level aggregation, report counts per company size band, never per company name.

## Media kit contents

A sponsor's buyer runs this checklist; answer it before they ask:

- Audience: role, seniority, company size, region, stack, and how you know.
- Engagement: median unique human clicks per sponsor slot over recent issues, with the filter named; delivered list size.
- Renewal: share of sponsors who booked again, and one past sponsor willing to talk.
- Slots: spec, position, label, cap per issue, sample issue link.
- Price: card or "on request", bundle rule, exclusivity add-on.
- Policy: link to the acceptance policy.
