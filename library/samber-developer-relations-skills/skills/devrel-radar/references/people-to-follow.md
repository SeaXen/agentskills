## How to use this list

- Match the Pillar column to the pillars the user actually owns; a depth-focused user gets 3-5 people on their pillars, not the whole table.
- Rank by cross-surface recurrence - the same person as podcast host, conference speaker, and hub author predicts continued relevance far better than any follower count, which is unobservable here anyway.
- Before linking any personal domain, confirm the page's content is about developer relations. Two entries above have personal domains that are parked or DNS-dead while the person publishes elsewhere.
- To find people beyond this table, mine the fetchable high-yield surfaces:
  - Podcast guest archives.
  - Conference speaker rosters.
  - The practice hub's guide bylines.
  - Community leadership pages.
  - For anyone working inside an open foundation, the code host, where project-lead tables name people and commit dates prove they are still active.

  Vendor and agency blogs cite practitioners in research posts as a secondary layer.

- Treat a role change as a source change: someone who moves from a devtool to a platform vendor keeps the expertise but changes the incentive. Re-read the disclosure column at every refresh.
