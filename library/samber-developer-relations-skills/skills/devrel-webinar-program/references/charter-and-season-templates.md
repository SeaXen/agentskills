## 4. Speaker outreach and brief

Outreach (internal engineer):

```
Subject: 40 minutes on <system> for our <series> on <date>

<Name>, developers keep asking <question from backlog>. You built the part that answers it.
Would you join <host> for a 40-minute conversation on <date> at <time>? Prep is one 30-minute call
and five questions sent a week ahead. <Manager> has agreed the time. Backup date: <date>.
```

Outreach (community member):

```
Subject: Speak on <series>: your answer on <thread> deserves a bigger room

<Name>, your answer to <thread> is the clearest explanation we have seen of <topic>.
We run <series>, a <cadence> session for <audience>. Would you present it for 20 minutes
with a host, on <date>? We handle the setup, the recording and the promotion, and you get
the recording to reuse.
```

Speaker brief (send a week ahead):

- The series' job and this episode's single takeaway.
- The audience's level, and the three questions they most likely bring.
- No product pitch; one next step at the end, chosen by the host.
- What the host may say about sales follow-up (the handoff contract's public wording).
- What the recording will be used for, and where it will be published.

## 6. Filled charter

```
Series name: Trace Talks
Job (first): activation of trial developers; Secondary: feature depth for existing users
Audience: backend and SRE engineers, intermediate; 55% North America, 35% Europe, 10% other
Format mix: engineer conversation and live demo alternating; one follow-along lab per
  season on the free tier (job = activation and the free tier exists, interview Q1/Q4)
Cadence and length: monthly, 40 minutes; lab 55 minutes
Slot: Tuesday 11:00 ET (17:00 Paris); test slot Thursday 11:00 ET in episodes 3 and 5
Season: 5 episodes, February to June
Speaker pool: 4 engineers (2 h/month each, manager-agreed), 1 customer (approval started
  8 weeks ahead), 2 community answerers invited; named backup for each episode
Replay policy: ungated chaptered copy within 48 h, captions and transcript; 2 clips per
  episode; lab environment gated behind free-tier sign-up
Budget: platform licence, captioning, lab cloud credits; no paid promotion in season one
Data retention: registrant and engagement data deleted 12 months after season end (baseline)
Handoff contract: Tiers A-D, signed with the mid-market sales lead; acceptance bar 50% (baseline)
Continue bar:
  - no metric among total reach, live attendance and returning share below the
    first-two-episode median on two of episodes 3-5
  - returning-attendee share: 20% or more by episode 4 (baseline)
  - job metric: attendees' 30-day first-trace rate ahead of matched non-attendees
  - stop rule: two seasons failing the job metric, or no backlog for a season
Open decisions: partner co-host in episode 4 needs the joint arrangement signed first
```
