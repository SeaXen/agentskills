# Channel templates

Adapt each template with the user's facts. `[brackets]` are slots; never fill one with an invented feature, number or customer. The fictional product here is "Ferrule", a database migration tool.

## Pinned comment (long-form)

```
Code and steps: [tagged link]. Stuck on [the step people ask about most]? Reply with your [error / version] and we answer here.
```

## Short bridge

- Related video field: the long-form the Short was cut from.
- Last line spoken and on screen: "Full fix, with the rollback case, is the [topic] video - tap the related video link on this Short."

## Post poll (only when there is a real question; vary it)

```
Next video: which one blocks you most?
- [Problem A]
- [Problem B]
- [Problem C]
- Something else (comment)
```

## Creator outreach

```
Subject: [their video title] + [your product category]

Hi [name], [one specific sentence about their video and what it taught your team].
We build [product], [one-line category]. Would a Collaboration on [problem their audience has] fit your channel? You pick the format and the words; we bring [access, data, engineer time, budget]. Any paid version is disclosed and ticked as paid promotion.
[Your name], [role]
```

## Creator brief

- The problem the viewer has, in one sentence.
- 2-3 true points, each with its proof (a docs page, a benchmark you can share).
- The tagged link for the top three description lines, and the line "tell us you came from [creator] at signup".
- Never say: [claims you cannot back], competitor names in a comparison you did not run.
- Disclosure: spoken, plus the paid promotion box. One review round, for accuracy only.

## Reply to a critical comment

```
Fair point on [their specific criticism]. [What is true: the limit, the bug, the trade-off.] [What to do now: workaround or issue link]. [If fixed later: we'll pin the update here.]
```

Never argue tone, never delete criticism that breaks no rule, and never ask colleagues to like the reply.
