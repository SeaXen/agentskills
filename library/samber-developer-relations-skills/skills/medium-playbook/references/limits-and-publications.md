# Medium limits and publication rules

Contents: platform limits; publication submission settings; established tech publications; company publications; Partner Program numbers.

**Enforced** means Medium or the publication's settings apply it automatically or by moderation. **Policed** means editors or curators apply it by judgement, unwritten or written only in a guideline.

## Your own publication's submission settings

Set these on purpose; writers cannot submit until a story meets them [platform].

- Mode: Open to all (any follower), Approved writers only, Closed. A company blog usually runs Approved writers only.
- Story type: drafts, published stories, or both. Accept published stories so engineers import under their own names first.
- Conditions: image, subtitle, minimum read time, a matching topic, paywalled. Leave "paywalled" off for vendor reach.
- Link a submission guidelines page; editors may not add large footers or calls to action to a writer's story, or remove it from the paywall, without the writer [platform].

- Pick by topic fit first, then by whether its rules allow your call to action.
- An established publication may require an original. Write a native story for it rather than importing a published post.
- Ask before a paid-product or trial mention; a free tier or open-source repo usually reads as acceptable.
- Never buy a sponsored slot inside a Medium publication: Medium bans third-party advertising there [platform]. A paid post on a network's own site is off Medium and outside these rules; get it in writing that it will not be syndicated into the network's Medium publications.

## Company publications

- Netflix TechBlog and The Airbnb Tech Blog still publish on Medium: engineering stories by named staff, no product pitch. They are closed to outside writers.
- Treat them as the model for a vendor publication: incident write-ups, migrations, benchmarks, architecture decisions.

## Partner Program numbers [platform]

- Eligibility: active paid membership, 1 published story, complete profile, eligible bank and tax country, 18+. No review.
- Payout: Stripe and tax information; $10 minimum. A $10 balance with incomplete payouts for 30 days makes enrollment inactive and removes stories from the paywall. 180 consecutive days inactive forfeits the whole balance.
- Earnings: member reading time (reads of 30 seconds or more), engagement points, Boost bonus, 5% external traffic bonus, extra share for search and email-notification reads, one-time amount per member conversion, read-ratio adjustment.
- Editor Partner Program: editors earn 25% of a claimed story's earnings on top of the writer's; publication needs its owner enrolled in the Partner Program, open submissions, $200+ monthly paywalled earnings, 1-100 stories and 2+ writers in 3 of 6 months.
- Writers report single- to low-double-digit dollars a month on small followings [practitioner]; Medium publishes no per-read rate.
