## Student offers and verification

- GitHub Education verifies students 13+ with documents (dated school ID, schedule, transcript, enrolment letter); an academic email becomes mandatory for a school once recent applicants from it verified with one.
- GitHub's Student Developer Pack onboards "5-10 new partners per year"; partner tools must be free to the student. Do not plan around being accepted.
- MongoDB gates its student credits on GitHub Student Developer Pack verification: piggybacking an existing verification is the cheapest control.
- Verification vendors claim discount abuse "can be as high as 35%" and that "more than 40%" of college students never receive a .edu address (SheerID, a vendor: marketing claims, not independent research).
- Graduation bridge: JetBrains gives former students 40% off for two years after the educational licence expires (raised from 25% in 2023).

## Faculty kits

- Databricks Free Edition for teaching: "no credit card, no cloud contract, and nothing to install"; each student signs up individually; assignments distributed "through an existing LMS or GitHub". Read the free tier's data-use terms before a school's privacy review does.
- MongoDB for Educators: $500 of Atlas credits for educators, free certification, an educator forum.
- US adoption deadlines: UCF 60 days before term; FAU April 15 for fall, October 15 for spring; Florida law posts required materials 45 days before term.

## Planning frame

W.K. Kellogg Foundation Logic Model Development Guide (2004): outputs are "the direct products of program activities"; outcomes are changes in participants' behaviour and skills, short-term within 1 to 3 years; impact lands within 7 to 10 years and "often occurs after the conclusion of project funding". The guide's planning order is "do the outcomes first".
