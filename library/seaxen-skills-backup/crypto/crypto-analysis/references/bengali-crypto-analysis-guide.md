## Common Pitfalls to Avoid
1. **Overleveraging** → High risk in volatile markets
2. **Ignoring Stop Loss** → Always set a stop loss
3. **Chasing Pumps** → Wait for confirmation (volume + breakout)
4. **FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)** → Stick to your strategy
5. **Ignoring Macro Trends** → Fed policy, DXY, stock market impact BTC
