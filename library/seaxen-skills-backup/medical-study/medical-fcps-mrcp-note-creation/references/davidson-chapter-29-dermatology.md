## Key Algorithms & Scoring Systems (Memorise)

| System | Use Case | Key Thresholds |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **PASI** | Psoriasis severity | <10 mild, 10-20 moderate, >20 severe |
| **EASI/SCORAD/POEM** | Atopic dermatitis severity | EASI <7 mild, 7-21 mod, >21 severe |
| **SCORTEN** | SJS/TEN mortality | ≥3 = 35% mortality |
| **RegiSCAR** | DRESS diagnosis | ≥5 = definite |
| **EUROSCAR** | AGEP diagnosis | ≥8 = confirmed |
| **UAS7** | Urticaria activity | Daily scoring ×7 days |
| **DLQI** | QoL impact | 0-30 (higher = worse) |
| **CASPAR** | Psoriatic arthritis | ≥3 points = PsA |
| **DAPSA** | PsA disease activity | Remission <4, LDA 4-14, MDA 15-28, HDA >28 |

## High-Yield FCPS/MRCP Topics (Priority Order)

### Tier 1: Core (Must Know - 80% of marks)
1. **Psoriasis** - variants, PASI, comorbidities, biologic algorithm
2. **Atopic dermatitis** - UK criteria, E-T-P-S-C-D steps, dupilumab/JAKi
3. **SJS/TEN/DRESS/AGEP** - differentiation, SCORTEN, RegiSCAR, EUROSCAR
4. **Bullous pemphigoid & pemphigus** - DIF patterns, management
5. **Skin tumours** - BCC, SCC, Melanoma (ABCDE, Breslow, TNM 8th)
6. **Urticaria/angioedema** - stepwise, HAE, omalizumab
7. **Cutaneous vasculitis** - palpable purpura approach, IgA, ANCA
8. **Skin infections** - cellulitis/erysipelas, nec fasc, HSV/VZV, scabies
9. **Connective tissue diseases** - SLE, DM, SSc criteria, antibodies
10. **Vitiligo** - segmental vs non-segmental, NB-UVB, JAKi
11. **Alopecia** - androgenetic, areata, scarring, trichoscopy
12. **HIV/immunocompromise** - KS, eosinophilic folliculitis, drug reactions
13. **Pregnancy dermatoses** - PUPPP, PG, ICP, teratogens
14. **Paediatric** - AD, haemangiomas, infections, genodermatoses

### Tier 2: High Probability (Should Know - 15%)
- Lichen planus, pityriasis rosea, erythroderma
- Contact dermatitis (patch testing)
- Erythema multiforme, figurate erythemas
- Inherited bullous (EB), dermatitis herpetiformis
- Targeted therapy/immunotherapy toxicity
- Fungal infections (dermatophytes, candidiasis)
- Benign melanocytic lesions, dysplastic naevus syndrome
- Pigmentation disorders (melasma, PIH, albinism)
- Nail disorders (psoriasis, LP, onychomycosis, systemic signs)
- Genodermatoses (ichthyoses, ectodermal dysplasia, phakomatoses)
- Post-transplant skin cancer surveillance

### Tier 3: Differentiator (Nice to Know - 5%)
- Rare papulosquamous (PRP, parapsoriasis, CTCL early)
- Rare bullous (pemphigus variants, bullous lupus, EBA)
- Rare drug reactions (SDRIFE, FDE variants)
- Rare infections (mycobacteria, leishmaniasis)
- Rare tumours (Merkel cell, DFSP, AFX, adnexal)
- Rare genodermatoses (Darier, Hailey-Hailey, porokeratosis)
