## High-Yield Viva Patterns for Public Health

Topics are heavily **calculation/methodology-based** rather than clinical. Viva Qs cluster around:

- "Define X and explain how it's calculated" (e.g., PAF, R₀, HIT, ICER)
- "When would you use X vs Y?" (e.g., RR vs OR, screening vs case-finding, SR vs MA fixed vs random)
- "What are the criteria for Z?" (Wilson-Jungner 10, PHEIC 4, IHR core capacities, GRADE downgrade)
- "Explain the policy framework" (Ottawa Charter 5 actions, Beattie 4 quadrants, COM-B, Walt-Gilson)
- "Interpret this 2×2 table" (RR/OR calculation from case-control)
- "What threshold triggers action?" (QRISK3 ≥10%, NICE £20-30K/QALY, CMR <1/10K/d)

## Mnemonics Style

Public health mnemonics are **acronym/abbreviation** style, not visual:

- **SnNout** (high Sensitivity → Negative rules OUT)
- **SpPin** (high Specificity → Positive rules IN)
- **P = I × D** (Prevalence = Incidence × Duration, steady state)
- **HIT = 1 - 1/R₀** (Herd Immunity Threshold)
- **R₀ = β × c × D** (transmissibility × contact rate × duration)
- **MUDPILES** (metabolic acidosis causes)
- **PARF = Pₑ(RR-1)/[1+Pₑ(RR-1)]** (Population Attributable Risk Formula)
- **GRADE RBI²** (Risk of bias, Inconsistency, Imprecision, Indirectness, Publication bias)
- **WHO HNII** (Humanity, Neutrality, Impartiality, Independence - humanitarian)
- **Haddon PEHAPS** (Pre/Event/Post × Host/Agent/Physical/Social)
