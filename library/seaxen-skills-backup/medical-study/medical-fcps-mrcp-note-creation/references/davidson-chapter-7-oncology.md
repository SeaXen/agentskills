### Oncology-Specific Template Sections
The Oncology Topic Template includes these sections beyond the generic template:
- **Staging & Classification** (TNM 8th / WHO / ICC tables)
- **Differential Diagnosis** (structured table with distinguishing features)
- **Management Algorithms** (mermaid flowcharts for each decision branch)
- **FCPS/MRCP High-Yield Summary** (exam-focused bullet table)
- **Viva Questions** (minimum 10, PACES/FCPS style)
- **Confusions & Mnemonics** (minimum 3 mnemonics)
- **Mind Map** (mermaid)
- **One-Page Revision Card** (condensed table)
- **Spaced Repetition Trackers** (24h/7d/15d/30d/90d)
- **Self-Test Scorecard** (section-by-section)
- **Local Navigation** (relative links to MOC, Hierarchy, Drugs, Related)

### Key Oncology-Specific Requirements
1. **TNM 8th Edition staging** for all solid tumours
2. **WHO/ICC classification** for haematological malignancies
3. **Molecular/biomarker tables** (driver mutations, IHC panels, NGS panels)
4. **Treatment algorithms** as mermaid flowcharts with trial evidence
5. **Drug regimen details** (doses, schedules, toxicity management)
6. **Resistance mechanisms** for targeted therapies
7. **Emerging therapies** section where applicable
