# Davidson chapter boundary validation

Use this when the user asks to keep a medicine chapter channel or MOC strictly inside a Davidson chapter.

## Proven workflow
1. Check the vault's roadmap/tracker notes first, especially `00_Index/Davidson Chapter Roadmap.md` and `00_Index/Davidson Chapter Progress Tracker.md`.
2. Confirm whether an adjacent chapter exists that could absorb the disputed topic.
3. If the boundary is still uncertain, use a reliable preview/searchable source snippet to confirm the chapter's actual internal headings.
4. Update the chapter rule note, pinned message, and MOC together so they stay consistent.

## Proven example from session
- Vault roadmap showed:
  - Chapter 28: Neurology
  - Chapter 29: Stroke medicine
- Google Books preview snippets for Davidson 24th edition supported Neurology chapter headings including:
  - Clinical examination of the nervous system
  - Functional anatomy and physiology
  - Localising lesions in the central nervous system
  - Neuroimaging
  - Neurophysiological testing
  - Headache syndromes
  - Functional neurological disorder
  - Epilepsy
  - Vestibular disorders
  - Disorders of sleep
  - Neuro-inflammatory diseases
  - Paraneoplastic neurological disorders
  - Neurodegenerative diseases
  - Movement disorders
  - Meningitis
  - Parenchymal viral / bacterial / parasitic CNS infections
- Therefore stroke was removed from the Neurology pinned message and starter topic queue, with an explicit note that it belongs under the separate Stroke medicine chapter.

## Why this matters
Without explicit boundary checking, chapter channels drift into neighboring Davidson chapters and the user's study structure becomes inconsistent.
