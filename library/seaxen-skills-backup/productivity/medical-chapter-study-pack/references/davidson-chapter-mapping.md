# Davidson Chapter Mapping (Validated Approach)

## Source
Davidson's Principles and Practice of Medicine, 24th Edition — Chapter 26: Rheumatology and Bone Disease

## Mapping Process

1. **Read chapter structure** — Identify major sections, subsections, and disease entities
2. **Create heading hubs** — One per major Davidson section
3. **Create topic-group hubs** — One per Davidson subsection/disease cluster
4. **Map disease topics** — Each named disease/entity becomes a scaffold file
5. **Preserve core notes** — High-yield diseases that deserve full notes at root level
6. **Validate hierarchy** — Cross-check scaffold count vs chapter content
