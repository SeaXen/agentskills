## Vault Conventions Proven In Session

- Keep a roadmap note at `00_Index/Davidson Chapter Roadmap.md`.
- Keep a progress tracker at `00_Index/Davidson Chapter Progress Tracker.md`.
- Use chapter-specific templates in `Templates/` for Davidson-aligned note creation.
- Keep the tracker columns simple: status, notes link, topic count, revision rounds, remarks.
- Preserve links to existing chapter MOCs where they already exist.

## Template Coverage Pattern

When creating Davidson-aligned templates, include:
- frontmatter with `davidson_part` and `davidson_chapter`
- the full exam-oriented study structure
- interpretation frameworks when relevant
- procedure mini-sections when relevant
- revision tracker, weak-points section, and exam-answer modes
