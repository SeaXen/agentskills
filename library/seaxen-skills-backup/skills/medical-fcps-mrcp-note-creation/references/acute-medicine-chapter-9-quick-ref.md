# Chapter 9 Acute Medicine - Quick Reference for FCPS/MRCP Note Creation

## Shock Classification Quick ID (Must Know)
| Shock Type | Mechanism | Key Features | CVP | CO | Skin |
|------------|-----------|--------------|-----|-----|------|
| **Hypovolaemic** | ↓ Preload | Cool, clammy, narrow pulse pressure | Low | Low | Cool |
| **Cardiogenic** | Pump failure | Cool, pulmonary oedema, JVD | High | Low | Cool |
| **Obstructive** | Mechanical obstruction | PE/tamponade/tension PTX, JVD | High | Low | Cool |
| **Distributive** | Vasodilation | **Warm**, bounding pulses, wide PP | Low | High | Warm |
| **Septic** | Distributive + myocardial depression | Warm → cold, lactate high, source control | Low → High | High → Low | Warm → cold |

## Sepsis-3 Definitions (Memorize)
| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Sepsis** | Life-threatening organ dysfunction (SOFA ≥2) due to infection |
| **Septic Shock** | Vasopressor requirement for MAP ≥65 **AND** lactate >2 mmol/L **AFTER** 30 mL/kg fluid |
| **qSOFA** | RR ≥22, SBP ≤100, GCS <15 (≥2 = high risk) |
| **SOFA** | 6-organ score (Resp, Coag, Liver, CV, CNS, Renal) |

## Hour-1 Bundle (Surviving Sepsis 2021)
1. **Lactate** (repeat if >2)
2. **Blood cultures** before antibiotics (2 sets)
3. **Broad-spectrum antibiotics** within 1 hour
4. **30 mL/kg crystalloid** for hypotension/lactate ≥4
5. **Vasopressors** if hypotensive during/after fluids (MAP ≥65)

## Vasopressor Ladder (Memorize Order)
| Step | Agent | Dose | Role |
|------|-------|------|------|
| **1st** | **Noradrenaline** | 0.01-2 µg/kg/min | α₁ > β₁, first-line |
| **2nd** | **Vasopressin** | 0.01-0.04 U/min | Add-on, spares catecholamines |
| **3rd** | **Adrenaline** | 0.01-1 µg/kg/min | α + β₁ + β₂, if NA high/refractory |
| **Adjunct** | **Hydrocortisone** | 200 mg/day IV | Vasopressor-dependent shock |
| **Rescue** | **Angiotensin II / ECMO** | Specialist | Refractory shock |

## Fluid Responsiveness (Dynamic > Static)
| Method | Indication | Requirements |
|--------|------------|--------------|
| **PLR + CO monitoring** | Gold standard | Any ventilated/non-ventilated |
| **SVV/PPV** | Ventilated patients | Sinus rhythm, TV ≥8 mL/kg, no ARR |
| **Mini-fluid challenge** | Quick bedside | 100 mL over 1 min |

## KDIGO AKI Staging (Memorize)
| Stage | Creatinine | Urine Output |
|-------|------------|--------------|
| **1** | 1.5-1.9× OR ↑26.5 µmol/L (0.3 mg/dL) in 48h | <0.5 mL/kg/h × 6-12h |
| **2** | 2.0-2.9× | <0.5 mL/kg/h × ≥12h |
| **3** | 3.0× OR ≥354 µmol/L OR RRT | <0.3 mL/kg/h × ≥24h OR anuria ≥12h |

## RRT Indications: AEIOU (Memorize)
| Letter | Indication | Threshold |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| **A**cidosis | Metabolic acidosis | pH <7.15 refractory |
| **E**lectrolytes | Hyperkalaemia | K⁺ >6.5 (or >6.0 + ECG changes) |
| **I**ngestions | Dialysable toxins | Methanol, EG, Lithium, Salicylate, Metformin |
| **O**verload | Fluid overload | Pulmonary oedema refractory to diuretics |
| **U**raemia | Uraemic complications | Pericarditis, encephalopathy, bleeding, neuropathy |

## RRT Modality Choice
| Modality | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| **IHD** | Haemodynamically stable, hypercatabolic, intoxication |
| **SLED** | Moderate instability | 6-12h sessions |
| **CRRT (CVVHDF)** | **Haemodynamically unstable**, sepsis, cerebral oedema | Continuous |
| **PD** | Resource-limited, no access | Lower clearance |

## King's College Criteria (ALF Transplant Referral)

### Paracetamol ALF - ANY ONE
1. **pH <7.30** (after resuscitation) **OR**
2. **ALL THREE**: INR >6.5 + Creatinine >300 µmol/L + Grade III/IV encephalopathy

### Non-Paracetamol ALF - ANY ONE
1. **INR >6.5** **OR**
2. **ANY THREE**: Age <10 or >40 + Drug/Indeterminate/HBV aetiology + Jaundice-Encephalopathy >7 days + INR >3.5 + Bilirubin >300 µmol/L

## DKA vs HHS Diagnostic Criteria (Must Differentiate)
| Feature | DKA | HHS |
|---------|-----|-----|
| **Glucose** | >11 mmol/L | **>30 mmol/L** |
| **Ketones (β-OHB)** | **≥3 mmol/L** | <3 mmol/L |
| **pH** | **<7.3** | >7.3 |
| **Bicarbonate** | **<15 mmol/L** | >15 mmol/L |
| **Osmolality** | <320 | **≥320 mOsm/kg** |
| **Dehydration** | 5-7 L | **8-10+ L** |
| **Abdominal pain** | Common (ketones) | Less common |
| **Mental status** | Alert → drowsy | **Confusion, coma, seizures** |
| **Mortality** | 1-5% | **10-20%** |

## DKA/HHS Management Protocol (JBDS)

### Fluid Resuscitation (FIRST - Before Insulin)
| Phase | Fluid | Rate |
|-------|-------|------|
| **0-1h** | 0.9% NaCl | **1 L** |
| **1-2h** | 0.9% NaCl | 1 L |
| **2-4h** | 0.9% NaCl | 1 L |
| **4-6h** | 0.9% NaCl | 1 L |
| **6-12h** | 0.9% → 0.45% if Na⁺ rising | 500 mL/h |
| **12-24h** | 0.45% NaCl / Hartmann's | 250-500 mL/h |

### FRII (Fixed-Rate Insulin Infusion)
- **Dose**: **0.1 U/kg/h** (actual body weight)
- **Preparation**: 50 U Actrapid in 49.5 mL 0.9% NaCl = 1 U/mL
- **Start**: After 1st hour fluids (or immediately if K⁺ >5.5)
- **Monitor**: Hourly glucose + K⁺
- **Transition**: Overlap SC insulin 1-2h BEFORE stopping FRII

### K⁺ Replacement Protocol (CRITICAL)
| Serum K⁺ | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **>5.5** | **Hold insulin**, rehydrate, recheck 1h |
| **4.0-5.5** | **40 mmol/L** in infusion (target 4-5.5 mmol/L) |
| **<4.0** | **STOP insulin**, **40 mmol IV bolus** (max 20 mmol/h), restart when K⁺ >4.0 |

### Cerebral Oedema Prevention (DKA)
- **Risk**: Children, new-onset T1DM, severe acidosis, rapid correction
- **Avoid**: Rapid Na⁺ rise, rapid fluid/insulin
- **Rx**: Mannitol 0.5-1 g/kg OR 3% saline 2-5 mL/kg, head 30°, reduce fluids, intubate/hyperventilate (PaCO₂ 30-35)

## ABG Interpretation - Stepwise (Memorize)
1. **pH** → acidaemia (<7.35) / alkalaemia (>7.45)
2. **PaCO₂** → respiratory (↑ = acidosis, ↓ = alkalosis)
3. **HCO₃⁻** → metabolic (↓ = acidosis, ↑ = alkalosis)
4. **Primary vs Compensatory** → direction of pH change
5. **Compensation** → formula appropriate?
6. **Anion Gap** = Na - (Cl + HCO₃) → **albumin-corrected**: AG + 2.5×(40-Alb)
7. **Delta-Delta** = ΔAG - ΔHCO₃ → **>+6** = metabolic alkalosis; **<-6** = NAGMA
8. **Clinical correlation**

## Compensation Formulas (Memorize)
| Disorder | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| **Metabolic Acidosis** | **Winter's**: PaCO₂ = 1.5×HCO₃ + 8 ± 2 |
| **Metabolic Alkalosis** | PaCO₂ = 0.7×HCO₃ + 20 ± 5 |
| **Resp Acidosis (acute)** | HCO₃ ↑ 1 per 10 PaCO₂ ↑ |
| **Resp Acidosis (chronic)** | HCO₃ ↑ 4 per 10 PaCO₂ ↑ |
| **Resp Alkalosis (acute)** | HCO₃ ↓ 2 per 10 PaCO₂ ↓ |
| **Resp Alkalosis (chronic)** | HCO₃ ↓ 5 per 10 PaCO₂ ↓ |

## Anion Gap MUDPILES (High AG Metabolic Acidosis)
**M**ethanol, **U**raemia, **D**KA, **P**araldehyde, **I**ron/**I**soniazid, **L**actic acidosis, **E**thylene glycol, **S**alicylate

## Electrolyte Emergency Ladders

### Hyperkalaemia (K⁺ >6.5 or ECG changes) - CARDIAC EMERGENCY
1. **Calcium gluconate 10% 10 mL IV** (membrane stabilisation) - **1st, minutes**
2. **Insulin 10 U + 25g dextrose** (shift K⁺ in) - 15-30 min
3. **Salbutamol 5-10 mg neb** (shift K⁺ in) - 15-30 min
4. **Sodium bicarbonate** (if acidosis) - 15-30 min
5. **Dialysis** (if renal failure/refractory) - definitive

### Hyponatraemia (Na⁺ <130) - NEUROLOGICAL EMERGENCY if <120 + symptoms
- **Volume status** → Urine osm/Na⁺ → **SIADH (euvolaemic) vs Hypovolaemic vs Hypervolaemic**
- **Correction**: **≤8/24h chronic**, **≤10-12/24h acute/symptomatic**
- **Severe symptomatic**: 3% NaCl 100-150 mL bolus, repeat if needed (max 12/24h)

### Hypernatraemia (Na⁺ >145)
- **Correction**: **≤10/24h chronic**, **≤12/24h acute**
- **Hypovolaemic**: 0.9% NaCl → 0.45%/D5W when euvolaemic
- **Euvolaemic (DI)**: D5W or 0.45% NaCl + Desmopressin (central DI)

### Hypocalcaemia (Ionised <1.0 / Corrected <2.0) - CARDIAC EMERGENCY if symptomatic
- **ECG**: Prolonged QT, Torsades risk
- **Acute symptomatic**: **Calcium gluconate 10% 10 mL IV over 10 min** (repeat q10min)
- **Correct Mg²⁺** if hypomagnesaemia (refractory without Mg²⁺)

### Hypercalcaemia (Corrected >2.6) - Severe if >3.5
1. **IV 0.9% NaCl** 2-4 L/day (target UOP >200 mL/h)
2. **Furosemide 40-80 mg** (AFTER volume repletion)
3. **Zoledronic acid 4 mg IV** over 15 min (or Pamidronate 60-90 mg)
4. **Denosumab 120 mg SC** (if bisphosphonate contraindicated)
5. **Calcitonin** 4-8 U/kg SC q6-12h (rapid, tachyphylaxis)
6. **Dialysis** (if severe + renal failure/refractory)

## ARDS Berlin Criteria (Memorize)
| Severity | PaO₂/FiO₂ | PEEP |
|----------|-----------|------|
| **Mild** | 200-300 | ≥5 |
| **Moderate** | 100-200 | ≥5 |
| **Severe** | <100 | ≥5 |

## ARDSnet Lung-Protective Ventilation
- **VT**: 6 mL/kg **PBW** (predicted body weight)
- **PEEP/FiO₂**: Use ARDSnet table (FiO₂ 0.3→PEEP 5 → FiO₂ 1.0→PEEP 18-24)
- **Plateau Pressure**: ≤30 cmH₂O
- **Driving Pressure**: ≤15 cmH₂O (ΔP = Pplat - PEEP)
- **Prone**: ≥12-16h/day if severe (P/F <150)
- **Neuromuscular blockade**: 48h if severe (cisatracurium)

## Delta-Delta (ΔΔ) Interpretation
- **ΔΔ ≈ 0** → Pure high AG metabolic acidosis
- **ΔΔ > +6** → **Concomitant metabolic alkalosis**
- **ΔΔ < -6** → **Concomitant normal AG metabolic acidosis**
