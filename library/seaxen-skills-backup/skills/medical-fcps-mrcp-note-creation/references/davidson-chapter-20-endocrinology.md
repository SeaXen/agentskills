## Priority FCPS/MRCP Topics (High-Yield)

### Endocrine Emergencies (CRITICAL - Must Know)
- **Adrenal Crisis**: 100mg hydrocortisone IV stat, fluids, glucose, monitor K+/glucose
- **Thyroid Storm**: Burch-Wartofsky score ≥45, PTU 600mg load → 200mg q6h + propranolol + iodine (after PTU) + hydrocortisone + fluids
- **Myxoedema Coma**: IV thyroxine 300-500mcg load + hydrocortisone + fluids + rewarming + ventilation
- **Pituitary Apoplexy**: Dexamethasone 4-8mg IV q6h + fluids + urgent MRI + transsphenoidal surgery if deteriorating
- **Phaeochromocytoma Crisis**: Phentolamine 1-5mg IV bolus (alpha-blockade) + beta-blockade ONLY after alpha-blockade
- **Hypocalcaemic Tetany**: 10% Calcium Gluconate 10-20ml IV over 10min (cardiac monitor) + check Mg first

### Cushing Syndrome & Adrenal (VERY HIGH)
- Cushing Syndrome workup: 24h UFC → DST → ACTH → HDDST/IPSS → imaging (cortisol suppression patterns)
- Cushing Disease vs Ectopic ACTH vs Adrenal Cushing vs Cyclical Cushing
- Adrenal Insufficiency: Primary vs Secondary vs Tertiary (Synacthen test interpretation)
- Adrenal Crisis (above)
- Phaeochromocytoma: ARR, metanephrines, CT/MRI/MIBG, alpha-blockade pre-op
- Primary Aldosteronism: ARR screening → confirmatory → CT → AVS (gold standard)
- Adrenal Incidentaloma workup: 1mg DST + metanephrines + aldosterone/renin + imaging criteria (HU, washout)
- CAH: 21-hydroxylase (salt-wasting vs simple virilising), 11β-hydroxylase, 17α-hydroxylase
- Cyclical Cushing

### Thyroid (VERY HIGH)
- Graves Disease: TRAb, RAI diffuse uptake, ophthalmopathy, pretibial myxoedema
- Toxic Nodular Goitre vs Thyroiditis (Subacute/Silent/Postpartum) vs Drug-induced
- Hypothyroidism: Hashimoto (TPOAb), central, subclinical, myxoedema coma
- Thyroid Nodules: TI-RADS classification, Bethesda cytology, FNA criteria
- Thyroid Cancer: Papillary (BRAF), Follicular (RAS, PAX8-PPARG), Medullary (calcitonin, CEA, RET), Anaplastic
- Goitre management
- Drug-induced thyroid dysfunction (amiodarone, lithium)

### Pituitary (HIGH)
- Acromegaly: IGF-1 screening → OGTT GH suppression → MRI → surgery first → somatostatin analogues → pegvisomant
- Prolactinoma: Prolactin >1000 diagnostic; cabergoline first-line; macro vs micro; pregnancy management
- TSH-secreting adenoma: TSH inappropriately normal/high + fT4/fT3 high
- Gonadotroph adenoma: FSH/LH/α-subunit elevation
- Non-functioning adenoma: mass effect, visual fields, hypopituitarism → surgery if macro
- Cushing Disease (covered in Adrenal)
- Hypopituitarism: order of loss (GH → LH/FSH → TSH → ACTH), Sheehan, lymphocytic hypophysitis
- Diabetes Insipidus vs SIADH: water deprivation test, desmopressin response, Schwartz-Bartter criteria
- Empty Sella Syndrome
- Craniopharyngioma
- Pituitary Apoplexy (emergency)

### Parathyroid/Calcium (HIGH)
- Primary HPT: surgical criteria (NICE/AAES), subtotal vs total+autotransplant
- FHH vs Primary HPT: Urine Ca/Cr <0.02 = FHH; CaSR mutation
- Secondary HPT (CKD): phosphate binders, Vit D analogues, cinacalcet
- Tertiary HPT: autonomous after secondary → surgery
- Hypoparathyroidism: post-thyroidectomy, autoimmune (APS-1), Mg deficiency
- Hypercalcaemia of malignancy: PTHrP (humoral), osteolytic, 1,25 Vit D mediated (lymphoma, sarcoid)
- Hypocalcaemia: acute (tetany) vs chronic, Mg deficiency → give Mg first
- Vitamin D: metabolism (D3→25OHD→1,25OHD), deficiency vs insufficiency, alfacalcidol/calcifediol

### MEN Syndromes (MEDIUM-HIGH)
- MEN1: MEN1 gene, 3Ps (Parathyroid, Pituitary, Pancreas) - surveillance, subtotal parathyroidectomy
- MEN2A: RET codon 634, MTC + Phaeo + HPT, prophylactic thyroidectomy by age 5
- MEN2B: RET M918T, MTC + Phaeo + Marfanoid/neuromas, thyroidectomy by age 1
- MEN4: CDKN1B, MEN1-like but no thymic carcinoid

### Neuroendocrine Tumours (MEDIUM)
- WHO 2019 Grading: NET G1 (<3% Ki-67), NET G2 (3-20%), NEC G3 (>20%), MiNEN
- Carcinoid syndrome: midgut NET → liver mets → serotonin → flushing + diarrhoea + R-sided valvular disease
- pNETs: Insulinoma (72h fast), Gastrinoma (ZES, secretin test), Glucagonoma (NME), VIPoma (WDHA), Somatostatinoma
- PRRT: 177Lu-DOTATATE (NETTER-1), SSTR+, NET G1/G2 progressive
- Bronchial/Kulchitsky NETs, Merkel Cell Carcinoma

### Special Topics (MEDIUM)
- Endocrine in Pregnancy: thyroid targets by trimester, adrenal, pituitary, diabetes
- Endocrine in Elderly: subclinical thyroid, frailty, bone, drug dosing
- Endocrine in CKD/Dialysis: CKD-MBD (phosphate binders, Vit D analogues, cinacalcet), drug dosing adjustments
- Immunotherapy Endocrinopathy: thyroiditis (5-10%), hypophysitis (hold ICI + steroids), adrenal insufficiency
- Transgender Hormone Therapy: MTF (oestrogen + anti-androgen), FTM (testosterone), monitoring
- Genetic Endocrinology: MODY (GCK, HNF1A, HNF4A), Neonatal DM (KCNJ11, ABCC8), Thyroid dysgenesis (PAX8, NKX2-1), Resistance syndromes (THRB, INSR)
- Lipodystrophy: CGL (generalised, metreleptin), FPLD (LMNA), acquired (AQL/APL), HIV-associated
- Metabolic Bone Disease: Osteoporosis (bisphosphonates, denosumab, teriparatide), Osteomalacia, Paget (zoledronic acid)
