## The 49 Missing Topics (grouped by subcategory)

### A. Early Pregnancy and Pregnancy Loss (4 topics — NEW HUB)

These were a complete omission from Round 1. Any maternal medicine chapter MUST include these.

1. **Spontaneous miscarriage** — 10-20% of confirmed pregnancies; types (threatened/inevitable/incomplete/complete/missed/septic); management (expectant/medical/surgical).
2. **Recurrent miscarriage** — ≥3 consecutive losses; APS (15%), uterine anomalies (10%), parental karyotype (3-5%); 70-80% live birth with treatment.
3. **Ectopic pregnancy** — 1-2% of pregnancies; ampullary (70%); methotrexate vs salpingectomy vs salpingostomy; rupture mortality.
4. **Termination of pregnancy** — UK Abortion Act 1967; medical (mifepristone + misoprostol) vs surgical; complications; contraception post-procedure.

### B. Obstetric Complications (9 topics — NEW HUB)

Critical omissions from Round 1. These are obstetric, not "medical" — but Davidson 24e Ch 32 covers them.

5. **Placenta praevia** — painless bleeding; risk factors (prior C-section, multiparity); planned C-section 36-37 weeks.
6. **Placental abruption** — painful bleeding + 'woody' uterus; DIC; fetal distress; risk factors (HTN, smoking, cocaine).
7. **Placenta accreta spectrum** — accreta/increta/percreta; prior C-section + praevia = highest risk; MDT delivery 34-36 weeks.
8. **Rhesus isoimmunisation** — anti-D 28 weeks; Kleihauer; HDFN spectrum; intrauterine transfusion.
9. **Pre-labour rupture of membranes (PROM/PPROM)** — PPROM = latency antibiotics + steroids + magnesium; chorioamnionitis signs.
10. **Fetal growth restriction (FGR/IUGR)** — EFW <10th centile; symmetric vs asymmetric; umbilical artery + MCA Doppler; timing of delivery.
11. **Macrosomia** — EFW >90th centile; GDM-related; shoulder dystocia risk; mode of delivery.
12. **Polyhydramnios** — AFI >25; causes (idiopathic 60%, fetal anomalies, GDM); amnioreduction; indomethacin.
13. **Oligohydramnios** — AFI <5; PPROM, renal anomalies, IUGR; pulmonary hypoplasia if early.
14. **Breech presentation and external cephalic version** — ECV 36-37 weeks; vaginal breech selection; C-section for failed ECV.

### C. Cardiac Disease Additions (3 topics)

15. **Arrhythmias in pregnancy** — SVT (adenosine); AF (LMWH, rate control); VT (DC cardioversion).
16. **Ischaemic heart disease in pregnancy** — MI 1-3 per 100,000; P-SCAD (30%); PCI preferred; avoid statins.
17. **Aortic disease in pregnancy** — Marfan (aortic root >45 mm contraindicated); dissection; coarctation; bicuspid aortic valve.

### D. Respiratory Disease Additions (2 topics)

18. **Cystic fibrosis in pregnancy** — pregnancy safe if FEV1 >60%; CFTR modulators; CFRD; pre-conception counselling.
19. **Sarcoidosis in pregnancy** — often improves (cortisol rise); cardiac sarcoid (arrhythmia, heart block); avoid methotrexate.

### E. Renal Disease Additions (2 topics)

20. **Glomerulonephritis in pregnancy** — IgA nephropathy, membranous, FSGS, lupus nephritis; proteinuria; CKD progression.
21. **Renal stones in pregnancy** — 1 in 1500; right > left; USS diagnosis; tamsulosin; stenting if obstructed + infected.

### F. Haematology Additions (3 topics)

22. **Thalassaemia in pregnancy** — alpha (Hb Barts hydrops) and beta (transfusion-dependent); partner screening; iron overload.
23. **Vitamin B12 and folate deficiency in pregnancy** — megaloblastic anaemia; NTD prevention; vegan mothers; bariatric.
24. **Myeloproliferative disorders in pregnancy** — ET, PV; JAK2 V617F; LMWH + aspirin; IFN-α if cytoreduction needed.

### G. Endocrine Disease Additions (3 topics)

25. **Parathyroid disease in pregnancy** — primary hyperparathyroidism (adenoma); neonatal hypocalcaemia; parathyroidectomy 2nd trimester if severe.
26. **Postpartum thyroiditis** — 5-10% postpartum; biphasic (hyper then hypo); TPO antibodies; self-limiting.
27. **Diabetes insipidus in pregnancy** — central, nephrogenic, gestational (vasopressinase); desmopressin safe.

### H. Liver/GI Disease Additions (5 topics)

28. **Inflammatory bowel disease in pregnancy** — Crohn's, UC; mesalazine/steroids/anti-TNF safe; avoid MTX, MMF.
29. **Acute pancreatitis in pregnancy** — gallstones (60-70%); supportive; ERCP if cholangitis; cholecystectomy 2nd trimester.
30. **Gallstones and biliary disease in pregnancy** — biliary colic, cholecystitis, choledocholithiasis; conservative vs surgical.
31. **Viral hepatitis C and E in pregnancy** — HCV (5% vertical, 15% with HIV); HEV (3rd-trimester mortality 20-30%, especially in endemic areas).
32. **Cirrhosis and portal hypertension in pregnancy** — variceal bleeding 5-30%; beta-blocker; band ligation; LMWH.

### I. Neurology Additions (3 topics)

33. **Myasthenia gravis in pregnancy** — variable course; pyridostigmine + steroids; AVOID magnesium sulphate; neonatal myasthenia.
34. **Idiopathic intracranial hypertension in pregnancy** — obese women of childbearing age; papilloedema; acetazolamide (limited data).
35. **Bell's palsy and cranial neuropathies in pregnancy** — 3x non-pregnant; prednisolone within 72 h; eye protection.

### J. Connective Tissue Additions (2 topics)

36. **Rheumatoid arthritis in pregnancy** — 50-75% improve; flares postpartum; sulfasalazine/HCQ/azathioprine safe; AVOID MTX.
37. **Scleroderma in pregnancy** — renal crisis (5-10% mortality); ACE inhibitors (life-saving despite pregnancy); PAH risk.

### K. Mental Health Additions (3 topics — was a single combined note in Round 1)

The Round 1 "Perinatal mental health disorders" note combined multiple disorders. For exam-readiness, split them.

38. **Antenatal and postnatal depression** — split from combined note; 10-15% prevalence; Whooley + EPDS screening; sertraline first-line.
39. **Postpartum psychosis** — split from combined note; 0.1-0.2%; psychiatric emergency; lithium; Mother and Baby Unit; 25-50% recurrence.
40. **Substance misuse in pregnancy** — alcohol (FASD), tobacco (abruption, IUGR, SIDS), opioids (NAS), stimulants; supervised OST.

### L. Labour and Delivery Additions (5 topics)

These are obstetric, not "medical" — but Davidson 24e Ch 32 covers them.

41. **Induction of labour** — 20-30% of pregnancies; Bishop score; prostaglandin E2, Foley catheter, ARM, oxytocin.
42. **Operative vaginal delivery** — ventouse vs forceps; outlet/low/mid-cavity; outlet OVD low risk; complications.
43. **Vaginal birth after caesarean (VBAC)** — 60-80% success; uterine rupture 0.5%; continuous CTG; tertiary unit.
44. **Fetal monitoring in labour (CTG)** — baseline/variability/decelerations; reassuring/non-reassuring/pathological; FBS if needed.
45. **Mastitis and breast abscess** — S. aureus; continue feeding; flucloxacillin; needle aspiration if abscess.

### M. Infections Additions (4 topics)

46. **Influenza in pregnancy** — disproportionately severe (3rd trimester); oseltamivir within 48 h; inactivated vaccine safe any trimester.
47. **Malaria in pregnancy** — endemic regions; placental sequestration; IPTp-SP; severe disease in non-immune.
48. **Group B Streptococcus in pregnancy** — universal screening 35-37 weeks (US) or risk-based (UK); intrapartum penicillin.
49. **Varicella zoster in pregnancy** — fetal varicella syndrome (<20 weeks); neonatal varicella (5 days before to 2 days after delivery); VZIG + aciclovir.

## Reusable Pre-Flight Checklist (for any pregnancy/obstetric chapter build)

If the chapter scope is "Maternal Medicine", "Obstetric Medicine", or "Pregnancy and Medical Disorders", the build is INCOMPLETE until ALL of these are covered:

- [ ] Early pregnancy loss (4 conditions) — often in a separate chapter, but if not, must be in this one
- [ ] Obstetric complications (placental disease, fetal growth, amniotic fluid, rupture of membranes) — common omission
- [ ] Organ-system additions beyond the standard list (CF, sarcoidosis, GN, MPN, B12/folate, thalassaemia, parathyroid, DI, IBD, cirrhosis, MG, IIH, Bell's palsy, etc.)
- [ ] Mental health split (depression, psychosis, substance misuse) — single combined note is too vague for exams
- [ ] Labour and delivery (induction, instrumental, VBAC, CTG, mastitis) — common omission in medical-focused chapters
- [ ] Infection specifics (influenza, malaria, GBS, varicella) — not just the umbrella "infections in pregnancy"

## The Scope Lesson (Most Important)

The chapter title "Maternal Medicine" implies a **broad umbrella** that includes:

1. **Medical disorders in pregnancy** (the obvious: HTN, DM, cardiac, renal, etc.)
2. **Obstetric complications** (placental, fetal growth, amniotic fluid, early pregnancy loss)
3. **Fetal medicine** (FGR, macrosomia, Rhesus, antenatal screening)
4. **Labour and delivery** (induction, instrumental, VBAC, CTG, mastitis)
5. **Postpartum conditions** (PPH, sepsis, mood disorders, lactation)

A Round 1 build that only covers category 1 produces 60% of the chapter. The other 40% are the obstetric, fetal, and intrapartum topics that are the main reason the chapter is called "Maternal Medicine" rather than "Medical Disorders in Pregnancy."

**Rule for greenfield builds:** when designing the heading-hub structure, ask: "What does the textbook cover that is NOT in any of the other Davidson chapters (Cardiology, Renal, Neurology, etc.)?" The answer for Maternal Medicine is: obstetric complications, fetal medicine, labour/delivery, early pregnancy loss, postpartum. These MUST be in the chapter.
