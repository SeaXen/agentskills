# FCPS/MRCP Exam Format Requirements

## FCPS (Fellowship of College of Physicians and Surgeons)

### Part 1
- **Format**: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
- **Duration**: 3 hours
- **Questions**: 100 MCQs
- **Format**: Single best answer (SBA) / Multiple true/false
- **Content**: Basic sciences, clinical medicine, pathology, pharmacology

### Part 2
- **Format**: Written papers + Clinical examination
- **Written**: 2 papers (3 hours each) - SAQs, SBAs
- **Clinical**: Long case (60 min) + Short cases (20 min each) + OSCE stations
- **Content**: Clinical medicine, practical skills, communication

## MRCP (Membership of Royal College of Physicians)

### Part 1
- **Format**: Two 3-hour papers (200 questions total)
- **Question Types**: Best of five (BOF), Multiple true/false
- **Content**: Basic sciences, clinical sciences, data interpretation

### Part 2 Written
- **Format**: Two 3-hour papers (200 questions total)
- **Question Types**: Best of five (BOF), Multiple true/false
- **Content**: Clinical medicine, data interpretation, images

### Part 2 Clinical (PACES)
- **Format**: 5 stations (20 minutes each)
- **Stations**:
  1. Respiratory + Abdominal examination
  2. History taking
  3. Cardiovascular + Neurological examination
  4. Communication skills + Ethics
  5. Integrated clinical assessment
- **Duration**: ~3 hours total

## Question Style Requirements

### Viva/SAQ Style
- **Structure**: Clinical scenario → Specific questions
- **Expected Answer**: Structured, prioritized, evidence-based
- **Key Elements**: Diagnosis, differential, investigations, management, complications, prognosis

### SBA/BOF Style
- **Stem**: Clinical vignette (50-150 words)
- **Lead-in**: Specific question
- **Options**: 5 options (A-E), single best answer
- **Explanation**: Required for learning notes

### PACES Station Style
- **Scenario**: Clinical problem
- **Tasks**: Examination, history, communication, management
- **Time**: 20 minutes per station
- **Marking**: Structured mark sheets

## Answer Style Guidelines

### For Written Answers (SAQ/SAQ)
1. **Structure**: Diagnosis → Differential → Investigations → Management → Complications → Prognosis
2. **Prioritize**: Life-threatening first, then organ-threatening, then symptom control
3. **Evidence**: Cite guidelines (EULAR, ACR, BSR, NICE) where applicable
4. **Specifics**: Drug names, doses, monitoring, durations

### For Viva/PACES
1. **Think aloud**: Demonstrate clinical reasoning
2. **Prioritize**: "First I would..." "The most important thing is..."
3. **Safety first**: "Red flags I would exclude..."
4. **Patient-centered**: "I would explain to the patient..."

## Content Depth Guidelines

### For Full Notes (FCPS/MRCP Level)
- **Epidemiology**: Incidence, prevalence, peak age, sex ratio, ethnicity
- **Pathophysiology**: Mechanisms with mermaid diagrams
- **Clinical**: Features by system, red flags, atypical presentations
- **Diagnosis**: Criteria, investigations, differential diagnosis table
- **Management**: Stepwise algorithms, drug doses, monitoring, escalation
- **Complications**: Early, late, prevention
- **Special populations**: Pregnancy, elderly, renal/hepatic impairment
- **Exam focus**: Viva questions, mnemonics, mind maps, one-page revision card

### Priority Levels
- **Critical**: Core topics (must know for exams)
- **High**: Frequently tested variants
- **Medium**: Less common but testable
- **Low**: Rare, niche knowledge
