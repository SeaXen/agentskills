## Multi-topic Synapse lectures

Synapse sometimes bundles multiple topics into one lecture (e.g., "Environmental Medicine, Oncology, Pain & palliative care, Ageing and disease"). Map to the PRIMARY topic's chapter number. In this case: Ch08 (Oncology) with the full topic list in the folder name.

## Key differences from 24th edition

- HIV/AIDS integrated into Infectious Disease (was separate Ch 14)
- Stroke medicine integrated into Neurology (was separate Ch 29)
- Hepatology merged into Gastroenterology
- New chapters: Maternal medicine (30), Adolescent medicine (31)
- Geriatrics renamed to "Older adult medicine and frailty"
